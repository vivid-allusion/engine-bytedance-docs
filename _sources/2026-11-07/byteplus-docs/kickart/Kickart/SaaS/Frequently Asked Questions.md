# Related to video creation

<span id="S4yfGqnJ"></span>
### **1. Q: What image and video formats does the platform support for uploading?** 

A: To ensure optimal generation results, please upload materials that meet the requirements: Supported image formats: JPG, PNG, JPEG; recommended file size: no larger than 8MB, resolution no less than 300×300; supported video formats: MP4, AVI, MOV; recommended file size: no larger than 50MB.

<span id="zEYrqMWq"></span>
### **2. ** Q: Where are all the images and videos generated by the platform stored?

A: All AI\-generated images and videos are automatically saved in My Space \- My Projects. Videos are additionally stored in My Space \- My Assets \- Creation History. You can preview, download, and manage them here.

<span id="4iKSo17O"></span>
### **3. Q: Can I reuse the materials I uploaded locally later?** 

A: Yes. All materials uploaded from your local device will be automatically saved in My Space \- My Assets \- Materials, making them easy to reuse across different projects.

<span id="8u66Tmei"></span>
### **4. Q: How long do I need to wait for video generation?** 

A: The generation time depends on the video complexity and the current task queue, and usually takes a few minutes. Leaving the conversation page will not terminate the process. All your creation progress will be automatically saved in My Space \- My Projects. You can click to continue creating.

<span id="RCA94xPA"></span>
### **5. Q: Why did my video generation fail with a prompt "Service busy" or "Violates platform rules"?** 

A: Generation failure may be caused by several reasons:


1. Service busy: Server resources are limited during peak hours. You may try again later. We are continuously expanding capacity to improve stability.

2. Content review: The materials you uploaded or text you entered may have triggered content security rules. Please check for sensitive words, content with copyright risks, etc.

3. Model limitations: The product or instructions you entered may exceed the current model’s capabilities.


<span id="ga0iopKQ"></span>
### **6. Q: I received a prompt "Violates platform rules", but I don’t know what the exact problem is. What should I do?** 

A: The materials you uploaded or text you entered may have triggered content security rules. Please check if they contain sensitive words, content with copyright risks, etc. If you cannot identify the non\-compliant elements, try simplifying your prompts and replacing them with cleaner background images before retrying.

<span id="hFg0t4Rd"></span>
### **7. The generated video looks great, but the download failed. What should I do?** 

A: This is an occasional rendering or network issue. Please locate the video within the project and try downloading again. If the problem persists after multiple attempts, please contact us and provide the project information.

<span id="SFi4I04c"></span>
### **8. Q: Why doesn’t anything happen when I click the Generate button?** 

A: This is usually because you have insufficient Creator Points balance. When your Creator Points are low, the Generate button will appear grayed out and unclickable. Hover your mouse over the button, and you will see the message: "Insufficient Creator Points. Please go to purchase." You can click the link to buy an add\-on pack or a new plan.

<span id="jhRFj4Nf"></span>
### **9. Q: Will my Creator Points be deducted if video generation fails?** 

A: No, it won't. The platform operates on a pre\-charge mechanism. When you click Generate, the system will first pre\-deduct the required Creator Points. If the task ultimately fails to generate (either due to a system error or failure to pass the review), the pre\-deducted points will be automatically refunded to your account.

# Related to AI effect templates

<span id="bbEOaQlo"></span>
### **1. Q: Does the AI effect template support customization?** 

A: Yes, it is supported. We recommend consulting with the frontline team by providing the platform and expected effect (a sample video is preferred). Since the capabilities and models required for each template effect vary, each request needs to be evaluated case by case, including feasibility assessment, timeline estimation, etc.

<span id="kNdOrHVi"></span>
### **2. Q: Does the AI effect template support fine\-tuning for a specific template?** 

A: Technically feasible, but not recommended. Our current standard template tools are designed to cover most user scenarios, especially Douyin effects. Fine\-tuning or modifying PE may cause bad cases in other scenarios.
