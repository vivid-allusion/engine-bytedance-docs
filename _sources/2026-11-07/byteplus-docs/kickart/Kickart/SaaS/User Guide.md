# Video Creation

1. Open the link to the Kickart product page: [https://console.byteplus.com/kickart](https://console.byteplus.com/kickart)

2. The page opens to the Video Creation tab by default, as shown in the image below. Below the main input field, you can toggle to the Product URL input mode, then paste or type the product URL into the input box.

3. If no product URL is available, you may click the Upload button located below the input field to upload local images or videos, as well as existing images or videos that you have created on this platform.

4. Users can freely select the model type required for video creation. Three modes are currently supported: "Kickart Pro", "Kickart Plus", and "Kickart Lite".<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260508120406-441271578253.png) </span>5. Interface Redirection: Upon completion of content input, the system will redirect you to the conversational interaction interface. Kickart will perform a professional analysis of the content you have submitted.<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260331064910-1461950391533.png) </span>6. Product Selling Points & Related Information Extraction: Kickart will extract product details, video parameters, and core product selling points. You can edit and adjust the content in the input fields within the purple highlighted areas.<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260330092251-118564081493.png) </span>7. After the configuration and adjustment of Product Content and Video Parameters are completed, the user shall enter the Product Selling Points confirmation stage. The user may modify, delete and add product selling points. Upon completion of information confirmation and adjustment, the user may first click Save, and then click Generate Video Creative. If no adjustment is made to the information, the user can directly click Generate Video Creative to enter the next step.<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260330094152-816545640775.png) </span>8. Video Creative Module: Upon clicking the Generate Video Creative button, Kickart will generate multiple creative proposals by integrating user inputs, product assets, and key selling points. Each proposal specifies the video style, core storyline, and estimated duration. The user may select a creative proposal and click the Generate button to proceed to the subsequent video generation phase.<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260508115133-1150928409357.png) </span>9. Instant Video from a Link: After clicking "Generate", Kickart will start creating based on the previously confirmed content and generate a video.<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260330095311-610420136549.png) </span>

# Al Effect Templates

1. Select Effects: Click "AI Effect Templates" in the left sidebar. Users can click the categories at the top to filter by different types. Templates are updated from time to time according to festivals, hot topics, and other trends.<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260330100133-1242642182035.png) </span>

2. Template API Details: Select a template and click to open it. and its detailed introduction will be displayed. The template supports API invocation and direct creation of the same style on the interface. If users need API information, they can click **API Documentation** on the right to view details.<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260330100242-758512935556.png) </span>

3. Create Identical Work: If users want to create an identical work directly, they can click the Create Identical Work button below the template on the right, and upload images and other content as required by the template.<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260330104817-1182296542023.png) </span>

# Ad Variations

1. On the creation homepage, select the **Ad variations** tab, upload or select a reference video from your assets to analyze.<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260512123937-1228360006729.png) </span>

2. Provide product information: use "auto\-fill" by entering a product URL to analyze its details, or choose "manual fill" to manually enter product details.<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260512123937-1328631414068.png) </span>

3. Click "generate video", system will create a video based on the reference video and product information.<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260512123938-706839311020.png) </span>

# Content Distribution

1. Click the "publish" button on any video asset. This action can be found in your asset library or on a completed video's detail page.<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260512125335-1390613242702.png) </span>

2. Select the accounts you want to publish to (you must bind your accounts first). Configure the publishing content. Use "global edit" for common settings or "single edit" to customize content for specific accounts. <span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260512125336-551746301161.png) </span>

3. In "task center", You may monitor publishing progress and status.<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260512125335-1520648574947.png) </span>
