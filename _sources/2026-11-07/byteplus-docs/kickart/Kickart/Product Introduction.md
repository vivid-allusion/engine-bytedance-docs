**What it is:**  BytePlus Kickart is an AI\-powered creative platform for commerce marketing. 

**What you provide:**  a product link, product images or videos, product details, or a reference ad. 

**What you get:**  a reviewable concept and storyboard, a generated marketing video, editing tools, reusable variations, and a path to publish and monitor content.

&nbsp;

Kickart brings planning, video generation, editing, publishing, analytics, and asset management into one workspace. It is built for e\-commerce marketers, brands, advertisers, content teams, and first\-time AI video creators who want a clear path from product to finished video.

New to Kickart? Read this page top to bottom: pick a workflow, get the inputs in **Before you begin** ready, then follow the **Quick Start**. Review each plan before you spend time generating the final video.

## Which workflow should I use?

The three creation workflows solve different jobs. Pick the one that matches the result you need now; you can move a generated video into Smart Editor later.


|**Workflow** |**Best for** |**What to provide** |**What happens next** |
|---|---|---|---|
|**Video Agent** |Creating a complete marketing video when you have a product but need help with the idea, script, and shots |A supported product URL, uploaded product assets, or assets from your Asset library, plus a short creative request |Kickart analyzes the product, proposes concepts, builds a storyboard, and generates a video after your review |
|**Ad Variations** |Producing multiple product ads from reference videos while keeping a useful structure, style, or pacing pattern |One or more products and one or more reference videos |Kickart creates an N products × M reference videos batch and tracks each variation as a task |
|**Smart Editor** |Refining a generated video, changing individual shots, or assembling a video with more direct control |An existing Kickart result or uploaded media, plus instructions for the clip or edit |You edit the timeline, split or remove clips, and generate replacement or additional clips up to 15 seconds each |


**Fast choice:**  use Video Agent for a first complete draft, Ad Variations for structured batch production, and Smart Editor when the job is precise revision rather than end\-to\-end planning.

## Before you begin

Get these ready before you open a workflow. You do not need every item for every route, but the first video moves faster when the inputs and the goal are clear.


* **Workspace access:**  an active BytePlus Kickart account with permission to create and, if needed, publish.

* **At least one product input:**  a supported product\-page link, product images or videos, or existing assets in your Asset library.

* **A clear brief:**  target market and language, intended audience, aspect ratio, duration, and one concrete goal.

* **Reference videos (for Ad Variations):**  one or more reference ads if you plan to run batch variations.

* **A connected account (for direct publishing):**  a connected TikTok account if you want to publish from Kickart. Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube are not open yet.


**Where to find things:**  the current workspace navigation shows Home, Agent, Variations, Editor, Inspiration, AI effect, Publish, and Asset. This guide uses the full feature names—Video Agent, Ad Variations, Smart Editor—that these tabs open.

## Quick Start

A first video can follow this sequence. The review points are part of the workflow, not optional cleanup after generation.

<div style="text-align: center">
<img src="https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260915100853-684384554700.png" width="900px" /></div>



1. **Add the product or assets.**  Paste a supported product\-page URL, upload local images or videos, or select existing material from your Asset library. Add a plain\-language request such as “Create a 20\-second vertical product demo for first\-time buyers.”

2. **Check the product insights.**  Review the extracted product name, selling points, audience, use cases, and any content requirements. Correct missing or inaccurate details before creative planning.

3. **Choose the production route.**  Select Video Agent for a new concept\-led video or Ad Variations when reference ads should guide a batch. Use Smart Editor directly if you already have footage and mainly need editing or new clips.

4. **Review the concept.**  Compare the proposed creative directions. Edit the message, audience, tone, offer, or scenario in conversation before moving forward. Keep only claims you can substantiate.

5. **Review the storyboard.**  Inspect the shot order, script, product placement, character and scene references, and frame images. Ask for changes or replace individual storyboard images before generating the final video.

6. **Generate the video.**  Confirm the language, aspect ratio, duration, model, and generation mode. Start generation only when the concept and storyboard reflect the brief.

7. **Refine the result.**  Open the video in Smart Editor to split or remove clips, adjust the sequence, replace a weak shot, add a generated clip, remove subtitles or background music where the option is available, and update audio.

8. **Publish and monitor.**  Download the asset or publish through a connected account. Use Publish & Analytics to review task status and available post\-performance data.


<div style="text-align: center">
<img src="https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260915100850-1070176074503.png" width="900px" /></div>


**Best first run:**  use one product, a small set of clear product images, one audience, and one specific video goal. Review both the concept and storyboard before generation. This gives you a useful baseline before you create more variations.

## Core Features

Kickart is organized into focused modules that share the same products, assets, and results. Each module below owns one part of the path from product to published video.

<span id="D4tvGCaT"></span>
### 1. Video Agent

**What it does:**  turns a product link or your assets into a complete marketing video (up to 60 seconds) through four reviewable steps.


* **URL parsing:**  read a supported product page and pull in the name, images, and description automatically.

* **Material understanding:**  extract selling points, target audience, and use scenarios from the product, so the video stays close to what you sell.

* **Creative concepts:**  compare several creative directions, then refine copy, audience, tone, or scenario in conversation.

* **Storyboard:**  review the shot\-by\-shot script and frames; adjust by chat or replace a storyboard image before the final render.

<span id="CNRRtsQN"></span>
### 2. Ad Variations

**What it does:**  produces new videos for more products from reference ads, reusing what works instead of copying the source.


* **Structured reuse:**  Kickart breaks a reference video down by structure, pacing, and presentation, then rebuilds the idea around your product.

* **Batch logic:**  pair N products with M reference videos, up to 10 of each per batch. Three products and four references create up to twelve tasks.

* **Localization:**  generate variations across supported languages, adapting models, scenes, and cultural details for each market.

* **Task view:**  track queued, generating, successful, and failed items in one place.

<img src="https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260915100854-904002890547.png" width="900px" /></div>


<span id="H6YrTHu3"></span>
### 3. Smart Editor

**What it does:**  refines a generated video or assembles one with direct control, without rebuilding the project.


* **Timeline editing:**  split and delete clips, reorder shots, change background music, and update audio.

* **Cleanup:**  erase subtitles and remove background music to cut down on unusable takes.

* **Shot\-level regeneration:**  generate a new clip from a prompt (up to 15 seconds each) or replace a weak shot while keeping the rest.

* **Models:**  Seedance 2.0 Fast, Mini, and Pro—choose the balance you need between speed and quality.

* **Generation modes:**  Omni Reference guides consistency from reference images, video, or audio; First & Last Frame generates the motion between a chosen opening and closing frame.

### 4. Inspiration

**What it does:**  a curated gallery of high\-performing commerce videos across categories, for benchmarking before you create.


* **Browse by category:**  explore trending, high\-converting references across product types.

* **Add as reference:**  send a video straight into Ad Variations to reuse its proven structure.

### 5. AI Effect Templates

**What it does:**  applies popular effects to your own material for fast, interactive\-style content.


* **Preview and apply:**  browse trending, seasonal, portrait, and playful effects, then create the same style with your material.

* **API access:**  reuse templates through the API, with online API key management and billing.

### 6. Publish & Analytics

**What it does:**  distributes finished videos to social accounts and brings performance data back into one dashboard.


* **Publishing:**  publish to TikTok now; Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube are not open yet.

* **Batch and account management:**  connect multiple accounts and edit or publish many videos across them at once.

* **Flexible setup:**  set titles, descriptions, covers, and schedules globally or per account.

* **Task center:**  track publishing status; failed tasks show the reason and support one\-click retry.

* **Analytics:**  compare views, plays, likes, favorites, and comments across connected channels and over time.

### 7. Asset

**What it does:**  keeps source material, generated work, and product information in one place for reuse. Shown as the Asset tab; earlier releases named it Space.


* **Three areas:**  Materials for uploaded or imported media, Creations for AI\-generated outputs, and the Product Library for structured product information.

* **Search and filter:**  find assets by name, tag, date, or media type instead of uploading again.


*Because modules share the same products and assets, work flows from one to the next—reuse a Product Library entry in Video Agent, an Inspiration reference in Ad Variations, or a generated video in Smart Editor.*
