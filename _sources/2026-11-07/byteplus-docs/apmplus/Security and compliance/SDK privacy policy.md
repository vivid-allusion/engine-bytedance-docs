As the provider of the "**APMPlus SDK**" product/service, **Beijing BytePlus Technology Co., Ltd.**  (hereinafter referred to as "we") attaches great importance to the protection of personal information. The "**APMPlus SDK**" provides developers with "**application performance monitoring features and services**". The "**APMPlus SDK**" products and/or services under this privacy statement include the SDKs for apps, Web, servers, mini programs, PCs, and OS that have APM capabilities (hereinafter referred to as "This service"). When an end user (hereinafter referred to as "you") uses websites or applications (including apps, mini programs, and web pages) developed and/or operated by developers (hereinafter referred to as "developer apps"), and if developers have integrated this service, we will provide related features and services to you through developer apps. We are committed to processing your personal information in accordance with this privacy policy and applicable laws, and to protecting your personal information and privacy.

**Special statement**:


1. **This privacy policy is not a substitute for the developer app's own privacy policy**. Developers must disclose their own privacy policy for their application to inform you of how they collect, process, and protect your personal information. If you seek access to your data, or try to correct, modify, or delete incorrect data, or you no longer wish to use applications integrated with the "**APMPlus SDK**", please contact the relevant developer (the personal information processor) directly.

2. The developer, as the personal information processor, determines the purposes and means of processing user data. When we provide specific business functions of the "**APMPlus SDK**" for developers, we only collect data on behalf of the developer and process it according to their entrustment and instructions.

3. This service, which you use through developer apps, is configured by developers according to their application needs and may vary by the version of the developer app you are using. If a developer app version does not include certain features or services of ours, the provisions in this privacy policy regarding those features, services, and related personal information processing will not apply.

4. Before integrating or using this service, developers must carefully read this privacy policy, confirm that they fully understand its content, and make appropriate choices in accordance with this privacy policy. At the same time, developers must provide this privacy policy to end users so that they can understand the relevant content and obtain end\-user consent. Unless otherwise stated, if developers or end users do not agree to this statement or its updates, they must immediately stop integrating and/or using this service.

We attach great importance to the security of user information and will endeavor to adopt reasonable security measures (including both technical and administrative measures) to protect your information, and to prevent the information you provide from being improperly used or accessed, publicly disclosed, used, modified, damaged, lost, or leaked without authorization.

<span id="82a451ca"></span>
#### Security technical measures

We will use reasonable and feasible means, such as industry\-standard encryption technologies and anonymization, to protect your information, and employ security protection mechanisms to prevent your information from being subject to malicious attacks.

<span id="ab7aee9f"></span>
#### Security management system

We have established a dedicated security department, security management systems, and data security procedures to safeguard your information security. We adopt strict data usage and access policies to ensure that only authorized personnel can access your information, and we conduct regular security audits of data and technology.

We will develop emergency response plans and activate them immediately upon the occurrence of a user information security incident, in an effort to prevent the impact and consequences of such incident from escalating. In the event of a user information security incident (such as leakage or loss), we will, in accordance with laws and regulations, promptly inform you of: the basic circumstances and possible impact of the security incident, the measures we have taken or will take to address it, recommendations for you to independently prevent and mitigate risks, and remedies available to you.

We will promptly notify you of the incident\-related information through push notifications, emails, letters, text messages, or other means. If it is impractical to notify each individual, we will issue a public announcement through reasonable and effective means. At the same time, we will also report the handling of the user information security incident to the relevant regulatory authorities as required.

#### Security tips

Although we have taken reasonable and effective measures described above and have complied with the standards required by relevant laws and regulations, please understand that due to technical limitations and the various malicious means that may exist in the internet industry, even with every effort to strengthen security measures, it is not always possible to guarantee 100% security of information. We will, however, do our utmost to ensure the security of the information you provide to us. You acknowledge and understand that the systems and communication networks through which you access our services may encounter issues due to factors beyond our control. Therefore, we strongly recommend that you take proactive measures to protect the security of user information, including but not limited to using complex passwords, changing your passwords regularly, and not disclosing your account credentials or other information to others.

We hereby specifically remind you that the user information protection measures provided in this privacy policy apply only to this service. ### 6. Protection provisions for minors

If the developer's application involves the processing of personal information of minors under the age of 14, the developer shall provide detailed notice to the guardians of such minors regarding the processing of their personal information and obtain the guardians' authorization and consent for such processing. The developer shall also establish dedicated personal information processing rules specifically for minors under the age of 14. In general, developers shall take necessary measures to ensure that all personal information transmitted to us (or collected through the SDK with the developer's permission) has obtained the corresponding legal basis.

If you are the guardian of a minor under the age of 14 and you discover that personal information of such minor has been collected without your authorization, or if you wish to access, copy, correct, supplement, delete, or withdraw consent for the processing of personal information of such minor, you may contact the application developer into which the SDK is integrated, or contact us through the contact information provided in this privacy policy. We will handle it promptly in accordance with legal requirements. If we discover that an application developer who integrates the SDK has transmitted to us (or allowed the SDK to collect) personal information of minors under the age of 14 without obtaining the authorization and consent of their guardians, we will proactively delete such information in accordance with applicable laws.
