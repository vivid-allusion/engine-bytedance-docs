Dear user,

Starting from **2026\-02\-06 00:00:00**, Application Performance Monitoring will offer a free quota for the 'data written' billing method. Charges will only apply according to the actual billing standard when usage exceeds the free quota. For detailed billing rules, see: [Billing details](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/apmplus/Server_side_billing_instructions).

The specific free quota is as follows:


* For Trace, a free quota of 25GB of data is provided per month.

* For Metrics, a free quota of 100 million data points is provided per month.
