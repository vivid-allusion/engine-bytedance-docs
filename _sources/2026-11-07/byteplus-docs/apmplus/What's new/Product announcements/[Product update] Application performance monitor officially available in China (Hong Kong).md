The Application Performance Monitor service will officially launch and be available for purchase in the China (Hong Kong) region starting at 20:00:00, March 23, 2026 (Beijing time). To ensure stability and fault tolerance, it is recommended to select the region closest to the target resource to reduce network latency and improve access speed. For more information about service availability, see: [Service region](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/apmplus/server-launching_region).


* The China (Hong Kong) region currently does not support service topology\-related features.

* The pricing in the China (Hong Kong) region is the same as in other overseas regions. For more details about pricing, see: [Server side billing explanation](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/apmplus/Server_side_billing_instructions).
