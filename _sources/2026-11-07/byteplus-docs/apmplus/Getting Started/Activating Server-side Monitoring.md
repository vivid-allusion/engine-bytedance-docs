To use Server\-side Monitoring, you need to activate it and its associated TLS and complete cross‑service authorization first. You can perform the following steps to activate the services.

<span id="Jx9OpuA0"></span>
## Prerequisites

You have created a  root user and completed real\-name authentication.   

<span id="yVtfPB5h"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [Server-side Monitoring console](https://console.byteplus.com/observe/apmplus-server).

2. Click **Authorize Now** in the Cross\-service access request pop\-up window to authorize Server\-side Monitoring to access TLS. After authorization is complete, you will return to the Server\-side Monitoring management page.

3. Click **Click to activate** to go to the service activation page to view the billing method and price, and then click **Buy now**.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="1">Server\-side Monitoring only supports the pay\-as\-you\-go method. If coupons exist in your account, the system will match available coupons based on the product or bill amount and deduct them by priority.   </div>
   

4. Activate TLS and enable server\-side log\-related capabilities.


<span id="npKNg32a"></span>
## What to do next


* After Server\-side Monitoring and its related services are activated, integrate your application and enable end\-to\-end monitoring based on the instructions in [How do I integrate Server-side Monitoring?](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/apmplus/How_to_integrate_server-side_monitoring)

* When you create a workload through VKE, you can quickly enable full\-chain Server\-side Monitoring. For more details, see [Application performance observability](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/vke/Application_performance_observability).
