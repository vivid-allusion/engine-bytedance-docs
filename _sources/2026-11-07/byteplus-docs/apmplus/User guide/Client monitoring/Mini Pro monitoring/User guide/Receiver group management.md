Configure members by group to enable them to receive schedule reports, dashboard subscriptions, and alerts.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">Make sure you have already trusted the email address of the APMPlus client; otherwise, subscription, schedule report, or alert emails may be blocked or moved to the spam folder.</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">Domestic email address: apmplus@cnmail.apmplus.volces.com</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">Overseas email address: apmplus@rfmail.apmplus.volces.com</div>


1. On the **receiver group management** page, click **create receiver group**.

2. In the **Create receive group** dialog box, after completing the configuration, click **Confirm**.

   Configuration description:

   
   |**Parameter** |**DescriptionS** |
   |---|---|
   |Name |Customize the receiver group name to identify this receiver group. |
   |Email |Enter the email address to receive subscription emails. |
   

3. Verify an email address.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">After the receiving group is created, you must verify the email addresses. Only verified email addresses can properly receive messages from APMPlus.   </div>
   

   1. Click the **Go to verify** button next to the recipient group mailbox. The platform will send a verification email to this address. Please verify your email yourself.

   2. View the email content in your mailbox, then verify it yourself.
