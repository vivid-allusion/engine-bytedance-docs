Members can be configured by group to receive schedule report subscriptions, dashboard subscriptions, and alerts.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">Ensure that you have already trusted the email address of the APMPlus client; otherwise, subscription, dashboard subscription, or alert emails may be blocked or moved to the spam folder.</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">Domestic email address: apmplus@cnmail.apmplus.volces.com</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">Overseas email address: apmplus@rfmail.apmplus.volces.com</div>


1. On the **receiver group management** page, click **create receiver group**.

2. In the **Create receive group** dialog, after completing the configuration, click **Confirm**.

   Configuration description

   
   |**Parameter** |**DescriptionS** |
   |---|---|
   |Name |Customize the receiving group name to identify this receiving group. |
   |Email |Enter the email address to receive subscriptions. |
   

3. Verify an email address

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">After you create the receiving group, you need to verify the email address. Only email addresses that have been successfully verified can receive messages from APMPlus.   </div>
   

   1. Click **Go to verify** next to the group mailbox. The platform will send a verification email to this email address. Please check your inbox and complete the verification.

   2. View the email content in your mailbox, then verify it yourself.
