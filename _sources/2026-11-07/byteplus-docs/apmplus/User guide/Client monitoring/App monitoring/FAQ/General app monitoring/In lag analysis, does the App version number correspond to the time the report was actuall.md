---
title: "In lag analysis, does the App version number correspond to the time the report was actually submitted, or to the time when the lag occurred?"
product: "Application Performance Management Plus"
product_code: "apmplus"
source: "https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/apmplus/In_lag_analysis_does_the_App_version_number_correspond_to_the_time_the_report_was_actually_submitted"
---

The version number at the time the lag is reported is used as the reference. Reporting usually occurs within 2 minutes after the lag. If there is a crash or the application is turned off, there may be a long interval between the lag collection time and the actual reporting time.
