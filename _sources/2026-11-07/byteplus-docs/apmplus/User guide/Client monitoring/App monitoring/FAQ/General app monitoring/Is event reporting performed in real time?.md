---
title: "Is event reporting performed in real time?"
product: "Application Performance Management Plus"
product_code: "apmplus"
source: "https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/apmplus/Is_event_reporting_performed_in_real_time"
---

Event records are not reported immediately. The client reporting rules are as follows: After the App starts, a report is triggered once; a report is triggered every 120 seconds thereafter; and a report is triggered when the App switches to the background.
