---
title: "How can you reduce the volume of disk detail reports?"
product: "Application Performance Management Plus"
product_code: "apmplus"
source: "https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/apmplus/How_can_you_reduce_the_volume_of_disk_detail_reports"
---

Both the disk metric sampling rate and the disk anomaly sampling rate affect disk details. To reduce disk detail reporting, you need to lower both the disk metric sampling rate and the disk anomaly sampling rate.
