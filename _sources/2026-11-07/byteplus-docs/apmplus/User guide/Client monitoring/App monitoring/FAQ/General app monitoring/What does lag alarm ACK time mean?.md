---
title: "What does lag alarm ACK time mean?"
product: "Application Performance Management Plus"
product_code: "apmplus"
source: "https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/apmplus/What_does_lag_alarm_ACK_time_mean"
---

The alarm has been acknowledged and needs to be suppressed for a period of time. The ACK duration can be modified in the **platform parameter settings**, with a default setting to block for 24 hours.
