---

After the client application is successfully created, click on the application to view the corresponding AppID and AppToken, which will be used for subsequent SDK integration.

If you enter the application and find that services such as App Monitoring, WebPro Monitoring, and PC Monitoring are not displayed, it means that your current account does not have read and write permissions for the relevant services in this application. The root user (APMPlus platform super administrator) needs to grant the sub\-account read and write permissions for the application and services. For details, see [Permission management](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/apmplus/Client_permissions_management).
