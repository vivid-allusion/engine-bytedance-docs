This section will explain how to edit the content of a slice.

<span id="61c4c924"></span>
# Operation steps

Under the "Slice details" tab on the knowledge base details page, you can edit the content of a slice.

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/d6cea35269924750a14609e460096024~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

Drag the mouse over the card and click the "Edit" button to modify the content of the slice.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">Editing a slice will re\-embed the slice and incur a fee. If the document is updated, the content of the edited slice will be overwritten by the updated document content.</div>
