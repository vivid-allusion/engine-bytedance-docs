This section explains how to edit a knowledge base.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">For official knowledge bases, only the description, tags, and CPU quota can be edited; other configurations cannot be modified.</div>


<span id="18195a1e"></span>
# Procedure


1. On the knowledge base list page, hover over the upper right corner of the card of the knowledge base you want to edit, click "Edit", and enter the **Edit knowledge base** page. Alternatively, on the knowledge base details page, click "Edit" in the upper right corner to also enter the **Edit knowledge base** page.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/7456d6926d154207abfe21ed0dde9265~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

2. On the **Edit knowledge base** page, you can modify the knowledge base description, tags, and CPU quota. After editing, click "Edit knowledge base" on the right.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/c9580a8529ee47bb886d84269a5e95a2~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>
