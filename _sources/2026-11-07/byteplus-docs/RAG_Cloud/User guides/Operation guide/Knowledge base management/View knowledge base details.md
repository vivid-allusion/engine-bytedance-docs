# Procedure

After creating a knowledge base, you may need to verify its contents and configuration. This guide provides step\-by\-step instructions on how to view the details of a knowledge base, including the original uploaded documents, the processed document slices, and its overall configuration.

In the knowledge base list, click the knowledge base card to go to the knowledge base details page.


1. On the details page, under the "Original documents" tab, you can view the list of documents uploaded to the knowledge base and their processing status.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/f4bed08aa1114f16927f9284d90aeac5~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

2. On the details page, under the "Slice details" tab, you can view the content of the processed document slices.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/b81377e2dfcd48c79e1f94ea9813c1df~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

3. Click "Knowledge base information" in the upper right corner to view the knowledge base configuration.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/9916e662fce04f849063462dca2a9c75~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>
