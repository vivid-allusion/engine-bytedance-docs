To lower the barrier for developers and allow users to quickly experience the effectiveness and value of the knowledge base, a **free 30\-day trial version** is now available! Each account can create a maximum of **one** free knowledge base!

## Note on the free knowledge base


|Specification details ||
|---|---|
|**Data types** |Images ✅ |
|**Number of documents** |500 documents per knowledge base |
|**Number of slices** |400,000 slices per knowledge base |
|**Retrievals** |300 times per day |
|**Model calls** |1,000,000 tokens free (quota applies only to the created free knowledge base) |
|**Free duration** |30 days (After expiration, calls are not allowed. You can only view the knowledge base or upgrade to resume usage. It is automatically deleted 30 days after expiration.) |


<span id="d446d221"></span>
## Free knowledge base upgrade

While using the free knowledge base, you can upgrade to the paid [Standard version knowledge base] at any time for production use. After clicking to upgrade, billing will immediately be based on the [Standard version knowledge base], and the free benefits will no longer apply. Please upgrade with caution.
