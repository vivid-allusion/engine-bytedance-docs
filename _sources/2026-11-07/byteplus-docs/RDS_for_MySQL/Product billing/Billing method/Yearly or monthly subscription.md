Using yearly/monthly subscription helps you greatly reduce costs. This billing method is applicable to long\-term use scenarios. This topic describes the billing description and settlement cycle when you purchase RDS for MySQL instances with the yearly/monthly subscription billing method.

<span id="introduction"></span>
## Introduction

In the scenario where RDS for MySQL needs to be used for a long time, you can purchase a yearly/monthly subscription mode instance for several months or up to three years and enjoy more favorable prices than a post paid billing instance. On the BytePlus platform, when you purchase an instance for more than one year, you enjoy an annual discount. For long\-term and stable businesses, yearly/monthly subscription is your best choice.

<span id="0lVljePG"></span>
## Purchase prerequisites


* A BytePlus account has been registered and completed verification. For the detailed method, refer to [Sign Up and Verify BytePlus account](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-signing-up-your-account).

* As per the regulations in the Chinese mainland, you must have completed real name registration before you can activate and use cloud services on regions in the Chinese mainland through BytePlus. For more details, refer to [Real name registration](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-real-name-registration).


<span id="billing-description"></span>
## Billing description

The monthly subscription (for 1 to 9 months) and yearly subscription (for 1 to 3 years) options are supported for yearly/monthly subscription instances. For specific discounts, refer to the actual order settlement page.

<span id="settlement-cycle"></span>
## Settlement cycle

The billing cycle of a yearly/monthly subscription RDS for MySQL instance is the duration of your subscription (subject to UTC+8). The start point of a billing cycle is the time when the resource is activated (accurate to the second) and the end point is 23:59:59 on the expiration date.

For example, when a yearly/monthly subscription instance is created at 14:00:00 on July 1, 2021 and the subscription duration is one month, then the billing cycle is: 14:00:00 on July 1, 2021 to 23:59:59 on August 1, 2021.

<span id="precautions"></span>
## Precautions

Even if your account is in arrears, you can still use the yearly/monthly subscription MySQL instances you have purchased normally. However, you cannot purchase a new instance or renew your subscription until you have paid the outstanding fees. For more details, refer to [Arrears](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/RDS_for_MySQL/arrears_and_expiration).
