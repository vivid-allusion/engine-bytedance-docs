In order to make the product specification names more consistent with their performance and application scenarios, and to make the product specification selection process easier, we have optimized the names of the specification types of Volcano Engine Cloud Database MySQL Edition according to the product naming system.

<span id="change-content"></span>
## Change content


* Rename "Shared type" to "General type"

* Rename "General type" to "Dedicated type".


<span id="change-time"></span>
## Change time

The change in the specification type name will take effect on November 15, 2024, Beijing time.

<span id="business-impact"></span>
## Business impact

None. The change in the specification type name will not affect the service's functions, performance, and billing rules, and will not affect the business. The service and contract content remain unchanged. The specification types displayed in newly generated orders, product consoles, etc. will display the new name.

<span id="subsequent-operations"></span>
## Subsequent operations

If you have any questions or need further support during use, you can contact our customer support team at any time.
