The operation and maintenance events of RDS for MySQL support delayed execution time. This article introduces the steps to postpone the execution time of operation and maintenance events.

<span id="prerequisites"></span>
## Prerequisites

The scheduled execution time of the operational event is more than 24 hours away.

<span id="steps"></span>
## Steps

<span id="procedure"></span>
## **Procedure**


1. Log on to the [RDS for MySQL console](https://console.byteplus.com/db/rds-mysql).

2. Select the region for the allowlist in the upper\-left corner of the top menu bar.

3. In the left navigation bar, click **Operation and maintenance Events** to enter the **Operation and maintenance events** list.

4. In the **Operation and Maintenance Event** list, click the **Postpone the execution time** button in the **Actions** column of the target operation and maintenance event.

5. In the pop\-up **Postpone the execution time** window, set the **Postponement date** and **Postponement time Period**, and click **OK**.
