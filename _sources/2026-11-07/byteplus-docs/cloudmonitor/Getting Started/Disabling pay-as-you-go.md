To stop receiving SMS or voice notifications from Alert Center or Event Center, disable pay\-as\-you\-go for Cloud Monitor.

<span id="e7824374"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [Cloud Monitor console](https://console.byteplus.com/cloud-monitor/overview).

2. On the **Overview** page, click **Disable Pay\-as\-you\-go**.

   <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_a60808d67889d7ddab6faba44d164439.png) </span>

3. In the **Are you sure to disable Pay\-as\-you\-go** message, click **Confirm**.

   <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_34114b6ffb30e9ce290049bac74391e4.png) </span>

   The page will display the **Disabled** message.
