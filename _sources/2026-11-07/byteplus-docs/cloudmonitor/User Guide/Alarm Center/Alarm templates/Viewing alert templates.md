You can view the details of an alert template in the Cloud Monitor console to check whether the alert policy generated by the alert template meets your business requirements.

<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## View an alert template


1. Log in to the [Cloud Monitor console](http://console.volcengine.com/cloud-monitor/overview). 

2. In the left navigation bar, click **Alert Center** \> **Alert Template**.

3. In the alert template list, click the name of the target alert template to view the details of the alert template.

   <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_6c7e80ba90355584fd287b197badbd9f.png) </span>

   The Overview tab displays the basic information and cloud service of the alert template.

   * **Basic information**: displays information about the alert template, such as the name, ID, description, alert level, and alert sending interval.

   * **Cloud Service**: displays the cloud service, dimension, rule name, and alert trigger condition configured in the alert template.
