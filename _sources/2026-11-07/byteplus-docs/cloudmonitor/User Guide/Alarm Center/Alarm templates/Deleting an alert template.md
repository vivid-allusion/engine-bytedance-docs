You can delete alert templates that you no longer need or that are not related to your business.

<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Delete an alert template

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">If you delete an alert template, or resources and rules in an alert template, the associated alert policies will be deleted and cannot be restored. Proceed with caution.</div>



1. Log in to the [Cloud Monitor console](http://console.volcengine.com/cloud-monitor/overview). 

2. In the left navigation bar, click **Alert Center** \> **Alert Template**.

3. Find the target alert template in the alert template list, and click **Delete** in the **Actions** column.

   <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_41b49bb70c8d6eddb7563cac74554fbd.png) </span>

4. In the message that appears, check the alert policies and object groups associated with the alert template, and then click **Delete**.

   <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_bdeebc6e6becc751e119305d3e506c2e.png) </span>
