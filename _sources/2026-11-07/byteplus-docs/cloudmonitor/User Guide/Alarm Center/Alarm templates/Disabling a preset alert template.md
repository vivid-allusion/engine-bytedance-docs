After you disable a preset alert template, the alert policy that is automatically generated by the system will be immediately deleted.

<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Disable a preset alert template


1. Log in to the [Cloud Monitor console](http://console.volcengine.com/cloud-monitor/overview). 

2. In the left navigation bar, click **Alert Center** \> **Alert Template**.

3. Click the **Preset alert template** tab.

4. On the **Preset alert template** page, in the **Enable** column, switch off the toggle to disable the preset alert template.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">warning   </div>
   

   * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-wrapper-indent="1">The alert policy will be immediately deleted after the preset alert template is disabled.   </div>
   

   * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-wrapper-indent="1">Disabling or enabling the preset alert template will not affect the contents of the alert policy.   </div>
