You can modify the name, resource range, alert rule, and notification method of an alert policy. After modification, the alert policy will take effect based on the new configuration.

<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Edit an alert policy


1. Log in to the [Cloud Monitor console](http://console.volcengine.com/cloud-monitor/overview). 

2. In the left navigation bar, click **Alert Center** \> **Alert Policy**.

3. Find the target alert policy and click **Edit** in the Actions column.

4. Configure the parameters of the alert policy. For parameter configuration, see [Creating alert policies](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/cloudmonitor/Creating-an-alarm-strategy).

5. After you confirm that the information is correct, click **Confirm** to complete the configuration.
