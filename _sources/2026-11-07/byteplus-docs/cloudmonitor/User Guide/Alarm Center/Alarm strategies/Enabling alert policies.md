After you enable an alert policy, the system starts monitoring metric data and triggers alerts based on the alert policy. You can enable alert policies based on your business requirements to flexibly detect and send alerts.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">After an alert policy is created, it is automatically enabled.</div>


<span id=".5ZCv55So5ZGK6K2m562W55Wl"></span>
## Enabe an alert policy


1. Log in to the [Cloud Monitor console](http://console.volcengine.com/cloud-monitor/overview). 

2. In the left navigation bar, click **Alert Center** \> **Alert policy**.

3. On the Alert policy page, click **Enable** in the Actions column that corresponds to the target alert policy.

4. In the message that appears, click **Enable** to enable the alert policy.


<span id=".5om56YeP5ZCv55So5ZGK6K2m562W55Wl"></span>
## Enable multiple alert policies at the same time


1. Log in to the [Cloud Monitor console](http://console.volcengine.com/cloud-monitor/overview). 

2. In the left navigation bar, click **Alert Center** \> **Alert Policy**.

3. On the **Alert policy** page, select multiple alert policies that you want to enable.

4. Click **Enable** above the list.

5. In the message that appears, click **Enable** to enable the alert policies.
