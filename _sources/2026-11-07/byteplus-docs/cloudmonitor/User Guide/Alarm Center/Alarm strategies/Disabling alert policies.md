After you disable an alert policy, the system stops monitoring the corresponding metric data. You can disable alert policies based on your business requirements to flexibly control the detection and generation of alerts.

<span id=".5YGc55So5ZGK6K2m562W55Wl"></span>
## Disable an alert policy


1. Log in to the [Cloud Monitor console](http://console.volcengine.com/cloud-monitor/overview). 

2. In the left navigation bar, click **Alert Center** \> **Alert Policy**.

3. On the Alert policy page, click **Disable** in the Actions column that corresponds to the target alert policy.

4. In the message that appears, click **Disable**.


<span id=".5om56YeP5YGc55So5ZGK6K2m562W55Wl"></span>
## Disable multiple alert policies at a time


1. Log in to the [Cloud Monitor console](http://console.volcengine.com/cloud-monitor/overview). 

2. In the left navigation bar, click **Alert Center** \> **Alert Policy**.

3. On the Alert policy page, select multiple alert policies that you want to disable.

4. Click **Disable** above the list.

5. In the message that appears, click **Confirm**.
