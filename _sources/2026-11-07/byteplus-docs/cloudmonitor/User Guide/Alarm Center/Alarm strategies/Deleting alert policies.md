After you delete an alert policy, the history data of generated alert still exists and can be retained for up to 14 days.

<span id="91848a43"></span>
## Prerequisites

You cannot delete an enabled alert policy. If you want to delete an enabled alert policy, disable it first. For more details, refer to [Disabling alert policies](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/cloudmonitor/Disabling-an-alarm-strategy).

<span id=".5Yig6Zmk5ZGK6K2m562W55Wl"></span>
## Delete an alert policy


1. Log in to the [Cloud Monitor console](http://console.volcengine.com/cloud-monitor/overview). 

2. In the left navigation bar, click **Alert Center** \> **Alert Policy**.

3. On the Alert policy page, click **Delete** in the Actions column that corresponds to the target alert policy.

4. In the message that appears, click **Delete** to delete the alert policy.


<span id=".5om56YeP5Yig6Zmk5ZGK6K2m562W55Wl"></span>
## Delete multiple alert policies at the same time


1. Log in to the [Cloud Monitor console](http://console.volcengine.com/cloud-monitor/overview). 

2. In the left navigation bar, click **Alert Center** \> **Alert Policy**.

3. On the Alert policy page, select multiple alert policies that you want to delete.

4. Click **Delete** above the list.

5. In the message that appears, click **Delete**.
