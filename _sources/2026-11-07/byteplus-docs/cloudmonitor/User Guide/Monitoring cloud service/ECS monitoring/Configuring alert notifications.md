You can configure an alert policy for ECS. When metrics do not meet expectations, alert notifications will be immediately sent to you.

<span id=".6K6-572u5ZGK6K2m6YCa55-l"></span>
## Configuring alert notifications


1. Log in to the [Cloud Monitor console](http://console.volcengine.com/cloud-monitor/overview).

2. In the left navigation bar, click **Cloud Service Monitor**. On the page that appears, click **ECS**.

3. In the top navigation bar, select the target region.

4. In the upper right corner of the page, click **Create alert policy**.

   <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_0df0c78b8037d510f4f6285efcb608b3.png) </span>

5. On the **Create alert policy** page, specify the parameters of the policy. ECS is automatically specified as the resource type and the specified region is automatically selected.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">For the configuration of other parameters, see <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/cloudmonitor/Creating-an-alarm-strategy">Creating alert policies</a>.   </div>
