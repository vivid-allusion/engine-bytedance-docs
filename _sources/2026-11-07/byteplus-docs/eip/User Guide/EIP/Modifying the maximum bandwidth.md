You can modify the maximum bandwidth of an Elastic IP Address (EIP) at any time according to your needs. The new bandwidth takes effect immediately after modification.

<span id=".5bi46KeE5Y-Y6YWN"></span>
## Normal modification

Normal modification means regularly modifying the maximum bandwidth of the EIP; the change takes effect immediately.

<span id=".5pON5L2c6K-05piO"></span>
### Instructions


* Reducing the maximum bandwidth may affect business traffic and cause packet loss. Evaluate the possible impacts on your business carefully and proceed with caution.

* Billing conversion requires the sum of your account's available balance and coupons applicable to the EIP to be at least USD 100.00, with the available balance not less than 0. For detailed steps, see the [Recharge guide](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/billing_account).


<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
### Procedure


1. Log in to [the EIP console](https://console.byteplus.com/eip/eips).

2. In the top menu bar, select the region and project of your target EIP.

3. Follow the steps below to modify the maximum bandwidth for a Pay\-as\-you\-go/Subscription EIP:

   
   <Tabs>
   <Tab zoneid="NEIRzhAsyz" title="Pay-as-you-go EIP">
   <TabTitle>Pay-as-you-go EIP</TabTitle>
   
   1. In the EIP list, click  **··· \> Change configuration** to the right of the target EIP.
   
   2. Select a metering method as needed for billing conversion.
   
   3. Modify the maximum bandwidth as needed.
   
   4. Follow the console guidance to review and confirm the legal terms.
   
   5. Click **Buy now** to complete the action.
   
   
   </Tab>
   <Tab zoneid="n1kw1BexpD" title="Subscription EIP">
   <TabTitle>Subscription EIP</TabTitle>
   
   1. In the EIP list, click  **··· \> Modify bandwidth** to the right of the target EIP.
   
   2. Modify the maximum bandwidth as needed.
   
   3. Click **Confirm order**.
   
   4. Follow the console guidance to review and confirm the legal terms, then click **Buy now**.
   
   5. Click **Next**, select a payment method, then click **Pay** to complete payment according to the chosen method.
   
   
   </Tab>
   </Tabs>
