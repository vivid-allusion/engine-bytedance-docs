## Elastic configuration


* Support flexible association and disassociation of cloud resources, including Elastic Compute Service (ECS), Internet NAT Gateway, secondary network interface, and Classic Load Balancer (CLB). Allow free switching of elastic IP addresses (EIPs) among various cloud resources to meet multiple internet access scenarios.

* You can adjust the maximum bandwidth of an EIP at any time based on business needs. The change takes effect immediately.


<span id=".6K6h6LS554G15rS7"></span>
## Flexible billing


* Support Subscription and Pay\-as\-you\-go. Pay\-as\-you\-go EIPs support adding to a Bandwidth Package to share bandwidth, reducing costs.

* Support instant conversions between Subscription and Pay\-by\-bandwidth, as well as between Pay\-by\-bandwidth and Pay\-by\-data\-transfer, meeting diverse billing requirements.


<span id=".5a6e5pe255uR5o6n"></span>
## Real\-time monitoring

By integrating with Cloud Monitor, business metrics can be monitored in real time. You can set metric thresholds and alarm notifications to promptly identify exceptions.
