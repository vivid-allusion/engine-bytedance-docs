This topic describes the impact of arrears on the Elastic IP Address (EIP). [View Available Balance](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-overview-2).

An account in arrears will cause the Pay\-as\-you\-go EIP to be suspended or even released. The system has set a grace period for resources. During this period, resources are retained, and you can continue to use them after you recharge and clear the overdue bill, without affecting resource features. After the grace period expires, the EIP resources will be forcibly reclaimed, and related data cannot be recovered. Regardless of whether your instance resources are suspended or released, you must settle the issued bills based on the actual charges.

## Subscription

Account in arrears has no impact on its unexpired Subscription EIPs. For more details, see [Expiration](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/eip/Expiration).
