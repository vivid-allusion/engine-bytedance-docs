If you no longer require your Subscription Elastic IP Addresses (EIPs), you can unsubscribe the EIPs to reduce costs. This topic describes how to unsubscribe Subscription EIPs.

<span id=".6YCA6K6i6K-05piO"></span>
## Unsubscription overview

Subscription EIP unsubscription includes seven\-day unconditional refund and non\-seven\-day refund.


|Unsubscription policy |7\-day unconditional refund |Non\-7\-day refund |
|---|---|---|
|Prerequisites |* You can request a refund within 7 days (≤ 7 x 24 hours) after the EIP order payment succeeds, provided you have not manually renewed or changed the bandwidth.<br><br>* During each calendar year (January 1 to December 31), one account can only unsubscribe from one EIP instance by default. |* The EIP resource is not suspended.<br><br>* Seven\-day unconditional refund is not supported. |
|Refund amount |The payments will be fully refunded to your available balance. The vouchers used are non\-refundable. |[Non-seven-day refund rules](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-unsubscription-management). |


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">If the unsubscription rules conflict with promotional rules for EIPs purchased in activities, the promotional rules prevail.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">If suspicious or malicious unsubscription occurs, the BytePlus platform reserves the right to deny your request.</div>

## Procedure


1. Log in to the EIP console[.](https://console.byteplus.com/eip/eips) 

2. Click **Billing \> Order management** at the top right.

3. On the Cancellation page, select **Unsubscription ( 7\-day no reason)**  or **Regular unsubscription**.

   * On the **Unsubscription ( 7\-day no reason)**  tab, click **Unsubscription** next to the target EIP.

   * On the **Regular unsubscription** tab, click **Unsubscription** next to the target EIP.

4. Confirm the EIP information to be unsubscribed. When unsubscribing an EIP, the associated subscription Elastic Compute Service (ECS) can be chosen to unsubscribe along with it.

   > For details, see [Elastic Compute Service Unsubscription](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ecs/Renewal-and-unsubscription#.6YCA6K6i6K-05piO).   
   

5. Click **Confirm** to pop up the confirmation window.

6. Click **Confirm** to complete the unsubscription.
