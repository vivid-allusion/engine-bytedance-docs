In accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, a Privacy Grievance Officer has been appointed to address complaints or other issues faced by users of our platform. A complaint may be submitted using the contact details set forth below. The complaint should provide: (i) the username of the relevant account holder, (ii) the specific content that is of concern, and (iii) the reason(s) for the request.
