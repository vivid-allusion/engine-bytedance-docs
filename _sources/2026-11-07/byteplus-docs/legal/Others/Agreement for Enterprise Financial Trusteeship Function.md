## **1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 “BytePlus Account” means the account you have registered on the BytePlus website and platform in order to use and purchase BytePlus products and services, as identified by your account name and account ID.

1.2 “Enterprise Financial Trusteeship Function” (or the “Function”) means a service provided by BytePlus that allows a Customer who has multiple BytePlus Accounts to manage all invoicing, billing, and payment processes through one single BytePlus Account, i.e. Main Accounts (as defined below), subject to verification and approval by BytePlus.

1.3 “Main Account” means the BytePlus Account that has been designated by the Customer to manage the Customer’s other BytePlus Account(s), i.e. Sub Accounts (as defined below). A Main Account can be associated with multiple “Sub Accounts”.

1.4 “Sub Account” means the BytePlus Account(s) that have been associated with a Customer’s Main Account. Each Sub Account can only be associated with only one Main Account. Sub Accounts cannot be associated with other Sub Accounts.

The Main Account and Sub Account(s) are accounts under the same customer name under the same entity on the BytePlus platform.

3.1 The Customer must establish the association between the Main Account and the Sub Account(s) through an invitation by the Main Account, which must be accepted by the Sub Account(s).

3.2 The Main Account and Sub Account(s) shall be associated upon acceptance by the Sub Accounts, and the Function will be activated.

3.3 BytePlus has the right to conduct secondary verification through mobile phone verification or other methods, by requiring customers to provide relevant identity certificates, or such other materials as BytePlus deems necessary to confirm that the conditions of use are met or that the association or delegation is duly authorized.

3.4 The Customer may set up or cancel further associations between the Main Account and one or more Sub Account(s) based on its own needs, subject to the terms set out herein.

## **4. Enterprise Financial Trusteeship Function**

4.1 The orders and consumption usage of the Sub Accounts shall be paid and managed by the Main Account. The billing threshold (as described in [BytePlus Payment Term](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/legal/docs/payment-terms)) of the Main Account and the Sub Account(s) would be unified and merged into one billing threshold, i.e. the Main Account's existing billing threshold, for future calculation and payment.

4.2 The financial status or the resource usage availability of the Sub Account shall be based on the Credit Limit of the Main Account.

4.3 The orders, bills and invoices of the Sub Accounts shall be automatically calculated on the Main Account, and consolidated and managed in the Main Account.

4.4 The invoicing data of the Sub Accounts shall be calculated and displayed on, and be issued to, the Main Account.

4.5. The consumption statements of the Sub Accounts shall be displayed on the Main Account.

4.6 All Sub Account(s) could use the discount benefits of the Main Account. If the discounts for the Main Account are inconsistent with the Sub Account(s), the discounts for the Sub Accounts shall become invalid and the discounts on the Main Account shall prevail.

4.7 The vouchers/coupons on the Sub Accounts will no longer be available after association with a Main Account. The vouchers/coupons on the Main Account can be used by the Sub Accounts after the association.

4.8 The configuration of the existing instance generated or purchased by Sub Account(s) cannot be changed after your activation of the Function.
