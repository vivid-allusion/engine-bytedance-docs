In accordance with the **Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021**, if you encounter any issues relating to prohibited content, intellectual property infringement, impersonation, or network abusive activities (including cyber attacks, spam, crawling, etc.) on our platform, please follow the steps below to raise a complaint:

&nbsp;

**Grievance Officer Contact Details：** 


* **Name:**  Rachel Zhang

* **Contact channel: ** 

   1. Please raise an  **“** [Abuse Report Ticket](https://www.byteplus.com/en/support-hub/report-abuse) **”**  using this link ([https://www.byteplus.com/en/support-hub/report-abuse](https://www.byteplus.com/en/support-hub/report-abuse)) and select the relevant abuse category and report your complaint.

   2. For grievances/complaints other than those covered under the [Abuse Report Ticket](https://www.byteplus.com/en/support-hub/report-abuse) categories or your complaint/issue remains unresolved, please contact us via email ([support@byteplus.com](mailto:support@byteplus.com)) for resolution of your complaint.


&nbsp;

**Note**


1. The complaint must include: (i) the resources (e.g., IP addresses, domain names) associated with BytePlus; (ii) a clear description of the specific content or issue of concern, supported by relevant evidence (e.g., copyright certificates, screenshots of illegal content, network abuse logs); and (iii) the reasons for the complaint.

2. We will use reasonable efforts to resolve the reported issue or complaint within **seven (7) days** from the date of receipt of such issue or complaint. Notwithstanding the foregoing, complaints including but not limited to copyright or other intellectual property claims may require additional time to process and resolve, depending on the nature and complexity of the claim.
