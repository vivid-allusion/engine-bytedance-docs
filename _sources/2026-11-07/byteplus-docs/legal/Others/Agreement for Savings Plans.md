1. ##  Definitions <span id="DB9UFC3m"></span>


Savings Plans is a discount benefit service provided by BytePlus. You can make a commitment to a spending amount over a period of time in advance in exchange for a lower discount on subsequent pay\-as\-you\-go bills. Savings Plans must be used with pay\-as\-you\-go instances. You can use the committed spend in a savings plan to offset the costs of pay\-as\-you\-go instances.

Savings Plans fall into the following two types:


* Diminishing Savings Plans: The committed spend is fixed throughout the term and is deducted until the balance is used up or the savings plan expires.

* Periodic Savings Plans: The committed spend is fixed for each period and deducted within a period without being carried forward to the next period. The committed spend resets at the beginning of each new period until the savings plan expires.
