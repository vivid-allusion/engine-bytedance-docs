1. ## Definitions <span id="k02ofzuy"></span>


Resource Transfer refers to the process of transferring resource instances (such as cloud server instances) purchased on BytePlus from one account to another. 

Specifically, by using Resource Transfer, the holder of the initiating account agrees to transfer the management and usage rights of the specified resource instances under such account to the holder of the receiving account, and the initiating account shall no longer be authorized to perform any management operations on such resource instances. If the holder of the initiating account has settled the usage fees corresponding to such resource instances prior to the Resource Transfer, such act shall constitute acknowledgment and consent that the receiving account holder may use the portion of the resource instance rights and interests corresponding to the settled fees that have been assigned by the holder of the initiating account.

This feature is mainly used to transfer resource ownership between different accounts, ensuring business continuity and management flexibility.


2. ## **Rules of Resource Transfer **  <span id="8QZVGHO2"></span>

1. **The applicable account types and service scope under Resource Transfer are limited. The applicable account type, resource instances scope, transfer and receiving conditions, transfer frequency, detailed operation procedures, and other relevant detailed rules shall be subject to the latest instructions on relevant pages of ** [Resource Transfer](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/Resource_Transfer?lang=en) **.** 

2. Fees related to the transfer of resource instances shall be charged and settled in accordance with the following rules:

* Prior to applying for the transfer of a resource instance, the initiating account holder shall settle any outstanding fees owed to Byteplus arising from the use of such resource instance (including arrears arising from used credit lines). For completed payment transactions related to the use of such resource instance (e.g., prepaid fees such as annual or monthly subscriptions, or settled portions of postpaid pay\-as\-you\-go fees), the rights and obligations related to contract performance, including contracts, bills and invoice applications in respect of such portion, shall be enjoyed and borne by the initiating account holder.

* After the transfer of the resource instance, any new outstanding fees arising from the receiving account’s use of such resource instance shall be paid by the receiving account holder. The rights and obligations related to contract performance in respect of such payments, including contracts, bills and invoice applications, shall be enjoyed and borne by the receiving account holder.

* **The specifications and configurations of the resource instances for which transfer is applied shall be transferred based on the current status of such resource instances at the time of transfer. After successful transfer, the receiving account holder may reset the transaction configurations of the resource instances in accordance with the rights and interests of its account. However, Byteplus shall not support any subscription cancellation or refund request filed on the grounds that the specifications and configurations at the time of transfer have been modified.** 

3. If the resource instance transfer process is terminated or fails for any reason (including but not limited to voluntary withdrawal by the initiating account, incomplete documentation, operational error, timeout, or rejection by the receiving account), the resource instance shall continue to be managed, used, and charged to the initiating account. The initiating account shall only lose its right to use the resource instance upon successful completion of the transfer.

During the resource instance transfer period, i.e., after you submit the transfer application and before the receiving account successfully accepts it, any operations performed on the resource instance by the initiating account, including configuration changes, renewal, and subscription cancellation, shall be deemed as acts of the initiating account holder. You shall be aware of and bear the operational risks associated with the account.

5. The actual time when the resource instance transfer operation is completed shall be subject to the records in the Byteplus system. Users performing the operation are requested to check the notifications sent by the system.

3. ## Notice on Risks of Resource Transfer <span id="0MhZ6afi"></span>

1. **The transfer operation only involves changing the information of the user to whom it belongs, while other information remains unchanged, such as expiration time, renewal type, etc. That is, the receiving account may obtain all configuration information and resource data carried by the resource instance at the time of transfer. Meanwhile, if the initiating account holder has bound any associated services to the resource instance, the transfer may affect the normal operation of such services. To ensure your business security, please exercise caution in the operation and promptly make necessary preparations before the transfer, including removing the relevant dependencies, clearing and backing up data, and making business adjustments.** 

2. **If you perform risk operations such as modification or change on instances or data, you must pay attention to disaster recovery and fault tolerance capabilities of the instances to ensure data security. ** 

3. **If you plan to configure or modify data for instance (including but not limited to ECS), it is recommended to create a snapshot in advance. **
