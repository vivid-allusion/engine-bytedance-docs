<span id="what-information-we-collect"></span>
## What Information We Collect

We collect your personal data in three ways: Information You Provide, Automatically Collected Information and Information From Other Sources. More detail is provided below.

**Information You Provide**


* **Account Data**: When you register an account in order to access the Services on behalf of your organization, you may provide us with personal data such as your username or account number/ID, password, name, email address, country/region, and professional information (company name, job title, contact details, company access code, etc.).

* **Financial Data**: When you purchase access to or use of the Services on behalf of your organization, we collect payment information, such as your payment card details.

* **Communications Data**: When you contact or otherwise communicate with us, such as when you submit complaints, inquiries, or other requests, we receive any information you provide in such communications.

* **Marketing Data**: When you participate in marketing or promotional activities organized by us, our affiliates or third parties, you may be required to provide personal data such as your name, correspondence address, and contact details.

* **User Content Data**: When you interact with select features of the Services, such as ModelArk's Playground or Starter Apps, we process the content you provide, upload, or generate, such as text, audio, images, videos, files, feedback, and prompt inputs and corresponding outputs. We request that you abstain from disclosing or otherwise providing personal or sensitive data, such as passwords, credit card numbers, or other confidential information, during chat interactions.


**Automatically Collected Information**


* **Technical Data**: We collect device, browser, and network connection related information when you access the Services, such as your device model and device ID, operating system, browser version, IP address, service logs (including error logs), and performance data. We automatically assign you a user ID.

* **Usage Data**: We collect information about how you interact with the Services, such as your token consumption, use logs, and the duration, frequency, and approximate location (which may be derived from your IP address) of your use of the Services, as well as metadata associated with your User Content Data.


**Information From Other Sources**


* **Third Party Service Data**: Where available, if you choose to sign up or log in to the Services using, or link your account to, a third party service, we may collect information from the third party service, such as your username and email address.

* **Information From Others**: We may receive information about you from others, such as your organisation, publicly\-available sources, and more. We also collect information about you if you are mentioned in User Content Data or in a complaint, appeal, request, or feedback submitted by a user or third party.

* **Cookies**: We use cookies and similar tracking technologies (collectively "Cookies") to operate and provide the Services. To learn more about our use of Cookies and your cookie\-related choices, please visit our [Cookie Policy](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/legal/docs-cookie-policy).
