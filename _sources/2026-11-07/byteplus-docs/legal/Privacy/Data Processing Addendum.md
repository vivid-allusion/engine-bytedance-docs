This BytePlus Data Processing Addendum ("**DPA**") forms part of the [Customer Agreement](https://docs.byteplus.com/legal/docs/customer-agreement) between BytePlus and Customer and governs the processing by BytePlus of Customer Data in connection with the Agreement. This DPA shall apply to all Agreements with an Effective Date on or after 1 June 2026. For the avoidance of doubt, where an Agreement has an Effective Date prior to 1 June 2026, the version of the DPA in effect at the time of such Agreement's Effective Date shall continue to govern and apply to that Agreement. Unless indicated otherwise, if there is any conflict between the provisions of this DPA and the remainder of the Agreement, this DPA shall prevail to the extent of such conflict.

This DPA includes and incorporates the Standard Terms and Conditions and the Exhibits attached hereto. Capitalized terms not defined herein shall have the meaning set forth in the Agreement.

**Standard Terms and Conditions**

**1. Definitions**

1.1 The following defined terms apply to this DPA:

" **Customer Data ** " means any Personal Data that is Processed by BytePlus on behalf of Customer in connection with its provision of Services to Customer under the Agreement;

“ **Controller ** ” shall have the meaning under Data Protection Laws;

“ **Data Protection Laws ** ” means any laws or regulations applicable to a Party relating to Personal Data, privacy or data protection, unless otherwise specified in the Local Law Annexes;

" **Data Subject ** " means the identified or identifiable natural person to whom the Personal Data relates; 

" **Local Law Annexes** " means the annexes attached hereto which set forth specific supplemental local law requirements relevant to the Processing hereunder;

“ **Personal Data ** ” shall have the meaning under Data Protection Laws;

“ **Personal Data Breach ** ” (and similar terms) shall have the meaning under Data Protection Laws;

“ **Process ** ”, “ **Processed ** ”, or “ **Processing ** ” (and their similar terms) shall have the meaning under Data Protection Laws; 

“ **Processor ** ” shall have the meaning under Data Protection Laws; 

" **Regulator ** " means any regulator with authority to enforce Data Protection Laws in any particular territory; 

" **Relevant Data Transfer** " means a transfer of Personal Data to Third Countries which would be prohibited by Data Protection Law in the absence of appropriate measures being followed by Customer and/or BytePlus;

" **Special Category Data ** " (and similar terms) have the meaning given to the terms under Data Protection Laws; and

" **Third Countries** " (and similar terms) means third countries as the term is understood under Data Protection Laws applicable to the processing concerned, excluding countries approved as providing adequate protection for Personal Data by the relevant body with power to issue such findings from time to time.

**2. Local Law Annexes**

2.1 Where and to the extent that any of the local law requirements set out in Exhibit A (*Local Law Annexes*) apply to the Processing of Customer Data by Customer and/or BytePlus (as applicable), the terms of that applicable Local Law Annex will apply to BytePlus' Processing of such Customer Data. If there is any conflict between the terms set forth in the DPA's Standard Terms and Conditions and the terms set forth in the Local Law Annexes incorporated herein, the terms of the Local Law Annexes shall prevail.

**3. Conditions of Processing**

3.1 This DPA, including the Local Law Annexes, governs the terms under which BytePlus shall Process Customer Data on behalf of Customer as a Processor in connection with its provision of the Services. The specific details of such Customer Data and Processing are further described in Exhibit B (*Processing Details*).

**4. Customer Obligations**

4.1 Customer represents, warrants and undertakes that it has at all times complied and shall continue to comply with Data Protection Laws with respect to the Customer Data Processed in connection with the Agreement. In particular, it has served any necessary notices and obtained any necessary consents, or established legitimate grounds to disclose to, and/or permit the collection of Customer Data to enable the Processing of the Customer Data by BytePlus for its provision of Services to Customer as set out in this DPA and as envisaged by the Agreement.

4.2 Customer shall not use, configure or deploy any of the Services in a way that will result in BytePlus collecting any Special Category Data;

4.3 Without prejudice to any other provision in this DPA, Customer shall not do anything or fail to do anything that will cause BytePlus to be in violation of any provision or requirement of any Data Protection Laws including regulations issued thereunder, whether now or in the future.

**5. BytePlus Obligations**

5.1 BytePlus shall only Process Customer Data:

5.1.1 in accordance with the documented instructions from Customer which are to process Customer Data in connection with the Services as described in this DPA and the Agreement; or

5.1.2 as required by Applicable Laws, provided that BytePlus shall inform Customer of such Processing unless such laws prohibit this on important grounds of public interest. 

5.2 BytePlus shall comply with Data Protection Laws including any such laws applicable to Relevant Data Transfers when performing its obligations under the Agreement (including this DPA).

5.3 BytePlus shall, unless prohibited by Applicable Laws, notify Customer about any binding request for disclosure of Customer Data by a Regulator, government agency, or law enforcement authority.

5.4 BytePlus shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures (as set out in Exhibit C) to protect the Customer Data against accidental, unauthorised or unlawful processing, loss, alteration, destruction, disclosure or damage.

5.5 Upon becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Data, unless specifically prohibited under Applicable Laws or any Regulator, BytePlus will, without undue delay, notify Customer in writing of the Personal Data Breach. BytePlus shall assist Customer with their obligations and take steps to mitigate the effects of the Personal Data Breach, including by providing Customer with a commercially reasonable description of the Personal Data Breach. BytePlus shall provide reasonable updates to this information and will not release or publicise the Personal Data Breach without Customer’s prior written consent (except where required to do so by law). 

5.6 BytePlus will, taking into account the nature of the Processing, provide technical and organizational measures, insofar as this is possible, to assist the Customer to fulfill its obligations to respond to requests for the exercise of rights by a Data Subject.

5.7 BytePlus shall, upon written request from the Customer, provide the Customer with the most recent certifications and/or corresponding summary audit report(s) to demonstrate compliance with the obligations detailed in this DPA. If further information is needed by Customer to comply with its own audit obligations or a competent Regulator's request, Customer will inform BytePlus in writing and BytePlus will, to the extent reasonable, provide such information or grant access to it.

5.8 To the extent that the information provided in accordance with paragraph 5.7 above is insufficient to satisfy an audit right mandated by Applicable Law or expressly agreed by the Parties, BytePlus shall permit Customer upon thirty (30) days' written notice, to procure an independent third\-party auditor chosen by the Customer, on the condition that such auditor shall be bound to an obligation of confidentiality required by BytePlus, to audit BytePlus' compliance with BytePlus' obligations under this DPA. Such audits shall (i) be at Customer's cost; (ii) be conducted between 9am\-5pm on business days (excluding, for the avoidance of doubt, weekends and public holidays); (iii) not be conducted by any competitor of BytePlus or any BytePlus Affiliate; (iv) not interfere with BytePlus' day\-to\-day business; and (v) to the extent an inspection is required, be limited to an inspection of BytePlus' processing facilities for providing the Service.

**6. Sub\-processing**

6.1 Customer hereby grants BytePlus general written authorisation to engage sub\-processors for the Processing of Customer Data under the Agreement. Customer hereby authorises BytePlus' engagement of the sub\-processors listed in Exhibit D and BytePlus Affiliates (" *BytePlus Sub\-Processors * ") provided that: (i) BytePlus agrees to provide at least fifteen (15) days' prior notice of the addition or removal of any sub\-processor (including details of the processing it performs or will perform) through updating the list of such sub\-processors, which BytePlus will do by notifying Customer through the email address provided by Customer during registration; (ii) Customer does not object to such changes within 15 days; and (iii) BytePlus imposes data protection terms on any sub\-processor it appoints that require it to protect the Customer Data to the standard required by Data Protection Laws and this DPA. If Customer objects to the appointment of a new sub\-processor within 15 days, BytePlus shall use reasonable efforts to make available to Customer a change in the Services to avoid the processing of Customer Data by the objected\-to sub\-processor. 

6.2 In the event that BytePlus engages a sub\-processor for carrying out specific Processing activities on behalf of Customer, where that sub\-processor fails to fulfil its obligations, BytePlus shall remain fully liable under the Data Protection Laws to Customer for the performance of that sub\-processor's obligations.

**7. Transfers ** 

7.1. BytePlus shall not transfer, access, or use, Customer Data in Third Countries without Customer’s prior authorisation. Customer hereby authorises BytePlus to transfer Customer Personal Data to its Affiliates and Subprocessors as listed in Exhibit D.

7.2 The provisions of Exhibit E apply to Relevant Data Transfers of Customer Data from Customer to BytePlus. 

**8. Termination**

8.1 This DPA will remain in force for the Term of the Agreement or for as long as BytePlus Processes Customer Data, whichever term is longer.

8.2 Unless otherwise stipulated in the Agreement and/or in any other Service\-related documentation or guides published by BytePlus, following termination of the Agreement or after suspension or termination of the provision of applicable Services under the Agreement, whichever is earlier, Customer shall be responsible for exporting any Customer Data it wishes to retain from the Services within 30 days, and after such period, Customer hereby instructs BytePlus to delete any Customer Data from its systems within 180 days (unless Data Protection Laws or other Applicable Laws require the Customer Data to be retained by BytePlus beyond this period).
