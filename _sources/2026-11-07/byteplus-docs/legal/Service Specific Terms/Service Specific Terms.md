<span id="JGYLreTk"></span>
## 1. Service Scope

BytePlus Certificate Center is a cloud service that provides one\-stop lifecycle management of digital certificates.

<span id="rtRJQ2Ch"></span>
## 2. Rules of Use

Customer understands and agrees to authorize BytePlus to apply for and obtain publicly trusted Secure Sockets Layer ("SSL") certificates (each, a “Certificate”) from third\-party Certificate Authorities ("CAs") for domains/IP addresses Customer provides to us ("Customer Domain"), in compliance with the CAs' requirements, requirements published in the technical documentation via BytePlus portal, or any other applicable requirements (“Requirements”). The validity period of a Certificate starts from the date it is issued by a CA through the Platform, and ends when the Certificate is expired or revoked.

Customer acknowledges and agrees that:

2.1 BytePlus will collect and transfer your data and contact information to CAs or the vendors entrusted by CAs ("Vendors") on your behalf subject to our [Data Processing Addendum](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/legal/docs/data-processing-addendum), to the extent necessary. CAs and Vendors will process and retain such information while providing the certificate services according to their own privacy policies. Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries where CAs and Vendors are located. Please refer to the applicable CAs' and/or their Vendors' privacy policies for further details.

2.2 All information you provide in connection with your application or use of Certificate(s) shall be and will be real, accurate, up\-to\-date, valid, and complete. In the event of incomplete, inaccurate, or incorrect information, Customer shall respond to BytePlus’ request for additional information, cease to use the Certificate, or be liable for any loss or legal consequences arising therefrom.

2.3 Customer controls and operates the Customer Domain or has authority to do so, and will immediately revoke the authorization provided to BytePlus if and when Customer suspends or loses such right.

2.4 In order for a quick Certificate issuance, in the event that Customer is also using BytePlus DNS service, BytePlus Certificate Center will automatically add Customer Domain’s DNS validation information received from CAs, including record type, domain name prefix and record value, to your DNS configuration.

2.5 BytePlus or CAs may at any time, without liability, suspend or revoke a Certificate, without seeking prior consent from Customer, if required by the Requirements or if Customer fails to comply with any applicable laws, regulations, the [Acceptable Use Policy](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/legal/docs/acceptable-use-policy), or for any other valid reasons.

2.6 Customer will promptly cease using a Certificate and notify BytePlus in the event that there is any actual or suspected misuse or compromise of the Certificate or the private key associated with the Certificate, and shall be liable for any loss or legal consequences that may arise therefrom.

2.7 Individuals using the Certificate(s) on behalf of the Customer have received security training appropriate to the Certificate(s).

2.8 When subscribing to services provided by free CAs, including but not limited to [Let's Encrypt](https://letsencrypt.org/), [Google Trust](https://pki.goog/) (collectively, "Free CAs"), through BytePlus, you acknowledge and agree that: (a) you are a subscriber of the services of Free CAs and agree to be bound by any terms and conditions set forth by these CAs, including but not limited to, terms of service, subscriber agreements, or privacy policies; (b) BytePlus makes no warranties or representations with respect to the services provided by the Free CAs and any other related services offered by BytePlus, and shall not be liable to you for any, direct or indirect, claims, liabilities or damages that may arise from your use of these services.

2.1 The following terms shall have the meanings assigned to each of them as below, unless otherwise provided hereunder:

"**BytePlus Domains** **Service**" **,**  or "**Service**" collectively refers to the Domain Name Registration Service, Domain Name Transfer Service, Domain Name Management Service, and other services offered to Customer under BytePlus Domains Service pursuant to this Service Specific Term.

"**ccTLD**" refers to a country code top\-level domain managed locally by an authority associated with the corresponding territory, for example, ".us" for the United States, ".tv" for Tuvalu.

"**Expired Registered Name**" refers to a Registered Name of which the validity period has expired. Registered Names within the Redemption Period and the Renewal Grace Period shall be deemed as Expired Registered Names.

"**gTLD**" refers to a Generic Top\-Level Domain, which is a non\-geographic top\-level domain within the Domain Name System (DNS) of the internet, designated to serve as the suffix of a domain name and governed by ICANN's policies, excluding ccTLDs or Internationalized Domain Name (IDN) country code TLDs. "**Registry Operator**" refers to the person or entity that is accountable for specific TLDs managing, maintaining and administering pursuant to an agreement with ICANN (or its assignee).

"**Top Level Domain**" or "**TLD**" refers to the uppermost level within the domain name structure. Here are some illustrative examples: ".com", ".org", ".cn ", ".us", and ".uk".

"**WHOIS**" refers to the internet\-based database facilitating the public to query the Registered Names and their Registered Name Holders maintained by the Registry Operator for the gTLDs or ccTLDs or the Registrar.
