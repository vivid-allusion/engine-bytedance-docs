*Last Update: April 27, 2025*

During the term of the **BytePlus Customer Agreement** pursuant to which BytePlus Pte.Ltd. (“**BytePlus**”) has agreed to provide the **BytePlus GTM Service** (the “**Service**”) to you (the “**Agreement**”), the Service will be subject to the service level metrics set out below in this Service Level Agreement ( this “**SLA**”). Capitalized terms used in this SLA, but not defined in this SLA, have the meaning stated in the Agreement.

<span id="a60788c8"></span>
# **1. Service Level**

BytePlus will use all commercially reasonable efforts to make the Service Available with a Monthly Uptime Percentage no less than 99.99% during any monthly billing cycle (the “**Service Levels**”) as calculated and subject to the exclusions outlined below in Section 4.

<span id="6b533b0c"></span>
# **2. Service Credits**

Subject to the terms and conditions of the Agreement, and your full compliance of the Agreement, in the event that the above Service Levels were not satisfied, you will be eligible to receive a service credit (the “Service Credit”) as described below.


|**Monthly Uptime Percentage** |**Percentage of monthly bill for the Service which does not meet SLA that will be credited to your future monthly bills** |
|---|---|
|Less than 99.99% but equal to or higher than 99.5% |10% of the monthly bill |
|Less than 99.5% but equal to or higher than 95% |30% of the monthly bill |
|Less than 95% |50% of the monthly bill |



* We will apply any Service Credits only against future Service payments otherwise due from you. At our discretion, we may exchange the Service Credit into Service hours.

* A Service Credit will be applicable and issued only if the credit amount for the applicable monthly billing cycle is greater than US one dollar (US$1).

* The aggregate maximum number of Service Credit you can claim in a single monthly billing cycle shall not exceed 100% of such monthly bill. Service Credit will not entitle you to any refund or other payment from BytePlus. Service Credit may not be transferred or applied to any other account.


Notwithstanding anything to the contrary hereunder and under the Agreement, you agree and acknowledge that the Service Credit (if eligible) is the sole and exclusive remedy for any unavailability, non\-performance, or other failure by us to provide the Service.

<span id="336eaa13"></span>
# **3. Service Credit Application and Payment Procedures**

In order to apply for the Service Credit, you must notify BytePlus within 30 days after the end of preceding calendar month where you become eligible to receive the Service Credit, and provide BytePlus with sufficient supporting information and materials evidencing the occurrence of the failure of SLA. Such written notification also must include:


1. the words “SLA Credit Request” in the subject line.

2. the dates, times of each lack of availability incident that you are claiming.

3. the affected Service.


If the **Monthly Uptime Percentage** of such a request is confirmed by us and is less than the Service Levels, then we will issue the Service Credit to you within one billing cycle following the month in which your request is confirmed by us.

If you fail to meet the aforesaid requirements, your eligibility to receive the Service Credit may be forfeited. In case of any disputes regarding the Service Credit, BytePlus shall be entitled to make a determination in good faith based on BytePlus’s information.

<span id="263b4129"></span>
# **4. Exclusion of SLA**

If any failure to achieve the Service Levels is due to any of the following reasons, you will not be eligible for the Service Credit:


1. System maintenance activities, including cutovers, repairs, upgrades, and simulated fault drills, which are notified to the customer in advance by BytePlus.

2. Network or equipment failures, configuration adjustments, ISP network failures, or client\-side network failures outside the control of BytePlus.

3. Downtime caused by hacker attacks on the customer's applications.

4. Data, passwords, or other confidential information lost or leaked due to improper maintenance or confidentiality by the customer.

5. Downtime caused by customer negligence or authorized operations.

6. Downtime caused by the customer's failure to follow the product documentation or usage recommendations provided by BytePlus.

7. Downtime caused by force majeure events or accidents.

8. Requests originating from IP addresses in Chinese mainland.

9. Downtime caused by hacker attacks on BytePlus GTM service, customer domains, and CNAME domains.

10. Downtime caused by exceeding the usage time limit of BytePlus.

11. Downtime caused by reasons other than those attributable to BytePlus.


<span id="97922762"></span>
# **5. Definitions**

The following definitions shall apply to this SLA:


* "**Service Cycle**": each calendar month is a Service Cycle. The period from the day on which you start to use the Service to the very last day of such month will be counted as one Service Cycle. The availability of the Service is calculated independently for each Service Cycle.

* "**Monthly Uptime Percentage**" means the total number of minutes in a Service Cycle, minus the number of minutes of Downtime in the same Service Cycle, divided by the total number of minutes in the Service Cycle. Monthly Uptime Percentage measurements exclude any Unavailability resulting directly or indirectly from any exclusion set forth below in Section 4.

* "**Downtime**" means the total number of minutes of Unavailability within a Service Cycle.

* "**Unavailability**" or "**Unavailable**" means the inability to respond to DNS queries for domains configured in BytePlus GTM service that continue for 1 (one) consecutive minutes or more. For the avoidance of doubt, if the Unavailability period is less than 1 (one) consecutive minutes, those minutes shall not be counted as the Unavailability. If you did not make any requests in an interval, that interval is assumed to be 100% available.

* "**Valid Requests**" are the Service requests received by our server that would normally result in a non\-error response. Valid Requests exclude: (i) any request that fails to pass authentication and/or parameter verification; (ii) any request initiated due to hacker attacks on your system/program or other security breaches.

* "**Monthly Bill**" means the total amount of bills paid by the customer for a single GTM instance in a calendar month. If the customer pays for multiple months of bills at once, the monthly bill will be calculated based on the number of months of your subscription.
