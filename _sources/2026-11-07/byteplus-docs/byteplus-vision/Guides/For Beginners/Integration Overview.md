BytePlus Vision offers a comprehensive suite of open APIs for processing and analyzing visual content such as images and videos. This documentation center serves as your central hub for getting started. Here, you can find a beginner's guide to the console, detailed integration instructions for our OpenAPI, service\-specific guides, and SDKs to quickly integrate powerful visual intelligence into your applications.

# Integration Guide

<span id="General Guide"></span>
## General Guide

BytePlus Vision delivers its services/capabilities via OpenAPI, please refer to the [BytePlus OpenAPI Guide](https://docs.byteplus.com/byteplus-platform/reference/overview-1)


* `Request address` should be `open.byteplusapi.com`

* `Service` should be `cv`

* `Region` should be `ap-singapore-1`
