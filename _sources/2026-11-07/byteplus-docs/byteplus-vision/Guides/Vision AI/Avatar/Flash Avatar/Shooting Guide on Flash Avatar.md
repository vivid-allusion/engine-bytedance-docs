Flash Avatar is a service that lets you generate a custom digital human in as little as 10 minutes using a 10\-15 second training video, which you can then use for video synthesis. This guide walks you through all the requirements and best practices for shooting a high\-quality training video to get optimal results from the service.

<span id="IzQprNFi"></span>
# Training Video Requirements


|Length |A 10\-15 second video |
|---|---|
|Video file format |.mp4/.mov |
|Video resolution |Upload a video file with a resolution of at least 720p. 1080p is recommended. |
|Video frame rate |25fps recommended |
|Video Bit Rate |Greater than 20Mbps |

2. # Recording   <span id="recording"></span>


<span id="cbWW5DJY"></span>
## Overview of the shooting process

Be silent for 1\-2 seconds, then read the script continuously for 10–15 seconds.


1. Be silent for 1\-2 seconds: Keep the recording environment quiet for 1\-2 seconds with no one speaking.

2. Continuous Script Reading:

3. It is acceptable to make mistakes or hesitate. If you misspeak, pause briefly and continue reading naturally.

4. After each sentence, it is recommended to pause for 1\-2 seconds with your mouth closed. Do not purse or lick your lips when your mouth is closed.
