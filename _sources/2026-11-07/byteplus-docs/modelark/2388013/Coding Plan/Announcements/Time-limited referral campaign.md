## Referrer benefits

When a referrer successfully refers a friend who completes a valid first purchase, the referrer is eligible to receive voucher rewards as follows:


1. Reward calculation:

* For each successfully referred valid user, the referrer receives a voucher equal to 10% of the referee's actual payment amount for their first order.

   * There is no limit on the number of referrals—the more you refer, the more you earn.

2. Definition of actual payment amount: The actual payment amount refers to the final cash amount paid by the referee for their first subscription order.

3. Voucher usage rules:

   * Vouchers are promotional benefits provided by the platform and cannot be withdrawn, transferred, or exchanged for cash.

   * Redemption scope: Referral reward vouchers may be applied to ModelArk Coding Plan subscriptions as well as postpaid API usage fees for Seed model services, including the latest Seed\-1.8 and Seedance\-1.5\-pro models, to support developers in exploring AI capabilities.

   * Validity: Vouchers are valid for 90 days from the issuance date and become invalid upon expiration.


<span id="c06550d5"></span>
## Referee benefits

When the referee completes their first subscription to any ModelArk Coding Plan package using a valid invitation link or invitation code, they are eligible for an additional 10% discount on the payable amount of their first subscription order.


1. Eligibility and Conditions

   * The referee must never have purchased any ModelArk Coding Plan package before.

   * Once a user successfully refers another user, they become a referrer and are no longer eligible for referee's benefits.

   * Each user may enjoy the 10% additional discount only once as a referee.

2. Recommendation: The 10% additional discount applies to the entire first order. Therefore, subscribing to a multi\-month plan in a single order is more cost\-effective than subscribing for one month with automatic renewal.

## Referral relationship binding


1. Refer friends: Click the **Refer friends** button on the campaign page to generate a unique invitation link and invitation code. When a friend logs in (or registers) using your invitation link or code, the invitation relationship between both parties is established.

2. Reward eligibility: The referee must complete their first payment within the campaign validity period for the invitation to be considered valid and counted toward reward calculation.

3. IMPORTANT: The invitation relationship is determined by the first successful binding only and serves as the sole basis for subsequent reward calculations.

## Coupon terms and conditions


* Coupon distribution may experience a 1\-2 hour delay and can be viewed in the [Billing-Coupon](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/coupon) module.

* Each coupon is valid for 90 days from the issuance date and is non\-transferable, non\-refundable, and cannot be extended. Expired coupons cannot be reactivated. Coupons can be used in [ModelArk available regions](https://ai.byteplus.com/ark/region:ap-southeast-1/docs/ModelArk/availability).

* After activating the large model API's pay\-as\-you\-go service, the system will first deduct from your account's free quota, followed by any available coupons. Once both the free quota and coupon are depleted, subsequent usage will be billed at standard rates based on your consumption.

* The coupons distributed during the campaign are exclusively applicable to the pay\-as\-you\-go services of eligible models on the ModelArk platform and ModelArk Coding Plan.
