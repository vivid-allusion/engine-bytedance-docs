## **First batch of model deprecations (August 15, 2025)** 

<span id="596ccb9b"></span>
### **Deprecation timetable**


|**First Notification Date** |**Deprecation Date** |**Deactivation Date** |**Impact Description** |
|---|---|---|---|
|August 15, 2025 |August 20, 2025 |October 16, 2025 |Model deprecation will proceed from the notification date; starting from the First Notification Date, quotas for deprecated models will be gradually reduced.<br><br>After the Deprecation Date, new inference endpoints or fine\-tuning tasks based on deprecated models cannot be created.<br><br>By the Deactivation Date, the model will be officially deactivated and cease service; switching to the recommended model is advised. |
