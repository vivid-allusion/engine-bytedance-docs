## **1. Campaign period**

00:00:00 UTC+8 April 24, 2025 to 23:59:59 UTC+8 December 31, 2025

<span id="a8229955"></span>
## **2.Eligible participants**

All BytePlus registered users.

**Referrer**: A user who logs into the campaign page, participates in the event, and actively shares referral links or QR codes.

**Referee**: An individual who accesses the campaign page through a referral link or QR code shared by the referrer, enters the referral code, and successfully registers an account.

<span id="3466fa0e"></span>
## **3.Campaign rules**


* Referrer

   * **Generate a unique referral code/link**: Log in to the BytePlus campaign page to create your personalized referral code and link.

   * **Share your referral code/link**: Distribute the referral QR code or link along with the referral code. A new user is considered successfully referred when they register with your referral code.

* Referee

   * **Valid referral**: A referral is considered valid when the referee accesses the campaign page through the referrer's QR code or link, and completes the registration with the referral code.

   * **Single referral per user**: Users can only be referred once. You'll earn bonuses for each successful milestone according to campaign guidelines.


<span id="f11d583e"></span>
## **4.Tiered bonus**


|Tier |Milestone |Referrer |Referee |
|---|---|---|---|
|Registration and usage bonus |The referee successfully **registers** and **consumes tokens** for eligible models (including granted free tokens). |Get a 10 USD coupon |Get a 5 USD coupon |
|Payment method bonus |The referee successfully adds a payment method **for the first time**. |Get a 5 USD coupon |Get a 5 USD coupon |
|First purchase bonus |The referee whose **first** paid usage of eligible models results in a bill with a total amount payable greater than $0. |Get a 15 USD coupon |Get a 5 USD coupon |


Bonus is available to be used in [selected regions](https://ai.byteplus.com/ark/region:ap-southeast-1/docs/ModelArk/availability).

## **6.Coupon Terms and Conditions**


* Coupon distribution may experience a 1\-2 hour delay and can be viewed in the [Billing-Coupon](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/coupon) module.

* Each coupon is valid for 90 days from the issuance date and is non\-transferable, non\-refundable, and cannot be extended. Expired coupons cannot be reactivated. Coupons can be used in [ModelArk available regions](https://ai.byteplus.com/ark/region:ap-southeast-1/docs/ModelArk/availability).

* After activating the large model API's pay\-as\-you\-go service, the system will first deduct from your account's free quota, followed by any available coupons. Once both the free quota and coupon are depleted, subsequent usage will be billed at standard rates based on your consumption.

* The coupons distributed during the campaign are exclusively applicable to the pay\-as\-you\-go services of eligible models on the ModelArk platform.
