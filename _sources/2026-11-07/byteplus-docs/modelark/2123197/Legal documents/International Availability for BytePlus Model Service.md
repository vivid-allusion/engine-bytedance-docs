## Service Scope


* "BytePlus Model Service" refers to the model services which are marked as provided by BytePlus or its affiliates.

* "Restricted Models" refers to the models with "Restricted Model" tag in the model card description and such other models as may be updated by BytePlus from time to time.


Due to regional regulatory variations, the versions of BytePlus Model Service available under the same Model Card may differ for customers in different countries/regions. The final version will be subject to what BytePlus actually provides.
