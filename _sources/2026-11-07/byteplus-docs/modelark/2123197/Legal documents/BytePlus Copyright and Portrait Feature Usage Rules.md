**Your use of the Copyright and Portrait feature on the BytePlus ModelArk Platform (the “ModelArk Platform”) is subject to the following rules:** 

**1. “Copyright and Portrait Materials”**  means licensed elements, materials, and similar assets made available on the ModelArk Platform through cooperation with rights holders, as well as templates, effects, and other derivative assets created by the ModelArk Platform based on such Licensed Materials. Copyright and Portrait Materials may include real\-person portraits and voices, character images, game footage, film and television clips, animation elements, music, sound effects, dubbing, and similar content, and may be provided in the form of text, images, video, audio, or other media.

**2. “Use of Copyright and Portrait Materials”**  means your use of Copyright and Portrait Materials as input or reference content in connection with content creation through the model services on the ModelArk Platform. Any resulting text, images, video, audio, or other materials are referred to collectively as “Generated Content.”

**3. Management and Use Rules.**  Your use of Copyright and Portrait Materials is subject to the following rules:

 **(1)**  Copyright and Portrait Materials are protected by intellectual property, publicity, and other legal rights. BytePlus may impose restrictions on your use of such materials and modify them from time to time. BytePlus makes no representations or warranties regarding the availability of such materials. You are solely responsible for any liabilities or losses arising from your use of such materials or any Generated Content.

 **(2) ** Your use of voice materials as part of the Copyright and Portrait Materials must comply with applicable laws. Without authorization from the relevant rights holder, you must not use voice materials to train speech synthesis models. You must not use voice materials to create deepfakes or otherwise generate misleading content involving public figures.

 **(3) ** Your use of the Copyright and Portrait Materials, including use of the Generated Content, must not, without authorization from the relevant rights holder, imply their endorsement of or association with your goods or services. You must not use such Generated Content in political, sensitive, or reputation\-damaging contexts.

 **(4)**  Access to Copyright and Portrait Materials may vary by account, subject to applicable rights holder restrictions and the terms of your order.

 **(5)**  Each Copyright and Portrait Material will carry applicable usage labels, such as “For Non\-Commercial Use Only” or “Commercial Use Permitted.” You must use the material only as permitted by the applicable label. If a material is labeled “For Non\-Commercial Use Only,” neither the material nor any Generated Content derived from it may be used for commercial purposes.

 **(6)**  Generated Content created using Copyright and Portrait Materials on the ModelArk Platform is subject to platform review. If your use violates applicable usage restrictions, generation of content may be blocked. You must ensure that your intended use is consistent with the applicable usage labels. BytePlus shall bear no responsibility or liability for any loss, damage, or claim arising out of or relating to the blocking of such content.

 **(7)**  Your account may be eligible for a free\-use period for Copyright and Portrait Materials, after which paid use will apply. During the free\-use period, such materials and any Generated Content derived from them may be used only for non\-commercial purposes. Upon payment of the applicable fees, such materials and Generated Content may be used for commercial purposes, subject to the applicable usage labels and authorized use restrictions.

 **(8)**  Generated Content constitutes AI\-generated or synthetic content and must comply with applicable labeling/marking laws and requirements.

 **(9)**  The authorized scope of Copyright and Portrait Materials is as indicated on the applicable display page. Unless otherwise authorized in writing, you must not use such materials or their Generated Content to train third\-party models, build public datasets, use such materials outside this feature, or use them in any manner separate from or competitive with the BytePlus platform and services, including to develop similar material libraries.

 **(10)**  Unless otherwise agreed, and subject to applicable laws, these Rules, and any rights holder restrictions, you shall own the rights and interests in Generated Content created through the platform’s model services using Copyright and Portrait Materials. For the avoidance of doubt, Copyright and Portrait Materials remain the property of their original rights holders.

 **(11)**  Your right to use the relevant Copyright and Portrait Materials for new creations ends upon expiration of the applicable authorization period or your subscription. Unless otherwise agreed, Generated Content lawfully created before such expiration may continue to be used, distributed, or sold, subject to these Rules and any applicable rights holder restrictions.

 **(12)**  You must have all necessary rights and permissions for any materials you upload and use with Copyright and Portrait Materials on the ModelArk Platform, and must ensure that such materials and any resulting Generated Content do not infringe any third\-party rights.

 **(13)**  Your use of Copyright and Portrait Materials remains subject to the applicable ModelArk Platform agreements and model service terms.

 **(14)**  BytePlus will not be liable if the Copyright and Portrait Materials feature becomes unavailable as a result of your violation of these Rules or any applicable rights holder restrictions. You are solely responsible for any losses, claims, damages, costs, or expenses arising from any such violation. If your violation results in any third\-party claim against BytePlus or the platform, BytePlus may recover its resulting losses from you and take appropriate action, including suspension or termination of your account and removal of violating content. Any such recovery will not exceed ten times the fees you paid for the disputed materials and, in all cases, will be limited to the amount actually paid by BytePlus to the relevant third party.

BytePlus may revise these Rules from time to time. Any revisions will become effective upon publication on the ModelArk Platform. Please review these Rules periodically.
