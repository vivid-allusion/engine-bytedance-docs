**Customer Acknowledgement**

By electronically signing or otherwise electronically accepting this Model Generations Quality Diagnostics Service Acknowledgement (this “Acknowledgement”), Customer acknowledges and agrees as follows.

(a) "Diagnostic Service" means generations quality diagnostics service offered by BytePlus for investigating issues relating to the quality, relevance, or other aspects of outputs generated by Seedance 2.0 and its subsequent iterations thereof.

(b) "Diagnostic Data" means the data associated with the task ID(s) designated by Customer for a Diagnostic Service request, including user inputs (including reference materials uploaded by user), outputs, associated account identifiers, and related metadata.

When Customer apply to use Diagnostic Service, BytePlus may retain Customer Data for up to 3 days solely to provide the Diagnostic Service. BytePlus may access and use Diagnostic Data only upon Customer’s submission of a diagnostic request, only for the task ID(s) designated by Customer, and only as reasonably necessary to investigate, diagnose, and resolve the relevant technical issue.

BytePlus may retain and use de\-identified Diagnostic Data after the applicable diagnostic matter is closed solely to support and improve Diagnostic services and for related quality assurance purposes. We do not use such data to train any general\-purpose or foundation model.

For the foregoing purposes, the Diagnostic Data may be accessed, transferred to, stored in, and processed in the People’s Republic of China by BytePlus and its affiliates, personnel involved in diagnostic and technical support. The cross\-border transfer of Diagnostic Data to the People's Republic of China is conducted pursuant to the transfer mechanisms set out in the [BytePlus DPA](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/legal/docs-data-processing-addendum) between the parties, which is incorporated into this Acknowledgement by reference.

Customer represents that it has all rights, consents, and other legal basis necessary to designate the relevant task ID(s) for diagnostic and to permit BytePlus to access and use the Diagnostic data as described in this Acknowledgement. Customer is responsible for ensuring that no Diagnostic Data includes personal data of individuals located in, or based in, the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland. BytePlus shall have no liability for any such data submitted in breach of this obligation.

BytePlus will provide diagnostic support only after Customer has electronically signed or otherwise electronically accepted this Acknowledgement.
