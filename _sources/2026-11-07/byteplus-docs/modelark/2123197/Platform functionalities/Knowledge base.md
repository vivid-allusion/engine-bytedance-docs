The Knowledge Engine is a retrieval\-augmented generation (RAG) service provided by BytePlus. It can intelligently parse structured and unstructured documents, split them into segments, achieve accurate semantic matching through vector retrieval, and combine this with large language model reasoning capabilities to provide enterprise customers with more accurate and professional question answering results.

<span id="entrance"></span>
## Console entry point

Open the [Knowledge Base](https://console.byteplus.com/vikingdb/knowledge/region:vdb-knowledge+ap-southeast-1/collection/list) page to create and manage knowledge bases.
