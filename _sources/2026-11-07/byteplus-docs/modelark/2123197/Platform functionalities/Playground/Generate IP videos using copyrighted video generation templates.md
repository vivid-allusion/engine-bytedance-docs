In the ModelArk Model Playground, you can use authorized copyrighted IP video generation templates together with your own assets or platform\-provided virtual assets to generate video. Powered by the video generation capabilities of Dreamina Seedance 2.0 and Dreamina Seedance 2.5, these templates help you dynamically generate videos that include the images you upload.

You only need to select a template and scenario from the copyrighted IP library and replace the image assets. The person or subject in your image can then be incorporated into the copyrighted template scene, helping you quickly generate videos with a copyrighted content style.


* **High\-quality video generation**: Use the powerful video generation capabilities of the Seedance 2.0 and Seedance 2.5 models to generate natural and realistic dynamic videos.

* **Authorized IP for compliant use**: Use copyrighted characters and templates provided by the platform to generate content within the authorized scope and reduce copyright risks.

* **Support for replacing images with your own images**: Use images you upload as subject references to generate videos that include features from the uploaded images.

* **Multiple templates and scenarios**: The copyrighted IP library provides different IPs, scenes, and scenarios, which you can choose from flexibly based on your creative goals.

* **Configurable parameters**: Configure the video aspect ratio, resolution, duration, number of generated videos, and whether to generate audio.

## How\-to

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">Tip</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">To generate videos using real\-human images, add real\-human assets to the asset library first. For details, see <a href="https://ai.byteplus.com/ark/region:ap-southeast-1/docs/ModelArk/2315856">Add real\-human assets to asset library</a>.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">The authorization validity period, supported scenarios, and restrictions of copyrighted characters are subject to the authorization information displayed in the console and the relevant agreements.</div>



Follow the steps described below to generate customized copyrighted videos:


1. Go to the [ModelArk Model Playground](https://ai.byteplus.com/ark/region:ap-southeast-1/experience/gen_video?model=dreamina-seedance-2-0-260128).

2. Click the **Copyright Library** tab, or select **Copyright Generation** from the **Mode** drop\-down menu in the lower\-left corner of the dialog box.

3. Hover over the cover of a copyrighted IP. You can choose to use the IP or view its copyright information.


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">Tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">When using a copyrighted character for the first time, you must read and agree to the related copyright and portrait asset usage rules.</div>



4. Click **Use** to preview and select a scenario, such as a movie scene or a specific setting.

5. In the asset area, click **Replace image** to replace the image with a real\-human asset or a platform\-provided virtual character. The image is used as the subject or portrait reference in the template.


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">Tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">For real\-human assets, we recommend using clear, unobstructed, well\-lit front\-facing or half\-body photos to improve generation quality.</div>



6. Select the video resolution, duration, and other parameters as needed, and choose whether to generate audio.

7. Click the Send icon to start video generation.


<span id="pricing"></span>
## Pricing

Videos generated with copyrighted IPs are billed based on output duration. Unit prices vary by IP. For specific pricing information, see [ModelArk Model Playground](https://ai.byteplus.com/ark/region:ap-southeast-1/experience/gen_video?model=dreamina-seedance-2-5-260628).

For a video generation task that uses multiple copyrighted IPs, calculate the fee for each input IP based on its unit price and the video duration in seconds, and then add the fees together:

`Copyright fee for one video = Σ (unit price of each input IP × video duration in seconds)`
