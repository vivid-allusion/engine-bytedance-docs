This article introduces the billing method and related information of SSL certificates.

<span id="billing-method"></span>
## Billing method

SSL certificates provided by BytePlus Certificate Center are billed in advance. You need to first pay for the SSL certificate in the BytePlus Certificate Center console, and then submit the certificate request to the CA.

The price of SSL certificates depends on the brand and specifications of the certificate. BytePlus Certificate Center provides SSL certificates of different brands and specifications for you to choose from. For details, please refer to [SSL certificate pricing](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/29/8579#ssl-certificate-pricing).

<span id="ssl-certificate-pricing"></span>
## SSL certificate pricing

The table below introduces the prices of SSL certificates of different brands and specifications sold by BytePlus Certificate Center.


* Certificate brand: The brand of the CA that issues the certificate.

* Certificate specification: Includes the type of the certificate and domain.

   * Certificate type: Includes DV (Domain Validation), OV (Organization Validation), EV (Extended Validation).

   * Domain type: Represents the type of domain protected by the certificate. The domain type includes single domain (FQDN) and wildcard domain.

      * A single domain certificate can only protect a specific domain (FQDN) or IP. For example, `example.com`, `abc.example.com`, `1.XX.xx.1`, etc.

      * A wildcard domain certificate can protect a wildcard domain. For example, `*.example.com`.


A wildcard domain can match all subdomains of the domain. For example, `*.example.com` can match `a.example.com`, `b.example.com`, etc., but cannot match `a.b.example.com`.
