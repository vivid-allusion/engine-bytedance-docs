BytePlus Certificate Center is a full lifecycle management platform for digital certificates. The SSL certificate service of BytePlus Certificate Center provides you with convenient SSL certificate ordering, issuance, renewal, upload, deployment, and other SSL certificate management services. You can order SSL certificates with different trust levels through BytePlus Certificate Center. These SSL certificates are issued by industry\-leading third\-party certificate authorities (CAs).

<span id="the-relationship-between-ssl-certificates-and-https"></span>
## The relationship between SSL certificates and HTTPS

After installing an SSL certificate on a website, a secure HTTPS connection can be established between the client and the server. The server proves its identity to the client through the SSL certificate. At the same time, HTTPS ensures that data transmission between the client and the server is encrypted, thus ensuring data security and preventing eavesdropping or tampering.

<span id="why-do-you-need-ssl-certificates"></span>
## Why do you need SSL certificates

SSL certificates bring the following benefits to websites:


* **Enhance brand image and strengthen customer trust.** 

   When users visit a website without an installed SSL certificate, the browser immediately displays a "Not Secure" warning, which may result in potential customer loss. However, when you install an SSL certificate for your website, users will see a trusted lock icon in the browser's address bar when accessing your website. This icon indicates that your website is trustworthy.

* **Protect sensitive data**.

   Online payment services involve valuable sensitive data such as usernames, passwords, and bank card numbers. This sensitive data is a common target for hackers. HTTPS guarantees secure transmission of data between the client and the server, creating a secure and reliable online payment environment for users.

* **Improve website SEO ranking**.

   Search engines assign more weight to HTTPS websites, thereby improving the website's SEO ranking. If your website supports HTTPS, it will increase the exposure of your website and bring more potential users.

## Supported SSL certificate types for ordering

Based on the trust level of SSL certificates, they can be divided into the following types: DV (Domain Validation), OV (Organization Validation), and EV (Extended Validation). You can order DV, OV, and EV certificates through BytePlus Certificate Center.


|**Certificate type** |**Trust level** |**Validation scope** |**Time required for issuance** |**Applicable scenarios** |**Supported certificate brands** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|DV |General |Domain ownership |Several hours |Personal websites, non\-commercial websites |DigiCert RapidSSL, DigiCert GeoTrust, DigiCert EE |
|OV |High |Domain ownership and authenticity of the enterprise/organization |3~5 business days |Websites, Apps, mini\-programs for small and medium enterprises |DigiCert SecureSite, DigiCert SecureSite Pro, DigiCert GeoTrust |
|EV |Highest |Domain ownership and authenticity of the enterprise/organization |7~10 business days |Websites, Apps, mini\-programs for large government, e\-commerce, education, finance, and banking organizations |DigiCert SecureSite, DigiCert SecureSite Pro, DigiCert GeoTrust |
