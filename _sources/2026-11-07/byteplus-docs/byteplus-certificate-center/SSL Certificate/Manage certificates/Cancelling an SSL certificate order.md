After ordering an SSL certificate through BytePlus Certificate Center, you can cancel the order if it meets the cancellation conditions. After canceling a paid certificate order, you will receive a full refund. This article introduces the conditions for canceling an order and how to cancel an order in the BytePlus Certificate Center.

<span id="conditions-for-canceling-an-order"></span>
## Conditions for canceling an order

You can cancel an SSL certificate order if it meets any of the following conditions:


* You have not yet submitted a certificate request.

* You have submitted a certificate request, but the certificate has not been issued.

* The certificate has been issued, but it has been less than 5 days since the certificate was issued.


<span id="results-of-canceling-an-order"></span>
## Results of canceling an order


* After canceling a paid certificate order, the payment you made for the order will be refunded through the corresponding payment method.

* After canceling a free certificate order, the free certificate quota consumed by the order will be returned to you.


<span id="operation-steps"></span>
## Operation steps


1. Log on to the [BytePlus Certificate Center console](https://console.byteplus.com/certificate-center).

2. In the left navigation bar, select **SSL Certificate** \> **Certificates**.

3. On the **Certificate management** page, find your certificate instance and click **Cancel order** in the **Actions** column.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">The <strong>Cancel order</strong> button will only appear when the certificate instance meets the order cancellation conditions.   </div>
   

4. In the pop\-up dialog box, select **Confirm** and then click **Confirm**.


After you successfully cancel the order, the status of the certificate will be changed to **Cancelled**.
