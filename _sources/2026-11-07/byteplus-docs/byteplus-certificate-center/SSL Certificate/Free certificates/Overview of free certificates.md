This article introduces the free certificate service provided by BytePlus Certificate Center.

In addition to paid certificates, BytePlus Certificate Center also provides free certificates. Free certificates are generally suitable for personal websites or HTTPS testing scenarios. For formal commercial sites, we recommend using paid certificates.

<span id="differences-between-free-and-paid-certificates"></span>
## Differences between free and paid certificates


|Comparison |Free certificate |Paid certificate |
|---|---|---|
|Certificate type |Only supports DV (Domain Validation). |Supports the following types:<br><br><br>* DV (Domain Validation)<br><br>* OV (Organization Validation)<br><br>* EV (Extended Validation) |
|Domain type |DigiCert EE only supports single domain (FQDN). |Supports the following types:<br><br><br>* FQDN and IP<br><br>* Wildcard domain |
|Domain extensions |Does not support .edu, .gov, .org, .jp, .pay, .live, .nuclear, etc. |No restrictions |
|Quantity limit |Each BytePlus account can get 20 free SSL certificates from DigiCert EE per calendar year. The quota cannot be refunded after the free certificates are revoked. |No restrictions |
|Renewal |Does not support renewal.<br><br>When a free certificate expires, you can only request a new free certificate or purchase a paid certificate. |Supports renewal.<br><br>Some CAs support transferring the remaining validity of the old certificate to the renewed one without exceeding the new 397\-day maximum certificate validity. |
|Trust level |Medium |High |
|Compatibility |Medium |High |
