If the free certificate does not meet your needs, you can upgrade it to a paid certificate. After the paid certificate is issued, you need to install it on the web server to replace the free certificate.

<span id="operation-steps"></span>
## Operation steps


1. Log on to the [BytePlus Certificate Center console](https://console.byteplus.com/certificate-center).

2. In the left navigation bar, select **SSL Certificate** \> **Certificates**.

3. On the **Certificate management** page, find the free certificate you want to upgrade and click **Upgrade** in the **Actions** column.

4. In the **Upgrade free certificate** dialog box, review the differences between the free certificate and the paid certificate, and then click **Upgrade**.


After completing the above steps, you will enter the **Purchase certificate** page. You can refer to [Creating an SSL certificate order](https://docs.byteplus.com/byteplus-certificate-center/docs/creating-certificate-order) to complete the purchase of the paid certificate.
