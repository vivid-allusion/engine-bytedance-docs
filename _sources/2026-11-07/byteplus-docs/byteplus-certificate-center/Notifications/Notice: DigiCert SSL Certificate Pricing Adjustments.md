Dear BytePlus Users,

In accordance with the latest requirements issued by the CA/Browser (CA/B) Forum regarding public certificate validity periods, DigiCert will cease issuing public SSL/TLS certificates with a validity period exceeding 199 days. This policy applies to all public TLS certificate brands under DigiCert, including DV, OV, and EV types. While the CA/B Forum has set a phased upper limit of 200 days, DigiCert has adopted a 199\-day maximum to ensure strict compliance and enhanced security.

<span id="U4ZHdY1B"></span>
### Key Adjustments

BytePlus Certificate Center will update the pricing for affected certificates in accordance with the latest upstream provider policies.


* **Effective Date:**  For specific effective dates and rules, please refer to the [Notice on Reduction of SSL Certificate Validity Period](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-certificate-center/notice-on-shortening-of-ssl-certificate-validity-period?lang=en#ch0s6Fcz).

* **Affected Brands:**  DigiCert, DigiCert Pro, GeoTrust (all under the DigiCert family)

* **Primary Change:**  Certificate validity period will be reduced from 1 year to 199 days, with pricing adjusted accordingly based on the new validity term.
