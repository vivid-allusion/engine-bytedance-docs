Dear BytePlus Users,

We would like to inform you that BytePlus Certificate Center will discontinue the application and hosting services for Let's Encrypt free SSL certificates starting from November 13, 2025.

After this date, the following changes will take effect:


1. You will no longer see Let's Encrypt as an option when applying for free certificates (i.e., on the "Request free certificate" page).

2. Hosting services for existing or new Let's Encrypt certificates will no longer be available.


As an alternative, you can continue to apply for DigiCert EE free SSL certificates through the same channel—this option will remain fully supported to meet your certificate needs.

We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience this adjustment may bring, and we greatly appreciate your understanding and ongoing support for BytePlus.
