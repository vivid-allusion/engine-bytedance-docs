Dear BytePlus Users,

Due to a pricing strategy adjustment by DigiCert, a globally recognized Certificate Authority (CA), BytePlus Certificate Center will update the prices for related certificate specifications accordingly. This adjustment covers certificates under the Secure Site, Secure Site Pro, GeoTrust, and RapidSSL brands (all under the DigiCert family).

The updated pricing will officially take effect on **May 15, 2026**. From that date onward, the new list prices will be applied to all relevant certificate specifications in the Certificate Center.

## Important Notices


* This price adjustment **only applies to new orders** submitted on or after **May 15, 2026**. The validity and service of certificates purchased before this date will not be affected.

* If you have plans for bulk purchases or renewals, we recommend completing your orders before the price adjustment takes effect to take advantage of the current pricing.
