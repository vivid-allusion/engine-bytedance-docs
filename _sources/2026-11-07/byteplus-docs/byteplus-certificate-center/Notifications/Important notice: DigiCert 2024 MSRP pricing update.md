Dear BytePlus Certificate Center Users,

In light of the notification received from DigiCert regarding the adjustment of MSRP (Manufacturer's Suggested Retail Price) in 2024, BytePlus Certificate Center will revise the pricing of specific certificate types in accordance with the latest pricing strategy of our upstream manufacturers.

These specifications include certificates from the RapidSSL, SecureSite, SecureSite Pro, and GeoTrust brands (all of which are subsidiaries of DigiCert).

The new adjusted prices will officially take effect from **June 10, 2024**. At that time, the relevant certificate types at BytePlus Certificate Center will be implemented at the new MSRP prices.

You can view the table below to understand the impacted certificate types and their MSRP price changes.
