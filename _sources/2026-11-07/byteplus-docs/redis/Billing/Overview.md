This topic explains the billing methods, instance life cycles, and prices of Cache for Redis.

<span id=".6K6h6LS557G75Z6L"></span>
## Billing method


|Billing method |Description |
|---|---|
|**Pay\-as\-you\-go** |Also known as postpaid. Expenses are calculated by second based on actual usage of the Redis instance and charged to the account by hour. It is suitable for short\-term needs and small business volumes, and can be released immediately when used up. It is flexible and convenient. For more information, refer to [Pay-as-you-go](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/redis/pay-as-you-go). |
|**Subscription** |Also known as prepaid. In the case of this billing method, when you create a new Redis instance, you prepay for it based on the duration of use. It is suitable for long\-term needs with large business volumes, and the price is more affordable than the pay\-as\-you\-go billing method. For more information, refer to [Yearly or monthly subscription](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/redis/subscription). |


<span id=".5a6e5L6L55Sf5ZG95ZGo5pyf"></span>
## Instance life cycle

The billing method determines how the Redis instance life cycle is calculated, where:


* **Pay\-as\-you\-go**: The life cycle of an instance starts from the time when you create the instance successfully to the time when you release it.

* **Yearly or monthly subscription**: The life cycle of the instance is from the time when you create the instance successfully, and the instance will be released automatically when it expires.
