As the provider of the Effects SDK, BytePlus Pte. Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "BytePlus", "we", "us", or "our") respects and is committed to protecting the security of your personal information. Our SDK offers developers a range of intelligent image rendering services, such as special effect stickers, filters, intelligent portrait processing, and computer vision\-based detection and recognition algorithms. When developers integrate our SDK into the applications and products they develop and/or manage, including apps, mini\-programs, and web pages (hereinafter referred to collectively as "applications" or "developer applications"), the SDK may collect and process data from users (hereinafter referred to as "you" or "your"). In this case, the developer, as the "personal information processor", decides the purposes and methods of processing of user data. We only collect data on behalf of the developer in the process of providing the developer with the specific functions of the Effects SDK, and process the data according to the developer's authorization and instructions. This Privacy Policy clearly, accurately, and completely explains to you how we collect, process, and protect all the information you provide when you use a developer application that integrates our Effects SDK. **Important Statement**:


1. This Privacy Policy does not supersede the privacy policies for developer applications.

2. Developers should disclose their own privacy policy to inform you of how they collect, process, and protect your personal information.

3. If you wish to access your data, or correct, modify, or delete incorrect data, or if you want to stop using an application that integrates the Effects SDK, please contact the relevant developer (personal information processor) directly.

4. Effects SDK is an offline SDK, which will not store and transfer any personal data locally or to BytePlus server.

# **1. How Do We Collect And Use Personal Information?** 

1.1 If you use a developer application that integrates the Effects SDK, the SDK will collect the following information programmatically on behalf of the developer:


|SDK Name |Function |Type of Personal Information |Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
|Effects SDK |By processing the collected images or video streams using computer vision algorithms, the SDK provides a range of features including computer vision\-based detection and recognition, special effects stickers, filters, and smart portrait processing, and is suitable for a variety of scenarios such as photography tools, live streaming, short videos, online education, and somatosensory games. |If "offline authorization" is used, the SDK will not collect any device or system information;<br><br>if "online authorization" is used, the SDK will collect the unique device identifier (Android IDs for Android devices and IDFV for iOS devices), and encrypt the collected information in an irreversible manner. |The information is used to verify the uniqueness of the device in the case of online authorization. |


We will not require you to provide your personal information. The information we collect is not sufficient to identify a specific individual on its own. Due to its technical characteristics, the SDK cannot obtain any information that can alone identify a specific individual during its operation.

1.2 List of permissions

The Effects SDK does not require any system permissions. However, developers can apply for permissions such as camera access or read/write access to external memory, depending on their business needs. These permissions are not essential for the functioning of the SDK.

# **2. How Do We Protect Personal Information?** 

2.1 We attach great importance to the security of your information and strive to take reasonable security measures (including both technical and management measures) to protect it from misuse or unauthorized access, disclosure, use, modification, damage, loss, or leakage.

2.2 We adopt feasible means, such as encryption and anonymization, that are not lower than industry standards to protect your information, and use security protection mechanisms to prevent malicious attacks on your information.

2.3 We have a dedicated security department and have established a security management system and a data security process to ensure the security of your information. We also have strict data use and access policies in place to ensure that only authorized personnel can access your information. In addition, we conduct security audits of our data and technology from time to time.

2.4 In the event of a user information security incident (such as a leak or loss), we will activate our emergency response plan and make every effort to prevent the impact and consequences of the incident from becoming worse. In addition, we will, in accordance with laws and regulations, inform you of the basic information and possible impacts of the incident, the measures we have taken or are about to take, suggestions for you to independently prevent and reduce risks, the remedies for you, etc., through push notification, email, letter, SMS, or other means. If it is difficult to inform each affected user individually, we will make an announcement in a reasonable and effective way. We will also report the handling of the incident to relevant regulatory authorities as required.

2.5 Please be aware that the user information protection measures described in this Privacy Policy only apply to our services. If you leave our service pages to access or use other websites, products, services, or content resources, we are not responsible for protecting any information you provide in the related software or on the related websites, regardless of whether you log in to, browse, or use those software or websites by using links or guides provided on our service pages.
