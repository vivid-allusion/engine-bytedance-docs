## About SDK Processing Time

Two main factors affect the time taken by the SDK to process an image:


1. Resolution of the input image.

2. Enabled functions.


When the SDK is used on a mobile device, the recommended resolution is **720p or 1080p** . A higher resolution may lead to freezing, crashes, or other problems. Use high\-resolution images with caution.
