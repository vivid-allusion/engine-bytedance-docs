# Technical Specifications


|**Category** |**Supported Formats** |
|---|---|
|Platforms |* Android<br><br>* iOS<br><br>* Windows<br><br>* macOS<br><br>* Linux |
|Input format |* Texture2D<br><br>* YUV<br><br>* RGBA |
|CPU architecture |* Mobile phone: armeabi\-v7, arm64\-v8a<br><br>* PC: x86 |
|Angles |* Yaw ≤ ±90°<br><br>* pitch ≤ ±90° |
|Portraits Number |≤ 10. |
|Face\-to\-screen ratio |A face shall account for more than 1/8 of the short side of the screen in video mode or 1/16 of the short side of the screen in photo mode. |
|Face detection speed |< 2 ms (on iPhone 7). |
|Operating system versions |* Android 6 and later<br><br>* iOS 10.0 and later |
