This SDK detects hands in images or videos in real time. It supports single\-hand, two\-hand, and multi\-person gesture recognition, bounding box detection, keypoints detection, and hand gesture classification.

<span id="Product Features"></span>
## Product Features

**Finger keypoints detection**

It can detect and dynamically track 22 keypoints of the hand, including fingertips, joints, palms, and other keypoints.

**Hand gesture recognition**

It can recognize 4 types of hand gestures, including static gestures such as a heart gesture, victory gesture, OK sign, and a fist, as well as dynamic gestures such as swiping left, right, up, and down.
