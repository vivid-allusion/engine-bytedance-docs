This document will give you an overview of our Beauty SDK. The SDK contains special effects and makeup filters, face and body morphing, and supports real\-time effects in videos and photos.

<span id="Product Overview"></span>
## Product Overview

**Portrait effects**

Multiple adjustable effects including skin retouching and facial morphing, including filters that allow users to augment individual facial features.

**Makeup effects**

Features various adjustable makeup and AR micro shaping effects, including lipstick, blush, eyeshadow, hair dyeing, and more.

**Portrait filters**

Diverse filter templates, which cover nearly 100 styles to meet a range of requirements.
