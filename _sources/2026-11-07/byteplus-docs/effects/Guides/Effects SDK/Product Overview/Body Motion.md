This SDK detects human bodies in images or videos in real\-time. It supports multi\-person detection, half\-body detection, bounding box detection, and keypoints generation.

<span id="Product Features"></span>
## Product Features

**Real\-time detection of human bodies**

It supports multi\-person detection, half\-body detection, bounding box detection, and generation of keypoints.

**Keypoint detection**

It can locate 18 keypoints of the human body, including the head, shoulders, feet, and other parts, to track actions such as raising hands, bending down and jumping.

<span id="Application Scenarios"></span>
## Application Scenarios

**Interactive games**

Accurately detect keypoints and recognize postures and actions in real time in motion\-sensing games.

**Sports and fitness**

Detect keypoints of your body in real time and intelligently score your actions by comparing them with the standard actions.

Recognize postures and actions based on the relative positions of keypoints in scenarios involving elderly fall detection and prevention.
