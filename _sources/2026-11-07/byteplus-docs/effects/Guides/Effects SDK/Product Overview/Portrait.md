This document will give you an overview of our Portrait SDK. This SDK will allow you to detect up to 280 key\-points in portraits, recognize face movements and features.

<span id="Product Overview"></span>
## Product Overview

**Object and key\-point detection**

Detect multiple faces within images or videos in real\-time, through object detection and key\-points for each portrait.

**Facial movement recognition**

Recognize six key facial movements — blinking, opening of the mouth, nodding and shaking, the raising of eyebrows, and lip pouting.

**Portrait attribute recognition**

Identify basic facial attributes including emotions and gestures.
