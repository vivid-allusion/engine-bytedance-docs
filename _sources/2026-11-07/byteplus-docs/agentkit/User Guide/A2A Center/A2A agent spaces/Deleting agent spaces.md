As your business grows, the number of spaces in the A2A Center typically keeps increasing. You can delete historical agent spaces that are no longer in use as needed, reducing unnecessary spaces and lowering management and O&M costs.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">Agent space deletion is irreversible and is a <strong>high\-risk operation</strong>. Once you delete a space, callers can no longer discover or schedule agents based on this space. We recommend that you confirm that the space is no longer in use and that all related business adjustments have been completed before proceeding.</div>


<span id="da6sx9KQ"></span>
## Prerequisites

All agents in the space have been [unregistered](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/agentkit/Unregistering_A2A_agents).

<span id="LlZ1uBbq"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in the [AgentKit console](https://console.byteplus.com/agentkit). 

2. In the left\-side navigation pane, select "Basic Components \> A2A Center".

3. In the A2A agent space drop\-down list, find the target A2A agent space, then click "... \> Delete".

4. After confirming there is no risk, click the "Delete" button to complete the operation.
