To provide you with higher\-quality services, BytePlus AgentKit will officially start billing from 00:00 on August 24, 2026 (UTC+8).

<span id="qaKvFmAB"></span>
## Billing start time

Billing will start from 00:00 on August 24, 2026 (UTC+8). The actual start time is subject to the launch time in the console.

<span id="wxOeESxQ"></span>
## Billing instructions

AgentKit adopts the pay\-as\-you\-go (postpaid) billing method. The system aggregates the actual usage of each billing item hourly and automatically deducts the fees from your account balance. To continue using AgentKit, please ensure that your account balance is sufficient. For pricing and other details, see: [AgentKit billing](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/agentkit/Billing_items?lang=en).

<span id="vsjZ18T6"></span>
## Considerations


* If you have created resources such as agent runtimes, tools, and gateways in your account and do not delete these resources before 00:00 on August 24, 2026, it indicates your intent to continue using the AgentKit service. Starting from August 24, 2026, AgentKit will calculate fees for relevant resources based on your actual usage. Please ensure that your account balance is sufficient to prevent service suspension due to arrears. For details on arrears, refer to [Instructions on arrears](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/agentkit/Instructions_on_arrears).

* If you delete all resources before 00:00 on August 24, 2026, no fees will be incurred.


If you have any questions, please [submit a ticket](https://console.byteplus.com/workorder/create?step=2&SubProductID=P00000240) to contact us. Thank you for your support and understanding of BytePlus!
