## **General standardized scenarios**

With the scenario\-based templates, preset components, knowledge bases, and runtime capabilities provided by AgentKit, enterprises can quickly build standardized agent applications for high\-frequency needs such as Q&A, retrieval, content generation, and workflow collaboration. By combining mainstream capabilities such as RAG, tool calling, LLM reasoning, and Agentic Workflow, the platform reduces development and integration complexity while improving deployment efficiency, delivery consistency, and production availability for general AI applications.

<span id="3aazL7lT"></span>
## **Customized scenarios for enterprise business needs**

With AgentKit's MCP, memory, knowledge base, tool integration, and permission governance capabilities, enterprises can deeply integrate proprietary business data, process rules, and industry knowledge to build customized agents for specific business processes. By introducing RAG, Multi\-Agent, multimodal understanding, tool calling, and short\-term and long\-term memory coordination mechanisms, the platform supports complex task decomposition, cross\-system collaboration, and continuous context awareness. This enhances business efficiency and enables more intelligent decision\-making.

<span id="Yb9QkGxO"></span>
## **Technical development empowerment scenarios**

Built on AgentKit's Serverless foundation, multi\-tenant isolation, elastic scaling, end\-to\-end observability, Guardrails, and evaluation system, technical teams can build a full\-lifecycle agent engineering system covering development, deployment, debugging, observability, and optimization. The platform supports multiple build and integration methods, including VeADK, MCP, A2A, and HTTP. It can support intelligent upgrades of existing systems, Agentic Workflow orchestration, toolchain integration, and large\-scale operations, providing stable, governable, and sustainably evolving technical infrastructure for enterprise\-grade AI applications.

## **Cross\-industry, large\-scale deployment adaptation**

AgentKit provides elastic scalability, security and compliance, component\-based integration, and flexible customization capabilities, supporting the large\-scale replication and deployment of AI agents across industries.


* **Internet and technology**: Intelligent development, content production, intelligent operations, and user growth.

* **Retail and e\-commerce**: Intelligent customer service, store inspection, after\-sales processing, product recommendations, and member operations.

* **Manufacturing**: Product quality inspection, equipment inspection, production process guidance, and fault early warning.

* **Education**: Educational content creation, intelligent Q&A, and multimodal interaction for online education.

* **Enterprise services**: Dedicated knowledge services, full\-lifecycle customer management, and office workflow automation.


By integrating RAG, multimodality, Multi\-Agent, MCP, tool calling, and other capabilities, the platform can meet the multidimensional requirements of different industries for efficiency, quality, security, and intelligent collaboration.
