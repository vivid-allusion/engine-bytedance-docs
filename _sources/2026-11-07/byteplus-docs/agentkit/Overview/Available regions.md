This topic describes the concept of a region, how to select one, and the regions in which AgentKit is currently available.

<span id="SJc32HV7"></span>
## What is a region?

**Region** refers to a geographic area where physical data centers are located. After a resource is created, you cannot change its region. Private networks between different regions are completely isolated, which ensures maximum stability and fault tolerance across regions.

<span id="hXbclSk0"></span>
## How to select a region?

When selecting a region, you need to consider the following factors:


* From a geographical perspective, we generally recommend that you select a region close to you or your target users to reduce network latency and improve access speed.

* From a resource price perspective, resource prices may vary by region. If your business or your target users are in the Chinese mainland, network latency caused by different regions is not a concern. This is because regional differences in the Chinese mainland are minimal across aspects, including infrastructure, BGP network quality, resource operations, and resource configuration.
