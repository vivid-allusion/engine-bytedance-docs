This topic describes the billing methods, billed resources, and settlement cycles supported by AgentKit.

## Billing method

AgentKit uses the **Pay\-as\-you\-go** (postpaid) billing method by default. The system accumulates the actual usage of each billing item by the hour and automatically deducts the fee from the balance.

> **tip**

> To ensure business continuity and prevent service suspension caused by overdue payments, we recommend that you keep at least 7 days of estimated consumption in your account balance.

## Billing cycle

* **Billing cycle**: Usage is billed hourly. At each hour mark, usage from the prior hour is settled.

* **View bills**: After a bill is generated, you can view hourly, daily, and monthly details in [Billing center > Bill details](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/bill/detail).

* **Deduction method**: Fees are automatically deducted from the available balance of your account. If the balance is insufficient, your account becomes overdue.
