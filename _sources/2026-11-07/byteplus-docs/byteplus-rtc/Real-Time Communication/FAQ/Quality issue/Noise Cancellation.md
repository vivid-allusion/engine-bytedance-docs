## Question

Regarding the issue of audio noise, it is usually caused by the audio collection environment or device. You can follow the following steps to solve the problem:

<span id="answer"></span>
## Answer

<span id="step-1-self-check"></span>
### Step 1: Self\-check

The following factors may cause audio noise. Please check and eliminate the relevant reasons:


* Multiple terminals that communicate with each other are too close to each other and in a speaker state, resulting in a cascade gain greater than 1, causing near\-field howling.

* One or more participants are in a strong noise environment during a call.

* The recording equipment is not working normally. Poor contact or connection quality of external devices.

* The microphone is shaking or rubbing against clothing.


<span id="step-2-contact-technical-support-for-assistance"></span>
### Step 2: Contact technical support for assistance

When contacting technical support, provide the following information:

<span id="necessary-information"></span>
#### Necessary information:


* The time and room ID of the occurrence of audio noise.

* The user ID that caused the audio noise, such as when a certain user in the room turned on the microphone and there was noise.


<span id="auxiliary-information"></span>
#### Auxiliary information:


* Audio recordings including the period when the problem happened.

* If there are more than 2 participants in the room, whether other participants can hear the noise;

* If the feedback person is the user who appeared noise, does the noise always exist when turning the microphone on and off;

* Try to use the built\-in recording device of the device for testing, and observe whether the test results are normal.
