## 10000\-minute free package


* **How to get**

   Each RTC AppId is eligible for a monthly 10,000\-minute free pack, which can be used to deduct audio and video consumption when utilizing RTC services.

* **Deduction items and rules**

   Free packages deduct usage at a 1:1 ratio. Voice calls and video calls of all resolutions (360P, 720P, and 1080P) are eligible for deduction, while usage time for services such as Cloud Recording, Push to CDN, Audio Segment, and Snapshot cannot be deducted.



|Deductible charge items |Audio\-only |360P video |720P video |1080P video |2K video |4K video |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|Deduction per minute from package |1 |1 |1 |1 |1 |1 |


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true">Tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">The resource package does not cover other bill items, such as fees for cloud recording, server\-side push to CDN, public stream, audio segment used during RTC service.</div>



* **Bill cycle**

   Fees are settled on a monthly basis.

* **Validation period**

   Free packages are valid for each month. When you create your AppId, you will receive a free package that expires on the last day of the month. For instance, if your AppId is created on March 12, the free package for that AppId will expire on March 31. Additionally, a new free package is provided for each AppId on the first day of every month. Unused minutes from the previous month will expire on the last day of the month and cannot be carried forward or redeemed for cash.

* **Viewing deduction details and alerts**

   You can check the details of the deduction and manage alerts for remaining minutes on [Resource Management](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/resource-package).

   RTC is billed monthly, and balances and deduction details are updated once per month.

## Prepaid packages

<span id="purchase-packages"></span>
### Purchase packages

Resource packages offer discounts compared to pay\-as\-you\-go billing. You can purchase resource packages for audio and video calls, cloud recording, and push to CDN.


* The usage duration for each package type is calculated independently.

* You can stack multiple package purchases. [Click to Purchase](https://console.byteplus.com/rtc/resource_package/resource_package_payment?type=resource)

<span id="validity-period"></span>
### Validity period

Prepaid packages remain valid for a duration of 12 months from the date of purchase. Please note that any unused minutes after the expiration date will not be eligible for a refund. Therefore, we recommend using all minutes in the package before it expires.

<span id="deduction-order"></span>
### Deduction order


* **Multiple packages of the same type**: Prioritize deduction from the package that expires first.

* **For call fees**: 10,000\-minute free package \> call package \> Pay\-As\-You\-Go.

* **When packages are depleted or expire**：Once the prepaid packages are depleted or expire, any additional service time will be charged according to the applicable billing rules under the Pay\-As\-You\-Go model.


<span id="deduction-details-remaining-balance-alerts"></span>
### Deduction details/Remaining balance alerts

You can check the details of the deduction and manage alerts for remaining minutes on [Resource Management](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/resource-package).
