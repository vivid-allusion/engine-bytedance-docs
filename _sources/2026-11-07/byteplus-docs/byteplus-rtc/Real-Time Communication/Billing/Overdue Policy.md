Starting from May 18, 2023, when there are overdue payments on your BytePlus account, you will receive an overdue reminder. You can recharge your account in the [Billing Center](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/bill) within 72 hours after it becomes overdue.

> An account is considered overdue when its balance is insufficient to cover all product and service bills, or when the balance falls below zero.


<span id="suspension"></span>
## Suspension


* Within 72 hours after the account is overdue, your services will continue to function normally.

* If your account remains overdue for more than 72 hours, a suspension will be triggered. All existing rooms will be immediately closed, all in\-progress audio and video services will be interrupted, and you will not be able to initiate any new paid tasks. If the account is recharged within 60 days, your services will be restored.


<span id="termination"></span>
## Termination

If an account remains suspended for more than 60 days, BytePlus will permanently delete all associated historical data and records. This data cannot be recovered.
