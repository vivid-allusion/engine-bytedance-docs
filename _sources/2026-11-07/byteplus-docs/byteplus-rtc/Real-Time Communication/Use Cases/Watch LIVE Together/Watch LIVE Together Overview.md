Watch LIVE together creates a new scenario for social entertainment. You can experience the vibe of watching LIVE with friends offline via real\-time audio and video interactions. The room owner can create an online room for real\-time interactions, play LIVE videos in the room, and invite users to watch LIVE and have video chat together.

This use case supports adding image enhancement video effects, text chat in the room to enhance entertaining interactions with friends watching LIVE together.

You can choose LIVE content independently depending on your current business, including tournaments, games, concerts, parties, and live e\-commerce.

An RTC room can accommodate a maximum of 50 visible users, and 30 media streams can be published simultaneously. For more information, see [Room Capacity](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/12/docs-257549). There are usually the following 2 roles in this use case:


|Role |Description |
|---|---|
|Room owner |Room creator who participates in audio and video interactions in the room, and initiates watching LIVE together. The room owner can manage the room, and choose and switch the LIVE content. |
|Audience |A user who joins the room can participate in audio\-video interactions by enabling their microphone. |


<span id="how-to-play"></span>
## How to Play


* Watch LIVE together with friends

* You can watch video content including games, tournaments, shows, and live e\-commerce with your friends, while exchanging and discussing live content. This feature simulates the experience of watching live events with friends, which enhances user communication, promotes content consumption, and creates an immersive atmosphere.

* Online concerts

* For example, a concert organizer can sell tickets that grant online access, allowing thousands of fans to join a room to watch the concert, chat, and experience the event's atmosphere together. The host can initiate topic discussions and give live commentary based on the live content, create topics and build momentum for the live event.

<span id="feature-list"></span>
## Feature List


|Main feature |Description |
|---|---|
|Real\-time video/audio interaction |Users can have real\-time audio, video interactions in the room. |
|Device management |Users can enable/disable the camera and microphone, and switch between the front and rear lenses. |
|Image enhancement effects |Users can use Image Enhancement features such as skin smoothing, skin whitening, big eyes, AR effects, and 2D/3D video effect stickers. |
|LIVE video playback |After starting the Watch LIVE Together session, the room owner can watch live videos with the audience, and change the live content and end the session. |
|Volume adjustment |All users in the room can adjust the volume of the caller's voice or video playback volume, and enable the audio ducking feature to ensure a clear audio experience while users are watching and chatting. |
|Real\-time message interaction |Users in the room can communicate in real\-time by sending texts. |


<span id="advantages"></span>
## Advantages


* Comparable to existing LIVE effects

* The live image quality matches the original source.

* Supports hundreds of users with active microphones simultaneously, without echo or loss of sound quality.

* Smart volume equalization ensures a smoother watching and chatting experience.

* Ultra\-low latency creates an immersive and interactive atmosphere.

* A thousand users can watch videos together in a single room to meet the needs of large\-scale social interaction.

* Over 300 nodes worldwide guarantee low\-latency audio and video chat, creating a seamless experience for users regardless of their location.

* Extremely low migration/access costs

* Maximize the original LIVE experience, with no reconstruction required for first frames, event tracking, etc.
