## Instruction

For a better experience, prepare at least 2 mobile phones:


1. Download and install the installation package.

2. Log in \-\> select scenario module \-\> enter [Chatting room]. Use one of the mobile phones to tap [Create live streaming] to create a live streaming room, and use the other to enter the room.

3. Tap an empty guest seat/the guest icon at the bottom to invite audiences in the live streaming room to become guests.

4. By tapping the Music icon at the bottom, the host can play background music in the room, and adjust the volume by sliding the volume bar.

5. Viewers can tap an empty guest seat to become a guest. Guests can return to the audience by tapping their avatar. Viewers can also chat in the live stream.

6. The host can use Guest Seat Management to lock seats or mute guests. Users in locked guest seats cannot use their microphones.
