## Instruction

For a better experience, prepare at least 2 mobile phones:


1. Download and install the app.

2. Log in \-\> Select the scenario \-\> enter the [Interactive Video Streaming] mode.

3. Tap [Create a Room] to create a live streaming room and enter the live streaming preview page. On this page, you can configure beauty effects. Tap the button to begin live streaming and enter the live streaming room.

4. Co\-host battle: A host taps the battle button in the room to invite other hosts to a competition. The invited host can be muted.

5. Inviting audience members to be guests: Tap the open guest seat or the Co\-host icon at the bottom to invite audience members in the room to become guests. The host can block the guest seats or mute guests.
