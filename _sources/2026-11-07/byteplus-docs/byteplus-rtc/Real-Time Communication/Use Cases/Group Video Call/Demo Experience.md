## Instruction

For a better experience, prepare at least 2 mobile phones:

<span id="mobile"></span>
#### **Mobile**


1. Download and install the package.

2. Log in \> Click [Audio and Video Call] mode.

3. In the preview page, users can toggle the microphone and camera on or off.

4. To communicate in the same room, multiple users must enter the same room number and Click [Call now].

5. View call statistics: Click call statistics to view local and remote audio and video transmission parameters.

6. Resolution and call quality settings: Click [Settings] to select the resolution and call quality level.

7. Local mirror: Click [Settings] to turn the local mirror on or off.

8. Screen sharing: When Windows or Web users share their screens, all other participants can view and enlarge the shared screen.


<span id="windows"></span>
#### **Windows**


1. Download and install the trial package.

2. Log in \> Click [Audio and Video Call] mode.

3. On the room entry page, users can toggle the microphone and camera on or off.

4. To join the same room for audio and video calls, multiple users must enter the same room number and Click [Call now].

5. View call statistics: Click [Call statistics] to view local and remote audio and video transmission parameters.

6. Resolution and call quality settings: Click [Settings] to select the resolution and call quality level, and turn on/off local mirror.

7. Screen sharing: Click [Share Screen] to share the screen or window, and choose whether to prioritize clarity or fluency.
