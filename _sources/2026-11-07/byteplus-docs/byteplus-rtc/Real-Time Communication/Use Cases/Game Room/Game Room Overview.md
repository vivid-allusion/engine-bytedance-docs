Game room is a new gameplay in pan\-entertainment socializing, including mini\-games for lovers, and mini\-games in chatting rooms. Hosts and users choose different mini\-games in chatting rooms or during calling, such as five\-in\-a\-row, draw\-and\-guess, and finger\-guessing. Mini\-games prolong users' retention while creating more topics. In a chatting room, the host can invite audiences to play games.

An RTC room can accommodate a maximum of 50 visible users, and 30 media streams can be published simultaneously. For more information, see [Room Capacity](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/12/docs-257549). There are usually the following 3 roles in a game room:


|Role |Description |
|---|---|
|Host |The user creates the game room and invites audiences to become guests. |
|Audience |The user joins the game room to watch the live stream and can apply to become a guest to participate in the interaction. |
|Guest |After becoming a guest, the user can be seen and heard by other audience members in the room. |


<span id="how-to-play"></span>
## How to Play


* Dating or social networking apps

   In addition to traditional voice calling, users can initiate mini\-games to increase user retention and stickiness.

* ASR speech recognition

   ASR can convert a user's voice to text during voice calls, which is useful in scenarios like draw\-and\-guess. Pop\-up boxes can be displayed to indicate whether the recognition was correct.

* Smart beauty video effects

   The room owner or connected co\-hosts can also use the recognition feature to trigger a wide variety of video effects through facial expressions such as blinking and pouting, gestures such as hand heart or "OK", as well as moves such as swinging arms and squatting. These features appeal to users who want to enhance their appearance and make video interactions more fun.

## Feature List


|**Main feature** |**Description** |
|---|---|
|Real\-time video/audio interaction |Users can have real\-time audio/video interactions in the room. |
|Co\-host seat control |The room owner can add or remove guests and audience members from the guest seats. |
|Device management |Allows users to enable or disable their microphones. |
|Speech highlighting |When a user is speaking, their status can be highlighted. |
|Third\-party mini\-games |Supports integration with third\-party mini\-games, such as five\-in\-a\-row. |
|Volume adjustment |All users in the room can adjust the audio volume from other users or the mini\-game to suit their preferences. |
