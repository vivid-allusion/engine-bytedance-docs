## Quality Data Indicators


|Parameter |Return value |Description |
|---|---|---|
|`join_room_success_rate` |The success rate (%) of joining the room uder this AppId within the span of joining the room. |For example, `5s_join_room_success_rate` indicates the success rate of joining a room within 5 seconds. The default value of room entry time is `5s`. |
|`stall_rate` |The stall rate (%) of the profile corresponding to this AppId. |For example, `80ms_audio_stall_rate` indicates the percentage of audio stalls within 80ms during a call.<br><br>If there are audio and video calling at the same time, the indicators of audio and video will be returned respectively.<br><br><br>* `audio_stall_rate`: audio stall rate. The default value of freeze time is `80ms`.<br><br>* `video_stall_rate`: Video stall rate. The default value of freeze time is `500ms`. |
|`first_frame_send_success_rate` |The success rate (%) of first frame sent under this AppId. |If there are audio and video calling at the same time, the indicators of audio and video will be returned separately.<br><br><br>* `audio_first_frame_send_success_rate`: the success rate of the first auido frame sent<br><br>* `video_first_frame_send_success_rate`: the success rate of the first video frame sent |
|`first_frame_recv_success_rate` |The success rate (%) of the first frame decoding under this AppId. |If there are audio and video calling at the same time, the indicators of audio and video will be returned respectively.<br><br><br>* `audio_first_frame_recv_success_rate`: the decoding success rate of the first audio frame<br><br>* `video_first_frame_recv_success_rate`: the decoding success rate of the first video frame |
|`network_trans_delay` |The network transmission delay compliance rate (%) of the profile corresponding to this AppId. |For example, `400ms_network_trans_delay` indicates the percentage of time during the call that the transmission delay between the two parties is within 400ms.<br><br><br>* `audio_network_trans_delay`: Audio network transmission delay compliance rate. The default value of the transmission delay is `400ms`.<br><br>* `video_network_trans_delay`: Video network transmission delay compliance rate. The default value of the transmission delay is `400ms`. |

## Performance Indicators


|Indicator |Description |
|---|---|
|`join_room_success_rate` |The success rate of joining a room within a period of time(%).<br><br>For example, `5s_join_room_success_rate` means the success rate of joining a room within 5s.<br><br>The default value for the time used to join a room is **5s**. |
|`stall_rate` |The percentage of time during an audio and video call when the stuttering(ms) is within a period of time(%).<br><br>For example, `80ms_audio_stall_rate` means the percentage of time during a call when audio stuttering(ms) is within 80ms.<br><br><br>* The default value for audio stuttering is **80ms**.<br><br>* The default value for video stuttering is **500ms**. |
|`first_frame_send_success_rate` |The success rate of sending the first frame(%). |
|`first_frame_recv_success_rate` |The success rate of decoding the first frame (%). |
|`network_trans_delay` |The percentage of time during an audio and video call when the transmission delay (ms) between users is within a period of time(%).<br><br>For example, `400ms_video_network_trans_delay` means the percentage of time during a call when the delay in video transmission is within 400ms.<br><br>The default value for transmission delay is **400 ms**. |
