BytePlus conversational AI solution enables natural, smooth, and lifelike real\-time voice interactions between humans and an AI agent, beyond text\-based communication. It can be applied to scenarios such as AI assistants, AI customer service, AI companions, AI oral language teaching, AI game NPCs, and smart hardware.

<span id="technical-architecture"></span>
## Technical Architecture

Conversational AI leverages BytePlus RTC to achieve efficient collection, custom processing, and ultra\-low\-latency transmission of audio and video data. On the cloud side, it provides intelligent audio and video processing modules, including audio 3A, AI noise reduction, and snapshot capabilities, to reduce the impact of environmental noise and device performance on the conversational AI experience.

This solution offers two integration modes that simplify the voice\-to\-text and text\-to\-voice conversion process. These modes provide powerful capabilities for intelligent conversation, natural language processing, and multi\-modal interaction. These modes help applications quickly implement real\-time voice calls and multi\-modal interactions between users and cloud\-based large models.

Cascading Solution: It supports flexible combinations of ASR, TTS, and LLM vendors, including OpenAI, Amazon, and Google.

End\-to\-End Solution: OpenAI's Realtime API does not require additional ASR and TTS integration.

## Functional Features


* **Basic Functions**

&nbsp;

   
   |Function |Description |
   |---|---|
   |**AI Real\-Time Voice Interaction** |Engage in natural, smooth real\-time voice conversations with AI, similar to communicating with a real person, supporting instant interruption. |
   |**Intelligent Interruption** |Provides manual and automatic interruption methods. Users can interrupt AI's voice output at any time during the conversation to enable two\-way interaction. |
   |**Noise Reduction** |This feature combines BytePlus RTC's advanced audio 3A technology with deep learning\-based AI noise reduction algorithms. This combination balances strong noise reduction with high fidelity, ensuring the effective removal of background noise while preserving clear human voices in noisy environments. |
   |**Speech Recognition (ASR)**  |Converts user speech to text in real time for large model analysis, subtitle generation, and other purposes. |
   |**Large Model Processing (LLM)**  |Parses input text and generates semantic responses to manage the AI agent's conversational flow. |
   |**Text\-to\-Speech (TTS)**  |Converts text responses generated by large models into voice for playback via RTC. |
   

* **Advanced Functions**

<span id="solution-advantages"></span>
## Solution Advantages


* **Natural Interaction with Instant Interruption**

   * **Intelligent Interruption**: Supports full\-duplex communication and frame\-level voice activity detection (VAD), enabling instant interruption for more natural interaction.

   * **Noise Reduction**: BytePlus RTC SDK provides noise reduction capabilities for complex environments, effectively reducing interference from background noise and music, and improving the accuracy of detecting user interruptions.

* **Real\-Time Response with Smooth Conversation**

   * **Ultra\-Low Latency**: Based on full\-link streaming processing, the overall latency of RTC+ASR+LLM+TTS is reduced to 1 second.

   * **Resilience to Weak Networks**: Through intelligent access and cloud\-edge collaborative optimization of RTC, the solution ensures low latency and transmission reliability in complex/weak network environments, preventing misinterpretations by the large model caused by missing words.

* **Flexible Expansion**

   **Multi\-Party Interaction**: Extends from 1\-on\-1 to 1\-to\-N AI real\-time interaction.

* **Fast and Easy Integration**

   One\-stop integration: Enterprises can configure the required ASR, LLM, and TTS services by calling standard OpenAPI interfaces to quickly implement AI real\-time interaction applications.

* **Cross\-Platform Compatibility**

   **Multi\-Platform Support**: Supports iOS, Android, Windows, Linux, macOS, Web, Flutter, Unity, Electron and React Native, meeting application needs across diverse scenarios.

* **Multilingual Interaction**

   * **Multi\-Language Support**: Enables real\-time conversations in Chinese, English, Japanese, Spanish, and other languages.
