## Product Overview

BytePlus has introduced the WebRTC Transmission Network (WTN), a server\-side transmission network service designed for WebRTC developers. WTN is constructed on a global real\-time transmission network that can handle billions of DAU (Daily Active Users). It offers **standard WebRTC access** to a broad spectrum of developers and businesses, delivering real\-time audio and video communication with ultra\-low latency, stability, and reliability on a worldwide scale.

Developers can harness the standard WebRTC protocol to connect with WTN and seamlessly integrate real\-time audio and video communication into their applications.

<span id="product-advantages"></span>
## Product Advantages

<span id="standard-protocol"></span>
### Standard Protocol

WTN employs the standard WebRTC media protocol and the standard signaling interaction protocol (WHIP / WHEP) for seamless media communication across devices. It also supports robust default encryption and intelligent routing for optimal transmission.

<span id="open-source-ecosystem"></span>
### Open Source Ecosystem


* WTN offers a quickstart demo, enabling developers to rapidly integrate with the WTN service.

* OBS supports the WHIP protocol, facilitating direct access to the WTN service.


<span id="ultra-low-latency"></span>
### Ultra\-Low Latency

WTN achieves ultra\-low latency in audio and video transmission by selecting top\-tier global nodes and applying advanced network prediction and scheduling technologies. Here are some typical average latencies:


* Mainland China: 24ms;

* Asia\-Pacific region (excluding Mainland China): 43ms;

* Europe and the Americas: 58ms;

* Other regions (such as South America, the Middle East, Africa): 181ms.


<span id="stable-and-reliable"></span>
### Stable and Reliable

WTN consistently supports products with billions of DAU, boasting a media connection success rate of over 99.95%.

<span id="unlimited-concurrency"></span>
### Unlimited Concurrency

The network is elastic and scalable, capable of handling massive scale with unlimited concurrency, ensuring it can withstand the transmission demands during periods of rapid growth.
