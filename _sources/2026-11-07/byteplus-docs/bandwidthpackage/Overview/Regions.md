## Region

A region is a geographical zone where a data center is located. The intranet is completely isolated among different regions, maximizing stability and fault tolerance of different regions. In most cases, define regions based on the cities where data centers are located.

Select the same region as your target Elastic IP Address (EIP) for the Bandwidth Package. You cannot change its region after purchase.
