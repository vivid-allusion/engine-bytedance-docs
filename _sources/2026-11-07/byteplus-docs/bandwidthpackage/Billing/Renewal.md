At seven days, three days, and one day before, and on the expiration date for Subscription Bandwidth Packages, BytePlus platform will send text messages, emails, or internal messages based on your [reminder settings](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/message_center/Message-Subscription-and-Notification-Methods) to notify you that Bandwidth Packages are about to expire. To avoid disruption to your business, renew your bandwidth package promptly when receiving the notification.


* You cannot adjust the maximum bandwidth for the Bandwidth Package during renewal. If needed, you can modify it after the renewal.

* If you renew before expiration suspension (within 12 hours after expiration), the start time of the renewed term is the expiration time of the previous term. If you renew after expiration suspension (within 12 hours after expiration) but before expiration reclaim, the start time of the renewed term is the actual renewal time.

## Manual renewal

You can manually renew your Bandwidth Package at any time before it is reclaimed due to expiration.

<span id="P7GEq1Lr"></span>
### Procedure


1. Log in to [the Bandwidth Package console](https://console.byteplus.com/eip/cbwps).

2. In the top menu bar, select the region and project of your target Bandwidth Package.

3. In the Bandwidth Package list, click  **··· \> Renew** to the right of the target Bandwidth Package.

4. Confirm the information of the Bandwidth Package to be renewed.

5. Select a renewal duration, then click **Pay**.

6. Click **Next step**, select a payment method, and then click **Pay**.

7. Make the payment by the selected method. After submitting the payment information, complete the payment within the specified time. Otherwise, the order will be canceled.

## Automatic renewal

You can check the **Automatic renewal** option when creating a Subscription Bandwidth Package or enable automatic renewal before the subscription expires. For procedure, see [Automatic renewal](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-auto-renewal-rules).

After enabling automatic renewal, if you have available coupons in your account, the system will prioritize using them for renewal.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">For resources with automatic renewal enabled, you can also manually renew them at any time. Manual renewal does not invalidate automatic renewal. The system will deduct fees 7 days before the updated expiration date of the resource.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">You cannot enable or modify Automatic renewal for expired Bandwidth Packages.</div>
