BytePlus supports billing conversion. You can convert metering methods at any time based on your business requirements. Except for Pay\-by\-95th\-peak (which takes effect at 00:00:00 the next day), all other metering methods become effective immediately.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Pay\-by\-95th\-peak is in the beta stage. To apply for a trial, please contact your account manager.</div>

## Convert between Pay\-by\-main\-traffic and Pay\-by\-bandwidth

<span id="u9GZ3lg1"></span>
### Limits


* The sum of both your [available balance](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/6269/100429) and the coupons applicable to the Bandwidth Package is not less than USD 100.00, and the available balance must be no less than 0. For specific procedure, see [Recharge guide](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/6269/100434).

* When converting Pay\-by\-bandwidth to Pay\-by\-main\-traffic, the converted maximum bandwidth defaults to 2,000 Mbps if the pre\-conversion bandwidth is greater than the post\-conversion maximum (2,000 Mbps).


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Decreasing bandwidth cap may affect business traffic and lead to packet loss. Evaluate the possible impacts of the action on your business and proceed with caution.</div>


<span id="wrtuKC6X"></span>
### **Procedure**


1. Log in to [the Bandwidth Package console](https://console.byteplus.com/eip/cbwps).

2. In the top menu bar, select region and project of your target resource.

3. Click **Change configuration** to the right of the target Bandwidth Package.

4. Select **Pay\-by\-bandwidth** or **Pay\-by\-main\-traffic** and adjust the maximum bandwidth.

5. Follow the console guidance to review and accept the legal terms, and click **Buy now** to complete the action.

## Convert Pay\-by\-main\-traffic/Pay\-by\-bandwidth to Pay\-by\-95th\-peak

<span id="gAMvK1Tu"></span>
### **Limits**


* The sum of both your [available balance](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/6269/100429) and the coupons applicable to the Bandwidth Package is not less than USD 100.00, and the available balance must be no less than 0. For specific procedure, see [Recharge guide](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/6269/100434).

* Pay\-by\-95th\-peak takes effect at 00:00:00 the next day after conversion. Before the metering method becomes effective, you can neither modify the specification nor delete the Bandwidth Package instance.


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Decreasing bandwidth cap may affect business traffic and lead to packet loss. Evaluate the possible impacts of the action on your business and proceed with caution.</div>


<span id="Jvg8Uzef"></span>
### **Procedure**


1. Log in to [the Bandwidth Package console](https://console.byteplus.com/eip/cbwps).

2. In the top menu bar, select region and project of your target resource.

3. Click **Change configuration** to the right of the target Bandwidth Package.

4. Click **Pay\-by\-95th\-peak** and adjust the bandwidth limit.

5. Follow the console guidance to review and accept Legal.

6. Click **Buy now** to complete the action.

## Convert between Pay\-by\-bandwidth and Subscription

<span id="FNm7O0Oc"></span>
### **Limits**


* You cannot adjust the maximum bandwidth during conversion.

* The start time of the billing cycle for Subscription is the time when the order of billing method change is created.

* When converting to Pay\-by\-bandwidth, the sum of both [available balance](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/6269/100429) and the coupons applicable for Bandwidth Package in your account is not less than USD 100.00, and the available balance must be no less than 0. For specific procedure, see [Recharge guide](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/6269/100434).

* The bandwidth of a Subscription Bandwidth Package ranges from 5 to 5000 Mbps.


<span id="l6ctDxGA"></span>
### **Procedure**


1. Log in to [the Bandwidth Package console](https://console.byteplus.com/eip/cbwps).

2. In the top menu bar, select region and project of your target resource.

3. Select conversion based on your business requirements:

   2. Select a specific duration for **Purchase time**. You can check **Automatic renewal** as needed.
   
   3. Follow the console guidance to review and accept the legal terms, and click **Buy now**. After successful payment, the metering method is converted.
