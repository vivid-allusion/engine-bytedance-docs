If you no longer need a Subscription Bandwidth Package, you can unsubscribe this Bandwidth Package to reduce costs. This topic describes how to unsubscribe a Subscription Bandwidth Package.

<span id=".6YCA6K6i6K-05piO"></span>
## Unsubscription description

Subscription Bandwidth Package supports two unsubscriptions: 7\-day unconditional refund and non\-7\-day refund.


|Unsubscription policy |7\-day unconditional refund |Non\-7\-day refund |
|---|---|---|
|Prerequisites |* Within seven days (≤ 7x24h) after successful payment of the Bandwidth Package order. No manual renewal or bandwidth modification was performed.<br><br>* An account defaults to support unsubscribing only one Bandwidth Package instance in each calendar year (January 1 to December 31). |* The Bandwidth Package resource is not suspended.<br><br>* Do not meet the conditions for 7\-day free cancellation. |
|Refund amount |The payments will be fully refunded to your available balance. The coupons used are non\-refundable. |For details, see [Non-seven-day unconditional unsubscribe rules](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/6269/81013#.5LqM44CB6Z2e5LiD5aSp5peg55CG55Sx6YCA6K6i6K-05piO). |


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">For Bandwidth Packages purchased during promotions, the promotion terms and conditions take precedence over the unsubscription rules in case of any conflict.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">If suspicious or malicious unsubscription occurs, BytePlus reserves the right to deny your request.</div>

## Procedure


1. Log in to [the Bandwidth Package console](https://console.byteplus.com/eip/cbwps).

2. Click **Billing \> Cancellation** at the right side of top menu bar.

3. On the Cancellation page, select **Unsubscription (7\-day no reason)**  or **Regular unsubscription**.

   * On the **Unsubscription (7\-day no reason)**  tab, click **Unsubscription** to the right of the target Bandwidth Package.

   * On the **Regular unsubscription** tab, click **Unsubscription** to the right of the target Bandwidth Package.

4. Confirm the information of the Bandwidth Package to be unsubscribed.

5. Click **Submit an order**. A dialog box appears to confirm the cancellation.

6. Click **OK** to complete the action.
