This topic describes Subscription details for Bandwidth Package.

<span id=".6LSt5Lmw5YmN5o-Q"></span>
## Prerequisites

You have completed enterprise real\-name authentication for your account.

<span id=".6K6h6LS55YWs5byP"></span>
## Formula

**Bandwidth Package fee = Bandwidth unit price (USD/month per Mbps) × Maximum bandwidth (Mbps) × Duration (months).** 


* **Maximum bandwidth**: 5\-5000 Mbps.

* **Duration:**  The selected purchase time.


<span id=".6K6h6LS55ZGo5pyf"></span>
## Billing cycle

Non\-calendar month.

For example, Mr. X created a Subscription Bandwidth Package with a purchase time of 2 months at 14:12:32 on July 10, 2023. The Subscription Bandwidth Package would expire at 23:59:59 on September 10, 2023.

## Fee example

Mr. A created a Subscription Bandwidth Package with a 2\-month duration, 15 Mbps bandwidth, and BGP (multi\-ISP) on July 10, 2023.

When creating the Bandwidth Package, Mr. A was required to pay a total fee of: 100.00 (USD/month per Mbps) × 15 (Mbps) × 2 (months) = 3,000 (USD). This Bandwidth Package would expire at 23:59:59 on September 10, 2023.
