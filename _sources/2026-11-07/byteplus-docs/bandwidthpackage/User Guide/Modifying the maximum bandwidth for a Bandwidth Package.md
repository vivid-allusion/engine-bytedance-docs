You can adjust the maximum bandwidth of a Bandwidth Package instance anytime as your business scales. Adjustment takes effect immediately.

<span id=".57qm5p2f6ZmQ5Yi2"></span>
## Limits

You can increase or decrease the maximum bandwidth for Pay\-as\-you\-go Bandwidth Package instances, and only increase the maximum bandwidth for Subscription Bandwidth Package instances.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true">Tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Decreasing bandwidth cap may affect business traffic and lead to packet loss. Evaluate the possible impacts of the action on your business and proceed with caution.</div>


<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Procedure


1. log in to [the Bandwidth Package console](https://console.byteplus.com/eip/cbwps).

2. In the top menu bar, select the region and project of your target Bandwidth Package.

3. In the Shared Bandwidth Package list, click **Change configuration** to the right of the target Bandwidth Package.

   
   <Tabs>
   <Tab zoneid="bvCTSikJlH" title="Pay-as-you-go">
   <TabTitle>Pay-as-you-go</TabTitle>
   
   1. Configure the new maximum bandwidth, and follow the console guidance to review and accept the legal terms.
   
   2. Click **Buy now** to complete the action.
   
   
   </Tab>
   <Tab zoneid="bE6YNfbUWm" title="Subscription">
   <TabTitle>Subscription</TabTitle>
   
   1. Configure the new maximum bandwidth, and follow the console guidance to review and accept the Legal.
   
   2. Click **Buy now**.
   
   3. Click **Next**, select a payment method, and then click **Pay** to complete the payment by the selected payment method.
   
   
   </Tab>
   </Tabs>
   


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">The new bandwidth ​​takes effect immediately​​. Fees are billed based on the new configuration.</div>
