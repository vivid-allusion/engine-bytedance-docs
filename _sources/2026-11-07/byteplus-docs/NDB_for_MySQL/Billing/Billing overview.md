This topic describes the supported billing methods, instance lifecycles, and prices for NDB for MySQL.

<span id=".6K6h6LS557G75Z6L"></span>
## Billing method


|Billing method |Description |Applicable scenarios |
|---|---|---|
|Pay\-as\-you\-go |Also known as post\-paid, where fees are generated per second based on actual usage and deducted hourly from your account. For more details, refer to [Pay-as-you-go](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/NDB_for_MySQL/Pay-as-you-go). |Suitable for short\-term needs with low business volume. Instances can be released immediately after use for flexibility and convenience. |
|Yearly or monthly subscription |Also known as prepaid, where you pay in advance for the purchased duration when creating a new instance. For more details, refer to [Yearly or monthly subscription](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/NDB_for_MySQL/Yearly_or_monthly_subscription). |Suitable for business scenarios with stable peak traffic and long\-term usage requirements, as it is more affordable than Pay\-as\-you\-go. |


<span id=".5a6e5L6L55Sf5ZG95ZGo5pyf"></span>
## Instance lifecycle

The lifecycle of an instance is calculated differently based on its billing method.


|Billing method |Instance lifecycle |
|---|---|
|Pay\-as\-you\-go |Billing starts after you successfully create the instance and stops when you release it. |
|Yearly or monthly subscription |Billing starts after successful creation, and the instance is automatically released upon expiration.<br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div><br><br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">After a veDB MySQL Subscription instance expires, it is not released immediately. Instead, a specific retention policy applies. For details, refer to <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/NDB_for_MySQL/Arrears_and_expiration">Expiration policy</a>.</div><br> |
