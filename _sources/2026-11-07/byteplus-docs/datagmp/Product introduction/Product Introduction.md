## Product Introduction

Growth Marketing, abbreviated as GMP (Growth Marketing Platform), is a data\-driven intelligent marketing platform for enterprise operations and marketing departments, based on user data and intelligent strategies.

Through various channels and growth strategy capabilities provided by the platform, enterprises can adopt the most suitable operational actions for different segments based on business needs, flexibly create and manage operational activities, and achieve a complete business process loop from activity creation, strategy design, approval, execution to feedback.

The main features of the platform include:


* Channels integration

* Content management

* Segmentation

* Algorithm recommendation

* Marketing automation

* Marketing strategy templates

* Campaign performance analytics


Based on these capabilities, operators can self\-configure campaigns, achieve refined operations, improve key business metrics and operational efficiency, and assist in the digital transformation of the enterprise.
