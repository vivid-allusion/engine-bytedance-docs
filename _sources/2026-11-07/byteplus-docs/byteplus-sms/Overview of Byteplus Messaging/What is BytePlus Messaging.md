BytePlus Messaging provides OpenAPIs that allow you to send messages from your system to users around the world and also supports receiving user replies, enabling seamless conversations with customers through a single interface. For detailed pricing, please refer to the [Pricing Table](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-sms/docs-sms-pricing-4042bb23).

Currently, BytePlus Messaging APIs supports the following communication channels:


* **SMS**: Quickly send important notifications or marketing content to users via text messages.

* **WhatsApp**: Engage in real\-time interactions through the world’s leading messaging platform.


We are actively expanding to integrate more channels for cross\-scenario and regional needs.

### Product advantages


1. **Multi\-Channel Communication**


Reach users across diverse channels without switching between platforms.


2. **Multimedia Messaging Capability**


In addition to text messages, send dynamic multimedia content—including images, audio, and videos—to captivate your audience and boost engagement.


3. **Interactive Chat Experience**


Real\-time response capabilities allow you to receive user replies promptly, providing a personalized and responsive customer experience.


4. **Quick Integration**


With a straightforward and developer\-friendly interface design, integration can be completed quickly without complex configurations.
