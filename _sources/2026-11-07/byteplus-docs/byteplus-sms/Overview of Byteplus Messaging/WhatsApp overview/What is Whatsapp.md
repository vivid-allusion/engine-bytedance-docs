WhatsApp is a cross\-platform instant messaging service available for mobile communication devices and PCs. As of 2022, WhatsApp is the world's most popular mobile communication application and the third most popular social network globally, following Facebook and YouTube.

Currently, WhatsApp has a global user base of 2 billion, spanning over 180 countries and regions. Every day, more than 65 billion messages are sent via WhatsApp, with an average message read rate exceeding 75%.

For details, see [WhatsApp official website](https://www.whatsapp.com/).

### WhatsApp template types

WhatsApp supports various types of templates designed for specific purposes:


|**Template Type** |**Description** |**Use Cases** |
|---|---|---|
|Authentication |Authentication templates are exclusively used for sending one\-time passwords (OTPs). Marketing and transaction\-related templates cannot be used for this purpose. |One\-time authentication passwords |
|Marketing |Marketing templates are the most flexible and can help achieve a wide range of goals, including increasing brand awareness and boosting sales. |Promotions, marketing campaigns, recalls, feature announcements, etc. |
|Transaction |Transaction templates are typically triggered by user actions or requests. These templates must include specific information related to the activity, ongoing transaction, account, subscription, or interaction. For example, an order confirmation must include the order number. |Surveys, account updates, meal booking management, etc. |

### Sending limit

WhatsApp enforces a tiered structure to manage daily message\-sending volumes. Tiers determine the maximum number of messages that can be sent within a 24\-hour window.


|**Tier** |**Sending limit** (business\-initiated conversations/24h) |
|---|---|
|Tier 1 |250 |
|Tier 2 |1,000 |
|Tier 3 |10,000 |
|Tier 4 |100,000 |
|Tier 5 |Unlimited |



* When daily message sends reach 50% of the current tier’s limit, the account automatically qualifies for the next tier on the following day. The highest tier “Unlimited” is the maximum achievable.

* New Official Business Accounts (OBAs) start at Tier 1 (250 messages/day). After verification, the limit upgrades to 1,000 messages/day. Well\-established, verified OBAs may apply for higher daily limits.
