AI Live from BytePlus Live helps you create unattended livestreams hosted by AI avatars. You can clone a real person's appearance and voice, generate livestream content for different business scenarios, and let the AI host answer audience questions in real time.

This overview introduces AI Live's key features and use cases, and points you to the guides for each stage, from preparing assets to going live.

## Key features

* **Self\-trained, personalized AI hosts**: Train digital avatars and voices directly in the console using your own video, creating customized hosts that represent your brand, instructor, or business role.

* **Script\-driven control**: Edit and manage livestream scripts, or use AI script generation to create scripts that control the host's broadcast content, pacing, and flow for a stable, professional livestream.

* **Intelligent interaction to boost engagement**: The host replies to audience comments in real time. You can customize knowledge bases, reply strategies, interactive responses, and filter rules to keep interactions relevant to your business.

* **Knowledge\-based content and Q&A**: Prepare knowledge bases for products, courses, brands, or events so the AI host can deliver accurate content and answer audience questions with relevant information.

* **Multi\-platform streaming and brand customization**: Configure stream forwarding, avatars, voices, watermarks, and other parameters to create a livestream room that matches your brand style, and stream to third\-party platforms.

## Use cases

AI Live applies to the following use cases:

* **E\-commerce livestreaming**: Sync products from TikTok Shop, Shopify, or your own product library, use product knowledge bases to generate product explanations and real\-time answers, and stream to platforms such as TikTok, Shopee, and Lazada to extend livestream duration and reach more shoppers.

* **Education livestreaming**: Clone an instructor's appearance and voice, generate livestream courses that can answer learner questions in real time, and run sessions 24/7. This is suitable for course delivery, enrollment consultation, and knowledge Q&A.

* **Extending livestream duration and scaling accounts**: When human hosts are limited but you want longer livestream hours or more livestream accounts, AI avatars can keep streaming without being constrained by human availability.

* **Multilingual expansion**: If you already have livestreaming experience in one region but lack hosts for other languages, AI avatars can be trained with multilingual assets and deliver livestream content in foreign languages. Specifically, AI Live supports 41 languages: Chinese, Cantonese, English, Arabic, Russian, Spanish, French, Portuguese, German, Turkish, Dutch, Ukrainian, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Japanese, Italian, Korean, Thai, Polish, Romanian, Greek, Czech, Finnish, Hindi, Bulgarian, Dari, Danish, Hebrew, Malay, Persian, Slovak, Swedish, Croatian, Filipino, Hungarian, Norwegian, Slovenian, Catalan, Norwegian Nynorsk, Tamil, and Afrikaans.

## AI Live setup workflow

Use the following workflow to prepare reusable assets, configure a live room, and choose how to go live. After the first setup, you can reuse trained avatars, voices, products, knowledge bases, and room configurations across future livestreams.

1. **Subscribe to AI Live service**: Refer to [Subscription billing method](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/subscription-billing-method) and contact sales to subscribe to AI Live service.

2. **Prepare and train your host**: Record the video and audio used to clone the host's appearance, movements, and voice, then upload them to create your avatar and voice. See [Preparing avatar training assets](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/preparing-audio-and-video-for-digital-human-livestreaming) and [Training avatar and voice](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/training_digital_human_and_voice).

3. **Prepare products, knowledge, and visual materials**: Build your **Product Library**, sync or add products, upload product or general knowledge base files, and prepare the images or videos used to decorate the live room. See [Managing product library](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/managing-products-for-digital-human-livestreams), [Configuring knowledge bases](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/configuring-product-kb), and [Managing live room visual materials](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/manage-live-room-visual-materials).

4. **Prepare livestream content**: Generate text scripts and product scripts, then review the script length, structure, and pronunciation before using them in a live room. See [Generating livestream scripts](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/generating-ai-live-scripts).

5. **Create and configure a live room**: Create a live room, select the avatar and voice, add scripts, configure interaction rules, and decorate the live room with images or videos. See [Starting an AI Live](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/digital-human-livestream), [Configuring AI Live interactions](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/configuring-ai-live-interactions), and [Decorating a live room](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/decorating-a-live-room).

6. **Choose a streaming method and go live**: Select the target platform or streaming workflow, complete the required stream settings, verify the output, and start the livestream. See [Going live on TikTok via RTMP](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/Synchronizing_a_live_stream_from_AI_Live_to_third-party_platforms), [Going live on TikTok via OBS virtual camera](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/Going_live_on_TikTok_via_Digital_Human_and_OBS), and [Going live on Shopee via RTMP](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/going-live-on-shopee-via-rtmp).
