To create a high\-quality digital avatar for AI Live, you must prepare specific video and audio assets. This guide provides the complete technical requirements and production best practices to ensure your files are optimized for our AI training models, resulting in a realistic and engaging digital avatar.

> **说明**

> Before you begin recording, we recommend contacting our sales team for a sample video.

## Preparing the avatar video

Your video is used for replicating the host's appearance, movements, voice, and facial expressions, including how their mouth moves when they speak. This ensures that the AI can accurately represent the brand's characteristics during the livestream, align the content with brand positioning, and enhance audience recognition.

### Notes

Your video directly impacts how natural the AI looks and moves. Footage with static or repetitive actions may be rejected. To create a dynamic and compliant avatar, design your movements around these two core principles:

> **注意**

* > **Use Versatile, Reusable Movements**: The system continuously loops the body movements from your video. Therefore, gestures must be generic enough to look natural when paired with any product script.

   * > **Do:** Use simple, versatile actions like gesturing with an open palm, or making small, illustrative hand movements.

   * > **Don't:** Perform complex, product\-specific actions like assembling, disassembling, or pointing to a specific feature.

* > **Add Variety to Avoid Penalties**: To avoid being flagged by platforms for "repetitive content," you must introduce a variety of movements.

   * > **Do:** Incorporate a range of different hand positions and natural, subtle shifts in posture throughout the video.

   * > **Don't:** Hold the same pose or repeat a single gesture for an extended period.

### Shooting options

You have three options for producing the source video. The recommended method is shooting in a real environment.

|**Shooting method** |**Description** |**Advantages** |**Disadvantages** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **(Recommended) Real Background** |The host records in the actual livestreaming environment, with all background and foreground elements in place. |Easiest to produce and delivers the most natural\-looking results. |The background is fixed and cannot be changed later without reshooting. |
| **(Optional) Green Screen** |The host records in front of a green screen. The background is then digitally added during the livestream. |A single avatar can be reused with various backgrounds. |Higher production cost and complexity. Requires careful lighting to avoid artifacts. |
| **(Optional) AI video based on a real person** |If filming a real person is not feasible, you can use AI tools to generate an AI video using high\-quality photos of a real person and product images. |Does not require the host to be physically present for a video shoot. |The final quality is generally not as high as a live\-action recording. |

### Specifications

For a quick reference, here are the final specifications for your video.

|**Parameter** |**Requirement** |
|---|---|
|Duration |Recommended: 3–5 minutes |
|Format |.mp4 |
|Resolution |1080p (1080×1920). Submissions that do not meet this resolution will be rejected. |
|File Size |Max 2 GB |

|Bit Rate |≥ 20 Mbps |

### Best practices

Follow these guidelines to ensure your video footage meets the quality standards for avatar training.

#### **Performance & delivery**

> **注意**

> Determine the shooting method based on the number of products to be featured:

* > **Single product**: Record a video of the host interacting with the product. (Simple interactions, for example, picking up the product, are fine. Avoid complex movements.)

* > **Multiple products**: Record a video of the host speaking, without interacting with any products. Hand gestures are allowed. (Do not pick up products to demonstrate them.)

|**Recommended (Do)**  |**To Avoid (Don't)**  |
|---|---|
|* Rehearse the script to ensure a smooth delivery.<br><br>* Maintain eye contact with the camera.<br><br>* Use a conversational, authentic tone.<br><br>* Use natural actions (e.g., touching the product, briefly checking your phone as if reading comments) |* **Do not block your mouth or face.**  This is critical for AI to learn lip movements.<br><br>* Avoid turning your head too far (excessive side profile).<br><br>* Avoid specific and instructional gestures like pointing, counting on fingers, or giving directions.<br><br>* Avoid reading from a script in a stiff, monotonous way. |

#### **Lighting**

|**Recommended (Do)**  |**To Avoid (Don't)**  |
|---|---|
|• Use a 2 or 3\-point soft lighting setup for even, consistent light.<br><br>• Ensure the host's face is clearly lit from the front. |• Avoid low\-light or dark environments.<br><br>• **Avoid strong backlighting** (e.g., a bright window behind the host).<br><br>• Avoid flickering or changing lights. |

#### **Background & environment**

|**Recommended (Do)**  |**To Avoid (Don't)**  |
|---|---|
|• Use your actual, clean livestream set as the background.<br><br>• If using a green screen, ensure it's evenly lit and free of wrinkles. |• Avoid cluttered, messy, or distracting backgrounds.<br><br>• Avoid backgrounds with strong reflections or moving elements. |

## Preparing the voice audio (Optional)

By default, the voice is cloned from the audio in your avatar video. However, for a higher\-fidelity voice clone, you can provide a separate, clean audio recording. This is highly recommended if the video's audio has background noise.

### Specifications

|**Parameter** |**Requirement** |
|---|---|
|Duration |10–15 seconds |
|Format |WAV or MP3 |
|File Size |Max 10 MB |

### Best practices

|**Category** |**Recommended (Do)**  |**To Avoid (Don't)**  |
|---|---|---|
|**Recording Environment** |• Record in a quiet, indoor space (like a small office or closet).<br><br>• Use headphones to monitor the audio as you record. |• **Avoid background noise.**  Turn off air conditioners, fans, or other sources of noise.<br><br>• **Avoid reverb.**  Avoid large, empty rooms that create echo. |
|**Equipment & Technique** |• Use a dedicated external microphone for the best results.<br><br>• If using a smartphone, place it 10–20 cm (4–8 inches) from your mouth.<br><br>• Maintain a consistent distance from the microphone. |• Do not place the microphone too close (causes popping) or too far (captures room noise). |
|**Speaking Style & Delivery** |• Speak at a natural, moderate pace.<br><br>• Use an expressive and engaging tone that matches your typical livestream style.<br><br>• Ensure your pronunciation is clear and accurate. |• Avoid a flat, monotonous, or robotic delivery.<br><br>• Avoid speaking too quickly or too slowly.<br><br>• Avoid mispronouncing key terms or brand names. |

## Final submission checklist

Before submitting your files, perform this final check.

### Video Checklist

* **Duration:**  3–5 minutes.

* **Format:**  `.mp4`.

* **Resolution:**  1080p.

* **File Size:**  Under 2 GB.

* **Frame Rate:**  At least 25 FPS.

* Host's face and mouth are never blocked.

* Lighting is bright and even; no strong shadows or backlighting.

* Background is clean and not distracting.

* Host's performance feels natural and conversational.

* Host uses versatile and varied natural movements, and avoids repetitive poses or complex product\-specific actions.

### Audio Checklist

* **Duration**: 10–15 seconds.

* **File Size**: Under 10 MB.

* **Format**: WAV or MP3

* No background noise.

* No echo or reverb.

* Only one person is speaking.

* Speaking pace and tone are natural and engaging.

* Pronunciation is clear and correct.
