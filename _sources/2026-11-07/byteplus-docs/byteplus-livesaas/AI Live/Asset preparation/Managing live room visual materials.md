The material library centrally manages the image and video materials required for AI Live room decoration. You can upload background images, foreground images, campaign stickers, price stickers, cutaway videos, and overlay videos to the material library, and then select these materials when decorating a live room.

Materials uploaded to the material library are not automatically added to a live room. To use a material in a live room, select it on the **Decoration** page of the live room. For details, see [Decorating a live room](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/decorating-a-live-room).

## Prerequisites

* You have activated AI Live. To activate the service, contact sales as described in [Subscription billing method](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/subscription-billing-method).

* You have an account that can access the BytePlus Live console.

* You have prepared image or video files that meet the material requirements.

## Data isolation

* **Root account**: Can view and manage all materials uploaded by sub\-accounts under the root account.

* **Sub\-account**: Can view and manage only the materials uploaded by the current sub\-account.

## Steps

### Open the material library

1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/).

2. In the top navigation bar, select **AI Live**.

3. In the left navigation pane, click **Material library**.

   ![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/c303a0127a814d34a0389a19ab406d57~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image)

### Upload materials

1. On the material library page, click **Upload material**.

   ![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/0dc8e0517da9487f9e54293a6b03bb82~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image)

2. In the **Upload material** dialog box, select a material type:

   * **Cutaway Video**: Close\-up product clips inserted in the middle of the live stream, such as product showcases or transition scenes. Only the video plays — the original audio is muted. The clip plays once, then disappears and the AI host resumes broadcasting.

   * **Overlay Video**: A video overlay superimposed on the live screen. The original audio is muted, and the clip loops within the scene.

   * **Image**: Image assets used as overlays on the live screen, including backgrounds, foregrounds, campaign stickers, and price tags.

3. Click the upload area and select a local file. Make sure the file meets the format, resolution, and size requirements of the selected material type.

4. After the upload is complete, click **Confirm**.

   ![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/60d65aa75569494c813b09dea63181cc~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image)

### Material types and requirements

|Type |Purpose |Supported formats |Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
|Image sticker |Used as image stickers in the live room, such as backgrounds, foregrounds, campaign stickers, and price stickers. |JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF |Images must be no larger than 10 MB. GIFs must be no larger than 35 MB. |
|Cutaway video |Used as a scene\-switching video during live explanations, such as product close\-ups or transition clips. |MP4 |1080p (1920 × 1080 or 1080 × 1920). File size must be no larger than 700 MB. |
|Overlay video |Used as a video overlay on the live screen. |MP4, MOV |1080p (1920 × 1080 or 1080 × 1920). File size must be no larger than 200 MB. |

### Manage materials

On the material library page, you can view the thumbnail, type, file name, and upload time of each uploaded material, and perform the following operations:

* **View images**: For image stickers, click **View** to preview the image.

   ![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/81124128379f4626b04957c1304a2a85~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image)

* **Play videos**: For cutaway videos and overlay videos, click **Play** to preview the video.

   ![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/826a32521d284d9f92c1e971485d46ab~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image)

* **Delete materials**: Click the delete icon to delete a material that is no longer needed.

   ![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/1f89b8f6df2b43f4ba3bc734973d9a1b~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image)

* **Filter and search for materials**: Find materials by type, format, material name, or material ID.

   ![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/537bfcb25f084410b7a29b5cbcbb8181~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image)
