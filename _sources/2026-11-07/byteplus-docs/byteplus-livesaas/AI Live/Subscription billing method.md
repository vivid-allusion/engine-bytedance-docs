The subscription billing method for BytePlus AI Live allows you to subscribe to feature versions, AI Live figures, and streaming resources as needed. This guide introduces the available subscription options, including feature versions, AI Live figure cloning, streaming time, and streaming concurrency billing items.

<span id="e375bc8b"></span>
## Prerequisites


* Sign up for a BytePlus account and add your billing information. For more information, see [Sign up and verify BytePlus account](https://docs.byteplus.com/byteplus-platform/docs/signing-up-your-account).

* You are in a country or region listed [here](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/byteplus-platform/docs/tax-rate-reference). Otherwise, contact your sales representative for help.

## Introduction to feature versions and resource packages

The formal billing of BytePlus AI Live consists of three parts: **feature version**, **AI Live figure cloning**, and **streaming billing**.

<span id="6eb2d604"></span>
### Feature version

The feature version is **mandatory** and determines whether you can use the AI Live tools. Once the version expires, you can no longer use the service.

<span id="20878225"></span>
### AI Live figure cloning

AI Live figure cloning is a prepaid item. Figure cloning refers to performing AI figure cloning on the video you submit. The fee is deducted once per 5 minutes, rounded up if it exceeds 5 minutes. The source video for a single figure supports up to 40 minutes, so make sure you have purchased sufficient resources before cloning.

You can also purchase the **public figure library monthly package** of BytePlus AI Live: within a one\-month validity period, you can use one public library AI Live figure. Auto\-renewal is supported. You need to contact Live technical support to obtain the ID of the public library figure.

<span id="7989389f"></span>
### Streaming billing

Streaming billing offers two modes. **A single account can only choose one of them, and they cannot coexist.**  When both types of resources exist in an account, **the unlimited streaming monthly package takes priority over the streaming time package.** 

<span id="247d5285"></span>
#### Mode 1: Streaming time package

**Streaming time resource package**: a prepaid monthly package. The usage is allocated separately each month, cleared upon expiration, and supports auto\-renewal. An account can purchase multiple streaming time resource packages, and the number of purchased packages represents the number of available **streaming concurrency** channels.

AI Live offers multiple duration packages for you to choose from. If you exceed the streaming time of the resource package, additional fees are charged on a pay\-as\-you\-go basis.

<span id="473ba2d9"></span>
#### Mode 2: Unlimited streaming monthly package

**Unlimited streaming monthly package**: prepaid and billed by "month", with auto\-renewal supported. An account can purchase multiple unlimited streaming monthly packages, and the number of purchased packages represents the number of available **streaming concurrency** channels. After subscription, you can stream unlimitedly 24 hours a day within the concurrency limit during the current month, and the price only varies with the number of concurrency channels.

<span id="41a02fec"></span>
## Notes


* The subscription takes effect immediately.

* If you purchase the same resource package more than once, the available usage accumulates within the validity period. Packages of the same type are used up in the order you purchased them. Each package has its own validity period. Therefore, even if you purchase multiple packages of the same type, their validity periods will not extend.

* For AI Live, no refunds or cancellations are available for any purchased services.

* If your feature version subscription expires, the service will become unavailable. Please renew it in time.

* The streaming\-time mode and the concurrency mode cannot coexist. When an available unlimited streaming monthly package exists in the account, billing follows the unlimited streaming monthly package first and stops pushing usage to the time package.

## Overdue payment


* **When overdue payment occurs**: After your streaming time resource package is used up, any further AI Live usage is charged at the pay\-as\-you\-go rate. If your account balance is insufficient to pay the pay\-as\-you\-go bill, your account enters the overdue payment status. You need to pay the overdue amount in time.

* **Billing delay and overdue payment**: AI Live uses **daily billing**, so fees are **not deducted immediately** after they are incurred. The platform generates the bill and deducts the fees **at around 08:00 on the next day**. After the deduction is completed, the platform determines whether your account enters the overdue payment status based on your account balance. Before that, you can still use AI Live and may continue to incur fees. For example, after your streaming time resource package is used up on January 10, you continue using AI Live and incur pay\-as\-you\-go usage. On January 11 at around 08:00, the platform generates the bill and deducts the fees for the pay\-as\-you\-go usage incurred on January 10. If your account balance is insufficient at that time, your account enters the overdue payment status. You cannot continue using AI Live until you pay the overdue amount.

* **Impact**: After your account enters the overdue payment status, AI Live is suspended immediately. After suspension, you cannot use AI Live\-related services until all overdue amounts are paid. To avoid business interruption, top up your account and settle overdue bills in time.

* **Prevention**: To avoid service interruption caused by insufficient balance, go to the [Billing center](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/bill/detail) to check your payable amounts in time and configure **bill consumption warning**.
