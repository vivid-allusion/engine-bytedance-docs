This setting enables you to set a restriction on your viewer's access. Only those who have the address to your live stream and log in with their phone numbers can join your live stream. You can also enable a whitelist mode to limit access.


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. Open your live stream on the live stream list.

3. Click **Producer** \> **Viewing Restrictions**.

4. Select **Phone number**.

5. (Optionally) You can enable **Whitelist mode** so that only the viewers on your whitelist can access your live stream.

   * Click **Add** to add the whitelist viewers manually.

   * Click **Batch import from Excel** to upload multiple whitelist viewers.

6. Confirm and click **Save**.
