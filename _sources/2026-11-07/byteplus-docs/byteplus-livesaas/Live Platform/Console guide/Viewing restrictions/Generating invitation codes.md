You can use invitation codes to limit access to your live stream. Only viewers with invitation codes can watch your live stream. Each invitation code can be used by one viewer only.


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. Open your live stream on the live stream list.

3. Click **Producer** \> **Viewing Restrictions**.

4. Select **Requires invitation code** and configure the settings:

   * **Hint text**: The prompt on the form where viewers enter the invitation code.

   * **Invitation code**: The number of viewers you want to invite.

5. Click **Generate** to generate the invitation codes.

6. Click **Download invitation code** to download the codes. You can then pass the codes to the viewers you want to invite.
