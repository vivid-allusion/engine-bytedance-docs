You can set a password for your live stream. By doing so, only invited viewers can join your live stream.


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. Open your live stream on the live stream list.

3. Click **Producer** \> **Viewing Restrictions**.

4. Select **Requires password**, then enter a prompt in the **Hint text** field and a password in the **Requires password** field respectively.

   Your viewers will be asked to enter the password when they open the viewing page.
