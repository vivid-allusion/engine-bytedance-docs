You can check your viewers' regional distribution from the Statistics section of a live stream. You can also download the data in excel format.


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. Open your live stream on the live stream list.

3. Click **Insight** \> **Basic** \> **Regional distribution**.

4. (Optional) Click **Export data** to download the data in excel format.
