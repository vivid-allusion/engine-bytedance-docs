You can add a splash ad for your live stream. When the mobile viewers open the viewing page, the splash ad appears on their screen for five seconds as a picture. It can engage your viewers and make a good first impression.


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. Open your live stream on the live stream list.

3. Click **Marketing toolkit** \> **Marketing Promotion** \> **Guided page**, and turn on the **Splash page settings** switch.

4. Upload an image from your local directory and click **Save**.
