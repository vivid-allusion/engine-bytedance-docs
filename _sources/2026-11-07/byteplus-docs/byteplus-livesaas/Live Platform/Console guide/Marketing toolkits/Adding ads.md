BytePlus Live provides multiple floating ads options, such as header ad, and more. They float or are embedded on the viewing page. You can set graphics, texts, and links for them. Floating ads can be a good idea:


* When you need to get viewers' attention without distracting them away from the live stream.

* When you have content that is related to the live stream but not necessarily to show on the live video.


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">To ensure your target page can be displayed, we recommend you to enter the embedded link which starts with https://.</div>


<span id="Adding ads"></span>
## Adding ads


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. Open your live stream on the live stream list.

3. Click **Marketing toolkit** \> **Marketing Promotion**.


<span id="Adding header ad"></span>
### Adding header ad


1. On the **Header ads** tab, turn on the **Ad Settings** switch.

2. (Optional) Enter the link starting with https:// and select whether to display the content in a floating layer on the viewing page after viewers click the ad.

3. Click **Save**.

4. On the **Producer** \> **Appearance** page, upload a header image for PCs and mobile devices.

5. Click **Save**.

<span id="Adding in-page ad"></span>
### Adding in\-page ad


1. On the **In\-page ads** tab, turn on the **Ad Settings** switch.

2. Configure the in\-page ads and select whether to display the content in a floating layer on the viewing page after viewers click the ad.

3. Click **Save**.

<span id="Adding floating ad"></span>
### Adding floating ad


1. On the **Floating ad** tab, turn on the **Ad Settings** switch.

2. Set your floating ad.

3. Select whether to allow viewers to close the floating ad and whether to display the content in a floating layer on the viewing page after viewers click the ad.

4. Click **Save**.
