This topic introduces two ways to promote your live stream and grow your viewers:


* Allow viewers to share the link of your live stream through Facebook, WhatsApp, and other social media platforms.

* Configure a poster for your live stream and allow viewers to generate, save and share the poster.


<span id="Allowing link sharing"></span>
## Allowing link sharing

To allow viewers to share the link of your live stream through social media platforms:


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. Open your live stream on the live stream list.

3. Go to **Marketing toolkit** \> **Share Poster** and enable **Live Share**.

4. Click **Save**.

<span id="Configuring a poster"></span>
## Configuring a poster


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. Open your live stream on the live stream list.

3. Go to **Marketing toolkit** \> **Share Poster** and enable **Invitation Poster**.

4. Configure the title, cover, and other information for the poster.
