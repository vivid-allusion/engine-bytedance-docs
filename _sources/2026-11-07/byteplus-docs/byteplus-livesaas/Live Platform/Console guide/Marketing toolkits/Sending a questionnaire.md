You can manually send a questionnaire in real time during a live stream, or one can be automatically sent to viewers when they enter the live room or when the live stream ends.

<span id="9b312f6a"></span>
## Scenarios

You can configure questionnaires with one of the following send times as needed. Questionnaires whose send time is **When viewers enter the live room** or **When the live stream ends** are sent automatically.


* **When viewers enter the live room**: Suitable for scenarios such as check\-in and registration. Viewers must submit the questionnaire before watching a live stream. For more information, see [Sending a questionnaire automatically](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/11/5438#964c76bd).

* **When the live stream ends**: Suitable for scenarios such as collecting viewers' feedback and opinions on a live stream. Viewers can select whether to submit the questionnaire. For more information, see [Sending a questionnaire automatically](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/11/5438#964c76bd).

* **Send manually**: Suitable for scenarios where you interact with viewers in real time during a live stream. For more information, see [Sending a questionnaire manually](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/11/5438#01784943).


<span id="38aa96f9"></span>
## Prerequisites


* Make sure the menu for interaction tools is added. For more information, see [Configuring the menu for interaction tools](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/docs-managing-the-interaction-area#f1b04790).

* Send a questionnaire manually: A BytePlus account or an IAM user with Live control \> Edit permission.

* Send a questionnaire automatically: A BytePlus account or an IAM user with Marketing toolkits \> Edit permission.

## Sending a questionnaire manually


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. On the live room list, click to open a live room.

3. On the **Live control** page, click **Questionnaire** under the menu for interaction tools.

   <div style="text-align: center">
   <img src="https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/f289ccd4770c48ef8c72b0a70b6001a0~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image" width="1831px" />   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">For a better viewing experience, we recommend that you turn on the <strong>Display menu</strong> switch in the lower\-right corner to display the menu on the viewing page.   </div>
   

4. Click **Create questionnaire** in the dialog box that appears.

5. In the **Questionnaire** dialog box that appears, complete the following configurations and click **Save**.

   If this is not the first questionnaire in the live room, click **Create** and then complete the following configurations.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/803a437aa48742cebaf7f092c30beb87~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

   * Enter the title and description of the questionnaire.

   * Add at least one question.

6. Click **Add to send area** and then click **Send** under the menu for interaction tools to send the questionnaire to the viewing page.

   If you have multiple unsent questionnaires, select the questionnaire on the left first and then click **Add to send area**.

   <div style="text-align: center">
   <img src="https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/a718f757e76a4a18a393ded6582a9325~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image" width="1193px" />   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">You can also click <strong>Questionnaire</strong> under the menu for interaction tools and select the questionnaire to be sent in the dialog box that appears. Click <strong>Send</strong> to send the questionnaire to the viewing page.   </div>
   

7. (Optional) You can copy, preview, edit, or delete unsent questionnaires, as well as copy, preview, share, or delete sent questionnaires.

8. (Optional) Under the menu for interaction tools, you can perform the following operations.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/60f18e86705d497da9e728c27cb3a2f5~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

   * Click the **Withdraw** icon to withdraw a sent questionnaire.

   * Click the **Preview** icon to preview the questionnaire.

   * Click the **Share** icon to share the questionnaire by copying or downloading the QR code.


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">You can also view the questionnaire data on the <strong>Insights</strong> \> <strong>Questionnaire data</strong> page. For more information, see <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-livesaas/docs-data-statistics">Data statistics</a>.</div>

## Sending a questionnaire automatically


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. On the live room list, click to open a live room.

3. On the left navigation pane, click **Marketing toolkits** \> **Questionnaires**.

4. Click **Create questionnaire**.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/5fa4935c45794a8d94bf43217c94e3fe~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

5. Complete the following configurations and click **OK**.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/9a44b4a9babf4986859f5785c5f15004~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

   * Select the send time of the questionnaire as needed.

      <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="2">tip      </div>
      

      <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="2">You can also configure a questionnaire whose send time is <strong>Send manually</strong> on the <strong>Live control</strong> page. For more information, see <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/11/5438#01784943">Sending a questionnaire manually</a>.      </div>
      

   * Enter the title and description of the questionnaire.

   * Add at least one question.

6. Click **Publish** in the **Actions** column to send the questionnaire to the viewing page. Then, the existing questionnaire with the same send time will be overwritten with this newly published one.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/29c5161353d7420d9efd25d3ccf08088~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

7. (Optional) You can copy, publish, preview, edit, or delete unsent questionnaires, as well as copy, withdraw, or preview sent questionnaires.


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">You can also view the questionnaire data on the <strong>Insights</strong> \> <strong>Questionnaire data</strong> page. For more information, see <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-livesaas/docs-data-statistics">Data statistics</a>.</div>

## Best practices

<span id="c86e4f3c"></span>
### Publishing a sign\-in questionnaire

If viewers do not enter their nicknames before submitting questionnaires, the questionnaires are anonymous, and the data may be invalid in some scenarios. To avoid this, you can publish a sign\-in questionnaire, asking viewers to enter their nicknames when entering a live room.

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/f191b228a730471d8df46f04a203bc35~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

Viewers' nicknames entered in the sign\-in questionnaire are the same as those used during the live stream. For example, a viewer who enters the nickname User A in the sign\-in questionnaire can automatically get the same nickname User A in the chat area. You can assume that the viewer with the nickname User A in the sign\-in questionnaire is the same person as the one with the nickname User A in the chat area. For viewers with the same nickname, you can further distinguish them by user ID.

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/3dd3ce2f53964ab69b406e003a81c819~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. On the live room list, click to open a live room.

3. On the left navigation pane, click **Marketing toolkits** \> **Questionnaires**.

4. Click **Create questionnaire**.

5. In the displayed **Create questionnaire** dialog box, select **When viewers enter the live room** as the send time. Click **Add question** and select **Personal information** \> **Nickname** as the question type. Complete the other configurations and then click **OK**.

6. Click **Publish** in the **Actions** column to publish the sign\-in questionnaire.

### Signing up for a live stream

You can allow viewers to sign up for a live stream by publishing a questionnaire. In other words, viewers must enter their phone numbers and emails when they enter the live room.

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/e6003d6a5f7f4e0ebd0653a11c0daf85~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. On the live room list, click to open a live room.

3. On the left navigation pane, click **Marketing toolkits** \> **Questionnaires**.

4. Click **Create questionnaire**.

5. In the displayed **Create questionnaire** dialog box, select **When viewers enter the live room** as the send time. Click **Add question** to add two questions and respectively select **Personal information** \> **Phone number** and **Personal information** \> **Email** as the question type. Complete the other configurations and then click **OK**.

6. Click **Publish** in the **Actions** column to publish the sign\-in questionnaire.
