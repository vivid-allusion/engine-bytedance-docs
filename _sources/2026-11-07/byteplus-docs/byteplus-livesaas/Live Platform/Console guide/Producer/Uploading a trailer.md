Before your live stream starts, you can loop play a trailer to give viewers a warm\-up or a preview of the live stream.

<span id="Steps"></span>
# Steps

To set a trailer, do the following:


1. Open a live stream on [BytePlus Live](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. Click **Producer** \> **Basic Information** and move to the **Advanced Features** section.

3. Click **Update video** beside **Trailer** to upload your video, and keep the **Trailer** switch on.

4. Click **Save** to save the settings.
