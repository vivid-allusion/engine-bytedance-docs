You can overlay your streaming with the cover image or trailer you've set for the live stream. It works well for you to:


* Test the live stream quality and performance prior to releasing your live stream.

* Overlay the live video when a problem emerges during streaming.


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">This feature is applicable only when you live stream through a web browser or in video carousel mode.</div>


<span id="Switching to preview before a live stream"></span>
## Switching to preview before a live stream


1. Select a live stream from [BytePlus Live](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. Click **Lock preview** \> **OK**.

   Your viewers will see the cover image or trailer you set for the live stream. But you can still see the real\-time live stream from the **Live Control** panel.

3. To end the preview and resume the live stream, you have two options:

   * On the viewing page, click **Release notice** \> **OK**.

   * On the **Live Control** panel, click **Release notice** \> **OK**.


<span id="Switching to preview during a live stream"></span>
## Switching to preview during a live stream


1. On the viewing page, click **Lock preview** \> **OK**.

   Your viewers will see the cover image or trailer you've set for the live stream.

2. To end the preview and resume the live stream, click **Release notice** \> **OK.** 

   Your viewers will see your live stream.
