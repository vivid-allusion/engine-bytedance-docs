You can decorate your live stream by customizing its appearance, basic information, and menus.

<span id="957d9335"></span>
## Customizing appearance


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. Open your live stream on the live stream list.

3. On the left navigation pane, click **Producer** \> **Appearance**.

4. On the **Appearance** page, you can complete the following configurations as required and then click **Save**.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/88580ce4af9a424e87a6a4acb82d0f94~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">Make sure that uploaded pictures meet the requirements described in <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/byteplus-livesaas/docs/graphics-guide-for-live-streaming">Graphics guide for live streaming</a>.   </div>
   

<span id="ed55f6a8"></span>
## Configuring basic information


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. Open your live stream on the live stream list.

3. On the left navigation pane, click **Producer** \> **Basic Information**.

4. On the **Basic Information** page, you can complete the following configurations as required and then click **Save**.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/2cbad49fba684572b9e432bf0829dea4~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">Turning on the <strong>Browser icon</strong> switch allows you to customize the logo in the viewing page's title bar on PCs.   </div>
   

<span id="9470efa2"></span>
## Configuring menus


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. Open your live stream on the live stream list.

3. On the left navigation pane, click **Producer** \> **Catalog**.

4. Click **Edit**.

5. In the dialog box that appears, click **Add**. Enter the menu name and select the type.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/c5b281ec78c9499584ffd7161029a766~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

   * Chat interaction: This is where the host and viewers can send comments. For more information, see [Managing the chat area](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/byteplus-livesaas/docs/managing-the-chat-area).

   * Interaction tool: Here, the host can send pictures, documents, questionnaires, lucky draws, and votes. For more information, see [Managing the interaction area](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/byteplus-livesaas/docs/managing-the-interaction-area).

   * Q&A: This is a place for the host and viewers to post and reply to questions. For more information, see [Managing the Q&A area](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/byteplus-livesaas/docs/managing-the-q-a-area).

   * Card: You can display product cards for product promotion here. For more information, see [Adding](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/byteplus-livesaas/docs/adding-product-cards-to-your-live-stream)[ product cards to your live stream](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/byteplus-livesaas/docs/adding-product-cards-to-your-live-stream).

   * Graphics: Texts, links, emojis, and pictures are displayed in this area.

      <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/fbffe000e86e467fb7695f9240612ccc~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

   * Aggregated live stream: This is used to redirect viewers to other live streams.

      <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/bcdf32bbd0c14eff8554abb28683b471~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">You can add at most five menus at a time.   </div>
   

   * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">For chat interaction, interaction tool, and Q&A types, you can add only one menu to a live stream. More than one can be added for card, graphics, and aggregated live stream types.   </div>
   

6. (Optional) You can adjust the order of menus by dragging them into your desired position.

7. Click **OK**.

8. Configure the menus as required. You can also configure menus by clicking the settings icon in the upper\-right corner of the **Live control** page.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/97e039805ba8438f9f91d28a5638ade6~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>
