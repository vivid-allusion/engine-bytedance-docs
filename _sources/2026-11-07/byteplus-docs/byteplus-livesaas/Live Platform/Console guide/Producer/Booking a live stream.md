This topic introduces how to allow viewers to book a live stream. Once viewers successfully register with their phone number and book a live stream, they will receive an SMS notification before the live stream starts. You can use this feature to avoid losing potential viewers and increase your conversion rate.

<span id="Steps"></span>
## Steps


1. Select a live stream on [BytePlus Live](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. Before enabling booking, you should first add a countdown timer to your live stream.

3. Go to **Producer** \> **Basic Information**, enable **Booking**, and then complete the configurations. By default, viewers receive the SMS notification five minutes before a live stream starts. You can change the time as needed.
