This feature is applicable to all prerecorded videos and pull\-stream scenarios.

<span id="Setting an auto-start"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. Open your live stream on the live stream list.

3. On the left navigation pane, click **Producer** \> **Basic Information**.

4. Turn on the **Start automatically** switch and select **Start stream** or **Pull stream**.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">Be sure that the date and time for the live stream is for some point in the future.   </div>
   

   * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">Upload a video as a prerecorded video or configure a source stream address before the configured date and time.   </div>
   

5. Click **Save**.

   The live stream will start automatically as soon as the preset time has arrived.
