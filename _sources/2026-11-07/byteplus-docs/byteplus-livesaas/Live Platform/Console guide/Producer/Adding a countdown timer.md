This topic introduces how to add a countdown timer to your live stream.


1. Select a live stream on [BytePlus Live](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. Go to **Producer** \> **Basic Information** and then enable **Countdown**.

3. Click **Save** .
