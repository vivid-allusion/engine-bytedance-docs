To improve your livestream performance, AI Livestream Analysis intelligently segments your video content and provides AI\-powered optimization suggestions for hosts and operations teams. This feature helps you quickly evaluate how different segments were presented, identify effective selling scripts, and define clearer optimization goals for your next livestream.

<span id="e3ef9248"></span>
## Use Cases

AI Livestream Analysis is suitable for scenarios such as e\-commerce livestream reviews, where teams need to analyze and improve livestream content after a session. The system segments the livestream based on speech transcription and displays the topic, transcript, and analysis tags for each segment. You can also generate segment\-level AI analysis suggestions on demand.

With AI Livestream Analysis, you can quickly evaluate how different livestream segments were presented, identify effective selling scripts, find parts that need improvement, and define clearer optimization goals for the next livestream.

<span id="09cc439f"></span>
## Prerequisites


* **Activate the service**: The AI analysis feature is currently in invite\-only beta. Please contact BytePlus Sales to activate the service.

* You have a BytePlus account or an IAM user account with **Video library** \> **View** permission. For more information on how to add IAM users, see [Managing users, organizations, and roles](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/docs-managing-iam-users-and-roles).


<span id="7bb6ed7f"></span>
## Notes


* AI Livestream Analysis results are automatically generated based on video content and speech transcription. They are intended for livestream operations review only. Evaluate the actual optimization strategy based on your business goals, host style, and product characteristics.

* If the video lacks valid speech, the livestream content is abnormal, the speech transcription quality is low, or related data is insufficient, the analysis result may be incomplete.


<span id="830ec89b"></span>
## Procedure

Follow these steps to analyze a livestream video and generate AI analysis suggestions in BytePlus Live.

<span id="6dd0ef3f"></span>
### Step 1: Access the AI Livestream Analysis page


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. On BytePlus Live's menu bar, click **Media library**.

3. On the left navigation pane, click **Videos** \> **Video library** or **Temporary storage**.

4. On the right of the target video, click **AI analysis** in the **Actions** column.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/817cdab1ca8646cb94114a0bab59dac2~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

   The AI Livestream Analysis page opens.

5. Click Start generation to start the analysis. The analysis may take some time to complete.

### Step 2: Analyze the Livestream

The AI analysis page displays video data and content analysis. You can refer to the practical scenario examples below to interpret your own livestream.

## Interpreting Content Analysis Results

<span id="7d60531f"></span>
### Notes

AI analysis suggestions are generated by segment. Suggestions are not generated in advance by default. You must click **AI Analysis** in the target segment to obtain the result.

<span id="e3893808"></span>
### Live metrics

In the **Live metrics** section, view the duration, speaking speed, and average sentence length of the video to help you evaluate the host's speaking pace and content continuity.

<span id="75535080"></span>
### **Content Analysis**

In the Content Analysis module, you can:


* Use the filters at the top of the page to quickly find livestream segments of interest by **time range**, **keywords**, or **whether AI analysis has been performed**.

* Click the **AI Analysis** button for any segment to generate an AI\-powered summary and optimization suggestions.

## FAQs

<span id="fb38ceed"></span>
### Why can't I see the AI Livestream Analysis entry?

Confirm that AI Livestream Analysis has been activated for your account and that the current account has permission to access the video library, live replays, or videos in temporary storage. If the entry is still unavailable, contact BytePlus Live technical support.

<span id="43916130"></span>
### Why does a segment have no AI analysis result?

Segment\-level AI analysis is generated on demand by default. You must click **AI Analysis** in the target segment before the system generates and displays suggestions.

<span id="027b720d"></span>
### Why can't I find the target content by searching?

Check whether the keyword is consistent with the speech transcription content. If the livestream speech is unclear or the transcription quality is low, search results may be affected.
