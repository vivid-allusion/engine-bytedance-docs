This topic describes how to use the BytePlus Live app on a phone to start a live stream anywhere, anytime, outdoors or on the go. The app offers features such as Beauty AR, products, and widgets, and supports real\-time interaction with viewers. It is widely used in scenarios such as e\-commerce live streaming and online classes.

<span id="cc07bf9c"></span>
## Prerequisite

Make sure the Android version of your phone is 6.0 or later.

<span id="Downloading the app"></span>
## Download and installation

Search for **BytePlus Live** in the Google Play Store to download and install the app. The BytePlus Live app is available and supported in the following countries or regions:


* Hong Kong, China

* Taiwan, China

* Macao, China

* Japan

* South Korea

* Indonesia

* Singapore

* Malaysia

* Philippines

* Thailand

* Vietnam

* United Arab Emirates

* Kuwait

* Saudi Arabia

* Turkey

* Qatar
