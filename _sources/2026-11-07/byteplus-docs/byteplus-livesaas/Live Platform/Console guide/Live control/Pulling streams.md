You can pull a live stream from an external source to BytePlus Live. In this way, viewers can watch the same live stream simultaneously on different platforms.

<span id="ddc51feb"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. Open your live stream on the live stream list.

3. Click **Pull stream** on the **Broadcast method** tab under the player.

4. In the **Source stream address** field, enter the URL of the source stream. Make sure the source stream is valid and stable.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/3d45eebc03034a0ca7b3c7c53bddcad2~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

5. Click **Start pulling** to synchronize the live stream from the specified external source to BytePlus Live.
