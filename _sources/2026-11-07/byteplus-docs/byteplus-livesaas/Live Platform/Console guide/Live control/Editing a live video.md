You can edit a live video in real time and export the video clip without waiting for the live stream to end. Then, you can post the video clip on social media platforms, such as TikTok, for marketing and promotion.

<span id="a096b28e"></span>
## Context

Editing a live video is a use case of the cloud clips feature. For more information about how to use cloud clips, see [Editing a video in cloud clips](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/editing-a-video-in-cloud-clips).

<span id="96a079c9"></span>
## Page overview

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/3747d118941c4a019772854451cf11de~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>


|**No.**  |**Name** |**Description** |
|---|---|---|
|1 |Material area |Select and drag a video on the **Live** tab to the track in the working area. |
|2 |Preview area |Control the video on the track in real time by using play control components, preview the video on the track, and change the canvas size. |
|3 |Play control components |Play, fast forward, and rewind the video on the track, and jump to the start point and end point of the selected video. |
|4 |Working area |Edit the video, including splitting and deleting the selected video, selecting and dragging the video to adjust the sequence, adjusting the video volume, and zooming in or out the view on the track. |
|5 |Undo |Click the **Undo** icon to cancel the previous operation. To undo multiple operations, you can repeatedly click the icon. |
|6 |Redo |Click the **Redo** icon to restore the undone operation. To restore multiple operations, you can repeatedly click the icon. |
|7 |Split |Locate where you want to split the video and click the **Split** icon to split the selected video into two parts. |
|8 |Delete |Select the video that you want to delete and click the **Delete** icon. |
|9 |Adjust volume |Adjust the volume of the video. |
|10 |Zoom in or out the view on the track |Zoom in or out the view on the track, thereby adjusting the granularity in the timeline. |
|11 |Pull the latest live content |The live content on the track only contains the content from the time when the live stream starts to the time when the live video is added to the track. Click the **Pull the latest live content** icon to pull the latest live content in real time. |

## Prerequisite

A BytePlus account or an IAM user with **Cloud clips** \> **Edit** permission. For more information on how to add IAM users, see [Managing users, organizations, and roles](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/docs-managing-iam-users-and-roles).

<span id="da826803"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. You can use one of the following ways to enter the **Cloud clips** page.

   * On BytePlus Live's menu bar, click **Media library**. On the left navigation pane, click **Cloud clips**. Click **New clip**.

      <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/3a1a500a19534eac9bae89eb34fb7337~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

   * On the live room list, click to open a live room that is in the **Live** status. On the **Live control** page, click **Clip live stream** under the player.

      <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/65f8a42e4bb245f29ca213334e04b8b6~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

3. Select and drag a video on the **Live** tab in the material area to the track in the working area.

4. Edit the video based on your requirements.

5. (Optional) You can also select and drag a video on the **Video library** or **Live caching** tab to the track in the working area, or click **Local file** to upload a local video to the video library and then select and drag the video on the **Video library** tab to the track. Merge the live video with other videos, and edit the merged video to generate a new video.

6. After the editing, click **Export to video library** in the upper\-right corner of the page.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/a3bbb10bee074deb9b4cdfa512ad918c~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

7. In the **Export to video library** dialog box that appears, enter the video name, select the video quality, and then click **Confirm**.

   It takes about one third of the video length to export the video clip to the video library. For example, if a video clip is 15 minutes, it will take about five minutes to export it to the video library. Then, you can go to **Media library** \> **Videos** \> **Video library** and click **Download** in the **Actions** column on the right of the video clip to download it.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/e3f3a26422d3491fa205a93d551cb87b~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">Currently, the cloud clips module does not support features such as video transitions and filters. If you want to use such features, we recommend that you use a professional video editing app to process the downloaded video clip and then post it to social media platforms, such as TikTok.   </div>
