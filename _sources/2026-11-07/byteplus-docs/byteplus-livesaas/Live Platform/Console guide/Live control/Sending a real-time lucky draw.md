You can send a real\-time lucky draw to increase the interaction between the host and viewers and to attract viewers.

<span id="fd439fcd"></span>
## Prerequisites


* Make sure the menu for interaction tools is added. For more information, see [Configuring the menu for interaction tools](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/docs-managing-the-interaction-area#f1b04790).

* A BytePlus account or an IAM user with Live control \> Edit permission. For more information on how to add IAM users, see [Managing users, organizations, and roles](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/docs-managing-iam-users-and-roles).


<span id="bf03ec87"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. On the live room list, click to open a live room.

3. On the **Live control** page, click **Lucky draw** under the menu for interaction tools.

   <div style="text-align: center">
   <img src="https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/4761337922b8406a86cf79edd0fe4ea1~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image" width="1824px" />   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">For a better viewing experience, we recommend that you turn on the <strong>Display menu</strong> switch in the lower\-right corner to display the menu on the viewing page.   </div>
   

   * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">You can also click <strong>Configure lucky draw</strong> on the <strong>Real\-time lucky draws</strong> tab of the <strong>Marketing toolkits</strong> \> <strong>Lucky draws</strong> page to redirect to the menu for interactive tools on the <strong>Live control</strong> page. If the menu is not added, you will be redirected to the <strong>Live control</strong> page.   </div>
   

4. Click **Create lucky draw** in the dialog box that appears.

5. In the **Create lucky draw** dialog box that appears, complete the following configurations and then click **Confirm**.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/ae5a09f330504de583e08fb7d16dbb40~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

   * **Prize name**: Enter a prize name.

   * **Number of prizes**: Configure the number. You can send at most 2,000 prizes in a lucky draw.

   * **Draw method**: Select **Automatic** or **Manual**.

      * **Automatic**: Prizes are automatically drawn after the countdown is over.

      * **Manual**: You must click **Draw** to draw prizes.

   * **Countdown** **to** **draw**: Configure the number of minutes before prizes are automatically drawn. You can configure at most 999 minutes. This field only appears after you select **Automatic** as the **Draw method**.

   * **Winners must enter**: Select the information that winners must enter.

      * Select **Phone number** and **Shipping address** for physical prizes.

      * Select **Phone number** for virtual prizes.

      * For custom prizes, nothing needs to be entered for winners. You must contact them by yourself.

   * **Rules**: Select the chance of winning for viewers.

      * **Everyone**: Every viewer has an equal chance of winning.

      * **Priority to viewers who have not yet won prizes**: Viewers who haven't won any prizes in the live room will have a higher probability of winning.

   * **Show number of participants**: Turn on the switch to display the actual number of participants in the lucky draw on the viewing page.

   * **Bullet comment password**: If configured, viewers must send the corresponding bullet comment before participating in the lucky draw.

   * **VIP users**: Configure the VIP users as instructed. VIP users have a higher probability of winning than other viewers.

6. (Optional) Enable the lucky draw notifications to allow viewers to view the notifications in the chat area on the viewing page.

   1. Click the **Manage notifications** icon under the menu for chat interaction.

   2. Turn on the switch for lucky draw notifications and then click **OK**.

      <div style="text-align: center">
      <img src="https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/70b7a8a47e93455c8e0499db02527aa6~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image" width="1244px" />      </div>
      

7. Click **Lucky draw** under the menu for interaction tools and select the lucky draw to be sent in the dialog box that appears. Click **Send** to send the lucky draw to the viewing page.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/92d4dfb20dc54bb7bdc24d92e523d33f~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

8. (Optional) You can edit or delete unsent lucky draws, as well as preview or delete sent lucky draws.

9. (Optional) Under the menu for interaction tools, you can perform the following operations.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/b8c3dcc23abf45c7bf9d8b6740a6380a~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

   * If **Manual** is selected as the **Draw method**, click **Draw** to draw the prize manually. If **Automatic** is selected as **Draw method**, winners are selected automatically after the countdown is over.

   * Click the **Withdraw** icon to withdraw a sent lucky draw.

   * Click **View winner list** after the prize is drawn to view the winner list.

   * View the number of participants.


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">You can also view the lucky draw data on the <strong>Real\-time lucky draws</strong> tab of the <strong>Insights</strong> \> <strong>Lucky draw data</strong> page. For more information, see <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-livesaas/docs-data-statistics">Data statistics</a>.</div>


<span id="1e3424d8"></span>
## What your viewers see

Viewers must log in to the live room before participating in lucky draws. They can only open the lucky draw most recently sent by clicking the **Draw** icon or the lucky draw notification. However, they can open all the lucky draws sent in the live room under the menu for interaction tools.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">If <strong>Public</strong> is set as a comment restriction on the <strong>Producer</strong> \> <strong>Viewing restrictions</strong> page, viewers must enter their phone numbers to log in to the live room.</div>
