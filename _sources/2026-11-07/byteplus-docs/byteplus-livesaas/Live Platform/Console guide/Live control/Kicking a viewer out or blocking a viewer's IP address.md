You can kick a viewer out of a live room or block a viewer's IP address if the viewer disrupts the order of the live room during a live stream. This can help you promptly prevent the entry of unruly viewers, thus ensuring the order in the live room and the viewing experience of other viewers.

<span id="43e253f7"></span>
## Prerequisites


* A BytePlus account or an IAM user with Live control \> Edit permission. For more information on how to add IAM users, see [Managing users, organizations, and roles](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/docs-managing-iam-users-and-roles).

* The menu for chat interaction. For more information, see [Managing the chat area](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/docs-managing-the-chat-area).


<span id="8169c2a5"></span>
## Scenarios

During a live stream, situations that may disrupt the order and safety of a live room and affect other viewers' experience could occur, such as:


* Spamming maliciously

* Sending improper comments

* Spreading false information

* Harassing other viewers


To maintain the smooth operation of the live stream, you can kick a viewer out of the live room or block a viewer's IP address.

<span id="076aca59"></span>
## Procedure

You can kick a viewer out of a live room or block a viewer's IP address under the menu for chat interaction and on the **Viewer list** page both on the BytePlus Live console and the live streaming page of your web browser.

The feature operations are similar on different pages. In this topic, take the menu for chat interaction on the BytePlus Live console as an example to introduce how to kick a viewer out of a live room or block a viewer's IP address if the viewer disrupts the order of the live room.


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. On the live room list, click to open a live room.

3. Under the menu for chat interaction of the **Live control** page, click the ellipsis icon on the right of a comment and then select **Kick out** or **Block IP**.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/90ae8cf49d0c48dea8f62a77920cb072~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

   * Kick a viewer out of the live room: After selecting **Kick out**, the viewer who sent the comment is kicked out of the live room and cannot enter the live room again. The comment is also deleted from the viewing page. To allow the viewer to enter the live room again, click the ellipsis icon on the right of a comment sent by the viewer and select **Unkick**.

      <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="2">tip      </div>
      

      <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="2">Viewers with the same user ID or device ID are considered the same viewer. However, user IDs are different when a viewer logs in to a live room with different methods. If a viewer logs in to the live room using a different method and device after being kicked out, the viewer will be considered a different viewer from the kicked out viewer, thus the viewer can enter the live room again.      </div>
      

   * Block a viewer's IP address: After selecting **Block IP**, the viewer who sent the comment is kicked out of the live room and no one from the blocked IP address can enter the live room. The comment is also deleted from the viewing page. To unblock the IP address, click the ellipsis icon on the right of a comment sent by the viewer and select **Unblock**.
