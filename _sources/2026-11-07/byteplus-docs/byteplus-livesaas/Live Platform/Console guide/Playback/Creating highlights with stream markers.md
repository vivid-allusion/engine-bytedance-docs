You can create stream markers and add descriptions for them to curate the best part of your playback. This feature is good for you to:


* Add stream markers to your desired location to create highlights and draw attention to the best content in order to engage and expand viewers.

* Help the PC viewers have a brief of the streaming content and quickly locate their most\-interested part.


To create highlights with stream markers:


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. Open your live stream on the live stream list.

3. In the left navigation panel, click **Playback**.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">Before a live stream starts, you can switch on <strong>Automatic shelf playback</strong>. In this case, you'll see the playback(s) of your live streaming on the list after you end streaming. You can also upload videos as playback.   </div>
   

4. Select the playback you want to create markers for, and then click **Edit** on the **Operation** column.

5. Click **Mark dot.** 

   You will see the Mark dot window which consists of two parts:

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/9b3a2c3133a045f5bdeb56fffa496c0e~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

   * **Source playback**: Drag the progress bar to check the content you want to highlight with the markers.

   * **Marker queue:**  This section contains a list of all the markers you've made so far that are waiting to be published. Here, you can change the timestamp and description for any existing marker, or click **Delete** to remove a marker from the queue.

6. To create a new marker, you can choose to:

   * Drag the progress bar of the source playback to a desired location and click **Create** **RBI** to create a timestamp for your highlight.

      <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/f5cdf714fee34e05b2c4b1f38263ecb2~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

   * Click **New mark** when the progress bar of the source playback reaches your desired location.

7. After you add a description for each mark, click **Save**.

8. Back to the playback list, select the playback you've created markers, click **Release** on the **Operation** column.

   Your viewers will see the markers and descriptions when they watch the playback via PC.
