The subscription billing method for BytePlus Live allows you to pre\-purchase feature versions and resource packages for a fixed period. This guide explains the available subscription options, such as feature versions, watch time, and co\-host time, and provides step\-by\-step instructions on how to subscribe in the console.

## Introduction to feature versions and resource packages

Before you subscribe to any packages, you can activate a 1\-month free trial version, which gives you up to 5 GB of media library capacity, 3000 minutes of watch time, and 120 minutes of co\-host time.

To use more features and resources, see [Subscription method](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/docs-billing-methods#48f4bdcd).


* You can subscribe to different feature versions provided by BytePlus Live. Each version includes different features.

* You can subscribe to watch time packages with varying duration (in minutes).

   After your subscription, viewers can watch live videos, playbacks, and previews for a certain number of minutes.

   For example, if you have 1,000 viewers and each of them has watched live videos, playbacks, or previews for an average of 30 minutes, then 30,000 (1,000×30) minutes of watch time is consumed. Time spent on pause does not count toward the watch time.

* You can subscribe to co\-host time packages with varying duration (in minutes).

   After your subscription, hosts can co\-host with guests for a certain number of minutes.

   For example, if you have 10 hosts and 40 guests co\-hosting, with each host co\-hosting for an average of 100 minutes and each guest co\-hosting for an average of 80 minutes, then 4,200 (10×100+40×80) minutes of co\-host time is consumed.

* You can subscribe to media library capacity packages with varying sizes (in GB).

   Uploaded videos and playbacks that are transferred to the video library consume capacity from your media library package.

   Package consumption is calculated based on your peak daily storage usage. For example, if you start the day with 100 GB of stored videos and delete 50 GB, your peak usage for that day is 100 GB. If you start the next day with 50 GB of stored videos and upload another 20 GB, your peak usage for that day is 70 GB.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">1 TB equals 1,024 GB, and 1 PB equals 1,024 TB.   </div>
   

   * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">During the validity period, if the package is used up, you can delete existing videos to free up media library space. You can then use the freed capacity.   </div>
   

* You can subscribe to the live concurrent viewers package.

   After your subscription, you can have up to a certain number of viewers watching live streams at the same time without additional charges.

   Suppose you subscribe to a package designed for 100 viewers for 2 months. Then, you can have up to 100 viewers watching live streams at the same time for two consecutive months, without extra cost.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">The number of concurrent viewers is tracked every second to ensure fair use.   </div>

## Subscription method

Follow these steps to subscribe to the feature version and resource packages:


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/) with your BytePlus account. Subscription is not available for IAM users.

2. Click **Pricing** in the upper\-right corner of the page.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/5ed89d000b50460a91466fa55e9d8616~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

3. Select the feature version and resource packages as needed. The price displayed on this page is the list price. You can view the actual price after clicking **Confirm your order**.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/12822b0f65964acaa82eac529736dd50~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

   * You can select only one feature version: either **Standard** or **Advanced**.

      * The **Standard** version includes all console features, server\-side OpenAPI, embedding the viewing page in an iframe, the Web Viewer SDK, and the BytePlus Live app for iOS and Android.

      * The **Advanced** version includes all features of the **Standard** version, as well as the Live Streaming SDK for iOS and Android, the Viewer SDK for iOS, Android, and Flutter, and embedding the console in an iframe.

   * Select one or more watch time packages, co\-host time packages, and media library capacity packages as needed. You can also configure the quantity of packages of the same type.

4. Select the **Have read and agree to BytePlus Customer Agreement, Service Specific Terms and BytePlus Live service level agreement** checkbox and then click **Confirm your order** in the lower\-right corner of the page.

5. On the **Confirm order information** page, redeem your coupon if you have one. Then, click **Next step** to complete your payment.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/e929f7fa3dfa46e49d57884e9ba6a9c1~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

## Notes


* The subscription takes effect immediately.

* If you purchase the same feature version more than once, the validity period is extended.

* If you purchase the same resource package more than once, the available usage accumulates within the validity period. Packages of the same type are used up in the order you purchased them. Each package has its own validity period. Therefore, even if you purchase multiple packages of the same type, their validity periods will not extend.

* No refunds or cancellations are available for your purchased feature versions and resource packages.

* If your feature version subscription expires, the BytePlus Live service will become unavailable.

* When you use the watch time package and the co\-host time package, the pay\-as\-you\-go billing method for watch time and co\-host time is also activated. Once such packages are used up, you cannot use the live streaming and co\-host features. However, due to a 1\-to\-2\-day delay in usage reporting, any consumption that occurs after a package is depleted is billed at the pay\-as\-you\-go rate. If you have any questions, contact your sales representative.
