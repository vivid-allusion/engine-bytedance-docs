BytePlus Live is an enterprise\-level live streaming platform designed to provide a superior, interactive, and intelligent streaming experience.


* It ensures stable, smooth 4K streaming for millions of simultaneous viewers while enhancing engagement with ML\-powered data analysis and chatbots.

* The platform also supports AI Live, which uses AI\-powered AI figures to run 24/7 unattended shopping streams.

* It further offers a rich set of interactive tools, deep customization for themed sessions, and security measures to help you create professional, immersive, and secure live events.


<span id="what-we-provide"></span>
## What we provide

**Stable and smooth streaming experience**


* 4K Ultra HD streaming.

* Excellent track record in medium and large\-scale cross\-regional streaming.

* A high\-availability architecture and resource redundancy that can easily support hundreds of millions of concurrent viewers.


**AI Live with AI figures**


* Run 24/7 unattended shopping streams hosted by AI\-powered avatars cloned from real people.

* Train personalized avatars and voices from your own video and audio to represent your brand.

* Showcase products from TikTok Shop, Shopify, and other channels, and let the avatar answer viewer questions in real time using a customizable knowledge base.

* Drive script\-based control over what the avatar says, its pacing, and the overall flow for stable, professional streams. For details, see [AI Live overview](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/digital-human-overview).


**AI Live Analysis**


* Intelligently segments your livestream video based on speech transcription and displays the topic, transcript, and analysis tags for each segment.

* Generates AI\-powered summaries and optimization suggestions on demand for individual segments, helping hosts and operations teams review performance.

* Provides live metrics such as duration, speaking speed, and average sentence length to help you evaluate the host's pace and content continuity. For details, see [AI Live analysis](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-livesaas/AI-Live-analysis).


**Live streaming powered by machine learning**


* Equipped with chatbot to engage your audience.

* Monitors your live stream's popularity and make informed decisions with data analysis on metrics like playback count, viewers, audience engagement, and conversion rates.


**A rich set of interactive tools**


* Enable audience interaction through chat, polls, lucky draws, Q&A, and more.

* Supports live ecommerce, digital window displays, pop\-up ads, and more.


**Themed live stream sessions**


* Build immersive, themed live stream sessions with custom menus, backgrounds, and other decorations.

* Switch between portrait and landscape modes during streaming.

* Highly configurable live stream templates to help you go live in minutes.


**Security measures**

Moderate the live chat with automatic sensitive word filtering or message approval before messages are displayed.
