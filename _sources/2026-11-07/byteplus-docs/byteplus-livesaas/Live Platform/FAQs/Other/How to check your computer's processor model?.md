Check the processor model of your Windows or macOS computer in the following ways:


* Windows

   1. Right\-click **This PC** and select **Properties**.

   2. On the page that appears, check your processor model.

* macOS

   1. Click the Apple logo and select **About This Mac**.

   2. On the **Overview** tab of the page that appears, check your processor model.
