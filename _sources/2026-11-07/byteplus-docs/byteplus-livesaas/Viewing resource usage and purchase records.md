On the **Service center** page under **AI Live** tab, you can view your resource usage and purchase records.

<span id="prerequisite"></span>
## Prerequisite

A BytePlus admin account. The **Service center** page for AI Live and AI Analysis is available only to the admin account. Subaccounts do not have access to the **Service center** page.

<span id="procedure"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Live console](https://console.byteplus.com/livesaas/liveManagement/).

2. On the top menu bar, click **AI Live**.

3. On the left navigation bar, click **Service center**.

4. On the **Service center** page, view the following data.
