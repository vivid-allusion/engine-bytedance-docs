ClawSentry provides a layered and in\-depth security protection system. By applying the following four core protection policies, you can build comprehensive security guardrails for AI assistants to ensure the security, compliance, and controllability of their interactions.

<span id="BJ2fDQOg"></span>
## Policy comparison


|Protection policy |Applicable scenarios |
|---|---|
|Prompt injection protection |Scenarios requiring strong constraints on user input or preventing model hijacking. |
|Interception of high\-risk operations |The assistant is capable of executing tools or actions, especially in scenarios involving operations on internal systems, databases, or files. |
|Sensitive data disclosure prevention |Scenarios where interactive content may involve personal information. |
|Risk scanning |Plan to introduce or have already integrated any third\-party or external skills into the AI assistant. |
