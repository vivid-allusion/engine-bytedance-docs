This article describes how to assign an employee to a Claw instance or switch the employee assigned to a Claw instance.

<span id="pTU6IAmh"></span>
## Operation instructions

Switching the employee ** does not delete the original data of the Claw instance**. If needed, the administrator can [restore factory settings](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Restore_Claw_instance_to_factory_settings?lang=zh) to reset the instance to its initial available state.

<span id="HcoFEDTi"></span>
## Steps


1. Log in to the [ArkClaw Enterprise Edition console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/enterprise).

2. In the left\-side navigation pane, choose "Claw Management \> Claw List".

3. On the Claw List page, click the "More \> Assign employee" button for the target Claw instance.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="1">If there are multiple tabs on the Claw List page, perform the operation under the "Independently purchased instances" tab.   </div>
   

4. In the dialog box that pops up, select the employee to assign to the Claw instance, click the "OK" button, and complete the operation.
