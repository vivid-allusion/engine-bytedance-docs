This article describes how to purchase an independent Claw instance.

<span id="FW5Unljn"></span>
## Operation instructions


* **You can purchase an independent Claw instance only if you have enabled the "space management type" feature and selected "Instance management" as the space management type when creating the ArkClaw space.**  "Space management type" is a Beta feature. The "Space management type" feature is in beta. Contact your account manager to enable it.

* The differences between Claw instances under the two ArkClaw space types are as follows:

   * **Seat management** type space: Subscription is based on the overall number of seats. Seats of the same specification have the same expiration time. Within the purchased seat quota, you can freely create or assign Claw instances.

   * **Instance management** type space: There is no concept of "seat". Management is based on individual Claw instances. That is, all Claw instances in the space are created, assigned, and managed separately.


<span id="Rgx8AhJx"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to [ArkClaw Enterprise Edition console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/enterprise).

2. In the left\-side navigation pane, select "Claw management \> Claw list".

3. On the Claw list page, click the "Create Claw instance" button.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="1">If there are multiple tabs on the Claw list page, click the "Create Claw instance" button under the "Independent purchase instance" tab.   </div>
   

4. Refer to the table below to configure relevant parameters.

   
   |Parameter |Explain |
   |---|---|
   |Select type |Choose the specification type for the Claw instance. Different specifications have varying fees and applicable scenarios. Select the specification that matches your business type.<br><br><br>* **Starter**: Designed for entry\-level experience and lightweight tasks; supports simple conversations and a small number of tool calls.<br><br>* **Standard**: The main version for daily production; supports moderately complex workflows and multi\-turn conversations.<br><br>* **Premium**: Supports complex tasks, enables parallel tool calls and medium\-sized project workflows.<br><br>* **Ultimate**: Designed for large projects and high\-load scenarios; supports end\-to\-end complex process orchestration. |
   |Quantity |Set the number of Claw instances to purchase. You can purchase up to 10 Claw instances in a single action. |
   |Purchase time |Set the subscription duration for the Claw instance, up to 12 months. |
   |Instance name |Set the Claw instance name. When purchasing multiple Claw instances in bulk, all instances will have the same name. |
   |Assigned employee |Choose the employee to whom the Claw instance belongs. You can assign the Claw instance to an employee during purchase, or assign it after the Claw instance has been successfully purchased.<br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div><br><br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">If multiple Claw instances are purchased in a single action and assigned to an employee during purchase, all these Claw instances will belong to this employee.</div><br> |
   |Automatic renewal |Enable automatic renewal.<br><br><br>* **Enable**: After the Claw instance expires, it will be automatically renewed and payment will be deducted, preventing service interruption caused by expiration.<br><br>* **Do not enable**: After the Claw instance expires, it will not be automatically renewed. If you still need to use the Claw instance, please renew it in a timely manner.<br><br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div><br><br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">After the Claw instance expires, it will enter a frozen status. Employees cannot initiate new sessions, but session history and associated resources will remain accessible in read\-only mode during the 7\-day retention period. After the retention period ends, the data will be permanently deleted and cannot be restored.</div><br> |
   

5. After confirming that the purchase information is correct, check "I have read and agree to the ArkClaw Terms of Service", click the "Pay now" button, and follow the page guidance to complete the payment operation.
