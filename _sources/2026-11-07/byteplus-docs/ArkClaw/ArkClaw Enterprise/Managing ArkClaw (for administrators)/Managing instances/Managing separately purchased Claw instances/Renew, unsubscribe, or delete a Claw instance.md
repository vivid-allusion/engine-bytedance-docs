This article introduces how to renew or unsubscribe a Claw instance.

<span id="onDhtKoW"></span>
## Renew or unsubscribe a Claw instance


1. Log in to [ArkClaw Enterprise Edition console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/enterprise).

2. In the left\-side navigation pane, select "Claw Management \> Claw list".

3. On the Claw list page, renew or unsubscribe the Claw instance.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="1">If there are multiple tabs on the Claw list page, perform the operation under the "Independently Purchased Instance" tab.   </div>
   

   
   <Tabs>
   <Tab zoneid="aVwGErXhOl" title="Renewal">
   <TabTitle>Renewal</TabTitle>
   
   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>
   
   
   
   * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">After a Claw instance reaches expiration, it will enter a frozen status. Users cannot initiate new sessions, but existing session history and associated resources can still be accessed in read\-only mode during the 7\-day retention period.</div>
   
   
   * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">To continue using a Claw instance, renew within the 7\-day retention period. Upon successful renewal, the Claw instance will be restored to available status. After the retention period expires, all data will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.</div>
   
   
   1. Click the "Renew" button in the Actions column for the target Claw instance.
   
   2. On the Platform Instance Management page, click the "Renew" button, select the renewal cycle, and follow the interface instructions to complete payment.
   
   
   </Tab>
   <Tab zoneid="kUhcQSRkwb" title="Unsubscribe">
   <TabTitle>Unsubscribe</TabTitle>
   
   1. Click the "More \> Unsubscribe" button in the Actions column for the target Claw instance.
   
   2. On the Platform Instance Management page, click the "Unsubscribe" button. After confirming the unsubscribe information is correct, click the "Submit Order" button. Once the unsubscribe is completed, the refund amount will be returned via the original payment method.
   
   
   </Tab>
   </Tabs>
   


<span id="VMmbqh5n"></span>
## Delete a Claw instance

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">Only unsubscribed Claw instances can be deleted.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">After a Claw instance is deleted, it will be released immediately, and all related configurations and internal data will be cleared. <strong>This operation is irreversible. Once a Claw instance is deleted, it cannot be restored.</strong></div>


1. Log in to [ArkClaw Enterprise Edition console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/enterprise).

2. In the left\-side navigation pane, select "Claw Management \> Claw list".

3. On the Claw list page, click the "Delete" button in the Actions column for the target Claw instance.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="1">If there are multiple tabs on the Claw list page, perform the operation under the "Independently Purchased Instance" tab.   </div>
   

4. In the pop\-up dialog box, click the "Confirm" button to complete the operation.
