To prevent business interruption or permanent data loss due to accidental deletion of Claw instances, we recommend that you select **Move to recycle bin** to delete Claw instances. Deleted Claw instances will be temporarily stored in the recycle bin and will be automatically and permanently cleared only after the retention period expires. During the retention period, you can restore Claw instances at any time.

<span id="Pkibe0RZ"></span>
## Recycle bin retention period

The default retention period is 7 days. You can restore Claw instances at any time during the retention period.

<span id="d77sWsic"></span>
## Restoring Claw instances

Administrators can restore deleted Claw instances for employees from the recycle bin. During restoration, the system will check whether the quotas of the employees are sufficient. If so, the instances will be re\-enabled and started, which will occupy the corresponding quota.

If the quotas are insufficient, the system will prompt that the restoration failed. The administrators need to delete other Claw instances of the employees first  before performing the restoration again.


1. Log in to [ArkClaw Enterprise console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/enterprise).

2. In the left navigation bar​, click "Claw management \> Claw list".

3. Click the "Recycle bin" tab.

4. Restore one or more Claw instances.

   * Restore a single instance: Click "Restore" in the Actions column that corresponds to the target Claw instance.

   * Restore multiple instances at the same time: Select the Claw instances to restore and then click "Restore" at the bottom of the page.

5. In the dialog box that appears, click "Restore" to complete the operation.

## Permanently deleting Claw instances

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">After Claw instances in the recycle bin are deleted, the Claw instances will be immediately released, and their related configurations and data will be cleared. <strong>This operation is irreversible. Proceed with caution</strong>.</div>



1. Log in to [ArkClaw Enterprise console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/enterprise).

2. In the left navigation bar, click "Claw management \> Claw list".

3. Click "Recycle bin".

4. Permanently delete one or more Claw instances.

   * Delete a single instance

      1. Click "Permanently delete" in the Actions column that corresponds to the target Claw instance.

      2. In the dialog box that pops up, click "Confirm" to complete the operation.

   * Delete multiple instances at the same time

      1. Select the Claw instances to delete and then click "Permanently delete" at the bottom of the page.

      2. In the dialog box that pops up, click "Permanently delete" to complete the operation.
