For abnormal connections of business accounts (e.g., account removed from the allowlist, abnormal connection traffic), administrators can proactively disconnect the connection by rejecting it. Disconnected connections can be manually accepted to re\-establish access.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">Note</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">Private Network Ingress is currently in the invite\-only beta stage. To request a trial, contact your account manager.</div>


<span id="nHHF7Fvy"></span>
## Instructions

After an account is added to the allowlist, it can create an interface endpoint in the PrivateLink console to access the space. New connections are automatically accepted.

<span id="ZSs5IXTr"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [ArkClaw Enterprise console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/enterprise).

2. In the left navigation pane, select "Resource Configuration \> Network Configuration".

3. On the "Space Ingress" tab, perform the following actions as needed:

   * **Reject a connection**

      1. In the connection list, click "Reject" to the right of the target connection.

      2. After confirming the information is correct, click "OK" to complete the action.

   * **Accept** ** a connection**

      1. In the connection list, click "Accept" to the right of the target connection to complete the action.
