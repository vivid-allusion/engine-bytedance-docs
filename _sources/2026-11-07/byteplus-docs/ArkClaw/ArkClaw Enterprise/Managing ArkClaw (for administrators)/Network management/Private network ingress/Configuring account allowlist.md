Administrators control which business accounts can access Claw over the private network by adding them to the allowlist.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">Note</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">Private Network Ingress is currently in the invite\-only beta stage. To request a trial, contact your account manager.</div>


<span id="JHAr2Xhv"></span>
## Instructions


* Only accounts added to the allowlist can access Claw via the private network ingress.

* Up to 20 accounts can be added in a single batch.

* Removing an account from the allowlist does not disconnect existing connections. To disconnect them, see [Reject a connection](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Accepting-or-rejecting-a-connection).


<span id="D2CGMzoU"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the[ ArkClaw Enterprise console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/enterprise).

2. In the left navigation pane, select "Resource Configuration \> Network Configuration".

3. On the "Space Ingress" tab, perform the following actions as needed:

   * **Add to** ** allowlist**

      1. Click "Add to allowlist", enter the BytePlus account ID, and authorize this account to access the space resource through the private network ingress.

      2. Click "OK" to complete the action.

   * **Remove from** ** allowlist**

      1. Click the remove icon to the right of the target account.

      2. Click "OK" to complete the action.
