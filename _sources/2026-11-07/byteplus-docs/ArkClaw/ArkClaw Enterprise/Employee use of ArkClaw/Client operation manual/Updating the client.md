When a new version is detected, an update prompt window appears automatically. Follow the steps below to complete the upgrade.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">Upgrading the ArkClaw client does not upgrade the ArkClaw version or component versions. To upgrade the ArkClaw version, see <a href="https://docs.volcengine.com/docs/87732/2431026">Upgrading ArkClaw version</a>.</div>


<span id="TytxaW9v"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the ArkClaw client locally.

2. At the bottom of the left navigation tree, review the version information and update details, and then click "Update".

   * macOS: The download and installation complete automatically. No additional operation is required.

   * Windows: Follow the on\-screen prompts to complete the installation.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="1">To update later, click the close button in the upper\-right corner of the prompt window. After the window is closed, you can go to "Avatar \> Check for client updates" to view the release notes and version details, and then perform the update.   </div>
