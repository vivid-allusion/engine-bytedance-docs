This topic describes how to create and access a shared Claw.

<span id="4vz8xPw6"></span>
## Create shared Claw instance

In addition to shared Claws created by the enterprise administrator, you can also[ manually apply for a shared Claw](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Creating_ArkClaw_?lang=en) in the console and submit it to the enterprise administrator for approval. Once approved, you can configure and use this Claw.

After you submit the request, click "Application records" in the Claw switching drop\-down list to view the approval progress. You can also contact the enterprise administrator to confirm the approval progress.

<span id="vmeqKhrS"></span>
## View and access a shared Claw

<span id="HH00G1P9"></span>
### **Prerequisites**

To view and use a **shared Claw**, your account must meet either of the following conditions:


* You have been designated as an administrator of the **shared Claw** by the enterprise administrator.

* You have been granted access to a shared agent under the shared Claw by ** the enterprise administrator/shared Claw administrator**.


<span id="NymjsAhe"></span>
### Procedure


1. Open your browser, enter the ArkClaw login URL in the address bar, and press Enter.

2. Switch to the target shared Claw.

   > If an **Administrator ** tag appears to the right of a shared Claw name, your current account has management permissions for that instance.   
   

3. In the dialog box, select an available shared agent and [initiate a task](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Initiating_tasks).

   > Administrators can[ view and manage](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Configuring_agents_for_shared_Claw) all shared agents under the shared Claw. Regular users can view only the agents they are authorized to access.   
   

<span id="5PQcqYm4"></span>
## Add administrators for shared Claw

Administrators can view and manage all shared agents under the shared Claw, whereas regular users can view only the agents they are authorized to access.


1. Log in to the ArkClaw console as the shared Claw administrator and switch to the target shared Claw instance page in the left pane.

2. Hover over the target shared Claw instance, and click the "View Claw details" button.

3. On the details page, click <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_1b3c1fc207e7b2c46903c73ff55f584a.png) </span>.

4. In the search box, enter the name of the person you want to add as an administrator, and then click the corresponding username to complete the addition.
