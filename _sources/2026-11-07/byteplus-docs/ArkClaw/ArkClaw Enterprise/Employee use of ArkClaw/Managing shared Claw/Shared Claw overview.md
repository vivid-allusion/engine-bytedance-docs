## What is shared Claw

Shared Claw is a "public instance" provided by ArkClaw Enterprise that ** can be invoked by multiple employees**.

**Core features**


* **Unified capability supply:**  You can accumulate reusable agents, skills, and tool configurations in the same shared environment.

* **Unified knowledge accumulation:**  You can centrally maintain organization\-level assets such as documents, code repositories, and process specifications, which prevents knowledge fragmentation.

* **Role\-based management:**  Employees with management permission are responsible for maintaining public assets, and regular employees can only call shared capabilities to run tasks.

* **Conversation data isolation**: When multiple users share the same **shared Claw**, each member's tasks and session records remain isolated by initiator, which ensures data security.

## Differences between shared and personal Claw

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default"><strong>Shared Claw</strong> and <strong>personal Claw</strong> differ in the capabilities they support. The capabilities displayed in the console shall prevail. <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Employee-side_console_usage_overview">View the full capability list</a>.</div>



|**Dimension** |**Personal Claw** |**Shared Claw instance** |
|---|---|---|
|Positioning and target object |A dedicated productivity tool for ** individuals**. |A shared capability foundation for ** teams/multiple users**. |
|Managing permissions |Created and managed by **individuals**. |Centrally managed by **administrators authorized by the enterprise administrator**. |
|Visibility and usage scope |Visible and available only to the **creator ** by default. |Visible and available to **authorized employees**, with two permission types: administrator and regular user.<br><br><br>* Administrator: This role can modify configurations (such as switching models and editing souls) and manage the enterprise\-level knowledge base.<br><br>* User: This role can only call the enterprise\-level knowledge base and initiate tasks. |
|Knowledge and data isolation |Supports **personal** workflows (such as custom configurations and temporary tasks). |Supports **the team's** public assets. |
|Scope of change impact |Changing configurations (such as switching models and editing souls) **affects only the individual**. |Changing the configuration ** affects all employees who use the shared Claw**. Proceed with caution. |
|Quota attribution |Occupies the seat edition quotas allocated to individual employees. |Occupies the seat edition quota of the ArkClaw space. |
