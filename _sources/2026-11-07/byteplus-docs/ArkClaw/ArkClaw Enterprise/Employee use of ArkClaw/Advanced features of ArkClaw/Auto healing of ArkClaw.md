When your ArkClaw becomes unavailable due to tasks, setting adjustments, or more, you can use the auto healing feature to attempt to restore it to the latest available state. The healing process takes approximately 1–2 minutes. Wait patiently.

<span id="zHSnMTPy"></span>
## Procedure


1. Open your browser, enter the ArkClaw login URL in the address bar, and press Enter.

2. Switch to the target ArkClaw page and click the details icon in the upper\-right corner.

3. Click the "Automatic repair" button.

4. Click the "Auto healing" button, and then click the "Confirm" button in the dialog box. The platform will attempt to restore ArkClaw to the latest available state.
