If the service crashes due to corrupted configuration, or if system failures, abnormal status, and other unrecoverable issues occur, you can restore the ArkClaw service with one click to its original available state. The process takes about 1–2 minutes. Wait patiently.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">Restoring ArkClaw to factory settings will <strong>clear all ArkClaw memory and restore it to its initial state</strong>. Proceed with caution.</div>


<span id="US8QUdGk"></span>
## Procedure


1. Open your browser, enter the ArkClaw login URL in the address bar, and press Enter.

2. Switch to the target ArkClaw page and click the details icon in the upper\-right corner.

3. Click the "Restore factory settings" button.

4. In the dialog box, click the "Confirm" button. The platform will clear all data and restore ArkClaw to its initial state.
