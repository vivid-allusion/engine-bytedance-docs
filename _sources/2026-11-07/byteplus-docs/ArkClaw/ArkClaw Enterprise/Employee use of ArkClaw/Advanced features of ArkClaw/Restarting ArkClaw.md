You can manually restart ArkClaw when you need to load new ArkClaw configuration or when ArkClaw responds slowly.

<span id="EFExlGnl"></span>
## Procedure


1. Open your browser, enter the ArkClaw login URL in the address bar, and press Enter.

2. Switch to the target ArkClaw page and click the details icon in the upper\-right corner.

3. Click the "Restart" button.

4. In the dialog box, click the "Confirm" button to restart the ArkClaw service.
