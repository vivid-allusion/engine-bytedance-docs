You can exit the ArkClaw conversation page when you no longer need to use ArkClaw.

<span id="hyvcg3H3"></span>
## Procedure


1. Open your browser, enter the ArkClaw login URL in the address bar, and press Enter to access the ArkClaw conversation page.

2. Go to the target ArkClaw page.

3. At the bottom of the left directory tree, click "Avatar \> Log out" to complete the operation.
