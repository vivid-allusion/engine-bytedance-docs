This topic describes the payment rules for ArkClaw Enterprise orders and how to view pending payment orders.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">This topic applies only to workspaces of the seat management type. For details, see <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Managing_separately_purchased_Claw_instances">Managing independently purchased Claw instances</a>.</div>

## Order payment and change instructions

When you make [seat changes](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Managing_seats) (such as adding or reducing seats), the platform will automatically generate a corresponding order. Take note of the following payment rules:


* **Automatic order overwrite**: If you change seats and confirm the change before officially placing an order, the system will generate the latest order and automatically delete data of all previous unplaced orders.

* **Automatic cancellation upon timeout**: The generated order is valid for 30 minutes. If payment is not complete within 30 minutes, the system will automatically cancel the order. To continue the modification after the timeout, initiate the operation again.


<span id="Lv4y0vMP"></span>
## Pay for orders

An order will be retained for 30 minutes after it is generated. Perform the following steps to complete the payment.


1. Log in to the [ArkClaw Enterprise console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/enterprise).

2. In the left navigation pane, click "Resource Configuration" \> "Seats".

3. Click "Order management" in the upper\-right corner of the page.

4. In the Pending payment orders section, find the target order and click "Pay" in the Actions column to complete the payment.


<span id="juEr8Gr9"></span>
## View pending payment orders

A pending payment order is valid for 30 minutes. You can perform the following steps to view pending payment orders.


1. Log in to the [ArkClaw Enterprise console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/enterprise).

2. In the left navigation pane, click "Resource Configuration" \> "Seats".

3. Click "Order management" in the upper\-right corner of the page to view pending payment orders.


<span id="NfHzyxpQ"></span>
## Cancel pending payment orders

Before pending payment orders are automatically canceled due to a timeout, you can manually cancel them **.** 


1. Log in to the [ArkClaw Enterprise console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/enterprise).

2. In the left navigation pane, click "Resource Configuration" \> "Seats".

3. Click "Order management" in the upper\-right corner of the page.

4. In the Pending payment orders section, find the target order and click "Close order" in the Actions column to cancel the order.
