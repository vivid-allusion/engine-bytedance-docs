The expiration of subscription resources (including ArkClaw seats, Coding Plan specifications/Agent Plan specifications) may cause an interruption to the use of ArkClaw. To ensure continuous and stable business operations, please plan your renewal schedule appropriately and complete the renewal promptly.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">The renewal operations described in this topic apply only to workspaces of the seat management type. If your workspace is of the instance management type, the renewal entry point is different. Please locate the target instance directly in the instance list and complete the <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Renew_unsubscribe_or_delete_a_Claw_instance">renewal</a> by clicking the "Renew" button in the operation column.</div>

## Auto\-renewal (ArkClaw + Coding Plan)

When your ArkClaw and Coding Plan are about to expire, you can enable **Auto\-renewal**.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">If enabled, all ArkClaw orders and the managed Coding Plan orders will be automatically renewed before expiration. This will apply to all existing and new orders and cannot be disabled directly on this page. To disable auto\-renewal for ArkClaw resources, go to the <a href="https://console.byteplus.com/finance/renew">Order management</a> page. To disable auto\-renewal for Coding Plan resources, go to the <a href="https://console.byteplus.com/ark">ModelArk console</a>.</div>



1. Log in to the [ArkClaw Enterprise console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/enterprise). 

2. In the left navigation pane, click "Resource Configuration" \> "Seats".

3. Click "Order management" in the upper\-right corner of the page.

4. Click <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_c112e2106ef62c07ab7de233acb61f41.png) </span>icon to enable auto\-renewal.

5. In the dialog box that pops up, click "Confirm" to complete the operation.

   <span id="rVvtcQD3"></span>
   ## Separate renewal

   If you have not enabled**Auto\-renewal** or want to renew an individual resource, perform the following steps:


<Tabs>
<Tab zoneid="oMHG8hG2eJ" title="ArkClaw">
<TabTitle>ArkClaw</TabTitle>

1. Log in to the [ArkClaw Enterprise console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/enterprise). 

2. In the left navigation pane, click "Resource Configuration" \> "Seats".

3. Click "Order management" in the upper\-right corner of the page.

4. In the **ArkClaw paid orders** section, find the target ArkClaw instance and click "Renewal" in the Actions column.

5. Go to the Instances page and click "Renewal" in the Actions column that corresponds to the target instance.

6. You will be redirected to the renewal management page. Review and confirm the instance information.

7. Select a renewal cycle and click "Pay".

8. Click "Next step" to submit the payment information. Then, click "Pay" to complete the payment.

   > If you have available coupons, ​select them for payment deduction.
