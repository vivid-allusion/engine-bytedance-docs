‌ArkClaw Enterprise is designed for enterprise\-wide AI\-powered office scenarios and is particularly suitable for teams that prioritize efficiency, data security, and compliance requirements. It seamlessly integrates into daily collaboration workflows while supporting centralized management and operations.

<span id="JOd4G5nO"></span>
## Applicable enterprises and roles

Typical adopters include:


* Traditional medium\-to\-large enterprises in industries such as finance, education, consumer goods, automotive, and healthcare

* Technology\-driven enterprises in sectors such as the Internet, e\-commerce, gaming, enterprise services, and software

* Roles in fields such as human resources, finance, operations, sales, IT management, O&M, development, and marketing

Deep Feishu integration

ArkClaw Enterprise deeply integrates with the Feishu ecosystem and supports Feishu SSO authentication. This allows users to invoke ArkClaw capabilities directly from Feishu bots, eliminating the need to switch platforms frequently. Employees can independently create Feishu applications, authorize Feishu bots, and perform other related operations. In addition to Feishu, integration with multiple channels (such as DingTalk and WeCom) will be gradually supported to facilitate cross\\-end collaboration.

Unified management and operations

ArkClaw Enterprise provides a dedicated management console for enterprise administrators. Enterprise administrators and regular employees have separate ArkClaw access portals. In the management console, administrators can perform management operations, such as service activation, workspace and seat configurations, and resource management. They can also view metrics such as token usage and skill invocations, and troubleshoot issues using end\\-to\\-end traces. Regular snapshot, backup, and restoration operations further ensure stable, low\\-effort daily operations.

Knowledge and skill asset accumulation

ArkClaw Enterprise enables continuous accumulation of knowledge scattered across internal wikis and code repositories. Through a three\\-tier memory architecture (individuals, teams, and enterprises), it manages internal knowledge and controls knowledge sharing with granular permission rules to safeguard core knowledge during reuse across teams. Employees can get information faster via vector\\-based retrieval and semantic recall. Additionally, enterprises can leverage versioning and access control in the private Skills Hub, and continuously reuse skill assets by using filtering, categorization, and certification capabilities of the public skill hub.
