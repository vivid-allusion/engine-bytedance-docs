ArkClaw is mainly designed for individual users and lightweight office scenarios. It supports issuing commands in natural language. With no complex operations required, it can automatically perform repetitive tasks, organize cross\-domain information, and assist in decision\-making. It helps individuals and lightweight office users cut down tedious manual work and focus on core value creation.

This topic mainly describes typical use cases of ArkClaw in personal affairs processing, lightweight office collaboration, learning assistance, and general intelligent support scenarios, helping you quickly find the usage method that suits your needs.
