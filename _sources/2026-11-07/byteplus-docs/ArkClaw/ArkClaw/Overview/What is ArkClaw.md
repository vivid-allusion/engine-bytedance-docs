## What is ArkClaw

ArkClaw is a cloud\-based AI agent service for individual users and lightweight office scenarios. It enables daily affair processing and lightweight office automation via natural language.


* **Lightweight office automation**

   It automates basic office tasks such as weekly report generation, document organization, spreadsheet synchronization, and meeting scheduling.

* **Daily affair processing**

   It automates daily routines including scheduled reminders, file organization, ticket booking, holiday greetings, and schedule management. It also supports content optimization and learning assistance to boost efficiency in study and daily life.
