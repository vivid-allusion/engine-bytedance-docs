## Usage notes


* After unsubscription, the ArkClaw you created will be stopped immediately, and the data will **be retained for 24 hours**. After 24 hours, the data will be automatically deleted. Confirm your unsubscription details. Unsubscribed resources cannot be restored.

* The refund amount covers only the actual payment amount. Used vouchers are non\-refundable.


<span id="3j5xApoS"></span>
## Unsubscription procedure


1. Log in to the [ArkClaw console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/welcome).

2. In the left navigation pane, select "ArkClaw Management".

3. On the target ArkClaw card, select the "···  **\>**  Unsubscribe" button.

   <span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260410075203-1392115462530.png) </span>

5. You will be redirected to the unsubscription management page. Confirm the information and click the "Submit order" button.

6. After you confirm the information again, select the unsubscription reason and click the "Confirm" button.

7. In the pop\-up window, enter the verification code sent to your email by BytePlus. Complete secondary verification to finish unsubscribing.
