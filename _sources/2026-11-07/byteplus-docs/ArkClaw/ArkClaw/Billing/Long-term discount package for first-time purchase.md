The regular first purchase discount package is a long\-term promotion for first\-time buyers. It is not time\-limited and can be purchased at any time. It offers a stable, discounted price without the need to wait for specific promotion activities. This package is suitable for users who have not yet activated the ModelArk Coding Plan and want to reduce anxiety about pay\-as\-you\-go token billing. It supports your daily personal use and keeps basic tasks running reliably.

## **Promotion rules**

This new purchase promotion is subject to **per\-user limits**. That is, a user can receive the discount **only on their first purchase**. Subsequent purchases are billed at the original price.

> If multiple accounts use the same mobile phone number, the same identification number, the same account ID, or are determined by BytePlus as having the same actual controller, they are considered the **same user**.



|Purchase and activation rules |Description |
|---|---|
|First purchase discount |When subscribing to any package for the first time, you can enjoy the first purchase discount (cannot be combined with continuous subscription discounts). |
|Purchase limit rule |You can enjoy the discount only once. It cannot be applied to multiple purchases. |

## How to access the promotion


1. Log in to the [ArkClaw console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/welcome).

2. Click "Create now" to access the purchase page and purchase ArkClaw based on your needs. For more information, see the bundle purchase section in [Obtaining your dedicated ArkClaw with one click](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Creating_your_dedicated_ArkClaw_with_one_click).
