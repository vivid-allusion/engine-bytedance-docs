To ensure your ArkClaw can be used continuously for a long period, renew it before expiration to extend its validity.

<span id="GsWCpHgI"></span>
## Considerations

For the [ArkClaw](https://console.byteplus.com/ark/region:ark+cn-beijing/experience/claw) (default name: **ArkClaw**) granted as a trial benefit when an Agent Plan/Coding Plan is subscribed to, the renewal rules are as follows:


|Benefit source |Description |
|---|---|
|Agent Plan Small/Medium/Large/Max |* If ArkClaw has not expired or has expired for less than 24 hours, you can [only renew Agent Plan](https://console.byteplus.com/ark/region:ark+cn-beijing/openManagement?LLM=%7B%7D&advancedActiveKey=agentPlan) large model subscription package separately to continue using ArkClaw for free.<br><br>* If more than 24 hours have passed since expiration of your ArkClaw, you need to [activate](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Activating_ModelArk_ArkClaw_) it before you can renew in the ArkClaw console.<br><br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div><br><br><br><br>* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">You can <strong>obtain entitled ArkClaw for free</strong> by subscribing to Agent Plan Medium, Large, or Max. The expiration date of the entitled ArkClaw will be the same as that of your subscribed Agent Plan.</div><br><br><br>* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">You will <strong>not obtain entitled ArkClaw for free</strong> by subscribing to Agent Plan Small. You can purchase <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Creating_your_dedicated_ArkClaw_with_one_click_#F0e9F6CX">ArkClaw</a> separately based on your needs.</div><br> |
|Coding Plan Lite |You can renew in the ArkClaw console only after your ArkClaw has expired and been [activated](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Activating_ModelArk_ArkClaw_). |
|Coding Plan Pro |* If ArkClaw has not expired or has expired for less than 24 hours, you can only **renew the Coding Plan Pro** LLM subscription package to continue using ArkClaw for free.<br><br>* If more than 24 hours have passed since expiration of your ArkClaw, you need to [activate](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Activating_ModelArk_ArkClaw_) it before you can renew in the ArkClaw console. |


<span id="SwITTSKC"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [ArkClaw console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/welcome).

2. Follow the steps below to enter the renewal page.

   
   <Tabs>
   <Tab zoneid="nwADhwG769" title="ArkClaw remaining days ≤ 7 days">
   <TabTitle>ArkClaw remaining days ≤ 7 days</TabTitle>
   
   If your ArkClaw has **7 days or fewer ** remaining before expiration, click "Renew now" in the upper\-right corner of the page to go to the renewal page.
   
   
   </Tab>
   <Tab zoneid="uDzzhPPXRA" title="ArkClaw expired">
   <TabTitle>ArkClaw expired</TabTitle>
   
   If your ArkClaw service has expired, click "Renew now" at the top of the dialog box to go to the renewal page.
   
   
   </Tab>
   <Tab zoneid="ibzMrl6GLv" title="ArkClaw remaining days > 7 days">
   <TabTitle>ArkClaw remaining days > 7 days</TabTitle>
   
   If your ArkClaw has more than 7 days remaining before expiration, follow these steps to go to the renewal page.
   
   
   1. Switch to the target ArkClaw page. In the upper\-right corner of the page, click the details icon.
   
   2. On the ArkClaw details page, select the "Status and usage" tab.
   
   3. Click the "Renew" button.
   
   
   </Tab>
   </Tabs>
   

3. Confirm the information of ArkClaw to be renewed and select a renewal duration.

   
   <Tabs>
   <Tab zoneid="bD3NOjEiYG" title="Bundle renewal (with ArkClaw and Agent Plan/Coding Plan)">
   <TabTitle>Bundle renewal (with ArkClaw and Agent Plan/Coding Plan)</TabTitle>
   
   When your ArkClaw and large model subscription package (Agent Plan or Coding Plan) are about to expire at the same time, you can complete renewal in one go via bundle renewal, without separate operations.
   
   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>
   
   
   
   * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">The ModelArk Agent Plan is a brand\-new upgraded subscription package built on the basis of the Coding Plan. It is integrated with a more comprehensive suite of model services and the exclusive Harness toolset. </div>
   
   
   * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">ModelArk Coding Plan is a developer‑tailored subscription service that supports multiple mainstream code models. It offers high cost‑effectiveness and frees you from Pay‑as‑you‑go token billing concerns.</div>
   
   
   
   
   |Configuration item |Description |
   |---|---|
   |ArkClaw & Coding Plan/Agent Plan |Confirm the ArkClaw and Agent Plan/Coding Plan specifications, expiration time, and other information for renewal. |
   |Renewal duration |Select a renewal duration for the package.<br><br><br>* Bundle renewal (including the **Agent Plan** large model subscription package): Supported renewal cycles include **1 month**, **6 months**, and **1 year**.<br><br>   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">warning   </div><br>   <br><br>   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-wrapper-indent="1">The validity period of an Agent Plan cannot exceed <strong>1</strong> year. Therefore, the sum of the selected renewal duration and the current remaining validity period cannot exceed 1 year. For example, if you have an Agent Plan with 3 months remaining validity period, you can only select a 1‑month or 6‑month renewal duration. The 1‑year option will exceed the maximum limit and is unavailable.   </div><br>   <br><br>* Bundle renewal (including the Coding Plan large model subscription package): Supported renewal cycles include **1 month**, **3 months**, and **6 months**.<br><br>   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">warning   </div><br>   <br><br>   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-wrapper-indent="1">The validity period of a Coding Plan cannot exceed 6 months. Therefore, the sum of the selected renewal duration and the current remaining validity period cannot exceed 6 months. For example, if your current Coding Plan expires in 2 months, you can select a renewal duration of <strong>1 month</strong> or <strong>3 months</strong>.   </div><br>    |
   
   
   
   
   
   </Tab>
   <Tab zoneid="PtA1DVCN7B" title="ArkClaw separate renewal (ArkClaw only)">
   <TabTitle>ArkClaw separate renewal (ArkClaw only)</TabTitle>
   
   Separate renewal for ArkClaw applies to scenarios where your Agent Plan/Coding Plan still has a long remaining validity period, or you only intend to renew ****ArkClaw.
   
   
   |Configuration item |Description |
   |---|---|
   |ArkClaw |Confirm the ArkClaw specification, expiration time, and other information for the renewal. |
   |Renewal duration |Select a renewal duration for your ArkClaw. You can choose to renew the ArkClaw for **1 month**, **6 months**, or **1 year**. |
   
   
   
   
   
   </Tab>
   </Tabs>
   

4. After reading and agreeing to the relevant ArkClaw service rules, click the "Pay now" button to enter the payment page.

5. On the payment page, confirm the order and amount information, select a payment method, and click "Pay now" to complete the payment.

6. After payment succeeds, you can click the "Enter chat" button to continue interacting with ArkClaw.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="1">At the top of the chat interface, you can check the remaining available days of ArkClaw at any time.   </div>
