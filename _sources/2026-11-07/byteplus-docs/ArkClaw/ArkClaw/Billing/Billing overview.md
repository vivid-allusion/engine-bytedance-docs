This topic describes package billing, first purchase discounts, unsubscription rules, and expiration instructions to help you understand the related billing rules and usage specifications.

<span id="y3BTU0jG"></span>
## Billing mode and promotion

ArkClaw uses a package\-based unified billing mode. You can choose a package based on your business requirements, and the expenses are settled based on the selected package.


* **Limited\-time first purchase discount promotion**

   When you purchase ArkClaw for the first time, you can enjoy a limited\-time special discount. For details, see [Limited-time first purchase discount promotion](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Limited-time_first-purchase_discount_promotion).

* **Long\-term discount packages for new purchase**

   When you purchase  ArkClaw via subscribing to certain packages, you can enjoy long\-term special discounts with stable discounted prices, without waiting for promotions. For details, see [Long-term first purchase discount package](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Long-term_discount_package_for_first-time_purchase).

* **Billing method and description**

   ArkClaw adopts a prepaid billing mode, requiring you to pay before using the service. For details, see [Billing method and description](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Billing_method_and_description).


<span id="o5TzWat8"></span>
## Instructions on renewal

To ensure your ArkClaw can be used continuously for a long period, [renew it](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Renewing_ArkClaw) before expiration to extend its validity.

<span id="C6XXHUtz"></span>
## Instructions on unsubscription

If you no longer need to use ArkClaw, you can [unsubscribe](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Unsubscribing_from_ArkClaw) from it.

* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">You can only submit an unsubscription request for a separately purchased ArkClaw package.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">You cannot unsubscribe from the combined package (with ArkClaw and Coding Plan Lite/Pro model subscription packages). For details, see <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Unsubscribing_from_ArkClaw">Instructions on unsubscription</a>.</div>

## Instructions on expiration

When your ArkClaw expires, it stops immediately. However, the platform reserves a resource retention period for you. Resources are automatically cleared after the period ends. [Learn more](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Activating_ModelArk_ArkClaw).

<span id="oomlPDyV"></span>
## Validity period

The validity period of ArkClaw is the duration you paid for (UTC+8 time). A billing cycle starts when the resources are delivered (accurate to the second) and ends at 23:59:59 on the expiration date.

For example, if you purchase 10 prepaid ArkClaw Standard at 14:00:00 on March 16, 2026, for a duration of 1 month, the billing cycle is from 14:00:00 on March 16, 2026, to 23:59:59 on April 16, 2026.
