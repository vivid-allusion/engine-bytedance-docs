If your ArkClaw obtained as a complimentary benefit through the ModelArk Playground expires, you can activate it by using the exclusive activation feature. After activation, you can continue using ArkClaw\-related features.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">You can also choose not to activate and directly purchase your dedicated ArkClaw to unlock all features. For details, see <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Creating_your_dedicated_ArkClaw_with_one_click">Obtaining your dedicated ArkClaw with one click</a>.</div>

## Limits

Only [ArkClaw ](https://console.byteplus.com/ark/region:ark+ap-southeast-1/experience/claw)obtained as a complimentary benefit with ModelArk Coding Plan subscriptions can be [renewed ](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Activating_ModelArk_ArkClaw)via activation after expiration.

<span id="M0fYM39X"></span>
## Activation rules

You qualify for the first purchase discount only if you activate the service for at least 1 month. If your current Coding Plan has less than 1 month remaining, you cannot apply the discount. We recommend that you [renew your Coding Plan](https://console.byteplus.com/ark/region:ark+cn-beijing/openManagement?LLM=%7B%7D&advancedActiveKey=subscribe) first.

For example, if your subscription expires in 3 days or less, a direct purchase will result in the loss of your discount entitlement. We recommend completing the renewal process first and applying the discount to activate a full month of discounted service.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">The Coding Plan validity period limits the ArkClaw subscription duration. Your ArkClaw cannot expire later than your Coding Plan. For more information, see <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Activating_ModelArk_ArkClaw">Activating ModelArk Playground ArkClaw</a>.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">You can apply the first purchase discount only once. If you apply it here, you cannot use it for future purchases of your <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Creating_your_dedicated_ArkClaw_with_one_click">dedicated ArkClaw</a>.</div>

## Activate ArkClaw

You can log in to the [ArkClaw management console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/management) for activation. For details, see [Activating ModelArk Playground ArkClaw](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Activating_ModelArk_ArkClaw).
