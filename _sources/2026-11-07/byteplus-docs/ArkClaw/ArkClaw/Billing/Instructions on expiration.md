You can view the instructions on expiration to check the validity period of your ArkClaw, understand the impact of expiration, plan renewal, and release resources. This helps you properly arrange usage and operations.

## Expiration rules

When your ArkClaw expires, it stops immediately, and its **associated resources are retained for 24 hours**. If you do not renew within this period, the system automatically deletes your data.

## View expiration time

You can view the ArkClaw package expiration time to plan renewals and prevent service interruptions when ArkClaw expires.


1. Log in to the [ArkClaw console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/welcome).

2. In the left navigation pane, select "ArkClaw Management".

3. You can view the expiration time directly on the corresponding ArkClaw card.
