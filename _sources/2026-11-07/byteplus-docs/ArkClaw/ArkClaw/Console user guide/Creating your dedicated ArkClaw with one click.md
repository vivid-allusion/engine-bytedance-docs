ArkClaw is a cloud\-based AI agent service provided by BytePlus for individual users. It allows you to deploy OpenClaw on the cloud with just one click, eliminating complex configurations and providing 24/7 online service for zero\-threshold access to OpenClaw.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning"><strong>Quota limit：</strong>A single account can create up to 50 ArkClaw instances (excluding the <strong>ArkClaw</strong> instances created in the ModelArk Playground). Currently, the quota cannot be increased.</div>

## Differences in ArkClaw creation methods

ArkClaw supports two creation methods: template\-based and template\-free. The differences and respective recommended scenarios are as follows:

<mark>
|Creation/purchase method |Differences |Recommended scenarios |
|---|---|---|
|Template\-based creation |Ready to use: Comes with a preset soul, core skills, and the corresponding permission rules. |**Recommended for beginners.** <br><br>Out\-of\-the\-box and applicable to high\-frequency, general\-purpose, and standardized business scenarios, without configuring from scratch. |
|Creation without a template |Does not include preset soul, skills, or permission rules. You need to set all configuration items manually. |**Suitable for professional users.** <br><br>Requires a clear understanding of your business logic, and applies to personalized, non\-standard, and custom scenarios. |
</mark>

## Procedure

<span id="UQEBIhvl"></span>
### **Bundle purchase (with an LLM subscription package)** 

A bundle purchase includes both ArkClaw and the ** Agent Plan** large model subscription package. It applies to scenarios where you have not subscribed to ModelArk **Agent Plan ** or your ModelArk **Agent Plan ** has expired. Bundle purchasers are also **eligible for additionalinclude additional discounts**.

> If you have subscribed to a **Agent Plan**, this package will not be displayed. You can purchase ArkClaw by referring to [Purchase ArkClaw separately (with an existing large model subscription package)](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Creating_your_dedicated_ArkClaw_with_one_click_?lang=en#F0e9F6CX).



1. Log in to the [ArkClaw console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw).

2. Click the "Create now" button to enter the configuration page.

3. Select the creation method as needed.

   * **Create now:**  Create an ArkClaw without a preset soul and skills. You can [manually configure a custom soul](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Configuring_soul), [select and associate the required skills](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Configuring_md_files), and perform other actions as needed.

   * **Create based on agent template**: You can quickly create ArkClaw via a preset template provided by the platform, without configuring from scratch.

      <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="2">You can also modify the skills and soul settings of ArkClaw created via a preset template. For details, refer to <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Learning_and_using_skills_-">Learning and using skills</a> and <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Configuring_soul">Configuring soul</a>.      </div>
      

4. Complete the purchase configuration based on the table below.

   
   |Configuration item |Description |
   |---|---|
   |Subscription duration |Select the subscription duration. Currently, only **1 month, 6 months**, and **1 year** are supported. You can select a subscription duration based on your needs. |
   |Package specification |ArkClaw offers three package editions: **Starter**, **Standard**, and **Premium**, which meet the needs in different scenarios. Select the ArkClaw edition you want to purchase.<br><br>The application scenarios for each edition are as follows. [Learn more](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/ArkClaw_specifications_and_applicable_scenarios).<br><br><br>* **Starter**: For basic personal agent use, meeting initial exploration and entry\-level needs.<br><br>* **Standard**: For personal workflow integration, meeting daily work and life needs.<br><br>* **Premium**: For scenarios such as One Person Company (OPC), with multi\-concurrent step reasoning and tool interaction capabilities. This edition currently only supports Coding Plan Pro. |
   |Subscribe to **Agent Plan** large model benefits |Check "Subscribe to **Agent Plan** large model benefits" and select "**Small**", "**Medium**", or "**Large**" from the drop\-down list.<br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div><br><br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">The applicable scenarios for different Agent Plan editions are as follows. Please select an edition as needed. .</div><br><br><br><br>* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">Small (Coding Plan Small): For medium\-intensity development tasks, suitable for most developers.</div><br><br><br>* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">Medium (Coding Plan Medium): For complex project development, suitable for developers with high\-intensity workloads.</div><br><br><br>* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">Large (Coding Plan Large): For ultra\-large projects, complex multi\-task collaboration and advanced intelligent business scenarios, suitable for professional teams and senior developers for in\-depth usage.</div><br> |
   |Automatic renewal |Select whether to enable automatic renewal upon expiration.<br><br><br>* **Enable automatic renewal**: If automatic renewal is enabled, ArkClaw will be automatically renewed for 1 month by default. The platform will deduct the fee for the next month of the current package when ArkClaw expires.<br><br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div><br><br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">If automatic renewal is enabled for your ArkClaw instance, automatic renewal is also enabled for the capacity package by default. 5. After reading and agreeing to the relevant ArkClaw service rules, click the "Pay now" button to enter the payment page.

6. On the payment page, confirm the order and amount information, select a payment method, and click "Pay now" to complete the payment.
