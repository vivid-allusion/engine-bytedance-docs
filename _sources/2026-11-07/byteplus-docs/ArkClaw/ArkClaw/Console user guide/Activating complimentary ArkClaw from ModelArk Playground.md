If the ArkClaw you obtained as a **complimentary** benefit from **ModelArk Playground** has expired, the service will stop running. After expiration, related data will **be retained for 7 days.**  During this period, you can quickly activate your ArkClaw in the **ArkClaw console** as instructed below to restore the service.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">The ArkClaw instance claimed or created in <a href="https://console.byteplus.com/ark/region:ark+cn-beijing/experience/claw">ModelArk Playground</a> can also be viewed and managed in the <a href="https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/welcome">ArkClaw console</a>. The default name is <strong>ArkClaw.</strong></div>


<span id="hgKlegLp"></span>
## Limits


* Only [ArkClaw](https://console.byteplus.com/ark/region:ark+cn-beijing/experience/claw) **granted with** an ModelArk Agent Plan/Coding Plan subscription can be activated.

* Any ArkClaw instance [created with one click in the ArkClaw console](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Creating_your_dedicated_ArkClaw_with_one_click_) **cannot be activated**. After the service expires, you can [renew](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Renewing_ArkClaw?lang=en) it to restore normal usage.

## Procedure


1. Log in to[ the ArkClaw console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/management).

2. Switch to the target ArkClaw page (default name: ArkClaw), and click the Claw details page icon in the upper\-right corner of the page.

3. On the ArkClaw details page, select the “Status and usage” tab, then click the “... \> Activate” button.
