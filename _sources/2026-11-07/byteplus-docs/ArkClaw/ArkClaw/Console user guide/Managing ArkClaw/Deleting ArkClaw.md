After your ArkClaw expires or is unsubscribed, the platform will retain the resources for 7 days. After the retention period, the system will automatically clear the related data and delete ArkClaw. If you no longer need to keep ArkClaw, you may actively delete it within the resource retention period. This will release unused resources in a timely manner and improve your experience.

<span id="DwFjpgEc"></span>
## Usage notes


* Only ArkClaw instances in **Package expired** or **Stopped** status can be manually deleted.

* Deleting ArkClaw will simultaneously and permanently delete all of its data. This operation is irreversible. Please proceed with caution.


<span id="LFxYcN3F"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [ArkClaw console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/).

2. Switch to the target ArkClaw page and click the Claw details page icon in the upper\-right corner.

3. Select the "Status and usage" tab, and click the "··· **\>**  Delete" button.

4. In the pop\-up window, confirm the ArkClaw information, check "I am aware of the relevant risks", and click "Confirm deletion" to complete the operation.
