If the current ArkClaw specification cannot meet your needs during use (for example, the cloud browser runs slowly), upgrade your ArkClaw specification for higher compute power, larger storage, and more features.

<span id="CB9a9zpx"></span>
## Usage notes


* Currently, only the specifications of **unexpired ** ArkClaw of the **Starter ** and **Standard** editions can be upgraded. ArkClaw specification downgrade is not supported.

* You can follow the instructions below to upgrade the specification for [entitled ArkClaw obtained from ModelArk Playground](https://console.byteplus.com/ark/region:ark+cn-beijing/experience/claw) (default name: **ArkClaw**):

   * Perform direct upgrade for entitled ArkClaw included with the Agent Plan Medium/Large/Max subscription if the subscription **is valid** or has expired **for less than 24 hours**. Otherwise, upgrade the specification via the [activation](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Activating_ModelArk_ArkClaw_) feature.

   * Upgrade the specification of entitled ArkClaw included with the Coding Plan Lite subscription via the [activation](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Activating_ModelArk_ArkClaw_) feature.

   * Perform direct upgrade for entitled ArkClaw included with the Coding Plan Pro subscription if the subscription is **valid** or **expired within 24 hours**. Otherwise, upgrade the specification via the [activation](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Activating_ModelArk_ArkClaw_) feature.

* To apply the new specification, the system will restart your ArkClaw instance. During this period, ArkClaw services will be temporarily interrupted. We recommend upgrading your ArkClaw during off\-peak hours to avoid impacting running tasks.

## Billing rules

<span id="WdCyvarw"></span>
### ArkClaw

Upgrade the specification of your ArkClaw purchased in the ArkClaw console​ by only paying the price difference between the new and old configurations for the remaining validity period. The specific calculation formula is as follows:


* **Payable amount for the upgrade order = Payable amount for new specification \- Remaining value of the previous specification**

   The calculation method for "Remaining value of the previous configuration" is as follows:

* **Remaining value of the previous specification = Value of original order × (Remaining duration of original order/Subscription duration of original order)** 

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">warning   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-wrapper-indent="1">The duration unit is <strong>accurate</strong> to seconds to ensure accurate calculation of the remaining value of the previous specification.   </div>
   

<span id="TKEHOGX5"></span>
### Entitled ArkClaw

When upgrading the specification of entitled ArkClaw included with your Agent Plan/Coding Plan Pro subscription in ModelArk Playground, you are required to pay the full fee for the target specification.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">After the specification upgrade, this entitled ArkClaw will be converted to an official ArkClaw, and you will no longer have free access during the validity period of your Coding Plan.</div>

## Procedure


1. Log in to the [ArkClaw console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/).

2. Switch to the target ArkClaw page and click the Claw details icon in the upper\-right corner.

3. Select the "Status and usage" tab and click the "Upgrade specification" button.

4. Select the target specification for upgrade, check the box to acknowledge the risks and agree to the relevant agreements, and click the "Pay now" button.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="1">For details and applicable scenarios of different ArkClaw specifications, refer to <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/ArkClaw_specifications_and_applicable_scenarios">ArkClaw specifications and applicable scenarios</a>.   </div>
   

5. On the payment page, confirm the order and amount information, select a payment method, and click "Pay now" to complete the payment.

6. Upon successful payment, the ArkClaw status changes to **Upgrading**.

7. Once the upgrade is completed, you can click the "Enter chat" button to continue interacting with ArkClaw.
