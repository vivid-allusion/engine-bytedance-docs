BytePlus incorporates industry best practices to preset exclusive scheduled tasks for partial agent templates, adapting to recurring business scenarios After you [add such a template to an agent team](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Building_agent_teams_with_one_click_), the system will automatically generate the scheduled tasks of the template. This eliminates tedious steps and allows you to use the template directly without manual configuration of scheduling rules.

<span id="HbzmezqC"></span>
## Prerequisites

This feature is only available for ArkClaw instances running version `ark-26.5.21` ** or later**. You can check your ArkClaw version and perform upgrades on the "Status and usage" tab of the ArkClaw details page. [Learn more](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Viewing_ArkClaw_status_and_usage_).

## Procedure

<span id="bUWKTdnX"></span>
### Step 1: View scheduled tasks preset in agent templates

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">Currently, only some agent templates have scheduled tasks preset.</div>



1. Log in to the [ArkClaw console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/).

2. Go to the target ArkClaw page.

3. In the left\-side navigation pane, click "Agent Center".

4. On the "Agent Gallery" page, select the target template and click the "Details" button.

5. View the configuration details of the scheduled tasks in the template and confirm that the current preset scheduled tasks meet your business requirements.


<span id="SgtGYIzY"></span>
### Step 2: Initiate and add scheduled tasks


1. Click the "Add and initiate task" button.

2. After scheduled tasks are added, click "Scheduled Tasks" in the left navigation pane to view the scheduled tasks automatically generated. You can manually enable or disable scheduled tasks here as needed. [Learn more](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Viewing_and_configuring_scheduled_tasks_9#LdJh3cTb).

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="1">The initial status (enabled or disabled) of scheduled tasks generated from templates is determined by the preset rules for the templates. After generation, some tasks may be enabled automatically, while tasks under certain scenarios are disabled by default. You can manually switch their status in the task list based on your business needs.   </div>

To edit, delete, or perform other actions on related scheduled tasks, refer to [Managing scheduled tasks](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ArkClaw/Viewing_and_configuring_scheduled_tasks_9#LdJh3cTb).
