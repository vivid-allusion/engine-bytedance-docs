You can add, modify the information about, or remove team members from your project after you create it.

<span id="EMrEBaYo"></span>
## Add a member


1. Log in to the [ArkClaw console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/welcome).

2. Switch to the target ArkClaw page. In the directory tree on the left, select "My tasks \> Target collaborative task".

3. In the dialog box, click the "Configure members" button.

4. On the **Project members** tab, click "Add member".

5. Configure the role, skills, model, and soul settings for the member as needed.

6. Click "Confirm" to complete the operation.


<span id="bHDWeyne"></span>
## Edit member information

You can modify the role, skills, and soul of a member. The information about the **project manager ** role cannot be modified.


1. In the dialog box, click the "Configure members" button.

2. On the **Project members** tab, click the "···  **\>**  Edit" button on the target member card.

3. In the pop\-up dialog box, modify the role, skills, or soul of the target member.

4. Click "Confirm" to complete the operation.


<span id="t3OgDdhi"></span>
## Remove a member

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">After you remove a member, the related task will stop, and you need to create a new conversation. Please proceed with caution.</div>



1. In the dialog box, click the "Configure members" button.

2. On the **Project members** tab, click the "···  **\>**  Remove" button on the target member card.

3. In the dialog box that appears, click "Confirm" to complete the operation.
