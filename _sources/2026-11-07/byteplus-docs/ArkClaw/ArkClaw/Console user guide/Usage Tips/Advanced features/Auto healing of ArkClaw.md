When your ArkClaw becomes unavailable due to tasks, setting adjustments, or other reasons, you can use the auto healing feature to attempt to restore it to the latest available state.

<span id="WSVyja2a"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to [the ArkClaw console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/), and switch to the target ArkClaw page.

2. In the upper\-right corner of the ArkClaw chat page, click "...  **\> ** Auto healing".

3. In the pop\-up window, click the "Confirm" button. BytePlus will attempt to repair ArkClaw to its latest available state.
