You can manually restart ArkClaw when you need to load new ArkClaw configuration or when ArkClaw responds slowly.

<span id="gSwJMVnE"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [ArkClaw console](https://console.byteplus.com/arkclaw/), and switch to the target ArkClaw page.

2. In the upper right corner of the ArkClaw chat page, click "...  **\>**  Restart".

3. In the pop\-up window, click "Confirm" to restart the ArkClaw service.
