# 1. Product Overview

The Customer Data Platform (Customer Data Platform) is a data platform for customer empowerment towards business growth, helping companies break data silos, establish unified customer and product profiles, empower companies to drive end\-to\-end marketing and in\-depth operations with data, and achieve digital transformation and growth.

\***This online documentation only explains the capabilities and operations of the VeCDP SaaS product**

# 4. Typical Application Scenarios

In the past, when companies engaged in marketing, they typically focused on the traditional funnel model. When the traffic dividend was exhausted, this method was clearly no longer sustainable. Companies need the ability to deeply serve customers, which is to use CDP for refined customer operations, shifting from traffic thinking to user thinking, and taking control of marketing initiatives.

Companies build user tags through CDP, segment users, and utilize group insight capabilities to discover significant group characteristics, deeply understand target user groups, and develop targeted operational strategies for users with different characteristics.

By reaching target users through various channels such as SMS, in\-site messages, Push, emails, etc., companies can promote personalized marketing or operational content to different user groups. After marketing campaigns, they collect data on the effects of outreach tasks, review the effectiveness based on clicks, UV, click\-through rates, conversion funnels, etc., iterate on strategies. Moreover, they can use BI tools to view the actual business conversion effects of corresponding marketing tasks, assisting in strategy optimization.

<span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_a00974d466ef884dda759f45f4eeaa78.png) </span>This refined operational chain mainly leverages the two major values of CDP:


1. **Accurate Positioning:**  Enhancing the understanding of target audience groups, accurately positioning user groups.

2. **Data\-Driven:**  Collecting effect data and attributing it to help operators clearly see the effect of different delivery tasks, enabling timely adjustments to delivery strategies.

<span id="_5-product-advantages"></span>
# 5. Product Advantages


1. **Data Integration Capability**：Supports integration of multiple subject IDs, automatically generates OneID, builds profiles for people and objects, and consolidates multi\-channel data.

2. **Open Product Ecosystem**：Interconnects with other BytePlus products at the application layer, expanding capabilities through tools like DataTester, DataFinder, DataWind, GMP, etc., to provide customers with an end\-to\-end digital and marketing solution.

3. **Robust Technical Capability**：High capacity for big data processing with excellent overall performance, supporting audience targeting and analysis of billion\-level data within minutes.

4. **Real\-Time Data Processing Capability**：Supports real\-time data ingestion and processing, enabling real\-time tag creation to meet downstream real\-time application needs.


\*Note: The data applied in customer data platform products is the customer's own data used in legal and compliant scenarios.
