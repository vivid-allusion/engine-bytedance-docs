# 2. Scenario Description

<span id="_2-1-精细化运营是什么？"></span>
## 2.1 What is Refined Operation?

Essentially, refined operation values every piece of acquired traffic, focuses more on effective utilization of traffic, emphasizes precision, pays more attention to retention and conversion, and attaches more importance to realizing the value of existing traffic. Therefore, paying more attention to the following four points:


* Precision Matching: Focus more on user segmentation, build a 360\-degree user profile, and match strategies based on the profile

* Continual Reach: aggregating multiple channels for more automated, sustained user reach.

* Data Loop: more diverse data analysis methods and dimensions, emphasizing the effective utilization and full exploitation of data value.

* Rapid Iteration: swiftly iterate and upgrade operational plans based on activity effectiveness to enhance overall operational efficiency.

## 2.2 Classic Business Process for Fine Operation

A classic business process for fine operations, including data analysis \-\> user segmentation \-\> designing operational strategies \-\> selecting target groups \-\> reaching user groups \-\> recovering effect data \-\> optimizing strategies guided by data to continuously improve conversion rates. In such a business process, the CDP product segments groups based on labels, achieving more precise, intelligent, and personalized reach to promote conversions and repeat purchases.

# 3. CDP+GMP Product Solutions

<span id="_3-1-产品联动，构建运营闭环"></span>
## 3.1 Product Collaboration, Building Operational Closure

**CDP** (Customer Data Platform): a platform empowering customer data across businesses for growth, helping companies break data silos, establish unified profiles of people and entities, empowering enterprises to drive end\-to\-end marketing and deep operations with data.

**GMP** (Growth Marketing Platform): a growth marketing platform that provides intelligent operational capabilities for precision operations to enterprise operational and marketing departments based on data and smart strategies.

<span>![图片](https://lf6-volc-editor.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/sop-public/upload_34a449629226053ac02b7a2c548af1a2) </span>For enterprise customers who have both GMP and CDP activated, they can utilize the linkage feature of GMP + CDP to meet the demands of refined operation scenarios: GMP leverages CDP's data capabilities for audience selection, data analysis, and insights. CDP, on the other hand, utilizes GMP for user reach, collaborating together to assist enterprise customers in closing the marketing loop and achieving "data\-driven growth".
