Customer Data Platform (referred to as CDP) serves as a mid\-tier customer\-oriented full\-domain data empowerment platform, connecting upstream and downstream, playing a critical role in various scenarios such as enterprise business decision\-making, management, and operations.

# Scenario One: Refined Operations

Develop differentiated operational strategies for groups with different value layers, reach users through multiple channels, and collect effect data.
