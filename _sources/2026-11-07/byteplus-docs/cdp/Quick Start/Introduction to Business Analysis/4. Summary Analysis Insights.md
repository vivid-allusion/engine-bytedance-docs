# 1. Comprehensive **analysis**

On the **Tag Analysis Overview** page, the overall characteristics of the crowd will be displayed by combining the UV (the proportion of this feature in the current crowd) and TGI (the significance of this feature compared to the total crowd).

**1) Comprehensive Analysis of UV & TGI Indicators**


1. CDP will automatically display the top five features with the highest combined UV & TGI. In actual business scenarios, we can manually decide which features to use to describe the target audience based on the coordinate graph.

2. From the graph, it can be seen that **purchasing channel \- offline stores, payment preference \- WeChat Pay** are the most significant features of this group, and **product style \- sporty, limited product consumption quantity \- medium** are also significant (located in the upper right corner of the coordinates).

3. Therefore, a preliminary assessment of this group: **tends to prefer purchasing limited edition sporty items from offline stores and directly uses WeChat or Alipay for payment, less inclined to use credit cards, indicating a higher purchasing power.** 


<span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_0dac3ec9f679833d1828e2050b145607.png) </span>

**2) Individual Analysis of TGI or UV Indicators**


1. You can choose to focus on TGI or UV indicators individually for feature analysis. If you wish to match features with appropriate audiences, focus on labels with high TGI; if you wish to match audiences with appropriate features, focus on labels with high UV.

2. As an example of this overview, we find that **product style\-ladylike style** has the highest TGI among **high\-price users**, so when we want to promote a ladylike style product, we should focus on targeting high\-price users.

3. For example, we found that **high average spending users** account for the highest proportion of **purchasing channel \- offline stores**. Therefore, when we want to advertise to this group, we can focus on channels like store posters.

# 2. Dimension Analysis

On the previous **Tag Analysis Overview** page, we could quickly grasp the overall characteristics of the current group. Based on this, by viewing the **Tag Analysis Details**, we can understand the specific distribution of this group across different tag dimensions.


* Each tag's analysis result can be downloaded for viewing in the form of **graphics or tables**.

* Support changing the chart display type, for example, **color preferences**, using a pie chart can quickly identify that **black, white, and gray** are the majority of solid colors.


**1) Analyze the style tags**


1. In the analysis, we found that the majority of customers in this group prefer sporty and comfortable styles. To further explore their color preferences within these styles, we used chart linkage, setting the filter conditions to "sporty" and "comfortable" styles. This allows us to observe the characteristic distribution of this group.

2. For individuals who prefer sports style and comfortable style, they also tend to lean towards **black, white, gray** in color selection for designs.

3. Combining other dimensions, we ultimately depict the segmentation characteristics of **high average spending users**: **preferring to purchase the star\-approved loose fit or new limited edition single\-color series offline at physical stores, with relatively high spending power and less forward consumption.**

# 3. Cross Analysis

Now that we have a relatively detailed understanding of this group's overall characteristics and distribution across various dimensions, we can conduct further exploration by using **Cross Analysis** to examine how different dimensions intersect.

Click on **Add Chart**, select **Cross Analysis**, and then choose row and column indicators respectively to view the distribution across intersecting dimensions.


1. For example, since we already know that the target group tends to prefer offline purchases, we might want to further explore the level of engagement in offline stores across different cities. By setting **City of Residence** as the row indicator and **Interaction Frequency** as the column indicator, we can create a new cross analysis.

2. The results show that the proportion of high\-frequency interactions is higher in **Beijing** and **Shanghai**, indicating that offline events held in stores located in these two cities are likely to be more effective.

3. Hovering over specific values allows you to select and export the corresponding subgroups covered by that cross\-label, enabling even more refined operations.

# 4. Summary Analysis

After completing the group analysis, we can summarize the characteristics of this customer segment as follows:

They prefer to shop in offline stores for **sporty or comfortable styles** featuring **solid\-colored**, **limited\-edition or new arrivals**, especially **celebrity\-endorsed items**. This group also demonstrates **strong purchasing power** and tends to **avoid overspending or buying on credit**. Additionally, they are more likely to **participate in in\-store events** in **Beijing and Shanghai**.
