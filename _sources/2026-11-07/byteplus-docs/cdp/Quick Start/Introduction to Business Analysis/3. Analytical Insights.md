<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Using created labels, circle and analyze the corresponding audience for insights.</div>



1. Hover over the **Analysis and Insights** module and select **Group Portrait**.

2. After entering the Group Portrait page, click on **New Report** at the top right of the new page, select **Private Domain** and **Person (User) Subject**.


<span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_a1db9b64966cc7dc5502933168b3be1d.png) </span>


3. Choose the tags that the target insight group needs to meet based on business requirements: this time, we hope to gain insights into the **high\-spending loyal customers** group, select **Loyal Customers\-Purchase** and **Average Order Value\-High** tags. When there are multiple conditions in a stage, you can freely choose the relationship between conditions as **AND/OR**.

4. Click on **Estimated Segmentation** to view the number of segments for that group.


<span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_fb35c0ca029eba0a7239bcbfc2c8b02e.png) </span>


5. Click on **Add Analysis Charts** to create a new analysis report. Since our goal is to understand customers' product preferences, select some tags under the **Purchase Preference** category, click confirm after completion, and start the analysis and insights.


<span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_26c78ff79fa65f7d681c1acf59a048c7.png) </span>


6. In the **Label Analysis Overview**, **Label Analysis Details**, and **Indicator Analysis Details** pages of the Group Portrait report, you can view the analysis and insights results. (See [4. 总结分析洞察](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/undefined/6800afc623e47705259af528)Details for more information)


<span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_a503da03352fac2a5fe753050cc952aa.png) </span>
