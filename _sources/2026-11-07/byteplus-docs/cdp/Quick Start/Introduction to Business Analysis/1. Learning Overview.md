# 1. Case Introduction


1. **Background:**  A certain manufacturer plans to launch a special offer campaign for its high\-spending loyal customers. To increase the participation and conversion rate of the campaign, before the event, the staff will send personalized marketing SMS to these loyal customers to boost their purchase intention.

2. **Task objectives:** 

   The manufacturer has already imported all user data in the form of labels into the CDP platform. Now, they hope to gain deep insights into user data through the labeling and analytical functions of CDP, understand their preferences and consumption traits, and ultimately generate interest in them through personalized pushes before the special offer campaign.

   
   | |**Objective** |**Task** |**Related Product Features** |
   |---|---|---|---|
   |Step One |Target high\-value loyal customer groups |1. Create loyal **user group** labels to select the audience<br><br>2. Create **customer spending type** labels to segment the audience |Labeling System \- Lifecycle Labels (AIPL Model), Rule Labels |
   |Step Two |Obtain the preferences and consumption characteristics of this group |After obtaining the high\-value loyal customer group, conduct **group analysis** to discover the characteristics of this group. |Analytical Insights \- Audience Insights<br><br><br>* Label Analysis Overview, Label Analysis Details, Chart Display/Filtering/Linkage, Cross Analysis) |
   |Step Three |Push personalized marketing messages |Design marketing SMS copies based on characteristics of this group and push suitable product marketing SMS to the group through GMP, leading to user orders. |[增长营销平台GMP](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/undefined/672484e558c46e0316c898e1) |
