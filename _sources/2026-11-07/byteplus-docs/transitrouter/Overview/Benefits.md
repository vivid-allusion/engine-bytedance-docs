## Flexible networking

Transit Router (TR) delivers extensive routing capabilities, such as association forwarding, static routing, route learning, route policy, forwarding policy, and QoS management. It effectively controls the route propagation scope and enables routing rules based on your demands on interconnection and isolation, helping you implement complex networking scenarios flexibly and effortlessly.

<span id=".6LaF5by65omp5bGV"></span>
## Strong scalability

Combined with the flexible scaling capabilities of the underlying cluster, the TR service helps you quickly and effortlessly expand and modify your services by attaching the same Virtual Private Cloud (VPC) to multiple TRs.

<span id=".6auY5Y-v6Z2g5oCn"></span>
## High reliability

TR adopts a high availability cluster and supports multi\-availability zone deployment and dynamic routing protocols. Through active\-active or multi\-active deployment patterns, along with load balancing and dynamic routing among multiple links, it ensures business continuity effectively.
