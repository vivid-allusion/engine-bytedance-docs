## Instructions

Under the Recipient account, only cross\-account peering attachments with the status **Pending acceptance** support rejection.

<span id="MMRgTp5p"></span>
## Procedure


1. Use the Recipient account to log in to [the Transit Router console](https://console.byteplus.com/tr/transitRouter).

2. Click the name of the target TR instance.

3. In the section below **Basic Information**, select the **Inter\-region attachment ** tab.

4. Click **Reject** to the right of the target cross\-account peering attachment, and confirm the resource information.

6. Click **Reject** to complete the action.
