## Prerequisites


* The traffic queue policy is not used by an inter\-region attachment. If in use, disable the traffic queue policy for the inter\-region attachment first.

* No custom queues exist in the traffic queue policy. If present, delete the custom queues first.


<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to [the Transit Router console](https://console.byteplus.com/tr/transitRouter).

2. Click the name of the target Transit Router (TR) instance.

3. In the section below **Basic information**, select the **Traffic queue policy** tab.

4. Click **Delete** to the right of the target traffic queue policy and confirm resource information.

5. Click **Delete** to complete the action.
