## Instructions

A forwarding policy cannot be deleted while it is associated with a network instance attachment. To delete, first [disable the forwarding policy](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/transitrouter/Enabling-Disabling-Modifying-forwarding-policy).

<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to [the Transit Router console](https://console.byteplus.com/tr/transitRouter).

2. Click the name of the target TR instance.

3. In the section below **Basic information**, select the **Forwarding policy** tab.

4. Click **Delete** to the right of the target route policy, and confirm resource information.

5. Click **Delete** to complete the action.
