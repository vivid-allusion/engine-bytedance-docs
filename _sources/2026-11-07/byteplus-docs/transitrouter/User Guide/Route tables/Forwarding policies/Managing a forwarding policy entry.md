## Scenarios

You can add or delete a forwarding policy entry, or modify an existing entry as needed.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">If a forwarding policy is associated with a network instance attachment, service traffic forwarding may be affected by adding, deleting, or modifying its forwarding policy entry. Proceed with caution.</div>


<span id=".5re75Yqg6L2s5Y-R562W55Wl5p2h55uu"></span>
## Add a forwarding policy entry


1. Log in to [the Transit Router console](https://console.byteplus.com/tr/transitRouter).

2. Click the name of the target Transit Router (TR) instance.

3. In the section below **Basic information**, select the **Forwarding policy** tab.

4. Click **Edit** to the right of the target forwarding policy.

5. Click **Add policy entry** to add a new entry. For parameter description, see [Creating a forwarding policy](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/transitrouter/Creating-a-forwarding-policy).

6. Click **OK** to complete the action.


<span id=".5L-u5pS56L2s5Y-R562W55Wl5p2h55uu"></span>
## Modify a forwarding policy entry


1. Log in to [the Transit Router console](https://console.byteplus.com/tr/transitRouter).

2. Click the name of the target TR instance.

3. In the section below **Basic information**, select the **Forwarding policy** tab.

4. Click **Edit** to the right of the target forwarding policy.

5. Click **Edit** to the right of the target policy entry, and modify the entry as needed. For parameter description, see [Creating a forwarding policy](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/transitrouter/Creating-a-forwarding-policy).

6. Click **OK** to complete the action.


<span id=".5Yig6Zmk6L2s5Y-R562W55Wl5p2h55uu"></span>
## Delete a forwarding policy entry


1. Log in to [the Transit Router console](https://console.byteplus.com/tr/transitRouter).

2. Click the name of the target TR instance.

3. In the section below **Basic information**, select the **Forwarding policy** tab.

4. Click **Edit** to the right of the target forwarding policy.

5. Click **Delete** to the right of the target entry and confirm the resource information.

6. Click **Delete** to complete the action.
