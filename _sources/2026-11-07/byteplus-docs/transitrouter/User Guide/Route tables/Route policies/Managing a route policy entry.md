## Scenarios

You can add or delete a route policy entry, or modify an existing entry as needed.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">If a forwarding policy is associated with a Transit Router (TR) route table, service traffic forwarding may be affected by adding, deleting, or modifying its route policy entry. Proceed with caution.</div>


<span id=".5re75YqgL-S_ruaUuei3r-eUseetlueVpeadoeebrg=="></span>
## Add or modify a route policy entry


1. Log in to [the Transit Router console](https://console.byteplus.com/tr/transitRouter).

2. Click the name of the target TR instance.

3. In the section below **Basic information**, select the **Route policy** tab.

4. Click **Edit** to the right of the target route policy, and add or modify an entry as needed. For parameter description, see [Creating a route policy](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/transitrouter/Creating-a-routing-policy).

   * **Add a route policy entry**: Click **Add policy entry** to add a new entry.

   * **Modify a route policy entry**: Click **Edit** to the right of the target entry to modify it as needed.

5. Click **Create** to complete the action.


<span id=".5Yig6Zmk6Lev55Sx562W55Wl5p2h55uu"></span>
## Delete a route policy entry


1. Log in to [the Transit Router console](https://console.byteplus.com/tr/transitRouter).

2. Click the name of the target TR instance.

3. In the section below **Basic information**, select the **Route policy** tab.

4. Click **Edit** to the right of the target route policy, and delete your target entry.

5. Click **Delete** to the right of the target entry and confirm the resource information.

6. Click **Delete** to complete the action.
