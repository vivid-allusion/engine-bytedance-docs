## Instructions


* After a TR flow log is deleted, the associated log project and log topic are no longer managed by the Transit Router (TR), but they are not deleted.

* After a TR flow log is deleted, TR stops generating new log records. However, existing log records are retained for you to view.


<span id="k3aq2rTf"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to [the Transit Router console](https://console.byteplus.com/tr/transitRouter).

2. Click the name of the target TR instance.

3. In the zone below **Basic information**, select the **Flow Log** tab.

4. Click **Delete** to the right side of the target flow log.

5. Click **Delete** to complete the action.
