## Scenarios

With integration of the Network Advisor service, Transit Router (TR) offers analysis capabilities on cross\-region traffic. After enabling the traffic analysis feature, you can view cross\-region traffic data in different dimensions for all inter\-region attachments. This helps you understand service situations and quickly locate issues.


* Trend chart of outbound traffic from the specified region.

* Trend chart of traffic from the specified region to another region.

   * Top 10 region pairs with the largest cross\-region traffic volume.

   * Top 10 source/destination IP pairs that consume the most cross\-region bandwidth.

## Instructions


* After an inter\-region attachment is created, the traffic analysis feature is disabled by default.

* Once the traffic analysis feature is disabled, you cannot view cross\-region traffic data for inter\-region attachments. Proceed with caution.
