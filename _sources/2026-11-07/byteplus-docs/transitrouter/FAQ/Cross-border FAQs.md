## What is cross\-border service?

The cross\-border service supported by Transit Router (TR) refers to network interconnection services based on cross\-border links operated by China Unicom or China Telecom. Currently, it only supports China Mainland to Asia Pacific.

<span id=".6Leo5aKD5pyN5Yqh55qE6LS555So5aaC5L2V5pSv5LuY77yf5pSv5LuY57uZ6LCB77yf"></span>
## How are cross\-border service fees paid? To whom?

TR cross\-border service fees can be paid directly in the BytePlus console. BytePlus collects the fees on behalf of and pays them to China Unicom or China Telecom.

<span id=".6Leo5aKD5bim5a695YyF5pSv5oyB6ZmN6YWN5ZCX77yf"></span>
## Does the cross\-border bandwidth package support downgrades?

Yes, but refunds are not supported. 

<span id=".6Leo5aKD5bim5a695YyF5pSv5oyB6YCA6K6i5ZCX77yf"></span>
## Does the cross\-border bandwidth package support unsubscription?

Yes. However, refunds are not supported.
