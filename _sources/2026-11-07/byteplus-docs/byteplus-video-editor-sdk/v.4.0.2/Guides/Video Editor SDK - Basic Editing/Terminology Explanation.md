|**Term** |**Definition** |
|---|---|
|Capture |An operation of capturing images from the camera device. |
|Video recording |An operation of exporting the captured images to the file in the specified format. |
|Track |An entity that contains clips. Tracks can be categorized into video tracks and audio tracks and can be extended along with the increase of clips. |
|Clip |An entity that contains media content. Clips can be categorized into video clips and audio clips. Video clips can be either images or videos. |
|Timeline |A timeline entity for video editing. A timeline consists of multiple audio or video tracks, with its length determined by the length of the video or audio tracks. |
|Seek |The operation of rendering the image on a specified position and updating the timestamp on the timeline. |
|Transition |An effect of switching the playback from one clip to another clip. After transition is added, the clip may have an overlap effect in the transition period. |
|trimIn |The start point for trimming a clip. |
|trimOut |The end point for trimming a clip. |
|sequenceIn |The start point of a clip on a timeline. |
|sequenceOut |The end point of a clip on a timeline. |
