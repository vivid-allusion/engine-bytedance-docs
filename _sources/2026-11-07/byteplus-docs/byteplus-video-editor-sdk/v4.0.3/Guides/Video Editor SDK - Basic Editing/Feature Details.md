### (12) keyframe

<span id="6329cedc"></span>
#### **1. What’s a keyframe?** 

A keyframe is a frame that indicates a key action of a role or an object in movement. To put it simply, a keyframe on a timeline can be understood as the beginning or turning point of an action or animation and can be represented by a motion or mark on the timeline. The values of adjustable effect parameters of each keyframe such as position, size, and transparency at that position on the timeline are recorded.

<span id="218b1b25"></span>
#### **2. Application scope of keyframes**


* Keyframes are applicable for video tracks, picture tracks, audio tracks, text tracks (excluding text templates), and sticker tracks.


<span id="82dc4d53"></span>
#### **3.Basic logic of keyframes**


1. Between two keyframes in a fragment, each effect parameter recorded by the keyframes changes from one value to another smoothly and evenly.

2. To indicate a movement or change of assets, at least two keyframes are needed.

3. Keyframes take effects only for the currently selected asset fragment and don’t support multi\-track application.


<span id="ca29fe0f"></span>
#### **4. Benefits of keyframes**

In video production, keyframes can make your video more animated and visually exciting by showing dynamic change processes of audio and pictures. By providing adjustable curves, keyframes allow you to achieve various effects like in professional video editing applications such as Adobe After Effects and Final Cut Pro. For example, you can adjust the brightness, move or zoom objects in and out, set color gradation, freeze frames, fade audio in and out, or switch pictures. You can also set transitions, subtitle effects, or mattes.

* Undo a keyframe\-related operation：Select a keyframe:\*\* After you add a keyframe, the Undo icon becomes highlighted. Click the keyframe. The keyframe is selected, and the keyframe icon becomes highlighted and changes to a deletion (\-) icon. After you click this icon, the keyframe is deleted from the track and the icon changes back to an addition (+) icon. **Undo an operation:**  Click the Undo icon. The preview panel displays a toast reading of “Succeeded in undoing the operation.” The keyframe adding operation in the previous step is canceled, and both the keyframe icon and the keyframes on the track return to their status in the previous step. The Redo icon becomes highlighted. **After the keyframe adding operation is undone,**  the Redo icon becomes highlighted. The added keyframe disappears. The keyframe icon changes back to an addition (+) icon.

* Redo the keyframe\-related operation：Click the Redo icon. The preview panel displays a toast reading of “Succeeded in redoing the operation.” The undo operation in the previous step is canceled, and both the keyframe icon and the keyframes on the track return to their status before the undo operation. The Undo icon becomes highlighted.
