# I. Overview

BytePlus Video Editor (VE) SDK is a media processing SDK independently developed by ByteDance. VE SDK is widely used in ByteDance's own apps (e.g., TikTok, Douyin, CapCut, Xigua Video, FaceU, Ulike). It is also a standard SDK solution that can help business clients quickly develop advanced short video features at low costs.

VE SDK enables a wide range of media processing capabilities, including video recording, video editing, exporting, and can work with BytePlus Effects SDK to provide a wealth of special effects (2D/3D stickers based on face/body/background segmentation, and beauty effects/filters, etc.).

## 1. Core strengths

* Offers a complete set of features, including video recording, media editing, portrait retouching, export, and easier video creation tools.


<span id="ca75f39a"></span>
### • Professional video recording and editing capabilities


* ByteDance's self\-developed HD video recording, editing, and filter capabilities are proven by ByteDance's entire portfolio of apps, showcasing the technological prowess underlying VE SDK.

### • Best\-in\-class video effects in the industry


* Covers all advanced visual effects of Douyin, CapCut, Xigua Video, and other relevant apps in ByteDance's app arsenal, coupled with a massive asset library.

### • Smart visual production


* Years of algorithm training and iteration enables intelligent generation of sound library, thumbnails, and template editing, making video production faster and more efficient.

* Provide an asset consumption SDK with templates as the main assets, and develop mobile tools for exploring and producing assets to meet the needs of customers for "self\-produced and self\-consumed".
