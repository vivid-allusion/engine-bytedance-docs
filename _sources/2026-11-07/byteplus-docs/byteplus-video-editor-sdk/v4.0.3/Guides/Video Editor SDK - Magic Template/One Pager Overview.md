Magic Templates is a template\-based video editing tool that helps you create videos efficiently with minimal expense. It is independently developed by the Intelligent Creation team of BytePlus. It provides templates for you to choose from and helps you create a similar video with your own images and video fragments. You can also manage and share your videos.
