# I. Overview

<span id="What Are VE SDK and Magic Template?"></span>
## What Are VE SDK and Magic Template?

VE SDK (Video Editor SDK) is a media processing solution independently developed by the Intelligent Creation team of BytePlus. It is widely used in many world\-popular apps, such as TikTok, CapCut, Xigua Video, FaceU, and Ulike. It also provides a standard SDK solution that can help business clients quickly develop advanced short video features at low costs.

Magic Template is a template\-based video editing tool that helps you create videos efficiently with minimal expense. It provides templates for you to choose from and helps you create a similar video with your own images and video fragments. You can also manage and share your videos.

## 3.3 Select Assets

<span id="3.3.1 Open the Asset Selection Page"></span>
### 3.3.1 Open the Asset Selection Page

Choose a template, tap Create New Project, and the asset selection page opens.

<span>![图片](https://p-vcloud.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-em5hxbkur4/71e76363e9d74f01b39280970e764634~tplv-em5hxbkur4-noop.image) </span>

<span id="3.3.2 Preview Assets"></span>
### 3.3.2 Preview Assets

Selected assets will be displayed in thumbnails at the bottom\-left corner of the page, each with its time duration.

<span>![图片](https://p-vcloud.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-em5hxbkur4/7252bc57f97144438ac49fe726340395~tplv-em5hxbkur4-noop.image) </span>

<span id="3.3.3 Upload Assets&Remove an Asset"></span>
### 3.3.3 Upload Assets&Remove an Asset


* Tap the + icon at the top\-right corner of an asset to upload it. You can select both images and videos. You can also select multiple assets at a time.

* Supported video formats: .mov, .mp4, .avi, .flv, .asf, .rm, .mpeg, .wmv, and .mkv. Supported image formats: .jpeg, .jpg, and .png.

* To remove a selected asset, tap the x icon at the top\-right corner of its preview thumbnail. Then, you can select another asset.

<span id="3.5 Combine Assets"></span>
## 3.5 Combine Assets


* After editing, you can select the frame rate and resolution in the export settings area on top. Tap Done to combine assets. A prompt will indicate the progress in percentage.

> Supported frame rates: 25FPS, 30FPS, 50FPS, and 60FPS.

> Supported resolutions: 540P, 720P, 1080P, and 4K.


* You can find the saved final video in your phone album.
