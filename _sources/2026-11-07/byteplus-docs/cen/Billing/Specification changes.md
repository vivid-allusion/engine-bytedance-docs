After a Cloud Enterprise Network (CEN) bandwidth plan is created, you can modify its bandwidth capacity based on your actual requirements. Modifying the bandwidth will result in changes to the billing details of your CEN.

**Upgrade**: Increase bandwidth. Upgrading​ does not affect running services but increases costs.

**Downgrade**: Decrease bandwidth. Downgrading​ may affect running services if the reduced bandwidth is lower than current service requirements. Proceed with caution.

## Modifying the bandwidth

|Billing method |Supported action |Billing Change Description |
|---|---|---|
|Subscription |* Increase bandwidth<br><br>* Decrease bandwidth |* When you increase the bandwidth of a Subscription bandwidth plan, you need to pay the corresponding upgrade fee.<br><br>* When you decrease the bandwidth of a Subscription bandwidth plan, the corresponding downgrade fee will be refunded. |
