At seven days, three days, and one day before, and on the expiration date for Subscription bandwidth packages, the platform will send text messages, emails, or onsite messages based on your settings to notify you that bandwidth packages are about to expire. To avoid disruption to your business, renew your bandwidth package promptly when receving the notification.

If you renew before expiration suspension (within 12 hours after expiration), the start time of the renewed term is the expiration time of the previous term. If you renew after expiration suspension but before expiration reclaim, the start time of the renewed term is the actual renewal time.

## Manual renewal

Manual renewal is available at any time before the bandwidth package is reclaimed upon expiration.

Procedures:


1. Log in to the [CEN console](https://console.byteplus.com/cen/EnterpriseNetwork).

2. In the left navigation tree, select **Bandwidth Package**.

3. Click **Renewal fee** on the right side of the target Bandwidth Package.

4. Select the purchase duration and check the agreement.

5. Click **Save** and complete the payment according to the interface prompts.

   The renewal is completed immediately after payment is successfully processed.


<span id=".6Ieq5Yqo57ut6LS5"></span>
## Automatic renewal

You can check the **automatic renewal** option when creating a subscription bandwidth package or enable automatic renewal before the subscription expires. 

After enabling automatic renewal, if you have available coupons in your account, the system will prioritize using them for renewal.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">For resources with automatic renewal enabled, you can also manually renew them at any time. Manual renewal does not invalidate automatic renewal. The system will deduct fees 7 days before the updated expiration date of the resource.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">You cannot enable or modify auto\-renewal settings for a bandwidth package that has already expired.</div>
