After a Subscription bandwidth package expires, the platform reserves related resources for a certain period. During this grace period, you can renew or unsubscribe this bandwidth package. After renewal, you can continue use the package. While the resource is immediately released upon unsubscription. If you perform no actions during the grace period, the bandwidth package resources will be forcibly released after this period and cannot be recovered.

> **tip**

> The platform sends resource expiration reminders by SMS, email, or internal messages 7 days, 3 days, 1 day before, and on the expiration date of the subscription bandwidth package, according to your reminder settings. Please stay informed.

**Expiration stage description**

* Expiration time: At 23:59:59 on the expiration day.

* Suspension time: 12 hours after expiration.

* Release time: 14 days and 12 hours after suspension.

For a Subscription bandwidth package that has expired but has not yet been reclaimed, if you are certain you will not use it further, you can delete it directly. Upon deletion, the bandwidth package will be immediately released, and all data will be irrecoverable. Please thoroughly assess the impact on your current services and proceed with caution.
