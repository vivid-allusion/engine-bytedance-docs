This topic describes the impact of an overdue payment on your bandwidth packages. [View Available Balance](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/6269/100429#%E6%9D%BF%E5%9D%97%E8%AF%B4%E6%98%8E)

> **tip**

> Pay\-as\-you\-go Bandwidth Package is in the beta stage. If you need a trial, please contact your account manager.

## Pay\-as\-you\-go

An overdue account balance will lead to the suspension, and eventual release, of Pay\-As\-You\-Go bandwidth packages. The platform has set a grace period for resources. Within the grace period, the resources are retained and you can continue using the resources after you recharge your account and clear overdue payments. No impact on the features of resources. If the overdue payment is not settled beyond the resource retention period, the bandwidth package will be forcibly reclaimed, and all associated data will be irrecoverable. Regardless of whether your instances are suspended or released, you shall settle the issued bills based on the actual.

> **tip**

> Based on Your settings, the platform will send you reminders via SMS, email, or internal message during stages such as bandwidth package arrears, service suspension due to arrears, and resource release due to arrears. The specific suspension time is subject to the actual situation.

## Subscription

An overdue payment does not affect active Subscription resources under your account that have not yet expired. For more details, see [Expiration](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/cen/Expiration).
