This topic describes the unsubscription feature for Subscription bandwidth packages.

## Unsubscription overview

If a CEN cross\-region bandwidth package is no longer required due to business changes before its expiration date, you may choose to unsubscribe it. After unsubscription, the associated resources will be automatically deleted. Unsubscription is not supported after the bandwidth package is suspended upon expiration. You can manually delete the instance to release resources. For procedure, see [Deleting a bandwidth package](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/cen/CEN-bandwidth-packages-04).

CEN bandwidth packages support two unsubscription types: 7\-day free cancellation and Non\-7\-day cancellation.


* 7\-day free cancellation: Full refunds are available within 7 days after order payment, regardless of reason. Each account is limited to one 7\-day free cancellation CEN bandwidth package cancellation per calendar year. For more unsubscription rules, see[7-day unconditional refund rules](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-unsubscription-management#i-seven-day-unconditional-refund-policy).

* Non\-7\-day free cancellation: Refunds will be issued based on the remaining value of the actual payment amount. Used coupons are non\-refundable. For the detailed refund formula, see[Non-7-day unconditional refund rules](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-unsubscription-management#ii-non-seven-day-refund-policy).
