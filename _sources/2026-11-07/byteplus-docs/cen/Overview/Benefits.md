## Cloud\-Network Interconnectivity

Cloud Enterprise Network (CEN) enables any\-to\-any connectivity between network instances within the same region or across different regions. This includes direct private connections between different VPCs, different on\-premises IDCs, as well as between VPCs and IDCs.

## High\-efficiency transmission

CEN provides low\-latency, high\-quality transmission capabilities. By interconnecting any two points through CEN, traffic bypasses the internet, eliminating potential latency and significantly enhancing transmission efficiency.

## Easy to use

You can rapidly establish an interconnected network spanning multiple VPCs and IDCs across regions in just three simple steps. The service is ready to use immediately, offering a convenient and highly efficient solution.

## Real\-time monitoring

After integrating CEN with the CloudMonitor service, various business metrics can be monitored in real time. Through customizable metric thresholds and alert notifications, anomalies are detected promptly to ensure stable business operations.
