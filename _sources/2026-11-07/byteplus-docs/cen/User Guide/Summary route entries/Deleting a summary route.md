## Scenarios

You can see this procedure to delete summary routes that are no longer needed.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">The summary route entry is currently in beta stage. If you need a trial, please contact your account manager.</div>


<span id=".5pON5L2c6K-05piO"></span>
## Action description

Before deleting a summary route, please ensure that the switch routing configuration on your on\-premises IDC is complete to prevent connectivity loss between the VPC and the on\-premises IDC after the summary route is deleted. For the specific operation, see the [Summary route configuration best practices](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/cen/Summary-route-entry-configuration-practice).

<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Procedures


1. Log in to the [CEN console](https://console.byteplus.com/cen/EnterpriseNetwork).

2. Click the target CEN instance name.

3. In the **Summary route** tab, click **Delete** on the right side of the target summary route entry.

4. Click **Delete** to complete the action.
