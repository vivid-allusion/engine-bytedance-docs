## Scenarios

By configuring alert policies for your Cloud Enterprise Network (CEN), you can promptly detect exceptions in its monitoring metrics, ensuring stable and smooth operation of your services. You can customize alert policies for CEN resources, or use the preset alert templates provided by the system.

<span id=".5YmN5o-Q5p2h5Lu2"></span>
## Prerequisites

IAM users may not be able to directly configure alert rules. If you need to configure an alert rule, see [Why are IAM users unable to view monitoring data](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/clb/Log-and-monitoring-faq#.5Li65LuA5LmI5a2Q55So5oi35peg5rOV5p-l55yL55uR5o6n5pWw5o2u77yf) to grant the IAM user CloudMonitor management permissions.

## Procedures


1. Log in to the [CloudMonitor console](http://console.volcengine.com/cloud-monitor/overview).

2. In the left navigation pane, select **Alert Center \> Alert template**.

3. Select the **Preset alert template** tab.

4. In the search box, specify the search condition name and input **CEN** to find the target template.

5. You can click the name of **CEN preset alert template** to view the detailed rule information for triggering alerts and confirm whether the template meets your alert needs.

6. Click the switch in the **One\-click enable** column on the right side of the template list or **Enable** on the template details page to enable the alert template.

7. In the **Configure contact group** pop\-up window, specify the channel information where you want to receive alert notifications, and click **Apply** to complete the configuration.

8. After one\-click enable, the account will automatically generate and enable alert policies; after disabling one\-click enable, all alert policies generated by the preset alert template will be deleted immediately. For more details, see Using the Alert Center.
