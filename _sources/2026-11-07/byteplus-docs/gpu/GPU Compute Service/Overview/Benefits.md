## Superior performance

GPU instances boast characteristics such as high concurrency, high throughput, and low latency. Their performance in scientific computing is significantly superior to that of traditional architectures, while offering distinct advantages in scenarios like image processing.

<span id=".5L6_5o23566h55CG"></span>
## Easy management

GPU instances adopt the same management and operation methods as ECS instances, eliminating the need for repeated logins and ensuring ease of use. Clear guidelines for GPU driver installation and deployment reduce the learning curve.

<span id=".5a6J5YWo6Ziy5oqk"></span>
## Security protection

Resources are fully isolated between different users to safeguard your data security. Additionally, GPU instances inherit the same security foundation as ECS instances. They integrate with Cloud Monitor to deliver comprehensive network monitoring services, safeguarding your network.

<span id=".6LS555So5L2O5buJ"></span>
## Cost\-effectiveness

There is no need to pre\-deploy hardware resources, eliminating additional costs associated with hardware upgrades and reducing infrastructure investment. Currently, GPU instances fully support Subscription and Pay\-as\-you\-go billing methods, allowing you to select a suitable billing method based on your needs.
