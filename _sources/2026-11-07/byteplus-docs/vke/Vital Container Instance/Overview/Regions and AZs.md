This topic lists the regions where VCI is available, corresponding region IDs, and availability zones (AZs) within each region.

A Region refers to a physical data center.  Once resources are successfully created in a specific region, they cannot be relocated to another region. For details, see [VKE regions and AZs](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/vke/Regions-and-Availability-Zones).
