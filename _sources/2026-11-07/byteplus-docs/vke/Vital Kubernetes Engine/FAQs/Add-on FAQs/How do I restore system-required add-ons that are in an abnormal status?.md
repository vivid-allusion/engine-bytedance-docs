## Issue description

If system\-required add\-ons enter the **Fail** state due to improper operations, such as accidentally deleting their pods or YAML files, you cannot restore these add\-ons by uninstalling and reinstalling them.

<span id=".6Kej5Yaz5pa55rOV"></span>
## Solution

To prevent clusters from being unavailable caused by improper operations on system\-required add\-ons, you cannot uninstall system\-required add\-ons by yourself. Contact online customer service or [submit a ticket](https://console.byteplus.com/workorder/create?step=2&SubProductID=P00000210) to obtain technical support.
