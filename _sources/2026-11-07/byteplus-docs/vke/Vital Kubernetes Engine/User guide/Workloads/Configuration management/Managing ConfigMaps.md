After ConfigMaps have been created or added, you can manage them on the ConfigMap page. This topic describes how to manage ConfigMaps in the VKE console.

<span id=".5pu05paw6YWN572u6aG5"></span>
## Updating ConfigMaps

You can update ConfigMaps. After you update a ConfigMap, the new ConfigMap settings take effect. To do so, follow the steps below:


1. Log in to [VKE console](https://console.byteplus.com/vke/overview). In the left navigation bar, select **Cluster**.

2. In the cluster list, click the name of the target cluster. In the left navigation bar, click **Configurations** \> **ConfigMap**.

3. In the ConfigMap list, find the ConfigMap to update, and in the **Actions** column, click  **`...`**   \> **Update**.

4. Configure parameters as needed. For details, see [Creating ConfigMaps](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/vke/Creating-a-ConfigMap).


<span id=".57yW6L6R6YWN572u6aG5eWFtbA=="></span>
## Editing ConfigMap YAML files

You can change a ConfigMap by editing its YAML file. To do so, follow the steps below:


1. Log in to the [VKE console](https://console.byteplus.com/vke). In the left navigation bar, select **Cluster**.

2. In the cluster list, click the name of the target cluster. The cluster management page opens. In the left navigation bar, click **Configurations** \> **ConfigMap**.

3. In the ConfigMap list, find the ConfigMap to edit, and in the **Actions** column, click  **`...`**   \> **Edit Yaml**.

4. In the pop\-up window, edit the content of the YAML file. Click **Confirm** when you finish.


<span id=".5a-85Ye66YWN572u6aG5"></span>
## Exporting ConfigMaps

You can export a ConfigMap to your local storage by exporting its YAML configuration file. To do so, follow the steps below:


1. Log in to the [VKE console](https://console.byteplus.com/vke). In the left navigation bar, select **Cluster**.

2. In the cluster list, click the name of the target cluster. The cluster management page opens. In the left navigation bar, click **Configurations** \> **ConfigMap**.

3. In the ConfigMap list, find the ConfigMap to export, and in the **Actions** column, click  **`...`**   \> **Export**.

4. The file will be downloaded to your computer.


<span id=".5Yig6Zmk6YWN572u6aG5"></span>
## Deleting ConfigMaps

You can delete a ConfigMap. To do so, follow the steps below:


1. Log in to the [VKE console](https://console.byteplus.com/vke). In the left navigation bar, select **Cluster**.

2. In the cluster list, click the name of the target cluster. The cluster management page opens. In the left navigation bar, click **Configurations** \> **ConfigMap**.

3. In the ConfigMap list, find the ConfigMap to delete, and in the **Actions** column, click  **`...`**   \> **Delete**.

4. Click **Delete** in the confirmation dialog box to delete the ConfigMap.
