Cluster inspection helps you identify risks within VKE clusters, such as configuration risks, resource anomalies, insufficient quotas, and resource utilization. This topic describes how to configure cluster inspection.

<span id=".6YWN572u5ZGo5pyf5beh5qOA"></span>
## Configuring periodic inspection


1. Log in to the [VKE console](https://console.byteplus.com/vke).

2. In the left navigation bar, click **Cluster**.

3. In the cluster list, click the target cluster.

4. In the left navigation bar of the cluster management page, click **Cluster health check**. On the page that appears, click **Health check settings**.

5. Next to **Scheduled check**, click the <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_faab58d334756ffebd17be6c0e031050.png) </span>icon. In the pop\-up dialog box, enable scheduled inspection and specify the time at which periodic inspection is performed.

6. Click **Confirm**. The system will execute the inspection task at the specified time and generate an inspection report.


<span id=".6YWN572u5omL5bel5beh5qOA"></span>
## Configuring manual inspection


1. Log in to the [VKE console](https://console.byteplus.com/vke).

2. In the left navigation bar, click **Cluster**.

3. In the cluster list, click the target cluster.

4. In the left navigation bar of the cluster management page, click **Cluster inspection**.

5. Click **Start health check** to perform a manual inspection and generate an inspection report.


<span id=".6I635Y-W5beh5qOA57uT5p6c"></span>
## Obtaining inspection results

<span id=".5p-l55yL5beh5qOA5oql5ZGK"></span>
### Viewing inspection reports

After the scheduled or manual inspection task is complete, if the current inspection task is in the **Exception** state, it indicates that risks exist in the cluster. You can click **View details** in the **Action** column that corresponds to the target inspection task to view the inspection report.

The inspection result will display all inspection items in the current cluster, including successful and pending ones. You need to view the items on the **Pending** tab and resolve the issues. For details on fixing specific issues, see [Inspection item descriptions and fix suggestions](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/vke/Inspection-items-and-repair-suggestions).

<span id=".5LiL6L295beh5qOA5oql5ZGK"></span>
### Downloading inspection reports

After the inspection is complete, you can download the inspection report in PDF format.


1. After the inspection task is complete, you can click **View details** in the **Action** column that corresponds to the target inspection task to view the inspection report.

2. Click **Download the report** in the upper\-right corner to download the inspection report in PDF format.


<span id=".6K6i6ZiF5beh5qOA5oql5ZGK"></span>
### Subscribing to inspection reports

The inspection report subscription feature allows inspection reports to be sent to the target email address or Lark group at a specified time. The inspection reports are sent in the following logic:


* The latest inspection reports within the last 24 hours will be sent, including reports generated by both periodic and manual inspections.

* If no inspection reports are generated in the last 24 hours, a notification will be sent, and we recommend enabling scheduled inspection.


Perform the following steps to subscribe to inspection reports:


1. In the left navigation bar of the cluster management page, click **Cluster health check** and click **Health check settings**.

2. Next to **Inspection report**, click the <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_faab58d334756ffebd17be6c0e031050.png) </span>icon to enable inspection report subscription and specify the related parameters.



|Parameters |Description |
|---|---|
|Subscription |Whether to enable inspection report subscription. To subscribe to inspection reports, enable this feature. |
|Time to push (every day) |Select the desired push time for the inspection report from the drop\-down list and then click **Confirm**. |
|Receiver |Specify the method and recipient for receiving inspection reports. Valid values:<br><br><br>* **Mail**: Specify the email address for receiving inspection reports and its description.<br><br>* **Lark robot**: Specify the webhook address of the Larkbot for receiving inspection reports and its description.<br><br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div><br><br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">When you use Larkbot to receive inspection reports, you cannot use custom bots for which keys are enabled. Therefore, do not enable the <strong>signature verification</strong> feature of Larkbot.</div><br> |



3. Click **Confirm**.

## Configuring inspection silence

If some inspection items in your cluster do not meet the inspection requirements but can be temporarily ignored, you can configure an inspection silence policy. The policy is used to silence specified resources or inspection items to prevent noise interference during each inspection.


1. In the left navigation bar of the cluster management page, click **Cluster health check**. On the page that appears, click **Health check settings**.

2. Click **Create silence strategy**Create silence policy to configure an inspection silence policy.

   
   |Parameter |Description |
   |---|---|
   |Name |Specify the name of the inspection silence policy. |
   |Description |Enter the description of the inspection silence policy. |
   |Matching rules |* **Silenced for**: Configure the matching dimension for inspection silence. Valid values:<br><br>   * **Check item**: Inspection silence is performed based on inspection items and resources. The default inspection result for silenced rules or resources is **Passed**, which ensures that their abnormal status does not interfere with the inspection result.<br><br>   * **Resource**: Inspection silence is performed based on the resources in the cluster. The default inspection result for silenced rules or resources is **Pass**, which ensures that their abnormal status does not interfere with the inspection result.<br><br>* **Check item**: Configure the check items that need to be silenced.<br><br>* **Specify resources**: Specify the resources that need to be silenced. Common cluster resources, such as pods, nodes, Deployments, and StatefulSets, are supported. |
   

3. Click **Confirm**.
