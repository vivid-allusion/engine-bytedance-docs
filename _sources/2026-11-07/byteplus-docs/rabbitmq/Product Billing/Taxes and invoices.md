Unless otherwise stated, prices for products or services on the BytePlus platform include value\-added tax (VAT). During the service period or product usage period, if national tax policies change and cause any change in the VAT rate, the applicable VAT rate for products or services on the BytePlus platform will adjust accordingly. The tax\-exclusive price remains unchanged.

We support issuing VAT invoices corresponding to the order amounts you have successfully placed and/or billed through your BytePlus account. Unused prepaid amounts are not eligible for invoicing. You can apply for VAT invoices through [BytePlus Console - Billing - Invoice management](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/invoice/) or other channels designated and notified by BytePlus. We will issue VAT invoices promptly after receiving your application. If the service rules corresponding to the service you use differ from this agreement or if there is a separate agreement between both parties, the specific service rules or agreed terms and conditions will apply. For periodic settlement services and specific usage, the company reserves the right to require users to provide credit certificate, prepayment, deposit, or other conditions deemed necessary by the company for activation and ordering.
