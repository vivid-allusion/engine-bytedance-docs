ContextSearch has been officially available for sale since May 18, 2026 (UTC+8). ContextSearch leverages BytePlus database's core engine features combined with AI large model ecosystems and related capabilities to deeply transform the entire search process. It integrates data import, embedding, reranking, fusion search scoring, intent analysis, VLM, and other atomic functions to build an end\-to\-end search pipeline. It supports knowledge bases, image\-to\-image search, video search, and other scenario\-based search functions, providing more accurate and context\-relevant search and answer experiences for both structured and unstructured data.
