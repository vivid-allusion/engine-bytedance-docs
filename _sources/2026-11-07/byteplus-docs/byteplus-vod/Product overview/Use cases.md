BytePlus VOD enables you to build a VOD streaming platform or application for a range of businesses. This article outlines the typical scenarios where BytePlus VOD can be applied.

<span id="a3fe3798"></span>
## Short video apps

BytePlus VOD offers a comprehensive short video solution that goes beyond just media upload, management, processing, and distribution. It also includes the Player SDK, which can be integrated into short video applications in various industries, such as sports, e\-commerce, and social entertainment.

<span id="3d52dcc8"></span>
## Platforms that stream copyrighted content

For platforms that stream copyrighted content, BytePlus VOD offers a wide range of features, including high\-quality transcoding at low bitrates, timestamping authority, adaptive bitrate streaming, and the ability to switch between hardware and software decoding. These capabilities are particularly useful for live streaming websites, news websites, and applications that involve video playback and management.

<span id="2a760bca"></span>
## Online education

BytePlus VOD is suitable for educational scenarios such as corporate training and online recording classes. Teachers can record their lectures, upload them to BytePlus VOD, transcode videos, and share lecture videos with students. Additionally, BytePlus VOD can be used in conjunction with BytePlus MediaLive to deliver live online classes. Students can use the BytePlus VOD Player SDK to watch lectures on Android, iOS, and Web. The adaptive bitrate streaming of the SDK ensures a seamless viewing experience, regardless of the device students use or the quality of their network connection.

<span id="9999683c"></span>
## E\-commerce apps

By using the Upload SDKs for Android, iOS, and Web, along with media processing features like watermarking and screenshot capture, BytePlus VOD empowers online store owners and e\-commerce platform operators to showcase their products through engaging product videos. Customers can also share their experiences with purchased products by uploading videos, providing a more dynamic way to express their feedback. Furthermore, BytePlus VOD offers adaptable media asset management, allowing for timely removal of outdated videos and reducing storage costs.
