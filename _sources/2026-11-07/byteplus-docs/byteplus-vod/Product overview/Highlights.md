This article lists the highlights of BytePlus VOD.

## Highly stable and smooth playback

BytePlus VOD provides a cross\-platform suite of Player SDKs for seamless media playback on Android, iOS, and Web. The Player SDK has the following benefits:


* It offers a rich set of features that ensure a high\-quality and stable playback experience. It handles tricky issues such as blurry images, black screens, and out\-of\-sync audio. The Player SDK has an excellent track record of providing services for the most popular apps of ByteDance, such as TikTok, with a crash rate below 0.001%.

* It provides access control solutions such as referer blocklist and allowlist, URL authentication, and HLS encryption to protect your content.

* It supports preloading, network optimization, and decoding optimization, helping to eliminate freezing when switching videos and providing users with a high\-quality and smooth playback experience.

* It features small package sizes and efficient memory usage.

* Its well\-architected APIs enable straightforward and flexible integration into your applications.


<span id="0ef3b1a9"></span>
## Efficient and cost\-effective encoding and decoding technology

Using our own encoder, BytePlus VOD provides an end\-to\-end H.265 transcoding solution and an intelligent low\-bitrate HD transcoding solution. By effectively combining dynamic encoding and decoding technologies, BytePlus VOD significantly reduces your costs. The BytePlus encoding and decoding technology has the following advantages:


* It analyzes scenes, motions, content, and textures in videos and uses an image enhancement algorithm and an adaptive bitrate algorithm, outputting videos with better subjective quality at a low bitrate.

* It ensures an optimal viewing experience while using video compression that is 20%\-50% more efficient than standard H.264 transcoding.

* It dynamically adjusts decoding methods based on device models and a failover strategy, ensuring that the average hardware decoding coverage is over 85%.

## Rich media services

BytePlus VOD features rich media processing capabilities, helping to improve your content and drive business growth:


* It includes various media processing templates based on real business scenarios, including audio and video transcoding, intelligent screen capture, adding watermarks, and custom transcoding.

* It supports configuring workflows, which allows you to batch process videos efficiently.

* It provides an easy\-to\-use console and a variety of OpenAPIs. You can integrate the VOD solution quickly and flexibly.
