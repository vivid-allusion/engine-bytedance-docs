The BytePlus VOD console is a web\-based user interface for managing your entire video workflow, from uploading and processing to distribution and playback. This document provides a tour of the console's main sections and outlines the typical steps for using the VOD service, helping you get started quickly.

<span id="73504d71"></span>
## Signing in to the BytePlus VOD console


1. Sign in to the [BytePlus console](https://console.byteplus.com/).

2. Click the hamburger icon in the upper left corner of the page and select **BytePlus VOD**. By default, the onboarding page appears. The console consists of the following three sections: top navigation bar, left navigation pane, and workspace.

   1. The top navigation bar allows you to switch between products, manage bills and accounts, and view notifications.

   2. The left navigation pane gives you access to every feature of BytePlus VOD.

   3. The workspace allows you to use BytePlus VOD features.

## How to use the BytePlus VOD console

Follow these steps to use the BytePlus VOD console:

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/7e0edef31b5c4238a732c6821db07aac~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

The following table lists the console pages for these actions.


|Action |Console page |
|---|---|
|Create and enter a space |Spaces |
|(Optional) Create a media processing template |Templates |
|(Optional) Create a workflow template |Workflows |
|(Optional) Configure upload settings |System settings \> Post\-upload actions |
|Upload a video from your local PC |Media assets \> Videos \> Upload video \> Local upload |
|Upload a video from URL |Media assets \> Videos \> URL upload |
|(Optional) Process the video |Media assets \> Videos \> Upload video |
|Publish the video |Media assets \> Videos \> Publish |
|Create and configure a domain |Distribution acceleration \> Domain names |
|Play the video |Media assets \> Videos \> Details \> Playback address |
|(Optional) Get the Player SDK license and integrate the Player SDK |SDK management |
