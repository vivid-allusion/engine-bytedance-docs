The **Distribution** page offers detailed metrics on the bandwidth, traffic, number of requests, status codes, hit rates, and hot spot statistics of both edge servers and origin servers.

<span id="4f3ad752"></span>
## Notes


* The data on this page is not detailed metering information. For comprehensive metering data often used to verify your billing details, please visit the [Usage](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-vod/docs-usage) page.

* The data is subject to a 5\-minute delay.

* Only data for the past two years is available.

* Each query can only cover a maximum of 93 days.

* The time granularity changes depending on the timeframe of the query.

   * For a timeframe less than 7 days, the default time granularity is set to 5 minutes.

   * For a timeframe between 7 and 31 days, the default time granularity is set to 1 hour.

   * For a timeframe between 31 and 93 days, the default time granularity is set to 1 day.


<span id="29ac37f2"></span>
## Prerequisite

Ensure that you have created a domain name in the BytePlus VOD console.

<span id="440e989b"></span>
## Procedure


1. Sign in to the [BytePlus VOD console](https://console.byteplus.com/vodpaas/totalOverview/).

2. Click **Utilization insights** \> **Distribution** on the left navigation bar.

3. To access the desired data, simply click the **Bandwidth and traffic**, **Number of requests**, **Status code**, **Hit rate**, or **Hot spot statistics** tabs. You can adjust the filters to refine your search.


<span id="bb70dd3b"></span>
## Bandwidth and traffic

To refine your search for the bandwidth and traffic of edge servers and origin servers, you can apply filters based on region, space name, time granularity, and time range. You can move your cursor over the graph to see the bandwidth or traffic at a specific point of time and download details.

<span id="4001dc55"></span>
## Number of requests

To refine your search for the number of requests of edge servers and origin servers, you can apply filters based on region, space name, time granularity, and time range. You can move your cursor over the graph to see the number of requests at a specific point of time and download details.

<span id="22b84b50"></span>
## Status codes

You can view the number of times edge servers and origin servers receive status codes starting with 2, 3, 4, or 5. To refine your search for the status codes, you can apply filters based on region, space name, time granularity, and time range. You can move your cursor over the graph to see the count of status codes at a specific point of time and download details.

<span id="5e8898f3"></span>
## Hit rates

To refine your search for the hit rate of edge servers and origin servers, you can apply filters based on region, space name, time granularity, and time range. You can move your cursor over the graph to see the hit rate at a specific point of time and download details.

<span id="07b41549"></span>
## Hot spot statistics

To refine your search for the hot spot statistics on traffic and number of requests, you can apply filters based on region, space name, and time range.
