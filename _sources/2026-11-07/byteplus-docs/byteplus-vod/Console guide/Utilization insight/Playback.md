The **Playback** page provides detailed analytics on your video content's performance. On this page, you can access key metrics such as the top 100 most viewed videos, top videos by traffic consumption, and overall playback duration to gain insights into user engagement and content popularity.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">The Top 100 statistics for the previous day become available at 11:00 am on the current day.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">There is approximately a 3\-hour delay.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">If you delete a video that was ranked in the Top 100 list, the deleted video will still be displayed in the list. However, other video information, such as video duration, size, and creation time, will be removed and replaced by a hyphen.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Since the number of views is counted based on the CDN log, there may be instances where the number of requests exceeds the number of views. As a result, the data displayed on the <strong>Playback</strong> page may differ from the number of plays counted by the client\-side player.</div>

## Procedure


1. Sign in to the [BytePlus VOD console](https://console.byteplus.com/vodpaas/totalOverview/).

2. Click **Utilization insights** \> **Playback** on the left navigation bar.

3. To access the desired data, simply click the **Overview** or **Playback duration** tabs. You can adjust the filters to refine your search.


<span id="2fec60bd"></span>
## Playback overview

Under the **Overview** tab, you can view the following data:


* Top 100 most\-viewed videos

* Top 100 videos by playback traffic


You can also view the following details for a specified video:


* Video ID

* Video name

* Number of views

* Playback traffic

* Video duration

* Video size

* Creation time


<span id="b2d8eaf4"></span>
## Playback duration

To refine your search for the playback duration, you can apply filters based on region, application name, platform, time granularity, and time range.
