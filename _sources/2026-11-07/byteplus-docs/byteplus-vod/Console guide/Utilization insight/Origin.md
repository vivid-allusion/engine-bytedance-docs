For spaces configured with origin server IP addresses, the **Origin** page provides detailed metrics on the number of uploaded files, upload bandwidth and traffic, and data related to uploaded video files.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Only usage data for the past two years is available.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Each query can cover a maximum of 93 days.</div>



<span id="e9e41fb2"></span>
## Prerequisite

Ensure that you have configured the IP addresses of the origin servers in the BytePlus VOD console.

<span id="34593770"></span>
## Procedure


1. Sign in to the [BytePlus VOD console](https://console.byteplus.com/vodpaas/totalOverview/).

2. Click **Utilization insights** \> **Origin** on the left navigation bar.

3. To access the desired data, simply click the **Upload file**, **Upload bandwidth**, or **Upload video** tabs. You can adjust the filters to refine your search.


<span id="1d2496b4"></span>
## Number of uploaded files

To refine your search for the number of uploaded files, you can apply filters based on region, space name, time granularity, and time range.

## Upload bandwidth and traffic

To refine your search for upload bandwidth and traffic, you can apply filters based on region, space name, time granularity, and time range.

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/794b4791503841598ea4c7fc9d3e3032~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

## Data related to uploaded video files

To refine your search for data related to uploaded videos, you can apply filters based on region, space name, time granularity, and time range. The supported data includes:


* Average video size

* Average video length

* Average video bitrate

* Video size, length, and bitrate distributions
