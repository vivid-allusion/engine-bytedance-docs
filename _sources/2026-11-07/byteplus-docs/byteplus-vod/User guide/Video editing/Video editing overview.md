Video editing is a core capability of BytePlus VOD that allows you to produce professional\-grade videos. It offers a comprehensive suite of tools for tasks like clipping, merging, mixing, and applying special effects. VOD provides both a built\-in video editor in the console and corresponding OpenAPIs. For details, see [SubmitDirectEditTaskAsync](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-vod/reference-submitdirectedittaskasync).

<span id="aa19ffb9"></span>
## Features

BytePlus VOD supports the following video editing features:


|**Features** |**Description** |
|---|---|
|Multi\-clip video composition |Combine multiple videos and images into a single video. |
|Video mixing |Mix multiple video and image tracks, and adjust their layers and positions to create layered or picture\-in\-picture effects. |
|Rotating videos |Rotate videos and images up to 360 degrees. |
|Video splitting |Split videos at frame level. |
|Video speed adjustment |Adjust video playback speed. |
|Video cropping |Crop the video display area. |
|Video entry and exit animation |Apply a wide range of entry and exit animations to video clips. For details, see [Video animation ID](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-vod/editing_videos_via_openapi#425324e2). |
|Special effects |Add special effects to videos and images. |
|Adding multiple audio tracks |Add multiple music tracks during multi\-clip video editing. |
|Music trimming |Trim audio clips by setting in and out points. |
|Special effects and transitions |It supports various transition types, including camera motion, slideshow, special effects, and masks. For details, see [Special effect ID](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-vod/editing_videos_via_openapi#e2b43c22)and [Transition ID](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-vod/editing_videos_via_openapi#633b02c0). |
|Filters |It supports basic and advanced filters with multiple styles. For details, see [Filters ID](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-vod/editing_videos_via_openapi#67aba7c7). |
