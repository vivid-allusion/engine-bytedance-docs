This article introduces how to upload a FairPlay certificate to the Intertrust platform.

<span id="4d77edce"></span>
## Prerequisite


* An [Intertrust](https://portal.expressplay.com/) account.

* A FairPlay certificate. For instructions on acquiring the certificate and other related information, please refer to [Getting a FairPlay certificate from Apple](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-vod/docs-getting-fairplay-certificate).


<span id="c7241758"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [Intertrust](https://portal.expressplay.com/) console.

2. Click **Manage Account** in the left\-side navigation.

3. Click **Subscriptions** and select **FairPlay**.

4. Click **Click here if you would like to update your FairPlay Key/Certificate**.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/b0a11c6c60ef470c9d7e54402404b024~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

   Follow the instructions on the screen to upload the following information:

   
   |**Information** |**Example** |
   |---|---|
   |FairPlay Access Secret Key |ask.test |
   |FairPlay PKCS12 Certificate |cert.p12 |
   |Certificate password |12345 |
