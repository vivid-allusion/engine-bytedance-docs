To enhance service robustness and performance, BytePlus VOD will perform a scheduled migration of media services from the Singapore region to the new Asia Pacific (Johor) region.

<span id="1a01d159"></span>
## Maintenance window


* **Date:**  March 17, 2026

* **Time:**  02:00 to 02:10 AM (UTC+8)


<span id="9e171f3a"></span>
## Impact on services

During the migration window, you may experience the following temporary impacts:


* Increased processing time for the following ongoing tasks:

   * Asynchronous uploads

   * Media asset deletion

   * Transcoding tasks

* Possible failures for the following ongoing tasks:

   * Synchronous uploads

   * Media asset update operations

   * Transcoding tasks


To minimize any potential impact, we strongly advise avoiding media asset uploads and the initiation of new media processing tasks during this period.

This migration is part of our ongoing commitment to providing a more robust, scalable, and high\-performance infrastructure. We appreciate your understanding and cooperation.

If you have any questions or require further assistance, please do not hesitate to contact the BytePlus support team by [submitting a ticket](https://console.byteplus.com/workorder/create?step=2&SubProductID=P00001112).
