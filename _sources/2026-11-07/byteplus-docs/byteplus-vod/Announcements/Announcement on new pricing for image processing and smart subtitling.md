Starting from April 1st, 2025, we will introduce a new pricing structure for our image processing and smart subtitling features. These updates provide greater clarity and flexibility in our pricing model, allowing you to better manage your costs. For details on the pricing, please refer to [Image processing pricing](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-vod/docs-pay-as-you-go-pricing#8804d205) and [Smart subtitling pricing](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-vod/docs-pay-as-you-go-pricing#e250f532).

We're committed to providing you with the most valuable features and services. If you have any questions or concerns about this change, please feel free to contact BytePlus support team by [creating a ticket](https://console.byteplus.com/workorder/create?step=2&SubProductID=P00001112) at BytePlus Website.

Thank you for your continued support.
