BytePlus VOD operates on a flexible pay\-as\-you\-go pricing model, ensuring you only pay for the resources you consume. This document provides a comprehensive overview of the cost structure, covering the main billable items such as media storage, processing, video editing, and distribution acceleration, as well as the available payment methods and billing cycles.

## Payment method

BytePlus VOD offers pay\-as\-you\-go pricing, which allows you to easily adapt to changing business needs.

## Billing cycles

BytePlus VOD supports two billing cycles:

* Daily billing cycle: Fees generated daily are deducted the following day, and a corresponding bill is issued.

* Monthly billing cycle: Fees generated each calendar month are deducted the following month, and a corresponding bill is issued.

After activating the BytePlus VOD service, you will be billed on a daily basis by default. If you want to change to a monthly billing cycle, contact your [sales representative](https://www.byteplus.com/en/contact).

## Overdue payments

Overdue payments can result in service suspension and resource reclamation. For effective dates, restrictions, and service recovery instructions, see [Processing of overdue payments](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-vod/docs-processing-of-overdue-payments).

## Billable items

The costs associated with BytePlus VOD include media storage fees, media processing fees, content acceleration fees, and Player SDK fees. For details, see the table below:

|**Billable item** |**Description** |**Payment method** |
|---|---|---|
|Media storage |* Standard storage: Fees are calculated based on daily peak usage.<br><br>* Storage outbound: Fees are calculated based on total outbound traffic. |* Daily pay\-as\-you\-go<br><br>* Monthly pay\-as\-you\-go |
|Video editing |* Fees are calculated based on the duration of output files.<br><br>* The price is determined by the resolution of output files. |* Daily pay\-as\-you\-go<br><br>* Monthly pay\-as\-you\-go |
|Media processing |* Video transcoding: Fees are calculated based on the total transcoding duration.<br><br>* Video enhancement: Fees are calculated based on total output duration.<br><br>* Image processing: Fees are calculated based on the total size of the original images processed.<br><br>* Smart subtitling: Fees are calculated based on the total speech\-to\-text duration and the character count for machine translation. |* Daily pay\-as\-you\-go<br><br>* Monthly pay\-as\-you\-go |
|Distribution acceleration |Distribution acceleration fees are calculated using one of the following methods:<br><br><br>* Charging based on the daily peak bandwidth.<br><br>* Charging based on traffic with a tiered pricing structure. |* Daily pay\-as\-you\-go<br><br>* Monthly pay\-as\-you\-go |
|Player SDK license |BytePlus VOD offers two versions of the player SDK: standard and premium. To use the player SDK, you must purchase a license. |* Per license |
