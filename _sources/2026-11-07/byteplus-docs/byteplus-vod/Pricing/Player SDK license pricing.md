BytePlus VOD uses a license\-based system to manage access to Player SDK features. To use the Player SDKs for Android, iOS, and Web, you must obtain a license. You can choose from various SDK versions and add\-on features, allowing you to select the license type and pricing that best fits your specific needs.

## Free trial

BytePlus VOD offers a free **one\-month** trial license, so you can test the Player SDK in your own environment. To get started, see [Applying for a free trial license](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-vod/docs-license-management#aeaf94c3).
