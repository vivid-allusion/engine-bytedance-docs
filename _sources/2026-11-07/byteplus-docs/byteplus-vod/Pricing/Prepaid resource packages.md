BytePlus VOD offers pre\-paid resource packages, allowing you to pay in advance for a bundle of resources that can be used within a specific time period. This article provides information on the pre\-paid resource packages.

<span id="07917111"></span>
## General rules


* The validity period of resource packages is 12 months. Any unused resources will expire automatically after the package expires.

* You can request a refund for a resource package within seven days of purchase if it has not been used. Refunds cannot be requested seven days or more after purchase or if the resource package has been fully or partially used. Each account can unsubscribe from up to 10 unused resource packages per calendar year.

* A resource package becomes effective on the day of purchase but cannot be used to deduct any traffic or duration that occurred during a previous billing cycle.

* Resource packages can be used in combination. For example, if you purchase two 100 GB resource packages, you can use them to deduct a total of 200 GB of traffic. Deductions are made in the order of their purchase.

* The BytePlus VOD resource packages can only be used to deduct traffic or duration consumed when using BytePlus VOD's service.

* Any consumed traffic or transcoding duration is first deducted from your resource packages. Any excess usage is subject to content acceleration service fees and transcoding fees based on [Pay-as-you-go pricing](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-vod/docs-pay-as-you-go-pricing).
