The VideoOne Demo is an open\-source application that showcases the capabilities of the BytePlus Player SDK. Available for Android, iOS, Web, and React Native, the demo provides complete and product\-level source code for common playback scenarios like short videos, long videos, and short\-form series. By exploring the demo, you can quickly experience the Player SDK's features and use the open\-source code to significantly reduce your development time when building your own video app.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">Note</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">The built\-in service interfaces and resources in the VideoOne demo project are intended solely for demonstration purposes and should not be used directly in production applications. If you develop based on the demo, you must modify and replace them with your own product's service interfaces and resources before launch, according to your specific needs.</div>

## Android & iOS

The Demo for Android & iOS provides comprehensive support for video playback scenarios, including key interfaces such as vertical Feed streams, video channel pages, and playback pages. It also integrates a variety of playback control features like resolution switching, gesture control, and speed playback. Additionally, it offers standalone playback components such as picture\-in\-picture, playlists, and intelligent multilingual subtitles that can be flexibly combined for use.

<span id="928183ee"></span>
### Highlights

The VideoOne Demo app demonstrates Playback SDK features in two typical scenarios.

For the **Video Playback** scenario, the demo showcases three different video playback methods:


* Short Video: Similar to TikTok, where one video is displayed on the screen at a time, and users can swipe up or down to browse through various short videos.

* Medium Video: These can be played in landscape mode, with the feed showing more than two videos at once. Users can quickly swipe to navigate through multiple videos, often skipping several at a time.

* Long Video: Designed for a more immersive experience, these are played in full\-screen mode or on a dedicated playback page, suitable for movies, TV shows, and other lengthy content.


For the **Short Drama** scenario:


* Each episode lasts from a few seconds to about 15 minutes, featuring continuous and complete storylines in an immersive vertical full\-screen format. Users can swipe up or down to switch episodes, or click on specific episodes to watch them. Additional features include liking, commenting, and unlocking paid content.
