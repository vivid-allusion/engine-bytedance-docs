The BytePlus VOD Player SDK provides comprehensive video playback services for Android, iOS, Web, Flutter, and React Native. This document serves as a feature matrix, offering a complete list of all supported capabilities and their compatibility across platforms. You can use this reference to quickly check support for playback control, video effects, security, performance, and more.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">Feature support on web platforms also depends on the user's operating system and browser. For details, see <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-vod/docs-web-player-sdk-compatibility">Web Player SDK: Compatibility</a>.</div>

## Playback protocol and formats


|Feature |Description |Android/iOS |Flutter |React Native |Web |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|Audio and video container formats |Supports diverse audio and video container formats, including MP4, MP3, HLS, and Vorbis. |✓ |✓ |✓ |✓ |
|URL playback |Supports local and online video playback with a URL. |✓ |✓ |✓ |✓ |

|H.265 codec |Supports hardware decoding for H.265 video sources. |✓<br><br>The Premium Edition supports H.265 playback, providing a list of compatible hardware decoding devices and hardware decoding optimizations. ||||

|High Dynamic Range (HDR) |Supports playback of HDR10, HLG, and other types of HDR videos. |✓ |✓ |X |✓ |
|Automatically switches between software and hardware decoding |Automatically switches to software decoding when the user's device does not support hardware decoding. |✓ |✓ |✓ |✓ |
|Adaptive Multi\-Bitrate (ABR) |Automatically selects the appropriate bitrate based on the predicted bandwidth for playback of DASH or HLS videos. |✓<br><br>Only supported by Premium Edition |X |✓ |✓<br><br>Only supported by Premium Edition |
