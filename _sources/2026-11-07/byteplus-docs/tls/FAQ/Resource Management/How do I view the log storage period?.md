---
title: "How do I view the log storage period?"
product: "Torch Log Service"
product_code: "tls"
source: "https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/tls/How_to_view_log_storage_duration"
---

You can view the log storage period in the log topic list or on the log topic details page.


1. Log in to the [Torch log service console](https://console.byteplus.com/tls).

2. Go to Project page.

   1. Select the region where torch log service is located from the top navigation bar.

   2. On the left navigation pane, select **Resource management**  **\> Project**.

   3. Click the specified project name.

3. View the log storage period of the specified log topic.

   * Under the **Log Topic** tab, locate the specified log topic and check the storage period in the **Log storage period** column.

      <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_bb636f32d1b4dcdf97691439699c8107.png) </span>

   * Under the **Log topic** tab, locate and click the specified log topic. On its details page, view the storage period in **Log storage period**.

      <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_a2d17e19caed8708fc715f865219ec04.png) </span>
