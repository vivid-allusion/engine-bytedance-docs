This topic introduces the billing examples for pay\-by\-feature billing mode of Torch Log Service (TLS).

Website A generates about 1 billion logs daily. With an average size of 200 bytes per log, the total raw log data reaches about 200 GiB. The number of requests to upload logs daily is approximately 1 million. Website A uses 1 topic (2 shards) to store logs and retains them for 30 days. In addition, key\-value indexing is enabled for some fields. The total data volume of these fields is about 100 GiB.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">TLS automatically compresses data during collection. In this example, the compression rate is 20%.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">This example is based on the Asia Pacific (Johor) region, and the estimated results are for reference only. We recommend that you use <a href="https://www.byteplus.com/en/pricing?product=TLS&tab=2">price calculator</a> to estimate costs.</div>



The storage space of TLS accumulates daily. Therefore, storage costs increase as the log retention period grows. This example uses costs on day 1 and day 30 to illustrate the expense details.


* Day 1

   The cost is about $ 10.676359, detailed as follows:

   
   |Billing types |Billing items |Description |Daily measurement |Unit price |Daily billing |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   |Data transfer |Write traffic |The write traffic generated by uploading logs is about 200 GiB × 20% = 40 GiB. |40 GiB |0.043666 $/GiB |1.74664 |
   ||Index traffic |Configure key\-value indexes for some fields. These fields contain approximately 100 GiB of data. Therefore, the estimated index traffic generated is 100 GiB. |100 GiB |0.084906 $/GiB |8.4906 |
   |Storage |Log storage space |If 200 GiB log data is uploaded daily, log storage space of the first day is about 200 GiB/day × 20% × 1 day = 40 GiB. |40 GiB |0.00279 $/GiB/Day |0.1116 |
   ||Index storage space |The daily indexed field data is about 100 GiB, so the storage on day 1 equals 100 GiB/day × 1 day = 100 GiB. |100 GiB |0.00279 $/GiB/Day |0.279 |
   |Others |Number of service requests |Uploads logs to TLS in bulk. The number of requests is about 1 million. |1 million |0.029111 $/one million Requests/Day |0.029111 |
   ||Number of log shards |2 shards |2 |0.009704 $/per shard/Day |0.019408 |
   |Total |/ ||/ ||10.676359 |
