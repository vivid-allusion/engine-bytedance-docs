Database Transmission Service (DTS) provides the one\-click diagnosis function for data migration, data subscription, data synchronization, and data verification tasks. During task execution, you can use one\-click diagnosis to quickly understand the risks in task execution, and use the AI analysis function to analyze the risks in task execution or the reasons for execution failure, and provide repair suggestions.

<span id="SdTk92yq"></span>
## Background

During the DTS data migration, data subscription, data synchronization, and data verification task execution, when an abnormal occurs in the task (for example, the network delay keeps increasing or the task execution fails), you can use the one\-click diagnosis function to perform a comprehensive diagnosis on the running environment, the connectivity of the source and target database, and version compatibility, and provide diagnostic results and health level. You can use the AI analysis function to conduct root cause analysis based on the details of results that are **Failed** or **Prompt**, and perform self\-repairs by referring to the optimization suggestions provided by AI analysis.

<span id="cnTQKGH0"></span>
## Description

The one\-click diagnosis function is based on a preset check rule, which can quickly check and evaluate the running environment of the task, the connectivity of the source and target databases, and version compatibility. This function can quickly locate common problems, such as insufficient resources and version incompatibility. You can repair related problems according to the diagnosis results and combine them with the AI analysis results.

During the data migration, data subscription, data synchronization, and data verification task lifecycle, you can execute one\-click diagnosis in the following task stages.

<span id="6753WLUE"></span>
## Precautions


* During AI analysis, the analysis results and optimization suggestions are generated by the AI model, which may not be completely accurate and are for reference only.

* The AI analysis function can be used only when there are prompts and failed check items in the diagnosis results. An inspection report can only be analyzed by AI once. You can view the AI analysis results and optimization suggestions in the AI analysis results column of the check result list.


<span id="6753WLUE"></span>
## Prerequisites

A task has been created, and the task status is not To be started or Completed. For more information, refer to [Creating a migration task](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/DTS/overview_of_migration_solutions), [Creating a subscription task](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/DTS/overview_of_subscription_solutions), [Creating a synchronization task](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/DTS/overview_of_synchronization_solutions), or [Creating a data verification task](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/DTS/configuring_data_validation).

## Diagnose the task


1. Log on to the [DTS console](https://console.byteplus.com/dts).

2. On the top menu bar, select the project and region.

3. In the left navigation bar, select the target task type, including **Data migration**, ** Data subscription**, **Data synchronization**, and **Data verification**.

4. On the **Migration task list**, **Subscription task list**, **Synchronization task list**, or **Data validation list** page, click the target task name.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="1">You can diagnose only tasks that are in the <strong>In progress</strong>, <strong>Paused</strong>, <strong>Terminated</strong>, and <strong>Migration/Synchronization/Subscription/Verification failed</strong> states.   </div>
   

5. On the Task details page, click the **Health status** tab.

6. In the **Diagnostic report ** area, click **One\-click diagnosis** to diagnose the **current** **task**.

7. After the diagnosis is complete (usually within a few minutes), you can view the detailed information of the diagnostic report, as shown below.

   * Basic information of the diagnostic report: In the **Basic information** pane, view the check state, progress, creation time, and update time.

   * AI analysis: When the health level is not **Healthy**, click **AI analysis**, then click **OK** in the pop\-up confirmation window. DTS will analyze the current diagnosis results. Later, you can view the AI analysis results and optimization suggestions in the AI analysis results column of the check result list.

   * Health check results: In the **Health check results** pane, view the total completed check items, including prompt items, failed items, and passed items. At the same time, you can also view specific check items, check item descriptions, check results, and results details.

   * Export diagnostic report: In the top\-right corner of the **Basic information** area, click **Export report** to save the diagnostic report locally.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

      * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="1">When the check result for a check item is <strong>Failed</strong> or <strong>Prompt</strong>, you can click the <strong>AI Analysis</strong> option under the health level of the basic information, and refer to the AI analysis results and optimization suggestions to make corresponding adjustments.   </div>
   

      * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="1">Only one AI analysis can be performed per inspection report, and the AI analysis results can be viewed in the health check result list.   </div>
   

      * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="1">During AI analysis, the analysis results and optimization suggestions are generated by the AI model, which may not be completely accurate and are for reference only.   </div>

## View historical diagnostic report details


1. Log on to the [DTS console](https://console.byteplus.com/dts).

2. On the top menu bar, select the project and region.

3. In the left navigation bar, select the target task type, including **Data migration**, ** Data subscription**, **Data synchronization**, and **Data verification**.

4. On the **Migration task list**, **Subscription task list**, **Synchronization task list**, or **Data validation list** page, click the target task name.

5. On the Task details page, click the **Health status** tab.

6. In the **Diagnostic report** area, all historical diagnostic report records are saved. Click the target diagnostic report to view the report details, as follows.

   * Basic information of the diagnostic report: In the **Basic information** pane, view the check state, progress, creation time, and update time.

   * AI analysis: When the health level is not **Healthy**, click **AI analysis**, then click **OK** in the pop\-up confirmation window. DTS will analyze the current diagnosis results. Later, you can view the AI analysis results and optimization suggestions in the AI analysis results column of the check result list.

   * Health check results: In the **Health check results** pane, view the total completed check items, including prompt items, failed items, and passed items. At the same time, you can also view specific check items, check item descriptions, check results, and results details.

   * Export diagnostic report: In the top\-right corner of the **Basic information** area, click **Export report** to save the diagnostic report locally.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

      * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="1">When the check result for a check item is <strong>Failed</strong> or <strong>Prompt</strong>, you can click the <strong>AI Analysis</strong> option under the health level of the basic information, and refer to the AI analysis results and optimization suggestions to make corresponding adjustments.   </div>
   

      * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="1">Only one AI analysis can be performed per inspection report, and the AI analysis results can be viewed in the health check result list.   </div>
   

      * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="1">When the <strong>AI Analysis</strong> option is grayed out, it indicates that an AI analysis has already been performed on the current report. You can directly view the AI analysis results in the health check result list.   </div>
   

      * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="1">During AI analysis, the analysis results and optimization suggestions are generated by the AI model, which may not be completely accurate and are for reference only.   </div>
   

      * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="1">In the historical diagnostic report records, the earliest record is the pre\-check report generated when the task was created.   </div>
