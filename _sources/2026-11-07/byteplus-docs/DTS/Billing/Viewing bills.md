This topic describes how to view bill details of the Database Transmission Service (DTS).

<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log on to the [DTS console](https://console.byteplus.com/dts/region:dts+cn-beijing/).

2. In the upper\-right corner of the top menu bar, select **Fee** \> **Bill management**.

3. On the **Bill details** page, click **Bill details**.

4. On the **Bill details** tab, click the<span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_79c770c3f6aa33d860b5b95c7b046f05.jpeg) </span> icon next to the tag.

5. From the drop\-down list, check **Database Transmission Service** and click **OK**.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">You can also filter bills by <strong>Statistical item</strong>, <strong>Statistical period</strong>, and other options.   </div>
