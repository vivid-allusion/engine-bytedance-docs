This topic outlines the billing information for Database Transmission Service (DTS), including its billing items, billing methods, and pricing details.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">DTS officially started billing on April 10, 2023.</div>

## Billing method

Database Transmission Service (DTS) supports the following two billing methods:


* Pay\-as\-you\-go: This is a post\-payment model where you pay for computing resources actually consumed, with no charge for unused capacity. It is suitable for business scenarios with short\-term usage.

* Subscription: This is a prepaid model where payment is required before you can use the resources. It is best suited for business scenarios with stable traffic that require long\-term resource usage.


For more information about billing, refer to [Billing method](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/DTS/billing_methods).

## Billing items

DTS task's billing items include link fees, public network traffic fees, and data traffic fees. The specific billing method varies depending on the task type. For more information, refer to [Billing items](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/DTS/billing_items).

## Arrears and expiration

If DTS expires or is in arrears, DTS will stop the service. To avoid business losses, we recommend that you renew it in time. For more information, refer to [Arrears and expiration](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/DTS/expiration_or_arrears_instructions).

<span id=".5Y-Y5pu05ZKM6YCA6K6i6K-05piO"></span>
## Change and unsubscription

DTS tasks support changing the link specifications of synchronization and subscription tasks. For billing information related to changes and unsubscription, refer to [Change and unsubscription](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/DTS/modification_of_instance_specification_or_subscription_cancellation).

## Taxes and invoices


1. Unless otherwise specified, the pricing of products or services on the BytePlus platform includes value\-added tax. During the service provision or product usage period, if there are adjustments in national tax policies that result in changes to the VAT rate, the applicable VAT rate shall be adjusted accordingly in accordance with national tax policies, while the tax\-exclusive price remains unchanged.

2. We support issuing VAT invoices to you based on the amount of orders successfully placed and/or bills issued in your BytePlus account. You can apply for VAT invoices through the [Invoice management](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/invoice/) in the BytePlus console or other channels specified and notified by BytePlus from time to time. We will issue VAT invoices to you promptly upon receiving your invoice application. If the service rules corresponding to the services you use are inconsistent with the provisions of this section or if there are other agreements between the parties, such specific service rules or the terms and conditions separately agreed upon by the parties shall apply. For periodic settlement services and specific usage, the company also reserves the right to require users to provide credit certificates, prepayments, deposits, or other conditions deemed necessary by the company for activation and subscription.

3. We will issue you a VAT invoice according to the specific product or service type you purchased as follows:

   
   |Product or service delivery method |Invoice content |Tax rate |
   |---|---|---|
   |Database Transmission Service (DTS) |\*Information technology services\* Technical service fees |6% |
