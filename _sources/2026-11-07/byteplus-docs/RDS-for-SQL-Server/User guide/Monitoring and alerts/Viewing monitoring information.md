This article describes the steps for viewing monitoring information.

<span id="operation-steps"></span>
## **Operation steps**


1. Log in to [RDS for SQL Server console](https://console.byteplus.com/db/sql-server).

2. In the upper left corner of the top menu bar, select the project and region to which the instance belongs.

3. Click **Instance List** in the left navigation bar.

4. On the **instance list** page, click the target instance name.

5. At the top of the page, click the **Monitor Alerts** tab.

6. Under the **Monitoring Alerts** tab, you can click to view details of **resource monitoring** and **engine monitoring** for your instance.


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">For the description of each monitoring indicator, please refer to the <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/RDS-for-SQL-Server/monitoring-metrics">description of monitoring indicators </a>for details.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">You can view monitoring data for the past 1 hour, the past 3 hours, the past 6 hours, the past 12 hours, the past 1 day, the past 3 days, and the past 7 days. You can also define a start date and time in the time window to view monitoring information within a target time period of no more than 15 days.</div>
