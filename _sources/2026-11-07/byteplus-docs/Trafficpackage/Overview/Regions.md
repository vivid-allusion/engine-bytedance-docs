## Region

A region is a geographical area where a data center is located. Private networks are isolated among different regions. This ensures stability and fault tolerance among different regions. In most cases, regions are defined based on the cities where data centers are located.

Shared Traffic Package supports the deduction of Internet traffic fees generated by products billed for traffic in the same Region. After purchase, changing the Region is not supported.
