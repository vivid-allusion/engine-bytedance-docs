This article introduces how to unsubscribe from unused Shared Traffic Packages.

<span id="explanations"></span>
## Explanations

Shared Traffic Packages only support 7\-day unconditional unsubscriptions.


|Unsubscription Rules |Unsubscription conditions |Refund amount |
|---|---|---|
|7\-Day Unconditional Unsubscription |* You can request a refund within 7 days (≤ 7 x 24 hours of service) after you pay for a Shared Traffic Package order.<br><br>* During each calendar year (January 1 to December 31), a single Account can only unsubscribe from **10 Shared Traffic Packages that have not generated traffic deductions** by default. |The full refund is returned to your account. If you used cash coupons, these will not be refunded. |


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">If you purchase a Shared Traffic Package during a promotional event and unsubscription rules conflict with promotion rules, the promotion rules prevail.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">The platform reserves the right to reject any unsubscription requests that appear to be suspicious or malicious.</div>



<span id="procedure"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [Shared Traffic Package console](https://console.byteplus.com/eip/trafficPackages).

2. Click the **Unsubscribe** button on the right side of the target Shared Traffic Package.

3. On the **Unsubscription (7 days without reason)**  tab of the unsubscription management page, click the **Unsubscribe** button on the right of the target Shared Traffic Package.

4. Confirm the information of the Shared Traffic Package instance.

5. Click **Confirm unsubscription**. The unsubscription confirmation dialog box appears.

6. After confirming the information, click **OK**.
