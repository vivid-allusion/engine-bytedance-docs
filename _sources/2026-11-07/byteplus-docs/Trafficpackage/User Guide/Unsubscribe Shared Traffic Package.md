## Instructions


* Only Shared Traffic Packages purchased within 7 days and without traffic deduction can be unsubscribed. For specific rules, please refer to [Unsubscribe Instructions](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/Trafficpackage/Expiration_notice).

* Unsubscribing cannot be undone, please proceed with caution.


<span id="procedure"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [Shared Traffic Package console](https://console.byteplus.com/eip/trafficPackages).

2. Click the **Unsubscribe** button on the right side of the target Shared Traffic Package.

3. Click the **Confirm Unsubscribe** button.

4. In the pop\-up dialog box, click the **Confirm** button to complete the operation.
