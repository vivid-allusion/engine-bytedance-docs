This article provides a billing description for FileNAS, including billing cycle, billing type, and pricing.

<span id="81d57c6f"></span>
## Billing method

Currently, FileNAS only supports pay\-as\-you\-go billing. It is a postpaid billing method, which means you use the service first and then pay for it. The cost is calculated by multiplying the selected instance capacity configuration by the billing cycle, and the charges are included in your bill. You can settle the fees after the bill is generated.

<span id="cdcbf86a"></span>
## Billing cycle

FileNAS calculates the usage of all billing items on an hourly basis and generates bills based on the usage.
