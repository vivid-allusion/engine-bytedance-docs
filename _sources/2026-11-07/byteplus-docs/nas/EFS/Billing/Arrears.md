If the available balance in your account (including account balance and coupons) is less than the payable amount, your account will be considered overdue. You will then receive SMS or email reminders about insufficient balance. This topic explains the status of your resources and what you can do if your account is overdue.

<span id="ShWX48hZ"></span>
## Overdue details

Once your account becomes overdue, your resources will enter a reserved period and your usage will be restricted. Once the reserved period ends, your resources will be forcibly reclaimed, and they can not be recovered. Please top up and settle your overdue bill within the specified time (before the forced reclamation of resources) to avoid interruption of file storage resources.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Regardless of whether your resources are frozen or released, you are required to settle the fees as invoiced.</div>


<span id="tTyNHuSP"></span>
## Resource reclamation after overdue payment


* Suspension time: 2 hours after payment is overdue

* Reclamation time: 7 days and 2 hours after payment is overdue


<span id="OTaD76Hf"></span>
## Resource status

When your account is overdue for more than 2 hours, the instance status in the instance management list changes to **Suspended**.

The statuses of resources at the various stages of overdue payments are as follows:


|Within 2 hours |Between 2 hours and 7 days + 2 hours |After 7 days + 2 hours |
|---|---|---|
|Related data retained. |Related data retained. |The resources will be forcibly reclaimed, and the related configurations and data cannot be recovered after that. |
