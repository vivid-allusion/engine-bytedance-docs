Click the cluster name to enter the cluster details page. Select Service list to view basic service information. Specifically includes:


|Content |Note |
|---|---|
|Service name |Displays the names of installed services |
|Service status |There are two statuses: normal and warning. When in warning status, it indicates that some components within the service are in unavailable states such as stopped, stopping, or installing. |
|Service version |The version corresponding to the installed service in the cluster |
|Service operations |Includes operations for starting all, stopping all, and restarting all service components |
