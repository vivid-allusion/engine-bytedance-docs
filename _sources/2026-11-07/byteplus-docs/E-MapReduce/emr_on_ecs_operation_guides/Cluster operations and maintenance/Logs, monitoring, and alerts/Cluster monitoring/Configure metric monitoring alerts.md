The system provides a preset alarm template for E\-MapReduce. You can enable it with one click to receive instance resource alarm notifications.

<span id="f9bd2d2c"></span>
## Prerequisites


* You have enabled the EMR service.

* You have enabled the ECS service.

* You have enabled [CloudMonitor](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/cloudmonitor/What-is-Vitality-Cloud-Monitor).


<span id="dba5af9c"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log on to the [CloudMonitor console](https://console.byteplus.com/cloud-monitor/overview).

2. In the left navigation pane, select **Alert center** \> **Alarm Templates**.

3. Select the **Preset Alarm Templates** tab.

4. Specify the search criteria "Name" in the search box, and enter **E\-MapReduce preset alarm template** to find the target template.

5. Click the **E\-MapReduce preset alarm template** name to view information such as the resource types, dimensions, and trigger conditions for alarms, and confirm whether the template meets your requirements.


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">If the preset template rules do not meet your requirements, you can also create your own template according to the <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/cloudmonitor/Creating-an-alarm-template">Create Alarm Template</a> guide.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">For ECS\-related configuration, see: <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ecs/Collecting-alerts-triggered-by-ECS-metrics">Configure metric monitoring alarms for ECS</a>.</div>


6. Click the **E\-MapReduce preset alarm template** **Enable** switch on the right, or click the template name **E\-MapReduce preset alarm template** to enter the details page, then click the **Enable** button to enable the alarm template.

7. In the **Configure Contact Group** pop\-up window, specify the channel information for receiving alarm notifications, and click the **Confirm** button to complete the configuration.


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">You can configure the contacts and contact information for receiving alarms according to the <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/cloudmonitor/Creating-an-alarm-contact-and-an-alarm-contact-group">Alarm Contacts</a> guide.</div>


After the template is enabled, alarm policies will be automatically generated and applied to all E\-MapReduce resources under the account. If disabled, all alarm policies generated by the preset alarm template will be deleted immediately. For more details, see [Using Alarm Center](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/cloudmonitor/Using-Alarm-Center).

<span id="c72645b7"></span>
## Related operations

If the preset alarm template does not meet your requirements, select **Event Center** \> **Alarm Policies**, and click the **Create Alarm Policy** button to create a Customer managed. For operation details, see [Create Alarm Policy](https://www.volcengine.com/docs/6408/69472).
