<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">After releasing the cluster, data cannot be recovered. Please proceed with caution.</div>


<span id="操作步骤"></span>
## **Procedure**


1. Log in to [EMR console](https://console.byteplus.com/emr/region:emr_fe+ap-southeast-1/home).

2. In the top menu bar, select the region as appropriate.

3. Click the Cluster Management tab to view the **EMR on VKE** cluster list.

4. Click **Operations** in the Operations menu on the right side of the cluster you want to release, then click **Delete**.

5. In the pop\-up **Confirm cluster deletion** dialog box, click **OK**.

6. You can also click the upper right corner **Cluster operations** on the cluster details page, then click **Delete cluster**.
