During the use of Byteplus E\-MapReduce (EMR), if you have any questions about EMR consumption, you can view bills and consumption details through the **Billing Center**.

<span id="51775539"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the Byteplus Console.

2. In the top menu bar, click **Billing \> Bill Management,**  to enter the bill details page.

3. You can view all consumption details under your account through the **Bill Overview, Bill, Bill Details** tabs.

4. Switch the page to **Bill Details**, and in the **Product** field, filter for E\-MapReduce products to display EMR\-related order instance billing items.

5. As needed, you can also click the **Export Bill CSV** button in the upper right corner to export the bill details to your local device for viewing and analysis of consumption details.
