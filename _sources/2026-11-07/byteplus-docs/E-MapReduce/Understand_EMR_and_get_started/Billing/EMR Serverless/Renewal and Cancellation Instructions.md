## renewal fee

Pay\-per\-use Pay\-per\-use, no renewal required.

After the annual package expires, the fee can be renewed in the following two ways:


* Automatic renewal: **7 days before expiration** is automatically deducted from the Byteplus account balance. If the balance is not enough, the deduction will fail, and the processing mechanism of arrears suspension will be triggered.

* Manual renewal: Manually renew the fee at the resource queue of the console. If the fee is not renewed at the expiration date, the delinquent service suspension processing mechanism will be triggered.

## unsubscribe

Pay\-as\-you\-go does not support unsubscribe. This mode is billed according to the actual usage, and no use is billed.

The monthly subscription instance supports unsubscription and complies with the " **non\-7\-day no reason to unsubscribe** " rule.

Unsubscribe refund amount calculation formula:


* In the case of annual package and monthly package, the usage time is < 30 days: Unsubscribe refund amount = real value of the order \- monthly issue price at the time of purchase \* [usage time (days)/(365/12) ] \* applicable discount \* **1.5** \* [real value of the order/(real value of the order + voucher deduction amount) ]

* In the case of annual package and monthly package, the use time is ≥ 30 days: unsubscribe refund amount = real value of the order \- monthly issue price at the time of purchase \* [use time (days)/(365/12) ] \* applicable discount \* [real value of the order/(real value of the order + voucher deduction amount) ]
