During your use of E\-MapReduce (EMR), if you have any questions about your EMR consumption, you can view your billing statements and consumption details through the **Billing center**.

<span id="c0fe2afb"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the Byteplus Console.

2. Click **Billing \> Bill management,**  in the top menu bar to enter the billing details page.

3. You can view all consumption details for your account via the **Monthly bill**, and **Bill Details** tabs.

4. Switch to the **Bill details** section, and in the **Product** field, filter for the E\-MapReduce product to filter out billing items related to EMR order instances.

5. If needed, you can also click the **Export CSV** button in the upper right corner to export the billing details to your local device for viewing and analyzing consumption details.
