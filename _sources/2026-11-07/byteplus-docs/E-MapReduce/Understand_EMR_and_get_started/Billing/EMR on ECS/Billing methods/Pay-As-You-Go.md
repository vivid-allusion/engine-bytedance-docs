Pay\-As\-You\-Go, also known as postpaid, is a payment method in which you use resources first and pay afterward. This approach allows you to start and stop E\-MapReduce (EMR) clusters more flexibly.

## **Limitations**


* You must first complete [Preparation](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/undefined/6891e77253855305ecf7caa7) before you can purchase EMR.

## **Billing rules**


* The Pay\-As\-You\-Go price in the Johor region is $ 0.014 per core per hour.

* For Pay\-As\-You\-Go billing, the billing start time is the time when the EMR cluster creation order is successfully submitted. Billing is by the second, and bills are pushed every hour.

* The Pay\-As\-You\-Go amount for a cluster is calculated as the unit price multiplied by the total number of cluster cores and the usage duration. If the cluster is scaled out during operation, only the fee for the runtime of the newly added nodes will be charged.


For example, if you use an EMR cluster in the Johor region for 30 minutes between 10:00 and 11:00, and the EMR cluster contains a total of 32 cores (that is, vCPUs), the service fee generated by the EMR cluster is calculated as follows: 32 cores × 0.5 hours × $0.014 per core per hour = $0.224 . You will receive the bill for this billing cycle at 11:00.

<span id="3b103390"></span>
## **Settlement cycles**

Usage\-based billing is calculated according to the duration of cluster usage, with seconds as the billing unit. The EMR service settles charges at the beginning of each hour (based on UTC+8 time). When you enter the next billing cycle, if the available amount in your account (including your account balance and vouchers) is less than the bill to be settled, you will receive an insufficient balance notification via SMS or email. The actual billing statement shall prevail for detailed billing and statement issuance times. If you have questions about your EMR charges, you can view your billing statements and detailed charges through the **Billing Center**. For details, see [View bills](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/E-MapReduce/109026).
