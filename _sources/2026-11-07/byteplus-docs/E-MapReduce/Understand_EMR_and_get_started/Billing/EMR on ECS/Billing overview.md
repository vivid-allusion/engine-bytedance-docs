This document provides guidance on the billing of E\-MapReduce (EMR) clusters, including billing items, billing methods, billing type conversion, notes on overdue payments, notes on expiration, and other related product billing guidance.


|**Related items** |**Description** |**Related articles** |
|---|---|---|
|Billing items |Introduce the cost components and the calculation formula for EMR. |[Billing items](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/E-MapReduce/109025) |
|Billing methods |EMR supports two payment models: Pay\-As\-You\-Go and Subscription. |* [Pay-As-You-Go](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/E-MapReduce/109034)<br><br>* [Subscription](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/E-MapReduce/109037) |
|Billing method switching |EMR supports converting pay\-as\-you\-go clusters used temporarily into long\-term, stable subscription clusters, allowing resources to be reserved in advance while maximizing cost savings. |[Switch from Pay-As-You-Go to Subscription](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/E-MapReduce/109030) |
|Overdue payments |If your account is overdue, Pay\-As\-You\-Go clusters are at risk of being stopped. The system will remind or notify you, so please renew your account promptly to avoid any impact on your services. |[Arrears note](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/E-MapReduce/109018) |
|Unsubscribe policy |After your subscription cluster expires and is not renewed, your resources will enter a retention period and resource usage will be limited. Please renew your subscription as soon as possible to avoid any impact on your services. |[Expiration information](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/E-MapReduce/109032) |
|Renewal policy |Before a subscription cluster is automatically released, you can renew the cluster manually or automatically. |[Renewal instructions](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/E-MapReduce/109016) |
|View bills |If you have questions about EMR consumption, you can view the billing statement and consumption details. |[View bills](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/E-MapReduce/109026) |
