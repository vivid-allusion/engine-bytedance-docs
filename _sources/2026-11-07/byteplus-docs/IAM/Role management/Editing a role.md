You can edit a role's basic information as well as the users and policies bound to it using the BytePlus Console.

**To edit a role:** 


1. Sign in to the [BytePlus Console](https://console.byteplus.com/auth/login/).

2. Click **Username** \> **IAM**.

   <span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260522085108-935060956253.png) </span>

   The IAM dashboard appears.

3. On the left navigation pane, click **Role management** \> **Role**.

   The role list appears.

4. In the **Role name** column, click the name of a role.

   The role settings screen appears.

   <span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260522090236-1774326739331.png) </span>

5. Click the **Edit** icon to edit the role's display name, maximum session time, or description if needed.

6. Under the Permissions tab, edit permissions for the role, including global permissions and project permission.

   * **Global permission**

      The permissions defined in the selected policies are applied to all projects.

   * **Project permission**

      The permissions defined in the selected policies are applied to selected policies.

7. Under the Trust relationship tab, edit the policy that the role trusts through editing the policy syntax.
