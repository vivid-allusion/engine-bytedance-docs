You can create a role using the BytePlus Console.

**To create a role:** 


1. Sign in to the [BytePlus Console](https://console.byteplus.com/auth/login/).

2. Click **Username** \> **IAM**.

   <span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260522085108-935060956253.png) </span>

   The IAM dashboard appears.

3. On the left navigation pane, click **Role management** \> **Role**.

   The role list appears.

4. Click **Create new role**.

   The create new role pop\-up window appears.

5. Select a trusted identity type.

   If you select **Service** , you need to select a service from the drop\-down menu.

   <span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260522090122-1340871445492.png) </span>

   If you select **Account** , you need to select an identity type from **Current** **account** or **Other** **account** , and enter the account ID.

   <span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260522090141-191993986214.png) </span>

6. Click **Next**.

7. Configure the role's information, including role name, display name, and description.

8. Click **Next**.

9. Add permissions to the role through selecting policies from the list.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">If you do not want add permissions to the role for this time, click <strong>Skip</strong> , and the role will be created.   </div>
   

10. Click **Submit**.

11. The role is created.
