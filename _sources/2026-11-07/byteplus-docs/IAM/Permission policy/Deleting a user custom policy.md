You can delete a user custom policy using the BytePlus Console.

**To delete a user custom policy:** 


1. Sign in to the [BytePlus Console](https://console.byteplus.com/auth/login/).

2. Click **Username** \> **IAM**.

   <span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260522085108-935060956253.png) </span>

   The IAM dashboard appears.

3. On the left navigation pane, click **Permission policy**.

   The Permission policy screen appears.

   <span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260522090721-1146453911992.png) </span>

4. In the Actions column, click **Delete**.

   The policy removal confirmation pop\-up window appears.

5. Click **Delete**.

   The user custom policy is deleted.
