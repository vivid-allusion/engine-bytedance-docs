You can edit a user custom policy using the BytePlus Console, including its basic information, the role(s) and user(s) bound to it, and its syntax.

**To edit a user custom policy:** 


1. Sign in to the [BytePlus Console](https://console.byteplus.com/auth/login/).

2. Click **Username** \> **IAM**.

   <span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260522085108-935060956253.png) </span>

   The IAM dashboard appears.

3. On the left navigation pane, click **Permission policy**.

   The Permission policy screen appears.

4. In the Policy name column, click the name of a policy.

   The policy details screen appears.

   <span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260522090544-545733148599.png) </span>

5. Perform the following operations as needed:

   * Click the **Edit** icon to edit policy name and description.

   * Under the Policy Syntax tab, view the syntax of the policy. Click here for more details about policy syntax.

   * Under the Authorization tab, attach this policy to or detach this policy from specified identities.
