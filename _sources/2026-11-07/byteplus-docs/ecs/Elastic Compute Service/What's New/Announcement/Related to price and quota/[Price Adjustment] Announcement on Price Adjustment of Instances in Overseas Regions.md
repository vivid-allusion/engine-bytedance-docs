Dear BytePlus users, the BytePlus ECS service in Asia Pacific (Johor) Region has adjusted the price discount of the General\-purpose, Compute\-optimized, Memory\-optimized instance families on January 07, 2025.

<span id="adjustment-details"></span>
## Adjustment details


* Applicable Region: Asia Pacific (Johor)

* Instance families:

   * General\-purpose g3i, g3il, g3a, g3al

   * Compute\-optimized c3i, c3il, c3a, c3al

   * Memory\-optimized r3i, r3il, r3a, r3al

* Discount details:

   
   |Period |Pay\-as\-you\-go |Subscription ||||||
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   |||1\-9 months |1 year |2 years |3 years |4 years |5 years |
   |Before adjustment |NA |NA |6.8 |5.7 |4.5 |3.7 |3 |
   |After adjustment |7.2 |7.5 |5.1 |4.2 |3.3 |2.7 |2.2 |
   

* The adjusted prices are effective from January 07, 2025, and is applicable to eligible orders initiated after the effective date for Subscription and Pay\-as\-you\-go, including purchases, renewal, and configuration upgrades/downgrades.

* This article describes price adjustments only. For specific pricing for ECS instances, please go to the [pricing calculator](https://www.byteplus.com/en/pricing?product=ECS&tab=2). The price calculator calculates reference prices. For specific costs, refer to the actual order results in the [ECS console](https://console.byteplus.com/ecs/instance).

* If you have any questions, please contact the customer manager.
