Dear BytePlus users, the General\-purpose g2i, Compute\-optimized c2i, and Memory\-optimized r2i instance families of BytePlus ECS in Asia Pacific Southeast (Johor) Region were discontinued since the end of July 2024. You can continue to use the instances purchased before the end\-of\-sale date as normal. For subsequent new instance creations, it is recommended to choose [General-purpose g3i](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ecs/General-purpose#general-purpose-g3i), [Compute-optimized c3i](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ecs/Computing-optimized#compute-optimized-c3i), and [Memory-optimized r3i](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ecs/Memory-optimized#memory-optimized-r3i) instance families.

<span id="content"></span>
## Content

The plan is to gradually discontinue the sale of the General\-purpose g2i, Compute\-optimized c2i, and Memory\-optimized r2i instance families of the Elastic Compute Service (ECS) on BytePlus. The specific timeline and impact on users are as follows:

<span id="phase-1"></span>
### Phase 1

**Discontinuation Date**: July 1, 2024

**User Scope**: Users who have not held General\-purpose g2i, Compute\-optimized c2i, or Memory\-optimized r2i instances in May or June 2024

<span id="phase-2"></span>
### Phase 2

**Discontinuation Date**: July 31, 2024

**User Scope**: All users

<span id="scope-of-discontinuation"></span>
## Scope of discontinuation

Asia Pacific (Johor) Region, Availability Zone A

<span id="effect-of-discontinuation"></span>
## Effect of discontinuation


* Starting from the corresponding retirement date, you will not be able to purchase or change the specifications of the General\-purpose g2i, Compute\-optimized c2i, and Memory\-optimized r2i instance families of Elastic Compute Service (ECS) products in the regions and availability zones covered by the above retirement range.

* ECS instances of the General\-purpose g2i, Compute\-optimized c2i, and Memory\-optimized r2i instance families that have already been purchased can be used normally, but there may be usage impacts in the following scenarios:

   * For pay\-as\-you\-go instances that use the [Economical Mode](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ecs/Economical-mode-for-pay-as-you-go-instances), instances may not be able to start due to sellout.

   * For subscription instances, they can be renewed and used normally. If resources are released due to overdue renewals, they cannot be recreated.

   * Neither pay\-as\-you\-go nor subscription instances can be changed within or between the retired instance families (including upgrading or downgrading).
