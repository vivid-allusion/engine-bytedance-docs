Dear BytePlus users, the Local\-SSD i2, and Big Data    d2 instance families of BytePlus ECS in Asia Pacific Southeast (Johor) Region were discontinued since the end of 28 February 2025. You can continue to use the instances purchased before the end\-of\-sale date as normal. For subsequent new instance creations, it is recommended to choose [Local-SSD i3s](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ecs/Local-SSD-based#local-ssd-i3s) and [Big Data d2c](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ecs/Big-data-based#big-data-d2c) instance families.

<span id="content"></span>
## Content

The plan is to gradually discontinue the sale of the Local\-SSD i2, and Big Data    d2 instance families of the Elastic Compute Service (ECS) on BytePlus. The specific timeline and impact on users are as follows:

<span id="phase-1"></span>
### Phase 1

**Discontinuation Date**: December 30, 2024

**User Scope**: Users who have not held Local\-SSD i2, or Big Data    d2 instances in October, November or December 2024

<span id="phase-2"></span>
### Phase 2

**Discontinuation Date**: February 28, 2025

**User Scope**: All users

<span id="scope-of-discontinuation"></span>
## Scope of discontinuation

Asia Pacific (Johor) Region, Availability Zone A

<span id="effect-of-discontinuation"></span>
## Effect of discontinuation


* Starting from the corresponding retirement date, you will not be able to purchase the Local\-SSD i2, and Big Data d2 instance families of Elastic Compute Service (ECS) products in the regions and availability zones covered by the above retirement range.

* ECS instances of the Local\-SSD i2, and Big Data    d2 instance families that have already been purchased can be used normally. However, if resources of subscription instances are released due to overdue renewals, they cannot be recreated.
