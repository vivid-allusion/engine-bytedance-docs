Elastic Compute Service (ECS) is a collection of resources such as CPUs, memory, and volumes. Each resource is logically mapped to a physical computing hardware in a data center. You can buy resources of different sizes and instance types to meet your specific load needs. You do not have to worry about the location or status of the underlying hardware servers.

## Product architecture

ECS primarily includes [a variety of feature components](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ecs/Features) such as instances, images, EBS Volumes, network interfaces, security groups, and private/Elastic IPs. By integrating ECS with [other cloud products](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ecs/Related_cloud_services), you can enable capabilities like monitoring and intelligent deployment. This helps you build a reliable, secure, efficient, and flexible operational environment.
