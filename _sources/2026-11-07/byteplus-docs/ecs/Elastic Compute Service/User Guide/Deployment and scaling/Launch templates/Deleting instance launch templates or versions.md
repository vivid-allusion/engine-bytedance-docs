You can delete templates or versions that are no longer in use based on your business requirements.

<span id=".5pON5L2c6K-05piO"></span>
## Usage notes


* After you delete a template, all versions of the template are deleted.

* You cannot delete the default version.


<span id=".5Yig6Zmk5a6e5L6L5ZCv5Yqo5qih5p2_"></span>
## Delete an instance launch template


1. Log in to the [Launch Templates console](https://console.byteplus.com/ecs/template).

2. In the top menu bar, select a region.

3. In the template list, click the "Delete" button in the "Actions" column of the target template, or select multiple templates and then click the "Delete" button at the top of the list.

4. Click "Delete" to complete the action.


<span id=".5Yig6Zmk54mI5pys"></span>
## Delete versions


5. Log in to the [Launch Templates console](https://console.byteplus.com/ecs/template).

6. In the top menu bar, select a region.

7. Click the name of the target template, and then click the "Version" tab.

8. Click the "Delete" button on the right side of the target version, or select multiple versions and then click the "Delete" button at the top of the list.

9. Click "Delete" to complete the action.
