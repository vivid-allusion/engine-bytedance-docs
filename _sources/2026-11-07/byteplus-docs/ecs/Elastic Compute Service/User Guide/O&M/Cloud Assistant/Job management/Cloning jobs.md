Cloning refers to copying the definition information of a cloud assistant job to a new job. The "Clone task" feature enables you to retain all information from cloned jobs to execute jobs repeatedly. When cloning jobs, you can also change information such as the job name, description, command, execution target, and timeout period.

<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [ECS console](https://console.byteplus.com/ecs/instance). 

2. In the left\-side navigation pane, select "Maintenance & Monitoring \> Cloud Assistant" and then select the "Job management" tab. 

3. In the top menu bar, select the region where your business is located. 

4. Find the target job in the job list or search by "Job name".

5. Click the "Clone" button in the "Action" column on the right side of the target job to enter the Clone task page.

6.  **(Optional)**  Change the cloud assistant information as needed, such as name, command, and execution object. For parameter descriptions, refer to [Creating jobs.](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ecs/Creating-a-job)

7. Click the "Confirm" button to start cloning the job.

8. After the job is created, the system automatically redirects you to the Job management page. The new job then executes automatically based on its execution method.
