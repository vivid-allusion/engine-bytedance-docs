You can manually stop cloud assistant jobs in scheduled or periodic execution mode.

<span id=".5YmN5o-Q5p2h5Lu2"></span>
## Prerequisites


* To stop jobs in scheduled execution mode, the job status must be **Waiting for execution** or **In progress**.

* To stop jobs in periodic execution mode, the job status must be **Waiting for execution** or **Executing periodically**.


<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [ECS console](https://console.byteplus.com/ecs/instance). 

2. In the left\-side navigation pane, select "Maintenance & Monitoring \> Cloud Assistant," and then select the "Job management" tab. 

3. In the top menu bar, select the region where your business is located. 

4. Find the target cloud assistant job in the job list or search by "Job name".

5. Click the "Stop" button in the "Action" column on the right side of the target job.

6. By clicking the "Stop" button in the pop\-up window, you can stop the target job.
