By viewing custom templates, you can determine whether the target template meets your business requirements. If there are currently no custom templates that meet your business requirements, you can [create a custom template](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ecs/Creating_custom_templates) or [update a custom template](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ecs/Updating_custom_templates).

<span id="SFbotUX2"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [ECS console](https://console.byteplus.com/ecs/instance).

2. In the left\-side navigation pane, click "Maintenance & Monitoring \> Orchestration".

3. In the top menu bar, select the project and region where your business is located.

4. On the CloudOps Orchestration page, click the "Custom template" tab.

5. On the custom template list page, you can view information of all custom templates, including "Template name/ID", "Latest version", "Project", and "Tag".

6. Click the name of the target custom template to view the following information:

   * Current version information: By default, the basic information and content of the latest version are displayed.

   * Historical version information: If there are multiple versions, you can click the version drop\-down list to view the basic information and content of each version of the custom template.


<span id="LMGhWuQp"></span>
## Subsequent steps

If the current custom template meets your business requirements, you can click the "execution template" button [to create an orchestration plan](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ecs/Create_an_orchestration_plan). This activates the O&M workflow defined by the template, facilitating O&M operations through standardized and streamlined orchestration processes.
