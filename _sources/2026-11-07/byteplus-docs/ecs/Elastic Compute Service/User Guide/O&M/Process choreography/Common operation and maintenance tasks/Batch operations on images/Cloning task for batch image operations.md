You can use the cloning feature to copy all information of an existing image task. You can also modify information when you copy the information.

<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [ECS console](https://console.byteplus.com/ecs/instance).

2. In the left\-side navigation pane, select the "Maintenance & Monitoring \> Orchestration" menu.

3. In the top menu bar, select the region where your business is located.

4. On the "CloudOps Orchestration" page, click the "Common tasks" tab.

5. After you click the "Batch operations on images" menu, click the "Clone" button in the "Actions" column of the target task.

6. (**Optional**) Modify the parameters based on your business requirements. For more information, see [Creating tasks for batch image operations](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ecs/Creating_a_batch_actions_image_tasks).

7. Click the "Clone" button.

8. After you confirm that the information to be cloned is correct, click the "Confirm" button to start the cloning task. Once cloning is complete, task execution immediately starts.
