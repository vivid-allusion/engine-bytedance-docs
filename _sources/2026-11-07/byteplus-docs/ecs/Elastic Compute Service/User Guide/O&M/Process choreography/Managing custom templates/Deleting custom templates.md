You can delete any custom template at any time. A deleted custom template cannot be recovered. Proceed with caution.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">If you delete a custom template used by an orchestration plan, the orchestration plan still works as expected. However, the template cannot be used by other orchestration plans.</div>



1. Log in to the [ECS console](https://console.byteplus.com/ecs/instance).

2. In the left\-side navigation pane, click "Maintenance & Monitoring \> Orchestration".

3. In the top menu bar, select the project and region where your business is located.

4. On the CloudOps Orchestration page, click the "Custom template" tab.

5. In the "Actions" column of the target template, click the "Delete" button.

6. After you confirm the information of the template you want to delete, click the "Delete" button to complete the operation.
