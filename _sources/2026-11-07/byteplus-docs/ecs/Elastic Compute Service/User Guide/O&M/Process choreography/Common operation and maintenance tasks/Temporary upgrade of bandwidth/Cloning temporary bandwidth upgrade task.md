You can use the cloning feature to copy all information of an existing temporary bandwidth upgrade task. You can also modify information when you copy the information to improve O&M efficiency.

<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [ECS console](https://console.byteplus.com/ecs/instance).

2. In the left\-side navigation pane, select the "Maintenance & Monitoring \> Orchestration" menu.

3. In the top menu bar, select the region where your business is located.

4. On the "CloudOps Orchestration" page, click the "Common tasks" tab.

5. Click the "Temporary upgrade of bandwidth" menu, and click the "Clone" button in the "Actions" column of the target task.

6. (**Optional**) Modify the parameters based on your business requirements. For more information, see [Creating temporary bandwidth upgrade tasks](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ecs/Creating_a_temporary_bandwidth_upgrade_task).

7. Click the "Clone" button.

8. After you confirm that the information to be cloned is correct, click the "Confirm" button to start cloning. After the cloning is complete, the system automatically redirects to the temporary bandwidth upgrade tasks page and starts executing the task according to the specified execution method and execution time.
