You can view the execution results and status of temporary bandwidth upgrade tasks, as well as the overview, log, and workflow for each task.

<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [ECS console](https://console.byteplus.com/ecs/instance).

2. In the left\-side navigation pane, select the "Maintenance & Monitoring \> Orchestration" menu.

3. In the top menu bar, select the region where your business is located.

4. On the "CloudOps Orchestration" page, click the "Common tasks" tab.

5. Click the "Temporary upgrade of bandwidth" menu to view all tasks, including their names/IDs, status, associated EIPs, execution methods, and maximum public bandwidth.

6. Click the target task name to view information in the following table.

   
   |Parameter |Description |
   |---|---|
   |Basic information |Display the task name, task ID, role, and associated project. |
   |Task Logs |Display the execution logs of the current task. |
   |Task flow |Display the execution status of the task flow, as well as its basic information, input/output parameters, and log details. |
