You can cancel a temporary bandwidth upgrade task whose status is **in progress** or **Waiting for execution**. A canceled task cannot be re\-executed.​ Proceed with caution.

<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [ECS console](https://console.byteplus.com/ecs/instance).

2. In the left\-side navigation pane, select the "Maintenance & Monitoring \> Orchestration" menu.

3. In the top menu bar, select the region where your business is located.

4. On the "CloudOps Orchestration" page, click the "Common tasks" tab.

5. Click the "Temporary upgrade of bandwidth" menu, and click the "Cancel" button in the "Actions" column of the target task.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">When the task status is not <strong>in progress</strong> or <strong>Waiting for execution</strong>, the task cannot be canceled and the "Cancel" button is not displayed.   </div>
   

6. Click "Confirm" to complete the operation.
