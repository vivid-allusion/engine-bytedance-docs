After you create an ESI order, you can cancel the order within 30 minutes. After cancellation, the reservation becomes invalid, and associated instances do not launch during the scheduled delivery period.

<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [Elastic Scheduled Instances console](https://console.byteplus.com/ecs/esi).

2. In the top menu bar, select the region and project of your business.

3. On the order list page, select any of the following options to enter the cancellation page.

   * Click the "Cancel appointment" in the "Actions" column of the target order.

   * Click on the order name to enter the details page. Then, click the "Cancel appointment" at the top right.

4. Click the "OK" to cancel the order.
