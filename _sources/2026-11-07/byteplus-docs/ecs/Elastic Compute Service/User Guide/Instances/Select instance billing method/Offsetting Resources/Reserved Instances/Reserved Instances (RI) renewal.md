Before your Reserved Instances (RI) expire, you can follow the procedures described in this document to renew them and extend their expiration date. After a successful renewal, the new billing cycle will begin from the original expiration date.

<span id=".5rOo5oSP5LqL6aG5"></span>
## Precautions


* Reserved Instances (RI) pending renewal must be in the **Active** status.

* Renewal is not supported for Reserved Instances (RI) that have expired or been unsubscribed.


<span id=".5omL5Yqo57ut6LS5"></span>
## Manual renewal


1. Log in to the [Offsetting Resources console](https://console.byteplus.com/ecs/ri).

2. In the top menu bar, select the region and project where your business is located.

3. On the Reserved Instances (RI) list page under the "Compute" tab, click the "... \> Renewal" button in the "Actions" column to the right of the target Reserved Instance (RI) to go to the renewal management page.

4. Confirm information for Reserved Instances (RI) pending renewal and select the renewal duration.

5. Click the "Pay" button, submit payment information, and pay the fee.

   > If there are available coupons, you can select a coupon to apply a deduction to the payment.   
   

<span id=".6Ieq5Yqo57ut6LS5"></span>
## Auto\-renewal

**Enable automatic renewal in the billing center**

Before the expiration of Reserved Instances (RI), you can enable automatic renewal in the [Billing center > renewal management](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/renew). For detailed instructions, refer to [automatic renewal](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-auto-renewal-rules). Seven days before the expiration of your Reserved Instances (RI), the system will automatically deduct the renewal fee from your account balance. Please ensure that your balance is sufficient.

**Enable automatic renewal when purchasing Reserved Instances (RI)** 

You can select the "Enable auto\-renewal" option when [purchasing Reserved Instances (RI)](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ecs/Buying-Reserved-instances). After selecting this option, the platform will automatically deduct the renewal fee for Reserved Instances (RI) before expiration, and manual renewal is not required.
