## What is ECS

An ECS instance consists of fundamental computing components such as CPU, memory, image, and volume. When you purchase an ECS instance, you are required to configure items such as instance type, image type, volume type, network type, and login credentials. After this, your instance is configured with features such as computing, storage, and network. During usage, you can customise your ECS instance at any time based on business needs. This allows you to create a secure, stable, flexible, and efficient computing environment.

## ECS instance types


* Based on system architecture, ECS instances are categorized into three types: x86 computing, Elastic Bare Metal (EBM), and heterogeneous computing.

* Based on business scenario, ECS instances are categorized into General\-purpose, Compute\-optimized, Memory\-optimized, Big Data, and other types.

## ECS billing

ECS provides two main billing methods: Pay\-as\-you\-go and Subscription. You can also choose discounted billing options such as Spot instances or Reserved Instances (RIs) based on your needs.

For more information about billing, refer to [Billing overview](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ecs/Billing-overview).
