This topic describes the billing items and methods for Elastic IP Address (EIP).


* For more information, refer to [Pricing](https://www.byteplus.com/en/pricing?product=EIP&tab=1).

* For more information about EIP billing, refer to [Billing overview](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/eip/Billing-overview).

## Billing methods

Currently, EIP supports two billing methods: pay\-as\-you\-go and subscription. Each billing method has its own pricing formula. For more information, refer to [Pay-as-you-go](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/eip/Pay-as-you-go) and [Subscription](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/eip/Subscription).


|Billing method |Billing rule |Billing description |
|---|---|---|
|Pay\-as\-you\-go |Pay\-by\-bandwidth<br><br>Pay\-by\-data\-transfer |Pay\-by\-bandwidth: Billing depends on the bandwidth limit specified when you purchase the EIP, and is independent of the actual traffic usage. This billing method is recommended for businesses that require stable bandwidth.<br><br>Pay\-by\-data\-transfer: Billing depends on the actual amount of data traffic consumed. The bandwidth usage during peak hours will not exceed the bandwidth limit specified when you purchase the EIP. This billing method is recommended for businesses with fluctuating bandwidth.<br><br>The billing cycle is calculated in natural hours. |
|Subscription |Prepaid |In the subscription billing scenario, only the bandwidth fee is applicable. The billing cycle is calculated on a monthly basis. |


<span id=".6K6h6LS55pa55byP6L2s5o2i"></span>
## Changing billing method

EIP supports switching between different billing methods. For more information, refer to [Changing billing method for EIPs](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/eip/Billing-methods-change).
