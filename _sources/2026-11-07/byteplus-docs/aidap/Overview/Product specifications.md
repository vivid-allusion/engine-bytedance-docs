AI Native BaaS for Supabase relies on the AI Native Database Platform (AIDAP) and provides services through three database engines: Supabase, PostgreSQL, and veDB MySQL. The compute resources of the AI Native Database Platform have the greatest impact on the service. This topic introduces the compute specifications provided by the AI Native Database Platform for three engines.

<span id="bfKP9Uu1"></span>
## Compute specifications

The compute resources (compute power) of the AI Native Database Platform are measured in CUs. 1 CU equals the computing power of 1 core CPU + 4 GiB memory. The compute specifications supported by the AI Native Database Platform are shown in the following table.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">Workspaces using the Supabase engine use two types of compute: database management service compute and Supabase service compute. Supabase service compute does not offer a 0.25 CU specification.</div>
