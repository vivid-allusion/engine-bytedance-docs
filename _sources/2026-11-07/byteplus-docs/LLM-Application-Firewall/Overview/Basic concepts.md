This document introduces the relevant concepts of the LLM Application Firewall.

<span id="978acba8"></span>
## Prompt injection

A prompt injection attack is a type of cyberattack primarily targeting large language models. Attackers disguise malicious input as legitimate prompts to trick large language models into generating unexpected or harmful content, such as controlling systems or exfiltrating sensitive information.

<span id="b8491808"></span>
## Model abuse

Model abuse refers to the use of large language models for purposes that do not align with their intended design, or that violate laws, regulations, or ethical standards. Examples include causing the model to generate misleading content or responses that deviate from its assigned role.

<span id="3f57745b"></span>
## LLM

LLM (Large Language Model) refers to a type of advanced artificial intelligence model trained on massive amounts of text data. It can understand, generate, and manipulate human language by learning patterns, semantics, and contextual relationships from the training corpus. LLMs can perform a wide range of natural language processing tasks, such as text generation, translation, summarization, and question answering, making them foundational components in numerous AI\-powered applications.
