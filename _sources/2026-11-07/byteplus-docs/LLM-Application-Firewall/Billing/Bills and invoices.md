To effectively manage the billing and expenses for the LLM Application Firewall, this guide explains how to view detailed bills in the billing center, subscribe to billing notifications, and request invoices. These instructions enable you to clearly understand your spending details and manage your financial expenditures.

<span id="a650d10d"></span>
## View bills

All billing items for the LLM Application Firewall are billed hourly. You can view detailed records of each expense on the [Billing Center > Bill details](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/bill/detail) page.

<span id="3ad85740"></span>
## Subscribe to bill notifications

To facilitate expense management, we recommend subscribing to the billing notification service by referring to [Set bill consumption warning](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/bill_consumption_warning) on the [Billing Center > Bill details](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/bill/detail) page. After successful setup, the system will regularly send your bills to designated contacts via SMS, email, or site messages.

<span id="b46ad92f"></span>
## Taxes and invoices

You can view and download your invoices using [Billing Center > Invoices](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/invoice-manage). For more details, refer to [Manage invoices](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-managing-invoices).
