To flexibly manage your LLM Application Firewall subscription according to your business needs, you can change your billing method, upgrade or downgrade your plan, renew, or cancel the service at any time. This guide provides instructions for these actions, helping you effectively control costs and ensure business continuity.

<span id="d1d9a24a"></span>
### Plan changes

If your current plan is no longer suitable, you can change it at any time in the console. We support the following four types of changes:


* Switch from **subscription** to **pay\-as\-you\-go**

* Switch from **pay\-as\-you\-go** to **subscription**

* Upgrade the **subscription** plan

* Downgrade the **subscription** plan


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">For specific change rules and fee calculations, please refer to the <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/Billing_Rules_for_Resource_Subscription_Change">Billing Rules for Resource Subscription Change</a>.</div>


<span id="50480f1b"></span>
### Renewal

To avoid service disruption when your subscription plan is about to expire, please renew promptly in the [LLM Application Firewall Console > Instance management](https://console.byteplus.com/omni-shield/llm-shield/region:llm-shield+ap-southeast-1/instance-manage). The system will notify you via the [Message Center](https://console.byteplus.com/message/innermsg) before expiration.

<span id="6a3cb421"></span>
### Cancellation

If you no longer need to use the LLM Application Firewall, you can cancel at any time. Caution: After cancellation, all protection configurations and policies in your account will be cleared and cannot be restored.


* Pay\-as\-you\-go cancellation: Cancel on the [LLM Application Firewall Console > Instance management](https://console.byteplus.com/omni-shield/llm-shield/region:llm-shield+ap-southeast-1/instance-manage) page.

* Subscription cancellation: Cancel on the [Billing Center > Unsubscription management](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/unsubscribe) page.
