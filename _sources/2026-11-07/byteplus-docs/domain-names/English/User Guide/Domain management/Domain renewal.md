This document introduces domain renewal in BytePlus Domains.

<span id="overview"></span>
## Overview

Each domain has an expiration date. Before the domain expires, you need to manually renew it in time to extend its validity. We will send you reminders via notifications in BytePlus console and email starting 30 days before the domain's expiration date. It is recommended to enable [auto-renew](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/171/36435#auto-renew) for your domain to prevent expiration due to untimely renewal.

<span id="renewal-period"></span>
### Renewal Period


* The minimum renewal period for a domain is 1 year. If the domain suffix is .ai, the minimum renewal period is 2 years.

* The maximum renewal period for a domain = the maximum registration period for the domain \- the remaining validity period of the domain.


The unit of period is years. A domain can be registered for a maximum of 10 years.

<span id="domain-lifecycle"></span>
### Domain Lifecycle

The lifecycle of a registered domain includes the following phases:


1. Active Period: After a domain is registered, it enters the active period. You can extend the active period by renewing the domain.

2. Grace Period: Once the domain expires, it enters the grace period.

3. Redemption Period: Once the domain passes the grace period, it enters the redemption period.

4. Deletion Period: Once the domain passes the redemption period, it enters the deletion period. The registry may delete your domain from its database at any time.


<span id="grace-period-and-redemption-period"></span>
### Grace Period and Redemption Period

Once a domain expires, it will go through the grace period and redemption period:


|**Lifecycle Phase** |**Description** |**Renewable Period** |**Domain Status in Console** |
|---|---|---|---|
|Grace Period |The grace period is within 30 or 35 days after the domain expiration date, accurate to the day. |At least 1 year (minimum 2 years for .ai domains), up to the maximum allowable renewal period for the domain. |Expired |
|Redemption Period |The redemption period is within 15 or 30 days after the grace period ends, accurate to the day.<br><br>&nbsp;<br><br>During the redemption period, you need to pay a redemption fee to retain the domain name. The redemption fee is higher than the renewal fee. |When redeeming your domain name, you can only renew it for 1 year (2 years for .ai domains).<br><br>&nbsp;<br><br>After redeeming the domain name, you can renew it to extend its validity period. |* If the domain is not renewed, the status is "Pending Redemption".<br><br>* If the domain is renewed but the redemption process is not yet complete, the status is "Redeeming".<br><br>* If the domain is redeemed, the status is "Normal". |



* For renewal and redemption pricing, see [Pricing](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/domain-names/pricing_en).

* For the duration of the grace period and redemption period, see [FAQs on Domain Renewal](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/domain-names/renewal-and-redemption-faqs_en).


During the grace period and redemption period,


* Domain resolution will be unavailable, and users will not be able to access your site using the domain.

* If you renew the domain, domain resolution will be restored within 24 to 48 hours after renewal.


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">You cannot renew a domain with the status "In Transfer".</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">If the domain has passed the redemption period, you lose ownership of the domain, and anyone can register it.</div>



<span id="steps"></span>
## Steps


1. Visit [BytePlus Domains](https://console.byteplus.com/domain-service/search).

2. In the left navigation bar, click **Domain list**.

3. On the **Domain list** page, find the domain you want to renew.

4. In the **Actions** column, click **Renew**.

   <span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_63b475c8470009a466e344210211f212.png) </span>

5. Select a renewal period, check the agreement, and then click **Submit**.

   <span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_91d1fc1187eb8b64c861b8d4e08ed210.png) </span>

6. Follow the on\-screen instructions to proceed.


<span id="auto-renew"></span>
## Auto\-renew

To prevent your domain from expiring due to untimely renewal, you can enable auto\-renew for your domain. Once auto\-renew is enabled, BytePlus Domains will automatically renew your domain for 1 year 15 days before the expiration date. The renewal period for domains with the .ai suffix is 2 years. Please ensure that the [payment method](https://console.byteplus.com//finance/payment-methods) set in your BytePlus account is valid to avoid renewal failure due to payment issues. For more details, refer to the [Service Specific Terms](https://docs.byteplus.com/legal/docs/service-specific-terms).

<span id="enable-auto-renew"></span>
### Enable Auto\-renew

<span id="method-1-enable-auto-renew-for-a-domain-in-the-domain-list"></span>
#### Method 1: Enable Auto\-renew for a Domain in the Domain List


1. Visit [BytePlus Domains](https://console.byteplus.com/domain-service/search).

2. In the left navigation bar, click **Domain list**.

3. On the **Domain list** page, find the domain you want to enable auto\-renew for.

4. In the **Auto\-renew** column, set the switch to enable.


<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_afe8fcb5b96a400db385a21d7d7804f2.png) </span>

<span id="method-2-enable-auto-renew-for-a-domain-on-the-domain-details-page"></span>
#### Method 2: Enable Auto\-renew for a Domain on the Domain Details Page


1. Visit [BytePlus Domains](https://console.byteplus.com/domain-service/search).

2. In the left navigation bar, click **Domain list**.

3. On the **Domain list** page, find the domain you want to enable auto\-renew for.

4. In the **Actions** column, click **Manage**.

5. On the domain details page, set **Auto\-renew** to enable.


<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_d09731e671fc1e5de17a000e5559e9ce.png) </span>
