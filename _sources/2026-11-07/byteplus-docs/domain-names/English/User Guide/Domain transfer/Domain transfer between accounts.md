This document describes how to transfer a domain registered under BytePlus Domains from your current BytePlus account to another BytePlus account.

<span id="DqWeUNGs"></span>
## Use Case

If you need to move a domain from your BytePlus account to a different BytePlus account, you can use the **Transfer domain to another BytePlus account** feature.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">Only domains that have completed real\-name verification and are in the <strong>Active</strong> status are eligible for transfer.</div>


<span id="RE7w1xjy"></span>
## Precautions


* If you transfer a domain to another BytePlus account, this action cannot be undone, so please think carefully before proceeding. If you need to manage the domain again, you must ask the owner of the target account to transfer it back to you.

* After the transfer is completed, you will no longer have management permissions for the domain. The owner of the target account will have full control, including modifying registrant information or transferring the domain to another registrar.

* DNS configurations for the domain and its subdomains will remain unchanged after the transfer. You will continue to manage the DNS records of the domain.

* Domains transferred between accounts cannot be recovered using domain name redemption.

* If **Auto\-Renew** is enabled for the domain, it will be automatically disabled after the transfer. You may enable it again in the target account if needed.


<span id="ShaIyhXk"></span>
## Procedure


1. Visit [BytePlus Domains](https://console.byteplus.com/domain-service/domain).

2. In the domain list, click the domain name you want to transfer.

3. In the upper\-right corner of the domain management page, click **Transfer domain to another BytePlus account**.<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_e33418ffd3a3e8fa6a1c8c8aaf0d3b8d.png) </span>

4. On the **Transfer domain to another BytePlus account ** page, enter the target BytePlus account ID and click **OK**.

   <span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_d3890c2522bbc242119cd9a27b471fdd.png) </span>

5. In the **Confirm Transfer** dialog box:

   * Review the transfer details and associated risk warnings.

   * Select **Agree to the above content and accept the related risks**.

   * Click **OK**.

   <span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_5f339593dfef6a23a42cacbdf19ce881.png) </span>

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="1">After your domain name is transferred to a new account, automatic renewal will be turned off. If necessary, you can restart it in the new account.   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="1"><img src="https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_a564adffddd41dfb667bda420519af76.png" width="359px" />   </div>
   

6. A verification code will be sent via SMS to the mobile number associated with your BytePlus account.

   Enter the verification code and click **Confirm**.

   <span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_5bb140391d6078d263aae7fbb977343e.png) </span>

7. The console will display a message indicating that the transfer request has been submitted.

   Click **View Operation Records** to check the status and confirm whether the transfer is successful.<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_c56efecca8573c424f04e36d2aa9d83c.png) </span>
