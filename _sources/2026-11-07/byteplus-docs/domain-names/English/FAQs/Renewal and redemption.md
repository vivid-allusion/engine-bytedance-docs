## What are the domain renewal grace period and domain redemption period, and how long are they?


* Renewal grace period: Once a domain expires, it enters the renewal grace period. During the grace period, domain resolution will be unavailable. You can renew the domain at the regular renewal price.

* Redemption period: Once the domain passes the grace period, it enters the redemption period. During the redemption period, domain resolution remains unavailable. If you renew the domain during the redemption period, you will have to pay a higher fee.


For renewal pricing, see [Pricing](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/domain-names/pricing_en). For detailed information on renewing a domain during the grace period and redemption period, see [Domain Renewal](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/domain-names/domain-renewal_en).

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">If you renew a domain during the grace period and redemption period, domain resolution will be restored within 24\-48 hours after renewal.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Once the domain passes the redemption period, the registry may delete your domain at any time.</div>
