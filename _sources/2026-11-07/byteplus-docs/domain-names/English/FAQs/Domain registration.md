<span id="how-to-choose-a-domain"></span>
## How to choose a domain?

A good domain has the following characteristics: simplicity, easy to remember, personality, and meaningfulness. When brainstorming a domain name, you might consider using your company, product, or trademark name as the domain name.

Choose a domain suffix that represents the industry related to your business. You can also plan for the future of your business by registering relevant domains in advance for fields your business will expand into, to gain a market advantage. For example, if you anticipate that your business will expand into the AI field in the future, you can register a domain with the .ai suffix in advance.

Assuming your envisioned domain name is example, you can register both example.com and example.ai.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">It is strongly recommended not to register domains that contain someone else's trademark or a celebrity's name to avoid infringing on others' rights and potentially causing legal disputes.</div>


<span id="how-to-determine-if-a-domain-can-be-registered"></span>
## How to determine if a domain can be registered?

In the domain search results on the **Domain Registration** page, if a domain is marked as **Registered**, it means that the domain has already been registered by someone else.

<span id="what-to-do-if-your-desired-domain-is-already-registered"></span>
## What to do if your desired domain is already registered?

Since domains are unique, once a domain is registered, others cannot register it. You can choose a domain with a different suffix or change the domain name for your search.

<span id="what-to-do-if-the-order-shows-active-after-successful-domain-registration-payment"></span>
## What to do if the order shows "Active" after successful domain registration payment?

This is expected. The "Active" status indicates that you have successfully registered the domain. You can visit the [Domain List](https://console.byteplus.com/domain-service/domain) to manage the domain.

<span id="how-to-manage-registered-domains"></span>
## How to manage registered domains?


1. Visit [BytePlus Domains](https://console.byteplus.com/domain-service/search).

2. In the left navigation bar, click **Domain list**.


In the domain list, for the domain you want to manage, you can click the corresponding action in the **Actions** column.

<span id="can-i-get-a-refund-if-domain-registration-fails"></span>
## Can I get a refund if domain registration fails?

If domain registration fails, you will receive a full refund. The fee will be returned to the payment method used at the time of payment.

<span id="can-i-modify-request-deletion-or-request-a-refund-for-a-registered-domain"></span>
## Can I modify, request deletion, or request a refund for a registered domain?

Sorry, you cannot modify, delete, or request a refund for a registered domain. This is because the domain has already been recorded in the registry database. If the domain you registered does not meet your expectations, such as containing a spelling error, you must register a new domain. For the current domain, you can let it expire without renewing it. Once the domain passes the redemption period, the registry will delete the domain at any time.

<span id="reasons-for-domain-registration-failure"></span>
## Reasons for domain registration failure

Common reasons for domain registration failure include:


* The domain has already been registered. One possible scenario is that the domain was not registered when you searched for it, but it was registered by someone else when you submitted the domain registration application.

* There was a network fluctuation when submitting the domain application, causing the submission to fail.

* The domain name to be registered is a reserved word of the registry. In this case, you cannot register this domain name. Please choose a different domain name and try the registration again.


<span id="what-is-the-maximum-registration-and-renewal-period-for-a-domain"></span>
## What is the maximum registration and renewal period for a domain?


* The maximum registration period for a domain is 10 years.

* The maximum renewal period for a domain is calculated using the following formula:

   * Maximum renewal period = Maximum registration period \- Remaining valid period of the domain
