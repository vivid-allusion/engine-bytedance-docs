## Introduction to Domains

A domain is like an address on the internet, used to identify and locate servers providing services on the internet. Domains correspond to the IP addresses of servers. IP addresses are difficult to understand and remember. Compared to IP addresses, domains describe the address of services in an easy\-to\-understand and memorable text form. You can use domains to access these services.

A domain consists of two parts: the second\-level label and the Top\-Level Domain (TLD). For example, in the domain `example.com`, `example` is the second\-level label, and `.com` is the Top\-Level Domain.

The Internet Corporation for Assigned Names and Numbers (ICANN) is responsible for managing and maintaining the domain name system on the Internet. In the domain name system, Top\-Level Domains include the following categories:


* **Country Code Top Level Domain (ccTLD)** : Represents countries and regions, ending with a two\-character code, mainly following [ISO 3166-1](https://www.iso.org/iso-3166-country-codes.html) alpha\-2. For example, .cn represents China, .us represents the United States.

* **Generic Top Level Domain (gTLD)** : Represents general categories. Common gTLDs include .com, .net, etc. In recent years, ICANN has added some new gTLDs, such as .biz, .info, etc.

## Basic Concepts

**Domain Registry**

A domain registry is an organization responsible for managing Top\-Level Domains (TLDs). The domain you purchase is registered in the database of the domain registry. For example, Verisign is the registry managing .com domains, while CNNIC is the registry managing .cn domains.

**Domain Registrar**

Domain registrars are service providers authorized by registries to offer domain registration services to the public. Users register domains through registrars, and registrars submit the registered domain information to the corresponding registry's database. In addition to domain registration, registrars also provide services such as DNS resolution and domain renewal.

BytePlus partners with the registrar Domainipr to provide domain registration, transfer, renewal, and management services. For domains registered through BytePlus Domains, the actual registrar is Domainipr.

**Premium Domain**

Premium domains are high\-value domains reserved by domain registries, such as `rose.store`. Premium domains reserved by registries comply with ICANN regulations. Whether for investment or website use, premium domains can add credibility, trustworthiness, and better brand effect to your online business.
