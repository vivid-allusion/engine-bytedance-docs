## Is Auto Scaling a paid service?

The Auto Scaling service itself is free of charge. However, fees may apply to associated cloud products such as ECS, EIP, and Load Balancer. The billing methods, bills, orders, and account arrears all follow the billing rules of the respective cloud products. For more information, see [Billing guide](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/as/Billing).

When a scaling group scales out or in, it may automatically create, delete, or remove resource instances of each cloud product. Billing starts immediately when a resource is created. Billing stops when the resource is deleted. If the resource is removed (but not deleted), you can still view and use it in the product console. Therefore, you are still charged for the resource. For information about how each cloud product is created, removed, or deleted in a scaling group, see [Related cloud products](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/as/Related-cloud-services).
