## Scenario

You can perform this operation to disable a scaling group. After being disabled, the scaling group will no longer perform adjustments.

<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [Scaling Group console](https://console.byteplus.com/as/group). 

2. In the top menu bar, select the project and region of the target resource.

3. Click the "Disable" button on the right side of the scaling group that you want to disable.

4. Click the "Confirm" button to complete the operation.
