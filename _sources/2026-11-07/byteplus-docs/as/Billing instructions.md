The AS service itself does not charge any fees; however, related cloud products (for example, Elastic Computing Service (ECS), Elastic IP (EIP), Classic Load Balancer (CLB), Application Load Balancer (ALB), and others) may incur fees. The billing methods, overdue accounts, orders, and bills for these products all follow the billing logic of each product. For details, refer to [Elastic Computing Service billing guide](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ecs/Instance-type-billing), [EIP billing guide](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/eip/Billing-overview), [CLB billing guide](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/clb/Billing-01), and [ALB billing guide](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/clb/alb-billing).
