# 2. User list

You can also see a list of all users who have entered the group. The information in the user list includes the user's name, email address, role, status, and operation, as shown below:

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/b3cee681531941909d9a2dcc036a0470~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

<span id="_2-1-编辑-删除成员角色信息"></span>
## 2.1 Editing and deleting member role information


* Members with group administrator rights can click "Edit" in the operation bar to add or modify the role information of other members, as shown below.

* At the same time, you can also click "Delete" to delete group members.

   * **Once a member is deleted, they will no longer have any access to the group or access to its information.** 


<span id="_2-2-邀请用户"></span>
## 2.2 Invite users


* Group members with invitation permission can click the "Invite User" button in the user list, fill in the new user's "Volcano Account ID/Account Name/Email", and click "Confirm" to complete the invitation process after adding the corresponding role information for them.

* To add multiple users at the same time, please click the "+ Add New User" button at the bottom of the window.
