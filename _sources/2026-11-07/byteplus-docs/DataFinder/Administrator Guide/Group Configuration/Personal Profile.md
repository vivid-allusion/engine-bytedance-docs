Profile page, mainly for the display of personal information and permissions.

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/ace23fff8b5e4335b0feaaae9c300a21~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>


1. "Name, email, role, position" information will be displayed.

* Click "Modify Information" to enter the BytePlus account management page and modify: account name (ie name), mobile phone number, secure email and other information;

* Click "Change Password", and you can change the password after verifying your mobile phone number or email.

   Reminder: Non\-administrators do not have permission to modify their own or others' roles.

2. You can view the data permissions owned by individuals.
