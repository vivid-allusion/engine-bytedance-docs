<span id="_1-概述"></span>
# 1. Overview

Role management refers to the management and setting of identities, applications, products, functions, data permissions, and users for roles in the growth analysis platform.

<span id="_2-进入角色管理"></span>
# 2. Enter role management

After entering the growth analysis platform, click the settings icon in the upper right corner to enter the group settings and data management.

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/0945f33aa54040ddae7c57a1dfd8db41~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/f0adfdfca649424db604ebdb65da6a01~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

<span id="_3-查看角色信息"></span>
# 3. View character information

In the role management, you can see the basic information of all the roles under the group, as shown below:

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/478c277d10fe495b90b1fda9a49a7328~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>


* **Members** cannot see menus related to group management, including user management, role management, and group information;

* **Administrators** can add, delete or edit roles under applications and products with administrative privileges;

* **Group administrator** can add or delete all roles under the group;


<span id="_4-添加新角色"></span>
# Step 4 Add a new character

If you have the identity of administrator or above, you can click "Add New Role" in the upper right corner to add the role, as shown below:

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/761e6210abcb4f11bc1b31d4f5762786~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

"Add New Role" page, as shown below:

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/f482cc482cd14bcb80ed7b9d00ebde1f~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

**Settings Description**


|**Settings** |**Explain** |
|---|---|
|Role name |Set according to the actual business |
|Identity |Select the identity assigned to the character |
|Open application |Select the application open for this role (multiple options are available), which refers to the application already connected to your group, such as your Mini Program, or APP, etc |
|Open product |Select the products and features open for this role (multiple options are available), which refers to BytePlus user analysis (and other related data products) and corresponding product\-specific features, etc |
|Group settings permissions |Select Open access management and user management permissions in Group Management for this role, and view permissions in Data Management |
|Data permission |See which data in growth analysis can be used by the role |
|Affiliated member |Select Add relevant members for this role |


**Operation demonstration**

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/48f633e25e8b402da7cdeb59a9ceebea~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

<span id="_5-编辑角色"></span>
# 5. Editing roles

If you have the identity of an administrator or above, you can click the specific "Number of Users, Blue Number" or "Edit" button to further edit the permissions of the role, as shown below:

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/645daab1d5604fc99ce6d7ca0a1761e8~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

"Edit Roles" page, as follows:

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/37dce8060225480b9e2c462fbcc7b207~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>


* Editing applications open for this role.

* Edit the applications open for this role, the specific functions of the corresponding product, and the permissions set by the group.

* Click "OK" to complete the editing of the character and take effect.


<span id="_6-删除角色"></span>
# Step 6 Delete a character

If you have the identity of an administrator, you can click "Delete" in the operation bar to delete the role, as shown below:

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/00bee3a3b656470d8f9b142c0e048c27~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

After clicking the "Delete" button, the "Pop\-up Character" page will pop up, as shown below:

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/263ac29967db44b285ec7c2d3f9c9164~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>


* **Please note:** 

   * Once the role is deleted, the role will be removed from the list of roles in all users, and the corresponding permissions will also be removed. Please confirm further before deleting.

* If a user has only one role, and that role is deleted, the user will not be able to view any applications and products under that group.

* Click "OK" to complete the deletion of the character.
