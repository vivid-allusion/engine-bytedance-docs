### 1. DataFinder cloud hosting product model summary

BytePlus DataFinder provides enterprises with digital consumer behavior analysis insights, optimize digital touchpoints, user experience, support refined user operations, identify key growth points of business, and improve enterprise efficiency.

**The DataFinder cloud hosting model is a model that hosts data storage and computing** **to the cloud customer\-owned Bytehouse, providing enterprises with a growth solution of cloud\-based behavioral data + business data analytics, with powerful computing power, data security and controllability, and low\-cost operation and maintenance.**

### 3. Application scenarios of cloud hosting for DataFinder: **help online + offline business data fusion analysis**

**1. Business background**

At present, the cost of online customer acquisition in various industries is getting higher and higher, and customers are beginning to pursue operational transformation after customer acquisition. Online behavioral data (such as exposure, clicks, interactions, and orders) and offline transaction data (such as orders and GMV) are often stored in silos and cannot be integrated for analysis.

**2. The value of the plan**

Online behavioral data and offline business data are stored uniformly in Bytehouse, breaking data silos and deeply supporting user growth and operation transformation.

### 4. The four major advantages of cloud hosting for DataFinder

**1. Independent access to data**

Behavioral data is written into the user's own ByteHouse engine, giving customers complete autonomy to support scenarios such as online and offline data fusion, marketing conversion, operation, and recommendation.

**2. Data security**

Data storage and computing are run on the user's own volcano cloud resources (independent Bytehouse account), and the resources are isolated from tenants.

**3. Price advantage**

Cloud hosting is about 20% cheaper than privatization, providing a price advantage while maintaining data security and flexibility.

**4. Operation and maintenance are worry\-free**

The overall service is hosted on the volcano cloud, which saves the cost of cluster operation and maintenance, and the cluster resources are flexible in volume expansion and contraction.
