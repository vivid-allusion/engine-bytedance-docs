SaaS\-cloud native environment services provide you with DataFinder services in multiple regions. You can choose a region that is closer to your business service when using DataFinder. This article introduces you to the geography supported by SaaS\-cloud native environments and how to view them geographically.

<span id="9f9b4601"></span>
# Supported regions


|**SaaS\-cloud native environment** |
|---|
|Overseas regions:<br><br><br>* Southeast Asia (Johor): ap\-south\-east\-1 |


<span id="c1de510d"></span>
# Precautions


* DataFinder's SaaS\-cloud native provides you with multiple service regions. When purchasing DataFinder, you can choose the service region of DataFinder according to the region where your business is located. If you use other BytePlus products in combination, it is recommended to open the services of other BytePlus products in the same region. See the current geography supported by DataFinder [Supported Regions](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/DataFinder/Supported_Regions).

* After clarifying the DataFinder region you use, you need to report the data to the address of the corresponding region when reporting data access. If the reporting address does not match the region, the data cannot be queried and analyzed normally. Please refer to the data reporting address for various domains [Data Reporting Address](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/DataFinder/Data_Reporting_Address).

# How to check which region the current service is in?

You can refer to the following steps to see where your current service is located in a SaaS\-cloud native environment.


1. Log in and enter the DataFinder console to open any page of the product. Take the example of opening an event analysis page.

2. In the upper right corner of the page, you can view the geographical information of the DataFinder service you are currently using.

   * Asia\-Pacific Southeast (Johor): Indicate that your current service is in the Asia\-Pacific Southeast (Johor) region (ap\-southwest\-1).
