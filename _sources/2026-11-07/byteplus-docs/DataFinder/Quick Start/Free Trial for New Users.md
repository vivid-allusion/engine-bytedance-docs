DataFinder offers a free trial version for all new users. New users can apply for a free trial. The free trial version is SaaS\-Cloud Native, with a free MTU of 20,000. This article introduces you to the process of applying for a free trial for new users.

<span id="_1-账号注册和登录"></span>
# Request a free trial


1. Log in with your BytePlus account and open the [DataFinder product homepage ](https://www.byteplus.com/en/product/datafinder)and click the **Try it now** card on the page.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/08f80a0d179b4db4a0436c0fd9054e92~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

2. Fill in the applicant information on the free trial application page that pops up.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">The same user can only apply for one free trial.   </div>
   

   * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">Users who have previously purchased DataFinder products cannot apply for a free trial.   </div>
   

3. After completing the applicant submission, you will be granted free trial of DataFinder. You can then log in to the DataFinder console to start your trial.

# Transfer payment after the trial ends


* After the trial period ends, you can place an order to purchase the paid version of DataFinder. After the paid purchase is successful, you can continue to use DataFinder with your original account.

* There is one type of SaaS environment for DataFinder: SaaS\-cloud\-native. If your SaaS environment changes after the trial and you officially pay to use DataFinder, you need to contact the technical support staff to assist in the cross\-environment account and resource migration.
