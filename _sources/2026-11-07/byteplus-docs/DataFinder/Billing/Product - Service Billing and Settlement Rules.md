## Billing Description

**4.1** To protect your rights and interests, the minimum billing unit for data displayed in the BytePlus console is 10,000 MTUs or the number of events.


* **MTU** refers to monthly tracked user; in DataFinder services, MTU is defined as the number of deduplicated users reported in a given month.

* **Event volume** refers to the behavioral data you report via SDKs for trial use or via server‑side APIs, etc.; DataFinder services will count the total number of events you report each month.


**4.2** **Resource package billing description**

**4.2.1** Resource package services are ordered on an annual basis and are charged at the time of subscription. The service takes effect upon successful subscription. The validity period of each resource package of any specification is calculated from the date you successfully place the order to purchase it in the console. The specific activation time is subject to the records of the BytePlus platform.

**4.2.2** The validity period of each resource package is calculated independently. Even if multiple resource packages share the same specification, their validity periods will not be combined.

**4.2.3** If multiple resource packages you purchased are all within their validity period, the system will deduct usage from the package whose validity period ends earlier as a priority.

**4.2.4** The term of the resource package service lasts until the earlier of (i) the expiry of the service term of the resource packages you subscribed to or (ii) the complete exhaustion of the services contained in the resource packages.

**4.2.5** If there is any unused resource quota remaining when the resource package services you purchased expire, BytePlus does not support carry‑over or continued usage of such unused resources and does not support any form of refund.

**4.2.6** During the validity period of a resource package, specification upgrades or downgrades are not supported.

**4.3** If you purchase additional resource packages after the original resource package service expires or after its resources are exhausted, the usage generated from (and including) the date of such additional purchase will be deducted from the newly purchased resource package.

**4.4** **Postpaid billing description**

**4.4.1** **Billing scenarios**


* If you purchase an additional resource package within 720 hours after the quotas of your existing resource package are exhausted or its service term has expired: the service usage generated from the time the quotas are exhausted or the service term expires up to the time of the additional purchase will be billed on a postpaid basis.

* If you do not purchase an additional resource package within 720 hours after the quotas of your existing resource package are exhausted or its service term has expired: the usage generated from the time the quotas are exhausted or the service term expires up to the time the service is shut down will be billed on a postpaid basis.


**4.4.2** Postpaid billing is based on billing cycles. The bill for the previous calendar month is generated and payment is deducted within the first three working days of each month. The detailed billing and billing dates are subject to the actual bills.

## DataFinder – Arrears and Service Suspension

**6.1** **Product overage and account in arrears**:


* **Trial users:**  services will be shut down and data collection and computation will stop 12 hours after your usage quota is exhausted.

* **Formal users:** 

   * Overage data will be settled using postpaid unit prices.

   * Product services will be shut down 24 hours after your BytePlus account falls into arrears; if you top up during this period, services can be restored.

   * Data collection and computation will stop 168 hours after shutdown; if you top up during this period, services can be restored.

   * If the shutdown period exceeds 90 days, the relevant data under your BytePlus DataFinder services will be deleted and cannot be recovered.


**6.2** **Product expiration**:

> The service term of a resource package lasts until the earlier of (i) the expiry of the service term of the resource package you subscribed to or (ii) the complete exhaustion of the services contained in the resource package. After the service expires, BytePlus will no longer support your continued use of the corresponding prepaid services.


* **Trial users:**  services will be shut down and data collection and computation will stop 12 hours after expiration.

* **Formal users:** 

   * Services will be shut down 12 hours after expiration; if you purchase additional resource packages during this period, services can be restored.

   * Data collection and computation will stop 720 hours after shutdown; if you purchase additional resource packages during this period, services can be restored.

   * If the shutdown period exceeds 90 days, the relevant data under your BytePlus DataFinder services will be deleted and cannot be recovered.

   * When the remaining resources in a resource package are less than 10% of the total resources, you should promptly purchase additional resource packages or contact BytePlus to arrange service shutdown upon expiry.


**6.3** You acknowledge and agree that if service suspension or reclamation occurs due to your failure to make timely payments or renewals, you shall bear all losses arising therefrom.
