An order form is a standard contract signed between you and the BytePlus platform, which contains information on business discounts that BytePlus offers to you. It is signed electronically and will be affixed with the electronic seal of BytePlus after you click Agree. You can log in to the BytePlus console and go to Billing center \> Contract to sign the electronic version of the contract by clicking Agree. The electronic version of the contract has the same legal effect as the paper version.

<span id="order-form-querying"></span>
# Order form querying

After your customer manager initiates a quotation and an order form and completes the internal approval process of BytePlus, the order form will be displayed on the Contract page in the Billing center of your BytePlus console. Then, BytePlus will send an email to the mailbox associated with your BytePlus account and an on\-site message on the BytePlus website to remind you that you have received a to\-be\-confirmed order form. The specific sending channel is subject to the channel that you choose to receive messages in the message center of the BytePlus website.

You can query your order forms in the following ways:

1. Click the URL in your email or on\-site message to jump to the Order Form page.

2. Log in to the BytePlus console, go to the Billing center, and click [Contract to go to the Order Form page.](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/contract)

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_ca429f83595793f04ed32f4d94ac7301.png) </span>

The Order Form page displays details about each order form in the following fields: Contract number, Service Term, Customer Confirmation Date, Status, and Remark. You can search for order forms by their contract number, service term, and creation time on this page.

To download the PDF version of an order form to check the terms and business discounts, click Download in the Operation column on the right side.

1. For a "To be confirmed" order form, the downloaded document is not affixed with an electronic seal.

2. For a "To be effective" or "Become effective" order form, the downloaded document is affixed with an electronic seal.

<span id="signing-an-order-form"></span>
# Signing an order form

<span id="_1-window-period-for-signing-an-order-form"></span>
## 1. Window period for signing an order form

After receiving a "To be confirmed" order form, you must sign or reject it within thirty (30) days. Otherwise, it becomes "Invalid".

You cannot sign an invalid order form. If you still need it, request your customer manager to initiate the order form again.

<span id="_2-status-description"></span>
## 2. Status description


* To be confirmed: Your customer manager has pushed the order form to you. You have received it for no more than thirty (30) days, and you have not confirmed it.

* Have been rejected: You have clicked Reject to reject the order form.

* In contract generation: You have clicked Agree to sign the order form, and the system is processing it.

* Contract generation failed: You have clicked Agree to sign the order form, but an error occurred while processing it. Please contact your customer manager.

* To be effective: You have clicked Agree to sign the order form. The system has processed it, but the current time is earlier than the service start date.

* Become effective: You have clicked Agree to sign the order form. The system has processed it, and the current time is within the service term of the order form.

* Expired: You have clicked Agree to sign the order form. The system has processed it, but the current time is later than the expiration time of the order form.

* Terminated: You have clicked Agree to sign the order form. The system has processed it, but it is terminated before it expires.

* Invalid: Your customer manager has pushed the order form to you, but you have not signed or rejected it within thirty (30) days.


<span id="_3-agreeing-to-sign-an-order-form"></span>
## 3. Agreeing to sign an order form

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_ffc9acd90bd301b31fd95f153c92de6b.png) </span>

If you agree to the content of an order form, you can sign it by clicking Agree in the Operation column.

After you click Agree and confirm an order form, its status changes to "In contract generation", and it goes to the system signing process. The status of the order form becomes "To be effective" after it is signed. When the current time is later than or equal to the start time of the validity period, the status of the order form changes to "Effective". The business discounts of an order form apply only when the order form is in the "Become effective" state.

Note: If you agree to sign an order form after the start date of the validity period of its business discounts, the system automatically sets the signing date as the new start date of the validity period. If you agree to sign an order form before the start date of the validity period of its business discounts, the original start date remains unchanged.

<span id="_4-rejecting-an-order-form"></span>
## 4. Rejecting an order form

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_fcc2cc8097c28f874f9750c64c2955d0.png) </span>

If you do not accept the terms and conditions of an order form, you can click the More icon in the Operation column and click Reject to reject signing it. After you click Reject, a pop\-up window appears, inquiring about the reason why you want to reject signing the order form. Please select the true reason based on the actual situation. Then, the status of the order form changes to "Have been rejected". If you want to sign it later on, please request your customer manager to reinitiate it.

Reasons for rejection: the contact information is incorrect, the customer name is incorrect, the business discount needs to be discussed, and the signing method needs to be modified.


* The contact information is incorrect: The contact information in the order form is different from your actual contact information.

* The customer name is incorrect: The customer name in the order form is different from your real name.

* The signing method needs to be modified: You want to sign the paper contract offline instead of signing an order form electronically.

* The business discount needs to be discussed: The business discount in the order form is not the same as what you expected.
