Coupons are account assets issued by BytePlus. You can use coupons to offset prepaid order amounts and postpaid bill amounts. To view your coupons, log in to the BytePlus console and go to **Billing** \> [Coupon](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/coupon).

Coupons cannot be withdrawn, transferred, invoiced, extended, shared across accounts, or applied to overdue payments. After a coupon is used, it is non\-refundable.

<span id="query-coupons"></span>
# Query Coupons


1. Log into the BytePlus console and click [Billing](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/overview) in the upper right corner to enter the Billing Center dashboard.

2. Click [Coupon](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/coupon) in the left navigation pane to open the coupon list page.

3. On the coupon list page, you can view each coupon's name, ID, denomination, balance, applicable products, payment method, applicable order types, usage restrictions, validity period, status, and other relevant information. You can also filter coupons by status and effective time.


<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260521095012-402379462669.png) </span>


4. Click the coupon name in the **Coupon Name/ID** column to view its usage details.

<span id="use-coupons"></span>
# Use Coupons

Coupons issued by BytePlus can currently be used for prepaid (yearly/monthly) orders and postpaid (pay\-as\-you\-go) bills.

For prepaid orders, on the order confirmation page, the system matches and displays available coupons based on multiple factors, such as the product associated with the order, order type, and order amount. Only one coupon can be used per order. When multiple coupons are available, the system selects one by default based on priority. You can choose another available coupon based on your needs, or choose not to use a coupon for the current order. If the coupon balance is less than the order amount, the remaining amount must be paid using another payment method, such as a credit card, PayPal, or credit limit.

<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260521095013-573830337890.png) </span>

For postpaid bills, BytePlus matches available coupons when a bill is generated and payment is deducted. The matching process considers factors such as the product and bill amount. If multiple coupons are available, BytePlus applies them in priority order until the bill is fully offset or all available coupon balances are used. If the total coupon balance is lower than the bill amount, you must pay the remaining amount.

<span id="coupon-usage-restrictions"></span>
# Coupon Usage Restrictions

<span id="_1-coupon-status"></span>
## 1. Coupon Status

Coupons may have one of the following statuses: **Not Yet Effective**, **Effective**, **Invalid**, **Used Up**, or **Obsolete**.


* **Not Yet Effective**: The coupon has not yet reached its effective date and still has a remaining balance.

* **Effective**: The coupon is within its validity period and still has a remaining balance.

* **Invalid**: The coupon becomes invalid if either of the following applies: it has expired while still having a remaining balance, or it is a single\-use coupon that has already been used.

* **Used Up**: The coupon balance has been fully used during the validity period.

* **Obsolete**: The coupon has been reclaimed by the platform backend.


<span id="_2-applicable-product-scope"></span>
## 2. Applicable Product Scope

A coupon may apply to one or more BytePlus product configurations. The applicable product scope depends on the channel or campaign through which the coupon is issued. The actual applicable scope is based on the **Applicable Products** information on the Coupon Management page, or on the available coupons automatically matched on the order confirmation page.


1. **Product**:


The coupon applies to spending generated by the specified product.


2. **Configuration**:

   1. **All Configurations**: The coupon applies to spending generated under all configurations of the applicable product.

   2. **All Prepaid Configurations**: The coupon applies to spending generated under all prepaid configurations of the applicable product.

   3. **All Postpaid Configurations**: The coupon applies to spending generated under all postpaid configurations of the applicable product.

   4. **Specified Configurations** (specific configuration names displayed): The coupon applies to spending generated under the specified configurations of the applicable product.

3. **Billing Item**:

   1. **All Billing Items**: The coupon applies to spending generated by all billing items under the specified configuration of the applicable product.

   2. **Specified Billing Items** (specific billing items displayed): The coupon applies to spending generated by the specified billing items under the specified configuration of the applicable product.


<span id="_3-prepaid-purchase-duration-restriction"></span>
## 3. Prepaid Purchase Duration Restriction

Within the validity period, the duration of each purchase, configuration change, or renewal is restricted. For example, for a product configuration with monthly billing, if the coupon's purchase duration restriction is 0\-6 months, the purchase duration must fall within that range in order to use the coupon.

**Purchase duration restrictions for daily, monthly, and yearly billing are independent and cannot be converted between billing methods.**  For example, if a coupon's purchase duration restriction is monthly: 0\-6 months and yearly: 0\-1 year, then when purchasing a product configuration with monthly billing, the system applies the monthly purchase duration restriction. In that case, a 12\-month purchase would fall outside the 0\-6 month range, so the coupon could not be used.

<span id="_4-applicable-order-types"></span>
## 4. Applicable Order Types

For prepaid orders, a coupon may apply to one or more of the following order types: **Purchase**, **End Trial**, **Change Configuration**, **Renew**, and **Temporary Upgrade**.

The applicable order types depend on the channel or campaign through which the coupon is issued. The actual supported order types are based on the **Applicable Orders** information on the Coupon Management page, or on the available coupons automatically matched on the order confirmation page.

<span id="_5-minimum-amount-threshold"></span>
## 5. Minimum Amount Threshold

For a single payment, the order or bill amount must reach a specified threshold (USD XX) before the coupon can be applied.

<span id="_6-valid-period"></span>
## 6. Valid Period

A coupon can only be used within its valid period. If the coupon has not yet become effective when you place a prepaid order or when a postpaid bill is generated, please wait until it becomes effective before using it. If the coupon has already expired when you place a prepaid order or when a postpaid bill is generated, it can no longer be used. The validity period of a coupon cannot be extended.

<span id="_7-usage-limits"></span>
## 7. Usage Limits

Depending on the usage count setting, a coupon can be either a single\-use coupon or a multi\-use coupon.


* **Single Use**: The coupon can only be used once. After it is used, if the remaining balance is greater than 0, the status changes to **Invalid**; if the remaining balance is 0, the status changes to **Used Up**. Regardless of any remaining balance, the coupon cannot be used again.

* **Multiple Uses**: The coupon can be used multiple times until it expires or the balance is used up.


<span id="_8-specified-applicable-accounts"></span>
## 8. Specified Applicable Accounts

If your account is a finance trusteeship master account, commercial representatives can specify the applicable account scope when issuing a coupon.


* If there is no restriction on the applicable account scope, the coupon page will not display the **Specified Applicable Accounts** section. This means that the financially managed master account and all of its managed sub\-accounts can share and use the coupon.

* If the applicable account scope is restricted, the coupon page will display the **Specified Applicable Accounts** field. This means that only the account IDs listed there can use the coupon.

* If the **Specified Applicable Accounts** field shows the managed master account, only that managed master account itself can use the coupon when placing orders or generating bills.

* If the **Specified Applicable Accounts** field includes managed sub\-accounts, only those specified managed sub\-accounts can use the coupon when placing orders or generating bills during the management period. Once the management relationship is terminated, those sub\-accounts can no longer use the coupon.


&nbsp;

If your account is a Tier\-1 distributor partner, commercial representatives can specify the applicable account scope when issuing a coupon.


* If there is no restriction on the applicable account scope, the coupon page will not display the **Specified Applicable Accounts** section. This means that the coupon can be used both for your own billing and for supplementary orders placed for end customers.

* If the applicable account scope is restricted, the coupon page will display the **Specified Applicable Accounts** field. This means that only the account IDs listed there can use the coupon.

* If the **Specified Applicable Accounts** field shows the Tier\-1 distributor partner, only that Tier\-1 distributor partner can use the coupon when placing orders or generating bills for itself.

* If the **Specified Applicable Accounts** field shows a specified end customer, the coupon can only be used when you place supplementary orders for that end customer. It cannot be used for supplementary orders placed for non\-specified end customers.


<span id="_9-coupon-deduction-priority"></span>
## 9. Coupon Deduction Priority

To improve payment success rates, BytePlus determines coupon priority using the rules below. If multiple coupons have the same priority under one rule, BytePlus applies the next rule.


1. **Larger deductible amount \> Smaller deductible amount**: The coupon with the larger deductible amount is prioritized.

2. **Closer expiration date \> Later expiration date**: The coupon that expires sooner is prioritized.

3. **Multiple Uses \> Single Use**: Multi\-use coupons are prioritized.

4. **Smaller remaining balance \> Larger remaining balance**: The coupon with the smaller remaining balance is prioritized.

5. **Narrower applicable scope \> Broader applicable scope**: The coupon with the narrower applicable product scope is prioritized.

6. **Higher amount threshold \> Lower amount threshold**: The coupon with the higher spending threshold, such as spend USD XX to use, is prioritized.

7. **Fewer specified applicable account restrictions \> More specified applicable account restrictions**: The coupon with fewer specified applicable account restrictions is prioritized.

8. If coupon priority still cannot be determined after all rules are applied, BytePlus randomly displays coupons with the same priority first in prepaid scenarios. For postpaid and auto\-renewal scenarios, BytePlus randomly deducts from coupons with the same priority.


<span id="obtain-and-redeem-coupons"></span>
# Obtain and Redeem Coupons

<span id="_1-obtain-coupons"></span>
## 1. Obtain Coupons

You can obtain coupons through the following channels:


* BytePlus campaign pages. Coupons issued through different promotional campaigns may vary. For details about current campaigns, refer to the BytePlus official website.

* Other channels that provide coupon redemption codes. To redeem these coupons, follow the coupon redemption process below.


<span id="_2-redeem-coupons"></span>
## 2. Redeem Coupons


1. In the BytePlus console, go to **Billing Center** \> **Coupon**, click **Coupon Redemption** in the upper\-right corner of the coupon list, enter the redemption code in the pop\-up window, and click **Redeem**.


<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260521095012-1424393602728.png) </span>


1. When purchasing BytePlus products, go to the order confirmation page, click **Coupon Redemption** on the right side, enter the coupon redemption code in the pop\-up window, and then click **Redeem** to complete the redemption.


<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260521095013-42059916159.png) </span>
