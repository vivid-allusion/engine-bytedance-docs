## Rules


* You can specify the billing date for resources set to auto\-renewal. The system will attempt to charge the fee on your specified date. Please ensure there is sufficient balance in your account.

* If no auto\-renewal billing date is specified, the system starts charging seven days before resource expiration at 00:00 by default. If an auto\-renewal billing date has been set, the system will initiate auto\-renewal on the set date at 00:00 AM.

* Changes to the auto\-renewal billing date apply to renewals scheduled after the original date.


<span id="86c96ab6"></span>
### Steps


1. Go to the Renewal Management page and click Set Auto\-Renewal Billing Date at the top right of the renewal list.


<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_4c8776bd96ed06acda9ececb80abc212.png) </span>


2. Set the auto\-renewal billing date. Choose a day from seven days before expiration to the day of expiration (charging will start at 00:00:00 on the specified day).


<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_10d6dfce7178ba5da66cc9513002d308.png) </span>


3. Select the checkbox below to confirm the auto\-renewal billing date, and click OK to complete the setup.


<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_1b00e6d4defad0e02e4ffd7491d943b3.png) </span>
