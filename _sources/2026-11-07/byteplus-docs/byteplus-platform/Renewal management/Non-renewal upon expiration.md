## **Rules**


1. You can set instances that are no longer needed to non\-renewal upon expiration and find them in the Non\-Renewal Upon Expiration tab later.

2. Instances set to non\-renewal upon expiration will enter a retention period after expiration, during which the service will be suspended and unusable, but the resources and data will be preserved. If continued service is needed, manually renew the instance promptly. The instance will be automatically released at the end of the retention period.

3. Instances already expired do not support changing the renewal type to non\-renewal upon expiration.

4. "Running" instances set to "non\-renewal upon expiration" support batch renewal, manual renewal, switching to manual renewal, and enabling auto\-renewal. "To be terminated" instances only support batch renewal, manual renewal, and switching to manual renewal.

5. For instances set to non\-renewal upon expiration, the pre\-expiration warning, termination notice, and release notice will be sent through emails and site messages seven, three, and one day before expiration and on the day of expiration, respectively. Please watch for these notifications.


<span id="406d9fae"></span>
## Steps


1. Go to Billing Center \> Renewal Management \> Manual Renewal/Auto\-Renewal, select the target instances, and click Non\-Renewal Upon Expiration in the operation column, or the Non\-Renewal Upon Expiration button at the bottom of the page to perform a batch operation.


<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_5ec208d0bd67c351098fa62a8db05f8d.png) </span>


2. The Non\-Renewal Upon Expiration page will be displayed. Click OK to complete the setting.


<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_5289c25037e386de3125ed708d7c4581.png) </span>


3. To view the instances set to non\-renewal upon expiration, visit Billing Center \> Renewal Management \> Non\-Renewal Upon Expiration.

4. Instances set to non\-renewal upon expiration can be manually renewed by clicking Renew or Batch Renew. On the renewal confirmation page that appears, set the renewal duration, click Pay, and proceed with the payment to complete the renewal.

5. You can switch one or more instances to manual renewal or auto\-renewal (only before they expire) as needed. Upon successful update of the renewal mode, the instances will be automatically listed under the Manual Renewal or Automatic Renewal tab.
