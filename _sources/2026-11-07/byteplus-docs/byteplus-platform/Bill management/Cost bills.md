# Feature Overview

Cost bills feature offers fee amortization capabilities and allows you to allocate your cloud resource fees over calendar months based on designated allocation rules, detailing allocation data of both prepaid fees and postpaid fees.

Description: Cost data, as allocation data calculated based on specific algorithms, is for reference only and can not be used for reconciliation. Please use bill details or transaction details for reconciliation.

<span id="operation-procedure"></span>
# Operation Procedure

1. Activate Cost bills service. Choose "[Bill Management - Cost bills](https://console.volcengine.com/finance/bill/cost-bill/)" and activate cost bills service. After activation, you can view cost bills data generated starting from the month of activation. A two\-day delay exists between cost bills and bill details.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_a1de0d141f8808220ce1d6397abdb2fb.png) </span>

2. View cost bills data. After activation, the service needs one or two days for data initialization. After that, you can enter the [Cost bills](https://console.volcengine.com/finance/bill/cost-bill/) service to view the overview and detailed cost data, including the following fields:


* Month of allocation: a calendar month over which a fee is allocated. For example, if you spend USD 365 on a one\-year subscription of an Elastic Compute Service (ECS) instance, then the amount allocated to the month January is USD 31.

* Accounting period: the accounting period to which the bill of an allocated fee belongs. For example, if you purchase a one\-year subscription of an ECS instance in January, then the original bill belongs to January but Cost bills service can allocate the fee to months based on the actual period of resource usage.

* Allocation type: the type of allocation. Supported allocation types include new purchase fee allocation, renewal fee allocation, specification change fee allocation, and pay\-as\-you\-go fee allocation.

* Allocation start time and allocation end time: the shortest period for resource fee allocation. For prepaid fees, the allocation start time and end time are usually 00:00:00 and 23:59:59 on a day. For postpaid fees, the allocation period is the settlement period in the corresponding bill details.

* Daily allocated amount \- Pre\-tax amount: For a prepaid fee, this field indicates the average amount allocated to each day, with the third and later decimal places removed. The last period allocated amount is the difference between the total amount and the amount already allocated. For a postpaid fee, this field is the pre\-tax amount in the bill details. The allocation logic of fields like the gross amount is the same as that of the Pre\-tax amount.

* Number of days with allocated amounts: the number of days to which amounts have been allocated in a month. For example, if a prepaid fee is allocated from May 1 to 15, then the number of days with allocated amounts in May is 15. For a postpaid fee, the number of days with allocated amounts is the number of days over the allocation period in the corresponding cost details.

* Already allocated for current period \- Pre\-tax amount: the pre\-tax amount that has been allocated to the month of allocation. For example, if you spend USD 365 on a one\-year subscription of an ECS instance in January, then the amount allocated to May is USD 31. The allocation logic of fields like the gross amount is the same as that of the pre\-tax amount.

* Already allocated for previous period \- Pre\-tax amount: the pre\-tax amount that has so far been allocated before the beginning of the current month. For example, if you spend USD 365 on a one\-year subscription of an ECS instance in January, then the amount already allocated at the beginning of May is the sum of the amounts allocated from January to April. The allocation logic of fields like the gross amount is the same as that of the pre\-tax amount.

* Amount not allocated \- Pre\-tax amount: the amount left of the total bill amount minus the amount already allocated at the beginning of the current period and the amount allocated over the current period. The allocation logic of fields like the gross amount is the same as that of the pre\-tax amount.

* Other fields: refer to the description of bill details in [Bill details](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-bill-details).


3. Obtain the Cost bills data. You can obtain the data by exporting from the page. 1) Export from the page: Click "Export Bill CSV" and select the Cost bills data you want to export.
