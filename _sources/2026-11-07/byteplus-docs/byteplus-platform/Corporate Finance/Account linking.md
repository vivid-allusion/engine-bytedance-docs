Corporate Finance Account Linking allows you to link a sub\-account to a master account, so that the master account can centrally manage **billing, payment, contract discounts, and resource packages** across all of its sub\-accounts. Once linked, the consumption of each sub\-account is settled under the master account, and the master account is responsible for invoicing and payment on its behalf.

This guide walks you through the linkable conditions, the linking steps in the BytePlus Console, and how the linked account confirms the invitation.

<span id="j6IF0WHU"></span>
## **After linking, the account settlement will change as follows:** 


1. The sub\-account uses the contract discount of the master account; the contract discount of the sub\-account (if any) is no longer available.

2. The sub\-account prioritizes using its own resource package, and also shares the resource package of the master account.

3. The sub\-account uses the coupons and payment methods of the master account (coupons of the sub\-account are no longer available).

4. The consumption of the sub\-account is settled under the master account, and the master account is responsible for billing, invoicing, and payment.


<span id="w2A9VWca"></span>
## Linkable Conditions

**Master account**


1. The account profile is complete and has no link with other accounts.

2. Must have a credit line, and the available balance must be greater than or equal to $0.00.


**Sub account**


1. The organization name and registered country/region must be consistent with those of the master account.

2. Must not have an established corporate finance relationship with other accounts.

3. The available balance of a managed sub\-account must be ≥ 0.

4. A managed sub\-account must not have orders in intermediate statuses, including but not limited to:

   * Orders submitted successfully but not yet paid (pending payment orders).

   * Orders submitted or paid successfully but not yet finalized, including configuration upgrades in progress, refunds in progress, renewals in progress, etc.
