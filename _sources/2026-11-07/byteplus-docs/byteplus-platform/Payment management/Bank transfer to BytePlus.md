BytePlus assigned exclusive bank account to every customer who has verified BytePlus account. When you transfer to your exclusive bank account and the remittance currency is consistent with the payment settlement currency, the system will automatically recognize the transfer amount to your BytePlus account and automatically pay the unpaid bills.

**Notice:** 


* Please make sure to transfer to the correct **exclusive bank account** as your incorrect input may directly result in the funds being transferred to someone else's Byteplus account.

* If the currencies are inconsistent, please contact your account manager.

* The exclusive bank account number is different from the Byteplus bank account number in our contract. It is a virtual account number assigned by the bank for each individual customer. For any clarification, please refer to the bank account information in your BytePlus account.

* Time limit for funds to arrive in the account：**within 7 calendar days.**

## To make a bank transfer:

**Step1:**  Sign in to the [Billing center-Payment method](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/payment-methods), click【Bank transfer】

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_f624d9912a4eb2f3ba65029ea0f2c2c7.png) </span>

**Step2:**  Get **Bank account information**, especially **exclusive bank account**

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_69b7f3e7b252fa99387a9463d434bead.png) </span>

**Step3:**  transfer to your **exclusive bank account**
