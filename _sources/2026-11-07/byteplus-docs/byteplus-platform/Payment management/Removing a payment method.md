You can remove a payment method if necessary.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">The default payment method cannot be removed directly. You must change the default payment method before removing it.</div>


**To remove a payment method:** 


1. Sign in to the [BytePlus Console](https://console.byteplus.com/auth/login/).

2. On the top navigation bar, click **Billing** \> **Payment methods**.

3. Click **Three dots menu** \> **Remove card**.

   <span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/doc-center/1c543f7-remove_a_card.png) </span>

   The **Remove Card** pop\-up window appears.

4. Click **Remove Card**.

   The card is removed from the payment methods.
