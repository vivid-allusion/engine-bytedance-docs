You can add a payment method through the Billing Center to pay for BytePlus services.

<span id="54d597ca"></span>
## **Prerequisite**

Make sure you have set up billing for your BytePlus account. For more information, refer to [Setting up billing](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/byteplus-platform/docs/signing-up-your-account#fb6c7c91).

<span id="2646497a"></span>
## **Procedure**


1. Sign in to the [BytePlus Console](https://console.byteplus.com/auth/login/).

2. On the top navigation bar, click **Billing** \> **Payment method**.

3. Click **Add payment method**.

   <span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/doc-center/3f08686-add_payment_method.png) </span>

   The **Add credit or credit card** pop\-up window appears.

4. Fill in the card information, including the card number, expiry data, security code, and cardholder name.

5. (Optional) Check **Set as default payment method**.

6. Check **I have read and agree with** [BytePlus payment agreement](https://docs.byteplus.com/legal/docs/payment-terms) .

7. Click **Submit**.

   The card is added as a payment method.
