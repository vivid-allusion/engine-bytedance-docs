BytePlus provides all clients with the post payment option. Once you are granted the qualification for post payment, you can use BytePlus cloud services first and pay for them later. You can obtian the qualification through either of the following methods:


* Online granting: To obtain the qualification, you need to verify a phone number, add the billing information, and add a payment method. Complete these steps through [setting up billing](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/byteplus-platform/docs/setting-up-billing) for your BytePlus account.

   <span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/doc-center/9de502d-Setting_up_billing.png) </span>

* Offline granting: You need to contact your sales support specialist to enable post payment for you, otherwise BytePlus defaults to online payment.
