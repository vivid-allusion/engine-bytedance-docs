To provide a flexible payment option, BytePlus can grant certain credit for customers based on the contract.

There are 3 operation steps for customers to apply credit.

<span id="c5c2d672"></span>
# Apply the Credit Limit

Please contact your sales expert from Byteplus to apply the credit limit.

Usually the amount of credit limit is evaluated based on the 3 times of average monthly bill.

<span id="158ff64e"></span>
# Internal approval process

Once receiving the Credit Limit application, BytePlus will carry out an internal approval process based on the information provided and a reasonable caution judgement.

<span id="96fc7aa6"></span>
# Credit Agreement Acceptance

After the Byteplus configuration for Credit on your account is done, a Credit agreement signing request will be generated and pushed to the BytePlus console, and which has to be confirmed and accepted by you.

The detailed operation steps are shown below.
