# What is the AI savings plans?

The AI savings plans is a subscription plan provided by BytePlus for large model products. By committing to a consistent monthly spend amount for a fixed term (6/12 months), you secure significantly lower billing rates (credit usage) for pay\-as\-you\-go models.

AI Savings Plans is not a standalone large model service, it is designed to be used in conjunction with Pay\-As\-You\-Go large model products. For users with consistent, long\-term Pay\-As\-You\-Go usage, this plan can drive a significant reduction in model usage costs.

Key factors include:

* Flexible usage: Unlike subscriptions tied to specific resources, this plan applies to all listed BytePlus Model usage, allowing you to use them with significant discounts.

* Tiered discounts: Discounts are calculated based on your commitment tiers. Higher monthly commitments and longer contract terms unlock higher discount tiers.

Ideal Use Cases: Best suited for businesses with steady, predictable model usage who wish to reduce long\-term operational costs while retaining the flexibility of Pay\-As\-You\-Go billing.![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_c5d248a0222c3cc49d458f6260c95025.png)

# Key advantages

* Unused credits rollover: If your actual usage is lower than the monthly commitment, the unused credits (up to 20% of the monthly committed amount) will roll over to the next commitment period.

* Customizable commitment amount: The commitment amount can be customized based on customers' predicted usage.

* Automatic Credit Activation: If the quota for the current cycle is used up ahead of schedule, or when the current cycle reaches its scheduled end time, the system will automatically activate the quota for the next cycle.

* Comprehensive coverage: Applies to most BytePlus Large Models, with no restrictions on specific configurations.

* Maximize savings : Unlock greater discounts with higher commitment amounts and longer commitment terms, up to 50 **%.**

* Unified management: Monitor real\-time usage and manage your plans effortlessly through the centralized Billing Center.

> **Rollover Rules**

* > If unused credits exceeds 20% of the monthly commitment, the amount rolled over to the next period will be capped at 20% of the monthly commitment.

* > If unused credits is less than or equal to 20% of the monthly commitment, all unused credits will be rolled over to the next period.

* > Any unused credits in the last period of the commitment term will expire at the end of the billing cycle and will not be rolled over, regardless of renewal.

# Billing & Offset Rules

Your committed monthly spend & term entitles you to tiered discounts. These rules determine how those discounts are applied to your bill.

* **Discount Priority & Exclusion**

   * Discount Priority: When multiple discounts are available, they are applied in this order: Reserved Instances \> Resource Packs \> Savings Plans \> Vouchers.

   * Best Rate Guarantee: If you hold both a Contract Discount and a Savings Plans Discount, the system automatically applies the higher discount for your usage.

   * Exclusions: Please note that marketing and promotional discounts are not eligible for the Savings Plans.（**Note: From August 7, 14:00 to September 7, 14:00, promotional discounts apply to Dreamina\-Seedance 2.0 Mini and Dreamina\-Seedance 2.0 Fast. The promotion is available to all customers under the unified AI Savings Plan, and usage remains eligible for Savings Plan commitment deduction.** ）

* **Excess Usage**

   * Usage that exceeds your monthly committed spend limit is considered "Excess Usage."

   * Excess usage is billed at your standard Pay\-As\-You\-Go rate (incorporating any pre\-existing contract discounts you may have), and will not affect the next monthly renewal cycle.

* **Unused Credits**

   * If there is any unused credits at the end of a commitment period, up to 20% of the monthly commitment amount in unused credits may be rolled over to the next period. Notably, no rollover is provided for the final billing cycle, regardless of renewal. Also, you will still be billed for the full monthly commitment amount for each cycle.

Note: For more general offset rules, refer to: [Application of savings plans](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/application-of-savings-plans)

# **Credit Activation Rules**

Credit activation under the AI Unified Savings Plan follows the rule of “activate upon quota exhaustion or cycle expiry, whichever comes first.” During the effective term of the Savings Plan, each cycle has a corresponding committed quota.The system automatically activates the next cycle quota when either condition is met:

* The current cycle reaches its scheduled end time.

* The quota for the current cycle has been fully used.

If the current cycle quota is used up early, the system automatically activates the next cycle quota to continue offsetting eligible pay\-as\-you\-go usage. Otherwise, the next cycle quota is activated when the current cycle ends as scheduled.

Early activation of the quota for the next cycle only means that the quota for that cycle becomes available earlier. It does not change that cycle’s scheduled end time, nor does it increase the total committed quota of the Savings Plan.

|Payment Model |Credit Activation Description |Billing Description |
|---|---|---|
|No Upfront |The customer fulfills the contract by cycle. If the current cycle quota is used up early, the system automatically activates the next cycle quota. |The system also issues the next cycle Savings Plan bill at activation. No duplicate bill is generated when that cycle begins.<br><br> |
|All Upfront<br><br> |The customer has prepaid all cycles. If the current cycle quota is used up, the system automatically activates the purchased quota for the next cycle to continue offsetting eligible charges. |No new bill will be generated.<br><br> |

**Customer Examples**

* **Example 1 (No Upfront):** A customer purchases a 12\-month Savings Plan with a monthly quota of 1 million under No Upfront. If the January quota is used up on January 15, the system automatically activates the next cycle quota to offset eligible usage after January 15 and issues the next cycle bill at the same time.

* **Example 2(All Upfront): ** A customer purchases a 12\-month Savings Plan with a monthly quota of 1 million under All Upfront. If the January quota is used up on January 15, the system automatically activates the purchased quota for the next cycle to offset eligible usage after January 15. No new bill is generated.

In both cases, early activation only makes the next cycle quota available sooner and does not change that cycle’s scheduled end time.

**Quota Usage Rules**

The system will use available quota in the following order:

1. First, any available quota carried over from the previous cycle;

2. Then, the committed quota for the current cycle;

3. The next cycle quota is activated only after both the carried\-over quota and current cycle quota are used up.

IIf a customer has multiple AI savings plans, the system first uses an eligible plan in the current cycle. If current available quota is insufficient, it activates future\-cycle quota based on system rules, usually from the plan that expires earlier.

**Quota Cap**

Early quota activation means using quota from future cycles in advance, not receiving additional quota. Savings Plan discounts apply only up to the plan’s total committed amount. Once all quota under the plan has been used, any excess usage will be billed at the standard rates of the applicable product.

2. ### **Suspension Policy for Outstanding Balances**

* "All Upfront" Payment Type:

   * Since this plan is fully pre\-paid, its benefits are guaranteed for the entire contract term. It remains active and unaffected until the limit is reached or the plan expires, even if other products under your account are in non\-payment status.

* "No Upfront" Payment Type:

   * Billing Cycle: Bills are generated and charged at the start of each monthly cycle. (Monthly installment)

   * Grace Period: If a payment fails, your account enters a 3\-day grace period where credit offset from your remaining balance still applies.

   * Suspension: If the bill remains unpaid after 3 days, the plan enters "Restricted" status. During this time, the automated credit offset is paused, and usage will be billed at standard rates.

   * Reactivation: Savings benefits resume immediately once the outstanding balance is settled.

> Note:

Please settle promptly to avoid business disruption.

# Upgrade

You may upgrade your monthly commitment amount at any time during the AI Savings Plans validity. The upgrade takes effect from **the next billing cycle** after payment and does not affect discounts applied to prior cycles.

Two upgrade options are available:

| |**Upgrade without Renewal (Expiration Date Unchanged)**  |**Upgrade with Renewal (Expiration Date Extended)**  |
|---|---|---|
|**Core Logic** |Re\-match discount based on "New Amount + Remaining Term" |Re\-match discount based on "New Amount + New Term" |
|**Eligibility** |Remaining term ≥ 6 months |New term = Original commitment term |
|**Expiration Date** |**Unchanged** |**Original expiration date + new commitment term** |

## Examples

* **Example 1 (Without Renewal):**  Original plan: $100K/month, 12\-month term, 4 months elapsed (remaining 8 months ≥ 6 months). Customer upgrades to $450K/month → Per the downward\-matching principle, discount is matched based on "$450K + 6 months" and applies to the remaining 8 billing cycles. Expiration date unchanged.

* **Example 2 (With Renewal):**  Original plan: $100K/month, 12\-month term, 7 months elapsed (remaining 5 months < 6 months). Customer upgrades to $450/month → Must use "Upgrade with Renewal". New term = Original term = 12 months. Discount matched based on "$450 + 12 months". New expiration date = Original expiration date + 12 months.

**Note:**  Under "Upgrade Without Renewal," if the new amount does not reach a higher tier, your discount rate may increase. Example: original plan $150/month for 12 months, 1 month elapsed, upgraded to $160/month → matched based on "$160 + 6 months," Category A discount rises from 28%off to 26%off.

> Note:

* > If the customer has an existing cycle that has already been activated in advance, upgrades are not supported.

* > If an upgrade is made, the system will treat the upgraded plan as a new order that takes effect in the next cycle. If the quota for the current cycle is exhausted early, early offset is not supported for the upgraded portion due to audit risk. Once the upgraded cycle takes effect, early offset will continue to be supported.

### Upgrade Process

1. Log in to the BytePlus Console and navigate to the "[Savings Plans](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/saving-plan)" management page.

2. Select the Savings Plans order to upgrade and click "Upgrade."

3. Choose the upgrade method (Upgrade Without Renewal / Upgrade With Renewal).

4. Enter the new commitment amount; the system will automatically match the corresponding discount tier.

5. Confirm and complete payment. The upgrade takes effect from the next billing cycle.

# Renewal

You can manage plans through two methods prior to expiration: Manual Renewal or Auto\-Renewal.

By default, renewing your plan maintains your existing Commitment Tier and Commitment Term.

Instead of a standard renewal, you can choose to upgrade your plan. Increasing your Commitment Tier and/or extending your Contract Term will qualify you for better discount rates.

> **Additional Note:**

* > For "All Upfront" Payment Types: If Auto\-Renewal is enabled, please ensure funds are available prior to renewal date to avoid renewal failure and potential loss of savings coverage.

* > **Starting April 7, 2026, the legacy version of the Savings Plans will no longer be available for new purchases or renewals.**

# Unsubscribe

This product does not support unsubscription. Once an order has been successfully paid, refunds or modifications are not supported, except as otherwise required by law or explicitly committed by the platform.In case of special circumstances, please [submit a support ticket](https://console.byteplus.com/workorder/create) for review.
