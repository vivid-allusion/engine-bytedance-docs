* ## **Available Balance components** <span id="gFCbABcz"></span>


  The account available balance consists of four parts: credits, credit limit, unpaid amount, and frozen amount.


|Available balance |Available balance = credits balance \- frozen amount + credit limit \- unpaid amount |
|---|---|
|Credits |Refers to the funds pre\-deposited after you buy credits. |
|Credit limit |The credit limit granted by BytePlus based on your credit status. |
|Frozen amount |Refers to the funds frozen after you buy credits. |
|Unpaid amount |Refers to the unpaid amount on the bill. There may be a delay in bill generation. |

* ## **Balance notifications** <span id="d13Zh0Yy"></span>


**Overdue payment notification: ** Once the available balance is < 0, the system will immediately send an overdue payment notification;

**Usage ratio notification: ** If you are a customer with a credit limit and your available balance is less than a certain ratio (system default ratio), the system will trigger an insufficient balance reminder.

The above two types of notifications are triggered by system rules. Please buy credits promptly upon receiving the notifications to avoid service suspension.
