1. # **Set up billing account **  <span id="Rz6DG0IV"></span>


Billing account types include **Automatic billing** and **Pay by credits**. You must complete the account setup before purchasing services, otherwise, you cannot make a purchase.

* ## **Automatic billing** <span id="JkwwED1d"></span>


  When consumption accumulates to a specific amount (varies by account), the system will initiate an automatic deduction. On the 2nd of each month, a unified deduction will be made for accounts that have not reached the threshold. **Please ensure that your payment method is available to avoid overdue payments caused by deduction failures.** 

  Users can set the account type after completing user information upon registration or login. If you choose Automatic billing, you need to bind a payment method.

* ## **Pay by credits **  <span id="9vWXyO7q"></span>


  Billing statements are reflected in your account immediately upon generation. The system will not automatically deduct fees from your bound payment method based on your consumption bills. The maximum amount you can consume is your current available balance. If exceeded, your funding account will immediately show an overdue payment. You will receive relevant overdue payment notifications, and your services run the risk of being suspended.

  Users can set the account type after completing user information upon registration or login. If you choose Pay by credits, complete an online payment of at least 5 USD to switch to a Pay by credits account. The amount used to buy credits will be pre\-deposited into your credits balance.

  If BytePlus has granted you a credit limit, you will default to a Pay by credits account and are not allowed to change the account type.

* ## **Quota account** <span id="TsN5mack"></span>


  This account type means you are an end customer under a partner. Your settlement with BytePlus will be completed using your quota balance. (quota is allocated by partner)

2. # **Changing the billing account type** <span id="fV6tKs1q"></span>


Once your billing account is set, you can switch it through the [Billing Center](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/overview). Pay by credits customers (without credit limit) can switch to an Automatic billing account, and Automatic billing accounts can switch to a Pay by credits account.

<div style="text-align: center">
<img src="https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260812082057-149692035823.png" width="2545px" /></div>



* ## **Pay by credits \-\> Automatic billing** <span id="EkdoQ9lM"></span>


  To make the switch, you need at least one payment method. If you do not have one, you must complete the payment method binding before switching. If you already have a payment method, you need to complete MFA verification or add a new payment method to complete the switch.

3. # Add Credits <span id="5QFRSz9f"></span>


When your account type is Pay by credits (the account settlement currency is USD and the service provider of BytePlus is "Byteplus Pte. Ltd."), you can buy credits online. Your credits balance will increase after buying credits.
