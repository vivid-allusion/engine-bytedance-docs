## 🔥 Viking AI Search Overview

<div style="text-align: center">
<img src="https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/29ac9a45d2744dd5b1430d8f7d81e0fd" width="6100px" /></div>


**Viking AI Search** ** is a next\-generation search engine powered by advanced large language models and ByteDance’s extensive expertise in information retrieval. Beyond traditional search and recommendation capabilities, it empowers enterprises to deploy intuitive conversational assistants. By leveraging multi\-turn interactions for search, discovery and Q&A, AI Search helps deepen user engagement while accelerating an enterprise's AI transformation.** 

&nbsp;

Core Functions & Value Propositions:


1. **Multimodal Hybrid Search:**  Based on ByteDance’s self\-developed high\-performance vector database, we unify the understanding of text, images, video, and other content forms to achieve accurate cross\-modal retrieval and Q&A. For example, users can upload a poster, and the system will find video materials in a similar style.

2. **Personalized Recommendation: ** By synthesising the vast knowledge of large language models with deep behavioural analytics, the engine captures both users' long\-term preferences and real\-time intent. It generates natural language justifications based on unique user profiles.

3. **Conversational Search Assistant:**  Deploy AI agents with a single click to integrate discovery, search, and Q&A into one interface. It supports multi\-turn interaction clarification, improving user experience, reducing search costs, and enabling faster and deeper understanding of products or content.

4. **One\-Stop Setup for Search, Recommendation, and Q&A Assistants:**   Seamlessly integrate private enterprise data and user behaviour data to build a holistic "Search + Recommendation + Q&A" in one place, empowering platform growth at lower costs.

5. **Minimal Configuration, Fast Integration:**  As a ready\-to\-use product with minimal intelligent configuration, you can launch dedicated AI search and recommendation services in as few as four steps. We handle complexity, so you can focus on business innovation and rapid scaling.
