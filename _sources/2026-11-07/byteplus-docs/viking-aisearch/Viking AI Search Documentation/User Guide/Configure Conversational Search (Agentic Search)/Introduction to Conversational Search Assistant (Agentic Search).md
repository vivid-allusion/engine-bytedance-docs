# Function Overview

The **Intelligent Dialogue Search Assistant** represents a new paradigm in content distribution for the AI era. By leveraging natural language interaction to decode user intent, combined with an Agent’s proactive planning and tool\-calling capabilities, the assistant dynamically optimizes search scope and ranking logic to achieve **progressive, precision recommendations.** 

Through proactive clarification (follow\-up questions), refinement of search results, and multi\-turn guidance, it delivers a human\-like assistant experience, upgrading your platform’s search capabilities for the AI age.

You can quickly build assistants for the following scenarios using the Viking AI Search engine Experience Center:

<span id="e3732e83"></span>
## E\-commerce Shopping Assistant

The E\-commerce Shopping Assistant automatically plans the retrieval of product information and documentation based on a user’s **natural language queries,**  offering product recommendations, shopping guides, product comparisons, and product knowledge Q&A services, helping users accurately discover what they need and supporting decision\-making from **initial interest to final conversion**.

<span id="4f76e654"></span>
## Video Viewing Companion Assistant

This assistant provides long\-form video platforms with **conversational recommendation** capabilities. By leveraging Large Language Models (LLMs) for deep **video understanding and content analysis**, it offers personalized suggestions, plot interpretations, and interactive Q&A. This enhances the overall viewing experience and strengthens platform stickiness.

# Functional Advantages

**Built on Native Multimodal Search & Recommendation Engines**

The Intelligent Conversational Search Assistant is built upon a foundation of native AI search and recommendation capabilities. By utilizing **Agent\-based dynamic tool calling**, it intelligently integrates and presents multimodal search results and recommendation outputs. This ensures a **deep fusion** of search, recommendation, and conversational abilities within a single interface.

**Support for Structured & Unstructured Data Sources**

Beyond standard structured data tables (like product catalogs), the assistant supports the ingestion of **private documents** (such as PDF, Word, and Excel formats). These documents serve as a supplementary knowledge base for the Q&A process, enabling a complete experience loop—from **pre\-sales shopping guidance to post\-sales technical support.** 

**Out\-of\-the\-Box Experience**

There is **no need for complex ** **Agent** ** coding** or building workflows from scratch. Once your basic search configurations are complete, you can immediately test and integrate the Conversational Search Assistant's capabilities into your platform.
