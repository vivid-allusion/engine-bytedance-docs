# AI Recommendation

Stop choosing between speed and sophistication. Viking AI Search's Recommendation is the enterprise recommendation engine that lets your business teams move independently and your developers integrate without friction — all from a single shared platform.

Business users build and validate recommendation scenarios through a visual interface. Developers inspect logic, tune parameters, and complete API integration in the same place. No handoffs, no bottlenecks, no silos.

**Built wherever personalization matters:** 


* **E\-commerce** — "You May Also Like" and "Frequently Bought Together" placements that drive basket size

* **Content & News** — "Related Articles" and "Recommended for You" feeds that keep readers engaged

* **Streaming & Short\-form Video** — Watch\-history\-driven "You Might Enjoy" carousels that surface the right content at the right moment

# **What you can do with Viking AI Recommendation**


1. **Build and iterate without engineering handoffs.**  Create, modify, and retire recommendation scenarios directly from the Experience Center. Link live business data to validate performance in real context. No code changes, no deployment cycles.

2. **Test against real conditions before going live.**  Configure test users and item parameters per scenario to simulate actual business conditions precisely. Validate your recommendation logic against an e\-commerce homepage, a content feed, or any other surface before it reaches production.

3. **See exactly why each recommendation was made. ** Visualize recommended items alongside the strategy that produced them. Evaluate relevance, diversity, and business alignment at a glance. No raw log spelunking required.

4. **Express complex business rules as simple controls.**  Set quotas, deduplication rules, filter conditions, and fallback strategies from one configuration surface. Inventory filters, category caps, item freshness windows, all mapped directly to UI controls.

5. **Add explainability your users will actually trust.**  Configure prompt templates to generate recommendation reasons in your brand voice. Every recommendation surfaces a human\-readable explanation — building end\-user trust and keeping your system auditable.

6. **Go from configuration to production in minutes.**  One\-click deployment setup. Two standardized API endpoints handle end\-to\-end data ingestion and recommendation serving. Ready to integrate fast, built to scale with you.
