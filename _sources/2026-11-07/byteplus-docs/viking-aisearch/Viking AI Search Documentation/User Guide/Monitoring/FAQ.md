## **Q: Why do we need to activate an additional service? ** 

Application monitoring, including event tracking and statistics, utilizes the capabilities of **Volcengine \- Cloud Monitor**. Because this involves cross\-product operations, a one\-time activation is required to meet compliance requirements.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true">\*Note</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">Activating this service <strong>does not involve any additional costs</strong>. You can proceed with peace of mind!</div>

## **Q: Why is there no data showing after activation? ** 

There could be several reasons for this:


1. **Activation Latency:**  There is typically a delay of a few minutes between activating the  **"Cloud Monitor Service"**  and the appearance of monitoring data.

2. **Sub\-account Permissions:**  Please check if your account has been granted with the required permissions. For more details, refer to: [https://www.volcengine.com/docs/6257/1143911?lang=zh](https://www.volcengine.com/docs/6257/1143911?lang=zh)
