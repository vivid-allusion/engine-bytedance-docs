This topic describes the costs for changing instance specifications and unsubscribing from a Kafka instance.

<span id="变配费用说明"></span>
## Costs for changing instance specifications


* Pay\-as\-you\-go:

   Once the specifications of a pay\-as\-you\-go instance are changed, the instance is immediately billed based on the new specifications. The billing cycle includes the settlement cycle in which the instance specification change occurs.



|**Change Type** |**Cost** |
|---|---|
|Immediate upgrade |Total amount payable in your order after an upgrade<br><br>= Cost for new specifications \- Remaining value of previous specifications<br><br>= Official price of new specifications per hour \* Remaining subscription duration (hours) \* discount rate \- Official price of previous specifications per hour \* Remaining subscription duration (hours) \* discount rate |


<span id="退订费用说明"></span>
## Costs for unsubscription


* Unsubscribing from a pay\-as\-you\-go instance is realized by deleting the instance. No refund is involved in this process.
