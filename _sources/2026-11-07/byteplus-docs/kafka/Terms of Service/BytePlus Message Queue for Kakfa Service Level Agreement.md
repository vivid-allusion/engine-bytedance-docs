In order to apply for the Service Credits, you must notify BytePlus within 30 days after the end of preceding calendar month where you become eligible to receive the Service Credits, and provide BytePlus with sufficient supporting information and materials evidencing the occurrence of the failure of Service Levels. ## **DEFINITIONS**

The following definitions shall apply to this SLA:


* “Instance”: An instance refers to a virtual and independently operating message queue runtime environment.

* “Service Cycle”: A service cycle lasts for one natural month. If a customer uses a message queue instance for less than one month, the cumulative usage time of the instance in that month will be treated as one service cycle.

* “Total Minutes in Service Cycle”: It is calculated by multiplying the total number of days in the service cycle by 24 (hours) and 60 (minutes).

* "Monthly Service Fee": The total amount of service fees that a customer should pay for a single message queue instance in a natural month. It does not include the portion that has been purchased but not consumed. If a customer pays service fees for multiple months at one time, the monthly service fees will be calculated by apportioning according to the number of months purchased. (The monthly service fee here should exclude the amount deducted or exempted by using vouchers, coupons, discount offers, and service fee reductions, etc.)
