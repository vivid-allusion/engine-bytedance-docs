## Migrating self\-built data centers to the BytePlus platform

Self\-built data centers face issues such as aging infrastructure, complex O&M, slow upgrades and scale\-out, and high maintenance costs. You can use Server Migration Center (SMC) to migrate servers in the data centers to the platform. This not only eliminates the aforementioned pain points but also leverages high availability, high security, and high scalability of the platform to dynamically adjust ECS instance configurations and meet diverse business requirements.

<span id=".5pys5Zyw6Jma5ouf5py66L-B56e76Iez54Gr5bGx5byV5pOO"></span>
## Migrating on\-premises virtual machines to the BytePlus platform

On\-premises virtual machines are constrained by host resources, resulting in significantly lower availability and scalability compared to ECS instances. You can use SMC to migrate on\-premises virtual machines to the platform and leverage the abundant resources of ECS instances to remove constraints on your business operations.

<span id=".5YW25LuW5LqR5Y6C5ZWG6L-B56e76Iez54Gr5bGx5byV5pOO"></span>
## Migrating from other cloud platforms to the BytePlus platform

Different cloud platforms, such as Alibaba Cloud, Huawei Cloud, and Tencent Cloud, vary in terms of features, performance, security guarantees, and usage costs. If the cloud services you are currently using fail to meet your business needs, you can migrate your workloads to the platform via SMC.

<span id=".6Leo6LSm5Y-35oiW6Leo5Zyw5Z-f6L-B56e75LqR5pyN5Yqh5Zmo"></span>
## Migrating ECS instances across accounts or regions

If your ECS instances on the platform cannot access required products due to region or account permission restrictions, or fail to meet business needs through upgrades or scale\-out, you can migrate the ECS instances across accounts or regions via SMC.

<span id=".6L-B56e75LqR5pyN5Yqh5Zmo5pON5L2c57O757uf"></span>
## Migrating ECS instance operating systems

When you change the operating system of an ECS instance, you not only need to stop the instance, but also lose system volume data after the operating system is changed, which affects normal business operations. With the operating system migration feature of SMC, you can change the operating systems of platform instances without downtime, and system volume data will not be lost after the operating systems are changed.
