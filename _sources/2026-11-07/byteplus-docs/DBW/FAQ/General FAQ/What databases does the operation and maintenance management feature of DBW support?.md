product: "Database Workshop"
product_code: "DBW"

The operation and maintenance management currently supports databases including RDS for MySQL, NDB for MySQL, RDS for PostgreSQL, RDS for SQL Server, Cache for Redis, and Document Database for MongoDB. Among them, operation and maintenance observation includes features such as session management, slow log analysis, database auditing, space analysis, transactions and locks, and one\-click diagnostics.
