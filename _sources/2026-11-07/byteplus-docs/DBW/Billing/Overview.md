This topicr introduces the billing item, billing method and prices of DBW.

<span id="billing-type"></span>
## Billing method


|**Billing method** |Description |
|---|---|
|**Pay\-as\-you\-go** |Also known as postpaid, it is billed based on the actual usage of DBW and debited from the account by the hour. Suitable for short\-term needs with small business volume, it can be released immediately after use, making it flexible and convenient. For more details, see [Pay-as-you-go](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/DBW/pay-as-you-go). |


<span id="billing-item"></span>
## Billing item

The billing items for the database workshop are as follows:


* Database auditing: log storage fees, index storage fees, index traffic fees, and log collection fees.

* Full SQL Insights: Log storage fees, index storage fees, index traffic fees, log acquisition fees, and full aggregate analytics fees.
