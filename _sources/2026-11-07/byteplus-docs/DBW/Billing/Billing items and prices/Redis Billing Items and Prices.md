This article describes the billing items and price details for Cache for Redis in Database Workshop (DBW).

<span id=".6K6h6LS56aG55qaC6KeI"></span>
## Billing items

The following billing items are involved in using the Database Workshop (DBW).


|**Function type** |**Billing method** |**Billing items** |**Description** |
|---|---|---|---|
|Database audit |Pay\-as\-you\-go |Log storage fee |* The fee incurred by the log storage space occupied by the corresponding data volume after Database Workshop (DBW) collects the audit log.<br><br>* For details about the billing rules and prices of log storage fees, refer to [Database Audit Billing Rules](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/DBW/redis-billing-items-and-price#.5pWw5o2u5bqT5a6h6K6h6K6h6LS56KeE5YiZ).<br><br>* Billed based on actual usage, with a billing cycle of 1 hour, and the fee will be automatically deducted from your account balance. |
|||Log collection fee |* This is the write traffic fee incurred by the data volume collected when collecting audit logs.<br><br>* For detailed information on the billing rules and price of log collection fees, refer to [Database Audit Billing Rules](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/DBW/redis-billing-items-and-price#.5pWw5o2u5bqT5a6h6K6h6K6h6LS56KeE5YiZ).<br><br>* Billed based on actual usage, with a billing cycle of 1 hour, and the fee will be automatically deducted from your account balance. |
|||Index storage fee |* The fee incurred by the index data occupying storage space after the audit log is indexed.<br><br>* For details about the billing rules and prices of index storage fees, see [Database Audit Billing Rules](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/DBW/redis-billing-items-and-price#.5pWw5o2u5bqT5a6h6K6h6K6h6LS56KeE5YiZ).<br><br>* Billed based on actual usage, with a billing cycle of 1 hour, and the fee will be automatically deducted from your account balance. |
|||Index traffic fee |* Traffic costs generated by index data volume when audit log is indexed or re\-indexed.<br><br>* For detailed information on the billing rules and price of index traffic fees, refer to [Database Audit Billing Rules](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/DBW/redis-billing-items-and-price#.5pWw5o2u5bqT5a6h6K6h6K6h6LS56KeE5YiZ).<br><br>* Billed based on actual usage, with a billing cycle of 1 hour, and the fee will be automatically deducted from your account balance. |

### Cost calculation formula

Taking the South China 1 (Guangzhou) region as an example, the total cost of enabling Database Audit is as follows:

Database audit fee to be paid = 0.0042 × Log storage × Usage duration + 0.18 × Log collection volume + 0.0042 × Index storage × Usage duration + 0.35 × Index traffic
