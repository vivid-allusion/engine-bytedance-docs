This article introduces the billing items and prices of the veDB MySQL database in Database Workshop (DBW).

<span id=".6K6h6LS56aG55qaC6L-w"></span>
## Billing overview

The following billing items are involved in using the Database Workshop (DBW).

<span id=".djIt54mI5pWw5o2u5bqT5a6h6K6hLeWFqOmHjy1zcWwt5rSe5a-f6K6h6LS56aG5"></span>
### V2 Database Audit & Full SQL Insight Billing Items

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">V2 Database Audit corresponds to the SQL details feature of Full SQL Insight. If only SQL details are used during activation, only log storage, log collection, index storage, and index traffic fees will apply. If additional features like SQL write analysis are used, DBW will charge for full aggregation analysis fees on top of the aforementioned items. Actual charges may vary.</div>



|**Billing method** |**Billing items** |**Description** |
|---|---|---|
|Pay\-as\-you\-go<br><br> |Log storage fee |* The fee incurred by the log storage space occupied by the corresponding data volume after Database Workshop (DBW) collects the audit log.<br><br>* Billed based on actual usage, with a billing cycle of 1 hour, and the fee will be automatically deducted from your account balance. |
||Infrequent\-access log storage usage fee |* Audit logs exceeding the standard retention period will be automatically archived by DBW to infrequent\-access storage, and the storage space occupied by the archived data will incur corresponding infrequent\-access storage charges.<br><br>* For details about the billing rules and prices of log storage fees, refer to [Database Audit Billing Rules](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/DBW/vedb-billing-items-and-price?TagIDs=2&lang=en#.djIt54mI5pWw5o2u5bqT5a6h6K6hLeWFqOmHjy1zcWwt5rSe5a-f6K6h6LS56KeE5YiZ).<br><br>* Billed based on actual usage, with a billing cycle of 1 hour, and the fee will be automatically deducted from your account balance. |
||Log collection fee<br><br> |* This is the write traffic fee incurred by the data volume collected when collecting audit logs.<br><br>* For detailed information on the billing rules and price of log collection fees, refer to [Database Audit Billing Rules](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/DBW/vedb-billing-items-and-price?TagIDs=2&lang=en#.djIt54mI5pWw5o2u5bqT5a6h6K6hLeWFqOmHjy1zcWwt5rSe5a-f6K6h6LS56KeE5YiZ).<br><br>* Billed based on actual usage, with a billing cycle of 1 hour, and the fee will be automatically deducted from your account balance. |
||Index storage fee |* The fee incurred by the index data occupying storage space after the audit log is indexed.<br><br>* For details about the billing rules and prices of index storage fees, see [Database Audit Billing Rules](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/DBW/vedb-billing-items-and-price?TagIDs=2&lang=en#.djIt54mI5pWw5o2u5bqT5a6h6K6hLeWFqOmHjy1zcWwt5rSe5a-f6K6h6LS56KeE5YiZ).<br><br>* Billed based on actual usage, with a billing cycle of 1 hour, and the fee will be automatically deducted from your account balance. |
||Infrequent\-access index storage usage fee<br><br> |* After audit logs are indexed, those exceeding the standard retention period will be automatically archived by DBW to infrequent\-access storage, and the storage fees for the resulting index data will be billed at the infrequent\-access storage rate.<br><br>* For details about the billing rules and prices of index storage fees, see [Database Audit Billing Rules](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/DBW/vedb-billing-items-and-price?TagIDs=2&lang=en#.djIt54mI5pWw5o2u5bqT5a6h6K6hLeWFqOmHjy1zcWwt5rSe5a-f6K6h6LS56KeE5YiZ).<br><br>* Billed based on actual usage, with a billing cycle of 1 hour, and the fee will be automatically deducted from your account balance. |
||Index traffic fee |* Traffic costs generated by index data volume when audit log is indexed or re\-indexed.<br><br>* For detailed information on the billing rules and price of index traffic fees, refer to [Database Audit Billing Rules](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/DBW/vedb-billing-items-and-price?TagIDs=2&lang=en#.djIt54mI5pWw5o2u5bqT5a6h6K6hLeWFqOmHjy1zcWwt5rSe5a-f6K6h6LS56KeE5YiZ).<br><br>* Billed based on actual usage, with a billing cycle of 1 hour, and the fee will be automatically deducted from your account balance. |
||Total aggregation analysis fee |* This fee is charged based on the log volume when using automatic aggregation analysis with SQL insight.<br><br>* For detailed information on the billing rules and pricing of total aggregation analysis, refer to [Database Audit Billing Rules](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/DBW/vedb-billing-items-and-price?TagIDs=2&lang=en#.djIt54mI5pWw5o2u5bqT5a6h6K6hLeWFqOmHjy1zcWwt5rSe5a-f6K6h6LS56KeE5YiZ).<br><br>* Billed based on actual usage, with a billing cycle of 1 hour. |

### V1 Database Audit Billing Items

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Since V1 Database Audit is billed through TLS, enabling both V1 and V2 Database Audit may result in dual charges. It is recommended to disable V1 Database Audit.</div>



|**Function type** |**Billing method** |**Description** |

After the promotion period, fees will be charged based on the amount of logs collected.<br><br>* Storage fee: Each instance has 5 GiB of free storage space during the promotion period, and corresponding fees will be charged for exceeding this amount. |

#### Cost calculation formula

Taking the region China South 1 (Guangzhou) as an example, the total costs when full SQL insight is not enabled and when it is enabled are as follows:


* Costs when full SQL insight is not enabled:

   Total cost = 0.0042 (USD/GiB/hour) × Log storage × Usage time + 0.18 (USD/GiB) × Log collection volume + 0.0042 (USD/GiB/hour) × Index storage × Usage time + 0.35 (USD/GiB) × Index traffic

* Fees when full SQL insight is enabled:

   Total cost = 0.0042 (USD/GiB/hour) × Log storage × Usage time + 0.18 (USD/GiB) × Log collection volume + 0.0042 (USD/GiB/hour) × Index storage × Usage time + 0.35 (USD/GiB) × Index traffic + 0.08 (USD/GiB) × Total aggregation analysis volume
