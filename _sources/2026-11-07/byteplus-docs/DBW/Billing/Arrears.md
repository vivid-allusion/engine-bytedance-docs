When your account is in arrears, Database Workshop (DBW) services will be suspended. Make payments promptly to maintain business continuity.

<span id="arrears-description"></span>
## Arrears

Your account will be deemed in arrears if the available balance (including account balance and vouchers) is insufficient to cover the pending bill. In this case, you will receive SMS or email notifications reminding you to recharge promptly and settle the overdue payment. Failure to do so will result in the suspension of paid features in DBW.

The DBW currently supports pay\-as\-you\-go only, as detailed in the following table:

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Bills are generated hourly for pay\-as\-you\-go services. Upon generation, payments are deducted in real\-time. You are required to settle all issued bills based on actual usage.</div>



|**Time in arrears** |**Impact** |**Follow\-up operation** |
|---|---|---|
|Within 360 hours (that is, 15 days) |The product function can be used normally within 360 hours of arrears. |Enter the [Billing center](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/overview) to top up your current account. |
|Over 360 hours (that is, 15 days) |The product function cannot be used normally. |Enter the  [Billing center](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/overview) to recharge the current account. After clearing the arrears bill and the available amount is greater than 0 USD, the product function will automatically resume use. |
|720 hours (that is, 30 days) after shutdown |The product function is forcibly turned off and cannot be used normally. |Enter the  [Billing center](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/overview) to recharge the current account, and restart the function after clearing the arrears bill and the available amount is greater than 0 USD. |
