Pay\-as\-you\-go is a postpaid billing method where payment occurs after resource usage. You are not required to purchase resources in advance, and can enable or release resources at any time according to demand.

<span id="applicable-scenario"></span>
## Applicable scenario

Pay\-as\-you\-go is designed for short\-term business needs. You can disable resources at any time when they are no longer required, and charges will cease right afterwards.

<span id="scope-of-application"></span>
## Scope of application


|**Database products** |**Function type** |
|---|---|
|Cache for Redis |Database audit |
|RDS for MySQL |* V1 database audit<br><br>* V2 Database audit & Full SQL insights |
|NDB for MySQL |* V1 Database audit<br><br>* V2 Database audit & Full SQL insights |
|RDS for PostgreSQL |* V1 Database audit<br><br>* V2 Database audit & Full SQL insights |


<span id="purchase-requirements"></span>
## Purchase requirements


* A BytePlus account has been registered and completed verification. For the detailed method, refer to [Sign Up and Verify BytePlus account](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-signing-up-your-account).

* As per the regulations in the Chinese mainland, you must have completed real name registration before you can activate and use cloud services on regions in the Chinese mainland through BytePlus. For more details, refer to [Real name registration](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-real-name-registration).

* When enabling the DBW function, the total value of your account balance (i.e. cash balance) and vouchers must be more than USD 0.


<span id="billing-hours"></span>
## Billing duration

For pay\-as\-you\-go (excluding service suspensions due to payment arrears), the billing period spans the entire service lifecycle: charges begin upon enabling and cease immediately upon disabling.

<span id="settlement-cycle"></span>
## Settlement cycle

Pay\-as\-you\-go is billed based on actual usage with an hourly settlement cycle. The system processes payments precisely at the top of each hour (UTC+8). Should your available account balance (including both cash credit and vouchers) become insufficient to cover the pending charges, you will receive low\-balance notifications via SMS or email.
