---
title: "What to do if the connection fails when logging into the data interaction platform?"
product: "Database Workshop"
product_code: "DBW"
source: "https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/DBW/connectioni_fails_when_logging_into_SQL_console"
---

Possible cause 1: Incorrect username or password.

Solution: Verify that the username and password you entered are correct.

Possible cause 2: The Host bound to the account for the login instance does not match the IP segment of DBW.

Solution: Check whether the IP bound to your login account is within the DBW segment. For more details, refer to [DBW's allowlisted IPs](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/DBW/allowlist-IP-bound-to-DBW)
