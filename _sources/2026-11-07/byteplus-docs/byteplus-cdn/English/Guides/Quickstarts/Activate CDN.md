To start accelerating your content delivery, you first need to activate the CDN service. This guide provides step\-by\-step instructions on how to enable BytePlus CDN from the management console, including accepting the service agreement and understanding the postpaid billing model.


1. Open the [BytePlus CDN management console](https://console.byteplus.com/cdn)., and you will be guided to the CDN activation page.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/89b56968a0fd4f049f1647e0c7f3bdfc~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

2. Read the content on the page carefully to understand how CDN billing works. For billing details, see [Pricing](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-cdn/docs-pricing).

3. Select the service agreement, then click **Activate**.

4. In the **Confirm Order Information** step, click **Next**, then follow the on\-screen prompts to activate the CDN service.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">Since CDN is postpaid, the price displayed on the page is $0.00. This means that activating CDN is free of charge.   </div>
   

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/68fac744bf624155a6ca8d963ae8b306~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>


The items in the **Payment Details** list are explained as follows:

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Data transferred from the CDN to clients is aggregated and billed based on the region where the serving node is located. Data transfer prices vary by region, see <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-cdn/docs-pricing">Pricing</a> for details.</div>



* **Traffic to Internet By Region**: Represents the cost of data transfer from the CDN node to the client, calculated based on the node's region. These regions are Europe, North America, Middle East/Africa, South America, Asia Pacific Zone 1, Asia Pacific Zone 2, Asia Pacific Zone 3.

* **Traffic to Internet_Chinese Mainland**: Represents the cost of data transfer from CDN nodes in Mainland China to the client.

* **Traffic to Origin_Global**: Represents the cost of data transfer from CDN nodes to the origin server.

* **All Request_Chinese Mainland**: Represents the number of requests received by CDN nodes in Mainland China.

* **All Request**: Represents the number of requests received by CDN nodes outside Mainland China.
