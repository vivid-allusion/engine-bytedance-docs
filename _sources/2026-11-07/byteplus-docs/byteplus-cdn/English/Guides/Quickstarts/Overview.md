To solve slow page loads and improve the user experience for your website, you can integrate it with BytePlus CDN. This tutorial provides a step\-by\-step guide based on a common scenario, showing you how to add your site to the CDN and accelerate content delivery to your users. After integrating your site with CDN, user requests to your site are routed through CDN, which is responsible for retrieving content from your site and responding to user requests.

<span id="scenario"></span>
## Scenario

You have a site www.example.com, with an IP address of 203.0.113.8. The site only accepts requests that use the HTTPS protocol.

As your business grows, you receive frequent user feedback about slow page loads and occasional page load failures. To improve the user experience, you decide to use BytePlus CDN to accelerate your site's content delivery.

You must have a BytePlus account to use BytePlus CDN. This tutorial assumes you have already completed the registration.
