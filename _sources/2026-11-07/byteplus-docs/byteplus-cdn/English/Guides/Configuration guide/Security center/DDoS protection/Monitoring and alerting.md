Advanced DDoS Protection provides monitoring and alerting functions to help you understand historical attack trends and attack event details in a timely manner. You can also obtain information such as the total volume of data transferred and the number of packets sent per second within a specified period.

You can view the monitoring data on the **Dashboard** page in the Advanced DDoS Protection management console. For detailed information about the data displayed on the **Dashboard** page, see [View dashboard details](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-ddos-protection/docs-viewing-ddos-attack-protection-information).

When attack traffic reaches a certain threshold (default: 100 Mbps), the system will send you an alert notification. You can also customize the threshold on the **Dashboard** page.

You can access the **Dashboard** page and view the monitoring data of your Advanced DDoS Protection instance using either of the following ways:


* Method 1: Through the BytePlus CDN management console

   * Log in to the [BytePlus CDN management console](https://console.byteplus.com/cdn).

   * In the left navigation pane, choose **Security center** \> **DDoS protection**.

   * In the **Protected domains** section on the **DDoS protection** page, click **Protection management** to go to the **Dashboard** page in the Advanced DDoS Protection management console.

   * On the **DDoS attack protection** tab, select the Advanced DDoS Protection instance you want to view.

* Method 2: Through the Advanced DDoS Protection Management Console

   * Log in to the [Advanced DDoS Protection management console](https://console.byteplus.com/auth/login?redirectURI=%2Fddos-protection%2Fadvance%2Finstances).

   * In the left navigation pane, click **Dashboard**.

   * On the **DDoS attack protection** tab, select the Advanced DDoS Protection instance you want to view.
