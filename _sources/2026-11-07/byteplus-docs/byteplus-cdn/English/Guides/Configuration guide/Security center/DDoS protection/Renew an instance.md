This document describes how to renew an Advanced DDoS Protection instance in the BytePlus CDN management console.

<span id="steps"></span>
## Steps


1. Log in to the [BytePlus CDN management console](https://console.byteplus.com/cdn).

2. In the left navigation pane, choose **Security center** \> **DDoS protection**.

3. In the Advanced DDoS Protection instance list on the **DDoS protection** page, click **Renew** in the **Actions** column.

4. On the **Renew Advanced DDoS Protection instance** page, set the renewal duration.

5. After confirming that the configuration is correct, check **I have read and agree to Service Specific Terms,Service Level Agreement,Customer Agreement**, and then click **Renew**.

6. Complete the payment as prompted on the page.
