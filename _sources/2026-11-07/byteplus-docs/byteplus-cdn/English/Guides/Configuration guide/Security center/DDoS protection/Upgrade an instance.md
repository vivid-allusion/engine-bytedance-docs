This document describes how to upgrade an Advanced DDoS Protection instance in the BytePlus CDN management console. The upgrade operation increases the number of protected domains.

<span id="precautions"></span>
## Precautions

By default, an Advanced DDoS Protection instance can protect up to 50 domains. If more domains need to be protected, additional fees will be charged. For billing information, see [Pricing details](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-ddos-protection/docs-pricing).

<span id="steps"></span>
## Steps


1. Log in to the [BytePlus CDN management console](https://console.byteplus.com/cdn).

2. In the left navigation pane, choose **Security center** \> **DDoS protection**.

3. In the Advanced DDoS Protection instance list on the **DDoS protection** page, click **Upgrade** in the **Actions** column.

4. On the **Upgrade Advanced DDoS Protection instance** page, set the quota of protected domains. The quota should be larger than that before the upgrade and must be a multiple of 5.

5. After confirming that the configuration is correct, check **I have read and agree to Service Specific Terms,Service Level Agreement,Customer Agreement**, and then click **Upgrade**.

6. Complete the payment as prompted on the page.
