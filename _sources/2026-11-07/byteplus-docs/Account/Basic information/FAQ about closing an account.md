1. ## How do I delete my Byteplus account? <span data-label="purple">Account deletion</span> <span id="z99LAz1R"></span>

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">If you no longer need to use Byteplus, you can delete your account in Account Management \- Security Settings. For the account deletion process, see <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/Account/close-account">Close account</a>.<mark>Account deletion is irreversible. Be sure to back up important data before deleting your account</mark>. If you need to continue using Byteplus services after deletion, you can re\-register for a new account.   </div>
   



---




2. ## Will there still be fees during the account closure cooling\-off period? Can I still log in to my account? <span data-label="purple">Account Closure Cooling\-off Period</span> <span id="uRCtDUib"></span>

   1. The **account closure cooling\-off period is 45 days**, and **shortening the period is not supported**. After entering the account deactivation quiet period, logging in and purchasing new resources are not allowed. Outstanding fees corresponding to previous services may occur due to delayed billing. Details are as follows:

      1. During the quiet period, the account is in a dormant state, and logging in and purchasing new resources are not allowed. All resource instances in the account have been unsubscribed and closed, so no new consumption will be generated.

      2. If there are instances that have not been settled, outstanding fees may be generated during the quiet period due to delayed billing. This situation will cause the deactivation process to fail, and you need to log in to your account, pay the outstanding amount, and re\-initiate the deactivation.

      3. No further charges will be incurred after the account has been permanently deactivated.



---




3. ## Can I revoke/terminate the deactivation request if the original account has been deactivated or is in the deactivation quiet period? Can I use the same mobile number to register a new account? <span data-label="purple">Account Closure Cooling\-off Period</span> <span id="JtJTFh88"></span>

   1. Account cancellation is irreversible, and we do not support restoring canceled accounts. If you want to continue using Byteplus services, you can register a new account.

   2. If the account is currently in the cancellation grace period, you cannot terminate the cancellation process. You can directly register a new account without waiting for the grace period to end. 

      * One mobile phone number can be used to register or bind to six Byteplus accounts. One email can be used to register or bind to only one Byteplus account.



---




4. ## What should I do if the account cancellation fails? <span data-label="purple">Cancellation failed</span> <span id="8OKUPrsr"></span>

   If your account incurs overdue fees due to delayed billing during the account grace period, the cancellation process will fail. At that time, you need to log in to the account again, pay off the overdue fees, and then cancel the account again.



---




5. ## Will all the information and data in the account be cleared after the account is canceled? <span id="TCN0tq0a"></span>

   After the account is canceled, you will not be able to use the account to access Byteplus, and the data in the account will be completely deleted. To protect your account, property, resources, and information, please proceed with caution and back up all data under your account before submitting your application.
