## 1. Overview

BytePlus offers two distinct account types to cater to different user needs: **Personal** and **Business** . This document outlines the key differences between them in terms of account profile requirements, product access, and applicable tax rates.

<span id="Mn70ljxF"></span>
## 2. Account Profile Requirements

The information required to complete your account profile varies significantly between the two types, with Personal accounts designed for a streamlined onboarding experience.

**Table 1: Account Profile Information Requirements**


|**Information Item** |**Personal Account (Simplified Profile)**  |**Business Account (Full Profile)**  |**Purpose** |
|---|---|---|---|
|**Country/Region** |Required |Required |Determines service availability and tax rates. |
|**Address Line 1** |NO Need |Required |\- |
|**Address Line 2** |NO Need |Optional |\- |
|**City** |Required for some countries |Required |\- |
|**State/Province** |Required for some countries |Required |\- |
|**Postal Code** |Required for some countries |Required |\- |
|**Phone Number & OTP** |Required for some countries |Required |\- |
|**Full Name** |Automaticall filled  "Personal User" by system |Required |To be displayed on invoices for Personal. |
|**Organization Name** |NO Need |Required (Organization Name) |To be displayed on invoices for Business. |
|**Real\-Name Authentication** |Not supported |Supported |Only needed to purchase resource in China Mainland |


**Key Notes :** 


* The account type, country selected cannot be changed after the account profile submitted.

## 3. Product Access

Personal accounts have access to a limited subset of BytePlus products, while Business accounts can order the full catalog.

**Table 2: Product Access Comparison**


|**Account Type** |**Product Access** |**Notes** |
|---|---|---|
|**Personal** |Limited product catelogue is avaliable |As of the latest update, the available products include: <br><br>ModelArk,<br><br>Seedream<br><br>Seedance<br><br>AI\-Saving Plan<br><br>OneArt <br><br>ArkClaw<br><br>and other products as they become available. |
|**Business** |Full catalog of BytePlus products. |This includes all cloud computing, media processing, AI, and enterprise solutions. |

## 4. Tax Rate Differences

The applicable tax rate (GST/VAT) depends on both the account type and the user's registered country/region.

Please refer to detailed tax rate table:[Tax rate reference](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-tax-rate-reference)
