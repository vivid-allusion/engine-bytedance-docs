You can use the BytePlus Console to close your BytePlus account if necessary.

**To close a BytePlus account:** 


1. Sign in to the [BytePlus Console](https://console.byteplus.com/auth/login/), as a root user(IAM User is not allowed to close account).

2. Click **Username** \> **My account**.


<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260514124102-1632799290465.png) </span>

&nbsp;

The **My account** dashboard appears.


3. Click **Basic Information**.

4. In the **Account information** tab, click **Close this account**.<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260514124209-227972980217.png) </span>


The **Close your account** pop\-up window appears.


5. On the **Close your account** pop\-up window, click **Close account**.

6. On the Account closure screen, perform the following steps:

   1. Check **I have read and agree to the** [Customer Agreement](https://docs.byteplus.com/legal/docs/customer-agreement)  **,**  [Terms of Service](https://docs.byteplus.com/legal/docs/terms-of-service) **and** [Privacy Policy](https://docs.byteplus.com/legal/docs/privacy-policy)  **. I understand that my BytePlus account XXX will be closed, and all data associated with it will be permanently removed and cannot be recovered** .

   2. Select the reason for closing your account.

   3. Click **Submit account closure application**.

7. Enter the verification code.

   <span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260514124518-617484330474.png) </span>

   1. The system automatically checks whether your account meets the account closure requirements. If it meets the closure requirements, you can proceed to complete the closure as shown in the figure and click **Close Account**.

      * During the account closure check, if there are **required** tasks in the account as shown below, you need to manually complete them first before closing the account.

      * During the account closure check, if there are **optional** tasks in the account as shown below, clicking **Close Account** will be regarded as waiving all resources included in the tasks.

8. Click **Close account.** 


The account closure application is submitted.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default"><strong>Account Closure Cooling\-off Period</strong>: The account closure cooling\-off period lasts 45 days. During this period, the account and its sub\-users will be in a silent status, with <strong>no login or new resource purchases</strong> allowed. Once the cooling\-off period ends, the account will be permanently closed automatically with no further action required.</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default"><strong>Account Closure Failure Scenarios</strong>: If your account incurs arrears due to delayed billing or other reasons during the cooling\-off period, the closure process will fail. You will need to log in again, settle the outstanding balance, and then submit the account closure request once more.</div>
