This topic summarizes frequently asked questions about RDS for PostgreSQL storage space.

#### What parts does the storage space of the PostgreSQL instance I purchase contain?

The storage space includes a data space, a log space, a WAL log space, and a transaction file space. Be sure the control the space use of each part, as the database may become unavailable once the space is used up.

<span id=".UG9zdGdyZVNRTCDlrp7kvovlrZjlgqjnqbrpl7TnmoTkuIrpmZDlkozkuIvpmZDliIbliKvmmK_vvJ8="></span>
#### What are the upper and lower limits of a PostgreSQL instance storage space?

The storage space ranges from 20 to 3000 GB. If you need a bigger storage space, please contact your customer manager.
