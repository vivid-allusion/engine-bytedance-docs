Enterprise knowledge engine supports access through Internet public chain parsing files. This article introduces how to access knowledge through Internet public chain.

<span id="78cd5b6c"></span>
# Operation steps


1. In the console, select **Data Access \> Knowledge Access**, then click **Create**.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/14c3d4a96b2e491096bd6c1fb0db8e10~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

2. Select **Internet public chain** on the data connection page.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/f5f1458d4edb4e5e8fdcc22f5be732fe~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

3. Customize the **data connection name** and add a link in the **connection information** text box.

   Click the  **+ document link** to support batch access.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/eadaa9cf32ae44aa9d8d5e0e766eba0c~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

4. Click **Parse** , wait for the parse to complete and view the parse results.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">Supports automatic retrieval of secondary links within the page.   </div>
   

   * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">It supports parsing up to 20 links at a time.   </div>
   

   * Click on the title to jump to the source link to view the corresponding content.

   * Click **Download parsing results** to download the parsing content for local viewing.

   * Click **Delete** to delete the current parsing result.

      <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/3420e669e62c4274bb9aa78f3824f29b~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

5. In the upper right corner of the page, click **Test Connection** to ensure that the data connection is successful.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/f76df2f2361a4fe7aaf75626b5c87412~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

6. Click **Save** in the top right corner of the page.

   After saving, you can view the imported document information and lineage views in the data connection details.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/18ccff5789484060b65af0c0ce895551~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

7. In the upper right corner of the data connection details page, select **Application** \> **Create Visual Modeling** , and one\-click jumping for visual knowledge processing.

   See the specific operation of visual knowledge processing [Data Cleaning](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/dataagent/Data_Cleaning_).

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/326255ac1f984176b0df0fecdabf95cd~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>
