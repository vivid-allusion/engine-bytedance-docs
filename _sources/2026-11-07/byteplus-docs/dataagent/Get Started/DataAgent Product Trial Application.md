This article introduces how to apply for a trial of the Data Agent product.

<span id="0a6a9a0b"></span>
# Account registration and login

Data Agent is a big data product of BytePlus. You need to register a BytePlus account first, and then fill out the trial application.


1. Go to the BytePlus account registration page. Go to the [BytePlus official website](https://www.byteplus.com/en) and click the "Register Now" button in the upper right corner to start registration.


<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/a392417ad6e54d47a1bd6f50fa8f9a9c~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>


2. BytePlus account registration and login. Click "Account Registration", fill in the username, password, mobile number, verification code, and other relevant information, review and check the user notice, and click "Start Trial" to log in to your BytePlus account. If you already have a BytePlus account, you can click the "Log In Now" button below to log in. For detailed steps on account registration, see [Account Registration Process](https://www.volcengine.com/docs/6261/64925).

# Product trial application


1. Go to the official homepage of the Intelligent Agent product. After logging in to your BytePlus account, click "Products" \- "Big Data", select Data Agent under the Growth Marketing Suite, and you will enter the Data Agent product homepage.

2. Click "Consult Now" to go to the product consultation page.

3. Fill out and submit the trial application. The user needs to fill in the contact person, contact number, verification code, company name, and other relevant information. In the consultation section, the user can briefly describe their business needs (for example: Data Agent product trial). Next, review and check the user notice. After confirming that the information is correct, click "Submit". After the form is submitted, BytePlus staff will contact you by phone. If you need assistance immediately, you can also call for consultation right away.


<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/ab647d736dfe419e9acc80ca69459de1~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>


4. Please wait patiently. BytePlus staff will process your request as soon as possible.
