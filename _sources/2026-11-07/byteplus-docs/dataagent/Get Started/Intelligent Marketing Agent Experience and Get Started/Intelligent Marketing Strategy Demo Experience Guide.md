# Background introduction

Intelligent Marketing Strategy Agent is a functional module of Data Agent. The digital marketing advisor based on AI deep thinking and big data analytics realizes the automation of the whole process from marketing element analysis, intelligent circle group matching to omni\-channel strategy output through the intelligent closed loop of "target input\-strategy generation\-task configuration\-dynamic optimization", and creates an "AI general advisor" decision\-making system with second\-level response and continuous evolution capabilities for enterprises.

# Intelligent Marketing Strategy


1. After logging in to the Intelligent Marketing Agent page with your account password, click "Experience Now" in the "Intelligent Marketing Strategy Agent" card to start the functional experience generated by the marketing strategy.

2. Enter the marketing goal in the dialog box and click "Specify More Content" to configure it.

   This article takes simple configuration as an example, if you need more detailed configuration instructions, please refer to [Use the Marketing Strategy Assistant](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/dataagent/Use_the_Marketing_Strategy_Assistant).

   
   |Parameter |Configuration instructions |
   |---|---|
   |Reach activity description |* According to the actual needs, enter the description information such as the background, theme, content, audience orientation and activity goals of the event.<br><br>* Clicking "Change" will automatically generate a marketing goal description that can be used as a reference or adjusted based on an example description. |
   |Reach the opportunity |* This field can be set if you need to specify the reach time during the actual reach activity.<br><br>* After setting the "reach period", when each user actually delivers, the system will find the best reach time within the reach period range; if the "reach period" is not set, the system will filter the time range of the last 7 days by default. |
   |Reach content |* After setting the content of "access channels and channel materials", when each user generates a marketing plan and actually reaches, the system will use the specified content for generation and message reach. If "access channel and channel materials" are not set, the system will automatically generate marketing content and automatically select existing materials and benefits to generate marketing plans. |
   |Reach channel |* When selecting a reach channel, multiple channels can be selected. When each user makes a delivery, the system will determine the best reach channel and channel material based on historical marketing data performance, and can also specify channel materials. Click "Designated Channel Material".<br><br>* You can choose SMS or Webhook. |
   

3. After completing all the configurations, click "Generate Marketing Plan".

# Smart Generation Strategy

Intelligent generation of reach strategy, including reach timing, reach channel and content design.


1. Click "Generate Marketing Plan", and the system will generate a variety of split plans for clustering for precision marketing. You can choose the appropriate marketing plan according to the business scenario.

2. Strategy Generation: Click "Application Plan" to generate a detailed marketing reach strategy based on the selected marketing plan, including reach timing design, reach channels, and content design.

3. Touch task configuration: Click "Configure Policy" to automatically complete the touch task configuration based on the policy, and you can also edit and modify the configuration twice. Modify audience users, trigger conditions, reach configurations, follow metrics, and more. See the specific parameter configuration [Use the Marketing Strategy Assistant](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/dataagent/Use_the_Marketing_Strategy_Assistant).

4. Click Save as Draft, and then click Submit Task for Approval.
