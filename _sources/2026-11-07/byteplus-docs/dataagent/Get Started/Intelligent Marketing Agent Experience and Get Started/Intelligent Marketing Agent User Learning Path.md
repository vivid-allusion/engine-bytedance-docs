# Overview

Intelligent Marketing Agent is a new generation of AI marketing decision center, which integrates portrait analysis, real\-time intent recognition and dynamic strategy generation capabilities to drive the upgrade of the whole marketing link. The traditional marketing tools are upgraded to an agent matrix with the ability of "perception\-decision\-execution\-evolution" to achieve a full\-link upgrade from data to transformation, and a closed loop of data enhancement is formed between agents.

Intelligent Marketing Agent mainly includes the following functions:


* Unstructured data marking and processing: As a business empowering tool that supports multi\-modal data intelligent processing, it can help enterprises integrate multi\-channel unstructured data and convert text, voice, image, video and other unstructured data into standardized business labels. Support multi\-source data access, custom labeling systems, and labeled data output to help enterprises build reusable label assets and drive intelligent analysis and decision\-making.

* Enterprise knowledge engine: Relying on Volcano Engine's self\-developed large\-scale model and big data technology system, the enterprise knowledge engine provides structured and unstructured multi\-modal knowledge from access, processing, modeling, mining to application. It can widely support the implementation of business scenarios such as intelligent knowledge Q & A, product recommendation marketing, and knowledge summary analysis, and help enterprises achieve in\-depth mining and value transformation of global knowledge assets in the era of large models.

* Marketing Strategy Assistant: Marketing Strategy Assistant is a digital marketing decision center based on AI deep thinking and big data analytics. Through the intelligent closed loop of "target input \- strategy generation \- task configuration \- dynamic optimization", it realizes the whole process automation from marketing element analysis, intelligent circle group matching to omni\-channel strategy output, and creates an "AI general staff" decision\-making system with second\-level response and continuous evolution capabilities for enterprises.

* Marketing Interactive Assistant: A dialog marketing hub that integrates real\-time customer portrait insights and AI decision\-making engine. Through intention recognition, intelligent speech generation, accurate product recommendation and full\-link task automation, it realizes full\-scene intelligent interaction from customer demand analysis, rights recommendation to file update, and improves customer conversion efficiency and business fineness with multi\-system collaborative closed\-loop management  **.**

## Intelligent Marketing Agent Application Learning Path

<span id="b952ed4c"></span>
### Role definition

Intelligent marketing agent users are usually market operators, growth team members, or marketing digital specialists in enterprises, and are the core group of people who use intelligent marketing agents directly for marketing scenarios. They are mainly based on user behavior data, marketing reach data and conversion data, through intelligent strategy generation, accurate user segmentation, automated advertising, effect attribution, etc., to efficiently implement marketing actions, optimize customer acquisition and conversion, and focus on marketing reach efficiency, user conversion effect, delivery cost and ROI, as well as the automation of marketing processes.

<span id="fc09aed5"></span>
### Goal Vision

If you are an intelligent marketing agent, here are the goals and visions you can achieve through your current learning path:


* Proficient in the use of intelligent marketing agents, master the full\-link ability of perception\-decision\-execution\-evolution, and enable multiple agents to form a closed loop of data enhancement.

* Learn to use unstructured data marking and processing capabilities to integrate multi\-channel text, voice, image, and video data to build standardized and reusable enterprise label assets.

* Master the core applications, based on user portraits and real\-time intent recognition, to achieve personalized marketing recommendations with thousands of faces and precise timing.

* It can use the enterprise knowledge engine to complete global knowledge access, processing, mining and application, and support the efficient implementation of intelligent Q & A, product recommendation, knowledge summary and other scenarios.

* Proficient in using marketing strategy assistants, complete the entire process of intelligent closed\-loop from target input, strategy generation to task configuration and dynamic optimization, and create an enterprise\-specific AI marketing advisor.

## Intelligent Marketing Agent Builder Learning Path

<span id="eb23e138"></span>
### Role definition

Intelligent marketing agent builders are usually the marketing digitalization leaders, data product managers, AI application architects, or technical implementation team members in enterprises, and are the core group of people who build, configure, and operate intelligent marketing agents for enterprise marketing scenarios. They mainly build AI marketing hubs with "perception\-decision\-execution\-evolution" capabilities through capability configuration, model tuning, scenario implementation, and closed\-loop iteration based on enterprise business goals and user data. They focus on agent capability implementation, multi\-source data integration, strategy engine configuration, knowledge system construction, and full\-link marketing effectiveness improvement. They generally focus on technology implementation, product construction, and marketing digitalization team members.

<span id="cb6b063d"></span>
### Goal Vision

If you are an intelligent marketing agent builder, here are the essential foundational skills you can acquire through your current learning path:


* Master the construction and configuration methods of intelligent marketing agents, build an agent matrix with the ability of "perception\-decision\-execution\-evolution", and form a closed loop of data enhancement.

* Master the ability of unstructured data marking and processing, realize multi\-source multi\-modal data access, standardized label processing and label system reuse, and consolidate the enterprise's intelligent data base.

* It can integrate user tags, individual profiles, and real\-time intent recognition, enabling personalized marketing capabilities from a thousand people to a thousand people.

* Master the full\-link construction method of enterprise knowledge engine, complete multi\-modal knowledge access, processing, modeling and application mining, and support the large\-scale implementation of intelligent Q & A, product recommendation, knowledge summary and other scenarios.

* Skilled in configuring marketing strategy assistants, building a closed loop of intelligent decision\-making of "target input\-strategy generation\-task configuration\-dynamic optimization", and creating an AI marketing general staff with second\-level response and continuous evolution in enterprises.

* With the ability to design and deploy marketing interactive assistants, it can realize intention recognition, intelligent speech skills, accurate recommendation, and full\-link task automation, and improve the global interaction transformation and management precision.


Finally, the full\-link empowerment from data integration, capability construction to scenario landing is completed, which helps enterprises realize the upgrade of marketing full\-link digital intelligence and sustainable AI\-driven growth.

## Intelligent Marketing Agent Manager Learning Path

<span id="eda79652"></span>
### Role definition

The intelligent marketing agent manager is usually the marketing leader, operation director, data supervisor or member of the digital management team in the enterprise, and is the core group that coordinates the planning, resource coordination, effect control and value evaluation of the intelligent marketing agent system. They are mainly based on the overall business goals and marketing strategy of the enterprise, by coordinating the division of labor of the team, monitoring the application landing, evaluating the marketing ROI, optimizing the allocation of resources and formulating operation specifications, to ensure the efficiency, compliance and stable value of intelligent marketing agents, daily focus on the global marketing effect, data security compliance, policy iteration efficiency, input\-output ratio and the overall intelligent transformation progress, generally marketing management, data management and enterprise digital management members.

<span id="d6ef70cc"></span>
### Goal Vision

As an Intelligent Marketing Agent Manager, here are the goals and visions you can achieve through your current learning path:


* Proficient in coordinating the overall planning and implementation of intelligent marketing agents, controlling their core capabilities, managing the "perception\-decision\-execution\-evolution" agent matrix, and doing a good job in controlling the authority of the whole system, clarifying the authority boundaries of each role, and promoting the upgrade of the whole marketing link.

* Coordinate all kinds of resources, allocate the responsibilities of builders and users, do a good job in hierarchical management and control of authority, promote the collaborative implementation of core functions, and maximize the value of modules.

* Master the effect control and value evaluation methods, establish a scientific evaluation system and authority control mechanism, monitor the effectiveness of each function and the input\-output ratio, and ensure efficient marketing compliance.

* Build a long\-term operation iterative system, coordinate data security and knowledge asset precipitation, strengthen the whole life cycle management of authority, promote the continuous evolution of agent capabilities, and mine the value of knowledge assets.

* Promote the deep integration of intelligent marketing agents with the existing system of enterprises, integrate authority control into the whole process, break down collaboration barriers, and realize the intelligence of marketing full links to help enterprises grow sustainably.
