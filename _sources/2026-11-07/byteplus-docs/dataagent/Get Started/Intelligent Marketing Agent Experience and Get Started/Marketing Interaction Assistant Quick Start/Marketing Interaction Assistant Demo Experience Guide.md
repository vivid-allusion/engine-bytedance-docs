In order to make it easy for users who are using Data Agent for the first time to get started quickly, we provide an intelligent conversation assistant demo for you to experience. This article introduces the experience steps and examples of the intelligent conversation assistant demo for you.

<span id="4b2592be"></span>
# Background introduction

Intelligent conversation assistant is a functional module of Data Agent, a dialog marketing hub that integrates real\-time customer portrait insights and AI decision engine. It realizes full\-scene intelligent interaction from customer demand analysis, rights recommendation to file update through purchase intention recognition, intelligent speech generation, accurate product recommendation, and full\-link task automation, and enhances customer conversion efficiency and business fineness through multi\-system collaborative closed\-loop management. Common application scenarios include customer service/shopping guide scenarios, knowledge Q & A scenarios, and conversation analysis scenarios.

# Experience example

<span id="4c0029f5"></span>
## Big consumer industry

The demo has been entered into the consumer industry\-related products, returns, preferential policies, and other knowledge. On the left side of the page, customer portraits will be analyzed in real time, including users' basic information, follow\-up strategies, detailed inquiries, and behavioral inquiries. The right side is the user's intention, dialogue risk, shopping guide plan, and speech query analyzed by the intelligent conversation assistant, and finally generates the recommended speech. You can choose to reply automatically, and the intelligent conversation assistant will automatically engage in multiple rounds of conversations with the user. You can also choose to intervene manually to modify the content of the reply.

Example of a question:


* What colors are available for the new sports jackets on the shelves?

* What is your membership system and what are your rights?

* Is there a discount for buying multiple items, and what is the price\-break discounts campaign like?
