# What can DataAgent do?

As an enterprise\-level DataAgent, DataAgent is built on multi\-scenario coverage capabilities. By proactively uncovering data relationships, intelligently gaining insights into business trends, accurately analyzing the essence of problems, and driving automated execution, it efficiently and continuously unlocks data value around the clock. It serves as an intelligent bridge between data resources and business actions, enabling a complete closed loop from data insights to business implementation.
