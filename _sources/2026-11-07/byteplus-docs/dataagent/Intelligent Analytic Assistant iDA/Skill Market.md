The Skill Market is the skill discovery and usage portal for the consumer side of iDA, which is used to centrally display the currently visible published skills. You can find skills by category, name, or use scenarios in the Skill Market, add the skills you need to your own Skill Kit, and enable or remove them as needed by different agents.

Through the Skill Market, skills are further used from "release to platform" to "personal availability, agent availability", helping you reuse existing skills and abilities in the enterprise more efficiently. The use of the iDA Skill Center remains unchanged. The following are the differences between the Skill Center and the Skill Market:


|Comparison term |**Skill Center** |**Skill Market** |
|---|---|---|
|Core positioning |Skills creation, configuration, publishing, and management backend |Skill discovery, addition, use, and personal dimension management portal |
|Object oriented |Administrators, skill creators, users with edit privileges |Ordinary users, skill creators |
|Main action |Create, configure, edit, publish, unpublish, download, replace files, configure permissions, configure agent authorization |View, filter, add, remove, edit/download/publish self\-built or authorized skills |
|Permission focus |Whether management skills are published, who is visible, and which agents are available |Manage user\-created and usable skills |
|Outcome impact |Decide whether skills are published to the platform and to which users or groups of users they are visible |Users add skills to their personal Skill Kit for use in their own conversations or in managed agents |

# Enter the Skill Market


1. Click on  **"Skill Market"**  in the left navigation of iDA to enter the Skill Market homepage.

2. The Skill Market is classified according to **the four tabs of "All", "Official", "Featured" and "My"**  , and you can quickly enter the corresponding skills to browse and select as needed. At the same time, it supports quick search by scene under different page labels.

3. A search text box is provided at the top right, where you can find related skills by skill name.

# Acquire and manage skills

In the Skill Market, you can add publicly available skills as individuals, as well as relevant agent configurations.


* **Add as an individual skill:**  On the card of the target skill, click the "Add" button, and the skill will go to  **"My"** , which can be used by any agent, corresponding to "Available for me" in the status filter.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/8e42b303684c4fdbbcde5361a2becab4~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

* **Add as an agent skill:**  Click "Agent", search and tick the agent you manage in the pop\-up list, and then click "Confirm Add", all users of the agent can use this skill.

   * **Adjust the range at any time:**  Skills that have been added can also be re\-entered into the "agent" from the card or details page, and continue to increase or decrease the applicable agent.

      <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/08afcb76eb95499ba2e974e9484a062b~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

* **Download Skills:**  If the skill creator has opened the download permission, you can download the skill pack locally on the details page; if not, the page will show Not Open for Download.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/43493bb47b8e4284884678970ff42184~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

# Create skills

Click the **Create Skill button** in the upper right corner of the Skill Market, and two ways to create will appear:


* **Create through session** : Enter the dialogue through the "Skill Creation" Skill, explain the purpose, scene and desired effect, and let iDA help you create the skill;

* **Upload Skills:**  Select this option in the pop\-up menu to upload a locally packaged skill file. Both .zip and .skill formats are supported, please make sure the compressed package contains SKILL.md .


<span id="4ae034dd"></span>
## Create through session

**Skills Creation Skill** can help you organize daily repetitive workflows into reusable iDA skills. You only need to specify the problem you want to solve, the applicable scenarios, and the desired effects, and it will assist you in sorting out the purpose, usage, and delivery of the skills. For detailed skill creation steps, please refer to the skill creation document in the skill center. **Skills Creation Skill** mainly supports the following abilities:


|Ability name |Description |
|---|---|
|**Create new skills** |Generate a usable skill based on your description. |
|**Optimize existing skills** |Help improve expression, supplementary explanation, make it easier to understand and use. |
|**Translate existing experience** |You can organize existing workflows, operating instructions, and documentation into skills. |
|**Reuse platform capabilities** |When creating skills, you can automatically combine iDA's existing built\-in tool capabilities to enable skills to complete practical operations such as query, processing, generation, and invocation. |
|**Automatically find reusable skills** |It will be combined with the ability to search for skills to help you see if there are similar or reusable skills in the market, avoid duplicate construction, and can also be expanded on the basis of existing skills. |
|**Ready to publish content** |Help organize into skill packs suitable for uploading, sharing, or publishing. |

## Upload skills

You can choose any local skill that supports both .zip and .skill formats when uploading, and include SKILL.md files in the compressed file.

After uploading, you can also configure your own skills to facilitate your own use and reuse and accumulation with other users.

# Use skills in session

In the input area of the dialog box, you will see `⚡`icon. Clicking on it will bring up a list of skills available for the current conversation, including both the agent's skills and those available to you personally.

At the same time, you can also select the skills you want to use by entering "@" directly in the dialog box. At the top of the panel, you can search for skills directly; if you hover your mouse over a skill, its skill introduction will be displayed on the right, and you can choose according to your needs.
