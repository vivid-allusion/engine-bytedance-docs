It revolves around real business tasks, actively understanding user intent, connecting enterprise data and knowledge, orchestrating tools, skills, and agent capabilities, generating deliverable business results, and driving subsequent actions to execute, helping enterprises build first\-line digital partners.

# Applicable Scenario

iDA is suitable for enterprise scenarios where certain data assets, business capabilities, and collaborative processes have been formed. At this point, the user's question is usually not just to query a single indicator, but to integrate data, knowledge, and subsequent actions into a complete task link.

<span id="d534e8bf"></span>
## **Data Analysis decision\-making scenario**


* Facing high\-frequency data consumption tasks such as business analysis, indicator diagnosis, marketing review, and abnormal attribution.

* Focusing on solving the problem that business personnel cannot find numbers, interpret the caliber, or organize analysis.

* Typical deliveries include indicator interpretation, attribution analysis, weekly operating reports, thematic reviews, and strategic judgment criteria.


<span id="86118635"></span>
## **Knowledge research delivery scenario**


* For in\-depth research, industry research, customer research, product research, new product opportunity evaluation and other tasks.

* Focusing on solving the problem that data, documents, knowledge bases, and external materials are scattered, and the research process relies on manual sorting.

* Typical deliveries include research reports, client briefs, competitive analysis, opportunity judgments, and draft proposals.


<span id="127469a7"></span>
## **Business collaborative execution scenario**


* Facing tasks that require the continuation of analysis results into communication, collaboration, and process actions.

* Focus on solving the breakpoint problem of "manual paraphrasing, sorting, distribution, and follow\-up after the analysis is completed".

* Typical deliveries include meeting materials, task lists, sales follow\-up summaries, marketing recommendations, and system action triggers.



|Three applicable scenarios |Typical task |Core value |
|---|---|---|
|Data analytics decision |Weekly report generation, GMV/revenue diagnosis, activity review, abnormal attribution, channel evaluation, store or regional performance comparison |Let business personnel go from "asking questions" to "getting conclusions" faster, lower the threshold for data use, and improve the speed of analysis response and judgment quality |
|Knowledge Research Delivery |Industry research, customer visit preparation, competitor analysis, user feedback analysis, new product opportunity evaluation, strategic data collation |Merge structured data and unstructured knowledge into the same task link, reduce the cost of data collection and viewpoint collation, and improve research system |
|Business Collaboration |Meeting notes and reporting materials generation, sales assistance, marketing suggestion output, task dismantling, notification synchronization, process connection |Let the results not only stay in the question and answer layer, but also enter the team collaboration and follow\-up execution, improving the closed\-loop efficiency from analysis to action |

# Main Functions of the Product

The product capabilities of iDA are not designed around a single question and answer portal, but around a complete execution link for real\-world tasks in the enterprise. From user\-initiated tasks to the analysis and generation of system call data, knowledge, skills, and tools, to configuration governance and operation optimization, the product has gradually formed a modular system covering front\-end use, middle\-end capacity precipitation, and back\-end management. Starting from the core functional modules, the following will introduce how iDA supports enterprises to achieve a complete closed loop from information acquisition to task delivery.

<span id="be6e2b90"></span>
## Skill Center

**Skill Center** is the core module for unified management, distribution and invocation of skill capabilities in iDA. Its function is to further disassemble the general question answering ability into a reusable, configurable and operable professional capability unit.

The skill center is not "can answer the question", but "**for different tasks, what ability should the system call to complete**".

It essentially has three categories of responsibilities:


* **Ability precipitation** : encapsulate high\-frequency tasks such as checking, analysis, writing, translation, summary, and generation into skills

* **Ability management** : support skills to view, search, copy, log off, delete, download and other life cycle management

* **Ability distribution** : allow skills to be directly selected, ticked, or called by scenario in the question box


<span id="dfc304c8"></span>
## Ability scenario Q & A

iDA organizes capabilities by scenario around the high\-frequency tasks that users perform in their daily work, rather than presenting them as a stack of fragmented tools. Allows you to first enter the corresponding scene according to the task objective, and then invoke the appropriate skills to complete tasks such as information acquisition, data processing, content production, and idea generation. As a whole, functional scenarios are divided into **knowledge retrieval, data analytics, document writing, and creative generation**, forming a clearer task entrance system, which is convenient for quick decision\-making and positioning.

## Configuration management

iDA provides a complete set of configuration tools to optimize the effect of intelligent quizzes, covering semantic models, enterprise knowledge engine configuration, recall logic customization, prompt intervention, recommendation question/indicator summary management and operation, etc., to help the quizzing tool integrate into business scenarios and make it a professional assistant for business quizzes.

<span id="a3975f14"></span>
## Open integration

iDA supports integration with Feishu robots, supports embedding into customer application systems through H5, supports secondary open integration through OpenAPI, and supports embedding with BytePlus's HiAgent products to realize linkage applications.

<span id="aea26f5e"></span>
## Data preparation


* The iDA dock can be connected to external data sources, offline data, public data, and data from the BytePlus data product matrix, and data can be added to the Agent dock from these data sources to complete data processing and integration, and perform intelligent querying.

* iDA base comes with ByteHouse, a self\-developed speed engine as a query acceleration engine, which greatly improves the speed of data query and writing, and realizes an excellent user experience of hundreds of billions of data second\-level queries.

* The iDA base also supports data processing, which can process data through visual modeling, prepare data cleaning, transformation, fusion, and pre\-calculation for subsequent data consumption, and manage and maintain data sets.

# General Operating Process

<span id="01fc549e"></span>
## Create a project and authorize it

Users can create projects according to business scenarios, and set data access and functional operation permissions for different users or user groups according to the responsibilities of internal personnel and data security requirements of the enterprise. For details, please refer to [Project Information](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/dataagent/Project_Information) and [User Management](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/dataagent/User_Management).

<span id="937de79b"></span>
## Data access and processing

Through the data connection function, iDA can connect with external data sources, offline data, public data, BytePlus data product matrix data, and support the processing of data through visual modeling, please refer to the [Data Preparation Overview](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/dataagent/Data_Preparation_Overview_).

<span id="0f013fa7"></span>
## Create and configure iDA

Configure semantic model, business knowledge, recommendation problem, fuzzy problem disassembly, indicator summary, prompt words, etc., so that iDA fits business needs, accurately understands user problems and provides effective analysis. For details, please refer to [Quick Overview of Creation and Configuration for Agents](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/dataagent/Quick_Overview_of_Creation_and_Configuration_for_Agents).

<span id="08ccc7cd"></span>
## Use iDA

Users initiate data queries or analysis requirements in natural language, and iDA performs analysis processing after receiving instructions, providing query results or in\-depth analysis reports, and supports multiple rounds of interaction to improve analysis. Please refer to [Deep Research Agent](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/dataagent/Deep_Research_Agent).
