Usage management is used to view the usage records and consumption generated by enterprise members in iDA. Administrators can view the details by time, and can also check the usage generated at a specific time based on member conditions.

# Prerequisite

* You have logged in to the BytePlus console and entered the iDA enterprise management background.

* Super administrators and administrators can view the usage of enterprise members.

* **Existing history customers** do not have the ability to control **member usage** by default, and need to contact the product side to open it separately before supporting member usage control.

# Enter usage management

1. Log in to the BytePlus console, enter iDA, and select the corresponding agent to enter the Q&A interface.

2. Move the mouse to the user name at the bottom left, and click the Settings button to enter the management background.

3. Click **Usage Management** in the side navigation of the Administration background, and you can view **member usage** or **usage details** by switching tabs.

## View member usage

If you are an administrator, you can view the consumption corresponding to each seat member. Support searching by member name, and filter to view the consumption according to the time range.

**Alocate Usage**: You can click the **Alocate Usage** button in the operation column to increase or decrease the usage of each seat member according to the actual situation.

View usage details

You can check the current account usage by time range (yesterday / today/this week / this month / last 7 days / last 30 days / custom range).

Click on a specific conversation name to enter the corresponding conversation.
