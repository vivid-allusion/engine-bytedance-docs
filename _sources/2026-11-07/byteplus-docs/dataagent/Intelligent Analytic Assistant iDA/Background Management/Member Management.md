# Overview

Member management is used to help **administrators** uniformly maintain the range of users within the enterprise that can use iDA. Administrators can add, view, adjust or remove members from the member list, and configure the corresponding usage permission and points/tokens for members according to the seats purchased by the enterprise and the usage policy. Through member management, companies can clearly control "who can use iDA, which capabilities can be used, and how much can be consumed".

# Prerequisite

* The administrator has logged into the iDA SaaS console and entered iDA;

* The company has completed the purchase or opening of iDA seats, and there is still an allocable balance for the number of seats.

# Enter member management

1. Log in to the BytePlus console, enter iDA, and select the corresponding agent to enter the Q&A interface.

2. Move the mouse to the user name at the bottom left, and click Settings button to enter the management background.

   ![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/8674f4c4c9084a2fbee8f390385fa3e9~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image)

3. Navigate to the side of the management background and select **Member Management \> Member List** to view the current members and seat usage.

   ![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/8b6c33d0963c48cd9ed7af46bd575589~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image)

# Invite members

When your seats are not used up, you can click the invite member button in the upper right corner of the member list to invite the sub\-account under the current main account to join iDA and assign seats to it.

> **注意**

> Invited members must be BytePlus sub\-account under the main account. For adding sub\-account operation, see [Add system members](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/dataagent/Intelligent_Analysis_Agent_Administrator_Quick_Start_Guide#586c7a05).

![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/e2d2f1dab3cb45679a61defdf525dc71~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image)

# Release seats

Releasing a seat means **taking back the product usage seat currently occupied by a member**. After the release, the member will no longer occupy the seat and will **not be able to continue using the product.**

If you want to release the seat, in the **member list corresponding to the member, find release button of the operation column. After clicking the release button,**  you can release the seat. After the seat is released, the points corresponding to the seat will enter the public points pool, and the administrator can redistribute the points.
