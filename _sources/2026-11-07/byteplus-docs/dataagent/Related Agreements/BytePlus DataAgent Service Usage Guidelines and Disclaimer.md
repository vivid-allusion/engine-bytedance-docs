# **1. Service description**

**1.1**【**DataAgent/Data Intelligent Agent**】is designed for vertical scenarios such as data analysis, intelligent marketing, and data development, providing enterprises with intelligent agents that can understand business issues, actively participate in the data production and consumption chain, and enable deep utilization of data within the enterprise.

**1.2**You may use DataAgent using the large model already configured in DataAgent; you may also purchase a legally compliant third\-party large model ("third\-party large model") to use DataAgent (**only for private deployment scenarios**). If you purchase a third\-party large model yourself, you must verify and confirm the legality, compliance, security, and suitability for your business of the third\-party large model. You must register a third\-party large model account yourself and comply with the authorization, usage guidelines, and other requirements of the third\-party large model. BytePlus is not responsible if you are unable to use DataAgent normally due to reasons attributable to you or the third party.

# **2. Usage guidelines**

**2.1**You may only use DataAgent functions within your own business and internally in combination with your actual business scenarios. You must not input content unrelated to your business into this service, and you must not provide generative artificial intelligence services to the public through DataAgent functions.

**2.2**You must not use this service, prompts, or content generated through prompts in this service for the development, training, annotation, fine\-tuning, optimization, iteration, or other similar activities of new models or other models outside this service. You must not use this service to develop, train, or improve algorithms or models that compete with BytePlus or its affiliates.

**2.3**When using this service, you must comply with relevant laws and regulations concerning cybersecurity, data security, personal information protection, intellectual property rights, and content ecosystem governance. You are required to maintain internet order and network security, and must not use this service to publish statements that endanger national security, undermine ethnic unity, disrupt social order, are discriminatory, or improper, or infringe upon any third party’s rights to personal information, reputation, personality, intellectual property, property, or any other rights. You must also not engage in activities related to pornography, gambling, drugs, or any actions that violate laws, regulations, or public order and good morals.

**2.4**To fulfill legal and compliance requirements, BytePlus has the right to use technical or manual means to review your activities and information when using this service, including but not limited to reviewing content you submit to or output from this service, and establishing and improving risk filtering mechanisms and databases of illegal content characteristics.

**2.5**BytePlus will not use content you submit to this service, content you output using the service, or data you analyze and process with DataAgent to train or fine\-tune large models.

**2.6**You understand and agree that BytePlus will automatically receive and record the dialogue information you enter during interactions with [DataAgent], and use this information to output content to you in order to provide the [DataAgent] service. We will also use dialogue information to improve [DataAgent]'s ability to understand your input, so as to continuously enhance the speed and quality of [DataAgent]'s recognition and response, and improve service effectiveness. During the dialogue process, you can also evaluate the content output by [DataAgent], including actions such as liking or disliking. We will collect your evaluation information, including error reasons you actively submit, to continuously improve the quality of [DataAgent]'s output content.
