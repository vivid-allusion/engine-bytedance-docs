**Effective date: 7 April 2025**

This "Data Intelligent Agent Service Level Agreement" (hereinafter referred to as "this Agreement") specifies the service availability level indicators and compensation plan for services provided by BytePlus to customers.


1. Definitions

   1. Service period: A service period is a calendar month. If the period is less than a full calendar month, it is calculated as a full calendar month.

   2. Total service period time: Total number of days in the service period × 24 (hours) × 60 (minutes).

   3. Service unavailability per unit time: Calculated in 5\-minute intervals. If all consecutive attempts by the customer to connect to the Data Intelligent Agent service fail within a 5\-minute interval, that 5\-minute interval is considered as service unavailability. In a service period, the number of minutes of service unavailability = total number of 5\-minute intervals of service unavailability during the period × 5.

   4. Monthly service fee: The total service fee paid by the customer for the Data Intelligent Agent service in a calendar month. If the customer pays for multiple months at once, the monthly service fee is calculated by apportioning the total amount over the number of months purchased.

2. Service availability

   1. Service availability calculation method


The service availability of the Data Intelligent Agent will be calculated based on the service period as follows:

Service availability = (total number of minutes in the service period \- number of minutes of service unavailability) / total number of minutes in the service period × 100%


2. Service availability commitment


The service availability provided by BytePlus for this service shall not be less than 99.00%. If this service does not meet the above service availability commitment, the customer may obtain compensation in accordance with Article 3 of this Agreement.


3. Exclusions


The following exclusions will not be counted as service unavailability time or failed requests:

```Plain Text
  1. System maintenance performed by BytePlus after prior notice to the customer, including cutovers, repairs, upgrades, and simulated failure drills;
  2. Caused by network or device failures, or configuration changes, outside of any BytePlus-owned devices;
  3. Caused by hacker attacks on the customer's application;
  4. Caused by improper maintenance or insufficient confidentiality by the customer, resulting in the loss or leakage of data, credentials, passwords, and more;
  5. Caused by the customer's negligence or actions authorized by the customer;
  6. Caused by the customer's failure to follow BytePlus product documentation or usage recommendations;
  7. Caused by force majeure and unexpected events;
  8. Requests originating from IP addresses outside the Chinese mainland;
  9. 
```



3. Compensation plan

   1. Compensation criteria


Compensation will be issued in the form of vouchers, which cannot be redeemed for cash, transferred, or given as a gift. They are only valid for ordering Data Intelligence services through the BytePlus official website. No invoice will be issued for the distribution of vouchers. This compensation is the full and sole compensation we provide to you for the service not meeting the service availability commitment, and under any circumstances, the total compensation amount shall not exceed the customer's actual consumption for the month in which the service availability commitment was not met.


|Service availability |Compensation |
|---|---|
|Less than 99.00% but equal to or greater than 95.00% |25% of the monthly service fee |
|Less than 95.00% |50% of the monthly service fee |



2. Compensation application deadline


The customer may apply for compensation for services that did not meet availability after the fifth business day of each month for the previous month. **Compensation applications must be submitted within two months after the end of the relevant month in which the service delivery did not meet availability.**  **Compensation applications submitted beyond the deadline will not be accepted.** 


4. **Other**


BytePlus reserves the right to modify this service level agreement. If any terms of this service level agreement are modified, BytePlus will notify you 30 days in advance via website announcement or by sending an in\-site message or email. If you do not agree with the modifications made by BytePlus to this service level agreement, you have the right to stop using this service. If you continue to use this service, it will be deemed that you accept the modified service level agreement.
