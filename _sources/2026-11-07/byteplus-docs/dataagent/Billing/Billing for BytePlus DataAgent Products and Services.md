<span id="be9f5a2c"></span>
## Renewal

You can renew the purchased version or value\-added services separately. The renewal duration is calculated in months. Note: If you only renew a value\-added service but do not renew the version, you will not be able to access DataAgent after your version expires. If you purchase both the version and value\-added services but only renew the version, the value\-added services will be automatically disabled upon expiration.

<span id="299d2ea2"></span>
## Service expiration and shutdown


* If you do not complete the renewal by 24:00 on the day the service expires, BytePlus will shut down the service and the corresponding service entry.

* If you purchase or renew the BytePlus DataAgent service within 72 hours after the service expires, the service will be restored to normal.

* If the service expires for more than 72 hours, it will be deemed that you have voluntarily abandoned the services you have purchased on the BytePlus platform. The related resources and instances will be released, and released data cannot be recovered.

## Refunds

Paid services are non\-refundable.
