<span id="一-营销：智能定向，千人千面，数据洞察"></span>
# 1. Marketing: intelligent targeting, thousands of people, thousands of custom requirements, data insights

Feature flag's rich directional circle ability can realize thousands of people including **landing pages**, **activity strategies**, **product functions** and even **product processes**. For example, for Geek users, open up the latest and most interesting capabilities and catch their attention. For new users who are less accurate, shorten the conversion process and retain customers in the most convenient way. Wouldn't it be interesting if you could divide the customer group into 10 categories and turn the product into 10 versions of various flag arrangements?

<span id="二-稳定：360°-全方位上线保护"></span>
# 2. Stable: 360 ° all\-round online protection

The bottom layer of APM is directly empowered to access all metrics including crashes, errors, and performance problems, and supports Feature\-level and even variant\-level online monitoring to ensure that problems are found and located at the first time, minimizing the risk of release. In the future, a wealth of technical debt processing capabilities, Feature Maps, and detailed file management will be launched to ensure that variants maintain an optimal and usable layout at all times.

<span id="三-高效：多角色衔接，加速迭代"></span>
# 3. Efficient: Multi\-role integration, accelerated iteration

When the version is launched abnormally, it can be easily rolled back at the feature level, rather than directly removing the version from the shelves, without affecting the customer experience of the downloaded new version or the launch of other normal functions. Through access control, some Flag supports non\-technical personnel to intervene in management, allowing business requirements to be reflected in functions more quickly and delivered to users in a timely manner, while also avoiding complex online approvals.
