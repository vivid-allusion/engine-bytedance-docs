<span id="service-scope"></span>
# Service Scope

BytePlus support covers:


* Consultation on BytePlus products

* Best practices for using BytePlus services

* Troubleshooting and issue resolution for BytePlus products

* API and SDK usage guidance

* Inquiries related to BytePlus billing, financial matters, and contracts


Our support does NOT include:


* Customer\-specific business operations or cloud\-based maintenance services

* Development, collaboration, or troubleshooting of customer\-specific business applications

* Support for third\-party applications built on BytePlus cloud\-native capabilities

* Inquiries submitted through unauthorized or non\-BytePlus supported IM groups

* Routine maintenance services for third\-party software, including installation, patch updates, testing, fault diagnosis, and optimization

<span id="best-practices-for-engaging-support"></span>
# Best Practices for Engaging Support


1. **Provide detailed information**: When submitting a support request, include comprehensive details about the issue, including any error messages, logs, and steps to reproduce the problem. Select the correct product and issue category when creating a ticket. This helps us quickly and accurately identify the relevant product and understand the nature of your issue. Include specific examples, timestamps, and any recent changes that might be related to the problem.

2. **Set appropriate severity levels**: Accurately categorize the business impact of your issue to ensure proper prioritization and response.

3. **Be available for follow\-up**: Ensure that your contact information is up\-to\-date and be prepared to respond promptly to requests for additional information.

4. **Utilize self\-service resources**: Explore our extensive documentation, FAQs, and community forums for quick solutions to common issues.


<span id="byteplus-customer-success-managers-csms"></span>
# BytePlus Customer Success Managers (CSMs)

For Premium Service customers, a BytePlus Customer Success Manager (CSM) serves as your trusted advisor, partnering with you proactively to maximize platform value, maintain operational stability, and accelerate your business growth. Your CSM supports you across:


1. **Service reviews and planning**: Estimate your throughput, volume, and cost, and make optimization recommendations to keep your environment efficient and cost\-effective.

2. **Critical event readiness**: Prepare for launches, campaigns, and migrations through architecture reviews and capacity planning that minimize risk.

3. **Training and workshops**: Deliver enablement sessions and share best practices to help your teams build expertise on BytePlus products.

4. **AI use\-case co\-creation**: Understand your business context and collaboratively create AI use cases that drive meaningful outcomes.

5. **Cross\-team advocacy**: Coordinate across our support, product, and R&D teams and advocate on your behalf to ensure your long\-term success.
