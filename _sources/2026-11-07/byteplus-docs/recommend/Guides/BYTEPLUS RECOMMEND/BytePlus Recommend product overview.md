# Use cases

BytePlus Recommend covers use cases across e\-commerce, media and entertainment, advertising, and more. Common use cases include:


* **Retail/E\-commerce:**  Creates an immersive shopping experience through personalized recommendations. Our solution covers a wide range of customizations tailored to your requirements. This includes integrating recommendations on the homepage, product detail screen, shopping cart, category page, as well as newsletters. Enhance user engagement and drive conversions by delivering relevant product suggestions throughout the customer journey.

* **News/Content:**  BytePlus Recommend enables immediacy and interactivity in news and content consumption from articles to posts. Our technology provides accurate recommendations across various content types, including articles, posts, and more. Keep users engaged and encourage exploration by offering personalized recommendations that match their interests and preferences.

* **Live\-streaming/Video**: Drive user watch time and enhance engagement with BytePlus Recommend for live\-streaming and video platforms. Our solution suggests live content that aligns with users' interests, increasing customer loyalty and retention. Delivering personalized recommendations can keep users engaged longer and offer a tailored viewing experience.

* **Advertisement**: Maximize advertising performance with highly personalized recommendations powered by BytePlus Recommend. Our solution enables you to deliver targeted advertisements at various touchpoints, from in\-app searches to product display advertisements. By leveraging user preferences and behavior data, you can optimize ad targeting and increase conversion rates, maximizing your advertising ROI.

# BytePlus Recommend products

Within BytePlus Recommend, we have two products catering to clients in various industries:

<span id="BytePlus Recommend Standard"></span>
## BytePlus Recommend Standard

BytePlus Recommend Standard is the recommended choice for companies in the e\-commerce or media and entertainment industry who aim to provide recommendations on their websites or applications. With minimal configurations, you can create a customized recommendation model that aligns with your business objectives. For more information, please contact the BytePlus Recommend team.

<span id="BytePlus Recommend Premium"></span>
## BytePlus Recommend Premium

BytePlus Recommend Premium is recommended for companies with complicated data requirements and business objectives. Our BytePlus Recommend algorithm engineers and data engineering team will reach out and work together with you to build a customized model to achieve your intended outcomes. BytePlus Recommend Premium covers use cases across e\-commerce, media and entertainment, over\-the\-air, advertising, and many more. If you are interested, contact the BytePlus Recommend team for more information.
