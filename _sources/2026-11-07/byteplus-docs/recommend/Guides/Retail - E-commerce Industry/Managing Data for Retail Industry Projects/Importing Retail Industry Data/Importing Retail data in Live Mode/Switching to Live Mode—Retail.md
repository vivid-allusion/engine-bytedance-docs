Once you've completed the data import and validation in **Trial** Mode, it's essential to ensure that all fatal errors have been resolved. Once these errors are cleared, you can transition to **Live** Mode and proceed to import your complete historical and incremental data.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Once you switch your project into Live Mode, you will not be able to modify your data configurations.</div>


**To switch your project to Live:** 


1. Sign in to [BytePlus Recommend](https://console.byteplus.com/recommend/home).

2. Click on the project you want to view the data validation.

3. On the Configure screen, click **Next**.

4. On the Import screen, click **Next**.

   The Data Validate screen appears.

   <div style="text-align: center">
   <img src="https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/2cbbeb8fbe504987873af2ddd4d537a0~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image" width="864px" />   </div>
   

5. Click **Import data in Live mode**.

   OR

   If all your data errors have been cleared, the **Switch to Live Mode** toggle will be active. You can toggle it on and the project will switch to Live mode.

   The *Switch to Live Mode* pop\-up window appears.

6. Click **Switch mode**.

   The project switches to Live mode.
