This article introduces the Regions and Availability Zones supported by the API Gateway.


* **Region**: Refers to the geographic area where the physical data centers are located, and the region cannot be changed once resources are created. Different regions are completely isolated internally to ensure maximum stability and fault tolerance between regions.

* **Availability Zone (AZ)** : Refers to physically separate areas within the same region that have independent power and networking. Whether to place resources in the same availability zone mainly depends on your requirements for disaster recovery capability and network latency.
