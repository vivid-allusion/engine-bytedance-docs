This article introduces the product advantages of the API Gateway.

<span id=".5oul5oqx5LqR5Y6f55Sf"></span>
## High\-performance AI add\-ons

It supports add\-on development in multiple languages, including C++, Lua, and Golang, to meet the needs of AI applications for rapid scaling, fast iteration, and high\-performance service.

<span id=".5LyY5byC5oCn5Lu35q-U"></span>
## AI\-powered routing

Load capabilities better suited to inference scenarios are provided for LLM and multimodal models, and an intelligent routing and scheduling mechanism is adopted.

<span id=".55yB5b-D5YWo5omY566h"></span>
## Enterprise\-level permissions

Provides an identity\-based token rate limiting mechanism that manages authentication and usage quotas for different users of the AI service, and allocates resources for AI service calls.

<span id=".56iz5a6a6auY5Y-v55So"></span>
## Embrace MCP

Fully supports the MCP SSE stateful protocol and authentication and authorization, and can automatically convert REST APIs to the MCP Server, enabling online services to quickly benefit from MCP.
