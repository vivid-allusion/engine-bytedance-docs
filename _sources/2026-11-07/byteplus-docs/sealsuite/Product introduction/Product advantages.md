SealSuite is a unified security platform for digital office environments, built on the concept of "preset security." It simplifies IT administration and enhances employee convenience by integrating identity, network, and device management. This document outlines the core product advantages, including seamless tool integration for users, centralized resource authorization for administrators, optimized cross\-platform device management, and a dynamic, zero\-trust security implementation. SealSuite improves critical IT management security features in digital office environments, covering identity, network, and device systems. It streamlines administration and offers flexible office security solutions to tackle the challenges of enterprise IT management.

<span id="b74b075f"></span>
# Seamless integration of office tools across various devices

SealSuite is an efficient office platform for corporate employees, featuring robust security measures. It includes Single Sign\-on, one\-click VPN access, one\-click Wi\-Fi connection, and one\-click IT service activation. Employees won't need to install multiple clients for services like VPN, network access control (NAC), antivirus, data security, and device management, or remember, replace, or enter various passwords. This simplifies daily office tasks for employees.

<span id="ec316a4a"></span>
# Centralized authorization for enterprise resources

SealSuite facilitates the creation of a multi\-dimensional authorization model utilizing members, roles, organizational structures, and custom tags. This system provides best practices for managing user access throughout the employee lifecycle. Enterprises can streamline the management of internal network connections and app access permissions using SealSuite. This simplifies administration by eliminating the need for administrators to establish multiple account systems for VPN, corporate Wi\-Fi, and business applications. For VPN and network resources, SealSuite utilizes a node deployment that allows enterprises to effortlessly and flexibly scale based on business volume or office or site expansion.

<span id="131d5ec9"></span>
# Optimized management of devices

In digital office environments, SealSuite adeptly manages diverse device types across Mac, Windows, Linux, iOS, and Android systems. It offers consistent management capabilities, including status statistics, software management, baseline settings, and troubleshooting. This addresses the previously challenging issue of managing and maintaining devices connected to the network.

Enterprise administrators can filter device information and status by department and role, understand the IT requirements of different groups, and optimize IT investments. Device grouping enables differentiated management of various devices, allowing for a more granular and effective device access policy. Additionally, analyzing client\-side logs improves troubleshooting efficiency.

<span id="b23320fd"></span>
# Secure software\-based systems implementation

Leveraging the interconnected capabilities of identity, network, and device modules, the SealSuite dynamic control engine provides a variety of risk definition parameters and settings. This enables enterprise administrators to easily develop a security management framework that is better tailored to their specific business scenarios. Data aggregation analysis improves the accuracy of risk identification, while integration between modules supports the implementation of corporate office security management policies. For example, if a device is infected with a virus, SealSuite can automatically downgrade network connections or apply restrictive ACL policies. It can also enforce policies related to screen lock passwords, weak passwords, and connections to insecure Wi\-Fi.

For corporate office networks, SealSuite adopts a zero\-trust security philosophy, which consistently enforces the principle of never trusting and always verifying. It continually evaluates the security status of identities and devices, provides dynamic access control, and provides comprehensive network access control. Additionally, SealSuite offers traffic encryption, firewall alerts, and other functionalities. This approach effectively establishes a secure and trusted link across identity, network, and devices.
