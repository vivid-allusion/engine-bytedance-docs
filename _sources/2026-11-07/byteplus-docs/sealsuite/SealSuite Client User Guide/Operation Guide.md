The SealSuite client is an application that enables enterprise employees to work securely and efficiently from both desktop and mobile devices. Get started by learning about the core features available on the desktop and mobile clients, including secure network access (VPN/Wi\-Fi), endpoint security checks, network diagnostics, and account management.

<span id="34bb5111"></span>
## Notes


* The specific features available in the SealSuite client are customized by enterprise administrators in the SealSuite Admin Console. If you cannot find a feature described in this document, it indicates that your enterprise has not configured this feature. You can contact your enterprise administrator for assistance.

* The SealSuite client is available for desktop and mobile devices. Unless otherwise specified in the corresponding document, all operations described in the desktop client documentation are performed on desktop endpoints, and all operations described in the mobile client documentation are performed on mobile endpoints.

* SealSuite provides a portal website that can be accessed from desktop or mobile endpoints. Through the portal website, you can obtain the SealSuite client download link, modify your personal information, change your password, and more.

## Desktop Client

The SealSuite client for desktop supports macOS, Windows, and Linux. After your enterprise enables SealSuite services, you need to download and use the SealSuite client on the corresponding desktop endpoint to begin working securely and stably. The desktop client operation guide is as follows:


* Before using the SealSuite client for desktop, you need to download and activate the SealSuite client.

* The following operations are supported on the SealSuite client for desktop:

   * Log in to the SealSuite client to begin working securely and efficiently.

   * When your office environment cannot connect to the enterprise wired network or Wi\-Fi network, you can connect to the enterprise VPN through the SealSuite client for remote work.

   * When you are in the enterprise office area, you can connect to the Wi\-Fi or wired network for work.

   * You can use the SealSuite client to perform security checks on the current endpoint. SealSuite checks the device baseline, applications, and running programs. You can view and fix each risk item.

   * The SealSuite client provides network diagnostics. When you encounter issues such as network access failure, VPN connection failure, or message receiving failure, you can use **Network Diagnostics** to check the network connection, network proxy, and VPN network of the current endpoint, and then fix or report issues based on the diagnostic results.

   * If network issues require a DNS reset, you can use the **DNS Reset** feature in the SealSuite desktop client to reset DNS manually or automatically.

   * When enterprise IT administrators troubleshoot network issues, they may need to ask you for endpoint\-related information. Device information, network information, routing information, and VPN connection information can all be obtained from **Device Information** in the SealSuite client.

   * When you encounter network issues, account issues, client issues, or other problems, you can search for solutions in the **Help Center** of the SealSuite client. If existing solutions cannot resolve your issue, you can also contact your enterprise IT administrator for technical support.

   * The SealSuite client supports settings such as startup page, language, shortcuts, and version update policy. You can customize client settings to match your operating preferences.

   * If you forget the login password when logging in to the SealSuite client or SealSuite portal website with your phone number or email address, you can click **Forgot Password** on the login page to log in directly or contact your IT administrator to reset the password.

   * When you need to change your password regularly or forget your password, you can reset the password after logging in to the SealSuite portal website.

   * The SealSuite client supports switching the enterprise identification number. If you need to leave the previous enterprise and join a new enterprise, you can manually switch the enterprise identification number.

   * The SealSuite client for desktop also provides features such as quick application access, issue feedback, and device registration.

## Mobile Client

The SealSuite client for mobile supports iOS and Android. After your enterprise enables SealSuite services, you need to download and use the SealSuite client on the corresponding mobile endpoint to begin working securely and stably. The mobile client operation guide is as follows:


* Before using the SealSuite client for mobile, you need to download and activate the SealSuite client.

* The following operations are supported on the SealSuite client for mobile:

   * Log in to the SealSuite client to begin working efficiently and securely.

   * SealSuite supports integration with third\-party custom one\-time password (OTP) services starting from version 2.0.11. If your enterprise administrator has configured multiple OTP services, you can add additional OTP services by using **Scan** in the SealSuite client for mobile. You can then complete account secondary authentication through the OTP service.

   * When your office environment cannot connect to the enterprise Wi\-Fi, you can connect to the enterprise VPN through the SealSuite client for remote work.

   * When you are in the enterprise office area, you can connect to the Wi\-Fi on your mobile endpoint for work.

   * The SealSuite client provides network diagnostics. When you encounter issues such as network access failure, VPN connection failure, or message receiving failure, you can use network diagnostics to check the network connection, network proxy, and VPN network of the current endpoint, and then fix or report issues based on the diagnostic results.

   * The SealSuite client supports security baseline checks for the endpoint to detect potential security risks on the current endpoint.

   * In **Account and Security** of the SealSuite client for mobile, you can view information such as your name, email address, phone number, and operation logs. You can also modify your phone number or login password in **Account and Security**.

   * When you encounter network issues, account issues, client issues, or other problems, you can search for solutions in **FAQ** of the SealSuite client.

   * In **Settings** of the SealSuite client, you can adjust settings related to sound alerts, vibration alerts, and VPN sticky.

   * If your endpoint has network issues or you want to submit optimization suggestions, you can submit feedback to your enterprise administrator through **Feedback** in the SealSuite client. The enterprise administrator will handle your issue, and you can also proactively contact the administrator to follow up on the progress.

   * If you forget the login password when logging in to the SealSuite client or SealSuite portal website with your phone number or email address, you can click **Forgot Password** on the login page to complete secondary authentication and log in directly, or contact your IT administrator to reset the password.

   * The SealSuite client supports switching the enterprise identification number. If you need to leave the previous enterprise and join a new enterprise, you can manually switch the enterprise identification number.

   * The SealSuite client for mobile also provides features such as quick application access, issue feedback, and device registration.
