If the screen watermark you configured is invisible, you can use the invisible watermark extraction function to quickly trace the invisible watermark information when you need to trace the watermark within the screenshot.

<span id="edcb029c"></span>
# Instructions for use

An invisible watermark policy must take effect on the device associated with watermarked screenshots, otherwise the watermark information cannot be extracted.

<span id="92e0e592"></span>
# Procedure


1. Log in to the Admin Console.

2. In the left navigation bar, click **DLP** \> **Watermark**.

3. On the **Watermark** page, click the **Invisible Watermark Extraction** tab.

4. In the **Invisible Watermark Extraction** tab, upload a screenshot that needs the extraction of invisible watermarks.

   1. Drag and drop files or click the plus sign (+) to upload your desired files.

   2. Enter the image URL address or click **Upload** to upload a new file.

   3. The size of the image needs to be smaller than 20 M, and there are no restrictions on the image format or resolution.

      <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/7f7fa025c2ef45a998d77919fcb6e02e~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

5. View the **Invisible Watermark Extraction and Detection Report** after the extraction of invisible watermarks is completed.

   * The report contains image information, corresponding device information, and user information.

   * At the bottom of the page, you can re\-upload images, or download the current invisible watermark report and save it locally.

   * If you haven't extracted relevant information from the embedded invisible watermark, try re\-uploading images by clicking **Upload** at the bottom of the page.
