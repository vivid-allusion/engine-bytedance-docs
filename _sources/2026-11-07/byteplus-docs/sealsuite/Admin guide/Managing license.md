In the License function, you can view the expiration date, basic functions, and resource usage corresponding to each function of the SealSuite service, such as the number of members or nodes.

# Procedure

1. Log in to the Admin Console.

2. In the top navigation bar, click **Entitlements Data**.

3. On the **License** page, you can view the following information.

   * **Product expiration**: Your service's expiration date is displayed in the top\-right corner.

      ![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/46c709f258914e579901c458872a5d84~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-topic.webp)

   * **Resource Usage**: Each feature card displays your current usage of licensed resources, such as users and nodes. The color of the usage numbers indicates your consumption level.

      * **Black:**  Usage is below 85% of your total quota.

      * **Orange:**  Usage is at or above 85% of your total quota.

      * **Red:**  Usage is at or above 100% of your total quota.

If your current feature set or resource quotas are insufficient for your needs, you can upgrade your service plan.
