Utilizing the Employee View function of Permissions in the Admin Portal, you can view VPN access permissions of each employee within your enterprise. This includes details such as visible gateway, authorization status, expiration time, and more.

<span id="6aa779e7"></span>
# Procedure


1. Log in to the Admin Console.

2. From the left navigation bar, go to **Zero Trust Access** \> **VPN** \> **ACL Policy**.

3. Under the **Detailed** **View** tab of the page, view users' VPN permissions.

   By utilizing filters or searching for specific employees by name, you can easily locate and view their VPN permissions. This includes information such as **gateway**, **Authorization source**, **Status**, and **Expiration Date**.
