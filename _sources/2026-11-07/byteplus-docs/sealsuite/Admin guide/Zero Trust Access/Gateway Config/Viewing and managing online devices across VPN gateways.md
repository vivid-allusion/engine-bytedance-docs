You can view and manage all online devices connected to the corporate intranet across VPN gateways. This feature enables you to monitor device statistics and manage devices in a unified manner.

<span id="dab27be4"></span>
# Viewing online devices


1. Log in to the Admin Console.

2. In the left navigation bar, click **Zero Trust Access** \> **Gateway Config** \> **Online Endpoints**.

3. On the **Online Endpoints** page, view aggregate online devices and the device list.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/00a13b6d4e28465e94b31ebf9da9fd71~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

   Specific descriptions of each information item in the device list are as follows.

   
   |**Information Item** |**Description** |
   |---|---|
   |**Device** |The device type and DID. To view details of the device, you can hover over the device name. |
   |**Name** |The name and mobile phone number of the user relating to the device. To view details of the user, you can hover over the user's name. |
   |**Connect to gateway** |The VPN gateway to which the device is currently connected. |
   |**Network Mode** |The network mode enabled on the device. |
   |**Upstream** |The cumulative uplink traffic generated by the device. You can sort it out by ranking. |
   |**Download** |The cumulative downlink traffic generated by the device. You can sort it out by ranking. |
   |**Connection Time** |The total connection time of the device. You can sort it out by ranking. |
   |**Intranet IPv4** |The internal IPv4 address of the device. |
   |**Intranet IPv6** |The internal IPv6 address of the device. |
   |**External IP** |The public IP address of the device. |
   

4. (Optional) Search for your target online devices by using filters. Filter criteria are described in the table below.

# Managing offline devices


1. Log in to the Admin Console.

2. In the left navigation bar, click **Zero Trust Access** \> **Gateway Config** \> **Online Endpoints**.

3. In the device list, you can perform the following operations:

   * To take a single device offline, locate your target device and click **Forced** **Offline** in the **Action** column.

   * To take multiple devices offline, check the Select All checkbox in the upper left corner of the device list, and click **Batch Offline**.

      <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/f9407ce8ddde47bcb557834dbc215d8b~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

4. In the confirmation dialog box, click **offline**.

   There is a delay in disconnecting the VPN connection. It may take several minutes for the device to officially go offline.
