The Admin Console homepage serves as your central dashboard, providing a high\-level overview of your organization's core operational status and offering quick access to frequently used management tasks.

<span id="143bc7c9"></span>
# How to Access the Homepage


1. Log in to the [BytePlus SealSuite Console](https://console.byteplus.com/auth/login).

2. From the **Business Dashboard**, find the access link for the Admin Console and your administrator account details.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/03b0ca1281334bda9d5011cf8ed30bae~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

3. Use the provided link and credentials to log in.

4. Upon successful login, you will land on the Admin Console homepage.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/b87306aca71849b39935edcd97c66731~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

# Understanding the Homepage Layout

The homepage is divided into three main sections, from top to bottom: **Corporate Overview & Key Metrics**, **Quick Access**, and **Data Statistics**.

<span id="9bb8c3e7"></span>
## Corporate Overview & Key Metrics

This top section provides a snapshot of your organization's overall status.

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/a6af1af998194622839d7dce6bab2319~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>


* **Basic Company Information:**  Displays your company name, logo, and a unique **Enterprise identification code** that can be copied with a single click.

* **Key Metrics:** 

   * **Members:**  The total number of users in your organization. Click **Add** to go directly to the **Directory** page.

   * **Assets:**  The total number of managed endpoint devices. Click the number to go to the **Assets** page.

   * **Software:**  The total number of software applications added to your catalog. Click **Add** to go to the **Software** management page.

   * **Super Admin | Sub Admin:**  A breakdown of Super Admins and Sub\-Admins. Click the number to go to the **Admin Permission** page.

* **License Exp:**  Shows your service expiration date and displays a reminder as it approaches.


<span id="bd254e08"></span>
## Quick Access

This section provides direct links to five commonly used management functions to streamline your daily workflow.

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/6ffe904d08684b4ba0c0beb12f6827af~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>


* **Directory:**  Jump to the identity management module to manage your organizational structure and users.

* **Asset List:**  Go directly to the endpoint asset list to view and manage all devices.

* **Dashboard:**  Navigate to the in\-depth data analytics page to view multi\-dimensional operational charts. For details, see [Viewing the Dashboard](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/sealsuite/viewing-the-dashboard).

* **Branding:**  Access the branding configuration page to customize the appearance of the client and portals.

* **Audit Logs:**  Quickly access the log center to search for various operational and system event records.


<span id="f4c253e9"></span>
## Data Statistics

This section features two charts that dynamically display key operational metrics.

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/6d577d2cd6e043848aac8e508f23c78f~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">If you are a new user and have not yet added any members or devices, this area will display helpful links (e.g., "Add Member") to guide you through the initial setup process.</div>



* **Member:**  A donut chart showing the distribution of active versus inactive members. Click the chart to go to the **Directory** page.

* **Devices:**  A donut chart showing the count and percentage of active, dormant, and inactive devices. Click the chart to go to the **Assets** page.
