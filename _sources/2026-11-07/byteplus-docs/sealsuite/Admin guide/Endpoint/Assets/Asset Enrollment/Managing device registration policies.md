You can manage existing device registration rules in the **Device Registration** page, where you can enable or disable rules, as well as edit, copy, or delete them.

<span id="Procedure"></span>
# Procedure


1. Log in to the Admin Console.

2. In the left navigation bar, click **Endpoint** \> **Assets** \> **Enrollment**.

3. (Optional) Under the **Policy Management** tab, search for target rules by using filters.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/e1660b53d0984e7199b31ec168ed407c~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

4. Locate your target rule and then:

   * In the **Priority** column, you can adjust its priority and click **Confirm**.

      <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/aa38c73850bc4a408cb9d2155ef94c62~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

   * In the **Action** column, you can enable, disable, edit, copy, or delete it.

      For instructions on editing a rule, please refer to [Adding device registration rules](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/sealsuite/docs-adding-device-registration-rules).

      <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/b6a7464703964a4da1a7b042c2f2fd59~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

      <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="2">tip      </div>
      

      * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="2">The rule switch controls whether the rule is active for the corresponding client.      </div>
      

      * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="2">Proceed with caution when deleting rules, because they cannot be restored after being deleted.      </div>
