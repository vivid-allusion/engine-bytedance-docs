In the risk alerts list, you can view details of detected risk alerts on users’ devices across the enterprise, including alarm events, risk levels, users, IP addresses of the devices, and other information of risk detection. This allows you to detect device risks promptly and send remediation notes to specific users.

<span id="d68e1099"></span>
# Prerequisite


* You have built an organizational structure for your enterprise and imported user information via the identity management function in the Admin Console. Besides, users have logged in to SealSuite clients on their devices.

* You have enabled control rules for devices. For more information, please refer to [Managing control rules](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/sealsuite/docs-control-rules).


<span id="8be119e5"></span>
# Viewing risk alerts


1. Log in to the Admin Console.

2. In the left navigation bar, click **Endpoint** \> **Endpoint Control** \> **Endpoint Baseline**.

3. On the **Risk Alerts** tab, use the following steps to view information in the risk event list.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/0e7825d302c4445e89213dfc7af700f7~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

   1. (Optional) In the first area circled in red, set filtering criteria such as **OS**, **Risk Level**, **Type**, **Remediation Date**, and **Rule Name**.

      If no filtering conditions are set, the event list displays risk events for all devices by default.

   2. In the second area circled in red, view risk events on the device, including the information of **Type**, **Events**, **Risk Level**, **Name**, **Status**, **Rule**, **Device IP**, **Device**, **Report Time**, and **OS Model**.
