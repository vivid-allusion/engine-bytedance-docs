When you have added categories and subcategories for frequently asked questions and provided specific solutions to these questions in the Admin Portal, users can view the relevant questions on their SealSuite clients through the help center function. The following figure shows an example of the help center of the SealSuite client. The areas are divided into the help center:


* As shown in area ① below, questions that are specially marked in the SealSuite Admin Portal will be displayed on the **Common problems** tab. For information about how to set top frequently asked questions, please refer to [Adding and Managing Help Center Articles](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/sealsuite/docs-adding-specific-problems).

* The area ② below shows the categories of the questions, and the area ③ shows the subcategories under each category. For information about how to add categories and subcategories, please refer to [Adding categories and subcategories for frequently asked questions](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/sealsuite/docs-adding-categories-and-subcategories).

* The area ④ below displays a redirect URL for solutions to specific questions. For information about how to add specific questions, please refer to [Adding and Managing Help Center Articles](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/sealsuite/docs-adding-specific-problems).

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">The question must be published on the client in the Admin Console before it can be displayed in the client. For specific instructions on how to publish questions, please refer to <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/sealsuite/docs-adding-specific-problems">Adding and Managing Help Center Articles</a>.</div>
