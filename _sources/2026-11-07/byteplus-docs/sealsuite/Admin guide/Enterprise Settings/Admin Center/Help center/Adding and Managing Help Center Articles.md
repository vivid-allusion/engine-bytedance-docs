Once you have created categories and subcategories for your Help Center, you can begin populating them with articles that provide solutions to common problems.

<span id="2dd4294b"></span>
# Prerequisite

You must have already created at least one category and subcategory. For instructions, see [Adding Categories and Subcategories](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/sealsuite/docs-adding-categories-and-subcategories#c26c4dcc).

<span id="c6b9fadf"></span>
# Step 1: Adding questions


1. Log in to the Admin Portal.

2. In the left navigation bar, click **IT Application** \> **Help Center**.

3. On the **Help Center** page, locate the specific question category and click **Expand** on the right side of the category card.

4. Locate the specific subcategory and click on the subcategory name.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/81d8b5b6128743dda7603a012b4ed42c~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

5. On the **Edit question** page, click **New problem**.

6. In the upper right corner of the **Edit question** page, click **Edit**.

7. In the **English** tab of the **Edit question**, specify the question and its solution in area ② as shown in the below screenshot (you can add the corresponding Chinese solution on the **Chinese** tab if necessary), and click **Save**.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/1a585dc631ff4309a4fed301c15f80d7~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>


<span id="a4e88c99"></span>
# Step 2: Publishing questions to the SealSuite client

After adding specific questions, you also need to publish them on the SealSuite client. The specific operations are as follows:


1. Log in to the Admin Portal.

2. In the left navigation bar, click **IT Application** \> **Help Center**.

3. On the **Help Center** page, locate the specific question category and click **Expand** on the right side of the category card.

4. Locate the specific subcategory and click the subcategory name.

5. Locate the question that needs to be published and publish it to the SealSuite client through any of the following methods.

   * In the area ① shown below, select the check box to the left of the specific question name and click **Publish** to publish the question to the SealSuite client.

   * In the area ② shown below, select the **Select All** check box and click **Publish** to publish all the questions under the current subcategory to the SealSuite client.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">Uncheck the check box and the question is not displayed in the SealSuite client. If you uncheck the check boxes for all questions under the current category, the current category is not displayed in the SealSuite client.   </div>

# **Adding a New Article**


1. Log in to the Admin Console.

2. From the left navigation bar, go to **Enterprise Settings** \> **Admin Center** \> **Help Center**.

3. Find the category where you want to add an article.

4. Within that category, find the appropriate subcategory and click **Actions** \> **Add Article**.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/662d8cb832cd4c5c88a64b75e641600c~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

5. On the **Add Article** page:

   1. Enter the question or problem in the **Title** field.

   2. Provide the solution or answer in the **Content** field.

   3. (Optional) Click **Add Language** to provide a version of the article in another language.

   4. Click **Save and Publish**.


<span id="91a563dc"></span>
# **Managing Existing Articles**


1. Log in to the Admin Console.

2. From the left navigation bar, go to **Enterprise Settings** \> **Admin Center** \> **Help Center**.

3. Navigate to the category and subcategory containing the article you want to manage.

4. Click the  **...**  (more options) icon to the right of the article's title. From here, you can:

   * **Edit:**  Modify the article's content.

   * **Move to...:**  Relocate the article to a different subcategory.

   * **Move to Drafts:**  Unpublish the article and save it as a draft.

   * **Delete:**  Permanently remove the article.

# **Setting Up Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)** 

You can feature high\-frequency questions in a dedicated FAQ section to make them easier for employees to find.


1. Log in to the Admin Console.

2. From the left navigation bar, go to **Enterprise Settings** \> **Admin Center** \> **Help Center**.

3. On the right side of the page, click **Common Questions**.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/d8aa805f2b77489fbc05c2415508c9a7~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

4. On the **Common Questions** page, click **Add** to select existing articles to feature.

5. Click **Confirm** to save your FAQ list.
