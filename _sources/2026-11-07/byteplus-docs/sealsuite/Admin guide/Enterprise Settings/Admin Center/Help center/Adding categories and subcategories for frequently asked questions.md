The Help Center feature allows you to build a knowledge base of solutions to common problems directly within the SealSuite client. By providing self\-service resources, you can empower employees to resolve issues quickly on their own.

<span id="c26c4dcc"></span>
# **Adding Categories and Subcategories**

Categories are the foundation of your Help Center's structure.


1. Log in to the Admin Console.

2. From the left navigation bar, go to **Enterprise Settings** \> **Admin Center** \> **Help Center**.

3. On the **Help Center** page, click **Add Category** in the lower left corner

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/2a096b53277f4f25abcc565868c7105f~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

4. In the **Add Category** dialog, enter a **Category Name**, then click **OK**.

5. In the newly created category card, click **Actions** \> **Add Sub\-Category**.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/dbd10843ed0242259ec389766edf9c3b~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

6. In the **Add Sub\-Category** dialog, provide a **Name** and an **Icon**, then click **OK**.

   Once your categories and subcategories are in place, you can start adding articles. For instructions, see [Adding and Managing Help Center Articles](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=%2F4q5lwbix%2F158960).

# **Editing or Deleting Categories and Subcategories**

**Editing or Deleting a Category**


1. Log in to the Admin Console.

2. From the left navigation bar, go to **Enterprise Settings** \> **Admin Center** \> **Help Center**.

3. You can edit or delete a category in two ways:

   * **From the left panel:**  In the list of published categories, find the one you want to edit, click the  **...**  (more options) icon, and select **Edit** or **Delete**.

   * **From the main content area:**  Find the category card, click the **Actions** button on the right, and select **Edit** or **Delete**.


**Editing or Deleting a Subcategory**


1. Log in to the Admin Console.

2. From the left navigation bar, go to **Enterprise Settings** \> **Admin Center** \> **Help Center**.

3. You can edit or delete a subcategory in two ways:

   * **From the left panel:**  Find the subcategory, click the  **...**  (more options) icon, and select **Edit** or **Delete**.

   * **From the main content area:**  Find the subcategory, click the **Actions** button on the right, and select **Edit** or **Delete**.
