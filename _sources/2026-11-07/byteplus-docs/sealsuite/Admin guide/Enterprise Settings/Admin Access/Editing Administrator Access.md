This guide explains how to modify and remove administrator permissions and management scopes in the Admin Console. Please ensure you understand the impact of these actions before proceeding.

<span id="0dd48846"></span>
# Prerequisite


* You must be logged in with a SealSuite administrator account.

* Your account must have permission to manage other administrators.


<span id="86cd5bdc"></span>
# Managing an Administrator's Role Assignment

This section explains how to edit or remove an administrator's assignment to a specific role.


1. Log in to the Admin Console.

2. From the left navigation bar, go to **Enterprise Settings** \> **Admin Access**.

3. Click the **Admin Role** tab.

4. Find the role you want to manage and click **View** in its row.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/2389670afda64e7bae594264ba5fe9df~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

5. On the role details page, find the employee whose permissions you want to change, and then click **Edit** or **Remove**.

   * **Edit:**  In the **Update Administrator** dialog, you can change the employee's assigned **Admin Scope** and the **Expiration** of their role.

   * **Remove:**  This will unassign the role from the employee, revoking their permissions associated with it.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">The default system\-generated administrators cannot be edited or removed.   </div>
   

<span id="3482d66d"></span>
# Editing a Management Scope


1. Log in to the Admin Console.

2. From the left navigation bar, go to **Enterprise Settings** \> **Admin Access**.

3. Click the **Admin Scope** tab.

4. Find the scope you want to modify and click **Edit** in its row.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">Built\-in scopes cannot be edited.   </div>
   

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/a36f137ac1af42b5ac0d5afa0745d0d0~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

5. On the **Edit Unit** page, make your desired changes and click **Finish**.


<span id="f8078b23"></span>
# Deleting a Management Scope


1. Log in to the Admin Console.

2. From the left navigation bar, go to **Enterprise Settings** \> **Admin Access**.

3. Click the **Admin Scope** tab.

4. Find the scope you want to delete and click **Delete** in its row.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">Built\-in scopes cannot be deleted.   </div>
   

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/439cddfeb3f048629b07575903d9b9ca~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

5. In the confirmation dialog, click **Delete**.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">warning   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-wrapper-indent="1">Deleting a management scope will invalidate any administrator role policies that are associated with it. This action cannot be undone.   </div>
