While using the SealSuite system settings module, if you encounter any issues or have questions, refer to the following frequently asked questions and its provided solution for assistance.

<span id="0265bf56"></span>
## Does the SealSuite SaaS version support synchronizing logs to other vendors' devices or internal enterprise log analysis platforms?

No, it does not.

<span id="af0a4416"></span>
## How to modify the enterprise information (icon and enterprise name) in the department structure list?

Edit the basic information of the enterprise in the **System settings** \> **System settings** section of the SealSuite Admin Portal.
