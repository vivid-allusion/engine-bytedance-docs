## Prerequisites

* You have completed real\-name authentication for your account.

* Your account's available balance is not less than USD 0.

## **Billing rules**

The table below lists the supported products and billing methods for Cloud Data Transfer (CDT) cross\-region traffic. Cross\-region traffic is categorized into two types:

* Non\-cross\-border:​ Traffic between regions within China Mainland, or between regions outside China Mainland.

* Cross\-border:​ Traffic between a region within China Mainland​ and a region outside China Mainland.

|**Cloud product** |Metering method |Settlement cycle |
|---|---|---|
|Transit Router |Pay\-by\-outbound\-traffic |* Both the billing cycle and settlement cycle are based on calendar hours.<br><br>* Fees are settled at the top of every calendar hour (UTC+8). A bill is generated and fees are deducted accordingly.​ For bill details, see [Bill management](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-bill-details). |

## Resource pricing

The table below details the cross\-region traffic prices. For the exact fee, see the actual order placed in the console.

|Region of outbound/inbound traffic |China Mainland  **(USD/GB)**  |Asia Pacific  **(USD/GB)**  |
|---|---|---|
|China Mainland |0.072 |0.800 |
|Asia Pacific |0.800 |0.080 |

## Billing example

On June 1, 2026, Mr. Z enables the Transit Router (TR) service for connectivity between the China Mainland and region outside the China Mainland. By June 30, 2026, 500 GB of cross\-region traffic is used. The price is 0.800 USD/GB for traffic between China Mainland and region outside the China Mainland.

**The fee payable by Mr. Z is 500 x 0.800 = 400.000 USD.​**
