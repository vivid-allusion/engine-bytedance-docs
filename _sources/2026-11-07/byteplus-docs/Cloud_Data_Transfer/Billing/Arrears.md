This topic describes the impact of arrears on Cloud Data Transfer (CDT).

An account in arrears may cause pay\-as\-you\-go resources to be suspended or even released. The platform has set a grace period for resources. Within the grace period, the resources are retained and you can continue using the resources after you recharge your account and clear overdue payments. No impact on the features of resources. After the grace period expires, the resources are forcibly reclaimed and related data cannot be recovered. Regardless of whether your instances are suspended or released, you shall settle the issued bills based on the actual usage.

For the status of cloud resource and billing after arrears, see the respective cloud service billing documentation.

> **tip**

> The specific suspension time is subject to the actual situation. BytePlus platform will send SMS/email/internal messages based on [your settings](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/message_center/Basic-Message-Receiving-Configuration) to remind you at each stage, including resource enters arrears, suspension due to arrears, and resource release due to arrears.
