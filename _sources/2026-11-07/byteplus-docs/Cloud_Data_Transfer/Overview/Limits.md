## Usage limits

* Once a cloud product is enabled for Cloud Data Transfer (CDT) billing, all existing and newly created pay\-by\-data\-transfer instances for that product will be switched to unified billing and invoicing through CDT.

* After a cloud product is enabled for CDT billing, you cannot switch back​ to the original billing and invoicing method.

* Actions such as upgrading/downgrading configurations or adjusting bandwidth limits for cloud products must be performed through the respective product's own console.

## Billing limits

* The free tier​ and tiered cumulative pricing​ currently only apply to traffic in the public network BGP (multi\-ISP)​ scenario.

* The billing cycle for tiered cumulative pricing​ is a calendar month. Traffic usage resets to zero and starts accumulating anew at the beginning of each calendar month.
