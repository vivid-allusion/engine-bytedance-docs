## Region

A region refers to a distinct geographic area where physical data centers are located. Networks are fully isolated between regions, ensuring maximum stability and fault tolerance. Regions are typically divided by the city where the physical data center is located.

## Region list

The currently available regions are listed in the following table. More regions will be available for your use in the near future.

> **warning**

> Product features may vary across regions. See the console for the features actually supported.
