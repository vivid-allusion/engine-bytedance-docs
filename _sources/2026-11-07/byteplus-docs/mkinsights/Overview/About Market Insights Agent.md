1. ### Product positioning <span id="vhsLMOO8"></span>


&nbsp;

**We’re not a traditional monitoring platform — we’re a dedicated decision intelligence agent built for enterprises.** 


* BytePlus Market Insights Agent is a decision agent designed for five key scenarios, completing the first 99 steps before a business decision is made.

* **Core value**: It streamlines data collection, information verification, multidimensional analysis, preliminary evaluation, and draft recommendation creation across the full workflow. Work that once took experts 80% of their time is compressed into minutes, so decision\-makers can focus on the final judgment that truly requires human expertise.
