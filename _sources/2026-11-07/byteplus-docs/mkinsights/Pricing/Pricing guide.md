2. ## Renewal and service expiration <span id="mB0Q5SrB"></span>


<span id="pmB7KLwv"></span>
### Renewal instructions

To ensure uninterrupted service, please renew your subscription before it expires.


* Renewal method: Renewals are currently supported manually through the BytePlus billing center.

* Benefits update: After renewal, a new subscription cycle begins, and your Monitoring tasks, AI evaluation count, and AI report credits will be refreshed. Unused benefits from the previous cycle will not roll over.


<span id="JDWnZoo4"></span>
### Service expiration and shutdown

<span id="GTCgF3Vz"></span>
#### Stage 1: Service suspended upon expiration


* Time: Within 12 hours after service expiration

* Status: Platform features will be temporarily unavailable, but your historical data will be retained. You may restore service at any time by renewing.


<span id="T4BUfSpj"></span>
#### Stage 2: Data recycled after expiration


* Time: 30 days (720 hours) after service expiration

* Status: All your data, including tasks and reports, will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

3. ## Cancellation and refund policy <span id="FhYF8I4N"></span>


Important notice: This service supports cancellation within 7 days without reason. After 7 days, self\-service cancellation or refunds are not supported.


&nbsp;

4. ## FAQ <span id="9NybTpAl"></span>

1. ### What happens to my service and data if I forget to renew? <span id="yui8JGhz"></span>


Your service will be suspended 12 hours after expiration. You will still have a 30\-day grace period to renew and restore service. If you do not renew within 30 days, your account and all data will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.


2. ### What if the reports or tasks included in the Standard plan are not enough? <span id="9VRUjk6U"></span>


The current plan includes a fixed quota allocation. If your usage exceeds the included scope, please contact your account manager for a tailored plan.


3. ### Can I stop using the service midway and apply for a refund? <span id="yUa2qymr"></span>


No. According to the service terms, Market Insights Agent is a prepaid subscription service. Once the purchase has been active for more than 7 days, refunds or cancellations in any form are not supported.
