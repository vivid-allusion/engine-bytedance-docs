&nbsp;

This Service Level Agreement for the Market Insights Agent (the “Agreement”) sets out the service availability metrics and compensation mechanism under which BytePlus provides the Market Insights Agent service to customers.


&nbsp;

1. Definitions

   1. Service Cycle: One Service Cycle shall mean one calendar month. Any period of less than one full calendar month shall be calculated as one calendar month.

   2. Total Time of a Service Cycle: The total number of days in the Service Cycle × 24 (hours) × 60 (minutes).

   3. Service Unavailability per Unit Time: Statistics shall be calculated in 5\-minute units. If all consecutive attempts by the customer to establish a connection to the Market Insights Agent service during any such 5\-minute period fail, the service shall be deemed unavailable for that 5\-minute period. Within one Service Cycle, the number of unavailable service minutes = the total number of 5\-minute periods during which the service is unavailable within the Service Cycle × 5.

   4. Monthly Service Fee: The total service fee paid by the customer for the Market Insights Agent service during one calendar month. If the customer has made a one\-time payment covering multiple months of service fees, the Monthly Service Fee shall be apportioned on a monthly basis according to the number of months purchased.

&nbsp;

2. Service Availability

   1. Method of Calculating Service Availability


The service availability of the Market Insights Agent shall be calculated for each Service Cycle as follows:

Service Availability = (Total Service Minutes in the Service Cycle \- Service Unavailable Minutes) / Total Service Minutes in the Service Cycle × 100%


&nbsp;

3. Service Availability Commitment


The service availability commitment provided by BytePlus for this service shall be no less than 99.00%. If the service fails to meet the foregoing service availability commitment, the customer may claim compensation in accordance with Article 3 of this Agreement.


&nbsp;

4. Exclusions


The duration of service unavailability caused by any of the following circumstances shall not be counted toward service unavailability time / failed requests:

1. System maintenance carried out by BytePlus after prior notice has been given to the customer, including cutover, repair, upgrade, and simulated fault drills;

2. Any network or equipment failure, or configuration adjustment, occurring outside BytePlus\-owned equipment;

3. Any cyberattack against the customer’s application program;

4. Any loss or leakage of data, passcodes, passwords, or similar information caused by the customer’s improper maintenance or improper confidentiality management;

5. Any negligence of the customer or any operation authorized by the customer;

6. The customer’s failure to follow BytePlus product documentation or usage recommendations;

7. Force majeure events and unforeseen incidents;

8. Requests originating from IP addresses outside mainland China.


&nbsp;

5. Compensation Plan

   1. Compensation Standards


Compensation shall be issued in the form of vouchers. Such vouchers are non\-cashable, non\-transferable, and may not be gifted, and may only be used by you to purchase the Market Insights Agent service through the BytePlus official website. No invoice will be issued for any vouchers granted. Such compensation shall constitute our full and sole compensation to you for any failure of the service to meet the service availability commitment, and in any event, the total amount of compensation shall not exceed the customer’s actual consumption for the month in which the service availability commitment was not met.


|Service Availability |Compensation |
|---|---|
|Less than or equal to 99.00% and greater than or equal to 95.00% |25% of the monthly service fee |
|Less than 95.00% |50% of the monthly service fee |



&nbsp;

6. Time Limit for Compensation Claims


The customer may submit a compensation claim after the fifth working day of each month with respect to services in the previous month that did not achieve the committed availability level. **Any compensation claim must be submitted within two (2) months after the end of the relevant month in which the service delivered failed to meet the availability commitment. Any compensation claim submitted after the foregoing deadline shall not be accepted.** 


&nbsp;

7. **Miscellaneous**

   BytePlus reserves the right to amend this Service Level Agreement. If any provision of this Service Level Agreement is amended, BytePlus will provide you with at least 30 days’ prior notice by means of [website announcement or on\-site message/email]. If you do not agree to the amendments made by BytePlus to this Service Level Agreement, you shall have the right to discontinue use of the service. If you continue to use the service, such continued use shall be deemed your acceptance of the amended Service Level Agreement.
