The message center is a service platform between Byteplus and users. It allows you to easily and timely receive and manage various notification messages from Byteplus products and services, avoiding any inconvenience caused by missing information.

In the message center, you can subscribe to message types and notification methods on demand, as well as manage message recipients and chatbots.
