The message center provides the IAM identity receiving capability. In conventional message receiving, messages are received in the account dimension. While in IAM identity receiving, messages are received in the identity dimension in certain scenarios.

The channels for the IAM identity receiving include emails and chatbots. emails are managed by IAM. In addition to basic methods, the message center allows you to configure a chatbot to receive messages with an identity.

For more information about business services that support IAM identity receiving, contact the business personnel.
