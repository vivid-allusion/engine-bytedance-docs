You can enter Recipient management according to the following procedure:


1. Log in to the Byetplus [console](https://console.byteplus.com/auth/login?redirectURI=%2Fhome).

2. Click the message center icon in the top navigation bar to preview platform messages and then click View more or Message receiving management to enter Message center.

3. Click Recipient management in the left navigation pane.
