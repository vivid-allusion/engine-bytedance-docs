# Overview of Canvas

**Lumina Canvas ** is a brand\-new AI professional video creation Workplace, featuring **visualization, controllability and iterability**. It breaks the templated limitation of traditional one\-click generation, and visually breaks down the whole process of script, storyboard, shooting, audio and video creation. Beginners can quickly produce videos with AI, while creators can fine\-tune details and customize workflows to easily and efficiently create creative short videos, advertisements, short dramas and film clips, enabling AI creation to deliver free and professional content output.

**Key Highlights**

* **Agent Efficient Collaboration: ** The Canvas Collaboration Agent is an intelligent collaboration assistant for creation, design, content production, and visual expression.

   * With the canvas as the core workspace, it can understand users' natural language commands and assist in completing tasks such as content generation, creatives editing, structure organization, visual typesetting and multimodal creation.

   * Users can describe their goals, provide creatives, or specify revision directions to the agent just as they would converse with a creative partner. Based on the current canvas content and the user's intent, the agent will assist users in completing the entire creative workflow from ideation, structuring, and adjustment to result verification, lowering the barrier to using complex creative tools and boosting content production efficiency.

* **Infinite Free Canvas**: It supports boundary\-free zooming and panning, allows free arrangement of shots and creatives, and is equipped with efficient management capabilities such as grouping, alignment, layering and reuse.

* **Visual node creation**: The entire creation process is independently controllable, with a clear and transparent workflow that supports traceability, iteration and reuse, making the creation logic completely self\-evident

* **End\-to\-end AI empowerment**: intelligently completes storyboard generation, image optimization, style unification, dubbing and scoring, significantly lowering the threshold for video creation

* **Refined Content Creation Control**: Equipped with rich controllable editing functions for images, videos and audios, it allows you to freely adjust shots, control the plot cadence, ensure the consistency between characters and scenes, and get rid of finished videos produced via rigid templates.

* **Efficient large\-scale output**: The creative workflow is savable and reusable, suitable for both individual creative creation and mass content production of teams.

2. #### Layer Separation

Brief Introduction: Seedream 5.0 Pro's Layer Decomposition feature supports automatically breaking down an input image into multiple independent and editable layers. After selecting the image on the canvas and clicking Layer Decomposition, the model will automatically identify and separate the foreground entity, background, and visual elements according to the image semantics, automatically name them, sort them based on occlusion relationships, and supplement the occluded areas.

Enter the layer editor, where each layer can be shown/hidden, locked, and precisely adjusted in terms of position, size, rotation, flip, alignment, corner radius, opacity and blend mode; you can also add new image or text layers. After editing, you can either add a single layer back to the canvas individually or download it, or export the entire group for derivative content scenarios such as subject matting, background replacement, copy modification, local order picking and deletion, eliminating the need for manual matting and retouching, and turning AI\-generated images from "a single finished image" into "a set of reusable and finely adjustable layer assets".

You do not need to export layers to the canvas; you can directly reference the layers and full\-image assets of the layer editing node under a new generation node for reference\-based generation.

3. ## Canvas Collaboration Agent: What is a Canvas Collaboration Agent?

The canvas collaboration Agent is an Intelligent Creation assistant built into the Lumina intelligent canvas. Taking the canvas as its workspace, it understands your natural language instructions, and based on the existing creatives and key marketing moments on the canvas, assists you in completing tasks including script planning, character and scene setting, storyboard design, image and video generation, task tracking and result verification.
