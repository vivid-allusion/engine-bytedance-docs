## Lumina Agent

### Agent Overview

Lumina Agent is an AI Director for video creation in Lumina. It offers four modes: Music Video, Explainer Video, UGC Video, and Product Video Ads. Select a mode, add your materials, and describe your topic and visual direction. Agent plans the content and generates video results without requiring you to build a complex workflow.

Music Video creates visuals around music, characters, and a visual description. Explainer Video turns a topic into an outline, scene scripts, and video clips. UGC Video presents person\-led stories and product usage, while Product Video Ads focuses on product displays and selling points.

### Key Highlights

* **A simple starting point:**  Begin with music, a topic, or a description to move from an idea to a video.

* **Music\-driven visuals:**  Combine music, character references, scenes, and mood for music promotion, stage visuals, and branded music videos.

* **Topic\-led explainers:**  Generate an outline, characters, props, scenes, scripts, and clips for educational content, product introductions, and feature explanations.

* **Marketing and e\-commerce content:**  Use UGC Video for person\-led product experiences and Product Video Ads for product displays, benefits, and usage scenes.

* **Flexible settings and previews:**  Choose music, characters, and visual style for Music Video. Configure aspect ratio, duration, style, and language for Explainer Video, leaving uncertain options on auto.

### Core Features

#### Music Video

Create an MV from an existing song or audio asset. Agent plans visuals around the music, main character, and video description.

* **Select or upload music:**  Click the Music card and browse All / Public / Private, or upload a track through Add New Music.

* **Select or add a character:**  Type @ to open the subject library. Browse All / Public / Private and filter by Gender, Age, or Keyword Search. Use Add New Subject to add a character.

* **Describe the visual direction:**  Specify the subject, mood, scenes, colors, and visual style, then set the aspect ratio and style.

* **Review plans, storyboards, and clips: Check the story plan and assets before generating storyboards. Once all storyboards are complete, select a model and generate videos, then review scenes and the full video for character consistency, visual style, scene continuity, and music alignment.**

#### Explainer Video

Create educational videos, product introductions, feature explanations, concept explainers, and lightweight ads. Enter a topic, set aspect ratio, duration, style, and language, and optionally attach references. Agent organizes the explanation before generating scene scripts and clips.

* **Define the topic and audience:**  Describe what to explain, who it is for, and the key points.

* **Configure output:**  Set aspect ratio, duration, style, and language. Leave uncertain options on auto.

* **Add references:**  Upload character, product, or brand materials as visual and content references.

* **Plan and generate scenes: Review the story plan, characters, scenes, and scene scripts before generating and checking storyboards. Once all scene storyboards are complete, select a model and generate video clips.**

* **Preview and review: Check the explanation, visual style, character consistency, and narration, then download or save the reviewed result.**

#### UGC Video

Upload a personal photo and product image, and describe the selling points to create an AI UGC promotional video. Person\-led storytelling and usage scenes support social product introductions.

* **Character and product references:**  Add your photo and product image.

* **Selling points:**  Describe the product benefits you want the video to highlight.

* **Review the result:**  Check the character, product features, narration, usage scenes, and overall style.

#### Product Video Ads

Create product\-focused promotional videos for new product introductions, selling\-point demonstrations, and brand recommendations.

* **Keep the product central:**  Focus attention on the product and why viewers should care about it.

* **Describe benefits and scenes:**  Organize the display around selling points and usage scenarios.

* **Check product fidelity:**  Review structure, colors, logos, and details, including unwanted extra products or mismatches with the reference.

### Reviewing and Refining Results

* **Music Video:**  Check visual consistency, character identity, music alignment, and scene continuity. Refine the input description and regenerate when needed.

* **Explainer Video:**  Check the outline and scene scripts against the topic, then review the clips. Adjust the topic and style requirements if the content drifts.

* **UGC Video:**  Check the character identity, product appearance, spoken content, and usage scenario. Make sure the selling points match the provided information. If the result differs from expectations, add clear character and product reference images and specify the speaking tone, selling points, and scene requirements.

* **Product Video Ads:**  Check the product color, shape, logos, structure, and details. Make sure the selling points are shown accurately and no unintended products appear in the frame. If the result differs from expectations, add product reference images and specify the features to preserve, viewing angles, and elements to exclude.

### Quick Start

#### 1. Choose a creation mode

Open [Lumina Agent](https://ai.byteplus.com/lumina/en/agent) and select Music Video, Explainer Video, UGC Video, or Product Video Ads according to your goal.

 **[Figure 8 | Agent entry and creation modes]**

![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20261019122311-1416987338420.png)

#### 2. Add materials and describe your video

Provide the materials for your chosen mode and describe what you want to create:

* **Music Video:**  Click Music to select or upload audio. Type @ to choose a character, then describe the style, scenes, and mood.

* **Explainer Video:**  Enter the topic, target audience, and key points. Upload reference attachments if needed.

* **UGC Video:**  Upload a character photo and product image, then enter the selling points.

* **Product Video Ads:**  Upload product references and describe the selling points and display direction.

 **[Figure 9 | Materials and creation input]**

Add materials and describe your video in the input area, then configure the settings below it.

![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20261019122322-954221645315.png)

#### 3. Configure video settings

Set the available parameters below the input area. Music Video offers aspect ratio and style. For Explainer Video, UGC Video, and Product Video Ads, configure aspect ratio, duration, style, and language as shown in the selected mode. For Explainer Video, leave uncertain options on auto.

#### 4. Generate and preview

Review your materials, description, and settings, then click the generation button on the right of the input area. Open the result to preview the video when generation finishes.

Music Video offers storyboard scenes and clip previews. Explainer Video offers an outline, scene scripts, and video clips. For UGC Video and Product Video Ads, preview the finished video and check the character, product, and selling points.
