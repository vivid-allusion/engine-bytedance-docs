### Campaign Terms & Conditions

&nbsp;

Campaign Period: 2026/07/05\-2026/07/31 UTC


* Campaign Content: For active orders, 35% of the base credits of the subscription will be given as a one\-time bonus. 35% of the base credits of the monthly subscription will be given, and 35% of the monthly base credits of the annual subscription will be given.



&nbsp;

* Constraints:

   * Scope

      * Subscription orders

         * 0.99 is not eligible for bonus credits

         * Team plan, other annual and monthly subscriptions are all eligible

      * Credit orders

         * Not eligible

   * Bonus credit

      * Bonus credit will only be granted once during the entire campaign period

   * Behavior: Active order placement 

      * Including:

         * First purchase by new users

         * Upgrade of existing users with active subscription

         * Existing users without an active subscription and place an order

      * Excluding: 

         * Automatic renewal for existing users with active subscription
