#### **Credit Validity & Rules:** 


* **Seedance 2.5 Exclusive Credits:**  

1. Valid for **1 month** after issuance for each order. 

2. Can only be used in **AI Video generation** using Seedance 2.5 model. (**not eligible** in canvas/canvas pro)

3. Only credit packs may be stacked upon purchase and shall remain valid in accordance with the original standard rules.
