# Requirements


1. An enterprise account with enterprise verification is required.

2. For Team‑plan subscriptions, the main account needs to finish KYC verification. Then, each sub‑account may adjust its own real‑person facial and moderation policy settings independently.

# Step 1 — Information Confirmation (Compliance Risk Self\-Assessment)

**Page title:**  Compliance Risk Assessment

<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260904025210-490864854316.png) </span>

&nbsp;

The page uses Yes/No radio questions to have the enterprise customer self\-attest to its compliance posture. There are 3 questions in total:


| **#**  |**Question summary** |
|---|---|
|01 |Has your company established **adequate content\-control policies and emergency response mechanisms** to ensure that use of this service and dissemination of generated content comply with applicable laws and regulations, and that inappropriate content can be promptly addressed? |
|02 |Does your company **fully understand** that uploading and using personal portraits requires **legal authorization** from the individual portrayed, and that without such authorization your company assumes all legal responsibility? |
|03 |Is your company **aware** that in scenarios potentially involving deep synthesis / deepfake / content that may mislead the audience, measures such as **watermarks, identifiers, and risk warnings** provided by this service or owned by your company must be used to reasonably alert the public? |


All three questions must be answered Yes to continue.

# Step 2 — Read the Authorization Agreement

**Page title:**  Compliance Commitment

<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260904025223-965166524687.png) </span>

&nbsp;

The preamble states that the customer is **voluntarily requesting, based on legitimate business needs, to disable certain content\-moderation mechanisms** in order to more accurately meet its intended business purposes, and on that basis makes the following 7 commitments:


1. The customer has read and agreed to the General Terms for AI Services / Terms of Service / Customer Agreement / Acceptable Use Policy and applicable laws and regulations.

2. The customer will use the services and any generated content solely for **lawful and compliant** purposes, and not for any illegal, infringing, harmful, or otherwise unauthorized purposes.

3. All input content and materials submitted by the customer are **lawful and properly authorized**, and do not infringe any third party's lawful rights and interests.

4. The customer shall **independently review** all generated content before use or dissemination, ensuring compliance with applicable laws, platform rules, and third\-party rights.

5. The customer expressly acknowledges that **disabling certain content\-moderation mechanisms** increases the risk of unlawful or harmful content, and shall **bear full responsibility** for any consequences arising therefrom.

6. For any complaint, claim, dispute, penalty, investigation, or legal proceeding arising therefrom, the customer shall bear all corresponding legal liabilities and **indemnify** the affected parties for losses.

7. Throughout the use of the services, the customer shall **continuously comply** with applicable laws, regulations, industry standards, and platform rules, and shall properly **retain** authorization, review, and compliance records.

# Step 3 — Security Verification (Email Verification)

**Page title:**  Email Verification

<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260904025243-933416595006.png) </span>

&nbsp;

Enter the email address; below it are the verification code input box and the Send code button.

&nbsp;

After the user enters the verification code received by email, clicking Complete verification at the bottom right finishes the entire KYC flow.

&nbsp;

<span id="dZzjP3oB"></span>
# Step 4 — Control Real‑Human Face and Moderation Policies

After completing KYC verification, click your profile avatar to self‑configure real‑human face settings and moderation policies under the KYC\-only Controls section.

(If you have purchased the Team plan, real‑human face settings and moderation policies can be controlled at the sub‑account level.)

Seedance real‑human upload：When enabled, support real‑person uploads 

Baseline content moderation: When enabled, only baseline‑level moderation will be applied
