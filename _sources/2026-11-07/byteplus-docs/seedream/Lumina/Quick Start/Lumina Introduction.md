# 1. Product Introduction

<span id="c89eAO5d"></span>
## Product Overview

The all\-in\-one AI productivity tool of choice for enterprises and creators, aggregating high\-quality global MultiModal Machine Learning models such as video, image, audio, and digital humans, as well as professional workflow orchestration tools, to provide users with one\-stop, customizable, and high\-efficiency AI content production capabilities from inspiration to content delivery. 

Lumina not only provides basic AIGC capabilities such as image/video, but also offers a rich array of AI applications and visual creative canvases, supporting the free orchestration of multi\-model and multi\-tool workflows to achieve batch and standardized creation in complex business scenarios, and fundamentally solving the closed\-loop problem of "professional creation + mass production".

1. ## Image Creation <span id="Y630tRTx"></span>


<span id="nsKbjxod"></span>
### Function Introduction

Supports two image creation functions: text\-to\-image and image\-to\-image. The Doubao (Seed Series) large image model is not a single model but a collective term for multiple text\-to\-image/image\-to\-image models, currently including: exclusive self\-developed models and exclusive custom models by official designers, with more high\-quality models to be continuously updated in the future. 

Suggestions for the structure of text\-to\-image prompts: style positioning + subject details (characters) + environmental and dynamic elements + color elements. Style words control the content style, composition words control the layout, and content words control the output content of the image.

#### Reference Image (Optional)

Selecting a reference image can make text\-to\-image generation more closely match your expectations. Its core function is to provide AI with a clear creative "benchmark", which is specifically reflected in three aspects: 


1. Lock in core features, such as when referencing a character photo, AI can restore details like appearance and outfit to avoid Role Deviation; when referencing an illustration, it can inherit style attributes such as content style and color tone.

2. Reduce the complexity of instruction descriptions. For complex elements (such as special compositions and niche scenarios), there is no need for lengthy text. AI directly extracts key information from reference images, reducing communication costs.

3. Ensuring consistency in creation, reference images can maintain unity in character features, content style, and scene elements during multi\-image generation or series creation, avoiding a sense of disharmony. <span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260611062506-1365406251400.png) </span>

1. ## Video Creation <span id="khIlyZ8s"></span>


<span id="6n63ZB0r"></span>
### Function Introduction

Currently, the platform integrates self\-developed models from the Seedance 1.5 and 1.0 series, supporting text\-to\-video/image\-to\-video/first\-and\-last\-frame video generation, among which the 1.5 series supports Audio And Video Synchronization generation.

The Video Unified Model O1 supports multiple video creation modes, including text\-to\-video, image\-to\-video, first and last frame, subject reference, video editing, multi\-frame editing, and more. 

<span id="FIVV4jDe"></span>
#### Video Generation


* **Text\-to\-Video**: Generate videos by inputting text, supporting custom parameters such as resolution, frame rate, and duration. It is easy to operate and suitable for quick creation scenarios such as short videos and creative clips. 

* **Image\-to\-Video**: Dynamizes static images, preserves the style and subject consistency of the original image, combines text to generate motion effects, and is suitable for product displays, poster animations, IP animations, etc.

* **First and Last Frame Video**: Fix the first and last frames, and AI automatically generates the intermediate transitions to ensure a unified and smooth picture, suitable for refined creation of product demonstrations, plot segments, brand short videos, etc.

* **Subject Reference:**  By uploading any image or text instruction, AI can automatically and accurately identify the core subject (product, person, IP, scene, etc.), support manual box selection for enhanced anchoring, ensuring that the subject remains unchanged, undistorted, and not lost during subsequent generation, editing, and camera movement processes. For scenarios such as e\-commerce products, brand materials, and portraits, it can lock the subject features with one click, ensuring that the creative content is highly consistent with the core requirements and addressing the pain points of subject blurring and style deviation in AI creation.

* **Video Editing:**  Supports importing local videos and platform\-generated videos for video editing, and supports changing video subjects, styles, effects, and other video creation features

* **Multi\-frame editing:**  Supports single\-frame individual editing and multi\-frame editing, allowing precise adjustment of the content, color, and effects of any frame, or batch modification of the transition effects of consecutive frames.<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260611063007-1196349408575.png) </span>


<span id="1Cfa8hP5"></span>
#### Inspiration and Template Reuse (Efficient Output) 


* **Same Style as High\-Quality Work Templates**: The dedicated area features a vast collection of high\-quality video work templates, covering multiple style categories such as marketing, daily life, anime, realism, and technology. It supports one\-click generation of the same style, automatically updating the model parameters, style settings, visual composition, and motion effect logic of the template. Users only need to fine\-tune the copy or creatives to quickly produce high\-quality videos of the same style, significantly reducing the cost of creative trial and error.

##### Batch Generation (Entry: Create Task within Canvas Pro)

The batch function of the workflow for the open professional printing canvas has been launched. The batch generation function is a core efficiency feature specifically designed by the platform for  ** large\-scale content production and high\-efficiency creative implementation ** . Relying on cloud computing power support and AI model optimization, it breaks through the efficiency bottleneck of single\-item generation, and supports batch content creation with single instruction and multiple versions, multiple templates and multiple creatives, and multiple parameters and multiple styles. 

**Fast** | Batch generate creative works in a short time

**Comprehensive** | Linked with the creative canvas, it can batch output the effects under different datasets and parameters of the workflow with a single click, being highly efficient and convenient

**Customizable** | Compare multiple parameters and easily obtain the optimal image generation configuration
