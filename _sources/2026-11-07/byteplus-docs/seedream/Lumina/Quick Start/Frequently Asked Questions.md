### Related to subscription

### Q1: What is a subscription?

Subscription refers to the user's purchase of access rights to a particular product or service, usually billed on a periodic basis (monthly/annually).

### Q2: How to start and cancel a subscription?

* Select a suitable subscription plan \-\> Confirm the price and duration \-\> Complete the payment process \-\> Gain corresponding permissions immediately after the subscription takes effect.

* The Personal Plan only supports subscription and use by the main account.

* **The platform defaults to automatic continuous subscription ** , automatically renewing on an annual/monthly basis. To cancel, you can unsubscribe on the top\-right page to cancel the automatic renewal.

### Q3: When credits for renewed plans will be issues? Can I change the time when auto\-renewal fee is deducted?

* Credits for renewed plans will be issued once the renewed subscription takes effect.

* By default, the auto\-renwal fee is deducted 7 days befeore current subscription expires. You may change the auto\-renewal pre\-authorization time.

> **Please note**

> Renewal settings are platform\-level global configurations. Changes will apply to the renewal policy of all products on the Byteplus platform. Please confirm before submitting.

### Q4: What happens after the subscription expires?

* Services typically continue until the end of the current cycle

* Automatically stop service after expiration

* Can choose to renew to maintain the service

### Q5: Can subscriptions be refunded?

* Subscribed plans do not support refunds. You can cancel the auto\-renewal, which will take effect after the current service expires.

### Q6: What is an upgrade?

Upgrade refers to the process by which a user upgrades from a lower\-level subscription to a higher\-level subscription, such as from Basic to Advanced. Each upgrade is equivalent to repurchasing the corresponding resource service, and the system will automatically make up the payment based on the price difference between the old and new configurations.

### Q7: When can I upgrade my configuration?

* You can apply for an upgrade at any time. It is recommended to upgrade as early as possible within the current subscription cycle to enjoy more benefits.

### Q8: What are the billing rules for upgrading?

**Current billing method: **

**Monthly plan: **  Upgrade to a monthly payment plan, valid for 1 month;  ** **

**Annual plan: **  Upgrade to an annual payment plan, valid for 1 year.  ** **

**Upgrading only supports upgrading from a monthly plan to a monthly plan and from an annual plan to an annual plan. **

* **Credits Allocation:**  After the upgrade, the credits from the original package will be cleared, and the credits for the current month of the new package will be immediately allocated.

* **Proportional conversion ** : Calculate the spread based on the remaining subscription time

* **Effective Immediately**: Obtain advanced features immediately after upgrading

* **Make up the spread ** : Only pay the spread for the upgraded part, with transparent fees clearly displayed before the upgrade

### Q9: Will auto\-renewal affect upgrades?

* **It does not affect, **  automatic renewal is controlled by yourself

*  Recommendation: Manually renew or set up automatic renewal before expiration to avoid service interruption

### Q10: If I have already renewed, can I still upgrade?

* **Yes, it can** support upgrades at any time

* **Upgrade cycle calculation:**

   * If the remaining period is less than 1 month, it will be calculated as 1 month

   * Remaining 1.5 months → Calculated as 2 months

* 💡 **Tip: ** Package changes are calculated on a full\-month cycle, and the expiration time will be updated accordingly.

### Q11: Will the original data be retained after the upgrade?

* All historical data is fully retained

* Generated content remains unaffected

* Only permissions and functional scope are expanded

### Q12: What should I do if the upgrade fails?

* Check if the payment method is valid

* Confirm that the account balance is sufficient

* Contact technical support for assistance

### Q13: Can the same account have multiple subscription plans simultaneously?

* Only one active subscription plan can be available for the same account during the same time period, but the credits add\-on package can be purchased multiple times.

### Q14: Does it support downgrading?

* Downgrading is not supported. It is recommended to adjust and resubscribe after the current cycle ends.

### Q15: What are the rules for the effectiveness of credits?

* credits in the subscription plan are distributed monthly, with the current month's credits automatically cleared and updated each month. The credits in the credits increment package are valid for 2 years and will be used upon the activation of the plan.

### Q16: What are the rules for automatic renewal?

* Accounts with auto\-renewal enabled will start attempting to deduct fees to renew the package **at 0:00, 7 days before the expiration of the current plan ** .

### Q17: Does it support free trial?

* Register and log in to receive 68 Credits, which can be used to experience basic creation features, but the canvas feature and batch generation capabilities cannot be experienced.

### Q18: How to issue an invoice?

* If you have a need for invoicing, please visit the official Byteplus website to issue invoices self\-service [https://console.byteplus.com/finance/invoice-manage](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/invoice-manage)

### Q19: Will a failed task consume credits?

* No. Pre\-deducted credits will be refunded if the task fails. You may check the details in your credits history.

### Q21: The credits displayed on the page do not match the actual credit balance, or tasks cannot be created even when credits remain available.

![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260911033809-656426562448.png)

* Lumina credits are divided into general credits and dedicated credits. The credits displayed in right corner is general credits.

* General credits can be used for all Lumina tasks.

* Dedicated credits are issued via promotions and can only be used to create Seedance 2.5 tasks under the AI Video mode.

### Q22: How is the 3\-day validity period of the trial package calculated?

It is calculated from the date of purchase, inclusive of the purchase date, for a total of 3 calendar days (UTC+8).
