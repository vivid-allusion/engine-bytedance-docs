2. ## Sub\-account Creation and Activation <span id="uHBWzOS2"></span>


After the main account owner purchases the Team Package, in addition to being able to log in to Lumina using their own account registered on Byteplus (i.e., the main account), they can also create several sub\-accounts based on the number of subscribed seats. Other team members can use the sub\-accounts to log in. The steps to create sub\-accounts are as follows: 


1. [More] in the lower left corner of the platform ➡️ [Team Management] ➡️ Sub\-account Management page, click [Import Users from IAM] to evoke the sub\-account creation page, where you can choose to import existing sub\-accounts or click [New Sub\-User] to create a new sub\-account.

Fill in the sub\-account name, relevant basic information, and remarks, select [Console access] to enable, and choose the password generation method at your own discretion. <span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260515030722-495570300287.png) </span>

If you need to grant sub\-accounts the permission to place orders and make purchases, you can enable the: billing permission policy.

3. ## Sub\-account Sign in <span id="1SY0bXDX"></span>


After accessing through the link shared by the main account or the platform's login entry, the sign in/sign up pop\-up window can be invoked. By switching to the sub\-account login entry below, users can fill in sub\-account information to log in. For some sub\-accounts that are set to require password reset upon first login during the creation process, a password reset pop\-up window will be triggered. After completing the relevant information, users can log in to the platform.

5. ## Query of credits usage <span id="NBc1063k"></span>


Both the main and sub\-accounts with team management permissions can query the trends and details of the team's overall consumption and each user's usage, support filtering by time dimension, and support exporting detailed usage data.
