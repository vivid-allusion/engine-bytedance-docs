This topic describes how to view automatically generated silence policies.

<span id="5lCJnqrG"></span>
## Background

Click the **Silence** button in the alert message sent by the Feishu application bot to automatically generate a silence policy. For best practices, see [Receiving alerts by Feishu application bot](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/vmp/Receiving_alerting_notifications_through_Lark_application_robot).

<span id="eNawAWnn"></span>
## Limits

Automatically generated silence policies are retained for up to 15 days.

<span id="SMVMiAvX"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [Observability Platform console](https://console.byteplus.com/observe/dashboard/dashboard).

2. In the left navigation pane, select "**Alert Center \-\> Silence Policy**" to go to the "Silence Policy" page.

3. Click the "**Auto\-generate policy**" tab to view silence policies generated automatically within the past 15 days.
