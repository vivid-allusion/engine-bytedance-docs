This topic describes the management operations available for silence policies.

<span id="yANRuDEI"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [Observability Platform console](https://console.byteplus.com/observe).

2. In the left navigation pane, select "**Alert Center \-\> Silence Policy**" to go to the "Silence Policy" page.

3. Click a button in the "Actions" column to the right of the target silence policy to perform the corresponding operation.


<span id="ERaFWPrS"></span>
## Management operations

The currently supported management operations are as follows:


|Actions |Description |
|---|---|
|Edit |Modify the configuration of an existing silence policy. |
|Enable/disable |Manually enable or disable the current silence policy. After being disabled, the silence policy becomes unavailable. |
|Delete |Delete a silence policy that is no longer needed.<br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div><br><br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">Deleted silence policies cannot be recovered. Proceed with caution.</div><br> |
