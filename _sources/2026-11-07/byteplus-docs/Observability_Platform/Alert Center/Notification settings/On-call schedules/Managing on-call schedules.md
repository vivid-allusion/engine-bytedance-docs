This topic describes the management operations available for on\-call schedules.

<span id="QRhPhbkX"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [Observability Platform console](https://console.byteplus.com/observe).

2. In the left navigation pane, select "**Alert Center \-\> Notification configuration**" to go to the "Notification configuration" page.

3. Select the **Duty plan** tab. Click a button in the "Actions" column to the right of the target on\-call schedule to perform the corresponding operation.


<span id="uloENbVz"></span>
## Management operations

The currently supported management operations are as follows:


|Actions |Description |
|---|---|
|Edit |Modify the configuration of an existing on\-call schedule. |
|Delete |Delete an on\-call schedule that is no longer needed.<br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div><br><br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">Deleting an on\-call schedule also removes its association with the corresponding notification policy. Deleted on\-call schedules cannot be recovered. Proceed with caution.</div><br> |
