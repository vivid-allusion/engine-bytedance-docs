This topic describes the management operations available for notification policies.

<span id="TQ29T9GY"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [Observability Platform console](https://console.byteplus.com/observe/dashboard/dashboard).

2. In the left navigation pane, select "**Alert Center \-\> Notification configuration**" to go to the "Notification configuration" page.

3. On the **Notification Policy** tab, click a button in the "Actions" column to the right of the target notification policy to perform the corresponding operation.


<span id="jXUTn8wq"></span>
## Management operations

The currently supported management operations are as follows:


|Actions |Description |
|---|---|
|Edit |Modify the information of an existing notification policy. |
|Set as default |Set the current notification policy as the default policy. |
|Delete |Delete a notification policy that is no longer needed.<br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div><br><br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">Deleting a notification policy also removes its association with the alert rule. Deleted notification policies cannot be recovered. Proceed with caution.</div><br> |
