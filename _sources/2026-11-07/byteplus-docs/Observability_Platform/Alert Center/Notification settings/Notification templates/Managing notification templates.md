This topic describes the management operations supported by notification templates.

<span id="gKS2wnOj"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [Observability Platform console](https://console.byteplus.com/observe).

2. In the left navigation pane, select "**Alert Center \-\> Notification configuration**" to go to the "Notification configuration" page.

3. On the **Notification template** tab, click a button in the "Actions" column to the right of the target notification template to perform the corresponding operation.


<span id="R7rb2twv"></span>
## Management operations

The currently supported management operations are as follows:


|Actions |Description |
|---|---|
|Edit |Modify the information of an existing notification template. |
|Delete |Delete a notification template that is no longer needed.<br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div><br><br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">Deleting a notification template will also disassociate it from notification policies, and the notification template in those policies will revert to the default notification template.</div><br> |
