After adding charts, you can group them based on your actual scenarios. This facilitates batch management of charts by group during layout adjustment and allows for categorized chart viewing. This topic describes how to use the chart grouping feature.

<span id="BxoAgmmb"></span>
## Add a group


1. Log in to the [Observability Platform console](https://console.byteplus.com/observe/dashboard/dashboard), and in the left navigation bar, select ** Custom Dashboard**.

2. On the dashboard management page, click the name of the target dashboard.

3. In the upper\-right corner of the dashboard, click the **edit** button, then select "add" and "**Add group"**  in sequence.


<span id="sNyrezBl"></span>
## Manage a group


|Action |Descriptions |
|---|---|
|Chart grouping |Charts located from the start position of a group to the next group or the bottom of the dashboard belong to that group. You can group charts by dragging them under a specific group. |
|Edit group |Click the settings icon next to the group name to modify the group name. |
|Delete group |Click the delete icon next to the group name to delete groups that are no longer needed.<br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div><br><br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">You can choose to delete only the group or to delete both the group and all charts within it. Deleted groups and charts cannot be recovered. Proceed with caution.</div><br> |
