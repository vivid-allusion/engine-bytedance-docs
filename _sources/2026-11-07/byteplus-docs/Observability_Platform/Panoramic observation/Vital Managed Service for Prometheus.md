Vital Managed Service for Prometheus (VMP) is a standard Prometheus service hosted in the cloud. It fully inherits from and integrates with the open\-source Prometheus ecosystem. You can quickly create standard Prometheus instances. These instances provide a solution for storing monitoring metrics that is both highly available and O&M\-free. This solution serves AI products, cloud infrastructure, cloud\-native products, and cloud\-deployed business applications. In addition, VMP offers standard read/write endpoints such as RemoteWrite, Query, and Pushgateway to meet the requirements for writing and consuming monitoring data. For detailed descriptions and guides, see [VMP usage guide](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/vmp/what-is-vital-managed-service-for-prometheus).
