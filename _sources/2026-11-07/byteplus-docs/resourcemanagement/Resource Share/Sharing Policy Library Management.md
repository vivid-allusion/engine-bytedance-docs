Each shareable resource type contains at least one sharing policy that defines the actions that a shared user is allowed to perform on the resources in a shared unit.

> Currently, RS only support system managed policy, and custom managed sharing policy is not supported.


<span id="viewing-shared-policy-library"></span>
## Viewing shared policy library


1. Log in to Resource management and go to [Resource sharing > Sharing policy library](https://console.byteplus.com/resourcemanager/region:resource-manager+ap-southeast-1/resource_share/premission) in the left navigation bar.

2. You can view the shared units associated with a policy in the policy library.

> Sharing policies are globally configurable, and the region selected in the top navigation bar determines which associated shared units are displayed.


3. Click Details in the Actions column of a specified policy.

4. View the policy information in the visual text or JSON text format.

5. On the Reference record tab, view the shared units associated with the policy. You can also click Details in the Actions column of a specified shared unit to view their details.
