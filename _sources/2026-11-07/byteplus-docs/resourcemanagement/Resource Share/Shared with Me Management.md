Shared users can manage the units shared with them and related resources. The management process includes accepting/rejecting sharing invitations and viewing details of the resources shared with the users.

<span id="accepting-share-invitation"></span>
## Accepting share invitation


1. Log in to Resource management and go to [Resource sharing > Shared with me](https://console.byteplus.com/resourcemanager/region:resource-manager+ap-southeast-1/resource_share/shared) in the left navigation bar.

2. Select the region with the relevant shared unit in the top menu bar.

3. Click Accept in the Actions column of a specified shared unit.

4. After accepting the share invitation, shared users can access the resources in the shared unit. Any resources subsequently created in the shared unit are shared by default.

> When the sharing with organization feature is enabled, accounts in the relevant organization or organizational unit automatically accept share invitations by default.


<span id="rejecting-share-invitation"></span>
## Rejecting share invitation


1. Log in to Resource management and go to [Resource sharing > Shared with me](https://console.byteplus.com/resourcemanager/region:resource-manager+ap-southeast-1/resource_share/shared) in the left navigation bar.

2. Select the region with the relevant shared unit in the top menu bar.

3. Click Reject in the Actions column of a specified shared unit.

4. After rejecting the share invitation, users can no longer access the resources under the shared unit.

> The effective period of a share invitation is 14 days. It is rejected by default after the expiration date.


<span id="viewing-details-of-resources-shared-with-me"></span>
## Viewing details of resources shared with me


1. Log in to Resource management and go to [Resource sharing > Shared with me](https://console.byteplus.com/resourcemanager/region:resource-manager+ap-southeast-1/resource_share/shared) in the left navigation bar.

2. Select the region with the relevant shared unit in the top menu bar.

3. After accepting a share invitation, click Details in the Actions column of a specified shared unit.

4. You can view the policy details associated with a resource and click the resource to go to the relevant product console.
