After the quota template is enabled, multiple quota change applications will be automatically submitted at a time for new members in the same enterprise organization, enhancing the efficiency and automation of quota management across the organization.

> Note the prerequisites for using the quota template:


* Ensure that you have activated the enterprise organization service.

* Use the administrator account of the enterprise organization for management.


<span id="enabling-quota-template"></span>
## **Enabling quota template**


1. Log in to [Quota center](https://console.byteplus.com/quota/productList).

2. Click Quota template in the left navigation pane.

3. On the Quota template page, click Enable quota template.


<span id="disabling-quota-template"></span>
## **Disabling quota template**

When the quota template is enabled, you can click Disable quota template. After disabling, no quota change application will be automatically submitted for the new members.
