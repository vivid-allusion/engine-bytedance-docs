<span id="procedure"></span>
# Procedure


1. Log in to [Quota center](https://console.byteplus.com/quota/productList).

2. Click Alert strategy in the left navigation pane.

3. You can filter target quota alerts by product name and quota parameter in the Alert strategy page.

4. You can also view the relevant alert strategies in the product list by clicking Alert item in the Actions column of the quota item.
