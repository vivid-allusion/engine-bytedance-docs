You can query the alert history of a quota item.

<span id="procedure"></span>
## **Procedure**


1. Log in to [Quota center](https://console.byteplus.com/quota/productList).

2. Click Alert history in the left navigation pane.

3. You can filter target quota alerts by product name and quota parameter, and customize the alert interval for inquiry.
