You can delete the specified quota alert.

<span id="procedure"></span>
## **Procedure**


1. Log in to [Quota center](https://console.byteplus.com/quota/productList).

2. Click Alert strategy in the left navigation pane.

3. Select the alert configured for the quota item and click Delete in the Actions column.

4. Click OK to delete the alert.
