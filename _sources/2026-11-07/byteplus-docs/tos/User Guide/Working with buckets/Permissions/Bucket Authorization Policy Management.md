A bucket policy grants users access to a bucket and its resources.

<span id="bb6e8784"></span>
## **Scenarios**

Use a bucket policy if you need to:


* Authorize users of another account or anonymous users.

* Set authorization conditions.

* Differentiate permission conditions for users or resources.
