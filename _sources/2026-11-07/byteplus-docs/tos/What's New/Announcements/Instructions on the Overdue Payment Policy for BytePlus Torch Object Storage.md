Thank you for using the BytePlus TOS. In order to prevent service interruption in case of accidental overdue payment on your account, we have not implemented the policy of suspending services for overdue accounts.

To ensure the stability of the BytePlus TOS and enhance the service experience, BytePlus TOS plans to standardize the overdue payment policy on April 15, 2025. That is, starting from 10:00 on April 15, 2025, access to the TOS service for overdue accounts will be stopped, and the data of overdue accounts will be deleted at 10:00 (inclusive) on April 15.

If your account has an overdue balance and you wish to continue using the TOS service, please make sure to pay your overdue bill before April 15. After payment, log in the TOS Console and agree to the TOS Service Agreement to resume normal use.

If you have any questions regarding this notice, please feel free to contact us in a timely manner.

## Detailed Instructions

You can check your bill and payment methods in the following ways:


* **Check the bill**: Log in the BytePlus Console and view information such as the account overview. For detailed information, please refer to the [Billing Overview](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-overview-2.).

* **Pay the bill**: Log in the BytePlus Console, recharge and pay the bill. For detailed information, please refer to the [Paying bills](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-paying-bills.).
