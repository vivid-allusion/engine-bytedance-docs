To provide you with more stable, high\-performance services, we will upgrade the architecture of BytePlus Torch Object Storage in phases starting August 29, 2025.

<span id="4DAtQYzK"></span>
## Estimated Upgrade Time

**The first phase will take place from 00:00 to 02:00 on August 29, 2025.**  Please check your console inbox for information about future upgrades.

<span id="Q23BrObu"></span>
## Upgrade Impact

The following impacts are expected during the upgrade: 


* **Affected operations ** : Creating and deleting buckets. 

* **Unaffected operations**: All other operations, including uploading, downloading, deleting, and listing objects in your buckets. Your business access will not be interrupted.


The upgrade process is expected to take about two hours. We will try to finish within the scheduled time frame. If the upgrade is completed earlier than expected, no further notice will be given. 

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">This architectural upgrade only targets BytePlus Torch Object Storage. It does not affect Volcano Engine Torch Object Storage.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">We recommend planning your operations in advance. Avoid creating or deleting buckets during this period.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">If you have any questions, submit a ticket or contact your Customer Manager for support. </div>
