To ensure your continuous, stable, and secure use of the Torch Object Storage service, in accordance with relevant regulations and platform requirements, you are required to complete your account profile and payment verification.

<span id="435e0a01"></span>
### Account Profile Notice

The complete account should include the following information:


* Account Profile：

   * Country/Region

   * Address

   * Phone Number

* Payment Verification

   * Credit Card/Credit limit


You must complete the correct account profile and payment verification before April 15, 2025. Failure to do so by the due date may result in your inability to use the TOS service normally, and your resources may be deleted. Even if you supplement the account profile after April 15, 2025, the resources that were previously deleted cannot be restored.

If you wish to continue using the TOS service after this date, you can re\-activate the TOS service after completing the account profile

For the specific operations, please refer to [Sign Up and Verify BytePlus account](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-signing-up-your-account).
