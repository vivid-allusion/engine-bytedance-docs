To provide you with more high\-quality services, BytePlus Torch Object Storage product is expected to optimize its billing function in March 2025, offering more flexible billing forms. This billing change will not affect your business operations and billing. If you have any questions about this notice, please contact us in a timely manner.

<span id="89670580"></span>
## Estimated Change Time

From March 3, 2025, ~ March 28, 2025

<span id="5f186b88"></span>
## Change Details

After the change is completed, the following changes will occur on your **Order** and **Bill details**.

<span id="b3975f65"></span>
### Order

TOS will generate a new purchase order and N cancellation orders (to be executed later, not during this change period). You can view the order details on the **Order** page. The cancellation operation will not affect your business or billing, and no separate notice will be given when canceling. Both the **Creation time** and the **Update time** will be the time of the change.

<span id="f1fda7ec"></span>
### Bill details

After the change, TOS will generate a new instance with the format of `TOS<random number>`, and all subsequent billing will be based on this newly generated instance.


* If you rely on the **instance ID / name** parameter in your business, it is recommended that you make early modifications according to the change situation to avoid affecting the operation of your business after the change.

* If you need to use the region field for operations such as cost allocation, the region field is already included in the bill and will not affect your business.


<span id="485a9467"></span>
### Usage period

At the first billing time point after the change ends, an abnormal consumption time may occur, that is, the **usage period** is not an integer time point. However, the actual consumption will still be calculated based on 1\-hour increments. You can ignore this abnormal time point as it will not affect your business or billing.
