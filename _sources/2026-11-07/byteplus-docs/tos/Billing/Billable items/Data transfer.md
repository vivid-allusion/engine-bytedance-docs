Traffic refers to the total amount of data transferred when accessing and using TOS. You pay for traffic to and from TOS. This includes outbound traffic over the public network, inbound traffic over the public network, outbound traffic over the private network, inbound traffic over the private network, and CDN back\-to\-origin traffic. This topic explains the billable items and calculation formulas for data transfer.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">This topic only provides a description of billable items. For specific prices, see <a href="https://www.byteplus.com/en/pricing?product=TOS&tab=1">TOS pricing</a>.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">The traffic is calculated in decimal units, for example, 1 GB is equal to 1000 MB.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Accessing TOS will not incur data transfer charges for requests that return 4xx (client errors) or 5xx (server errors) status codes.</div>




|**Billable Item** |**Description** |**Formula** |
|---|---|---|
|Outbound traffic over the public network |Data transfer out from TOS to internet clients. |**Pay\-as\-you\-go**: Data transfer cost = Outbound traffic volume over the public network (GB) × Price per GB |
|Inbound traffic over the public network |Data transfer in from internet clients to TOS. |Free of charge |
|Outbound traffic over the private network |Data transfer out from TOS to BytePlus clients. |Free of charge |
|Inbound traffic over the private network |Data transfer in from BytePlus clients to TOS. |Free of charge |
|CDN back\-to\-origin traffic |The downstream traffic generated by back\-to\-origin from BytePlus CDN to TOS. |**Pay\-as\-you\-go**: Data transfer cost = CDN back\-to\-origin traffic volume (GB) × Price per GB |
