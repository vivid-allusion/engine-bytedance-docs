You pay to accelerate data transfers. This topic explains how you are charged for transfer acceleration.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Fees are only charged when the transfer acceleration feature is used. Enabling the feature without using it will not incur any charges. This topic only provides a description of billable items. For specific prices, see <a href="https://www.byteplus.com/en/pricing?product=TOS&tab=1">TOS pricing details</a>.</div>



|**Transfer direction** |**Billable item** |**Description** |**Formula** |
|---|---|---|---|
|Within Chinese mainland |Transfer acceleration (Transfer within Chinese mainland \- Inbound) |Accelerated data uploads from public networks to TOS buckets in the Chinese Mainland. |**Pay\-as\-you\-go**: Transfer acceleration cost = Accelerated traffic volume (GB) × Price per GB |
||Transfer acceleration (Transfer within Chinese mainland \- Outbound) |Accelerated data downloads from TOS buckets to public networks in the Chinese mainland. ||
|From other locations to Chinese mainland |Transfer acceleration (Transfer from other locations to Chinese mainland \- Inbound) |Accelerated data uploads from public networks in other locations to TOS buckets in the Chinese mainland. ||
||Transfer acceleration (Transfer from other locations to Chinese mainland \- Outbound) |Accelerated data downloads from TOS buckets in the Chinese mainland to public networks in other locations. ||
|From Chinese mainland to other locations |Transfer acceleration (Transfer from Chinese mainland to other locations \- Inbound) |Accelerated data uploads from public networks in the Chinese mainland to TOS buckets in other locations. ||
||Transfer acceleration (Transfer from Chinese mainland to other locations \- Outbound) |Accelerated data downloads from TOS buckets in other locations to public networks in the Chinese mainland. ||
|Across other locations |Transfer acceleration (Transfer across other locations \- Inbound) |Accelerated data uploads from public networks to TOS buckets across other locations. ||
||Transfer acceleration (Transfer across other locations \- Outbound) |Accelerated data downloads from TOS buckets to public networks across other locations. ||
