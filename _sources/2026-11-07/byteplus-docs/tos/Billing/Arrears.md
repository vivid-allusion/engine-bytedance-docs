TOS generates bills per hour and deducts fees accordingly in real time. If the available balance in your account (including account balance and coupons) is less than the payable amount, your account will be considered overdue. You will then receive SMS or email reminders about insufficient balance.

<span id="3d8e5045"></span>
## Pay\-as\-you\-go

Please settle the overdue payment within 360 hours or apply to increase your credit control limit; otherwise, you will not be able to use the Torch Object Storage service normally.

The changes in the service status after the occurrence of arrears in TOS are as follows:


* If arrears exceed 360 hours and all overdue bills are still unpaid, BytePlus will suspend the service but retain your data, which still occupies storage space resources. Therefore, arrears will accumulate, and the accumulation method refers to the settlement method of the [Storage](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/tos/docs-storage-fee).

* If you still fail to clear the arrears within 360 hours after suspension, it will be considered that you voluntarily give up the service, and BytePlus will terminate this product's service terms, delete all data saved in Torch Object Storage, and it will be irrevocable, regardless of whether you have purchased a resource package or whether the resource package is active.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Regardless of whether your account service is suspended, you should pay the billed charges accurately.</div>
