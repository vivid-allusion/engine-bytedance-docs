A Region refers to a physical data center.  Once resources are successfully created in a specific region, they can not be relocated to another region. This document introduces the regions supported by the Container Registry (CR).

<span id="YVIwwgt8"></span>
## What is a Region?

A Region refers to the geographical location of a physical data center. Once resources are created, they can not be relocated to a different region. Internal networks of different regions are isolated from each other to ensure stability and fault tolerance across regions as much as possible.


* Geographically, select a region close to you or your target users to reduce network latency and improve access speed.

* Resource pricing in different regions may differ. This is because the infrastructure, BGP network performance, and resource operation and configuration are largely consistent across regions in the Chinese mainland.
