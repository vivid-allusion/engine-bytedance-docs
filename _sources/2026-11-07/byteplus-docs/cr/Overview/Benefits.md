BytePlus Container Registry (CR) possesses the advantages of ease of use, guaranteed security, fast distribution, and integration with cloud\-native artifacts.

<span id="简单易用"></span>
## Ease of use

The challenges of setting up and managing image registries are solved. Users can focus on their business and enjoy efficient and reliable cloud\-native services.

<span id="安全保障"></span>
## Guaranteed security

Images are stored in an encrypted manner. Network controls and multi\-dimensional image scanning capabilities protect user business on the virtual private cloud (VPC) and public network.

<span id="极致性能"></span>
## Supreme performance

BytePlus CR allows for quick downloads in the case of high image pulling concurrency. P2P acceleration boosts the fast deployment of business.

<span id="拥抱原生"></span>
## Integration with cloud\-native artifacts

Open Container Initiative (OCI) artifacts are compatible with Helm Chart V3. The managing feature is offered throughout the lifecycle of container images.
