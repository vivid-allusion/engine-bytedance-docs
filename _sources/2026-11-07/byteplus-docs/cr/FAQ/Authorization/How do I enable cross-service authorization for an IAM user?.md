---
title: "How do I enable cross-service authorization for an IAM user?"
product: "Container Registry"
product_code: "cr"
source: "https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/cr/How_to_enable_cross-service_authorization_for_IAM_users"
---

If the following error appears when I log in to CR as an IAM user, the root user has not completed cross\-service authorization. Ask the root user to log in to the [CR console](https://console.byteplus.com/cr) and follow the prompts to complete cross\-service authorization.

`User is not authorized to perform: iam: GetServiceLinkedRoleTemplate on resource:`
