This document introduces the billing methods for instances.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">Normal Billing: Standard Edition or Micro Edition instance is billed normally during regular operation, as well as in abnormal states or when the instance is pending for recycling.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">Stop billing: Billing stops when an instance is destroyed or the account is overdue for 2 hours or more.</div>
