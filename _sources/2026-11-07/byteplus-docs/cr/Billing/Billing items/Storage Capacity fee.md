Storage capacity cost is incurred when storing OCI (Open Container Initiative) packages, such as container images and Helm Charts, in the Container Registry (CR).

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">The storage capacity cost incurred while an account is in arrears will continue to be billed. For details on the arrear status, please refer to <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/cr/overdue-payment">Arrears Description</a>.</div>


The specific charging rules are as follows:


|Billing item |Description |Billing method |Unit Price ||
|---|---|---|---|---|
||||Chinese mainland |China (Hong Kong) and other regions |
|Standard Storage Capacity Cost |Charged based on the size and duration of the Standard Storage Capacity. |Pay\-as\-you\-go billing |USD 0.01414/GiB/month |USD 0.01944/GiB/month |
