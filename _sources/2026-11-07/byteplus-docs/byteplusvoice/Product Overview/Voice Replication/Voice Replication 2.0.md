1. # Introduction <span id="7zPCdVBF"></span>


Powered by a proprietary, cutting\-edge speech large model algorithm, Seed Speech Voice Replication 2.0 offers a streamlined approach to personalized voice creation. With just 5\-15 seconds of audio recorded in any setting, the system can instantly capture and recreate a user's unique voice profile—including tone, style, accent, and ambient acoustics.

2. # Best Practice <span id="mGduSdau"></span>


Prompt audio is the input audio for voice replication. Selecting prompt audios that contain noise, are excessively long (\>30s) or short (<14s), feature multiple speakers, have unclear vocals, heavy accents, or audio artifacts will result in suboptimal voice replication quality.


* **What is a good prompt audio**

   * **Format & Length:**  Use **WAV** files. Aim for **14–30s**. Long files are auto\-truncated, risking the inclusion of low\-quality segments.

   * **Audio Quality:**  Use **mono tracks** with minimal noise and a single speaker. Avoid stereo recordings.

   * **Pre\-processing:**  Noise reduction is optional for clarity, but use it sparingly as it can diminish voice similarity.

   * **Prosody & Emotion:**  Maintain a steady emotional state. Avoid slurred speech, stiff delivery, or dramatic shifts in volume/speed.

   * **Multilingual Support:**  For mixed Chinese\-English use cases, the prompt should ideally contain both languages.

3. # Competetiveness <span id="sL7rxdty"></span>

1. **Stronger interactive human\-likeness**


Deeply understands user queries and accurately delivers tone, intonation, pauses, and other prosodic features matched to the scenario, making interactions as natural as real human communication.


2. **Richer emotional expression**


Empathizes with user emotions, accurately conveying joy, anger, sorrow, and delight during conversations. Emotions unfold progressively in interactions, enhancing user engagement and willingness to communicate.


3. **More flexible speech control**


Follows text\-based instructions for adjusting speed, emotion, timbre, style, and other parameters, providing users with extensive customization and control options.
