1. # Introduction <span id="CxzxBWev"></span>


Seed Speech ASR 2.0 delivers highly competitive overall performance in the industry. It includes two capabilities:


* Streaming ASR

* Audio File ASR


Compared to ASR 1.0, it supports image context input and further improved recognition accuracy (coming soon). It can meet users recognition needs in multiple scenarios, such as intelligent voice interaction, audio and video understanding & analysis.

With stronger context understanding, it supports the unique feature of image context, and optimizes recognition for multilingual and far\-field scenarios, adapting to high\-precision recognition requirements in a wider range of use cases.


---




2. # Competetiveness <span id="EBvE5tkb"></span>

* **Improvement in Reasoning Capability: ** Reinforcement learning is applied to the ASR 2.0 model. It can perform accurate recognition through deep context understanding without relying on historical occurrences of target words, making speech recognition more adaptable to dynamic real\-world interaction scenarios. The overall context keyword recall rate has been improved by **20%** .

* **Multilingual Recognition: ** While maintaining high recognition accuracy for Chinese, English and domestic dialects, ASR 2.0 supports recognition in **51 languages** including Indonesian, Spanish and Portuguese.

* **Enhanced Far\-Field Recognition Rate: ** Thousands of hours of high\-quality far\-field data have been added to specifically improve recognition accuracy for hardware scenarios such as speakers, TVs and glasses. On the far\-field test set, the error rate has been reduced by approximately **50%** .

* **Multimodal Understanding & Recognition (Coming Soon): ** Upgraded speech recognition from understanding text to comprehending scenarios. During recognition, the scope of context understanding is extended from pure text to the visual domain. By understanding image and video content, the model achieves more accurate speech transcription.
