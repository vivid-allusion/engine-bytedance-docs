1. # Overview <span id="vK1zZbmN"></span>


The Dola Seed Live Interpretation model adopts an end\-to\-end architecture, performing speech recognition, semantic understanding, and translation output within a single model, delivering high\-accuracy, low\-latency real\-time interpretation.


&nbsp;


The model supports two working modes:


* **Speech\-to\-Text (S2T):**  Streams speech in and outputs the translated text in the target language.

* **Speech\-to\-Speech (S2S):**  Streams speech in, and while producing the translation, the model automatically clones the speaker's voice and delivers the target\-language speech in that speaker's original voice.



&nbsp;

2. # Competetiveness <span id="OMra5b2t"></span>

* **Zero\-shot voice cloning:**  Built on a full\-duplex speech understanding and generation framework, it requires no pre\-recorded audio of the speaker—voice sampling is completed during interpretation, and the translated speech is delivered in the cloned voice.

* **High\-quality translation:**  Maintains stable translation quality even in complex scenarios such as multi\-party conversations, mixed Chinese\-English speech, and unclear pronunciation. It distinguishes among multiple speakers, offers semantic error correction, and excels particularly at handling Chinese cultural and domain\-specific content.

* **Low\-latency synchronization:**  Achieves synchronized generation of translated text and speech along with voice cloning, with speech latency as low as 2–3 seconds, significantly enhancing the real\-time interactive experience.

* **Adaptive pacing:**  Intelligently balances translation quality, latency, and speech rhythm, dynamically adjusting the output pace based on the speaker's clarity and fluency, and maintaining a stable speech rhythm throughout long interpretation sessions.



&nbsp;

3. # Use Cases <span id="hHOdkk8q"></span>

* **International and business meetings:**  Provides real\-time subtitles and simultaneous interpretation for cross\-language meetings, eliminating language barriers and boosting collaboration efficiency—applicable to scenarios such as meeting subtitles and AI\-powered live interpretation.

* **Phone calls and smart assistants:**  Integrates into mobile assistants or real\-time calling features to provide live interpretation for cross\-language calls.

* **Smart hardware:**  Can be embedded into hardware devices such as earbuds, smart glasses, and translation devices, supporting cross\-language communication in scenarios like cross\-border work and travel.

* **Online education:**  Delivers real\-time translated subtitles within educational apps or learning devices, helping users overcome language barriers and expanding the accessibility of learning content.
