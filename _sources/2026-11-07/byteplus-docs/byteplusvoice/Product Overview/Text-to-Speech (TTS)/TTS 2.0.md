1. # Overview <span id="awS2hcFp"></span>


TTS 2.0 presents **Query\-Response ** mechanism that brings the next generation of speech technologies. It can synchronously understand both Query and Response texts, greatly expanding the scope of contextual comprehension and significantly enhancing the anthropomorphism and emotional expression in conversations. In addition, it enables flexible regulation of tone, emotion and speech speed via simple commands, delivering more emotionally vivid speech experiences for diverse scenarios such as **AI assistants**, **AI companion**, **call center** and **video dubbing**.

TTS 2.0 has several exclusive features compared to TTS 1.0:


1. **Text Prompt**


You can enter the text prompt to control attributes including **emotion** (sad/angry), **tone** (coquettish/intimate/quarrelsome/high\-pitched cute voice), **speech rate**, and **pitch** of the output speech content.


2. **Emotion Tag**


You can enter emotion tag to control sentence level emotion.


3. **Context Understand**


You can enter the context of the synthesized text, and the model will understand it and continue the emotional tone of the context to perform synthesis.

2. # Competetiveness <span id="fIz40oof"></span>


Based on the conversational TTS paradigm, TTS 2.0 focuses on improvements in human\-likeness, emotional expression, and instruction control, enabling users to achieve superior vocal emotional performance when integrating TTS.


1. **Stronger interactive human\-likeness**


Deeply understands user queries and accurately delivers tone, intonation, pauses, and other prosodic features matched to the scenario, making interactions as natural as real human communication.


2. **Richer emotional expression**


Empathizes with user emotions, accurately conveying joy, anger, sorrow, and delight during conversations. Emotions unfold progressively in interactions, enhancing user engagement and willingness to communicate.


3. **More flexible speech control**


Follows text\-based instructions for adjusting speed, emotion, timbre, style, and other parameters, providing users with extensive customization and control options.


4. **Improved accuracy for complex formulas**


Through targeted data training and model optimization for educational scenarios, the average accuracy rate for reading complex formulas and symbols across all primary to high school subjects (mathematics, English, chemistry, physics, geography, biology) reaches approximately **90%** .
