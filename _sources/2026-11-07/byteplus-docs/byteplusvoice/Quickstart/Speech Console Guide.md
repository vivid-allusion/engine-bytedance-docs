# New Console Guide


&nbsp;

1. ## Account Registration <span id="FtevkedJ"></span>


Unregistered users can refer to BytePlus account registration page for details [Sign-up](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-signing-up-your-account). After registration, please make sure to **add credit card info** to your BytePlus account before activating Seed Speech service.

Click [Seed Speech Console](https://console.byteplus.com/voice/new/overview?projectName=default) to enter our console page.


&nbsp;

2. ## Feature Overview <span id="pGmtcvrj"></span>


In this section, you can enable either trial services or official services (including obtaining API keys and sample code).


* Enable free trial services


Click '**Activate**' to enable the trial service.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_44bb2fdd05a9637deb0d146363401d18.png) </span>


* Go to playground


Click '**Playground**' to go to our playground.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_3567c1885a5a0d131a9175fc34dab055.png) </span>


3. ## API Access <span id="2myTXhUn"></span>


<span id="55x5JNpe"></span>
### Step 1: Activate Service

Click 'Service Activation' to activate services.

<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260625125714-1572419486170.png) </span>

<span id="UNKqxv8Y"></span>
### Step 2: Sample Code

Please select the right model name when obtaining sample code. After this step, you can copy API keys and sample code and apply to your project.

<span>![图片](https://sf-bpcms.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/20260625125809-684437336276.png) </span>

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_4b2bcc88299f49174720e438c099f2ea.png) </span>

If you have any further questions regarding integration, you can click '**Completed Calling Guide**' to check details.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_cf3e6f92b1c317f26f13afe23f5c2a4f.png) </span>

<span id="9Ea3yczS"></span>
### Step 3: Management

Click '**API Keys**' to create, disable and delete API keys.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_a4f9cec316b2e2faca7c9b136d50e890.png) </span>


&nbsp;

4. ## Playground Experience <span id="KVJPdlcl"></span>


Find '**Playground**' on the left and click the corresponding feature to have online experience.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_d534cc4f55f9df42121d35f8f0dcc3a1.png) </span>

<span id="nk2Y0wk2"></span>
### Text\-to\-Speech

Click '**model**' to select TTS 1.0 (more Chinese and English voices) or TTS 2.0 (more languages support, text prompt & context understanding support)

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_15d887d935a26a323bc147859cda53a3.png) </span>

Click **dual\-arrow key** to select voices from our voice library.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_35af3b90463db971e3172b0fc7cd3ccf.png) </span>

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_852bbe0a3cca9314062e46c018a0026a.png) </span>

For some multi\-lingual voices, you can click **language button** to select the language.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_7a67089813e0defbe64d29b726ca6e77.png) </span>

Enter text to convert into speech or use the default sample text.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_a7a7fc28095425eaadfe3b7e475a89ce.png) </span>

Click '**Text prompt**' to enter content to control the emotion, dialect, tone and speed of the output speech.

Click '**Reference Context**' to help the model understand context and learn the emotional expression.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_c7e52a7a8d17942781100e91043a692b.png) </span>

After entering text, text prompt or reference context, click '**synthesize audition**' to generate the speech.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_25614aeddb72fc974b3246745fc8558a.png) </span>

<span id="AgQqy7dP"></span>
### Speech\-to\-Text

Click '**Upload Audio**' to upload the audio file.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_0aeeac0413727a98252a1a2097a442fe.png) </span>

Click to select, drag or paste the audio file here.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_1cf183f146c5ee1809804448e0689ceb.png) </span>

After uploading the audio file, click '**Start Recognition**'.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_26ccc7256ed5fcf6e34e227023fd3dbb.png) </span>

Check the recognized result here.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_660648bc228e2480b9cd4b15fcd941ac.png) </span>

# Old Console Guide


&nbsp;

1. ## Account Registration & Log\-in <span id="jtlFu9Dv"></span>

Unregistered users can refer to BytePlus account registration page for details [Sign-up](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-signing-up-your-account). After registration, please make sure to **add credit card info** to your BytePlus account before activating Seed Speech service.

Click [Seed Speech Console](https://console.byteplus.com/voice/app) to enter our console page.


&nbsp;

2. ## Activate Service <span id="aMlpLNA8"></span>


<span id="kJ5CDAM4"></span>
### Step 1: Create Application

Click "Create Application" to add a new application, enter app name & description and select service.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_194d77069a186db11629877e5d7a7264.png) </span><span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_79820ba3d94999d84c4cbbb8ea734dc6.png) </span><span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_d176f42bffb9a9722a776a60891cd9eb.png) </span>**Please note:** 


* After confirmation, a trial app will be generated and you need to activate the service within the trial app (see step 2) to have the authentication information.

* A maximum of 10 applications can be generated within 1 account.


<span id="w21RZ7hH"></span>
### Step 2: Trial Use

Go to API Service Center and select corresponding service, and click "**Trial**" to activate trial usage.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_21e2946585d1e75c425772758541f4cc.png) </span><span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_564ecff240c2fbffd39c79efb347bfa7.png) </span>After clicking "**OK**", the free trial usage will be added to your account for test use. The free trial usage will be 20,000 characters and 10 QPS.

You can collect your **App ID**, **Access** **Token** and **Secret Key** for authentication.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_552e348f8f0d7b13abd86c86dbe41d08.png) </span>

<span id="Vekvcj7w"></span>
### Step 3: Activate Service (with Free Trial Usage)

After the trial, and click "**Activate**" to activate official usage.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_0e2d8f595d8abbb28bbae6e14f4fb471.png) </span><span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_53cd435ad3533a2c1e927e5b1a5bde03.png) </span>

<span id="9Kf7izVR"></span>
### Step 4: Official Use

You can start integration with App ID, Access Token, Secret Key & free trial usage. If you want to have further usage, you can purchase our pre\-paid package or pay\-as\-you\-go.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_ed6f0cfe863e89138473b4785bcb13a5.png) </span>Select the corresponding package based on your usage expectation and accept our service terms.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_f4f81ae8f8de2a984786b8c53d540eb5.png) </span><span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_b99edb3a608d6a1ef6d00384091ea07e.png) </span><span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_c9b49ab52139c2135c767edd22a9d796.png) </span>You can refer to [payment methods](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-managing-payment-methods) for detailed payment info.

After the payment is done, you can find the corresponding usage in your account.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_90066d1c5d52766108700dd8d066b72d.png) </span>
