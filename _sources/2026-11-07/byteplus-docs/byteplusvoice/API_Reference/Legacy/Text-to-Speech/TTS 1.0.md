1. # Overview <span id="DyCeG3ys"></span>


Text\-to\-Speech (TTS) is a service that converts text into high\-quality, natural\-sounding speech by intelligently analyzing context and emotion. It offers features like bidirectional streaming to balance synthesis quality and latency, and provides instant access to a diverse range of professional\-grade voices. This allows you to avoid the expense and hassle of sourcing voice talent for your applications.


* **Natural Speech Synthesis**: Advanced TTS model intelligently analyzes context and emotion to deliver high\-quality, natural\-sounding speech

* **Bidirectional Streaming**: Balances synthesis quality and latency by enabling batch request processing and real\-time data exchange

* **Reduce Costs & Hassle**: Avoid the expense and challenge of sourcing voice talent; Seed Speech provides instant access to diverse, professional\-grade voices.
