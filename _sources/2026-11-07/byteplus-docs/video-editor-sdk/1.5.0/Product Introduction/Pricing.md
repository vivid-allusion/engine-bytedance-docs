The BytePlus Video Editor SDK is sold as a comprehensive package, providing access to all features with a single purchase. If certain features are deemed unnecessary for your Mobile App, the SDK allows easy configuration to disable those specific features within your App.

To obtain the listed prices, kindly contact your BytePlus Videosales representatives. The license for BytePlus Video Editor SDK operates on an annual subscription basis. Upon purchase, you will receive an SDK license file valid for 1 year, covering both iOS and Android platforms.

BytePlus offers paid customization services on top of the standard SDK or video editor assets. These services include UI adjustments, platform adaptations, version maintenance, and asset designs, among others. These customization options go beyond the standard feature offerings of the BytePlus Video Editor SDK.

Customized SDK or assets enable customers to integrate BytePlus technology into their products, enhancing business processes and improving user experience. For detailed pricing information on customization services, please contact BytePlus sales representatives and discuss your customization requirements with us. We will work with you to provide tailored solutions that meet your specific needs.
