The BytePlus Video Editor SDK camera feature focuses on capturing high\-quality video footage with precision and clarity.

These are the following camera module features available:


* Basic camera features

* Video recording features

* Aspect ratio & resolution adjust

* Music

* Media selection

* Filter

* Beauty (Only available with the purchase of BytePlus Effects)

* Effects Sticker (Only available with the purchase of BytePlus Effects)

# Basic camera features

The basic camera features include the following:

<span id="8b2232f2"></span>
## Photo taking

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/de34de026a0c4e96989ec109ec9aff68~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

The **Photo** mode allows users to take photos. After taking a photo, the user is directed to the preview editor page to edit the photo.

<span id="6c707717"></span>
## **Zoom**

&nbsp;

Users can leverage the zoom feature to adjust the focal length of the camera, allowing them to capture subjects from varying distances. This enhances flexibility in framing and composition during video recording.

<span id="d19652ca"></span>
## Flip

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/cc91f9c58ea74cdc9002d7d260387272~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

The flip feature enables users to switch between the front and rear cameras seamlessly. This functionality is useful for capturing different perspectives, facilitating both selfie\-style and outward\-facing video recording within the app.

<span id="5d876f55"></span>
## Flash

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/3f6301d31e2e487393fe198e2266734a~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

The flash feature provides users with the option to control the camera flash settings. They can choose to enable or disable the flash based on the lighting conditions, ensuring optimal visibility and image quality during video recording.

# Aspect ratio & resolution adjust

Aspect Ratio & Resolution Adjust in BytePlus Video Editor SDK allows developers to configure these settings programmatically. It's important to note that App users cannot independently adjust the aspect ratio and resolution through the UI button. Instead, developers, who are customers of BytePlus, have the capability to configure these video parameters according to their specific requirements. This approach ensures that the adjustment of aspect ratio and resolution remains under the control of developers, allowing for a more controlled and tailored video editing experience within the app.

# Media selection

App users have the option to choose images or videos from their mobile albums during the content creation process. This feature enables users to incorporate pre\-existing media from their device's gallery, providing them with a convenient and personalized way to enhance their videos. Whether it's adding images for a slideshow effect or integrating specific video clips, this functionality adds flexibility and creative freedom to the video editing process within the app.
