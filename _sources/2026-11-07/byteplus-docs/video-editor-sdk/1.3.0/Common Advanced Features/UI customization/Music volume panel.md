This topic guides you through the process of customizing the music volume panel of your user interface (UI). Learn how to change these essential elements to meet the specific visual and functional requirements of your application.

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/1b7d7a78758c4908bd502859cad5d0c2~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>


|**No.**  |**Description** |**Changeable Item** |**Change Method** |
|---|---|---|---|
|1 |Back button |Icon |* Replace the icon with another icon of the same name: `eo_base_back`.<br><br>* **Recommended size (pixels)** : 48 × 48 |
|2 |Text for original sound |* Text<br><br>* Text color |* Change the text.<br><br>   * **Text key:**  `eo_base_musicvoice_original_sound`<br><br>* Change the color.<br><br>   * **Color key**: `TextSecondary` |
|3 |Value of the volume on the seekbar |Color |Change the color.<br><br><br>* **Color key**: `TextSecondary` |
|4 |Selected part of the seekbar |Color |Change the color.<br><br><br>* **Color key**: `Primary` |
|5 |Unselected part of the seekbar |Color |Change the color.<br><br><br>* **Color key**: `BGInput3` |
|6 |Text for music |Text<br><br>Text color |* Change the text.<br><br>   * **Text key**: `eo_base_musicvoice_music`<br><br>* Change the color.<br><br>   * **Color key**: `TextSecondary` |
|7 |Slider |Color of the slider |Change the color.<br><br><br>* **Color key**: `TextPrimary` |
|8 |Background |Color |Change the color.<br><br><br>* **Color key**: `BGPrimary2` |
