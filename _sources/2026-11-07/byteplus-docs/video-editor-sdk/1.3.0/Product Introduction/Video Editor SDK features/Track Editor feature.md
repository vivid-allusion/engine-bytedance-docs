The BytePlus Video Editor SDK Track Editor feature enables the fine\-tuning of track arrangement and synchronization, offering precise control over the video's timeline and composition. Upon entering the Preview Editor Module, users can initiate advanced video editing actions by clicking the **Edit** button, leading them to the Track Editor. This isolated module provides users with a dedicated space to perform intricate and advanced video editing tasks, allowing for a more comprehensive and detailed editing experience.

# Transition

<img src="https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/6cc07883c46f4fb7b51680210eea5960~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image" width="200px" /></div>


<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/b6825b5e4cd240398b912847628a9706~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

Users can enhance the visual continuity of split video segments by applying transition effects within the Track Editor module. The duration of these transition effects is adjustable, allowing users to control the smoothness and style of the transitions between different segments of the video. This feature provides users with a seamless and polished transition experience, adding a professional touch to their edited videos.
