Video has become the predominant content format globally, with short\-form video content constituting 40% of all created content and experiencing rapid growth. Mobile app users actively seek more engaging forms of content that allow for creation and sharing. The willingness of app users to switch platforms is evident if they do not find the desired features and experiences. Users are on the lookout for tools to enhance their video creation, add a personal touch, and express their creativity uniquely. This highlights the increasing demand for versatile and creative content creation features in mobile applications.

Integrating with the BytePlus Video Editor SDK significantly shortens the development period for creating an in\-app video editor. With this SDK, developers can complete the integration process within 2\-3 weeks, as opposed to forming a development team of 10 people and working on the project for over 6 months.

BytePlus Video Editor SDK empowers the creation of an in\-app video User\-Generated Content (UGC) module, enhancing App profitability. Users can seamlessly generate and share short\-form videos within the App, eliminating the need to exit for the entire video creation process. This fosters increased user engagement and, consequently, boosts the App's profitability. The SDK provides a feature\-rich video editor, enabling users to craft compelling, shareable content with popular creative effects and editing features from major social platforms, seamlessly integrated into the App.
