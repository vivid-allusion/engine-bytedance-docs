This topic describes the costs of changing node specifications and unsubscribing from a Cloud Search instance.

<span id="变配费用说明"></span>
## **Costs of changing node specifications**


* Pay\-as\-you\-go: During the current settlement cycle in which the specification change occurs, instance fees are settled at the price before the change. Starting from the next settlement cycle after the change, the instance fees are settled at the price corresponding to the new specifications.

* Subscription: The following table describes the formula for calculating the costs of upgrading the specifications of a subscription instance. The actual payable amount is subject to that displayed on the upgrade page.



|**Change Type** |**Cost** |
|---|---|
|Immediate upgrade |* Order cost for upgrading specifications = Discounted price of the new specifications \- Remaining value of the instance<br><br>* Discounted price of the new specifications = Unit price of the new specifications per month × [Subscription duration after upgrade ÷ (365 ÷ 12)] × Applicable discount (corresponding to the subscription duration after upgrade)<br><br>* Remaining value of the instance = Order amount paid for the original specifications \- Unit price per month at the time of purchase × [Days of use ÷ (365 ÷ 12)] × Applicable discount |


<span id="退订费用说明"></span>
## **Costs of unsubscription**

Unsubscribing from an instance means deleting the instance.


* When you delete a pay\-as\-you\-go instance, no refund is involved.

* When you delete a subscription instance, refunds are handled according to the unsubscription and refund rules.
