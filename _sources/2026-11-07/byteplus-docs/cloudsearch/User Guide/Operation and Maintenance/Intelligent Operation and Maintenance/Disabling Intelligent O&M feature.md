You can disable health diagnosis when you no longer need it. This topic describes how to disable health diagnosis, i.e. Intelligent O&M.

<span id="9d77bbe5"></span>
### Precautions

Health diagnosis feature only retains the data of historical reports within 30 days by default. Once it is disabled, new diagnosis reports are not generated afterwards, and historical diagnosis reports are retained temporarily, and will be cleared 30 days after the featured is disabled. Please exercise caution in disabling health diagnosis feature.

If you re\-enable health diagnosis feature, and the previous disabling time does not exceed 30 days, the historical diagnosis reports can be viewed again.

<span id="d38145f0"></span>
### Procedure


1. Log into [BytePlus Cloud Search console](https://console.byteplus.com/es).

2. In the top menu bar, select the **Project** and **Region** where the desired instance resides.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/c11209b6da4c40859894dea23a18eaf7~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

3. On the **Instances** page, click the name of the desired instance.

4. On the left\-side navigation bar of **Instance details** page, select **Intelligent operation**  **\> Health overview**.

5. In the upper\-right corner of **Health overview** page, click **Close health diagnosis**.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/c3e39462fe5245469efd30d7545cb6a6~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

6. In the displayed dialog box, review prompt message and click **OK**.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/af8859cdd1ce4854ae92b4a5bb4d8dd2~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>
