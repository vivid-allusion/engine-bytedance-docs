After Intelligent O&M feature is enabled, instance diagnostics is performed automatically on a daily basis (01:00 a.m. by default), and diagnosis reports are generated afterwards. You can modify the time to perform automated diagnostics based on the actual business scenarios, normally the peak\-off hours of business workloads.

<span id="e818fb7a"></span>
### Procedure


1. Log into [BytePlus Cloud Search console](https://console.byteplus.com/es).

2. In the top menu bar, select the **Project** and **Region** where the desired instance resides.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/c11209b6da4c40859894dea23a18eaf7~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

3. On the **Instances** page, click the name of the desired instance.

4. On the left\-side navigation bar of **Instance details** page, select **Intelligent operation**  **\> Health overview**.

5. In the upper\-right corner of **Health overview** page, click the current diagnosis time, specify a new time in the dropdown list, and click **OK**.
