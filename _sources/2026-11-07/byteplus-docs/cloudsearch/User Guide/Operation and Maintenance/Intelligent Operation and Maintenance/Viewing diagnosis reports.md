This topic describes how to view health diagnosis reports and the diagnosis description, suggestion and result of check items, as well as download diagnosis reports.

<span id="b459bebf"></span>
### Prerequistes


* Health diagnostics has been performed and diagnosis reports have been generated previously.

* Health diagnostics is performed automatically at a specified time (01:00 a.m. by default) or instantly by manual trigger. For more information, please see [Performing a diagnostic on an instance](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/cloudsearch/32009).


<span id="5c83580b"></span>
### Procedure


1. Log into [BytePlus Cloud Search console](https://console.byteplus.com/es).

2. In the top menu bar, select the **Project** and **Region** where the desired instance resides.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/c11209b6da4c40859894dea23a18eaf7~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

3. On the **Instances** page, click the name of the desired instance.

4. On the left\-side navigation bar of **Instance details** page, select **Intelligent operation**  **\> Diagnosis report**.

5. On the right\-side **Report directory** pane, click the diagnosis time of the desired report and the corresponding report displays in the left\-side **Diagnosis report** pane. Then click the `+` icon before the check item to view the diagnosis description, suggestion and result.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/03b8c9a946924698a50db2affd9a1b3c~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

6. (Optional) On the upper\-right corner of **Diagnosis report** pane, click **Download the report**.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/f7a7d7df7b534712b95016983b871ea1~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>
