The Cloud Search service has preconfigured an alarm template in the Cloud Monitoring console, and we recommend that you use the one\-click alarm function.

<span id="4ce58f30"></span>
## Precautions

By enabling the one\-click alarm, an alarm policy is configured for all instances of the Cloud Search service in all regions.

<span id="239e85fc"></span>
## Enabling one\-click alarm


1. Log on to [BytePlus Cloud Search console](https://console.byteplus.com/es).

2. In the top menu bar, select the **Project** and **Region** where the desired instance resides.

3. On the **Instances** page, click **One\-click alarm** at the top of the page.

   You can also click **One\-click alarm** at the top of the page in the **Observability \> Monitoring overview** page of an instance details page.

4. On the **Preset alarm template** page of the [Cloud Monitoring console](https://console-stable.volcadocean.com/cloud-monitor/alert/template), locate the preset alarm template for the Cloud Search service, and then click the **One\-click enable** switch at the back.

5. In the **Configure contact groups** dialog box, set the alarm channel and alarm contact group, and then click **OK**.

   
   |**Configuration** |**Description** |
   |---|---|
   |Alarm channel |Select one or more channels. Supports **Email** and **Webhook.**  |
   |Alarm contact group |When **Email** is selected for the alarm channel, the page displays the alarm contact group.<br><br><br>* At least 1 contact group must be set up as the recipient of alarm notification. To create a contact group in advance, refer to [Creating an alarm contact group](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/cloudmonitor/Editing-an-alarm-contact).<br><br>* Up to 5 contact groups must be set as recipients of alarm notification. |
   |Webhook |When **Webhook** is selected for the alarm channel, the page displays the list of webhook selections. |
   

6. (Optional) On the **Alarm Strategy** page of Cloud Monitor console, view the generated alarm policy.


<span id="e8842e0a"></span>
## More Operations

The alarm policy generated by one\-click alarm does not support manual deletion, you need to disable the preset alarm template, and the corresponding alarm policy is deleted after disablement.

To disable a preset alarm template, refer to [Disabling a preconfigured alarm template](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/cloudmonitor/Disabling-a-preconfigured-alarm-template).
