To enhance the stability of instances, the back\-end system performs maintenance operations on instances from time to time. The default maintenance window is from 02:00 to 06:00 every morning. You can set the maintenance window during off\-peak hours based on your business patterns. This helps minimize the impact of maintenance on your business.

<span id="b2999f0e"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log into the [BytePlus Cloud Search console](https://console.byteplus.com/es).

2. In the top menu bar, select the Project and Region where the desired instance resides.

3. On the **Instances** page, click the name of the instance.

4. On the **Basic information** page, click **Modify** next to **Maintainable time**.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/594e79fdea39405d96503518ac838b91~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

5. In the **Maintainable time** dialog box displayed, configure **Day for Maintenance** and **Time Period for Maintenance**, and click **OK**.
