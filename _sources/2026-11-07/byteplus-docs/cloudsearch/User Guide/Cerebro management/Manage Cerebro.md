This topic describes how to manage Cerebro, including changing specifications, restarting, switching to subscription, deleting, and unsubscribing.

<span id="8GJI65nj"></span>
## Prerequisites

Cerebro has been created. For detailed operations, refer to [Create Cerebro](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/cloudsearch/Create_Cerebro).

<span id="eAb3SPmK"></span>
## Change specification of Cerebro

After creating Cerebro, you can modify its configuration as needed.


1. Log on to the[Cloud Search console](https://console.byteplus.com/es).

2. In the top navigation bar, select the target project and region.

3. In the left navigation bar, select **Cerebro**, then click **Change specification** in the top right corner.

4. On the **Change specification** page, confirm the Cerebro information, select the required specification from the **Node specification** dropdown menu, then click **Confirm order**.

5. On the **Configuration information** page, complete the following steps based on the selected billing type.

   * If you select **Pay\-as\-you\-go**: on the **Confirm order** page, review the order details, read and check the **Database product and service terms** and **Unsubscribe rules**, then click **Buy now**.

   * If you select **Subscription**:

      * On the **Confirm order** page, review the order details, read and check the **Database product and service terms** and **Unsubscribe rules**, then click **Submit order**.

      * On the **Confirm order information** page, review the order details again, then click **Next**.

      * On the **Submit payment information** page, confirm the payment details, select a payment method, click **Pay now,**  and complete the payment as instructed to finish the specification change.


<span id="WHAdH2HI"></span>
## Restart Cerebro

If an exception occurs in Cerebro, you can try restarting Cerebro.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">After restarting, the Elasticsearch and OpenSearch instances manageable in Cerebro will update automatically.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">If you change the Elasticsearch or OpenSearch instance during the Cerebro restart process, Cerebro’s configuration may not be the latest after restarting.</div>


1. Log on to the[Cloud Search console](https://console.byteplus.com/es).

2. In the top navigation bar, select the target project and region.

3. In the left navigation bar, select **Cerebro**, then click **restart** at the top right.

4. In the pop\-up dialog, confirm the Cerebro information, then click **OK**.


<span id="TzsQCWTS"></span>
## Cerebro subscription change

After creating a pay\-as\-you\-go Cerebro, you can change its billing method to subscription as needed.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">After switching pay\-as\-you\-go Cerebro to subscription, it cannot be reversed back to pay\-as\-you\-go. Proceed with caution.</div>



1. Log on to the[Cloud Search console](https://console.byteplus.com/es).

2. In the top navigation bar, select the target project and region.

3. Select **Cerebro** in the left navigation bar, then choose  **...**  at the top right corner  **\> change to subscription**

4. On the **Pay\-as\-you\-go to subscription** page, confirm Cerebro information, choose purchase duration, check **automatic renewal** if needed, read and agree to the **database product and service terms**, then click **confirm order**

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="1">Purchase duration supports monthly subscription (1 to 9 months) and yearly subscription (1 to 3 years), with longer durations offering greater discounts   </div>
   

5. On the **Confirm order information** page, review the order details again, then click **Next**.

6. On the **Submit payment information** page, review payment details and select a payment method, click **proceed to payment**, and complete the payment as prompted to finalize the subscription change

## Delete Pay\-as\-you\-go Cerebro

Delete Cerebro when you no longer need the Pay\-as\-you\-go service

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">After deleting Cerebro, all related resources and data will be cleared and cannot be recovered; proceed with caution</div>



1. Log on to the[Cloud Search console](https://console.byteplus.com/es).

2. In the top navigation bar, select the target project and region.

3. Select **Cerebro** in the left navigation bar, then select  **... \> Delete** in the upper right corner.

4. In the pop\-up dialog, confirm the Cerebro information, then click **OK**.


<span id="X6J3k21W"></span>
## Unsubscribe from subscription Cerebro

When you no longer need subscription Cerebro, you can unsubscribe from Cerebro.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">After unsubscribing from Cerebro, related resources and data will be cleared and cannot be recovered. Proceed with caution.</div>



1. Log on to the[Cloud Search console](https://console.byteplus.com/es).

2. In the top navigation bar, select the target project and region.

3. Select **Cerebro** in the left navigation bar, then select  **... \> Unsubscribe** in the upper right corner.

4. In the pop\-up dialog, confirm Cerebro information, then click **OK**.

5. On the **Unsubscribe** page, confirm refund information, then click **Submit order**.

6. In the popup dialog, click **OK**.
