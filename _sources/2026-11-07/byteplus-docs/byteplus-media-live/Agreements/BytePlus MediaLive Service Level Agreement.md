During the term of the BytePlus Customer Agreement pursuant to which BytePlus Pte. Ltd. (“BytePlus”) has agreed to provide the BytePlus MediaLive Service (the “Service”) to you (the “Agreement”), the Service will be subject to the service level metrics set out below in this Service Level Agreement (this “SLA”). Capitalized terms used in this SLA, but not defined in this SLA, have the meaning stated in the Agreement.

<span id="1. Definition"></span>
# 1. Definition

a.  **"Service Cycle":**  Each calendar month is a Service Cycle. Any period less than one month shall be calculated as one full calendar month. The period from the day on which a user starts to use the Service to the very last day of such month will be counted as one Service Cycle. The availability of the Service is calculated independently for each Service Cycle.

b.  **"Service Cycle Time"**  calculated by the total number of days in each Service Cycle \* 24 (hours) \* 60 (minutes).

c.  **"Number of Failed Requests"**  refers to requests with the response status code of “5XX” due to the Service’s own issue and the normal requests that fail to reach the BytePlus MediaLive server due to the BytePlus MediaLive server malfunction shall be recognized as a Failed Request and included in the statistics of the number of Failed Requests.

d.  **"Total Number of Valid Requests"**  the total number of requests for the Service from the domain under a particular BytePlus account of the Customer.

e.  **"Error Rate Per Unit Time"**  = (Number of Failed Requests per Unit Time / Total Number of Valid Requests per Unit Time) \*100%.

f.  **"Unit Time"**  every 5 minutes shall be deemed as a Unit Time for the calculation of availability, such as 00:00:00 \- 00:04:59 shall be deemed as a Unit Time, and less than 5 minutes will not be counted.

g.  **"Service Unavailable"**  If the Error Rate Per Unit Time exceeds 0.05% and lasts for 10 minutes or longer, such a time period shall be counted into Service Unavailable. Any such duration that is less than 10 minutes will not be counted.

h.  **"Service Unavailable Time"**  The accumulated minutes of Service Unavailable in that Service Cycle. Unavailable Time does not include any unavailability resulting from any Exclusion set forth below in Section 5.

<span id="2. Service Levels"></span>
# 2. Service Levels

BytePlus will use all commercially reasonable efforts to make the Service available with a Monthly Uptime Percentage no less than 99.9% during any monthly billing cycle (the “Service Levels”) as calculated and subject to the exclusions outlined below in Section 5.

**The Monthly Uptime Percentage = (Service Cycle Time \- Service Unavailable Time) / Service Cycle Time \* 100%** 

<span id="3. Service Credits"></span>
# 3. Service Credits

Subject to the terms and conditions of the Agreement, and your full compliance of the Agreement, in the event that the above Service Levels are not satisfied, you will be eligible to receive a service credit (the “Service Credits”) as described below.


|**Monthly Uptime Percentage (or "MUP")**  |**Percentage of monthly bill for the Service that will be credited to your future monthly bills** |
|---|---|
|95.00% < = MUP < 99.90% |5% |
|MUP < 95.00% |10% |


We will apply any Service Credits only against future Service payments otherwise due from you. At our discretion, we may exchange the Service Credit into Service hours.

Service Credits will be applicable and issued only if the credit amount for the applicable monthly billing cycle is greater than US one dollar (US$1).

The aggregate maximum amount of Service Credits you can claim in a single monthly billing cycle shall not exceed 10% of such monthly service fee. Service Credits will not entitle you to any refund or other payment from BytePlus. Service Credits may not be transferred or applied to any other account.

Notwithstanding anything to the contrary hereunder and under the Agreement, you agree and acknowledge that the Service Credits (if eligible) are the sole and exclusive remedy for any unavailability, non\-performance, or other failure by us to provide the Service.

<span id="4. Service Credit Application and Payment Procedures"></span>
# 4. Service Credit Application and Payment Procedures

In order to apply for the Service Credits, you must notify BytePlus within 30 days after the end of preceding calendar month where you become eligible to receive the Service Credits, and provide BytePlus with sufficient supporting information and materials evidencing the occurrence of the failure of Service Level. Such written notification also must include:

a. the words “SLA Credit Request” in the subject line;

b. the dates, times, and BytePlus regions of each lack of availability incident that you are claiming; and

c. the affected Service.

If the MUP of such request is confirmed by us and is less than the Service Levels, then we will issue the Service Credit to you within one billing cycle following the month in which your request is confirmed by us.

If you fail to meet the aforesaid requirements, your eligibility to receive the Service Credits may be forfeited. In case of any disputes regarding the Service Credits, BytePlus shall be entitled to make a determination in good faith based on BytePlus’s information.

<span id="5. Exclusion of SLA"></span>
# 5. Exclusion of SLA

If any failure to achieve the Service Levels is due to any of the following reasons, you will not be eligible for the Service Credit:

a. any system maintenance with prior notice by BytePlus to you, including system cutover, maintenance, upgrade and failure simulation test.

b. any features excluded from this SLA.

c. any matters that are out of BytePlus’s control or resulting from the software, hardware, network, equipment or resources that are not provided by BytePlus.

d. any lose or leak of data, pin or password due to your improper maintenance or improper security measures.

e. any negligence in authorization or mal\-operation by you.

f. any of your failure to comply with documentation or suggestions made available by BytePlus.

g. any failure of video playing due to expiration of any anti\-leech protection adopted by a client with anti\-leech authentication enabled.

h. any failure of video playing due to block of a domain name caused by any illegal or prohibited content of a client or otherwise.

i. any failure of video playing due to anomaly on the push end.

j. any Force Majeure Event or accident.

k. any hacker attacks on your website or any user’s system and/or application.

l. any impact on the availability of the Service due to impromptu increase of traffic of a client (impromptu increase by 200% of daily peak of which the bandwidth is greater than 200Gbps) unless the client has provided a two\-business day prior written notice to Byteplus and subscribed a event\-support service.

m. any service unavailability or failure of the Service not attributable to BytePlus, including without limitation, taking down of your application, or your websites' domain name blocking.

n. any circumstance, under the relevant laws, regulations, codes, rules or agreements, where BytePlus could be exempted from any liability.
