This page explains how you can manage screenshots and see their addresses. During a live stream session, screenshots are captured at specified intervals.

<span id="Prerequisites"></span>
## Prerequisites


* At least one screenshot configuration. For details, refer to [Configuring screenshot capturing](https://docs.byteplus.com/byteplus-media-live/docs/configuring-screenshot-capturing).

* The domain name, AppName, and StreamName of the screenshots you want to query.


<span id="Procedure"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [BytePlus MediaLive console](https://console.byteplus.com/live).

2. In the left\-side navigation pane, click **Live management** \> **Screenshots**.

3. Select a domain name space and fill in the appropriate AppName, StreamName, and time range. Then click **Search** to view the relevant screenshots.

4. Click on a screenshot to view the full\-size image.

5. To get the address of a screenshot, move the cursor to the thumbnail image and click **Copy image address** after it appears in the top\-right corner.
