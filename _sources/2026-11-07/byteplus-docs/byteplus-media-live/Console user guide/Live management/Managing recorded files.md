This page introduces how to view and manage recording files.

<span id="Prerequisites"></span>
## Prerequisites


* Ensure that at least one recording configuration is set, and you have used a live stream with the same stream parameters for live playback. Your live stream will be recorded and stored. For details, refer to [Configuring recordings](https://docs.byteplus.com/byteplus-media-live/docs/configuring-recordings).

* You will need the domain name space, AppName, and StreamName of the recordings you want to query.

* Please note that it may take a few minutes for the files to appear on the page after the stream has ended.


<span id="Procedure"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [BytePlus MediaLive console](https://console.byteplus.com/live).

2. In the left\-side navigation pane, click **Live management** \> **Recordings**.

3. Click **VOD** tab or **TOS** tab to view recording files stored in VOD or TOS.

4. Select a domain name space and fill in the appropriate AppName, StreamName, and time range. Then click **Search** to view the relevant recordings.
