To optimize performance or meet regional compliance for your live streams, you can modify the acceleration area for your domain names. This topic provides step\-by\-step instructions for changing the acceleration area for both domain names for stream pushing and pulling in the BytePlus MediaLive console.

<span id="ccfa0f3a"></span>
## Prerequisites


* If your domain's current acceleration area is **Outside the Chinese mainland**, you must complete the ICP filing process for mainland China before changing the area to **The Chinese mainland** or **Global acceleration**.

* The prices are different for the Chinese mainland and non\-Chinese regions. For more information, see [Live-streaming service fees](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-media-live/docs-live-streaming-service-fees).


<span id="82b42909"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [BytePlus MediaLive console](https://console.byteplus.com/live).

2. In the left\-side navigation pane, click **Domains**.

3. Click **Settings** for the domain name that you want to modify.

4. Click **Modify** in the **Basic information** section.

5. In the pop\-up window, select the new acceleration area, then click **Confirm**.
