You can explore the data usage, usage trend, and billing methods on the BytePlus MediaLive Dashboard, including traffic, bandwidth usage, resource packages usage, and more.

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/5e707da1a9c941fe99c2c19b383d7139~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

The **Dashboard** page consists of the following modules.


* **Data usage yesterday**: The data usage of the previous day, which includes the downstream traffic, peak downstream bandwidth, and transcoding duration.

* **Data usage over time**: The data usage trend of the previous day, which includes bandwidth usage, traffic, and transcoding duration.

* **Language and time zone settings**: Allows users to switch languages and time zones.

* **Billing method**: The billing method of the current account. Click **Change** to modify the billing method.

* **Documentation**: The BytePlus MediaLive documentation.

* **Prepaid resource packages**: The usage of your resource packs.


<span id="dd1a1bee"></span>
## Prerequisites


* Activate the BytePlus MediaLive service.

* Log in to the [BytePlus MediaLive console](https://console.byteplus.com/live).


<span id="b74648d2"></span>
## Viewing yesterday's data usage


1. In the left\-side navigation pane, click **Dashboard**.

2. In the **Data usage yesterday** section of the **Dashboard** page that appears, view downstream traffic, peak downstream bandwidth, and transcoding duration of the previous day.

   * Downstream traffic: The total downstream traffic of the previous day for all domain names for stream pulling.

   * Peak downstream bandwidth: The peak downstream bandwidth of the previous day for all domain names for stream pulling.

   * Transcoding duration: The total duration of transcoding tasks on BytePlus MediaLive of the previous day.

3. Click **View more** in the upper\-right corner to go to the **Usage** page. For more information, see [Usage](https://docs.byteplus.com/byteplus-media-live/docs/usage-statistics).


<span id="e8984370"></span>
## Viewing data usage over time


1. In the left\-side navigation pane, click **Dashboard**.

2. In the **Data usage over time** section of the **Dashboard** page that appears, view the line charts of bandwidth, traffic, and transcoding duration of the previous day.

3. Click **View more** in the upper\-right corner to go to the **Usage** page. For more information, see [Usage](https://docs.byteplus.com/byteplus-media-live/docs/usage-statistics).


<span id="705c6527"></span>
## Billing method

<span id="4017462b"></span>
### Viewing the billing method


1. In the left\-side navigation pane, click **Dashboard**.

2. In the **Billing method** section of the **Dashboard** page that appears, view your current billing method.


<span id="77ab9298"></span>
### Changing the billing method

If you are billed daily, you can change your billing method through the console by following the steps below. For monthly billing, [contact your sales representative](https://www.byteplus.com/en/contact) if you want to use a different billing method.


1. In the **Billing method** section, click **Change** to go to the **Change the billing method** page.

2. Change the billing method.

3. Read and accept the service agreement, and then click **Confirm**.tip

   * You can change your billing method only once every month.

   * The change will take effect at midnight.

## Prepaid resource packages

You can buy, view, and manage prepaid resource packages of the BytePlus MediaLive service.

&nbsp;

<span id="a230f762"></span>
### Buying prepaid resource packages


1. In the left\-side navigation pane, click **Dashboard**.

2. In the **Prepaid resource packages** section of the **Dashboard** page that appears, click **Buy now** to enter the purchase resource package page.

3. Select the resource pack that you want to buy based on your business requirements. For example, if you want to buy 300 GB of live\-stream data, select **Traffic packages**, and then select **100 GB** as the pack option and **3** as the quantity.

4. At the bottom of the page, check the total cost and details. Read and accept the service agreement.

5. Click **Buy now**. Confirm the information and pay for the packs as prompted.


<span id="909e440a"></span>
### Viewing resource packs

You can view the type, total quota, and remaining quota of resource packs in the **Prepaid resource packages** section.

<span id="c6aac8e7"></span>
### Managing resource packs


1. In the **Prepaid resource packages** section, click **Manage resource packs** to go to the **Resource package** page.

2. You can view the details of resource packs, search for and filter resource packs, export the information of resource packs, and set the remaining quota alert.
