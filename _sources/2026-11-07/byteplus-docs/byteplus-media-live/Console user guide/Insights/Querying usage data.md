The **Usage** page displays usage information of your live streams, including bandwidth, traffic, recording tasks, and others.

<span id="Prerequisite"></span>
## Prerequisite

You need to have the actual data generated before the query. For example, you should configure the transcoding settings and run transcoding tasks before you can query the total transcoding duration.

<span id="Procedure"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [BytePlus MediaLive console](https://console.byteplus.com/live).

2. In the left navigation pane, click **Usage**.

3. Click each tab to view the corresponding statistics.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/2189d8549362491d83b7ab819ef503a8~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>


<span id="Bandwidth usage"></span>
### Bandwidth


* You can query bandwidth statistics, including peak upstream and downstream bandwidths and 95th percentile bandwidth.

* You can customize your query by specifying the project, domain name, time granularity, time range, region, and streaming protocol.

* You can use a line graph, a bar graph or a list to display your data.

* You can download your search results.


<span id="Traffic"></span>
### Traffic


* You can query the traffic statistics such as total traffic, upstream traffic, and downstream traffic.

* You can customize your query by specifying the project, domain name, time granularity, time range, region, and streaming protocol.

* You can use a line graph, a bar graph or a list to display your data.

* You can download your search results.


<span id="Live recording"></span>
### Recording


* You can query **Peak recording tasks**, namely the peak number of concurrent recording tasks within a chosen time duration.

* You can customize your query by specifying the project, domain name, time granularity, and time range.

* You can download your search results.


<span id="Storage usage"></span>
### Storage usage


* You can query the usage of your storage space.

* You can customize your query by specifying the storage space, time granularity, and time range.

* You can download your search results.


<span id="Transcoding duration"></span>
### Transcoding duration


* You can view the total transcoding duration.

* You can customize your query by specifying the project, domain name, granularity and time range.

* You can use a line graph, a bar graph or a list to display your data. Different combinations of resolution and codec are displayed separately.

* You can download your search results.


<span id="Number of screenshots"></span>
### Screenshots


* You can view the total number of screenshots.

* You can customize your query by specifying the project, domain name, time granularity, and time range.

* You can use a line graph, a bar graph, or a list to display your data.

* You can download your search results.


<span id="Relay"></span>
### Relay


* You can view the total relay duration.

* You can customize your query by specifying the granularity and time range.

* You can download your search results.


<span id="CDN usage"></span>
### CDN usage


* You can query the CDN usage of your storage space.

* You can customize your query by specifying the domain name for storage, time granularity, time range, and region.

* You can download your search results.


<span id="Relay to third-party"></span>
### Relay to third\-party


* You can view the peak bandwidth for relays that push streams to third\-party addresses. For details, refer to [Relay](https://docs.byteplus.com/byteplus-media-live/docs/relay).

* You can customize your query by specifying the time granularity and time range.

* You can download your search results.
