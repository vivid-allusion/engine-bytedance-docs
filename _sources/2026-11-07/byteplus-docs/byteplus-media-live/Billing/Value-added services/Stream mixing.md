The cost of stream mixing will be calculated according to the duration of the mixing task with different output resolutions. This page introduces how stream mixing fees are charged.

<span id="38fbf21d"></span>
## Pricing


|Output resolutions |unit: USD/minute |
|---|---|
|480P |0.00270 |
|720P |0.00540 |
|1080P |0.01050 |
|2K |0.02280 |
|4K |0.04660 |


<span id="0dd8d95b"></span>
## Billing cycle

Transcoding supports both daily and monthly pay\-as\-you\-go.

<span id="13ec0734"></span>
## Billing example

User A used cloud remixing for live streaming on November 1st. For remixing task A, a video with 720P resolution was output for 2 hours continuously; for remixing task B, a video with 4K resolution was output for 1 hour continuously.

Then the cloud remixing bill to be paid is as follows:


* Cost of remixing task A = 0.0054 USD/minute x 120 minutes = 0.648 USD.

* Cost of remixing task B = 0.0466 USD/minute x 60 minutes = 2.796 USD.


Then the total cost of cloud remixing on November 1st is 3.444 USD, and the deduction will be made after the bill is generated the next day.
