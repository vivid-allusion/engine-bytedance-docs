You are charged for both transcoding and subtitle recognition fees when using the subtitle service. This chapter introduces the functional pricing of subtitle recognition to you. Please refer to [transcoding](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-media-live/docs-transcoding_2) for the transcoding fees.

<span id="26e3c660"></span>
## Pricing

The unit price is 0.055 USD/minute.

<span id="3519dd44"></span>
## Billing cycle

Subtitle supports both daily and monthly pay\-as\-you\-go.

<span id="c7462b03"></span>
## Billing example

The customer enabled the subtitle recognition function for live stream A on November 1st. This live stream was encoded with H.264 and had a resolution of 1080P. There were also two transcoding configurations encoded with H.264, with resolutions of 720P and 480P respectively. The live stream lasted for 30 minutes. Then the bill for using the subtitle function is as follows:


* Subtitle recognition cost = 0.055 USD/minute x 30 minutes = 1.65 USD

* Source stream transcoding cost = 0.0105 USD/minute x 30 minutes = 0.315 USD

* Transcoding stream transcoding cost = 0.0054 USD/minute x 30 minutes + 0.0027 USD/minute x 30 minutes = 0.243 USD


Then the total cost of using the subtitle function for live stream A on November 1st was 2.208 USD, and the fees will be charged after the billing is generated the next day.
