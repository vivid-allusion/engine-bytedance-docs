To effectively manage your BytePlus MediaLive expenses, it is essential to understand how costs are calculated. This topic provides a comprehensive overview of the billing structure, covering live\-streaming service fees, value\-added service fees, and other cloud service fees. You will also find details on payment methods, billing cycles, and regional pricing to help you accurately forecast your budget.

<span id="Billing cycles"></span>
## Billing cycles

BytePlus MediaLive supports two billing cycles:


* Daily billing cycle: Fees are calculated daily and deducted the following day.

* Monthly billing cycle: Fees are calculated for each calendar month and deducted the following month, with a bill sent accordingly.


After activating the BytePlus MediaLive service, you will be billed on a daily basis by default. If you want to change to a monthly billing cycle, contact your sales representative.

<span id="Payment methods"></span>
## Payment methods

BytePlus MediaLive supports both pay\-as\-you\-go and pre\-paid resource packs. Any live\-stream traffic or transcoding duration consumed is first offset by your resource packs. You are charged for any outstanding balance at the corresponding unit prices.

<span id="Billing regions"></span>
## Billing regions

You are charged based on the location of the edge server through which your content is served. Refer to the list below for the billing regions of BytePlus MediaLive and the corresponding countries and regions:


* Asia Pacific 1: Singapore, Japan, Thailand, Malaysia, Hong Kong (China), Macau (China)

* Asia Pacific 2: The Philippines, Indonesia, Vietnam, Taiwan (China)

* Asia Pacific 3: South Korea and other Asia\-Pacific countries and regions (excluding the Chinese Mainland)

* North America: The United States, Mexico

* Europe: The United Kingdom, the Netherlands, Germany, Italy, France, Russia, Spain

* Middle East: Turkey

* South America: Brazil
