If you use BytePlus MediaLive in conjunction with other services, you may incur additional fees as outlined below:


|**Product** |**Usage Scenario** |**Reference** |
|---|---|---|
|BytePlus VOD |If you store recorded files in BytePlus Video on Demand (VOD), you will need to pay VOD service fees. |[Pricing overview](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-vod/docs-pricing) |
|BytePlus RTC |If you integrate the BytePlus MediaLive SDK and use interactive live streaming features such as audience co\-hosting and host PK battles, you will need to pay RTC service fees. |[Pricing](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-rtc/docs-69871) |
|BytePlus Effects SDK |If you integrate the BytePlus MediaLive Broadcast SDK and use features provided by the Effects SDK, such as filters, beauty AR, and stickers, you will need to pay the corresponding fees. |[Contact your sales representative](https://www.byteplus.com/en/contact) for details about pricing. |
