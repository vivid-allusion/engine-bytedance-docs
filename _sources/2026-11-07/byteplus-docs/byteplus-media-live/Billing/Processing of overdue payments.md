This topic describes the process for handling overdue payments.

<span id="Overdue payments"></span>
## Overdue payments


* If your account has an overdue payment (with a balance below 0), you will receive a notification.

* If you settle your overdue payment within 24 hours, you can continue using the BytePlus MediaLive service. Otherwise, your service will be suspended.


<span id="Service suspension"></span>
## Service suspension


* Once your account is suspended, BytePlus MediaLive will not terminate any ongoing live streams, but you will no longer be able to create or start new tasks. Also, the BytePlus MediaLive Console, OpenAPI, recorded files, screenshots, and time\-shifted content will become inaccessible.

* If a payment is overdue and the account balance has not been topped up within the required period, BytePlus MediaLive reserves the right to automatically repossess the resources associated with the account within 15 days following the suspension date.

* To completely stop using the BytePlus MediaLive service, contact your sales representative.


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Overdue payments for other BytePlus services can also lead to the suspension of your access to the BytePlus MediaLive service.</div>


<span id="Account repossession"></span>
## Account repossession

For accounts suspended for over 15 days, BytePlus MediaLive will automatically deactivate your service, repossess your account, and clear all account\-related records, including configurations, logs, and client authorizations. BytePlus MediaLive will keep your files stored in the storage space, meaning you will continue to incur storage fees, thereby increasing the amount of the outstanding bill. To completely stop using the BytePlus MediaLive service, contact your sales representative.
