Powered by high\-availability and streaming media technologies, a self\-developed encoder, and rich experience serving different industries, BytePlus MediaLive provides an enterprise\-level, end\-to\-end streaming solution capable of supporting tens of millions of peak concurrent viewers.

<span id="Superior viewing experience"></span>
## Superior viewing experience

With the experience of serving billions of Daily Active Users (DAU), the BytePlus MediaLive player has overcome issues such as blurry images, out\-of\-sync sound and picture, and black screens. It achieves stable and reliable playback quality by reducing the crash rate to less than 0.001%. Dedicated to providing high\-definition and smooth viewing experience, BytePlus MediaLive minimizes lag when swiping to switch videos through features such as pre\-loading, network, and decoding optimizations.

<span id="Cost-effective encoding and decoding"></span>
## Cost\-effective encoding and decoding

BytePlus MediaLive provides complete H.265 transcoding as well as a high\-definition, low\-bitrate solution based on our self\-developed encoder, Byte Video Coding (BVC). Its video compression rate reaches 20% to 50% without affecting the viewing experience. By dynamically adjusting the decoding method according to the hardware model, and thanks to a graceful degradation strategy, the average coverage rate of hardware decoding exceeds 85%.

<span id="Industry-specific solutions"></span>
## Industry\-specific solutions

BytePlus MediaLive provides tailored services for a wide range of industries, including interactive entertainment, e\-commerce, education, and media. Its end\-to\-end streaming solutions are dedicated to solving the actual pain points of its customers.

<span id="Data-driven solutions"></span>
## Data\-driven solutions

BytePlus MediaLive is empowered with a comprehensive set of user\-experience metrics. Through in\-depth mining of playback statistics, it offers real\-time data monitoring and intelligent root\-cause analysis, with which you can track issues by user or by session to quickly respond to users' complaints and needs.

<span id="Global acceleration"></span>
## Global acceleration

With 11 media centers across the world, BytePlus MediaLive provides 15 Tbps bandwidth that can be scaled when needed.

<span id="Easy integration"></span>
## Easy integration

BytePlus MediaLive provides an easy\-to\-use console and featured SDKs, which help you integrate our streaming service with simple steps.
