This article introduces the common use cases of BytePlus MediaLive.

<span id="Interactive entertainment"></span>
## Interactive entertainment

Provides streaming and messaging services for shows, games, karaoke bars, and social apps, supporting Beauty AR, stickers, special effects, voice chat, and more.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/doc-center/e6c8818-use1.PNG) </span>

<span id="E-commerce"></span>
## E\-commerce

Helps online businesses increase revenue by allowing users to make purchases during a live stream via features such as time\-shifted product introductions and user interactions.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/doc-center/7799775-use2.PNG) </span>

<span id="Media"></span>
## Media

Supports video production on the cloud and onsite live\-streaming services that cater to the needs of new media, helping you build a professional media streaming platform at a low cost.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/doc-center/e8e5170-use3.PNG) </span>

<span id="Education"></span>
## Education

Helps you build an education streaming platform with simple steps, guaranteeing stability and security via high\-availability services and dynamic hotlink protection.
