Dear BytePlus MediaLive users:

BytePlus plans to switch the entire website time zone to UTC+8 on July 20, 2024(UTC+8), in order to provide a unified mechanism for ordering and charging for different products, and to provide a better experience of ordering and billing data.

This means that the ordering timepoint and the billing period will be based on UTC+8 timezone.

The impact of changing the time zone from UTC+0 is as follows:


* If your subscription's billing period is hourly, it will not be affected.

* If your subscription's billing period is daily or monthly, there will be an 8\-hour shortage of usage in the first billing cycle after July 20,2024, since the first 8 of the billing cycle have already been billed in the last billing cycle according to UTC+0.


<span id="33562bfc"></span>
## Impact on MediaLive

Since some functions of MediaLive have not completed the time zone switching transformation, you need to pay attention to the time zone selection when comparing the bill and console usage in the following scenario.


1. For bandwidth usage, traffic usage, and recording usage, if checking the usage data, choose the UTC+8 on the MediaLive console.

2. For storage usage, transcoding usage, relay usage, and screenshot usage, if checking the usage data, choose the UTC+0 on the MediaLive console.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">We MediaLive will complete the transformation ASAP and keep it consistent with the official website billing center.</div>
