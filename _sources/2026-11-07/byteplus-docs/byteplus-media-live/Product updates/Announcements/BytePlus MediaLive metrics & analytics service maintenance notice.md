To provide a more stable and efficient data statistics service, BytePlus MediaLive will upgrade its real\-time data query service.

During the upgrade, only the real\-time data query functions on the console and through APIs will be affected. All other live streaming functions and log data will not be impacted.

**Upgrade Time**


* 2026\-06\-09 10:00\-12:00 (UTC+8)

* 2026\-06\-10 10:00\-12:00 (UTC+8)


**Impact Scope**

During the upgrade, temporary anomalies may occur on the console’s **Usage**, **Analytics**, **Stream data query** pages and related data query APIs. Services will resume automatically after the upgrade is completed.

If you have urgent data query needs during this period, please contact [technical support](https://console.byteplus.com/workorder/create?step=2&SubProductID=P00000980) promptly.

Thank you for your understanding and support!
