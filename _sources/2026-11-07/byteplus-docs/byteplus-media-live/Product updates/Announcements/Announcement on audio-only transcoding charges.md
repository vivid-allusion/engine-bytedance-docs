Dear BytePlus MediaLive users:

BytePlus MediaLive will begin charging for audio\-only transcoding starting **June 1, 2026**.

Audio\-only transcoding means that only the audio in a live stream is transcoded, while the video remains unchanged. For example, if the audio codec is converted from AAC to Opus while the video codec, resolution, and other video settings remain unchanged, audio\-only transcoding fees will apply. For more information, see [Audio-only transcoding](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-media-live/docs-transcoding#audio-only-transcoding).

If you have any questions or concerns about the change, please feel free to contact the BytePlus support team by [creating a ticket](https://console.byteplus.com/workorder/create?step=2&SubProductID=P00000980) on the BytePlus website.
