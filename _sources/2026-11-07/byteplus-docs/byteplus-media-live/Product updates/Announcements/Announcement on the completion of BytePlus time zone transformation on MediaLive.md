**Dear BytePlus MediaLive users:** 

BytePlus switched its entire website's time zone to UTC+8 on July 20, 2024, to standardize product ordering and billing, enhancing user experience. MediaLive had partially completed time zone conversion; for specifics, see the related announcement. Now, MediaLive has fully completed the time zone conversion for all functions, effective December 4, 2024”

<span id="fc62922a"></span>
## Impact on MediaLive


* After it takes effect, when comparing billing and console usage, please select the UTC+8 time zone for accurate results in any scenario.

* This change only has the following impacts on the usage data of transcoding, relay, and screenshots in December:

   * The usage data for December 3rd, pushed on December 4th, will cover 16 hours only. Time range is 2024\-12\-03 00:00:00+00:00 ~ 2024\-12\-04 00:00:00+08:00.

   * The December usage data will be missing 8 hours of usage. Time range is 2024\-12\-01 00:00:00+00:00 ~ 2025\-01\-01 00:00:00+08:00.


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Storage usage data is not affected by time zone changes.</div>
