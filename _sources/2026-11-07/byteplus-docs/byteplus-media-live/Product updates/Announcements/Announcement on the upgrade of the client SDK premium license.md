Dear developers,

To provide more refined SDK services, BytePlus MediaLive will upgrade the client SDK premium license and update the billing rules. For detailed rules on license billing, see [Pricing](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-media-live/docs-sdk-licenses#5ee1abcd).


* Effective date: January 9, 2026

* Applicable versions:

   * Player SDK for Android: 1.49.100.2 and later

   * Player SDK for iOS: 1.49.100.2\-premium and later

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">Versions earlier than 1.49.100.2 of the Player SDK for Android and 1.49.100.2\-premium of the Player SDK for iOS can still be used, but we recommend upgrading to the latest version to enjoy more comprehensive services and support.   </div>
   

If you have any questions or concerns about the change, please feel free to contact the BytePlus support team by [creating a ticket](https://console.byteplus.com/workorder/create?step=2&SubProductID=P00000980) on the BytePlus website.
