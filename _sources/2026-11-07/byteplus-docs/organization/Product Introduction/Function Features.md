BytePlus Organization Service allows you to create an Organization and centrally manage your BytePlus accounts, including those of your subsidiaries. You can invite existing accounts or create new ones to join the Organization. Tags can be used to efficiently label, categorize, and manage accounts and Organizational Units, while Control Policies help control permissions of accounts within the Organization.

<span id="key-features"></span>
## **Key Features**

BytePlus Organization Service provides the following features:


1. **Enterprise\-Level Account Structure Management**


With a BytePlus account that has completed account profile, you can create and manage a tree\-structured Organization by using the Organization Service Console. You can create up to five levels of Organizational Units in your Organization.

You can invite existing BytePlus accounts from your enterprise to the Organization for centralized management. You can also create new accounts within the Organization.


2. **Centralized Management of BytePlus Account Permissions**


With Control Policies, you can define the maximum permission scope for accounts within the Organization based on its hierarchical structure. This enables standardized operations and maintenance (O&M) practices across the organization, enhanced security, and cost and budget management.


3. **Seamless Integration with Multiple Byteplus Cloud Services**


Through trusted services, you can leverage multi\-account capabilities across various BytePlus cloud services. This allows you to efficiently configure cloud services for accounts within your organization.
