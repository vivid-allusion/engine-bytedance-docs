The Master Account of an Organization can create Member Accounts within the Organization. A newly created Member Account:


1. Can choose whether to inherit the account profile from an existing account within the Organization.

2. Can enable consolidated billing if the Member Account inherits the account profile from an existing account.

3. Can only be removed from the Organization if the Member Account is configured to allow login to the console.


**Account Quota**


* An Organization can have up to 20 accounts.


<span id="procedure"></span>
## Procedure


1. Go to the Organizational Unit Management page, select the Organizational Unit, and click Add Member to create a new Member Account, as shown in the following figure.


<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_ccb9eddbc64f4c8f42492dca417b1df9.png) </span>


2. Enter the Member Account details on the Create Member Account page and click Submit.

   1. **Organizational Unit**: Select the Organizational Unit to which the new account belongs.

   2. **Member Account Name**: Enter an account name, which is also used for logging in to BytePlus.

   3. **Display Name**: Enter an alias for the Member Account within the Organization.

   4. **Description**: Provide a brief description of the Member Account.

   5. **Inherit Account Profile**: Choose whether to inherit the account profile from an existing account within the Organization.

      1. **Select an Account to Inherit Account Profile**: Select a verified account within the Organization for the Member Account to inherit its account profile.

   6. **Financial Relationship**: By default, a newly created Member Account is set to independent billing. However, you can enable consolidated billing for the Member Account.

      1. To enable consolidated billing, the Member Account must inherit the account profile from an existing account. This account serves as the main account managing financial assets of the Member Account.


<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_51840ebab7f7ad94dd72d5a49edb9627.png) </span>


3. Click Submit to create the Member Account. The created Member Account appears in the member list of the Organization. You can view and manage its financial relationship for consolidated billing in the [Corporate Finance Console](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/busniess-finance/relation).
