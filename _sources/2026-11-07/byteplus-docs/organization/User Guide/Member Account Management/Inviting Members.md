The Master Account of an Organization can invite existing BytePlus accounts to join the Organization.

**Account Quota**


* An Organization can have up to 20 accounts. If an invited account accepts the invitation but the number of accounts in the Organization has already reached the upper limit, the invitation fails.

* An Organization can have up to 20 pending invitations at a time. Accepted invitations do not count toward this limit.


<span id="procedure"></span>
## Procedure


1. Go to the Organizational Unit Management page, select the Organizational Unit, and click Add Member to invite an existing account to join the Organization, as shown in the following figure.


<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_721b76bc08216fc53a16da75dc3ff3a3.png) </span>


2. Enter the details of the account to invite on the Invite Member page and click Submit to send the invitation:

   1. **Organizational Unit**: Select the Organizational Unit to which the invited account belongs.

   2. **Member** **Account ID**: Enter the ID of the BytePlus account to invite.

   3. **Display Name**: Enter an alias for the Member Account within the Organization.

   4. **Description**: Provide a brief description of the Member Account.

   5. **Financial Relationship**: By default, invited accounts are set to independent billing. However, if the invited account has completed account verification, you can enable consolidated billing for the account.

      1. If consolidated billing is enabled, you can **select a main account for managing financial assets of the invited account**. This main account must be an existing account within the Organization that shares the same account profile as the invited account.

3. Click Submit. The invitation will be sent via email, SMS, and in\-console notifications.


<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_f04bcad7a4c33791ba9d93838fa7b7c7.png) </span>

<span id="viewing-invitations"></span>
## Viewing Invitations

The Master Account can view all invitations and cancel invitations if necessary. Go to Organization \> Organization Structure \> Invitations to view all sent invitations.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_5aeb8fcb03c2fea34573700a4f160b6f.png) </span>

<span id="accepting-an-invitation"></span>
## Accepting an Invitation

After receiving an invitation, the invitee can click the link in the invitation notification to access the page where they can accept or decline the invitation.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_a58f349c8c55ff1f717ab6f02aebfb07.png) </span>

The invitee can click Accept to join the Organization or Decline to decline the invitation.

<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_506c2cdd0823d034a2342699d952169d.png) </span>
