The Master Account of an Organization can deactivate Organization Service only after removing all Member Accounts and deleting all Organizational Units from the Organization.

<span id="procedure"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the Master Account, and then go to Organization \> Settings \> [Basic Information](https://console.byteplus.com/organization/basic/)，.

2. On the Basic Information page, click Disable Service.

3. In the pop\-up dialog box, click Confirm to deactivate Organization Service.


Notes: You cannot deactivate Organization Service for an Organization if the Organization contains **Organizational Units** or **Member Accounts**. You must remove all Member Accounts and delete Organizational Units from the Organization before deactivating Organization Service.

Deactivating Organization Service may affect related services, such as Control Policies and Trusted Services. Before deactivating Organization Service, confirm that you no longer need related services.
