<span id="prerequisites"></span>
## Prerequisites

A BytePlus account that has completed account profile can activate Organization Service in the Organization Service Console if it has not created or joined an Organization.

<span id="procedure"></span>
## **Procedure**


1. In the navigation panel of the console, click ["Organization"](https://console.byteplus.com/organization/) to open the "Create Organization" page.

2. Check your account status. If your BytePlus account has not fill up **account profile**, complete the process before activating Organization Service. If your account has already created or joined an Organization, the console displays the relevant organization details.


<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_42e1bfbe5d4102d6d83b416c31221bff.png) </span>


3. Activate Organization Service. If your account meets the prerequisites, click Enable Service to activate Organization Service, as shown in the following figure.


<span>![图片](https://sf16-resources.bytepluscdn.com/obj/byteplus-public-aiso/cloud-universal-doc/upload_aaad7a07fa7cc7c06a81b883b2690dc8.png) </span>
