This topic describes how to view network topology.

<span id=".5pON5L2c6K-05piO"></span>
## Action description


* Press and hold the left mouse to drag and reposition the topology map.

* Zoom in/out on the topology map by scrolling the mouse wheel.​​

* Click a resource name; a window displaying its information will pop up in the upper\-right corner.​​


<span id=".5p-l55yLVlBD55qE6LWE5rqQ5ouT5omR"></span>
## View VPC resource topology


1. Log in to the [Resource Topo console](https://console.byteplus.com/netadvisor/topology).

2. Click the expand icon in the upper\-right corner of a target region card to display all VPCs under that region.

3. Click the expand icon in the upper\-right corner of a target VPC card to display the Availability Zones where its subnets reside.

4. Click the expand icon in the upper\-right corner of a target Availability Zone card to display all subnets within that zone.

5. Click the expand icon in the upper\-right corner of a target subnet card to display all resources within that subnet.

6. Click the expand icon in the upper\-right corner of the ECS Collection card, Internet NAT Gateway Collection card, or CLB Collection card to display their respective resources.


<span id=".5p-l55yLQ0xC55qE6LWE5rqQ5ouT5omR"></span>
## View CLB resource topology


1. Repeat steps 1\-5 above for **Viewing VPC resource topology**.

2. Click the expand icon in the upper\-right corner of the CLB Collection card to display all CLBs within the target subnet.

3. Click the expand icon in the upper\-right corner of the target CLB card to expand the CLB resource topology.
