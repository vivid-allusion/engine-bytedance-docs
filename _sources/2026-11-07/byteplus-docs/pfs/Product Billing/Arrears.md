If the available balance in your account (including account balance and vouchers) is less than the outstanding bill, it will be considered as an overdue payment, and you will receive SMS or email reminders for insufficient balance. This article explains the situation of your resources and the actions you can take after being overdue.

<span id="790e18c6"></span>
## Overdue details

After your account becomes overdue, your resources will enter a reserved period, and resource usage will be restricted. Once the reserved period expires, your resources will be forcibly reclaimed, and once reclaimed, they cannot be recovered.Please recharge and settle the outstanding bill within the specified time (before resource reclamation) to ensure normal usage of file storage resources.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">Tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Regardless of whether your resources are frozen or released, you are required to settle the fees as invoiced.</div>


<span id="3c1425d4"></span>
## Overdue resource reclamation


* Shutdown time: 2 hours after becoming overdue

* Reclamation time: 7 days and 2 hours after becoming overdue


<span id="220d5cf7"></span>
## Instance status explanation

After 15 days of overdue payment, in the instance management list, the instance status will change to **Stopped**.

The service, resource, and billing status during different stages of overdue payment are as follows:


|**Time in arrears** |**Service status** |**Resource status** |**Billing status** |
|---|---|---|---|
|Within 15 days |Normal service usage |Reserved related data |Normal billing |
|Between 15 days and 30 days |Service temporarily suspended, will be restored after settling the outstanding bill. |Reserved related data |Normal billing |
|After 30 days |Service stopped |Data deleted and cannot be recovered |Billing stopped |
