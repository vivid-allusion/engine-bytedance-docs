PFS is a high\-throughput, low\-latency, and scalable parallel file system service launched by Byteplus which meets the data reading and writing requirements of high\-throughput and low\-latency in high\-performance computing scenarios. It can be widely used in HPC high\-performance computing, AI artificial intelligence training or inference, energy exploration, industrial simulation, film and television rendering, life sciences, meteorological analysis, and other scenarios. At the same time, PFS provides one\-click deployment and supporting monitoring and alarm capabilities, eliminating deployment and maintenance costs while maximizing your business efficiency.
