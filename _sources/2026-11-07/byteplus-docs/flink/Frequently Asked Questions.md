What are the differences between BytePlus Flink and open\-source Flink?

BytePlus Flink is fully compatible with Apache Flink and offers plugin\-based enterprise enhancements and the integration of ByteDance best practices based on the open\-source engine.

<span id="1f4ecd9a"></span>
## What are the differences between BytePlus Flink and other stream computing engines (such as Storm)?

Flink, as the most advanced distributed stream computing engine today, supports stateful processing and has rich windowing capabilities.

Storm lacks batch processing capabilities, supports smaller overall cluster scales, has severe resource fragmentation, operational difficulties, and stagnant community tech development.

<span id="f72d7f77"></span>
## What are the migration costs for customers moving from open\-source Flink to BytePlus Flink?

BytePlus Flink is 100% compatible with open\-source, allowing customers to migrate their code from open\-source Flink to the commercial cloud version seamlessly (at zero cost).

BytePlus Flink utilizes the ultimate elasticity of BytePlus VKE/VCI on Kubernetes Serverless, offering out\-of\-the\-box solutions, maintenance\-free operations, and enables customers to focus more on their business.

<span id="fc2a1e1f"></span>
## What is the difference between fully managed and semi\-managed big data products in the cloud?

The semi\-managed cloud product focuses on providing open\-source product hosting services based on cloud elasticity, concentrating on product adaptation and requiring customer resources for product maintenance.

The fully\-managed cloud product is an out\-of\-the\-box, commercially ready, enterprise\-level product with end\-to\-end support from cloud providers, original manufacturer backing, and high SLA guarantees, offering great cost\-effectiveness.
