You can activate the auto\-renewal feature for your resource pool or opt for manual renewal before the expiration of your annual or monthly subscription to avoid business disruptions due to pool expiration.

<span id="b6bb7098"></span>
## **Background Information**

Only resource pools on an annual or monthly basis support renewals. Pay\-as\-you\-go resource pools do not require renewals; you just need to ensure your account balance is sufficient.

BytePlus Flink resource pools with annual or monthly billing support the following two renewal methods:


* **Auto\-renewal**: You can enable the auto\-renewal feature when creating a new annual or monthly resource pool to prevent business impacts due to pool expiration. Once enabled, the system will automatically renew the pool 7 days before expiration each time; please ensure your account balance is sufficient.

* **manual renewal**: You can manually renew the resource pool at any time before it expires.


<span id="09b2a3be"></span>
## **Auto\-renewal**

The current version only supports enabling auto\-renewal when creating a resource pool. If auto\-renewal is not enabled, you can only renew manually thereafter.

<span id="d24312a1"></span>
## Manual renewal

You need to pay attention to the billing cycle of the resource pool and manually renew it before the resource pool expires.


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Flink console](https://console.byteplus.com/flink).

2. Select the target region from the top menu bar.

3. In the left navigation bar, select **Project Management**, then search and click the project block to enter the project.

4. In the left navigation bar of the project, select **Resource Management**, then click the **Resource Pool** tab.

5. On the resource pool list page, filter the target resource pool, then click on the resource pool name.

6. At the bottom of the resource pool details page, in the **payment information** area, click **renew**.


<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/d3a4e90efeb1449e98c7c3740896732c~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>


7. On the **renewal** page, confirm the renewal resource information, select the **renewal duration**, then click **proceed to payment**.


<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/2433c6e5ff7c4f1891bcc40fbb6d7959~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>


8. On the payment page, review the order cost details. You can opt to use vouchers to offset the order costs and then click **next step**.
