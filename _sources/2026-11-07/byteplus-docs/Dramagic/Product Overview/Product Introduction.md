# About Dramagic

**An enterprise\-level AIGC short drama one\-stop creation platform that enables teams to produce higher\-quality content in less time.** 


* **Intelligent Full\-Process Production**

   The platform leverages innovative multi\-agent collaborative technology to deeply analyze long\-form scripts, providing users with an end\-to\-end intelligent solution for “**Script Analysis \-\> Full Drama Asset Setting \-\> Storyboard Video Generation \-\> Final Preview**”. At the same time, every core node in the full process grants users editing permissions, ensuring users have absolute control over the final work.

* **Designed for Enterprise Teams**

   The platform natively supports enterprise\-level collaboration, with built\-in project management and refined access control mechanisms; it also supports flexible allocation of point quotas by team or member. It is also equipped with a dedicated team asset library to ensure the accumulation and reuse of digital assets. This allows short drama teams to eliminate fragmented toolchains and achieve industrial\-scale production and high\-quality asset accumulation.<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/3d3cd6e53cca437fa7f65fe2b52637d5) </span>

# **What Problem Are We Solving — "Efficiency Improvement".** 

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default"><strong>“Seedance All\-in\-one Reference + Industrial\-grade multi\-Agent collaborative architecture” reshapes productivity</strong></div>



* **Low Barrier, One\-Stop Completion**


The platform uses multiple agents to drive the entire creative process, allowing users to complete the full production chain from script to storyboard to finished film without switching between multiple tools. Each key stage supports visual editing, giving creators full control over every output, significantly shortening the production cycle while ensuring quality.


* **High Quality, Film\-Level Videos**


Visual expression is deeply adapted to the Seedance 2.0 model’s comprehensive reference mode. Combined with director\-level agents for intelligent storyboard planning, it precisely controls narrative rhythm, character emotions, and shot expressiveness, delivering video content close to film\-level standards.


* **Stable Visuals, Visual Consistency**


Through global style locking and continuous character status tracking mechanisms, visual coherence is ensured across storyboards and scenes. Agent performs auto validation before generating to ensure content meets standards; if not, it proactively intercepts, effectively preventing Role collapse and scenario inconsistencies.


* **High efficiency, enterprise\-level management**


Support real\-time collaboration among multiple users, eliminating the inefficient process of repeatedly transferring materials. Allocate points based on team members, with clear and controllable usage; Roles, scenarios, and other material assets are automatically deposited into team Spaces, enabling organization\-level reuse and asset accumulation.
