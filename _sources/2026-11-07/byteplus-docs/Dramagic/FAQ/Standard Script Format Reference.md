To ensure the system correctly understands your script, please review this document before uploading to verify your formatting. Items marked with an asterisk (\*) are required; missing them may result in parsing failure.

<span id="dab5f5de"></span>
### Script Front Matter

The beginning of each script file must include the following information:


* **Title**\*: Place the full title enclosed in title marks at the very top of the file. *Example: 《Rebirth: It's Still Her》* 

* **Outline** Briefly summarize the core concept of the story, ideally in 1–3 sentences.

* **Character Bios**: List the main characters and their brief backgrounds. *Example: Jack, 30, Surgeon, calm personality, highly disciplined lifestyle.* 


<span id="89bb5e35"></span>
### Script Body Content

Every scene must include all of the following fields:


* **Episode Number**\*: Place at the beginning of each episode on a separate line. *Example: Episode 1*

* **Scene Number**\*: Use the "Episode\-Scene" format. Every scene must have a unique identifier.

* **Scene Heading (Slugline)** \*: Must include the location, time (Day/Night), and setting (INT./EXT.). All three elements must be present, but the order is flexible. *Example: Riverside Plaza, Day, EXT.* 

* **Characters**\*: List all characters appearing in the scene, separated by commas or enumeration commas (、). *Example: Characters: Jack, Emily, Bystander A*

* **Action/Visuals**\*: Describe the visual framing and character actions. Each paragraph should be on its own line. You may use the △ symbol as a lead\-in (see Section 3).

* **Dialogue**\*: Formatted as `Character Name (Emotion): Dialogue content`. The emotion tag is optional, but the character name and colon are required. *Example: Jack (Happy): I didn't expect to run into you here.* 


<span id="bbf7f033"></span>
### Special Symbols Guide

The platform correctly recognizes the following symbols. Please format them accordingly:


*  **△ (Triangle)** : Used to lead into action descriptions and character expressions. Placed before the corresponding text.

* **OS (Off\-Screen)** : Character's inner monologue or voice coming from off\-screen.

* **VO (Voice\-Over)** : Voiceover narration. Only the voice is heard; the character does not appear in the frame.

*  **【Flashback】/【Flash Forward】** : Used to mark the beginning and end of memories or dream sequences. Must be used in pairs.

### Formatting Examples

**Example 1:** 

Episode 1

1\-1 Day EXT. Riverside Plaza

Characters: Jack, Emily

△ Jack and Emily sit together on a bench in the riverside plaza. Jack hands Emily a milk tea.

Jack (Happy): I didn't expect to run into you here.

Emily (Relaxed): I come here often. The breeze by the river is really nice.

**Example 2:** 

Episode 1: Crisis on Camera

Scene 1\-1 Hospital Office/Corridor Day INT.

Carter, wearing a white coat, stares intently at the computer screen. The background noise of a busy hospital office surrounds him.

VO: Dr. Carter, the patient in bed 3 has a slight fever post\-op.

Carter (Calm): Apply physical cooling and observe for two hours. I'll be right there.

Cut to: Fast\-cut montage. Dr. Carter doing ward rounds, consulting patients, working out after work, reading at home, and sleeping. A highly disciplined and monotonous life.

Carter (Flat): (Monologue/OS) If time could turn back, I would never believe I'd get involved in something like that. Back then, I was just an ordinary person among the masses. Others might find it boring, but I was very content.
