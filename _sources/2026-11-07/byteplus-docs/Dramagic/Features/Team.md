# Account Levels and Access

As an enterprise\-level platform, Volcano Short Drama Creation allows you to custom create teams, enabling multiple sub\-accounts to collaborate to complete short drama projects. For easier management, you can assign different access permissions to accounts of different levels. The specific access permissions for each level under team management are as follows:

# Main Account (Super Admin Access)

The main account (super admin access) has the highest access permissions and can view and edit all global projects, assets, and teams. On the team management page, there are two subpages: sub\-account management and team management.

   2. If you want to cancel the authorized account login access for an already authorized sub\-account, click **Cancel ** on the right side of the authorized sub\-account and confirm  **.**

## Points management

In the team management page, use **Points management** on the right side of each team to allocate points to the team; via **Member management**, you can set fixed points or daily consumption quotas for members within the team.

Points management has two states:


   * No points limit: Indicates that this team has no points limit, meaning the team consumes the main Account's allocatable balance. The default for newly created teams is no points limit.

   * Allocated fixed team points: Indicates that this team is allocated a fixed amount of points, meaning the team consumes team points with a fixed quota limit.


The main Account can view the total points at the top of the team management page, including:


* Main Account total points: The total points available under the main Account.

* Points allocated to teams: The total points allocated by the main Account to each team.

* Allocatable balance: The remaining points under the main Account that can be allocated to teams (Allocatable balance = Main Account total points \- Points allocated to teams).

* Unlimited Points Teams: The number of teams without points restrictions (Teams not yet allocated points are by default in an unlimited status, meaning these teams consume from the Account's distributable balance points pool).


<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/0f2cf2ed992c456dbc99d8c1332344ff) </span>

&nbsp;

Team Points Allocation: Click **Points Management** on the right side of the team to allocate team points, recover points, or set to unlimited.


* Points Allocation: The Account can allocate a specified number of points from the distributable balance points pool to a team. The maximum allocation cannot exceed the available balance in the distributable points pool.

* Points Recovery: The Account can recover team points back to the distributable balance points pool. The maximum recovery cannot exceed the team's remaining points.

* Unlimited: Indicates that this team has no points restriction, meaning it consumes from the Account's distributable balance points pool. If a team changes from having team points status to unlimited, All remaining points for the team will be recovered to the distributable balance points pool.


<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/8cad9b4d091f49e3b50443e2b3ea993a) </span>

&nbsp;

Member Points Allocation: The Account or team Admin can click **Member management** on the right side of the team, then select the **Points Quota Management** icon next to each Members to set daily points quota, fixed points allocation, or set to unlimited.

&nbsp;

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/0491a59b98ec405696e60f1eca80bb13) </span>


&nbsp;

* Points transfer: The Account or team Admin can allocate a specified number of points to Members. The maximum allocation cannot exceed the team's distributable balance points pool or the Account's distributable points balance. (Note: These points are not directly deducted from the points pool; this only limits the maximum points consumption for the Members.)

* Daily quota: The Account or team Admin can set a daily limit for Members, which is the maximum points consumption per day. After setting, the Members' daily consumption will not exceed this quota.

   * The maximum value for the daily quota cannot exceed the team's remaining points.

   * The quota automatically resets at midnight (UTC+8 00:00) each day and does not accumulate.

   * The daily quota is a consumption rate limit and will not be deducted in advance from the team's points.

* Unlimited: Indicates that this member has no daily points quota limit, meaning this member consumes from the team's remaining points pool (the team Admin is set to unlimited by default and cannot have a quota set).


<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/7a4cf0dc236e47f2994b578e9ab19dbd) </span>

## Points management

Click the **Member management** on the right side of the team, then select the **points quota management** icon next to each member to set the member's daily points quota, fixed points allocation, or adjust to unlimited.

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/cb6de9b2ea8a4c729da728fb87b55a4d) </span>


&nbsp;

* Points transfer: The main Account or team Admin can allocate a specified number of points to members. The allocation limit cannot exceed the team's available points pool or the main account's available points balance. (Note: These points are not deducted from the points pool in advance; this only limits the maximum points consumption for this member.)

* Daily quota: The main Account or team Admin can set a daily quota for members, which is the maximum points consumption per day. After setting, the member's daily consumption will not exceed this quota.

   * The daily quota value cannot exceed the team's remaining points.

   * The daily quota is automatically reset at midnight (UTC+8 00:00) and does not accumulate.

   * The daily quota is a consumption speed limit and will not be deducted in advance from the team's points.

* Unlimited: Indicates that this member has no daily points quota limit (team Admins are Unlimited by default and cannot set quotas).

   * If the team this member belongs to is also Unlimited, then this member consumes from the main Account's distributable balance points pool.

   * If the team this member belongs to has been allocated a fixed amount of team points, then this member consumes from the team's remaining points pool.


<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/5760eeb6eab5478c89ee924c140b13bc) </span>
