According to legal and regulatory requirements, the Dramagic ("Platform") provides AI Generated synthesis services. If such services may cause public confusion or misidentification, the Platform must add an explicit label (i.e., an "AI Generated" visible watermark) to AI Generated synthesized content to notify the public.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">Notice</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">The <strong>AI Generated Watermark Settings</strong> and its modifications are only visible on the main account page. By default, new users have the watermark added.</div>


However, considering the actual scenarios of short drama creation, users may apply to the Platform to provide AI Generated synthesized content **without explicit labeling**. The specific operation steps are as follows:


1. In the lower section of the left sidebar, locate **Watermark** and click.


<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/8ffb02b4846144d8ab76264986258760) </span>

&nbsp;


&nbsp;

2. Enter the **Watermark** page and carefully read the terms on this page.



&nbsp;

3. After opening the **Remove Watermark** switch, the user confirms full understanding of the situation described on this page and agrees that AI Generated synthesized content created or generated by accounts under the main account using the Volcano Drama Creation Platform will no longer have the “AI Generated” visible watermark added. Once the page prompt **Setup Successful** appears, the setting is successful.


<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/b64c97f750e9403e8b2043ccee17415c) </span>


&nbsp;

4. If the user wishes to cancel the removal of the watermark, please repeat the above steps to cancel the removal. After the setting is successful, AI Generated synthesized content (images and videos) created or generated by accounts under the main account using the Dramagic Platform will have the “AI Generated” visible watermark added.
