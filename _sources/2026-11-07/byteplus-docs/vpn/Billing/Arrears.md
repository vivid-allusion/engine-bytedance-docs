Account in arrears will affect the normal use of services. This article describes how the status of Pay\-as\-you\-go resources of Virtual Private Network (VPN) Product changes when the account to which they belong is in arrears. [View Available Balance](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/6269/100429#%E6%9D%BF%E5%9D%97%E8%AF%B4%E6%98%8E).

<span id=".5qyg6LS55qCH5YeG"></span>
## Rules

When your account is deemed overdue by the platform, you will receive SMS or email reminders for insufficient balance. To view the overdue details, see [Bill management](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-bill-details).

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">You can set message subscription and notification methods. If your account is deemed overdue by the platform, it will send reminders based on your settings. For procedure, see <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/message_center/Message-Subscription-and-Notification-Methods">Message Subscription and Notification Methods</a>.</div>


<span id=".5qyg6LS56K-05piO"></span>
## Arrears description

When your account is in arrears, all Pay\-as\-you\-go resources under the account are affected. Their status changes depending on the duration of arrears.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">Regardless of whether your instances are suspended or released, you shall settle the issued bills based on actual usage.</div>
