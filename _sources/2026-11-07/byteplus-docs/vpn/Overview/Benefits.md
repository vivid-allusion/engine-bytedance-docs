## High security

Data transmission is encrypted based on the IKE and IPsec protocols for highly secure communication.

<span id=".5L2O5oiQ5pys"></span>
## Low costs

VPN Connect creates a connection channel based on the Internet, resulting in lower costs compared to Direct Connect.

<span id=".5piT6YWN572u"></span>
## Easy configuration

Using VPN Connect requires minimal actions. Simply perform basic configuration in your on\-premises data center to establish communication tunnels and achieve connected communication.

<span id=".5Y2z5byA5Y2z55So"></span>
## Ready to use

VPN Connect are often temporary. They are ready for use once they are established.
