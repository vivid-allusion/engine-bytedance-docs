This topic describes how to view the usage of VPN connection resources and how to increase the corresponding quotas. For more quota information, see [Limits](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/vpn/Limits).

<span id=".5pON5L2c6ZmQ5Yi2"></span>
## Instructions


* To increase the maximum number of Local/Peer CIDR blocks for a single IPsec\-VPN connection, contact customer service support.

* The number of IPsec\-VPN connections that you can create by using the same VPN gateway and the same Remote ID cannot be increased.


<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the Quota Center.

2. On the product list page, click the **VPN attachment** card to enter the VPN connection quota details page. On this page, you can see the usage of VPN connection resources in each region under the current account.

3. To adjust a quota, click the **apply** button in the **Operations** column for the corresponding configuration item.

4. In the Quota Application page that appears on the right, adjust the relevant quota.

   * **Application target**: Confirm that the configuration item and region for quota increase are correct.

   * **Apply quota**: Enter or drag to adjust the quantity of quota to apply for. The applied quota quantity must be greater than the currently used resource quota quantity.

   * **Application reason**: Provide detailed reasons for the quota application, related usage scenarios, and the necessity of the quota increase.

5. Click **Submit** to complete the application. After receiving the application, the platform will fully evaluate the reasons you filled in and prioritize the approval process.
