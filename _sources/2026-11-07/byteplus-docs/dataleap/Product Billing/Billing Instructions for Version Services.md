DataLeap offers a professional version of its services. You can choose this version to purchase based on your business scenario. This article introduces recommended application scenarios for the professional version of DataLeap.


|Version |Description of recommended application scenarios |
|---|---|
|Professional version |Lightweight data warehouse construction solution:<br><br>Provide a lightweight data warehouse solution for enterprises with lower business complexity. ## **2 Billing for products, solutions, and services**

DataLeap provides service offerings, including the **Professional version**, based on actual business scenarios. The features include Data Integration, Data Development, intelligent monitoring, Data Catalog, Data Quality, Data Security, and so on. When each service runs a task, it needs to use a Scheduling Resource Group, a Computing Resource Group, or an Integrated Resource Group. The billing model for the Product Solution service is a prepaid model based on annual or monthly subscriptions. If you need to use the DataLeap service in multiple regions, then when you enable a new region, you are required to purchase the DataLeap product service for that region again.

### **2.2 Renewal fee description**

You can choose to renew your product service before it expires. If you do not renew your subscription before the expiration date, the features of the corresponding version will be disabled after expiration. The use of functions is not affected for purchased product or service versions that have not yet expired.

<span id="d0869d9e"></span>
### **2.3 Expiration shutdown description**

BytePlus will send expiration notifications to the mobile phone, email, or other contact methods bound to your BytePlus main account on the 7th, 3rd, and 1st day before the product or service you purchased expires, respectively. If you do not renew before the expiration date, the functionality of the product version that was not renewed will be immediately stopped at 12:00 on the day after the expiration shutdown date. After logging in to DataLeap, you can purchase again through the Console page to enable the product service for the corresponding version.

<span id="f5df0c83"></span>
### 2.4 Description of expiration recovery

Within 30 days after service expiration and shutdown, if the corresponding product service，such as the Professional Version solution，has not been repurchased, the associated product service resources will be reclaimed and released. Any projects, tasks, and other items included in the service will no longer be retained.

<span id="7e2ec04e"></span>
### 2.5 Unsubscribe instructions

The Professional Version subscription solution (annual or monthly package) supports unconditional refund within seven days of purchase. By default, an account can unsubscribe from only 3 Dataleap instances in each calendar year (January 1 to December 31). For detailed unsubscribe rules and procedures, see [unsubscription-management](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-unsubscription-management).

## 3 Taxes, fees, and invoices

Unless otherwise specified, all product or service pricing on the BytePlus platform not includes value\-added tax(VAT). If, during the service provision process or the product usage period, there is an adjustment to national tax policy resulting in a change to the value\-added tax rate, the applicable value\-added tax rate shall be adjusted in accordance with the national tax policy. The price excluding tax will remain unchanged.

We support issuing value\-added tax (VAT) invoices to you for an amount equal to the amounts of orders that have been successfully purchased and/or billed amounts in your BytePlus account. Invoicing is not supported for unconsumed prepaid amounts. You can apply for a VAT invoice through the **BytePlus Console \> Billing Center \> Invoice Management**, or through other channels that BytePlus may designate from time to time and promptly notify you of. We will issue a VAT invoice to you promptly after receiving your application. If the service rules applicable to the service you use are inconsistent with this agreement, or if both parties have otherwise agreed, the specific service rules or the separately agreed terms and conditions shall apply. For scheduled settlement services and specific usage amounts, the company also reserves the right to require the user to provide proof of creditworthiness, prepayment, a deposit, or other conditions for activation and subscription that the company deems necessary.

For more details, see [Managing invoices](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-managing-invoices).
