## 2 Data Asset Management Platform

In industries such as finance and manufacturing undergoing digital transformation and upgrading, internal data management generally exhibits the following phenomena: data is dispersed and isolated, lacks unified standards, has poor data quality, and lacks a unified data view. These problems result in low data usage efficiency, high costs for enterprises, inability to achieve data\-driven business, and lack the insights into real users/customers/business to create more value.

Through the construction of a data asset management platform, data in the big data platform can be reorganized by establishing standards, connecting upper and lower levels, and synchronizing data to form a visible and usable data asset map, providing a good data foundation for upper\-layer business application scenarios. For example, a data tagging platform can be built, leveraging multi\-dimensional data to construct data tags, and utilizing technical tools such as tag selection, data Profiling, AI drilling down, to aid various business segments like production innovation, testing, marketing, operations, after\-sales, and recycling, truly unleashing the value of data\-driven business.

## 4 Four major data sharing platforms

All levels of government are currently pursuing informatization and building a "digital government", treating data as a basic strategic resource and establishing resource service platforms. Generally, various commissions and offices under the district and county have their informatization systems, but most of them operate independently, resulting in isolated platform constructions with a lack of capabilities for interconnected coordinative platforms.

City incident information, as well as population, economic, industrial, and other information, together with the basic information and business data from various commissions and offices, can be uniformly applied to form a departmental data resource repository, build a unified data management channel, and achieve inter\-regional, inter\-departmental, inter\-level, and inter\-business data recognition and sharing. This completely breaks down information silos and data barriers, achieving vertical integration, horizontal coordination, sector\-block integration, and data connectivity, forming a unified urban operation management data resource system that supports city operation management, mutual cooperation, and leadership decision\-making, providing fundamental support for breaking down "information silos" and "data chimneys," deepening the "delegation\-oversight\-service" reform, and advancing "Internet + government services."

DataLeap can help customers such as governments and financial institutions, who regard data as a fundamental strategic resource, to build a shared platform for information resources. DataLeap can provide convenient data service capabilities, a unified information resource catalog system that meets industry standards, full lifecycle process management of shared resources, and approval processes for resource applications, offering high\-performance and highly available data sharing services for businesses or individuals.

## 5 Log Big Data Analytics

With the rapid development of informatization, how to analyze and utilize massive data has become a major challenge for current companies. Logs serve as the carrier of data and contain a wealth of information. Traditional log analysis methods are inefficient and rigid, unable to cope with the large volume and non\-uniform format of data as well as its rapid growth. These methods also fail to meet the demands for real\-time processing and quick response.

Log Big Data Analytics is aimed at fine\-grained corporate operations, aggregating business data, completing complex analyses based on the powerful capabilities of DataLeap, quickly responding to new demands, and providing effective personalized recommendations for users.
