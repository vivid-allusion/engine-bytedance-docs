Big Data R & D Governance Suite (DataLeap) is a one\-stop big data mid\-platform solution developed by BytePlus, integrating Real\-time & Batch Data integration, Data Development, Intelligent Operations, Data Governance, and Asset Management capabilities.

DataLeap is committed to solving problems such as data sharing, data governance, and asset management in multi\-business scenarios and multi\-business systems for enterprises. At the same time, it also integrates the internal distributed data governance methods and practices of ByteDance into the product tool around the whole life cycle management of enterprise data, helping enterprises accelerate the construction of data mid\-platform and Lakehouse platform, raise data research and development efficiency, reduce operations management costs, mine data value, and provide data support for enterprise decision\-making and digital transformation.

DataLeap Supported a variety of compute and storage engine services, including BytePlus E\-MapReduce (EMR), BytePlus cloud\-native data warehouse ByteHouse, Lakehouse analysis service (LAS), BytePlus Stream Computing Flink Edition, and more, to help you reduce adaptation costs and Raise the development experience.

## Product Architecture

Based on storage computing engines such as EMR, Serverless Flink, ByteHouse, and LAS, DataLeap provides three\-level capabilities of data asset mapping, global data governance, and full\-link research and development.
