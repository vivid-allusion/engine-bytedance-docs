The overview page manages DataLeap service information activated in the selected region of the current tenant and projects the current user has joined. It also provides quick entry points to create new projects and access products such as Data Integration, Data Development, Data Catalog, Data quality, and Data Security, and Data Governance. This helps you quickly understand the current tenant’s DataLeap service status, efficiently manage joined projects, and conveniently use other product features. This document explains how to activate and manage DataLeap services.

<span id="c00ed67a"></span>
## Constraints

Only the tenant's main account or sub\-accounts with **DataLeapFullAccess** permission can activate services and renew.

<span id="_1-dataleap-服务信息"></span>
## DataLeap Service Information

The overview page’s top section displays the DataLeap service information activated by the tenant in the current region. It also supports service renewal and activation of other services.

<span id="_1-1-开通服务"></span>
### Launch service

Follow these steps to launch DataLeap service:


1. Log in to the [DataLeap console](https://console.byteplus.com/dataleap/console/region:dataleap-console+ap-southeast-1/overview).

2. Select the region for service activation from the dropdown menu on the left side of the top navigation bar.

   * If the region is not activated, you will enter the **DataLeap service activation** page.

   * If the region is activated, overview information is displayed. You can click the **Purchase more services** button at the top right of the page to enter the **DataLeap service purchase** page.

      <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/cc6c3a168dad4f4d9c6dfd132d53e731~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

3. Set the purchase information.

   The following table explains the parameters related to service purchase. Parameters marked with \* are required; those without \* are optional.

   
   |Parameters |Description |
   |---|---|
   |**Basic information** ||
   |\*Region |The region to activate the service, defaulting to the currently selected region. You can select other regions from the dropdown or use the region dropdown in the top navigation bar. Currently supported region is **Asia Pacific (Johor)** .<br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div><br><br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Isolation of intranets in different regions. It is recommended to choose a region closer to your business to reduce network latency and improve access speed.</div><br> |
   |**Solution** ||
   |\*Basic services |Basic services that tenants can activate.<br><br>The capabilities of each service are detailed below:<br><br><br>* **Big Data Analytics**: standard edition covering core big data development features.<br><br>   * Fully compatible with multiple engine types<br><br>   * Covers both real\-time and offline scenarios<br><br>   * Query analysis and stream\-batch task operations<br><br>   * Data quality & data assets & security management<br><br>   * AI intelligent data discovery + development + operations<br><br>* **Enterprise Customization**: customized version tailored to business characteristics.<br><br>   * Integrate specific business scenarios and features<br><br>   * Best practices validated by 100+ business lines<br><br>   * Experience in full lifecycle data governance<br><br>   * Enterprise Data Governance Consulting Service<br><br>   * AI Intelligent Data Finding + Development + Operation & Maintenance |
   |**Payment information** ||
   |\*Purchase duration |Duration for purchase, supporting monthly or yearly options, including 14 selectable choices: 1–11 months, 1 year, 2 years, and 3 years.<br><br>Supports automatic renewal. If the **automatic renewal upon expiration** switch is enabled, the version will automatically renew monthly after expiration. |
   

4. Click to read the **DataLeap Terms of Service**, **DataSail Terms of Service**, and other terms. After no objection, check the option **I have read and agree** to the corresponding terms.

5. Click the **Submit** button to enter the payment page, then follow the instructions to complete the payment and activate the selected service.


<span id="_1-2-已开通服务续费"></span>
### Renewing Activated Services

If the activated service does not have automatic renewal selected, you can manually renew or enable automatic renewal by following these steps:


1. Log in to the DataLeap Console and directly enter the **Overview** page.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/78e14ef9bf754ab8a3f5488b68f37b54~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

2. Click the **Detail** button for a service. In the pop\-up side panel, you can view the service details.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/3e63ed1d3a5f4efc9b2cf03fcfad084d~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

3. You can choose the renewal method as needed:

   * Click the **Automatic renewal** **On/off** button. After confirming, you can enable automatic renewal for the service.

      <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="2">tip      </div>
      

      <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="2">With automatic renewal enabled, no manual action is needed; the system automatically charges the next cycle fee according to the current billing cycle (1 month/time) after the expiration date, preventing service interruption.      </div>
      

   * Click the **Manual renewal** button to enter the **DataLeap service renewal** page. In the **purchase duration** dropdown, select the duration to buy. The page will display the unit price, total price, selected service, and expiration date.

      <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/777ac03539764d5ca4566d097c0b06a1~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

4. Click to read the **DataLeap Terms of Service** and **DataSail Terms of Service**. If you agree, check the **I have read and agree** option for the corresponding terms.

5. Click the **Submit** button to enter the payment page, then follow the instructions to complete the payment.


<span id="_2-我加入的项目"></span>
## Projects I Joined

The overview page center displays the projects you have joined by default. You can directly view brief creation information of the projects and perform management actions as follows:


1. Log in to the DataLeap console and go directly to the **Overview** page.

2. From the **Account All Resources** dropdown in the top left corner, select a BytePlus Project under the current tenant account.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/372abc724e604540b055e876103623d9~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">If not selected, all DataLeap projects under the current tenant's BytePlus project resources will be shown.   </div>
   

3. You can view DataLeap project resources under the selected BytePlus Project and perform the following actions:

   * Click the **Create Project** button to enter the **Create Project** page for quick project creation. Created projects display in the **My Joined Projects** info panel.

   * Click a project name to enter the project console.

      * If you are the admin of the project, you can modify its specific configuration, manage members, and review configurations. For related configuration operations, refer to the description of [Project management](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/dataleap/project_management) each functional module.

      * If you are another member of the project, you can only view the specific configuration information of the current project.

   * Hover over a project and click the buttons below to perform the corresponding actions:

      * Click the **Config** button to go to the **Configuration** page of that project.

      * Click the **IDE** button to go to the **Data development** page of that project.

      * Click the **Maintenance** button to go to the **Task operations** page of that project.

## Quick Entries

The overview page top also provides quick entry points to DataLeap products. Select **DataLeap ≡ \> a product name** from the left sidebar or click a product name in the **quick entry** bar to jump quickly to the corresponding function interface.
