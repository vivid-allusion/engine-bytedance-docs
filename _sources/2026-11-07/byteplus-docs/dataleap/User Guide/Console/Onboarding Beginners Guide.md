Onboarding shows the operational guidance of DataLeap when binding different engines, including DataLeap on EMR Serverless Spark, DataLeap & ByteHouse and other combinations. It helps users quickly understand the full set of data middleground construction including data integration, development, operation, asset, and security.

<span id="bf22b4aa"></span>
## 1 DataLeap on EMR Serverless Spark Use Guide

DataLeap on EMR Serverless Spark's usage process mainly includes **Prerequisite \> Metadata Directory Configuration \> Catalog \> DataSail \> Studio \> Security**. You can click on specific action guides in each module on the page to quickly jump to the respective pages and execute related actions.


1. Log in to the [DataLeap console](https://console.byteplus.com/dataleap/console/region:dataleap-console+ap-southeast-1/overview).

2. Click **Onboarding** on the left sidebar to enter the **DataLeap on EMR Serverless Spark** page.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/9c9552a948474686aa13da62eff492aa~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

3. You can view the operational procedure guides for this engine combination and click on the specific step\-by\-step action guides to quickly jump to corresponding pages and execute related actions.


<span id="1168302d"></span>
## 2 DataLeap & ByteHouse Usage Guide

DataLeap & ByteHouse's usage process mainly includes **Prerequisite \> CDC realtime db sync \> Asset portal construction \> Digital warehouse development and construction \> Security**. You can click on specific action guides in each module on the page to quickly jump to the respective pages and execute related actions.


1. Log in to the DataLeap console.

2. Click Onboarding on the left navigation bar to enter the Onboarding page.

3. Click the **DataLeap & ByteHouse** tab to access the **DataLeap & ByteHouse** page.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/2ef65b60dea94f568fbc0de780329d7f~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

4. View the operational procedures for this engine combination and click on specific step\-by\-step guides to quickly jump to the respective pages and perform related actions.
