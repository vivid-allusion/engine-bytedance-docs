With this feature, you can view and manage all assets in your Favorites.

<span id="ee9b4e94"></span>
## 1. Prerequisite

Assets have been added to favorites. For related actions, please refer to [Retrieve Data](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/dataleap/Search_Data).

<span id="550bda64"></span>
## Step 2: Operation steps


1. Log in to the DataLeap Console.

2. Choose **Overview \> Data Catalog \> More \> User Center \> My Favorites** to enter the **My Favorites** page.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/cef615d4be2a4027ba7c95a959dc5c59~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

3. You can view all assets in your Favorites and execute the following actions:

   * Search data

      In the search bar, enter name keywords, click the search icon, and search your favorited assets.

   * View asset details

      Click the **Name** of an information item to enter the asset details page and view its details.

   * Remove from favorites

      * Click the **Remove from favorites** button in the **Action** column of a data entry. In the pop\-up secondary confirmation dialog box, click the **OK** button to remove the asset from Favorites.

      * Select one or more data items, click the **Batch remove from favorites** button, and in the secondary confirmation dialog that appears, click the **OK** button to batch remove from favorites.
