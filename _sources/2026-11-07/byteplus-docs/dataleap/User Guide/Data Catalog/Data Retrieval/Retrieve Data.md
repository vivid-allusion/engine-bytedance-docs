On the Search home page, you can view recently viewed databases or tables, data assets connected to the platform, business line recommendations, asset album recommendations, shortcuts to related functions, and other information. You can also enter search keywords to search for data. After retrieving the required data, you can view the data details of the table and manage the table information.

<span id="e1a10bd9"></span>
## **Prerequisites**

Before searching, it is required to create a collector on the **Catalog \> Metadata Ingestion** page to ingest the cluster's metadata into the Data Catalog. For related Operating Instructions, see [Metadata Ingestion](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/dataleap/Metadata_Ingestion).

<span id="911bc12d"></span>
## Retrieve all data


1. Log in to DataLeap Console.

2. Choose **Overview \> Catalog \> Search** to go to the **Search** page.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/1f1176ffc4184faeae26d6b463dca9cc~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

3. Quickly view information such as recently viewed DBs or tables, the types and quantities of data assets connected to the platform, business line recommendations, asset album recommendations, shortcuts to related functions, and other related information. You can also perform the following actions:

   * Click the data name to go to the corresponding data page.

   * Click the favorites icon for a data item under the **Recent**, **business line recommendation**, **Asset album recommendation**, or other regions to add the data to favorites or remove it from favorites.

4. You can also fill in search keywords to perform a comprehensive data search:

   1. In the page search box, or in the search box that appears when you hover the mouse over the search icon on the right side of the top\-level menu bar, enter the keyword for the data you want to search. Separate multiple keywords with blank spaces.

      <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="2">tip      </div>
      

      * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="2">When you click the page search box, the search history dropdown list will appear, so you can quickly search as needed.      </div>
      

      * <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="2">After entering a keyword, the drop\-down list will display search\-recommended table names. You can click a table name to go directly to its details page.      </div>
      

   2. Press the Enter key or click the **Search** icon button to go to the search results page, where you can browse a list of metadata for LAS, EMR, ByteHouse CDW, and others that match the conditions.

      <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/c34b5cec17c448bea52e8759f0a2300f~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

   3. You can also find items by using the following filtering actions.

      * In the left sidebar, use filtering actions to combine and locate items by data type, DB, owner, life status, business line, project, theme, tag, and more.

      * Click **List mode** in the upper right corner of the page. In the dropdown list that appears, you can switch the display mode to **Excel mode**.

         * **List mode**: Displays metadata information after a fuzzy search.

         * **Excel mode**: Perform filtering and precise searches within the table, as well as actions such as hiding columns, copying, and downloading.

            * Click the **Hide Column** button at the top of the table to hide or show a column.

            * Select a cell and click the **Copy this row** button to copy the data of the row containing the selected cell.

            * Select a cell and click the **Copy Column** button to copy the data in the column containing the selected cell.

            * Select a cell and click the **Copy Selected** button to copy the cell data.

            * Click the **Download \> File Format** button at the top of the table to download the current page's data to your local computer in xls, csv, or xlsx format.

## Search my favorites


1. Log in to the DataLeap Console.

2. Choose **Overview \> Catalog \> Search**, and go to the **Search** page.

3. Access the **My Favorites** page using any one of the following methods.

   * Click **My Favorites** to the right of the **Catalog** search box.

   * After searching for data, on the search results page, click **My Favorites**.

4. You can view and manage all your favorite data. For specific operations, see [My Favorites](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/dataleap/69460).
