Use the business line configuration function to edit the basic info and custom settings of the business line as needed.

<span id="ec58f8e7"></span>
## Constraints

Only the business line admin has configuration permission for the business line.

<span id="860cfc4b"></span>
## Prerequisites

A business line has been created.

<span id="2730773e"></span>
## Operation Steps


1. Log in to the DataLeap console.

2. Choose **Overview \> Catalog \> Management Center \> BusinessLine Management** to enter the **BusinessLine Management** page.

3. Click the **Configuration** button in the **Action** column of an item to enter the **BusinessLine configuration** page.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/e490a6423a864ee7bfd84180283955af~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

4. You can view the configuration information of the business line and click the **Edit** button to modify the information.

   For related parameters of the business line configuration, please refer to [Create Business Line](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/dataleap/Create_Business_Line).
