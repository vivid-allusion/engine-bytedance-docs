This feature allows you to create and manage business domains, themes, product lines, and album information. After creation, you can assign the related business domain, theme, product line, or album as needed when creating tables to facilitate user queries and management.

<span id="_1-约束限制"></span>
## **Constraints**

Users can only edit or delete configuration information they created.

<span id="_2-业务域管理"></span>
## **Business Domain Management**

Business domains are divided based on the enterprise's business scenarios and business rules, allowing for the categorization and management of various services within the same business area, such as education, payment, and supply chain domains. Steps to manage business domains are as follows:


1. Log in to the DataLeap console.

2. Choose **Overview \> Catalog \> Management Center \> Config Management \> Business Domain Management** to enter the business domain management page.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/b3e6c76841cf4a81b8772461bb7410d7~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

3. The following operations are available:

   * Click the **New Business Domain** button, fill in the name and description of the business domain, then click the **OK** button to create the business domain.

   * Click the **Edit** button in the **Action** column of a business domain entry to modify its information.

   * Click the **Delete** button in the **Action** column of a business domain entry. In the pop\-up confirm deletion dialog, click the **OK** button to delete the business domain.

   * Enter search information to find business domains that match the conditions.

You can add corresponding business domains, themes, and product lines as second\- and third\-level subdirectories under the Album to display tables of the corresponding business domains.</div>


<span id="_3-主题管理"></span>
## **Theme Management**

Each theme corresponds to a macro analysis domain. Example: "sales analysis" is an analysis field involving objects such as products, suppliers, customers, and warehouses. The data warehouse themes are defined as product theme, supplier theme, customer theme, and warehouse theme, while "sales analysis" can serve as a business domain.

Follow these steps to manage themes:


1. Log in to the DataLeap console.

2. Choose **Overview \> Catalog \> Management Center \> Config Management \> Theme Management** to enter the theme management page.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/74de60c88df441ad81d3c0c148d2d818~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

3. The following operations can be performed:

   * Click the **New Theme** button. After filling in the name and description, click the **OK** button to create the theme.

   * Click the **Edit** button in the **Action** column of a theme to modify its information.

   * Click the **Delete** button in the **Action** column of a theme. In the confirmation dialog that appears, click the **OK** button to delete the theme.

   * Enter search information to find themes that match the conditions.


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Other users canthe theme you created when creating tables, using it together with data albums, business domains, and product lines. You can add corresponding business domains, themes, and product lines as second\- and third\-level subdirectories under the Album to display tables of the corresponding themes.</div>


<span id="_4-产品线管理"></span>
## **Product Line Management**

A product line is a way to classify and access data, usually divided based on product features, technical features, platforms, target markets, or a combination of these dimensions, with product features generally as the primary standard. The steps to manage product lines are as follows:


1. Log in to the DataLeap console.

2. Choose **Overview \> Catalog \> Management Center \> Config Management \> Product Line Management** to enter the product line management page.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/a16cc848fb164b15990d9e8c06804a21~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

3. You can perform the following operations:

   * Click the **New Product Line** button, fill in the product line name and description, then click **OK** to create the product line.

   * Click the **Edit** button in the **Action** column of a product line to modify its information.

   * Click the **Delete** button in the **Action** column of a product line. In the confirmation dialog that appears, click **OK** to delete the product line.

   * Enter search terms to find product lines that match the conditions.
