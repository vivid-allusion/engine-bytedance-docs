Asset Albums introduce data assets exposed through the asset catalog in an organized document format. The album contents include but are not limited to a comprehensive introduction to the supported business, problems solved, production logic, internal relationships, and more, enabling data consumers to better and more conveniently understand how to use the relevant data.

<span id="3179d8f9"></span>
## Background Information

If a business team wants to **structurally organize** all data assets produced within the group and classify them based on the supported business, problems solved, functions implemented, etc., they can use [Asset Catalog](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/dataleap/Asset_Catalog) and **Asset Albums** to form an asset portal for their business. The establishment of an asset portal can effectively unify and organize scattered data; meanwhile, it also facilitates systematic data review for data consumers and improves data search efficiency.

<span id="bf640749"></span>
## Prerequisites

The business line has been created and related album information configured. For specific operations, refer to [Create Business Line](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/dataleap/Create_Business_Line) and subsequent steps.

<span id="666b8fa7"></span>
## Operation Steps


1. Log in to the DataLeap Console.

2. Choose **Overview \> Catalog \> Asset Album** to enter the **Asset Album** page.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/18b4e394843b45e7bf7481d3a3f57c0a~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

3. Use the business line dropdown in the upper right corner to select the business line to view.

4. View the album list under the current business line and search albums as needed:

   * Enter album name keywords in the search box and click the Search icon to see matching search results.

   * Select **My Favorites** to view assets favorited by the current user.

   * Choose a configured business line tag from the Business Line Tags dropdown to view assets under that tag. For configuration operations, see [Label Management](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/dataleap/Tag_Management).

   * Click the data category name in the left directory to view assets under that category.

   * Use the **Sort Dimensions** dropdown to select dimension options in order to view assets sorted by the chosen dimensions.

      The sort dimensions include **Creation time**, **Update time**, **Heat**, and **shelf time**. You can combine them as needed for sorting.

5. Click an album to view the album details.

   * Click the **Business Knowledge** tab to view the album introduction. Refer to [Album Management](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/dataleap/Album_Management) for content introduction editing operations.

   * Click the **Data assets** tab to view the asset list under the album. Refer to for the operation of assigning assets to albums [Asset Maintenance](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/dataleap/Asset_Maintenance).
