Tags classify and identify data assets by defining data attributes and relationships, making data organized and searchable. You can create and manage tags as needed. This article explains how.

<span id="c01b7a88"></span>
## Constraints

**BusinessLine administrators** have full permissions, while **tag group admins** can only edit, delete, or enable/disable tag groups they manage.

<span id="343d2a5f"></span>
## Prerequisites

The business line has been created.

<span id="35f9da9d"></span>
## Operation Steps


1. Log in to the DataLeap console.

2. Select **Overview \> Catalog \> Asset Management \> Basic Management \> Label Management** to enter the **Label Management** page.

3. You can perform the following actions:

   * View tag groups

      Click the **View** button in the **Action** column for an entry to see the detailed configuration of the tag group.

   * Edit tag groups

      Click the **Edit** button in the **Action** column for an entry to modify its configuration information.

   * Apply tag groups

      Click the **Application** button in the **Action** column of an entry to configure settings for that tag group.

   * Turn tag group on/off

      Click the **Enable/Close** toggle button in the **Action** column of an entry to turn the tag group on or off.

      <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="2">tip      </div>

   * Delete tag group

      Click the **Delete** button in the **Action** column of a closed entry and confirm again to delete the tag group.

   * View usage details

      Click the **Usage details** button in the **Action** column of an entry to view detailed usage information of this tag group for related assets.

   * Batch operations

      Select one or more entries and click the corresponding batch operation button to perform batch close, open, delete, and other actions.

## Subsequent Actions

After creating tag groups and their tags, use them as follows. After applying tags to assets, they will display on the specified pages according to the display range of the tags. By tagging assets properly, you can more easily perform asset statistics, integration, analysis, and mining to discover potential opportunities and risks in business.


* Text tag usage: users manually tag assets through the corresponding entry point.

   * Table Management \- Asset creation/management page: asset owners can tag assets in the meta tag information configuration.

   * Asset Management \- asset maintenance page: business line administrators can tag assets through **batch maintenance**.

   * Business line \- asset management list page: business line administrators/asset owners can tag assets through **batch label settings**.

   * Album creation/edit page: album owners can tag albums in the business line tag information configuration.

   * Business line \- album management list page: business line administrators/asset owners can tag assets through **batch label settings**.

* Data rule type tag usage: tags are automatically applied to matching assets through **application** settings by the system.
