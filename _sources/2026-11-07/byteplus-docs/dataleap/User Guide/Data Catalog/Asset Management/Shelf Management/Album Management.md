This feature allows you to create albums and perform editing operations such as activating or deactivating them.

<span id="1cd1c0f9"></span>
## Prerequisites

A business line has been created.

<span id="292aed66"></span>
## Operation Steps


1. Log in to the DataLeap console.

2. Select **Overview \> Catalog \> Asset Management \> Shelf Management \> Albums** to enter the album management page.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/3de9badeb4f74c56bd1240e7cd433620~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

3. The following operations are available:

   * Create a new album

      1. Click the **New album** button to enter the new album creation page.

      2. After setting the related parameters, click the **Next** button to complete album creation and enter the content editing page.

      3. After setting the content information, click the **Save draft** button to save the settings, or click the **Save and publish** button to publish the album directly.

      
      |Parameters |Description |
      |---|---|
      |**Basic information** ||
      |\*Album Type |The type of asset album supports the following options. OnePage is selected by default and can be edited.<br><br><br>* OnePage<br><br>* Report<br><br>* Instruction<br><br>* Standard<br><br>* Design<br><br>* Course<br><br>* Others |
      |\*Creation method |The album creation method, which supports options **Create locally** and **Existing link** **address**. |
      |\*Document link |Fill in the document link information.<br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div><br><br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Set this parameter when <strong>creation method</strong> is <strong>existing link address</strong>.</div><br> |
      |\*Album name |Name the new album as desired.<br><br>Album names of the same album type must be unique; the latest setting applies. |
      |Description |Fill in the album description as needed. |
      |\*Owner |The album owner defaults to the current user; others can be selected from the dropdown. Multiple owners can be set. |
      |**business line related settings** ||
      |\*Business line |Business line of the album. The system automatically fills in the current business line; it cannot be modified. |
      |\*Album Category |Choose the album category to assign to the new album; select from existing album categories in the dropdown.<br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div><br><br><br><div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">The available category options differ based on the current operator's role.</div><br><br><br><br>* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Business line administrators can choose from all existing album categories under the business line.</div><br><br><br>* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Album category administrators can only choose the categories they manage and their subcategories.</div><br> |
      |Business line tags |Select business line tags from the dropdown to assign; multiple tags can be set. |
      |**Content editing** ||
      |Content |Album content; enter as needed. |
      

   * Search album

      Set search criteria to view the list of albums that match the conditions.

   * Offline album

      Click the **Offline** button in the **Action** column of a published item, confirm the action to take the album offline. After going offline, the album will no longer appear under the corresponding category on the **Asset Album** page.

   * Edit album

      Click the **Edit** button in the **Action** column of an item to jump to the asset album editing page and modify the album information.

   * Manage unpublished albums

      * Post Album

         Click the **Post** button in the **Action** column on an item, then confirm to post the album. After posting, the album will appear under the corresponding album category on the **Asset Album** page.

      * Delete Album

         Click the  **...**   **\> Delete** button in the **Action** column, then confirm to delete the album.

      * Tag Settings

         Click the  **...**   **\> Tag Settings** button in the **Action** column to open a window allowing you to add specified tags to the asset album based on existing tags.

   * Batch Tag Settings

      After selecting one or more items, click the **Batch Label Settings** button below the list. In the popup window, you can add specified tags to the selected asset albums based on existing tags.


<span id="ebbe58de"></span>
## Operation Results


* After the album is published, it will display under the corresponding album category on the **Asset Album** page.

* After the album is taken offline, it will no longer display under the corresponding album category on the **Asset Album** page.
