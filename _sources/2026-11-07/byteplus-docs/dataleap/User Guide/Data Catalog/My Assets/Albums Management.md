This feature allows you to perform basic management operations such as posting, taking offline, and deleting created albums. For more actions, refer to [Album Management](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/dataleap/Album_Management).

<span id="c3f7d0b0"></span>
## Prerequisites

The album has been created. For related operations, please see [Album Management](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/dataleap/Album_Management).

<span id="4b49133d"></span>
## Operation Steps


1. Log in to the DataLeap Console.

2. Choose **Overview \> Catalog \> My Assets \> Albums** to enter the **Albums** page.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/960023c8975047e1bd637e7d2c999cf9~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

3. You can view all created albums and perform the following actions:

   * Search albums

      Set search criteria to view the list of albums that match the criteria.

   * View details

      Click the **Details** button in the **Action** column of an entry to view the album details.

   * Manage unpublished albums

      * Post album

         Click the **Post** button in the **Action** column of an entry, then confirm to post the album. After posting, the album will appear under the corresponding album category on the **Asset Album** page.

      * Edit album

         Click the **Edit** button in the **Action** column for an item to edit the album information.

      * Delete album

         Click the  **...**   **\> Delete** button in the **Action** column of an entry; after the second confirmation, the album can be deleted.

   * Take album offline

      Click the **Offline** button in the **Action** column of an entry, then confirm to take the album offline. After going offline, the album will no longer be displayed under the corresponding album category on the **Asset Album** page.


<span id="742c801d"></span>
## Operation Result


* After the album is activated, it will be displayed under the corresponding album category on the **Asset Album** page.

* After the album goes offline, it will no longer appear under the corresponding album category on the **Asset Album** page.
