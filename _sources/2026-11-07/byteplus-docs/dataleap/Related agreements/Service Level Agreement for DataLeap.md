During the term of the [BytePlus Customer Agreement](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/legal/docs/customer-agreement) pursuant to which BytePlus Pte. Ltd.("**BytePlus**") has agreed to provide the **DataLeap Service** (the "**Service**") to you (the "**Agreement**"), the Service will be subject to the service level metrics set out below in this Service Level Agreement (the "**SLA**"). Capitalized terms used in this SLA, but not defined in this SLA, have the meaning stated in the Agreement.

<span id="e682b429"></span>
## **1 Service Levels**

BytePlus will use all commercially reasonable efforts to make the Service available with a Monthly Uptime Percentage no less than 99.9% during any monthly billing cycle (the "**Service Levels**") as calculated and subject to the exclusions outlined below in Section 4.

Monthly Uptime Percentage is calculated at the instance level as follows:

**Monthly Uptime Percentage = ((Total Service Cycle Minutes \- Service Unavailable Minutes) / Total Service Cycle Minutes) \* 100%** 

<span id="b40b0849"></span>
## **2 Service Credits**

Subject to the terms and conditions of the Agreement, and your full compliance of the Agreement, in the event that the above Service Levels are not satisfied, you will be eligible to receive service credits (the "**Service Credits**") as described below.


|Monthly Uptime Percentage ("MUP") |Percentage of monthly bill for the Service which does not meet SLA that will be credited to your future monthly bills |
|---|---|
|99% <= MUP < 99.9% |10% of the monthly service bills |
|MUP < 99% |25% of the monthly service bills |



* We will apply any Service Credits only against future Service payments otherwise due from you. At our discretion, we may exchange Service Credits into Service hours.

* Service Credits will be applicable and issued only if the credit amount for the applicable monthly billing cycle is greater than US one dollar (US$1).

* The aggregate maximum amount of Service Credits you can claim in a single monthly billing cycle shall not exceed 25% of such monthly service fee. Service Credits will not entitle you to any refund or other payment from BytePlus. Service Credits may not be transferred or applied to any other account.


Notwithstanding anything to the contrary hereunder and under the Agreement, you agree and acknowledge that the Service Credits (if eligible) are the sole and exclusive remedy for any unavailability, non\-performance, or other failure by us to provide the Service.

## **3 Service Credit Application** **and Payment Procedures**

In order to apply for the Service Credits, you must notify BytePlus within two months after the end of preceding calendar month where you become eligible to receive the Service Credits, and provide BytePlus with sufficient supporting information and materials evidencing the occurrence of the failure of Service Levels. Such written notification must include:


1. the words "SLA Credit Request" in the subject line;

2. the dates, times, and BytePlus regions of each lack of availability incident that you are claiming;

3. the affected Services.


If the MUP of such a request is confirmed by us and is less than the Service Levels, then we will issue the Service Credits to you within one billing cycle following the month in which your request is confirmed by us.

If you fail to meet the aforesaid requirements, your eligibility to receive the Service Credits may be forfeited. In case of any disputes regarding the Service Credits, BytePlus shall be entitled to make a determination in good faith based on BytePlus' information.

## **4 Exclusions of** **SLA**

If any failure to achieve the Service Levels is due to any of the following reasons, you will not be eligible for the Service Credits:


1. any system maintenance with prior notice by BytePlus to you, including system cutover, maintenance, upgrade and failure simulation test;

2. any features excluded from this SLA;

3. any matters that are out of BytePlus' control or resulting from the software, hardware, network, equipment or resources that are not provided by BytePlus;

4. any loss or leak of data, pin or password due to your improper maintenance or improper security measures;

5. any negligence in authorization or mal\-operation by you;

6. any of your failure to comply with documentation or suggestions made available by BytePlus;

7. any Service unavailability or failure of the Service not attributable to BytePlus, including without limitation, force majeure event, your application and/or a domain is blocked due to any reason.

8. any hacker attacks on your or any user's system, application, and/or website.

9. any circumstance, under the relevant laws, regulations, codes, rules or agreements, where BytePlus could be exempted from any liability.

## **5 Definitions**

The following definitions shall apply to this SLA:


* **Service cycle**: each calendar month is a Service Cycle. The period from the day on which a user starts to use the Service to the very last day of such month will be counted as one Service Cycle. The availability of the Service is calculated independently for each Service Cycle.

* **Total Service Cycle Minutes**: calculated as the total number of days in the service schedule for a single instance \* 24 (hours) \* 60 (minutes).

* **Service unavailable**: If all customer connections to DataLeap on the specified instance fail, and this state lasts for more than one minute, the instance is considered unavailable for that minute.

* **Service unavailable minutes**: Refer to the total number of minutes during a service cycle when the DataLeap service is unavailable.

* **Monthly service fee**: The total amount of DataLeap fees incurred and paid by a single customer account during a service cycle (that is, a natural month). This does not include the portion that has been paid for but not yet consumed. If a customer pays the service fee for multiple months in a single payment, the monthly service fee will be calculated by allocating the total service fee evenly over the number of months purchased.
