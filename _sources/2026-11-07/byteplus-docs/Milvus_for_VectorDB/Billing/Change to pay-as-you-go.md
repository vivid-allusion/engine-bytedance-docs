Milvus for VectorDB provides two billing method: pay\-as\-you\-go and subscription. You can switch between the billing methods. After creating a subscription Dedicated instance, you can change the billing method to pay\-as\-you\-go in the console according to business needs. This article describes how to change the billing type of a Dedicated instance from subscription to pay\-as\-you\-go.

<span id="HGAlWYp2"></span>
## **Prerequisites**


* A Dedicated instance has been created and is in **Running** status. For more information about how to create an instance, see [Create an instance](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/Milvus_for_VectorDB/Create_an_instance).

* The billing method of the instance is **Subscription**. For more information about billing methods, see [Billing methods](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/Milvus_for_VectorDB/Billing_methods).


<span id="jjTmVDOv"></span>
## **Precautions**


* Ensure that your account balance is sufficient when switching from subscription to pay\-as\-you\-go to avoid instance suspension due to arrears.

* After switching from subscription to pay\-as\-you\-go, you can switch back to subscription. For detailed operations, see  [Change to subscription](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/Milvus_for_VectorDB/Change_to_subscription).


<span id="BMSpFv8z"></span>
## **Procedure**


1. Log on to the [Milvus for VectorDB console](https://console.byteplus.com/milvus).

2. In the top navigation bar, select the project and region where you want to create an instance.

3. In the **Instances list**, select the **Dedicated** tab, then click **Change to pay\-as\-you\-go** in the **Actions** column of the target pay\-as\-you\-go instance.

4. On the **Change to pay\-as\-you\-go** page, confirm the instance information, read and check the agreement, and then click **Confirm order**.

5. On the **Confirm order information** page, verify the payment details and click **Next step**.

6. On the **Submit payment information** page, choose a payment method and click **Pay**.
