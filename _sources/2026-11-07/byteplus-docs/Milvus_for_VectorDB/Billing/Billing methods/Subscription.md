Subscription is a billing method where payment is made in advance when creating a new instance. Compared to pay\-as\-you\-go, subscription offers more favorable prices for Milvus for VectorDB instances.

## Overview

The subscription billing method is suitable for scenarios where the business is stable or requires long\-term use of Milvus for VectorDB. You can prepay for several months or years based on the instance usage duration.

## Purchase duration

Milvus for VectorDB subscription instances offer multiple purchase duration options. You can choose the appropriate duration based on your business requirements, including:

* **Monthly purchase**: choose 1 to 9 months.

* **Yearly purchase**: choose 1 to 3 years.

   > **tip**

   > If the purchase duration is 1 year or longer, you have a discount. The specific discount is subject to the actual order page in the console.

## Settlement period

The settlement period for a Milvus for VectorDB subscription instance equals the instance's purchase duration (based on UTC+8 time). A settlement period starts from the resource activation time (accurate to the second) and ends at 23:59:59 on the due date.

## Unsubscribe from an instance

After unsubscribing from (deleting) a Milvus for VectorDB subscription instance, the refund amount is credited to the account balance. For the method to unsubscribe from an instance, see [Delete an instance](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/Milvus_for_VectorDB/Delete_an_instance).

For more information about refund calculation rules, see [Unsubscription](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/6269/81013#.5LqM44CB6Z2e5LiD5aSp5peg55CG55Sx6YCA6K6i6K-05piO).

## Change the configuration

Subscription instances support real\-time configuration changes. After you change the instance specifications, such as sharding number and shard nodes, you can view the new specifications on the **Instance list** page. For methods to change the configuration, see [Modify the instance configuration](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/Milvus_for_VectorDB/Modify_the_instance_configuration).

After you change the configuration of a subscription instance, you may need to refund or pay the price difference. For billing rules on change the configuration of subscription instances, see [Changing Annual or Monthly Subscription Resource Specifications](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/Billing_Rules_for_Resource_Subscription_Change#.5LiA44CB5Y-Y5pu05YyF5bm05YyF5pyI6LWE5rqQ6KeE5qC8).

## Precautions

If your account is overdue, you can still use the purchased Milvus for VectorDB subscription instance. However, before paying off the overdue fees, you cannot purchase or renew instances. For more information, see [Arrears](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/Milvus_for_VectorDB/Arrears).
