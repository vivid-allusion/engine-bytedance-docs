To continuously provide stable and efficient vector search services, Milvus for VectorDB will implement read vCU billing for Serverless instances progressively from July 9, 2026 (UTC+8) to July 13, 2026 (UTC+8). Please check your bill.

<span id="Loder1tc"></span>
## Effective time

Effective progressively from July 9, 2026 (UTC+8) to July 13, 2026 (UTC+8).

<span id="3ZrQ9FIL"></span>
## Effective scope

All Serverless instances.

<span id="d26VCoSA"></span>
## **Billing instruction**

Starting from the effective time, read vCU consumed by Serverless instances will be billed according to product billing rules. Please check your bill. Details are available in [Product billing](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/Milvus_for_VectorDB/Product_billing).

<span id="AaAme8Kx"></span>
## **Precautions**


* Read vCU usage before the effective time is not charged.

* Read vCU usage incurred after the effective time will be billed according to the billing rules. Refer to your bill for the final charges.

* Evaluate your resource costs based on your workload and access patterns.
