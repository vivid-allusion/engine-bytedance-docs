Milvus for VectorDB ended invitational testing on October 22, 2025, at 11:59:59 Beijing time, and started commercial public beta at 12:00:00 on the same day. Milvus for VectorDB looks forward to providing you with a better product experience.

<span id="jZNk80qy"></span>
## Billing instruction


* Starting from 12:00:00 on October 22, 2025, billing will be charged progressively for created and new instances according to the [resource calculator](https://console.byteplus.com/milvus/tool) prices in the Milvus for VectorDB console. Please monitor your bill and costs.

* The billing type for instances created during the invitational testing period is pay\-as\-you\-go. After enabling the commercial public beta, these instances will be billed hourly.

* If you need to use instances long\-term after enabling the commercial public beta, you can change the instance billing type to subscription in the console to save costs.

* For detailed product billing information, see [Product billing](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/Milvus_for_VectorDB/Product_billing).
