This topic provides a detailed explanation of the billing for FaaS, including billing types, overdue payment information, and unsubscription information.

<span id="billing-types"></span>
## Billing Types


|Billing Type |Description |
|---|---|
|[Pay-as-you-go](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/faas/Pay-as-you-go) |"Pay\-as\-you\-go" is a billing method where you use first and pay later. You do not need to pre\-purchase large amounts of resources. You can activate and release resources on demand anytime, paying only for the actual resource usage of the function services. |


<span id="unsubscription"></span>
## Unsubscription


* Deleting a function is equivalent to canceling the service; the function instance will be immediately released/destroyed.

* Resource packages purchased and not used within 7 days are eligible for a no\-questions\-asked refund within 7 days. For more details, refer to [Unsubscribe Description](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/faas/Unsubscribe_Description).


<span id="overdue"></span>
## Overdue

After an overdue payment occurs, your resources enter a grace period during which your resources are retained and can be used normally. To ensure uninterrupted use of Function Service resources, add funds to your account at the earliest opportunity and settle the outstanding bills within the specified time before the resources are forcibly reclaimed. For more details, refer to [Overdue payments](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/faas/Overdue_payments).

<span id="view-bill"></span>
## View Bill

In the [Billing center > Bill management](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/bill/detail) page, view the bills and consumption details related to FaaS expenses to understand the consumption status.
