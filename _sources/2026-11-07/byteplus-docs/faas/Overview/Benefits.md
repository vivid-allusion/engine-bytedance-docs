This topic describes the key advantages of Function Service (FaaS) as an event\-driven, serverless, fully managed compute platform.

<span id="easy-and-efficient"></span>
## Easy and efficient


* **Focus on core business logic**

   Helps users concentrate on developing core business logic while reducing the learning and modification costs related to cloud\-native technologies. Simplifies the development and operational processes.

* **Connect everything**

   Seamlessly integrates cloud\-based middleware and database products, helping users link upstream and downstream systems, enabling fast and efficient construction of complex service architectures.

* **Effortless operations**

   Provides online testing capabilities, rich multidimensional monitoring, and logging features to meet users' needs for visualizing function behavior, simplifying debugging and troubleshooting.


<span id="elastic-and-maintenance-free"></span>
## Elastic and maintenance\-free


* **Fully managed compute service**

   Functions are fully managed, eliminating the need to purchase or manage servers and other infrastructure. Operational challenges are handled by the platform.

* **Automated autoscaling**

   Simply set an upper scaling limit, and the FaaS platform will automatically adjust the resources based on business demand, adapting to usage peaks and valleys with ease.

* **Extreme cold\-start**

   Offers extreme cold\-start times, typically in the millisecond range. In high\-traffic scenarios, the platform can rapidly scale horizontally to handle user requests, ensuring high reliability and availability of the service.


<span id="cost-efficiency"></span>
## Cost Efficiency


* **Reduce development and operational costs**

   By focusing on business logic development without the need to purchase or manage servers, functions automatically scale based on business demand, significantly reducing both development and operational costs.

* **Lower traffic ingress costs**

   Supports multiple trigger event sources, enabling quick construction of complex applications while reducing traffic ingress costs.

* **Optimize resource usage**

   Supports multi\-concurrent requests per instance, improving resource utilization. Additionally, the pay\-per\-request and pay\-per\-time pricing model ensures that resources are only billed when used, further reducing operational costs.


<span id="security-and-high-availability"></span>
## Security and high availability


* **VM\-level isolation**

   The platform provides built\-in support for VM\-level multi\-tenancy and user code isolation, ensuring the security of user code.

* **Multi\-AZ flexible deployment**

   Functions can be deployed across multiple availability zones, allowing the platform to place function instances close to upstream or downstream systems, optimizing performance.

* **Disaster recovery assurance**

   Distributed clusters are deployed across multiple availability zones. If one availability zone faces outages due to disasters or power failures, function instances seamlessly shift to other zones, ensuring business continuity and high availability.
