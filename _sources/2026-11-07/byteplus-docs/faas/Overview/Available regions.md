This topic describes the concept of Regions, how to select a region, and the regions where Function Service (FaaS) is available.

<span id="what-is-a-region"></span>
## What is a Region?

**Region**: A region refers to the physical geographic area where the data center is located. Once resources are created, they cannot be moved to a different region. Each region is fully isolated from others within the internal network, ensuring maximum stability and fault tolerance between regions.

<span id="how-to-choose-a-region"></span>
## How to Choose a Region?

When selecting a region, consider the following factors:


* **Geographic Proximity**: It is generally recommended to choose a region that is geographically closer to you or your target users. This helps reduce network latency and improve access speed.

* **Resource Pricing**: Different regions may have varying resource prices. If you or your target users are located in mainland China, network latency between regions is less of a concern, as infrastructure, BGP network quality, and resource configuration are generally consistent across regions in mainland China.
