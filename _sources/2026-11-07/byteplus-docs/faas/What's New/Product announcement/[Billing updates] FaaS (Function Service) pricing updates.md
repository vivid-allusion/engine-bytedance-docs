A price reduction will take effect on some of the items in Function Service  (FaaS) in the Asia Pacific (Johor) region on **July 15, 2025**. This adjustment mainly covers the prices for function invocations and CPU usage, as well as the use of a new billing unit for function invocations.

<span id="dizFW1gE"></span>
## Details of updates


* **Involved region**: Asia Pacific (Johor)

* **Involved items**:

   * Billing unit:

      
      |Billing item |Original billing unit |New billing unit |Impact |
      |---|---|---|---|
      |request |Bits |Request |None. Unit changed only. |
      

   * Unit price:

      The detailed price changes are shown below. For more information about the billing model, see [Pay-as-you-go](https://www.volcengine.com/docs/faas/Pay-as-you-go):

      
      |Billing item |Original unit price |New unit price |Change impact |
      |---|---|---|---|
      |request |0.0000003 USD/Bits |0.0000002 USD/Request |Price reduction |
      |duration_mem |0.0000067 USD/GiB/Second |0.0000045 USD/GiB/Second ||
      |duration_vcpu |0.0000291 USD/vCPU/Second |0.0000195 USD/vCPU/Second ||
      |duration_mem_reserved |0.0000027 USD/GiB/Second |0.00000225 USD/GiB/Second ||
      |duration_vcpu_reserved |0.0000116 USD/vCPU/Second |0.00000975 USD/vCPU/Second ||
