This article introduces the impact of arrears on Vortex IP. [How to view available balance?](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/6269/100429#%E6%9D%BF%E5%9D%97%E8%AF%B4%E6%98%8E)

Account arrears will cause Pay\-as\-you\-go Vortex IP to be suspended, or even reclaimed. System has set a retention period for resources. Within the retention period, the resources are retained and you can continue using the resources after you top up your account and make the overdue payments. The features of the resources are not affected. After the retention period for arrears expires, Vortex IP resources will be forcibly reclaimed, and the relevant data will not be able to be recovered. Regardless of whether your instance resources are suspended or released, you must pay the generated outstanding bills.

**What happens when your account is in arrears**


* Shutdown time: 2 hours in arrears.

* Release time: 15 days and 2 hours in arrears.
