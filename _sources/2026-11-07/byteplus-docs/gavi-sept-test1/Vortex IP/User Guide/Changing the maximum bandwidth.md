You can change the maximum bandwidth of a Vortex IP at any time based on your business requirements. The change takes effect immediately. This topic describes how to change the maximum bandwidth of a Vortex IP.

<span id=".5pON5L2c6K-05piO"></span>
## Instructions


* Only instances in the **Running** status support modifying the maximum bandwidth.

* When the maximum bandwidth is reduced, business traffic may be affected and packet loss may occur. Please evaluate the possible impacts of the operation on your business and proceed with caution.


<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [Vortex IP console](https://console.byteplus.com/eip/region:eip+ap-southeast-1/vortexips).

2. In the top menu bar, select the region.

3. In the Vortex IP list, click ... \> **Modify bandwidth configuration** on the right of the target Vortex IP.

4. Change the maximum bandwidth as needed.

5. Click **Confirm unsubscription**.
