# 1. Overview of learning paths

Intelligent Data Insight DataWind is a platform for detail\-level, big data\-enhanced analytics. It is designed to address the needs of three distinct business stages—exploration, rapid growth, and mature scale—by providing corresponding DataWind products and services based on different data analysis and data product requirements, enabling one\-stop data analysis and collaboration. It offers advantages such as high performance, big data capabilities, high availability, and real\-time processing, allowing for greater data application efficiency, smoother multi\-party collaboration, and faster business decision\-making.

**Users within an enterprise can generally be categorized into three roles: data analysts, data builders, and platform administrators. Each role has different areas of focus and usage objectives during the learning and usage process. This document will share learning paths and a wealth of learning resources tailored to different types of users, enabling targeted learning based on your role and ensuring that you can systematically master the core features and application techniques of the product.**

## 4.1 Data analyst learning path

<span id="924c9b90"></span>
### 4.1.1 Role definition

Data analysts are typically members of business teams within the enterprise, dedicated data analysts, or members of big data teams. They primarily perform queries and analyses based on business data and focus on monitoring abnormal fluctuations in key business metrics on a daily basis. They are generally centered around members of the business team.

<span id="28c25ec9"></span>
### 4.1.2 Goals and vision

To acquire the essential foundational skills as a data analyst, the following can be learned through the current learning path:


* Master the Intelligent Data Insight data analysis and visualization tool, and gain proficiency in visual query and dashboard construction techniques.

* Learn data querying and filtering to quickly and accurately extract target information.

* Learn dashboard subscription, sharing, and alert rule configuration to ensure timely information delivery and effective risk prevention.

* Understand methods for presenting data across multiple endpoints, enabling more flexible and convenient data viewing and decision\-making, and enjoy an intelligent collaborative experience.

## 4.2 Data builder learning path

<span id="7fbaf7fa"></span>
### 4.2.1 Role definition

Data builders are typically members of an enterprise's big data team or professional data analysts and data engineers, primarily responsible for data integration, data preparation, data processing task scheduling, and data warehouse construction and development. Generally, dedicated IT or data teams are the main participants.

<span id="5d9b43c3"></span>
### 4.2.2 Target vision

To acquire the essential foundational skills as a data builder, the following can be learned through the current learning path:


* Become familiar with the data integration methods of this product and understand the integration processes for different data sources.

* Learn the functional modules for data preparation, including dataset processing, data cleansing, and multi\-table association.

* Master the operations for task creation and management in visual modeling to ensure stable operation of data processing tasks.

* Master the operations of advanced modules in this product, including attribution analysis and more.

## 4.3 Platform administrator learning path

<span id="6dfd168e"></span>
### 4.3.1 Role definition

Platform administrators are typically members of an enterprise's big data team or IT team, mainly responsible for daily operation and maintenance of this product, user management, permission allocation, system integration, platform promotion, product Q&A, and more, with technical personnel as the primary participants.

<span id="67f1aacf"></span>
### 4.3.2 Target vision

Essential foundational skills for data builders in the current learning path:


* Become familiar with daily platform monitoring and maintenance, promptly identify and resolve potential issues, and ensure stable platform operation and data security.

* Learn methods for user management and permission allocation to ensure proper data access control.

* Master techniques for system integration and openness to easily achieve collaboration with other systems.

* Master methods for promoting system adoption and operation, enabling more personnel within the enterprise to use the product effectively and unlock the value of data.
