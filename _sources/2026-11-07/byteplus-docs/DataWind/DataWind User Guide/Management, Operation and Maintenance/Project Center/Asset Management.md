# 1. Overview

The business line feature of asset management supports moving certain official resources to the corresponding business line directory. Resources are managed through folder directories and naming conventions, making it easier for users to quickly find the required resources and for producers to maintain these resources.

<span id="d4fd888f"></span>
# 2. Feature introduction

<span id="c355dad3"></span>
## 2.1 Create a business line

Before using official datasets, a business line must be created under the project. The structure of the business line serves as the folder directory structure for official datasets.

**Step 1**: Go to the asset management page in the Project Center and select "Business Line".

**Step 2**: On the business line page, select "Create Business Line".

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/606c3259747c457aa7a9267a9d951e85~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

**Step 3**: Enter the business line name and other relevant information, then click Confirm to create the business line.

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/3390e53405204d2694dc044b5d26e8f2~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

**Step 4**: You can create a sub\-business line under an existing business line.

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/cf101af3c46f4a3abb54691fc2105e22~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

<span id="123925c9"></span>
## 2.2 Edit a business line

After a business line is created, you can modify its information by clicking the Edit button next to the business line.

<span id="7a3bfa30"></span>
## 2.3 Delete a business line

You can delete an existing business line from the actions section next to the corresponding business line. If there are already assets under a business line, it cannot be deleted. The datasets must be removed from the business line before it can be deleted.
