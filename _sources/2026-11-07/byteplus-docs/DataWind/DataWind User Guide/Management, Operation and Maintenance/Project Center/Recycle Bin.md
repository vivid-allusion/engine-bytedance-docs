<span id="_1-概述"></span>
# 1. Overview

After dashboards, datasets, data source fields, and more are deleted, they are moved to the Recycle Bin. The Recycle Bin page displays the basic information of the deleted content.

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/dfa26b118b3747d186f6246163ed56bf~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

<span id="_2-功能介绍"></span>
# 2. Features

<span id="_2-1-剩余时间"></span>
## 2.1 Remaining time

Items in the Recycle Bin will be permanently deleted 15 days after they are deleted. You can view the remaining time on this page.

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/3f65904d0d6640cd8ab49e94d26393c2~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

<span id="_2-2-恢复"></span>
## 2.2 Restore

You can restore deleted dashboards, datasets, or data source fields. After restoration, dashboards can be viewed and used in Dashboards & Access Statistics.

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/613c8244b5174925b5f67682361169fc~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>

<span id="_2-3-删除"></span>
## 2.3 Delete

You can also delete dashboards, datasets, or data source fields in the Recycle Bin. After performing this operation, the content will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. Please proceed with caution.

<span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/26813e67126247eb9c0e9f6d0c6ae250~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image) </span>
