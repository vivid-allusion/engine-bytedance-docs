<span id="_1-异动数据监控分析"></span>
# 1. Abnormal data change monitoring and analysis


* **Business pain points**

   * Business data anomalies are discovered after the fact, resulting in low business efficiency

   * Difficulty in attributing data anomalies, making it hard to find data\-driven conclusions for business analysis

* **Target achievement**

   * Abnormal data changes are instantly synchronized via SMS or office tools, enabling "data finds people"

   * Built\-in algorithms enable one\-click attribution of abnormal data, providing effective business conclusions

<span id="_2-搭建企业级高性能bi分析平台"></span>
# 2. Building an enterprise\-level high\-performance BI analytics platform


* **Business pain points**

   * Extensive data sources involving multiple databases

   * Large data volumes and slow analysis affect the timeliness of business decision\-making

   * Complex organizational structure makes data permission management difficult

* **Target achievement**

   * Visual integration of data sources enables cross\-source data modeling

   * Supports hundred\-billion\-level data, with fast interactive analysis response

   * Combining data permissions with resource permissions enables department\- and business\-specific data permission management

<span id="_3-销售指标监控"></span>
# 3. Sales metrics monitoring


* **Business pain points**

   * Numerous sales regions and hierarchical divisions make report permission allocation difficult

   * Key sales data must be manually split across different regions and levels

   * Mobile work is common, and data reports cannot quickly adapt to mobile devices, requiring manual adjustment

* **Target achievement**

   * Combining data permissions with resource permissions enables highly personalized data displays for each user

   * Dashboards automatically adapt to mobile devices, providing an optimal viewing experience

   * Daily, weekly, and monthly monitoring reports can be automatically synchronized to email or office tools on a schedule
