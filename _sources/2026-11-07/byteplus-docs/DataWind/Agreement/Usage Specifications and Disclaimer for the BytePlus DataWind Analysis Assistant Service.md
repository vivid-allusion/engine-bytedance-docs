When using or using on a trial basis (collectively referred to as "using") the analysis assistant of DataWind ("this service"), please carefully read and comply with the "Usage Specifications and Disclaimer for the DataWind Analysis Assistant Service" (hereinafter referred to as "this statement").

<span id="_1-服务内容"></span>
# 1. **Service content**

**1.1** The [Analysis Assistant] is a self\-service analysis assistant within the DataWind product, powered by large model capabilities, which enables users to quickly perform a series of operations such as data preparation, data interpretation, and exploration through natural language conversation.

**1.2** You may use the analysis assistant functionality based on the large model already configured in DataWind; you may also independently purchase a third\-party, legally operated large model ("third\-party large model") to use the analysis assistant functionality (**only applicable to private deployment scenarios**). If you independently purchase a third\-party large model, you must verify and confirm the legality, compliance, security, and suitability of the third\-party large model for your business. You must register an account for the third\-party large model yourself and comply with the authorization, usage specifications, and other requirements of the third\-party large model. Volcano Engine is not responsible if you are unable to use the analysis assistant properly due to reasons attributable to you or the third party.

<span id="_2-使用规范"></span>
# 2. **Usage specifications**

**2.1** You may only use the analysis assistant functionality within your own business and internally in connection with your actual business scenarios. You must not input content unrelated to your business into this service, and you must not provide generative artificial intelligence services to the public through the analysis assistant functionality.

**2.2** You must not use this service, prompts, and content generated through this service for the development, training, annotation, fine\-tuning, optimization, iteration, or other similar activities of new models or other models outside this service. You must not use this service to develop, train, or improve algorithms or models that compete with Volcano Engine or its affiliates.

**2.3** When using this service, you must comply with all applicable laws and regulations regarding cybersecurity, data security, personal information protection, intellectual property rights, and content ecosystem governance. You must maintain internet order and network security, and must not use this service to publish statements that endanger national security, undermine national unity, disrupt social order, are discriminatory, or are unlawful, or infringe upon any third party's rights to personal information, reputation, personality, intellectual property, property, or any other rights, or engage in activities related to pornography, gambling, drugs, or other acts that violate laws, regulations, or public order and good morals.

**2.4** To fulfill legal and compliance requirements, Volcano Engine has the right to use technical or manual means to review your actions and information when using this service, including but not limited to reviewing content you submit to or output from this service, and to establish and improve risk filtering mechanisms, databases of illegal content characteristics, and more.

**2.5** Volcano Engine will not use content you submit to this service, content you output using the service, or data you process using DataWind for training or fine\-tuning large models.

**2.6** You understand and agree that Volcano Engine will automatically receive and record the conversation information you enter during your interaction with [Analysis Assistant], and use this information to output content to you in order to provide the [Analysis Assistant] service. We will also use conversation information to improve [Analysis Assistant]'s ability to understand your input, so as to continuously enhance the speed and quality of [Analysis Assistant]'s recognition and response, and improve service effectiveness. During the conversation, you can also evaluate the content output by [Analysis Assistant], including actions such as liking or disliking. We will collect your evaluation information, including the reasons for error reports you voluntarily submit, in order to continuously improve the quality of [Analysis Assistant]'s output content.
