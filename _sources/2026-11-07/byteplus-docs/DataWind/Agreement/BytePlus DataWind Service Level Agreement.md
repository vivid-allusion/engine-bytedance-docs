**Effective date: 27 September 2021**

**Applicable service: DataWind (SaaS version)** 

**This service level agreement specifies the availability level indicators and compensation plan for the DataWind service provided by Volcano Engine to customers.** 

**1 Definitions**

**1.1 Service period: A service period is one calendar month. If less than one calendar month, it is calculated as one calendar month.** 

**1.2 Total minutes in the service period: Total number of days in the service period × 24 (hours) × 60 (minutes).** 

**1.3 Service unavailable minutes: Calculated in 5\-minute units. If all consecutive attempts by the customer to establish a connection with the DataWind service fail within a 5\-minute period, the service is considered unavailable for that 5\-minute period. Within a service period, service unavailable minutes = total number of 5\-minute unavailable periods × 5.** 

**1.4 Monthly service fee: The total service fee paid by the customer for the DataWind service in a calendar month. If the customer pays the service fee for multiple months in a lump sum, the monthly service fee is calculated by allocating the total amount over the number of months purchased.** 

**2 Service availability**

**2.1 Service availability calculation method**

**The service availability of DataWind will be calculated for each service period as follows:** 

**Service availability = (Total minutes in the service period \- Service unavailable minutes) / Total minutes in the service period × 100%)** 

**2.2 Service availability commitment**

**The service availability of DataWind** in a service period is guaranteed to be no less than 99.00%;

**2.3 Exclusions**

**If DataWind does not meet the above availability commitment, the customer may receive compensation in accordance with the compensation plan. The compensation does not cover service unavailability caused by the following reasons:** 

 **(1) System maintenance performed by Volcano Engine after prior notice to the customer, including cutover, repair, upgrade, and simulated failure drills;** 

 **(2) Caused by network or device failures, or configuration adjustments, outside of Volcano Engine\-owned equipment;** 

 **(3) Caused by the customer's application or data information being attacked by hackers;** 

 **(4) Caused by improper maintenance or inadequate confidentiality by the customer, resulting in the loss or leakage of data, credentials, passwords, and so on;** 

 **(5) Caused by the customer's negligence or operations authorized by the customer;** 

 **(6) Caused by the customer's failure to follow the Volcano Engine product documentation or usage recommendations;** 

 **(7) Caused by force majeure;** 

 **(8) The request originates from a non\-Chinese mainland IP address.** 

**3 Compensation plan**

**3.1 Compensation standards**

**The DataWind Service calculates monthly service availability on a per\-project basis. If the promised service availability is not met, the compensation amount will be calculated according to the standards in the table below. Compensation is limited to vouchers for purchasing the DataWind Service, and the total compensation shall not exceed 50% of the monthly service fee paid by the customer for the DataWind Service project in the month when the service availability commitment was not met (excluding amounts offset by vouchers).** 


|**Service availability** |**Compensation** |
|---|---|
|Less than 99%, but equal to or greater than 95% |25% of the monthly service fee |
|Less than 95% |50% of the monthly service fee |


**3.2 Compensation application period**

**The customer may submit a compensation application for services that did not meet availability in the previous month after the fifth business day of each month.**  The compensation application must be submitted within two months after the end of the relevant month in which this product failed to meet availability. Compensation applications submitted after the application period will not be accepted.

**4 Other**

**Volcano Engine reserves the right to modify this Service Level Agreement. If any terms of this Service Level Agreement are modified, Volcano Engine will notify you 30 days in advance by means of a website announcement or by sending an in\-site message or email. If you do not agree to the modifications made by Volcano Engine to this Service Level Agreement, you have the right to stop using this product. If you continue to use this product, you will be deemed to have accepted the modified Service Level Agreement.**
