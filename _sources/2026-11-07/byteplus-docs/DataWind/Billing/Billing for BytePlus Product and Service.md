## 2. Configuration changes

During the validity period of the basic service, optional services support configuration upgrades, such as purchasing additional accounts or expanding storage capacity.

The fee required for upgrading the configuration = annual fee for optional services / 365


* remaining service days (configuration upgrades are billed daily; the remaining service days are equal to the remaining days of the basic service. The actual fee amount is subject to the information displayed on the relevant configuration upgrade page.)

    &nbsp;


<span id="_3-service-expiration-and-suspension"></span>
## 3. Service expiration and suspension


* If you do not complete the renewal by 24:00 on the day the service expires, BytePlus will suspend the service and the corresponding service portal.

* If you purchase or renew DataWind services within 72 hours after the service expires, the service will be restored to normal.

* If the service expires for more than 72 hours, it will be deemed that you have voluntarily abandoned the services purchased on the BytePlus platform. The related resources and instances will be released, and the released data cannot be recovered.


<span id="81740c91"></span>
## 4. Refunds

Services purchased and paid for are not eligible for any refunds.
