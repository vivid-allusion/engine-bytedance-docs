This topic describes the performance specifications of the NAT Gateway. Performance limits vary by billing method and specification. Choose a suitable configuration based on your business scale and network planning.

<span id=".5oyJ6KeE5qC86K6h6LS5L-WMheW5tOWMheaciA=="></span>
## Pay\-by\-specification/Subscription

Applicable only to Internet NAT Gateways.


|Specification |New connections per second |Max connections |Max forwarding throughput |
|---|---|---|---|
|Small |1,000 |10,000 |5 Gbps |
|Medium |5,000 |50,000 |5 Gbps |
|Large |10,000 |200,000 |5 Gbps |


<span id=".5oyJ5L2_55So6YeP6K6h6LS5"></span>
## Pay\-by\-CU

When the actual business traffic of a NAT Gateway exceeds the maximum metric limits, packet loss may occur. If higher performance is required, please contact your account manager.


|Metric |New connections per second |Max connections |Max forwarding throughput |
|---|---|---|---|
|Initial limit |100,000 |2,000,000 |5 Gbps |
|Elastic upper limit |400,000 |8,000,000 |40 Gbps |
