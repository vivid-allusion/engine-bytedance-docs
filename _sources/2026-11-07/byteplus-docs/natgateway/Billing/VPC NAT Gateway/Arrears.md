This topic describes the impact of arrears on the VPC NAT Gateway. [View Available Balance](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/6269/100429#%E6%9D%BF%E5%9D%97%E8%AF%B4%E6%98%8E).

## Pay\-by\-CU

Account arrears will cause the VPC NAT Gateway to shut down or even be released. The platform has set a grace period for resources. Within the grace period, the resources are retained and you can continue using the resources after you recharge your account and clear overdue payments. It will not impact resource features. After the resource arrears retention period, VPC NAT Gateway resources will be forcibly reclaimed, and related data cannot be restored. Regardless of whether your instances are suspended or released, you shall settle the issued bills based on actual usage.

**Overdue stages**


* Suspension time: Actual suspension time prevails.

* Release time: 15 days and 2 hours in arrears.
