## Pay\-by\-specification / Pay\-by\-CU

Account arrears may cause the Pay\-by\-specification / Pay\-by\-CU Internet NAT Gateways to be suspended or even released. The platform has set a grace period for resources. Within the grace period, the resources are retained and you can continue using the resources after you recharge your account and clear overdue payments. No impact on the features of resources. After the retention period expires, Internet NAT Gateway resources will be forcibly reclaimed, and related data cannot be restored. Regardless of whether your instances are suspended or released, you shall settle the issued bills based on the actual.

## Subscription

An overdue payment of an account does not affect the unexpired Subscription resources under the account. For more details, see [Expiration](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/natgateway/Expiration).
