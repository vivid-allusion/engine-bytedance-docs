This topic describes the metering methods for the Internet NAT gateway and related documentation.

<span id=".6K6h6LS55oC76KeI"></span>
## Billing


|Metering method |Payment time |Settlement cycle |
|---|---|---|
|Subscription |Prepaid |Non\-calendar month |
|Pay\-by\-specification |Postpaid |Fees are settled at the beginning of each natural hour (UTC+8). |
|Pay\-by\-CU |Postpaid |Fees are settled at the beginning of each natural hour (UTC+8). |


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">The Internet NAT Gateway must be associated with an Elastic IP Address (EIP) to function properly. For EIP billing methods, see <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/eip/Billing-overview">EIP Billing overview</a>. This section introduces the billing methods for the Internet NAT Gateway.</div>
