If you no longer need a Subscription Internet NAT Gateway, you can unsubscribe to reduce costs. This topic describes how to unsubscribe a Subscription Internet NAT Gateway.

<span id=".6YCA6K6i6K-05piO"></span>
## Unsubscription description

Subscription\-based Internet NAT Gateway unsubscription includes 7\-day unconditional unsubscription and non\-7\-day unconditional unsubscription, as follows:


|Unsubscription policy |7\-day free cancellation |Non\-7\-day cancellation |
|---|---|---|
|Prerequisites |Within 7 days (≤ 7 x 24 hours) after successful payment of the Internet NAT Gateway order, with no manual renewal or specification changes.<br><br>Each account supports unsubscribing from only one Internet NAT Gateway instance per calendar year (January 1 to December 31). |Outside the scope of **7\-day free cancellation** protection. |
|Refund amount |The payments will be fully refunded to your available balance. The coupons used are non\-refundable. |See [Non-7-day cancellation explanation](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/6269/81013#.5LqM44CB6Z2e5LiD5aSp5peg55CG55Sx6YCA6K6i6K-05piO). |


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">If the unsubscription rules conflict with the event rules for the Internet NAT Gateway purchased during the event, the event rules prevail.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">If suspicious or malicious unsubscription occurs, BytePlus reserves the right to deny your request.</div>



<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the[ Internet NAT Gateway console](https://console.byteplus.com/nat/nats).

2. In the top menu bar, select **Billing \> Cancel subscription**.

3. On the unsubscription management page, select **7\-day free cancellation** or **non\-7\-day cancellation**.

   * On the **Unsubscription (7\-day no reason)**  tab, click Unsubscribe on the right side of the target Internet NAT Gateway.

   * On the **Regular unsubscription** tab, click Unsubscribe on the right side of the target Internet NAT Gateway.

4. Click **Unsubscribe**.

5. Click **Confirm** to complete the unsubscription.
