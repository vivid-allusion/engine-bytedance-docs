After the subscription Internet NAT Gateway expires, the BytePlus platform reserves a grace period for you. During this time, you can renew or unsubscribe. After renewal, you can continue to use the Internet NAT Gateway. After unsubscribing, the resource will be immediately released. If no action is taken during the resource grace period, the Internet NAT Gateway resource will be forcibly released after the period ends. Once released, the resource cannot be recovered.

Please pay attention.</div>


**Expiration stage description**


* Expiration time: At 23:59:59 on the expiration day.

* Suspension time: 12 hours after expiration.

* Release time: 14 days and 12 hours after suspension.


For subscription Internet NAT Gateways, after expiration and before recovery, if you confirm you will no longer use the Internet NAT Gateway, you may delete it directly. Once deleted, the Internet NAT Gateway will be immediately released, and data cannot be recovered. Consider the impact on current operations carefully before proceeding.
