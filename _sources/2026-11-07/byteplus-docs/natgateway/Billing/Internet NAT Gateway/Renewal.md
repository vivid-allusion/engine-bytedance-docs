For annual or monthly subscription internet NAT gateways, BytePlus platform sends reminders via SMS, email, or internal messages 7 days, 3 days, 1 day before expiration, and on the expiration date according to your settings. To avoid disruption to your business, renew your bandwidth package promptly when receiving the notification.

If you renew before expiration suspension (within 12 hours after expiration), the start time of the renewed term is the expiration time of the previous term. If you renew after expiration suspension but before expiration reclaim, the start time of the renewed term is the actual renewal time.

<span id=".5omL5Yqo57ut6LS5"></span>
## Manual renewal

You can renew an internet NAT gateway manually before it expires and is reclaimed.

<span id="cbszUBLD"></span>
### Procedure


1. Log in to the [Internet NAT Gateway console](https://console.byteplus.com/nat/nats)。

2. In the top menu bar, select the region and project.

3. Click  **... \> Renew** on the right side of the target Internet NAT gateway.

4. Select the renewal duration.

5. Click **Pay** and complete the payment according to the guidance. The renewal is completed immediately after payment is successfully processed.


<span id=".6Ieq5Yqo57ut6LS5"></span>
## Automatic renewal

You can check **Automatic renewal** when creating a subscription Internet NAT Gateway, or enable automatic renewal before the subscription expires. For details, see the [Automatic renewal](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-auto-renewal-rules) section.

After enabling automatic renewal, if you have available coupons in your account, the system will prioritize using them for renewal.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">For resources with automatic renewal enabled, you can also manually renew them at any time. Manual renewal does not invalidate automatic renewal. The system will deduct fees 7 days before the updated expiration date of the resource.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Expired Internet NAT Gateways are not allowed to enable or modify automatic renewal.</div>
