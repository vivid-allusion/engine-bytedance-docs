To provide a more reliable and stable service, HBase has upgraded its Thrift service to offer high availability and has officially started billing. High\-availability Thrift supports the flexible configuration of Thrift Server node specifications and the number of nodes. You can adjust resources based on instance specifications and business requirements to meet the performance demands of different scenarios.

<span id="18OqBnUT"></span>
## Billing start date and regions


|**Billing region** |**Billing start time** |
|---|---|
|China (Beijing) |Billing starts on September 22, 2026. |
|China (Shanghai) |Billing starts on September 18, 2026. |
|China (Guangzhou) |Billing starts on September 18, 2026. |
|China (Hong Kong) |Billing starts on September 17, 2026. |
|Asia Pacific (Johor) |Billing starts on September 17, 2026. |
|Asia Pacific (Jakarta) |Billing starts on September 15, 2026. |


<span id="BUJCtt4p"></span>
## Scope of billing impact


|**Applicable scenarios** |**Billing instructions** |
|---|---|
|Legacy Thrift2 enabled before official billing |You applied for the legacy Thrift2 connection address before official billing started, so you can continue to use the Thrift2 connection address free of charge after official billing starts. |
|Upgrade from the legacy Thrift2 to high\-availability Thrift after billing begins<br><br> |If you applied for the legacy Thrift2 connection address before official billing started and want to use the high\-availability Thrift connection address after official billing starts, you need to perform the upgrade to the high\-availability Thrift connection address. After the upgrade is complete, HBase will be billed according to the specifications and number of Thrift Server nodes selected during the upgrade.  |
|New high\-availability Thrift enabled after official billing<br><br> |Once billing officially begins, applications for legacy Thrift2 connection addresses will no longer be supported. Only high\-availability Thrift connection addresses can be enabled.  |
