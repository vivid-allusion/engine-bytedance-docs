Pay\-as\-you\-go is a post\-paid billing method. You can create and delete resources on demand without purchasing them in advance.

<span id=".566A5LuL"></span>
## Introduction

Pay\-as\-you\-go is ideal for businesses that experience significant short\-term growth, such as flash sales. Fees are calculated per second based on your actual resource usage and deducted from your account hourly, providing a true pay\-as\-you\-go service. If you no longer need your purchased resources, you can delete them at any time. Billing stops immediately after deletion.

<span id=".6LSt5Lmw6KaB5rGC"></span>
## Purchase requirements

You must create a BytePlus account  before purchasing an HBase instance. For instructions on account registration, see [How to Register an Account](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-signing-up-your-account#create-byteplus-account).

<span id=".6K6h6LS55pe26ZW_"></span>
## Billing duration

Assuming no service interruptions due to overdue payments, the billing duration for a pay\-as\-you\-go HBase instance begins when the instance is created and ends when it is released.

<span id=".57uT566X5ZGo5pyf"></span>
## Billing cycle

Fees for pay\-as\-you\-go HBase instances are calculated by the second and settled hourly. The system settles the fees at the top of every hour. You will receive an insufficient balance notification via SMS or email if your available credit (including your account balance and vouchers) is less than your pending bill. 

<span id=".5Y-Y5pu06YWN572u"></span>
## Modify configuration

HBase instances support real\-time configuration changes. After you successfully change the instance specifications, such as node specifications, you can view the updated instance configuration on the **Instance List** page. To change the configuration, see [Changing instance configuration](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/HBase/Modify_configuration_instructions).
