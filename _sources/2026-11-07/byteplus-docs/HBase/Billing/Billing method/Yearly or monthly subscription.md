Subscription is a prepaid billing method for creating instances. Compared to the pay\-as\-you\-go option, a subscription can significantly reduce your costs and is ideal for long\-term usage. This article describes how to purchase HBase instances with a subscription.

<span id=".6LSt5Lmw5pe26ZW_"></span>
## Purchase duration

Subscription plans for HBase instances offer various durations. Select one based on your business needs:


* **Monthly**: You can purchase a 1 to 9\-month subscription.

* **Annual**: You can purchase a 1 to 3\-year subscription.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">You will receive a discount for subscriptions of one year or longer. The specific discount is displayed on the order page in the console.   </div>
   

<span id=".57uT566X5ZGo5pyf"></span>
## **Billing cycle**

The billing cycle for a subscription\-based HBase instance corresponds to your purchased duration (in UTC+8). It begins at the exact second the resource is activated and concludes at 23:59:59 on the expiration date. For example, if you create a subscription instance at 14:00:00 on July 1, 2023, for one month, the billing cycle is from 14:00:00 on July 1, 2023, to 23:59:59 on August 1, 2023.

## Modify configurations

You can modify the configuration of subscription instances in real time. After a successful modification, you can view the latest instance specifications on the **Instance List** page. To modify configurations, see [Changing instance configuration](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/HBase/Modify_configuration_instructions).

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">After modifying the configuration of a subscription instance, you may need to pay the difference or receive a refund. For details about the billing rules for modifying subscription resource specifications, see <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/Billing_Rules_for_Resource_Subscription_Change">Billing Rules for Resource Subscription Change</a>.</div>

## Unsubscribe from instances

After you unsubscribe from (delete) a subscription HBase instance, the refund will be credited to your account balance. Vouchers are non\-refundable once used. To unsubscribe from an instance, see [Unsubscribe from yearly/monthly subscription](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/HBase/Deleting_or_unsubscribing_an_instance#.6YCA6K6i5YyF5bm05YyF5pyI5a6e5L6L). For details about refund calculation rules, see [Unsubscription Management](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-unsubscription-management).

## Notes

If your account has overdue payments, you can still use your purchased subscription HBase instances. However, you cannot purchase new instances or renew existing ones until you pay the outstanding fees. For more details, see [Arrears and expiration](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/HBase/Arrears_and_expiration).
