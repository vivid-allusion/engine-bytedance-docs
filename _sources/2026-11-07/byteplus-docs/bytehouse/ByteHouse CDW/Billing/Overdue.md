## Post\-paid billing

The changes in the service status after ByteHouse cloud data warehouse incurs arrears are as follows:


* Within 24 hours of arrears, you can still use this service, but the system will remind you that you are in arrears. Please recharge and settle the arrears bill within 24 hours of arrears.

* If arrears exceed 24 hours and you still have not paid all overdue bills, BytePlus will suspend this service but will still retain your configuration information and data. The retained data will continue to occupy storage space, and arrears will accumulate.

* If you still have not cleared the arrears within 7 days after the service is suspended, it will be considered as you voluntarily canceling this service. BytePlus will terminate the service terms of this product, delete all data saved in ByteHouse and it will not be recoverable. After you pay the arrears, you can reactivate the ByteHouse cloud data warehouse service on BytePlus.

## Pre\-paid subscription

If the annual or monthly subscription instance expires and is not renewed, your resources will enter a retention period and resource usage will be limited. After the resource retention period expires, your resources will be forcibly reclaimed, and once reclaimed, they cannot be restored. Please renew your ByteHouse instance within the specified time (before the forced resource reclamation) to avoid disruption in usage.

**Expiration Release Description**


* Expiration moment: The selected day at 23:59:59, where you can continue to restore resource usage through renewal.

* Shutdown moment: 12 hours after expiration (after shutdown, if you do not renew, the instance cannot be used, and the data will be temporarily retained).

* Release moment: 15 days after expiration (after release, configuration and data cannot be retrieved).
