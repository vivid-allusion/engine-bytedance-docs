After purchasing a Cloud DDoS Protection instance, you can perform several key management operations directly from the instance list. This guide provides step\-by\-step instructions for adding protected EIPs, upgrading the service plan and number of protected IPs, renewing your subscription, and enabling or disabling automatic renewal.

<span id="422ecf86"></span>
## Constraints and limitations

You cannot edit instances with an expired billing status.

<span id="9977776d"></span>
## Prerequisites

You have successfully purchased a Cloud DDoS Protection  instance.

<span id="a7f8ee1a"></span>
## Add EIPs


1. Log in to the [Cloud DDoS Protection console](https://console.byteplus.com/ddos-protection/).

2. On the left side of the page, select **Cloud DDoS Protection \> Instance List**.

3. Select the target instance and click **Add a protection EIP** to open the protection resources page for that instance.

   For more details, see [Add an EIP to your instance](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-ddos-protection/Add_an_EIP).


<span id="59e63749"></span>
## Upgrade specifications

You can manually upgrade the number of protected IPs and the service plan for a Cloud DDoS Protection instance.


1. Log in to the [Cloud DDoS Protection console](https://console.byteplus.com/ddos-protection/).

2. On the left side of the page, select **Cloud DDoS Protection \> Instance List**.

3. Select the target instance, click **More \> Upgrade ** to open the specifications configuration page.

4. Select the new number of protected IPs and service plan.

5. After confirming the configuration, check the service agreement and click **Upgrade now**.

6. Confirm the order information and make the payment. The new specifications take effect immediately after payment.


<span id="ef75f10e"></span>
## Renewal

You can renew your instance at any time before it expires to extend the service period.


1. Log in to the [Cloud DDoS Protection console](https://console.byteplus.com/ddos-protection/).

2. On the left side of the page, select **Cloud DDoS Protection \> Instance List**.

3. Select the target instance and click **More \> Renew** to open the renewal page.

4. Select the renewal duration.

5. After confirming the configuration, check the service agreement and click **Renew now**.

6. Confirm the order information and make the payment. The new plan will take effect immediately after payment is completed.


<span id="6b6d9195"></span>
## Enable or disable automatic renewal

You can enable or disable automatic renewal for your instances at any time.


1. Log in to the [Cloud DDoS Protection console](https://console.byteplus.com/ddos-protection/).

2. On the left side of the page, select **Cloud DDoS Protection \> Instance List**.

3. Select one or more instances, and then click **More actions \> Enable automatic renewal** (or **Disable automatic renewal**).
