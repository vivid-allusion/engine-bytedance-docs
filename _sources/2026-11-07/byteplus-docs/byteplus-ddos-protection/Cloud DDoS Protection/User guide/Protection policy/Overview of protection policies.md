Cloud DDoS Protection's rule group feature allows you to centrally manage and simplify your protection policies. It is ideal for applying consistent security rules across multiple protected IPs, streamlining complex DDoS rule management. This guide explains the different rule group and rule types available, and shows you how to apply them to your resources to enhance your security posture.

<span id="5e863e4a"></span>
## Rule group types

The three types of rule groups are:


* **Default rule group**: The BytePlus DDoS protection engine generates universal protection rules based on extensive data from real\-world attack mitigation scenarios, aimed at mitigating network and transport layer DDoS attacks. The default rules automatically apply to any protected EIP that is not associated with a custom or expert rule group, and you do not need to manually set the IP range.

* **Expert rule group**: Our DDoS experts will customize dedicated protection rules for your business based on your business profile and attack patterns, and regularly update them to improve protection. You can view these custom rules and their associated IP ranges in the console, and you can adjust the associated IP ranges as needed.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">Caution   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-wrapper-indent="1">When an expert rule group is updated, your custom adjustments may be overridden, and the system applies the latest rules and associated IP configurations.   </div>
   

* **Custom rule group**: You can create and configure rule groups based on your business and security requirements, and view and modify the rule content and their associated IP ranges at any time.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">Caution   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-wrapper-indent="1">Each instance supports a maximum of 50 custom rule groups.   </div>

## Rule types


* **IP allowlists and blocklists**: Manage access permissions through preset IP address lists.

   * **Allowlist**: Allow specified IPs to access target resources. This is useful for allowing high\-frequency internal traffic that might otherwise be flagged as an attack. For related configuration, please refer to [Allow request traffic from the specified IP address](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-ddos-protection/Allow_request_traffic_from_the_specified_IP_address).

   * **Blocklist**: Block known attack source IPs from accessing target resources to prevent further attacks from those sources. For related configuration, please refer to[Block traffic from the specified IP address](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-ddos-protection/Block_traffic_from_the_specified_IP_address).

* **Protocol filtering**: Discard requests from specific protocols when an attack is detected and traffic scrubbing is initiated, thereby reducing the attack surface. This feature effectively mitigates DDoS attacks initiated using specific protocols, such as UDP Flood and ICMP Flood. For related configuration, please refer to[Block requests with specific protocol](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-ddos-protection/Block_requests_with_specific_protocol).

* **Port filtering**: Manage access permissions for source and destination ports of specific protocols. For related configuration, please refer to[Allow or block requests from/to specified ports](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-ddos-protection/Allow_or_block_requests_from_or_to_specified_ports).

   * **Port blocklist**: Block access from specified source ports or to specified destination ports.

   * **Port allowlist**: Allow access from specified source ports or permit access to specific destination ports.

* **Regional blocking**: Restrict access based on the geographic location of an IP address. When your business primarily targets a specific area, you can block traffic from regions outside your primary service area to effectively reduce attack risks. For related configuration, please refer to[Block traffic from the specified regions](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-ddos-protection/Block_traffic_from_the_specified_regions).

## How to apply rule group to your EIPs?

For a custom rule group to protect a target EIP, you must meet the following conditions:


* You have created a rule group.

* You have configured specific protection rules in the rule group.

* You have associated your resources with the rule group.


Based on your operational scenarios, you can choose one of the following two methods to apply the rule group to the target EIP:


* [Scenario 1: Associate rule groups when adding protection resources](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-ddos-protection/Associate_protection_resources_with_custom_rule_groups#8fb8f6a4): This applies to scenarios where a custom rule group needs to be configured when adding new protection resources.

* [Scenario 2: Associate existing resources with rule groups](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-ddos-protection/Associate_protection_resources_with_custom_rule_groups#4ef26fc9): This applies to scenarios where a rule group needs to be configured for existing protection resources.
