Cloud DDoS Protection is a security service that provides comprehensive DDoS protection for BytePlus cloud resources. Built on the native BytePlus network, it allows you to apply full protection to your BytePlus EIPs with a single click, requiring no changes to your service architecture. This ensures low\-latency access and precise traffic scrubbing to safeguard your service availability against attacks.

<span id="b479f039"></span>
## Limitations

Cloud DDoS Protection protects BytePlus EIP instances, including EIP instances bound to cloud resources such as Cloud Server, Load Balancer, NAT Gateway, and Secondary Network Interface. 

<span id="0117a17c"></span>
## Features

<span id="f698f58b"></span>
### Quick integration with cloud resources

A BytePlus EIP can be quickly bound to a native protection package, enabling full protection capabilities without the need to change the IP address.

<span id="471bc434"></span>
### Detailed visual reports

The system provides reports on attack data and trends for both protection groups and individual EIPs, and includes alerts for attack events.

<span id="ef5481fb"></span>
## Highlights

<span id="6e2d4305"></span>
### Quick and transparent access

You do not need to change your service architecture or modify proxy configurations. Directly apply comprehensive protection to your cloud\-based EIPs.

<span id="1871a304"></span>
### Shared protection capacity

Share protection capacity among EIPs within the same region to ensure all your critical IPs have enhanced protection.

<span id="f4766cdc"></span>
### Low latency

Utilize high\-quality bandwidth without introducing additional latency to deliver a fast access experience for your services.

<span id="10227ed7"></span>
### Quick response

When abnormal DDoS traffic is detected, you are notified of attack events in real time, allowing you to monitor both the attack and the protection effectiveness.
