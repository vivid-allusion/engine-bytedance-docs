To effectively manage your Advanced DDoS Protection instance after it has been created, you can perform several key operations. This guide provides instructions on how to modify the instance name for better identification, configure protection policies to secure your assets, and renew the instance to extend its service duration.

<span id="a548da50"></span>
## Before you begin

Create an instance of BytePlus Advanced DDoS Protection.

<span id="65fb00f1"></span>
## Editing the instance name

After an instance is created, it is assigned a default name. If the default name is unclear or does not accurately describe the instance's purpose, you can modify it for easier identification.


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Advanced DDoS Protection Console](https://console.byteplus.com/ddos-protection/advance/instances).

2. In the left\-side navigation pane, choose **Advanced DDoS Protection** \> **Instance list**.

3. Choose an instance, and click the edit icon to modify your instance name.


<span id="ebe78b48"></span>
## Configuring protection policies

From the Instance list page, you can quickly access the policy settings to configure protection for a specific instance.


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Advanced DDoS Protection Console](https://console.byteplus.com/ddos-protection/advance/instances).

2. In the left\-side navigation pane, choose **Advanced DDoS Protection** \> **Instance list**.

3. Choose an instance, and click **Protection settings**.

   You will be redirected to the policy configuration page for the instance.

4. Configure policies as needed.


<span id="1825d308"></span>
## Renewing an instance

You can manually renew an instance before it expires to extend its subscription duration.


1. Log in to the [BytePlus Advanced DDoS Protection Console](https://console.byteplus.com/ddos-protection/advance/instances).

2. In the left\-side navigation pane, choose **Advanced DDoS Protection** \> **Instance list**.

3. Choose an instance, and click `···` **\> Renew**.

4. Choose the duration and confirm the new expiration date.

5. Read and agree to the terms and conditions.

6. Click **Renew now**, and make the payment.
