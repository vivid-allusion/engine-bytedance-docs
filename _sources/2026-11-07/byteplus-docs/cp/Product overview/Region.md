## What is a Region?

A Region refers to the geographical location of a physical data center. Once resources are created, they can not be relocated to a different region. Internal networks of different regions are isolated from each other to ensure stability and fault tolerance across regions as much as possible.


* From a geographical point of view, we recommend that you select a region that is close to you or the region of your target users to reduce network latency and accelerate access.

* Resource pricing in different regions may differ. If you or your target users are in the Chinese mainland, you do not need to consider network latency in different regions. This is because the infrastructure, BGP network performance, and resource operation and configuration differ slightly between regions in the Chinese mainland.
