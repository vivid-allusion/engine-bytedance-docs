Support deleting service connection configurations that are not currently needed.

<span id=".5YmN5o-Q5p2h5Lu2"></span>
## Prerequisites

The target service is not used by other pipelines or application delivery modules.

<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to [Code Pipeline Console](https://console.byteplus.com/cp).

2. In the left navigation bar, select **Global configurations \> Service connection**.

3. On the Service connection page, click **delete** under the **actions** column for the target Service connection.

4. On the secondary confirmation page, click **confirm**.
