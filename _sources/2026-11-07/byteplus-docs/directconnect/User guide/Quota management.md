This topic describes how to view the usage of Direct Connect (DC) resources and increase corresponding quotas. For more quota information, see [Limits](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/directconnect/Limits).

<span id=".5pON5L2c6ZmQ5Yi2"></span>
## Limits

The quota for the configuration item "The number of custom static route entries supported by a single Direct Connect Gateway" cannot be increased.

<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to BytePlus and go to the [Quota Center](https://console.byteplus.com/quota/productList).

2. Click the **All Products** module, navigate to the **Network** section, and select **DirectConnection** to access the quota details page for Direct Connect. On this page, you can view the usage of Direct Connect resources in each region under the current account.

3. To adjust the quota, click **Apply** in the **Actions** column of the configuration item.

4. In the **Quota application** page that appears on the right, adjust the relevant quota.

   * Applicant: Confirm whether the configuration item and its region for quota increase are correct.

   * Apply for quota to: Enter or drag to adjust the quantity of quota to apply for. The applied quota quantity must be greater than the currently used quota for the resource.

   * Application reason: Provide detailed reasons for the quota application, related use cases, and the necessity for the quota increase.

5. Click **Submit** to complete the application. The system will fully evaluate your application reason upon receipt and approve it as soon as possible.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">After submitting the application, click <strong>Application History</strong> in the <strong>Actions</strong> column of the configuration item to view the approval progress.   </div>
