## Step 1: Confirm your solution for connecting to the cloud

We provide diverse solutions to connect your on\-premises data center to the cloud. In the initial stage, you can consult a BytePlus account manager to understand the advantages, pricing, and other information of each solution, and select the most suitable one.

Among the many connection solutions, Direct Connect (DC) delivers high security, high stability, and low latency. If you decide to use DC to connect your services to the cloud, follow the next step to select an appropriate DC location.

<span id=".5q2l6aqk5LqM77ya6YCJ5oup5o6l5YWl54K5"></span>
## Step 2: Select a Direct Connect location

An access point is the geographical location where a DC Connection connects to cloud services. BytePlus access points support DC Connections from multiple line operators. Confirm your access point before applying for a DC Connection. For more information, see [Direct Connect access points](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/directconnect/Direct-Connect-access-point).

You can select the most suitable access point from the following aspects.


1. Region of the access point

   When selecting an access point, follow the "proximity principle". That is, select the access point which is closest to your data center. The closer your data center is to the access point, the lower the network latency. For example, if your on\-premises data center is in Beijing, you should select the access point in China (Beijing).

2. Line operator

   Different access points may support different line operators. After confirming the region of the access point, consult your BytePlus account manager to learn which line operators each access point supports in that region. Select an appropriate line operator by comprehensively considering all aspects, such as prices and network performance.

3. Port Spec

   Specify your port specification. Different access points support different specifications. Consult your BytePlus account manager for details. Port Spec determines the port rate. Default options are optical ports of 1 Gbps and 10 Gbps.

## Step 4: Line operator site survey

After confirming the Direct Connect access point and access solution, you can contact the line operator to confirm the Direct Connect access solution and submit a ticket for an indoor site survey application to BytePlus.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">China (Beijing), China (Shanghai), China (Guangzhou) access points: You can apply to enter the BytePlus data center for the site survey.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">China (Hong Kong), Asia Pacific (Johor) access points: No need to submit an indoor site survey application to BytePlus. The construction party only needs to apply to the provider to enter the line operator's Meet\-Me Room for the site survey. Entering the BytePlus data center is not required.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">Bare fiber DC connections may face link stability risks due to environmental factors, with potential downtime lasting hours or days. Be aware and fully assess your business situation to deploy disaster recovery (<a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/directconnect/best-practices-overview#.5LiT57q_6L-e5o6l6K6_6Zeu5LqR5LiKVlBD">Redundant\-link access</a>).</div>


1. When submitting a [Direct Connect indoor site survey application](https://console.byteplus.com/workorder/create?step=3&SubProductID=P00000250&FlowKey=FncxjUGJYSTSGBTQipeR&aiSearchQuestion=&aiID=), you should clearly provide the following information in the ticket:

   * **Access point**: The access point for the indoor site survey, e.g., "Beijing\-Chaoyang\-A".

   * **Entry and exit time**: The time when you will enter the access point for site survey and when the site survey will be completed, and the time when you will leave the access point.

   * **Provider name**: The name of the provider, such as "China Mobile".

   * **Name of entry personnel**: The names of the personnel entering for site survey.

   * **ID card**: The IDs of the personnel entering for site survey.

   * **Mobile phone number**: The mobile phone number of the personnel entering for site survey.

   * **Entry remarks**: Other special information that needs to be noted.

   * **Notification channel**: Set how to receive alarm notifications. Multiple selections are supported.

2. Upon receipt, BytePlus will handle the ticket promptly. Wait patiently for approval.

3. After approval, follow the entry time and personnel in the site survey application to enter on time and complete the survey.
