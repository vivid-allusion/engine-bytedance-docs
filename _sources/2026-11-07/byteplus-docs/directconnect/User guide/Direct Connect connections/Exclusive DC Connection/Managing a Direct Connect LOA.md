Letter of Authorization (LOA) is a letter authorizing access. The Direct Connect LOA authorizes third\-party line operators to enter the BytePlus access point data center for construction and DC Connection installation.

<span id=".57yW6L6RTE9B"></span>
## Edit a LOA

After submitting a LOA application for Direct Connect, if the LOA details are incorrect or the approval is denied, you can edit and resubmit the LOA application before the construction is completed and reported.


1. Log in to [the Direct Connect Connection console](https://console.byteplus.com/dc/PhysicalConnection).

2. In the top menu bar, select the region of the DC connection whose completion report is pending.

3. In the DC Connection list, click **Edit LOA** or  **··· \> Edit LOA** to the right of the target DC Connection, and modify related parameters based on parameters in [Apply for LOA](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/directconnect/Accessing-a-Direct-Connect-connection-1#.5q2l6aqk5LqM77ya55Sz6K-3TE9B).

4. Click **Submit** to resubmit the LOA application.


<span id=".5LiL6L29TE9B"></span>
## Download a LOA

After a LOA application for Direct Connect is approved, you can download the LOA and obtain a PDF file. The line operator must provide this file when entering the BytePlus data center for construction.


1. Log in to [the Direct Connect Connection console](https://console.byteplus.com/dc/PhysicalConnection).

2. In the top menu bar, select the region of the DC connection whose completion report is pending.

3. In the DC Connection list, click  **··· \> Download LOA** on the right side of the target DC Connection to obtain the LOA for entry construction.
