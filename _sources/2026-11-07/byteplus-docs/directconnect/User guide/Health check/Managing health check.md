## View health check instance details


1. Log in to [the Direct Connect console.](https://console.byteplus.com/dc/PhysicalConnection)

2. In the top menu bar, select your target region.

3. Select **Health Check** in the left\-side navigation pane.

4. Click the name of the target health check instance to view instance details.

   * **Overview**: Click the **Overview** tab to view the details of the current health check, including name, ID, Virtual Private Cloud (VPC), subnet, and other information.

   * **Probe**: Click the **Probe** tab to view the probe details configured for the current health check.

   * **Data monitoring**: Click the **Data monitoring** tab to view the monitoring metrics of the current health check.

## Delete a probe

You cannot manually start or end the health check detection process. To stop detection, delete the probe.


1. Log in to [the Direct Connect console.](https://console.byteplus.com/dc/PhysicalConnection)

2. In the top menu bar, select your target region.

3. Select **Health Check** in the left\-side navigation pane.

4. Click **Manage probe** in the **Actions** column on the right side of the target health check instance.

5. Click **Delete** in the **Actions** column on the right side of the target probe.

6. Click **Delete** to complete the action.


<span id="L4X0spiW"></span>
## Delete a health check instance

Before deletion, delete all probes in the health check.


1. Log in to [the Direct Connect console.](https://console.byteplus.com/dc/PhysicalConnection)

2. In the top menu bar, select your target region.

3. Select **Health Check** in the left\-side navigation pane.

4. Click **Delete** in the **Actions** column on the right side of the target health check instance.

5. Click **Delete** to complete the action.
