After enabling pay\-as\-you\-go billing, when the Token usage of members within the enterprise exceeds the quota included in the package, continued calls to large models will be billed based on actual Token consumption. For the list prices of Tokens for each model, refer to [Model pricing](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/trae/billing-items).

<span id="9415651f"></span>
## Important note

After purchasing a resource package, the system will automatically enable pay\-as\-you\-go for your enterprise.

<span id="685add02"></span>
## Procedure

The steps to enable pay\-as\-you\-go billing for your enterprise are as follows:


1. Log in to the [TRAE Enterprise Console](https://console.enterprise.trae.ai/).

2. Go to **Enterprise Management** \> **Subscription Management**.

3. In the **Pay\-as\-you\-go Billing** area, click the **Enable pay\-as\-you\-go** button.

   This directs you to the **Usage statistics** panel of the BytePlus TRAE Console.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/8ced1c54449a48bc8f7322f2fa71e382) </span>

4. Click the **Enable now** button.

5. Follow the on\-screen instructions to complete the process for enabling pay\-as\-you\-go.

   After enabling pay\-as\-you\-go, you can view the real\-time token usage for models in the **Usage statistics** panel of TRAE Console on BytePlus.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/bfad03d200e741be880f5ff62842f521) </span>
