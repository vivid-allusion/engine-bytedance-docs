When login fails and the system displays the prompt that the account does not exist, the underlying cause may vary. Please refer to the scenarios below.

<span id="e8fa1c47"></span>
## **TRAE ** Enterprise plan has not been purchased or account registration has not been completed

**Possible causes:** 


* The purchase of TRAE Enterprise planhas not been completed in the BytePlus Console.

* TRAE Enterprise plan has been purchased, but enterprise account registration has not been completed.


**Solution:** 

Refer to [Quickstart](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/trae/get-started-with-trae-enterprise) to purchase a TRAE Enterprise plan and register an enterprise account.

If the administrator has already completed these steps, verify that the member has been successfully invited.

<span id="89ed4fd0"></span>
## **Member has not completed registration through the invitation**

**Possible causes:** 


* The administrator has not yet sent an invitation to the member's email address.

* The member received the invitation email but did not click the registration link to complete registration.

* The invitation email was filtered into the spam folder or blocked by the mail system.


**Solution:** 


* **If you are a member:** 

   1. Check your inbox, including the spam/junk folder, for an invitation email from TRAE.

   2. Click the registration link in the invitation email and complete account registration.

   3. If you cannot find the invitation email, contact your administrator and ask them to resend the invitation.

* **If you are an administrator:** 

   Invite enterprise members to join your team.


<span id="4b6ece7d"></span>
### The login email does not match the registered email / Invalid email format

**Possible causes:** 


* The email address entered during login is different from the one used during registration or invitation.

* The email address contains a typo (for example, extra spaces or incorrect capitalization).

* The email address format is invalid (for example, a missing  **@**  symbol or a misspelled domain name).


**Solution:** 


* Check the email address carefully for typos, including leading/trailing spaces and capitalization.

* Ensure that the login email address exactly matches the email address used to receive the invitation.

* If you are unsure which email address was used for registration, contact your administrator for confirmation.
