You can manage the subscription for TRAE Enterprise Edition, including purchasing plans, upgrading plans, managing renewals, and more.

<span id="45b66b4d"></span>
## Plan details & billing rules

For details about the fees and benefits of the paid plan provided by TRAE Enterprise, as well as the billing rules, refer to [TRAE Enterprise plan](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/trae/trae-enterprise-plans).

## Purchase a plan


1. Go to the [BytePlus TRAE Console](https://console.byteplus.com/trae/overview).

2. In the **Order management** area, click the **Buy TRAE** button.

   This directs you to the TRAE Enterprise plan purchase page.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/de738d5927b14777b386850cd6f9f2ab) </span>

3. Select the "Teams" plan (usually selected by default).

4. Set the number of seats to purchase.

5. Set the purchase duration (in months).

6. (Optional) Use a voucher.

7. Check the **I have read and agree to TRAE Terms of Service** checkbox.

8. Click the **Buy it now** button, then complete the payment.

   After the payment is complete, you will enter the following page:

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/64d2a06f24604511987baa7b3f3356b8) </span>

9. Click the **Go to TRAE** button.

   Or

   If you have closed the above page after payment, go to the TRAE Console on BytePlus and click the **Go to TRAE** button.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/e94ad382c35849f691e41ad392182ad4) </span>

10. On the TRAE Enterprise registration page, register as the super administrator of your enterprise.

## Manage renewals


1. Log in to the [TRAE Enterprise Console](https://console.enterprise.trae.ai/).

2. Go to **Enterprise Management** \> **Subscription Management**.

3. In the upper\-right corner of the page, click the **Go to manage** button.

   This directs you to the BytePlus TRAE Console.

4. In the **Order management** section, complete the required actions based on the renewal type:

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/218ecd1a6a114905b5e17cd0e6e18e7f) </span>

   
   <Tabs>
   <Tab zoneid="J8C8NGdqP7" title="Renew manually now">
   <TabTitle>Renew manually now</TabTitle>
   
   4. Click the **Renew now** button.
   
   5. In the **TRAE Enterprise renewal** popup:
   
      1. Set the renewal duration (in months).
   
      2. Check the **I agree to Enterprise version charging terms of service Agreement** checkbox.
   
      3. Click the **Confirm renewal** button.
   
      This directs you to the renewal processing page.
   
   6. Confirm the order information, then follow the on\-page instructions to complete payment.
   
   
   </Tab>
   <Tab zoneid="irsKZlfJI5" title="Enable auto-renewal">
   <TabTitle>Enable auto-renewal</TabTitle>
   
   4. Toggle the **Auto\-renewal** switch on.
   
      The **Set auto\-renewal** popup appears.
   
   5. Set the cycle for each auto\-renewal: 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, or 12 months.
   
   6. Set the number of auto\-renewals:
   
      * If you select **Unlimited renewals**, auto\-renewal will occur an unlimited number of times as long as your account balance is sufficient.
   
      * If you select **Set auto\-renewal times**, you need to continue to choose the number of auto\-renewals to occur. After reaching the set number of renewals, auto\-renewal will stop.
   
   7. Click the **OK** button.
   
   
   </Tab>
   </Tabs>

## Cancel subscription

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true">Note</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default">Only the super administrator has permission to cancel the current subscription.</div>


After canceling the current subscription, your enterprise is unable to use TRAE Enterprise. The data of your enterprise will be retained and can continue to be used after resubscription.


1. Log in to the [TRAE Enterprise Console](https://console.enterprise.trae.ai/).

2. Go to **Enterprise Management** \> **Subscription Management**.

3. In the **Other operations** section, click the **Unsubscribe** button.

4. In the **Unsubscribe** popup, confirm your unsubscription.

### The TRAE Enterprise plan I purchased has expired and I am unable to purchase a new one. What should I do?

After the current TRAE Enterprise plan expires, there will be a recovery period during which you cannot immediately purchase a new one.

You can visit the [BytePlus Renewal Management Center](https://console.byteplus.com/finance/renew) and search for TRAE to view the recovery countdown, and the possible results are as follows:


* **Not expired**: The countdown shows the remaining time until the service expires.

* **Expired (shutting down)** : The countdown shows the remaining time until the service is officially shut down.

* **Shut down**: The countdown shows the remaining time until the service resources are released.


After the countdown ends, you can place a new order.
