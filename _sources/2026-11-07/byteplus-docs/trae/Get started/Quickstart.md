This article introduces how to get all required tasks done before you and other members can use TRAE Enterprise, including registering a BytePlus account, purchasing a TRAE Enterprise plan, registering a TRAE Enterprise account, inviting members, and more.

<span id="ae56ea22"></span>
## Step 1: Register a BytePlus account

Go to[ ](https://console.volcengine.com/auth/login?redirectURI=%2Ftrae%2Foverview)[BytePlus official website](https://www.byteplus.com/) and register an account.

<span id="fbdd5b7b"></span>
## Step 2: Purchase a TRAE Enterprise plan

After registering a BytePlus account, you need to purchase a TRAE Enterprise plan through the BytePlus TRAE Console.

Before purchasing, it is recommended to read these articles learn about TRAE Enterprise's subscription and billing rules: [Subscription system](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/trae/billing-overview), [TRAE Enterprise plan](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/trae/trae-enterprise-plans), and [Billing items](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/trae/billing-items). After understanding the billing information for TRAE Enterprise, follow these steps to purchase the required package:


1. Go to the [BytePlus TRAE Console](https://console.byteplus.com/trae/overview).

2. In the **Order management** area, click the **Buy TRAE** button.

   This directs you to the TRAE Enterprise plan purchase page.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/de738d5927b14777b386850cd6f9f2ab) </span>

3. Select the "Teams" plan (usually selected by default).

4. Set the number of seats to purchase.

5. Set the purchase duration (in months).

6. (Optional) Use a voucher.

7. Check the **I have read and agree to TRAE Terms of Service** checkbox.

8. Click the **Buy it now** button.

9. Complete the payment.


<span id="5959f090"></span>
## Step 3: Register a super admin account

Register a super admin account to manage enterprise settings, members, billing, and more. This account will be associated with your verified enterprise.


1. On the payment complete page, click the **Go to TRAE** button.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/64d2a06f24604511987baa7b3f3356b8) </span>

   Or

   If you have closed the above page, go back to the TRAE Console on BytePlus, then click the **Go to TRAE** button.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/e94ad382c35849f691e41ad392182ad4) </span>

   This directs you to the TRAE enterprise sign\-up page.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">Note   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="1">If you do not see this page, it means your current BytePlus account has already purchased TRAE Enterprise. Please contact the BytePlus administrator of your enterprise for confirmation.   </div>
   

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/6057686cffc6430aaa4001e61cd09b3a) </span>

2. Enter your email address. It is recommended to use an enterprise email address.

3. Select **I agree ** **to** ** the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy**.

4. Click the **Sign Up** button.

   TRAE Enterprise will send a verification code to your email address. At the same time, you will enter the following email verification page. 

5. Go to your email to get the verification code.

6. On the **Verify** ** Email** page, enter the verification code, then click the **Verify** button.

   You will enter the username and password setup page.

7. Set an account name and password, confirm the password again, then click the **Sign up** button.

   After registration is complete, your account will become the enterprise's super administrator by default, and you will enter the following TRAE Enterprise Console.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/0cc884e1d95641ab8f67945a5f97968c) </span>

## Step 4: Invite members

Your purchased plan includes a certain number of default seats, enabling you to invite the corresponding number of members to join your TRAE enterprise.


1. In the TRAE Enterprise Console, go to **Enterprise Management** \> **Member Management**.

2. In the upper\-right corner of the page, click the  **+ Invite Members** button.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/287466e630bc4df8b6d9c53be7c96fe0) </span>

3. Select an invitation method from the menu and complete the required actions. For more information, refer to [Invite members](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/trae/manage-members).

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">Note   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="1">If the default number of seats in your plan is insufficient, you can click the <strong>Add Seats</strong> button in the upper\-right corner and purchase additional seats on BytePlus. For more information, refer to <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/trae/manage-members">Add seats</a>.   </div>

Based on actual needs, configure general settings for your enterprise in the TRAE Enterprise Console, including data protection, SSO login, ticket configuration, and more.

## Step 6: Install a TRAE client and use it


1. Go to the [TRAE official website](https://www.trae.ai).

2. Download and install a desired TRAE client.

3. Log in to this client with your TRAE enterprise account.

4. Follow the on\-screen instructions to complete initial setup, and use this client.
