You can configure general settings that apply globally for TRAE Enterprise, including data protection, SSO login, and ticket configuration.

<span id="00ff4c4a"></span>
## Data protection

The data protection feature is enabled by default and cannot be disabled. All enterprise usage data is encrypted throughout its lifecycle. TRAE does not store the data of your enterprise and does not use the code of your enterprise for model training.

<span id="1f79342c"></span>
## SSO login

Single sign\-on (SSO) enables enterprise members to log in to TRAE Enterprise using the enterprise's unified identity authentication system.

After the enterprise administrator configures SSO login in the TRAE Enterprise Console, members can simply enter their enterprise email addresses to be redirected to the enterprise's identity authentication system to complete the login. For how to set up SSO login, refer to [SSO login](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/trae/sso-login).

<span id="fce4f5e5"></span>
## Ticket configuration

After complete ticket configuration, members can submit tickets directly to BytePlus from the TRAE Enterprise Console, TRAE IDE, and TRAE Plugin, without needing to use a BytePlus account to log in to BytePlus's ticket system. For more information, refer to [Ticket configuration](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/trae/ticket-configuration).
