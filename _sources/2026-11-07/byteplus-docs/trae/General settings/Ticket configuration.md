Through ticket feedback configuration, enterprise members can submit tickets directly to BytePlus from the TRAE Enterprise Console, TRAE IDE, and TRAE plugins, without needing to log in to the BytePlus Ticket Center using a BytePlus account.

<span id="dd52e06e"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [TRAE Enterprise Console](https://console.enterprise.trae.ai/).

2. Go to **Enterprise Setup** \> **General Settings**.

3. In the **Ticket Configuration** section, click the **Operation Guide** button.

   The **Operation Guide** window appears.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/11c0599b594c47b78aa9a00350a1f5cc) </span>

4. In the **Create identity provider** section, complete the following steps:

   1. Click the hyperlinked **Click to download the file** text to download the metadata file.

   2. Click the **Create** button in the upper\-right corner.

      This directs you to the login page of the BytePlus Console.

   3. Log in with a BytePlus account that has configuration permissions.

      This directs you to the **Identity provider** panel on the BytePlus Console.

   4. Click the **Add identity provider** button.

      The identity provider configuration panel appears on the right side of the page.

   5. Follow the instructions in the **Operation Guide** window to set the fields on the identity provider configuration panel, then click the **Submit** button.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/657e31e031fb481b8b35cd0cde909e8c) </span>

5. Return to the TRAE Enterprise Console.

6. In the lower\-right corner of the **Operation Guide** window, click the **Next** button.

7. In the **Create IAM role** section, complete the following steps:

   1. Click the **Create** button in the upper\-right corner.

      This directs you to the **Role management** panel on the BytePlus Console.

   2. Click the **Create New Role** button.

      The role configuration panel appears on the right side of the page.

   3. Follow the instructions in the **Operation Guide** window to set the fields on the role configuration panel (in the **Add Permissions** section, click the **Skip** button directly).

      <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="2">Note      </div>
      

      <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="default" data-wrapper-indent="2">When you enter the <strong>Authorize</strong> section, directly click the <strong>Skip</strong> button.      </div>
      

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/3235dd0bb6714123bf7507013b4bb14a) </span>

8. Return to the TRAE Enterprise Console.

9. In the lower\-right corner of the **Operation Guide** window, click the **Next** button.

10. In the **Login verification** section, click the hyperlinked **Click the link** text.

   If you are directed to the following TRAE Enterprise ticket creation panel on BytePlus Ticket Center, it indicates that your ticket configuration works properly.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/ae1c336252f74911b57bc85d40697571) </span>

## Issue a ticket

The way to submit a ticket via each TRAE Enterprise client is as follows:


* **TRAE Enterprise Console**: In the lower\-left corner of the interface, click your avatar, then select **Contact Us** from the menu.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/20836942ab7041fb9ffa0db62357f92b) </span>

* **TRAE IDE**: In the upper\-left corner of the interface, click your avatar, then select **Report Issue** from the menu.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/1c170f511a5945df838c10efa829f2e2) </span>

* **TRAE 插件**: In the TRAE AI chat panel, click the  **···**  icon in the upper\-left corner, then select **Issue report** from the menu.

   <span>![图片](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/obj/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/0ae302d09b2f42acba7fd8b80289c7db) </span>
