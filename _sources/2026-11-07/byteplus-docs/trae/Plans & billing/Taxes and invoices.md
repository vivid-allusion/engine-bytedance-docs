TRAE Enterprise follows BytePlus's official tax policy. You can refer to the [Tax Rate Reference Table](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-tax-rate-reference) to learn about the specific tax rates of BytePlus in different countries or regions.

You can view and download your invoices through the BytePlus Console. The invoice includes detailed information such as the invoice number, issue date, and invoice amount. For more information, refer to "[Invoice Management](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-managing-invoices)".
