After you create an alerting notification policy, you can edit or delete the alerting notification policy. This topic describes how to manage alerting notification policies.

<span id=".57yW6L6R5ZGK6K2m6YCa55-l562W55Wl"></span>
## Edit an alerting notification policy

You can edit a created alerting notification policy. To do so, follow the steps below:


1. Log in to the [VMP console](https://console.byteplus.com/observe/prometheus).

2. On the top navigation bar, select the target region.

3. On the left navigation bar, click **Alert center \> Alerting notification policy**. In the list of alerting notification policies, find the policy that you want to edit and click **Edit** in the **Actions** column to edit the alerting notification policy.


<span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_dc754e5263f33458ab7eedba45cf2fbc.png) </span>

<span id=".5Yig6Zmk5ZGK6K2m6YCa55-l562W55Wl"></span>
## Delete an alerting notification policy

You can delete a created alerting notification policy. You can perform the following steps to delete the policy:


1. Log in to the [VMP console](https://console.byteplus.com/observe/prometheus).

2. On the top navigation bar, select the target region.

3. On the left navigation bar, click **Alert center \> Alerting notification policy**. In the list of alerting notification policies, find the policy that you want to delete and click **Delete** in the **Actions** column to delete the alerting notification policy.


<span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_e9a98429b8a9ad52ae9df2422cb9758b.png) </span>

After you click OK in the message that appears, the policy is deleted.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">If an alert rule is associated with the alert notification policy, deleting the policy will prevent users from receiving notifications triggered by the rule. Proceed with caution.</div>
