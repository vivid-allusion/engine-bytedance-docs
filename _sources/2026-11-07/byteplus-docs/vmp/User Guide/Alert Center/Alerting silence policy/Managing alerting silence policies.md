After an alert silence policy is created, you can enable, disable, edit, or delete the policy. This topic describes how to manage alert silence policies.

<span id=".56aB55So5ZGK6K2m6Z2Z6buY562W55Wl"></span>
## Disable an alert silence policy

You can disable a created alert silence policy. You can perform the following steps to disable a policy:


1. Log in to the [VMP console](https://console.byteplus.com/observe/prometheus).

2. On the top navigation bar, select the target region.

3. On the left navigation bar, click **Alert center \> Alerting silence policy**. In the list of alert silence policies, find the target policy and click the icon in the **Actions** column.<span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_db95a20a0c9c632930153311a4a85bfa.png) </span>In this way, you can enable or disable the alert silence policy.


<span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_029b2244a76677b8edf16ccee03e7ce9.png) </span>

<span id=".57yW6L6R5ZGK6K2m6Z2Z6buY562W55Wl"></span>
## Edit an alert silence policy

You can edit a created alert silence policy. You can perform the following steps to edit a policy:


1. Log in to the [VMP console](https://console.byteplus.com/observe/prometheus).

2. On the top navigation bar, select the target region.

3. On the left navigation bar, click **Alert center \> Alerting silence policy**. In the list of alert silence policies, find the target policy and click **Edit** in the **Actions** column. In this way, you can edit the alert silence policy.


<span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_a48dbc355c8e76dee79f87426f4e2cd3.png) </span>

<span id=".5Yig6Zmk5ZGK6K2m6Z2Z6buY562W55Wl"></span>
## Delete an alert silence policy

You can delete a created alert silence policy. The procedure is as follows:


1. Log in to the [VMP console](https://console.byteplus.com/observe/prometheus).

2. On the top navigation bar, select the target region.

3. On the left navigation bar, click **Alert center \> Alerting silence policy**. In the list of alert silence policies, find the target policy and click **Delete** in the **Actions** column. In the message that appears, click Confirm deletion to delete the policy.


<span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_627ea4996828f67150bea02ff3e6c2b9.png) </span>
