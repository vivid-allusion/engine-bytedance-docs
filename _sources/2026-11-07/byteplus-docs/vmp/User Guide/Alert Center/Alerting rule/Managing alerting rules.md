You can edit, delete, enable, or disable an alert rule based on your business requirements. This topic describes how to manage alert rules.

<span id=".57yW6L6R5ZGK6K2m6KeE5YiZ"></span>
## Edit an alert rule

You can edit a created alert rule. You can perform the following steps to edit a rule:


1. Log in to the [VMP console](https://console.byteplus.com/observe/prometheus).

2. On the left navigation bar, click **Alert center \> Alerting rule**. Click the **Single alerting rule** tab. In the list of alert rules, find the alert rule that you want to edit, and click **Edit** in the **Actions** column to edit the alert rule.

   <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_1adb2752616b038c67e906f43f268261.png) </span>

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">After you edit the alert rule, the status of the original alerts in this rule will change to <strong>Disabled</strong>.   </div>
   

<span id=".5Yig6Zmk5ZGK6K2m6KeE5YiZ"></span>
## Delete an alert rule

You can delete a created alert rule. You can perform the following steps to delete a rule:


1. Log in to the [VMP console](https://console.byteplus.com/observe/prometheus).

2. On the top navigation bar, select the target region.

3. On the left navigation bar, click **Alert center \> Alerting rule**. Click the **Single alerting rule** tab. In the list of alert rules, find the alert rule that you want to delete, and click **Delete** in the **Actions** column to delete the alert rule.

   <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_5b6635d560fb726b08187308e8e8b2ab.png) </span>

4. After you click OK in the message that appears, the rule is deleted.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">After you delete the alert rule, the status of the original alerts in this rule will change to <strong>Disabled</strong>.   </div>
   

<span id=".5ZCv55SoL-emgeeUqOWRiuitpuinhOWImQ=="></span>
## Enable or disable an alert rule

After you configure an alert rule, you can enable or disable the alert rule. You can perform the following steps to enable or disable the rule.


1. Log in to the [VMP console](https://console.byteplus.com/observe/prometheus).

2. On the top navigation bar, select the target region.

3. On the left navigation bar, click **Alert center \> Alerting rule**. Click the **Single alerting rule** tab. In the list of alert rules, find the alert rule that you want to enable or disable, and toggle the Enable/Disable switch in the **Actions** column to enable or disable the alert rule.

   <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_f043276c274e4273d55a4dae97dc90d6.png) </span>
