<span id="_1-definitions-and-interpretation"></span>
### 1. Definitions and Interpretation

**1.1** The following terms shall have the meanings assigned to each of them as below, unless otherwise provided hereunder.

"VMP" means Vital Managed Service for Prometheus, which is a standardized Prometheus service hosted on the Byteplus. Users can quickly create cloud storage with logical or physical isolation on the Byteplus. Compared to self\-built Prometheus, users do not need to manually download or configure the Prometheus environment, nor do they need to consider the storage cost of maintaining massive amounts of data. At the same time, VMP is compatible with open\-source Prometheus interfaces and the community Exporter ecosystem, providing users with a Prometheus integrated environment that is free of construction and maintenance, reducing development and maintenance costs.
