VMP supports multiple alert notification methods. You can use the DingTalk bot to receive alerts generated in VMP. This topic describes the procedure and considerations for receiving alerts by DingTalk.

<span id=".5YmN5o-Q5p2h5Lu2"></span>
## Prerequisites


* A VMP workspace has been created. For more details, see [Creating workspaces](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/vmp/Creating-workspaces).

* Cloud services or custom businesses are integrated with VMP. For more details, refer to [Integration guide](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/vmp/Monitoring-overview).


<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Procedure

<span id=".5q2l6aqk5LiA77ya6YWN572u6ZKJ6ZKJ5py65Zmo5Lq6"></span>
### Step 1: Configure the DingTalk robot


1. Log in to DingTalk with your account and create or find the group that needs to receive alerts. Click the group settings icon in the upper\-right corner of the group<span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_f53ca395ce9b85f8353fdcb05a479a96.png) </span>。

2. In the pop\-up window, click **Bot**.

   <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_eed458b4955f401c6bcf55f1aace830d.png) </span>

3. In the **Bot Management** window, click **Add Robot**.

   <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_063428c62f88aed01dc65d5f4b75dfd1.png) </span>

4. In the bot list, click **Custom**.



&nbsp;

5. Click **Add** to configure the custom robot.

   <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_4993b0a503c1e4e41f8a166d7b57e59e.png) </span>

6. Specify the information about the robot.

   * Specify a name for the robot.

   * Configure the security settings of the robot. For this example, select **Additional Signature** and copy the generated key, which will be used in the subsequent configuration.

   * Read and agree to the terms of the agreement and click **Finished** to complete the configuration of the robot.

      <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_84e75af3316d10e1be0686baf01c6fdb.png) </span>

7. Copy the **webhook address** generated by the DingTalk robot, which will be used in the subsequent configuration.

   <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_e7c96e8a0df874a0f373a9f89e1dd4c6.png) </span>

8. Click **Finished** to add the DingTalk robot.


<span id=".5q2l6aqk5LqM77ya6YWN572u5ZGK6K2m6IGU57O75Lq6"></span>
### Step 2: Configure an alert contact


1. Log in to the [VMP console](https://console.byteplus.com/observe/prometheus). 

2. In the left navigation pane, click **Alert Center \> Alert Notification Policy**. Click the **Notification contact** tab.

3. Click **Create notification contact** to configure the alerting notification contact. Here:

   * Enter the **webhook address** generated by the DingTalk robot in the **DingTalk robot** field.

   * Select the **Enable** check box for the **bot key** parameter, and enter the **key** generated by the DingTalk robot.

      <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_18f5436facc4b59d438dc4259897eeb9.png) </span>

4. Click **Verify** to check the connectivity between VMP and the DingTalk robot. When the verification is passed, the DingTalk robot will prompt that the connection is successful in the DingTalk group.

   <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_2042ddeb4c07e3841c9e8e31afb42eff.png) </span>

5. Click **Add** to complete the configuration.


<span id=".5q2l6aqk5LiJ77ya6YWN572u5ZGK6K2m6IGU57O75Lq657uE"></span>
### Step 3: Configure an alert contact group


1. In the left navigation pane, click **Alert Center \> Alert Notification Policy**. Click the **Notification contact** tab.

2. Click **Create notification contact**. Select **Contact group** for the Object type parameter, configure the alert notification contact group, and add users to this group.

   <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_753de1f458fb55a13636a4d94a1a20d1.png) </span>

3. Click **Add** to complete the configuration.


<span id=".5q2l6aqk5Zub77ya6YWN572u5ZGK6K2m6YCa55-l562W55Wl"></span>
### Step 4: Configure an alert notification policy


1. In the left navigation pane, click **Alert Center \> Alert Notification Policy**. Click the **Policy list** tab.

2. Click **Create notification policy** to configure an alert notification policy. Notification method: Select **DingTalk robot**.

   <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_c928b28693663895116e50a31e8f7361.png) </span>

3. Click **Add** to complete the configuration.


<span id=".5q2l6aqk5LqU77ya6YWN572u5ZGK6K2m6KeE5YiZ"></span>
### Step 5: Configure an alert rule


1. In the left navigation pane, click **Alert Center \> Alert Rule**.

2. Click **Create** to configure an alert rule. Here:

   * Select the **workspace** to which the alert rule applies.

   * Configure the query statement. `count(up) by (job)` is used in this example.

   * Configure a trigger policy. You can specify different thresholds for different levels of alerts.

      <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_d8544439bce7a45200d1ed4c5583c6a8.png) </span>

   * Configure an alert notification. Specify the **Notify policy** and **Aggregation policy** parameters.

      <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_5c64e0cac10fee4d5a88d0d12224796c.png) </span>

      <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="2">tip      </div>
      

      <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="2">If you have not configured an aggregation policy, you can directly use the system default policy, named "default". For the configuration method of an aggregation policy, see <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/vmp/Creating-alerting-aggregation-rules">Creating alert aggregation policies</a>.      </div>
      

3. Click **Add** to complete the configuration.


<span id=".5p-l55yL57uT5p6c"></span>
## View results

After alert\-related configurations are complete, alert notifications will be sent to DingTalk through the DingTalk bot when an alert that meets the alert rule is generated. In this case, you can receive the details of alert notifications in the DingTalk group.
