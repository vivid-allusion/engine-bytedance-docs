VMP supports multiple alert notification methods. You can use the standard Feishu bot to receive alerts generated in VMP. This topic describes the procedure and considerations for receiving alerts by Lark.

<span id=".5YmN5o-Q5p2h5Lu2"></span>
## Prerequisites


* A VMP workspace has been created. For more details, see [Creating workspaces](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/vmp/Creating-workspaces).

* Cloud services or custom businesses have been integrated with VMP. For details, see [Integration guide](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/vmp/Monitoring-overview).

* The Feishu administrator has added the alert bot application to your Feishu organization. For details, see the [official documentation of Feishu](https://open.feishu.cn/document/uAjLw4CM/ukTMukTMukTM/bot-v3/bot-overview).


<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Procedure

<span id=".5q2l6aqk5LiA77ya6YWN572u6aOe5Lmm5py65Zmo5Lq6"></span>
### Step 1: Configure the Feishu bot

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">The Feishu alert bots may vary across different organizations. This topic uses a <strong>custom bot</strong> as an example. If you do not know the name of the alert bot in your organization, contact your Feishu administrator.</div>



1. Log in to Feishu with your account and create or find the group that needs to receive alerts. Click the `...` icon in the upper right corner of the group, and click **Settings** on the drop\-down menu.


&nbsp;


&nbsp;

<span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_74a8c272feefb09f9642191d8a3c0bf9.png) </span>



&nbsp;

2. In the pop\-up window, click **Bots** \> **Add Bot**.


&nbsp;


&nbsp;

<span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_834272a0dfaed1590e909e23e30b0e82.png) </span>


&nbsp;


&nbsp;

3. In the bot list, find **Custom Bot** and click **Add**.



&nbsp;

<span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_ee4cd729d7f382b78e4e294606841f3e.png) </span>



&nbsp;

4. Specify the information about the robot.

   * Specify a name and description for the robot.

   * View and specify the information about the robot.

      * Copy the **webhook address** of the robot, which will be used in the subsequent configuration.

      * Select the **Set signature verification** check box and copy the generated **key**, which will be used in the subsequent configuration.



&nbsp;

<span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_02dfee64a0a166f48bf988113798abf9.png) </span>



&nbsp;

5. Click **Finish** to add the robot.


<span id=".5q2l6aqk5LqM77ya6YWN572u5ZGK6K2m6IGU57O75Lq6"></span>
### Step 2: Configure an alert contact


1. Log in to the [VMP console](https://console.byteplus.com/observe/prometheus). 

2. In the left navigation pane, click **Alert Center \> Alert Notification Policy**. Click the **Notification contact** tab.

3. Click **Create notification contact** to configure the alerting notification contact. Here:

   * Enter the **webhook address** generated by the Feishu bot in the **Feishu bot** field.

   * Select the **Enable** check box for the **bot key** parameter, and enter the **key** generated by Feishu bot.

      <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_0fa6886cedb9305258ec3e900ee1f4f3.png) </span>

4. Click **Verify** to check the connectivity between VMP and the Feishu bot. When the verification is passed, the Feishu bot will prompt that the connection is successful in the Feishu group.



&nbsp;

<span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_13f2bb2403e68aadd605a9274378fac3.png) </span>



&nbsp;

5. Click **Add** to complete the configuration.


<span id=".5q2l6aqk5LiJ77ya6YWN572u5ZGK6K2m6IGU57O75Lq657uE"></span>
### Step 3: Configure an alert contact group


1. In the left navigation pane, click **Alert Center \> Alert Notification Policy**. Click the **Notification contact** tab.

2. Click **Create notification contact**. Select **Contact group** for the Object type parameter, configure the alert notification contact group, and add users to this group.

   <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_753de1f458fb55a13636a4d94a1a20d1.png) </span>



&nbsp;

3. Click **Add** to complete the configuration.


<span id=".5q2l6aqk5Zub77ya6YWN572u5ZGK6K2m6YCa55-l562W55Wl"></span>
### Step 4: Configure an alert notification policy


1. In the left navigation pane, click **Alert Center \> Alert Notification Policy**. Click the **Policy list** tab.

2. Click **Create notification policy** to configure an alert notification policy. Notification method: Select **Feishu bot**.

   <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_e691674c3837610fed783f729ce56780.png) </span>

3. Click **Add** to complete the configuration.


<span id=".5q2l6aqk5LqU77ya6YWN572u5ZGK6K2m6KeE5YiZ"></span>
### Step 5: Configure an alert rule


1. In the left navigation pane, click **Alert Center \> Alert Rule**.

2. Click **Create** to configure an alert rule. Here:

   * Select the **workspace** to which the alert rule applies.

   * Configure the query statement. `count(up) by (job)` is used in this example.

   * Configure a trigger policy. You can specify different thresholds for different levels of alerts.

      <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_d8544439bce7a45200d1ed4c5583c6a8.png) </span>

   * Configure an alert notification. Specify the **Notify policy** and **Aggregation policy** parameters.

      <span>![图片](https://portal.volccdn.com/obj/volcfe/cloud-universal-doc/upload_5c64e0cac10fee4d5a88d0d12224796c.png) </span>

      <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="2">tip      </div>
      

      <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="2">If you have not configured an aggregation policy, you can directly use the system default policy, named "default". For the configuration method of an aggregation policy, see <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/vmp/Creating-alerting-aggregation-rules">Creating alert aggregation policies</a>.      </div>
      

3. Click **Add** to complete the configuration.


<span id=".5p-l55yL57uT5p6c"></span>
## View results

After alert\-related configurations are complete, alert notifications will be sent to Feishu through the Feishu bot when an alert that meets the alert rule is generated. In this case, you can receive the details of alert notifications in the Feishu group.

&nbsp;

Click **View Details** to go to the **Alert event** page to view the detailed information about the alert. For more details, refer to [Viewing alerts](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/vmp/Viewing-alerts).
