Dear user,

Starting from ‌**00:00:00 on August 3**, **2026**, the billing\-by\-written\-metric\-volume option is gradually available across all regions to more accurately reflect your actual resource consumption and provide a more cost\-effective billing solution.


* **Billing ** **rules**: The billing\-by\-written\-metric\-volume option is based on the size of uncompressed metric samples ingested into Prometheus instances. It is suitable for scenarios where metrics carry only a small number of labels. For details on billing by written metric volume, see [Pay-as-you-go](https://docs.volcengine.com/docs/6731/447498).

* **Changing the billing method**: You can choose the billing method that fits your needs, but the billing method can be changed only once for a workspace. For details, see [Changing the workspace billing method](https://docs.volcengine.com/docs/6731/2488939).


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">Warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">The billing\-by\-written\-metric\-volume billing mode is currently in trial operation and available only for new users.</div>
