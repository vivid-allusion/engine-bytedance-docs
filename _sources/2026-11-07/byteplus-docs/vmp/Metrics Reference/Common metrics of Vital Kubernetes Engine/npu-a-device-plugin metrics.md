npu\-a\-device\-plugin serves as a management component for NPU devices in VKE clusters and supports the use of NPU devices in containers. This topic describes the common metrics of npu\-a\-device\-plugin in the VKE cluster.

Vital Managed Service for Prometheus (VMP) classifies the metrics reported by VKE clusters into the following types: **basic metrics**, **other metrics**, and **custom metrics**. Billing methods vary based on different metric types.
