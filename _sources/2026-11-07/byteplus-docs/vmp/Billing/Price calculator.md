## Procedure

Go to the [Pricing](https://www.byteplus.com/en/pricing?product=VMP&tab=2) page. On the Pricing calculator tab, specify parameters such as **Region**, **Billing Method**, **Instance Specifications**, and **Cloud Basic Metric** based on your business requirements to view the estimated costs.

<span id="lMVLzFOG"></span>
## What to do next

Click **Add to estimate** to add the selected configurations to the purchase list. Then, you can view price details on the **Cost details** tab for subsequent purchases.
