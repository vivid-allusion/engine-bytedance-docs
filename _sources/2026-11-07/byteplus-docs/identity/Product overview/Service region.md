A region refers to the geographic area where a physical data center is located. Once a resource is created, its region cannot be changed. Internal networks between different regions are isolated from each other to ensure maximum stability and fault tolerance.

<span id="7df997b3"></span>
## How to choose a service region?

When selecting a region, primarily consider the following two factors:


* **Geographic location**: To reduce network latency and improve access speed, it is recommended to choose a region close to you or your target users.

* **Resource pricing**: Resource prices may vary between regions. If your users are in Chinese mainland, network latency is not a primary consideration, as regions within Chinese mainland have minimal differences in infrastructure, BGP network quality, and resource configuration.
