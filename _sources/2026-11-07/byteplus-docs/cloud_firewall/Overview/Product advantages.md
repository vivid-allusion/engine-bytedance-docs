## **SaaS\-based deployment, plug and play**

Byteplus Cloud Firewall uses SDN technology to provide a SaaS\-based firewall solution. Users do not need to deploy any equipment; just click to start and immediately receive protection.

<span id="024bce46"></span>
## **Smooth scaling, on\-demand allocation**

Byteplus Cloud Firewall supports smooth scaling of performance and allows elastic expansion of business bandwidth, protective assets, and log capacity, enabling on\-demand purchase and use.

<span id="39a1c130"></span>
## **High reliability, business assurance**

Byteplus Cloud Firewall adopts a multi\-level disaster recovery solution, which is more reliable than primary and standby disaster recovery. It also uses a high\-performance product architecture to ensure that user business is not affected during access migration.

<span id="fe57fb93"></span>
## **Access relationships visualized, clear boundary security**

Real\-time display of asset access relationships and traffic trends helps users identify abnormal traffic and timely adjust protection strategies for security reinforcement.
