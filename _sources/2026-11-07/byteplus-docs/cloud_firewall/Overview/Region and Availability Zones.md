## Region (Region)

**Introduction**: The region (Region) refers to the geographical area where the physical data centers of cloud services are located, usually divided by the city where the data centers are situated. Different regions are completely isolated from each other, ensuring the stability and fault tolerance of cross\-region services.

<span id="91b025d2"></span>
## Availability Zone (AZ)

**Introduction**: An Availability Zone (Availability Zone) refers to a physical area within the same region that has independent power and network supply. Different availability zones are physically isolated but connected via network, ensuring business continuity and enabling nearby disaster recovery capabilities.
