You can subscribe to a Cloud Firewall instance on the **Instance management** page. There, choose your desired subscription duration, accept the service agreement, and confirm your order details and payment. Your subscribed package will be activated immediately upon successful payment.

<span id="instance-management"></span>
### Instance management

The **Instance management** page displays the information about the specifications and status of the Cloud Firewall instance you are subscribing to, such as the instance status, billing mode, automatic renewal status, subscription time, and expiration time. The following instance specification details can be found: edition of the instance, number of protected assets, business bandwidth, log service, log capacity, log retention period, and number of access control policies.

### Renewal management

To ensure that your network is always protected, make sure to renew your instance on the **Renew** page by clicking the **Renew** button. There, choose your desired renewal length, accept the service agreement, and confirm your order details and payment. Your renewed package will be activated immediately upon successful payment.
