## Main protocol support

Classic Load Balancer (CLB) provides listening and traffic forwarding capabilities for UDP, TCP, HTTP, and HTTPS protocols, improving the smoothness and fault tolerance of application system access.

<span id=".56iz5a6a5Y-v6Z2g"></span>
## Stable and reliable

CLB employs cluster architecture to eliminate single points of failure and complements by 24/7 technical support, providing continuous and stable enterprise\-grade assurance.

<span id=".6LaF6auY5oCn6IO9"></span>
## Ultra\-high performance

CLB offers multiple instance types in different performance with guaranteed ceilings, meeting diverse business scenarios with high forwarding performance and stability.

<span id=".54G15rS76LCD5bqm"></span>
## Flexible scheduling

CLB supports various routing algorithms and enables traffic scheduling based on different domain names and URLs. You can select appropriate algorithms and rules to allocate user traffic according to your needs, enhancing the flexibility of the application system.

<span id=".6auY5Y-v55So5oCn"></span>
## High availability

CLB service supports primary\-secondary disaster recovery across availability zones. When the instance's primary AZ network fails or becomes unavailable, access traffic can be forwarded through the secondary AZ to the corresponding backend servers, ensuring business continuity.
