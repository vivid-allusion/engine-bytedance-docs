This topic describes the impact of an overdue payment on your Classic Load Balancer (CLB) instances.

## Pay\-as\-you\-go

An overdue account balance will lead to the suspension, and eventual release, of Pay\-As\-You\-Go CLB instances. The platform has set a grace period for resources. Within the grace period, the resources are retained and you can continue using the resources after you recharge your account and clear overdue payments. No impact on the features of resources. If the overdue payment is not settled beyond the resource retention period, the CLB instance will be forcibly reclaimed, and all associated data will be irrecoverable. Regardless of whether your instances are suspended or released, you shall settle the issued bills based on actual usage.

> **tip**

> Based on Your settings, the platform will send you reminders via SMS, email, or internal message during stages such as CLB instance arrears, service suspension due to arrears, and resource release due to arrears. The specific suspension time is subject to the actual situation.

## Subscription

An overdue payment of an account does not affect the unexpired Subscription resources under the account. For more details, see [Expiration](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/clb/Billing-08).
