For subscription Classic Load Balancer (CLB) instances, the platform sends reminders via SMS, email, or internal messages 7 days, 3 days, 1 day before, and on the expiration day based on your settings. To avoid disruption to your business, renew your bandwidth package promptly when receving the notification.

If you renew before expiration suspension (within 12 hours after expiration), the start time of the renewed term is the expiration time of the previous term. If you renew after expiration suspension but before expiration reclaim, the start time of the renewed term is the actual renewal time.

<span id=".5omL5Yqo57ut6LS5"></span>
## Manual renewal

You can manually renew your CLB instance before it is reclaimed after expiration.

Procedures:


1. Log in to the [CLB console](https://console.byteplus.com/clb/LoadBalancer).

2. In the top menu bar, select your target region.

3. Click ··· \> Renew on the right side of the target instance.

4. Select the renewal period.

5. Click **Pay** and complete the payment according to the guidance.

   Renewal succeeds upon payment completion.


<span id=".6Ieq5Yqo57ut6LS5"></span>
## Automatic renewal

When creating a subscription CLB instance, check the **Automatic renewal** option; you can also enable automatic renewal before the subscription expires. See the instructions [for automatic renewal](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/byteplus-platform/docs-auto-renewal-rules).

If you have available coupons in your account, they will be prioritized for auto renewal.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">For resources with automatic renewal enabled, you can also manually renew them at any time. Manual renewal does not invalidate automatic renewal. The system will deduct fees 7 days before the updated expiration date of the resource.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">Automatic renewal cannot be enabled or modified for expired CLB instances.</div>
