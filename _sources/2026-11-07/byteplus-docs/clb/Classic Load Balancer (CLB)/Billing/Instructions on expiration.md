After a subscription CLB instance expires, the platform reserves a retention period during which you can renew or unsubscribe. Renewing will allow continued use of the CLB instance, while unsubscribing will result in the immediate release of the resource. If you do not perform any actions during the retention period, the CLB resources will be forcibly released and cannot be recovered once released.

**Instructions on release after expiration**


* Expiration time: 23:59:59 on the expiration day.

* Stopping time: 12 hours after expiration.

* Release time: 14 days and 12 hours after shutdown.


If you determine that you will no longer use your Subscription Classic Load Balancer (CLB) after it expires and before it is reclaimed, you can delete it. After deletion, the Classic Load Balancer (CLB) data cannot be recovered. Please fully consider the impact on your current business and perform this operation with caution.
