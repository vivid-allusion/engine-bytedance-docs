## Billing types

CLB instances support two billing types: post paid and subscription, as listed in the table below.


|Billing methods |Description |Applicable scenarios |
|---|---|---|
|Subscription |You must pay for a resource before you can use the resource. |Applicable to the scenarios with long\-term use and relatively stable bandwidth requirements. This billing method can significantly reduce your expenses. |
|Pay\-as\-you\-go |Post paid |Scenarios in which business increases significantly in the short term, such as flash sale scenarios. |
