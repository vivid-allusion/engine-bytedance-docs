## Operation scenario

By configuring alarm rules for Classic Load Balancer (CLB), you can promptly detect exceptions of CLB monitoring metrics and rectify faults accordingly to ensure the stable operation of your application. You can either customize alarm rules for CLB instances and listeners, or use the preset alarm templates provided by CLB.

<span id=".5YmN5o-Q5p2h5Lu2"></span>
## Prerequisites

IAM users may not be able to directly configure alarm rules. If you need to configure an alarm rule, refer to [Why are IAM users unable to view monitoring data](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/clb/Log-and-monitoring-faq#.5Li65LuA5LmI5a2Q55So5oi35peg5rOV5p-l55yL55uR5o6n5pWw5o2u77yf) to grant management permissions of Cloud Monitor to the IAM user.

## Procedure


1. Log in to [the Cloud Monitor console](http://console.volcengine.com/cloud-monitor/overview).

2. In the left navigation pane, select **Alert Center \> Alert Template**.

3. Select the **Preset alarm template** tab.

4. Specify **Name** as a search condition in the search box, and enter **Classic Load Balancer** to find the target template.

5. Click the preset template name CLB **preset alarm template**, view the detailed rules for triggering alerts, and check whether the template meets your requirements.

6. Click the switch in the **One\-Click Enable** column on the right side of the template list, or the **Enable** button on the template details page to enable the alarm template.

7. In the **Configure contact group** Pop\-up window, specify the channel information where you wish to receive alarm notifications, then click the **Confirm** button to complete the configuration.

   You can refer to [Alert contact person](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/cloudmonitor/Alarm-contacts) to configure the contact person and contact information for receiving alarms.

8. After one\-click enable, the BytePlus account automatically generates and enables an alert policy; disabling one\-click enable will immediately delete all alert policies generated by the preset alarm template.
