## Scenarios

You can refer to this operation to modify the certificate attached to the HTTPS Listener.

## Procedures

1. Log in to the [CLB console](https://console.byteplus.com/clb/LoadBalancer).

2. In the top menu bar, select the target project and region.

3. Click Configure Listener to the right of the target CLB instance.

4. Click the ··· \> Edit Certificate button on the right side of the target listener.

5. Re\-select the certificate.

6. Click **Migrate** to complete the action.
