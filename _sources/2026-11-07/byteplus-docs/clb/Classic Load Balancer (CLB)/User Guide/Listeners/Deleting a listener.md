## Prerequisites


* Confirm that the original business has been migrated or turned off before deleting the listener.

* The CLB instance has not enabled **modification protection** feature.

* The layer\-7 listener is unrelated to any custom forwarding rule.


<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Procedures


1. Log in to the [CLB console](https://console.byteplus.com/clb/LoadBalancer).

2. In the top menu bar, select the target project and region.

3. Click Edit Listener to the right of the target CLB instance.

4. Click ··· \> Delete on the right side of the target listener.

5. Click **Delete** to complete the action.
