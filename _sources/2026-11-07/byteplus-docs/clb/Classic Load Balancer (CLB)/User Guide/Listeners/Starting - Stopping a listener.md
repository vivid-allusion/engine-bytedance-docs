After a listener is created, it starts automatically. You can stop or start the listener at any time. A stopped listener no longer accepts or forwards client requests.

<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Procedures


1. Log in to the [CLB console](https://console.byteplus.com/clb/LoadBalancer).

2. In the top menu bar, select the project and region.

3. Click Configure Listener to the right of the target CLB instance.

   * Start: Click ··· \> Start to the right of the target listener to complete the action.

   * Stop: Click ··· \> Stop to the right of the target listener to complete the action.
