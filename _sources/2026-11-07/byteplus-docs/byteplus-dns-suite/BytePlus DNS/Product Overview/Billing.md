* **Billing Method**: Prepaid for annual or monthly subscription

* **Billing Item**: DNS service fee

   * **DNS service fee** = **Unit Price** \* **Number of domains** \* **Subscription duration**



|**Billing item** |**Billing method** |**Unit price** |
|---|---|---|
|DNS service fee |Prepaid for annual or monthly subscription |**Professional Edition**: $8.28/year, $0.69/month<br><br>**Enterprise Edition**: $23.88/year, $1.99/month<br><br>**Ultimate Edition**: $83.88/year, $6.99/month<br><br>**Exclusive Edition**: $4260/year, $355/month |


Refer to [Editions](https://docs.byteplus.com/byteplus-dns-suite/docs/editions) for more information about different editions.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true">tip</div>



* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">The minimum subscription duration is 1 month. The fee will be deducted monthly/yearly upon subscription and the DNS service takes effect immediately after successful subscription.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">You can upgrade the edition of a domain while it is still not expired. The price difference should be paid according to the displayed upgrade page.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">If you fail to renew a paid version of a domain before expiration, the domain is automatically downgraded to the free version. Renew your domain in time to avoid service interruptions.</div>


* <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">After subscribing to a paid version of a domain, you cannot cancel your subscription.</div>
