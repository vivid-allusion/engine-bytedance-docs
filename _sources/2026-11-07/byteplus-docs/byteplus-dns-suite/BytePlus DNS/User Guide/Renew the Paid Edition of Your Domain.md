This document introduces how to renew the paid edition of a domain in BytePlus DNS.

<span id="manually-renew-the-paid-edition-of-your-domain"></span>
## Manually renew the paid edition of your domain

Follow the steps below to learn how to manually renew the paid edition of your domain.


1. Open the [BytePlus DNS console](https://console.byteplus.com/dns-suite/dns).

2. On the **Domain management** page, find the domain you need to enable automatic renewal for. Click **Renew** in the **Actions** column.

3. On the **Renew** page, set the purchase duration and check the agreement. Click **Submit order**.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">The <strong>Enable auto renewal</strong> option is checked by default. Once automatic renewal is enabled, BytePlus will start deducting the fee at 0:00 on the seventh day before the resource expires, with a renewal duration of one year. You can cancel automatic renewal at any time. You can check or uncheck <strong>Enable auto renewal</strong> according to your needs.   </div>
   

4. On the **Confirm order information** page, click **Next step**.

5. On the **Submit payment information** page, select the payment method and click **Pay**.

6. After the payment is completed, you can find the upgraded domain on the **Domain management** page.


<span id="enable-automatic-renewal-for-your-domain"></span>
## Enable automatic renewal for your domain

Once automatic renewal is enabled, BytePlus will start deducting the fee at 0:00 on the seventh day before the resource expires. You can cancel automatic renewal at any time.

Follow the steps below to learn how to enable automatic renewal for a paid edition of your domain.


1. Open the [BytePlus DNS console](https://console.byteplus.com/dns-suite/dns).

2. On the **Domain management** page, click **Auto renewal**. The console will redirect to the **Renewal management** page in the BytePlus billing center.

3. On the **Renewal management** page, select the **Manual renewal** tab to view instances with the product **BytePlus DNS**.

4. Find the instance you need to enable automatic renewal for by its instance ID, and click **Enable auto\-renewal** in the **Actions** column.

5. On the **Enable auto\-renewal** page, enable automatic renewal for your instance.
