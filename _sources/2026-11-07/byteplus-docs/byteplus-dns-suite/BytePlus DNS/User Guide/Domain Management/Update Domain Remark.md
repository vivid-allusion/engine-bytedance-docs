This document describes how to update the remark for your domain in BytePlus DNS.

<span id="steps"></span>
## Steps

Follow the steps below to update the remark for your domain:


1. Open the [BytePlus DNS console](https://console.byteplus.com/dns-suite/dns).

2. On the **Domain management** page, find the domain name for which you want to update the remark. Click on the edit icon in the **Remark** column.

3. Enter the new remark and click **Confirm**.
