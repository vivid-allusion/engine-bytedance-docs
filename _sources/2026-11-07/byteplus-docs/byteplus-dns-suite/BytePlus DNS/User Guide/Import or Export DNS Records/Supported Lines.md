<span id="lines-supported-by-different-editions"></span>
# Lines supported by different editions

<span id="free"></span>
## Free


* Geolocation line: Chinese mainland, Global (excluding Chinese mainland).

* Line group: Allows you to add all ISP lines and geolocation lines supported by the free edition.

* ISP Line: Precise to ISPs in Chinese mainland, including China Telecom, China Unicom, and China Mobile.


<span id="professional"></span>
## Professional


* Geolocation line: Precise to regions in Chinese mainland, precise to global continents (excluding Chinese mainland).

* Line group: Allows you to add all ISP lines, geolocation lines, and cloud vendor lines supported by the professional edition.

* ISP Line: Precise to ISPs in Chinese mainland, including China Telecom, China Unicom, China Mobile, China Education and Research Network (CERN), and China Broadnet.


<span id="enterprise"></span>
## Enterprise


* Geolocation line: Precise to regions/provinces in Chinese mainland, precise to continents/countries or regions globally (excluding Chinese mainland).

* Cloud vendor line: Alibaba Cloud, Tencent Cloud, Baidu Cloud, Huawei Cloud, and Volcengine.

* Line group: Allows you to add all ISP lines, geolocation lines, and cloud vendor lines supported by the enterprise edition.

* ISP Line: Precise to ISPs in Chinese mainland, including China Telecom, China Unicom, China Mobile, China Education and Research Network (CERN), and China Broadnet, precise to regions/provinces.
