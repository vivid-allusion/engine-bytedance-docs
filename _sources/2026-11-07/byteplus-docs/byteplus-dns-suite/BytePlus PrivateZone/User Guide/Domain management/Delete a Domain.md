This document describes how to delete your domain in BytePlus PrivateZone.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="danger" data-tips-is-title="true">danger</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="danger">Once a domain is deleted, the domain's DNS records will be cleared, and the DNS resolution service will no longer be active. Therefore, you need to proceed with caution.</div>


<span id="prerequisites"></span>
## Prerequisites

You have [created a domain](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-dns-suite/PU6MMV).

<span id="steps"></span>
## Steps

Complete the following steps to delete a domain.


1. Open the [BytePlus PrivateZone console](https://console.byteplus.com/dns-suite/dns/privatezone/zones).

2. On the **Domain management** page, check the domain you wish to delete, and click **Delete**.

3. In the pop\-up dialog box, enter `delete` in the textbox and click **Delete**.
