This document describes how to update your policy configuration in BytePlus GTM.

<span id="prerequisites"></span>
## Prerequisites

You have already created and configured a GTM instance.

<span id="steps"></span>
## Steps

Follow the steps below to update your policy configuration.


1. On the [Instance management page](https://console.byteplus.com/dns-suite/gtm/instances), find your GTM instance. Click the domain name in the **Domain** column.

2. In the **Policy management** tab, update your policy configuration in the **Policy configuration** section.

   * For basic routing, you can:

      * Add rules in the **Custom rules** tab.

      * Update health check parameters in the **Common configuration** tab.

      * View the Sankey diagram of traffic routing in the **Expected traffic view** tab.

   * For intelligent routing, you can:

      * Update traffic targets, routing modes, and health check parameters in the **Common configuration** tab.

      * Add custom rules in the **Custom rules** tab. Custom rules take precedence over system\-generated rules.

      * View the Sankey diagram of traffic routing in the **Expected traffic view** tab.
