BytePlus GTM will automatically perform failover at the pool set and address levels. Additionally, BytePlus GTM can send alarm notifications to your configured alarm contact group or alarm callback through [BytePlus Cloud Monitor](https://www.byteplus.com/en/product/cloudmonitor). You can create alarm strategies for your GTM instance in BytePlus GTM. If an event in your GTM instance triggers an alarm strategy, BytePlus Cloud Monitor will send alarm notifications to your configured alarm contact group or alarm callback.
