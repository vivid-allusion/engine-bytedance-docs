This document introduces how to update an alarm strategy in BytePlus GTM.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning" data-tips-is-title="true">warning</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="warning">After you update an alarm strategy, all GTM instances associated with that alarm strategy uses the updated alarm strategy.</div>


<span id="steps"></span>
## Steps

Follow the steps below to update an alarm strategy.


1. On the [Alarm notification page](https://console.byteplus.com/dns-suite/gtm/alarm), find the alarm strategy you need to update. In the **Actions** column, click **Manage**.

2. On the **View alarm strategy** page, click **Edit**. You can then update the alarm strategy. You can refer to [Create an Alarm Strategy](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-dns-suite/2hG26c) to learn how to configure the parameters.

3. After completing the update, click **Confirm** to finish the update.
