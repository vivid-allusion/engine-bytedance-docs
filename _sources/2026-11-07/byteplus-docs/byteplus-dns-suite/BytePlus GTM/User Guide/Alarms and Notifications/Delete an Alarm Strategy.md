This document introduces how to delete an alarm strategy in BytePlus GTM.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="danger" data-tips-is-title="true">danger</div>


<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="danger">After you delete an alarm strategy, all GTM instances associated with that alarm strategy will automatically be disassociated from it. This operation cannot be undone. Please proceed with caution.</div>


<span id="steps"></span>
## Steps

Follow the steps below to delete an alarm strategy.


1. On the [Alarm notification page](https://console.byteplus.com/dns-suite/gtm/alarm), find the alarm strategy you need to delete. In the **Actions** column, click **More actions**.

2. In the dropdown menu, click **Delete**.

3. In the pop\-up dialog box, click **Confirm** to delete the alarm strategy.
