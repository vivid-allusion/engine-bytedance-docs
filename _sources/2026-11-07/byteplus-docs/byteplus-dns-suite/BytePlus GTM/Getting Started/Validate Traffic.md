After the GTM instance starts, user requests visiting your domain are routed per your policy configuration.

<span id="steps"></span>
## Steps

Next, verify whether user requests are routed per the requirements of your sample business scenario.

<span id="verify-traffic-via-the-actual-traffic-view"></span>
### Verify traffic via the actual traffic view

You can view the actual traffic view in the console to verify whether the traffic routing meets the business scenario's requirements. The traffic view is presented in the form of a Sankey diagram, showing how traffic is routed to different target addresses based on factors such as the user's geolocation, ISP, and the traffic capacity of the IDC. See [Check the Traffic View](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-dns-suite/LumlvU).

<span id="follow-up-steps"></span>
## Follow\-up steps

If the routing mode of your GTM instance is load feedback, you can report real\-time capacity, real\-time load, and target load to BytePlus GTM when the capacity of the pool or addresses changes. See [Report Load in Load Feedback Mode](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/byteplus-dns-suite/uO4mZu).
