## Convert Pay\-by\-bandwidth\-cap to Pay\-by\-data\-transfer

<span id=".57qm5p2f6ZmQ5Yi2"></span>
### Limits


* The total value of your account's [available balance](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/6269/100429) and coupons usable for IPv6 Internet bandwidth is not less than USD 100.00, and the available balance must be no less than 0. For details, see [Recharge guide](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/6269/100434).

* If the pre\-conversion bandwidth cap is greater than the post\-conversion bandwidth cap (200 Mbps), the converted bandwidth defaults to the maximum of 200 Mbps.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">Decreasing bandwidth cap may affect business traffic and lead to packet loss. Evaluate the possible impacts of the action on your business and proceed with caution.   </div>
   

<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
### Procedure


1. Log in to [the IPv6 Gateway console](https://console.byteplus.com/eip/ipv6s).

2. In the top menu bar, select your target region.

3. Click **Manage** to the right of the target IPv6 Gateway instance to enter the IPv6 Internet bandwidth tab on the details page.

4. In the IPv6 address list, click  **··· \> Change configuration** to the right of the target IPv6 Internet bandwidth, popping up the Modify IPv6 Internet bandwidth configuration window.

5. Select **Pay\-by\-data\-transfer** and change Bandwidth cap as needed.

6. Click **Save** to complete the conversion.

## Convert Pay\-as\-you\-go to Pay\-by\-bandwidth\-cap

<span id=".57qm5p2f6ZmQ5Yi2"></span>
### Limits


* The total value of your account's [available balance](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/6269/100429) and coupons usable for IPv6 Internet bandwidth is not less than USD 100.00, and the available balance must be no less than 0. For details, see [Recharge guide](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/6269/100434).

* During conversion, adjust the bandwidth cap based on your business requirements.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">Decreasing bandwidth cap may affect business traffic and lead to packet loss. Evaluate the possible impacts of the action on your business and proceed with caution.   </div>

### Procedure


1. Log in to [the IPv6 Gateway console](https://console.byteplus.com/eip/ipv6s).

2. In the top menu bar, select your target region.

3. Click **Manage** to the right of the target IPv6 Gateway instance to enter the IPv6 Internet bandwidth tab on the details page.

4. In the IPv6 address list, click  **··· \> Change configuration** to the right of the target IPv6 Internet bandwidth, popping up the Modify IPv6 Internet bandwidth configuration window.

5. Select **Pay\-by\-bandwidth\-cap** and change Bandwidth cap as needed.

6. Click **Save** to complete the conversion.
