This topic describes the impact of arrears on IPv6 Internet bandwidth. [View Available Balance](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/6269/100429#%E6%9D%BF%E5%9D%97%E8%AF%B4%E6%98%8E)

Under Pay\-as\-you\-go, an account in arrears may cause IPv6 Internet bandwidth to be suspended or even released. The system has set a grace period for resources. Within the grace period, the resources are retained and you can continue using the resources after you recharge your account and clear overdue payments. No impact on the features of resources. After the grace period has expired, the system will forcibly reclaim the IPv6 Internet bandwidth resources and permanently delete all related data. Regardless of whether your instances are suspended or released, you shall settle the issued bills based on the actual.

<div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip">BytePlus platform will send SMS/email/onsite messages to remind you during various stages of IPv6 Internet bandwidth (including Entering arrears, Suspension due to arrears, and Release due to arrears) based on <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/message_center/Basic-Message-Receiving-Configuration">your settings</a>.</div>


**Overdue stage description**


* Suspension time: 2 hours after entering arrears.

* Release time: 15 days and 2 hours after entering arrears.
