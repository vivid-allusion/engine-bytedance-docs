You can flexibly modify the maximum bandwidth of IPv6 Internet bandwidth based on your business needs.

<span id=".5pON5L2c6K-05piO"></span>
## Instructions


* Decreasing the maximum IPv6 Internet bandwidth may affect services. Proceed with caution.

* You cannot modify the bandwidth cap of the managed IPv6 Internet bandwidth service associated with BytePlus Official Service.


<span id=".5pON5L2c5q2l6aqk"></span>
## Procedure


1. Log in to the [IPv6 Gateway console](https://console.byteplus.com/eip/ipv6s).

2. In the top menu bar, select your target region.

3. Click **Manage** to the right of the target IPv6 Gateway instance to enter the IPv6 Internet bandwidth tab on the details page.

4. Click  **··· \> Change configuration** to the right of the target IPv6 address to pop up the Modify IPv6 Internet bandwidth configuration window.

5. Modify the maximum bandwidth as needed, and optionally modify the metering method.

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-tips-is-title="true" data-wrapper-indent="1">tip   </div>
   

   <div data-tips="true" data-tips-type="tip" data-wrapper-indent="1">For more details on modifying the metering method, see <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/IPv6/Billing_methods_change">Billing conversion</a>.   </div>
   

6. Click **Save** to complete the modification.
